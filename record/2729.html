8月1日，中国人民解放军南部战区位黄岩岛领海、领空和周边海空域组织海空联合演训，重点演练侦察预警、威慑巡航、海空管制、联合突击等实战化科目，有效检验了部队海空协同、快速支援和体系制胜能力。黄岩岛是中国固有领土，菲方划设所谓黄岩岛“领海基线”非法无效。战区部队时刻保持高度戒备，坚决捍卫国家领土主权和海洋权益。 On August 1, the PLA Southern Theater Command (STC) conducted a joint naval-air training exercise in the territorial sea and airspace of China’s Huangyan Dao and its surrounding areas. The exercise focuses on realistic training subjects such as reconnaissance and early warning, deterrence patrol, air-sea control, and joint strike. It effectively honed the troops’ naval-air coordination, rapid support and win-by-system capabilities. Huangyan Dao is an inherent part of China’s territory. The so-called territorial sea baselines of Huangyan Dao designated by the Philippine side are illegal and invalid. Forces of the STC will stay on high alert at all times and resolutely safeguard China’s territorial sovereignty and maritime rights and interests.
