Summary

The PLA Navy hospital ship Jixiang Fangzhou departed Conakry on October 10 after a seven-day port call under the Harmonious Mission-2026 (和谐使命-2026) tasking — the ship's first-ever visit to Guinea — during which medical teams conducted surgeries aboard ship, exchanged at the China-Guinea Friendship Hospital, and deployed to an unspecified Guinean military camp. Guinean Prime Minister Bah Oury and the ministers of national defense and foreign affairs all boarded the ship, a level of host-government engagement that extends the pattern of Harmonious Mission visits generating senior political access in West African states with limited prior PLA Navy presence. The article documents the visit's diplomatic choreography — Confucius Institute exchanges, basketball matches, Guinean state media coverage — as the standard soft-power layering the PLA Navy has used consistently across this mission series, not as evidence of any new military arrangement.
