The series documents the PLA political work apparatus's current formula for ideological messaging to enlisted personnel: binding service-branch identity to Long March geography and framing routine technical roles — track maintenance, aircraft carrier deck work, fuel supply, missile operations — as continuations of revolutionary sacrifice under the 建军一百年奋斗目标 (centenary army-building goal).
