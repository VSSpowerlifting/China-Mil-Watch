Access refusals are respected, not worked around

When a source refuses an honestly identified collector — a login wall, a redirect off its own domains, or a challenge page served in place of a listing — the refusal is recorded as the outcome and collection stops there. No browser impersonation, no challenge solving, no proxying. An institution that wants to refuse this collector must be able to recognise it and say so, and defeating a refusal would destroy the capability that rule protects.
