A desk appears here as soon as its scope is declared. Published IPR editorial research is shown separately from collection into the production source archive; corpus statistics still describe actual admitted government originals, not editorial articles.

What a status means

What each desk status means, and what it implies about the record.
StatusMeaning
Live — collecting Sources are enabled and a scheduled run collects into the record. Only these desks have record counts.
Shadow evaluation — not in corpus Source material is evaluated in isolated shadow state, not admitted to the public IPR corpus or its record counts. A public desk page may separately provide scope information or approved official-source links; those are not archived IPR records.
Access blocked — not collecting Researched and reachable in principle, but the institution's discovery surface is not available to an honestly identified collector. Nothing has been collected.
Researched — not yet collecting The source universe is documented and no source is enabled. Nothing is collected; where a collector has been built, it has not been launched.
Planned — nothing collected Scope declared, nothing researched, configured, enabled or collected.
Paused — collection stopped Configured and deliberately stopped. Its existing records stay in the record; nothing new arrives.

Only collecting desks show public-corpus record totals. A shadow desk\'s "Not admitted" means its independently preserved material is excluded from those totals and dates; it does not mean no private originals were collected.
