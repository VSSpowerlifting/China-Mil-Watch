What this is

An archive of what selected defense and security institutions publish themselves — the original text, the date, the institution, and a per-run record of whether collection actually worked. One desk is collecting today; the others are declared and empty, and are labeled that way on Desks.

Coverage is selective and heavily concentrated in one source. What the archive can and cannot support is set out in Methodology, and what each collection run actually returned is on Coverage. Citation guidance is on About.
