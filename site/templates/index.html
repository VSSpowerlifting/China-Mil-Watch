Reading China's military media in the original.

China Mil Watch is an independent project monitoring Chinese military and security reporting in the original Mandarin from official and authoritative PRC sources. Official reporting is where the PLA describes itself in its own terms; much of it never appears in English-language coverage. The daily record is indexed here. The PLA Watch, the weekly brief, is the analysis.
