Limits of this desk, stated plainly

  • This is not whole-of-government coverage, not armed-forces-wide, and not comprehensive Singapore defense coverage.
  • Other ministries, the parliamentary Hansard record, unit and recruitment pages, magazine features, social media, and procurement notices are all outside the declared scope.
  • The corpus is a one-time promotion, not a running daily collection: no scheduled production workflow collects new Singapore records yet, so the corpus will not grow until that is built as separate work.
  • Two records are held out of the promoted corpus: their CJK text was damaged by a historical extractor defect the correction overlay could not repair, and they are excluded rather than published with damaged text. See shadow/singapore_mindef/CORRECTIONS.md for which records and why.

These are properties of the desk a reader needs before citing it — see Coverage for what each collection run actually returned.
