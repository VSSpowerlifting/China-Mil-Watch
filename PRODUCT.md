# Product context for design tools

## Register

brand

## Users

Researchers, analysts and interested readers locating official Indo-Pacific
defense and security publications, following their provenance, and reading
human-controlled analysis. See `docs/PRODUCT_AND_EDITORIAL_DOCTRINE.md` for the
authoritative audience and editorial rules.

## Product Purpose

Preserve what institutions publish and make its context and limitations legible.
Source records, machine output and editorial judgment remain distinct.

## Brand Personality

Independent, contemporary, grounded. A publication rooted in Pacific oceans,
coasts, terrain and documentary naval photography.

## Anti-references

SaaS dashboards, surveillance terminals, generic floating cards, fabricated
geography and faint decorative watermarks standing in for composition.

## Design Principles

- Keep evidence authoritative and long-form reading clear.
- Give each page family a deliberate role within one publication identity.
- Compose material boundaries and visible contour profiles; keep decoration
  separate from geography, evidence and data.
- Preserve the selected supplied ipr lettering and circular accent.
- Keep the homepage's existing photographic opening and content hierarchy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable contrast, visible keyboard focus, native controls and touch targets,
responsive layouts, complete no-JS and reduced-motion states. Governing details
remain in `docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` and `docs/VISUAL_AND_MOTION_SYSTEM.md`.
