### Critical distinctions exposed only by inspecting all pages

1. **One notice / ten matters / multiple facilities.** There are ten distinct annex summaries, but a **single original publisher document and canonical PDF URL**. FAC 6009 and FAC 6044 each recur in different annex matters and must remain separate *matters*, not be merged away, while neither justifies minting extra source records.
2. **Heterogeneous actions.** The ten matters include planned airfield works, flood controls, clinic site works, Okinawa housing demolition, a **temporary exercise-use agreement** and **facility return/replacement**. They are **not** ten homogeneous completed infrastructure projects. In particular the Atsugi annex says power facilities **were completed before provision**—a substantive source claim that should be cited with its specific page, distinguished from the other approvals and corroborated independently before treating as verified completion.
3. **Full original ≠ extracted text.** The 10 annex pages have dense geographic diagrams with site boundaries and callouts. A 3,609-character text extraction might represent portions of the text-layer assertions, but **cannot preserve those visual relationships, site footprints, page layout or all map labels** without separate permitted graphic capture and specialist review. Even if the text extraction itself proves complete later, this is **not** whole-PDF parity.
4. **Date taxonomy.** Publisher notice dateline and annex approval dates are all Aug 27. The Kanoya temporary-use interval is **September 15–25**, while 2006/2013 historical policy references on other pages contextualize facilities decisions. Do not backdate publisher releases, count activity dates as separate source releases, or silently label approval statements as executed works.
5. **Published information versus verified reality.** Statements are the original MOD's account of Joint Committee actions. No independent contracting, on-site inspection, built-asset completion, bilateral corroboration or foreign counterpart acknowledgment was obtained in this review. Do not convert a release's completion wording into an independently substantiated historical fact.
