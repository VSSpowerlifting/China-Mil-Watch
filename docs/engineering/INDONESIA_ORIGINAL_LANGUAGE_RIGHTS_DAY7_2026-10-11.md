Published **10 October**; meeting **Friday 9 October**; 1,741 archive-text characters. First seen `38084226252-1`.

**Bacaan asli:** Kemhan menyebut audiensi Wamenhan Donny Ermawan Taufanto dengan Broto Wardoyo (HI FISIP UI) dan pihak Sasakawa Peace Foundation untuk *persiapan* simposium keamanan kawasan bertajuk *The Sudirman Dialogue 2027*. Dialog Februari 2026 hanya disebut sebagai pendahulu. Pernyataan bahwa Indonesia mitra strategis komprehensif Jepang adalah **pandangan perwakilan SPF yang dilaporkan Kemhan**, bukan keputusan resmi bersama atau status traktat yang diverifikasi mandiri.

**Restricted English reading:** A ministry account of discussions preparing a **future** Japan–Southeast Asia dialogue; not evidence that the 2027 forum convened, that a new bilateral arrangement was signed, or that the named organizations independently endorsed every account detail. Original page's `Menaggapi` spelling is retained as publisher wording, not silently corrected.

**Decision:** Suitable *candidate* for minimal dated official-source reference; restrict prospective public text to bibliographic facts and explicit ministry attribution. Human language/source and rights decisions remain open.

## Rights analysis and source-specific use decisions

**First-party evidence:** All five reviewed Kemhan pages present the footer **`Hak Cipta © Kementerian Pertahanan Republik Indonesia`**. No article-specific CC/public-domain/third-party republication licence was seen on these reviewed HTML pages. The original articles carry images; no photo licences or individual photographer permissions were established.

**Law context (not legal clearance):** Indonesia Law 28/2014, [government-hosted translated legislation](https://peraturan.go.id/files2/uu-no-28-tahun-2014_terjemah.pdf), Arts. 41–44, distinguishes unprotected **facts**, certain official speeches/open-state-meeting outputs, government-performed acts with exceptions, and purpose/attribution-bounded research use. **An entire Kemhan news report is not automatically an uncopyrighted official speech** merely because it describes an official speaking. Art. 43(b) is conditional and not equivalent to an open reuse licence for this external research website; Art. 44 research conditions likewise are not blanket full-text commercial redistribution or unrestricted AI/translation permission. The publisher's explicit copyright notice strengthens the need to avoid a blanket permission claim. Do not infer that a displayed photo is ministry-owned.
