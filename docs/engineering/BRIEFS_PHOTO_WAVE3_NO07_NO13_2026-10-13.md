## No. 13 — Scarborough Shoal (NASA)
- **Story:** The PLA Watch: Scarborough Shoal, Platform Disclosures, and the Limits of Anniversary Week, week ending 2026-08-08.
- **Candidate:** NASA Johnson Space Center / ISS Expedition 45 photograph **ISS045-E-77330**, photographed **24 October 2015 at 01:35:13 UTC**. [Wikimedia Commons original](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:ISS045-E-77330_-_View_of_Scarborough_Shoal.jpg), [NASA photo catalogue](https://eol.jsc.nasa.gov/SearchPhotos/photo.pl?mission=ISS045&roll=E&frame=77330). NASA/U.S. federal original, Commons marks **public domain**; Commons file has 4,928 × 3,280 pixels.
- **Context boundary:** A historical orbital photograph of the shoal, **not** an August 2026 patrol or capability test. Different original frame from No. 9's **ISS045-E-77332**, yet captured only two seconds earlier from the same orbital pass. **Strong editorial warning:** No. 9 and No. 13 could look visually near-identical even though the image bytes are distinct. The owner should reject No. 13's image if they prefer a different photographic subject or a more diverse catalogue. An independently credited Philippine Coast Guard near-shoal vessel image has been found, but the original PIA source URL is now 404, and the Commons file visibly includes a watermark; it has **not** been admitted.
- **Source requirements:** Verify the full Commons-original response against MediaWiki imageinfo SHA-1 and dimensions. Retain source, derivative and card SHA-256. Preserve date/orbit in visible caption.

## No. 7 — Chinese Coast Guard statement (U.S. Coast Guard photograph)
- **Story:** The PLA Watch: The CCG Statement That Stood Apart, week ending 2026-06-20.
- **Candidate:** [DVIDS photo No. 5771576](https://www.dvidshub.net/image/5771576/coast-guard-cutter-stratton-crew-conducts-operations-south-china-sea), photographed by **Petty Officer 1st Class Nate Littlejohn, U.S. Coast Guard**, 16 September 2019. DVIDS marks the official-duty work **public domain**. The image shows a **U.S. Coast Guard small boat in foreground and a China Coast Guard ship on the horizon** in the **South China Sea**. Original DVIDS resolution is 3,996 × 2,864; the locally staged JPEG may be the publisher's visibly lower-resolution **1,000px licensed preview**, not mislabelled as the full-resolution camera original.
- **Context boundary:** Do **not** misrepresent the 2019 South China Sea image as the June 2026 CCG activity east of Taiwan. The image does not show the 2026 statement, a Japanese or Philippine ship, or the relevant 2026 patrol. This is historical Chinese Coast Guard context only; caption must state different date and maritime area.
- **Terms:** [DVIDS copyright information](https://www.dvidshub.net/about/copyright) requires non-endorsement notice: “The appearance of U.S. Department of War (DoW) visual information does not imply or constitute DoW endorsement.” The entire notice must appear in the admitted card credit if the image is shown.
- **Source requirements:** Download only from verified `d1ldvf68ux039x.cloudfront.net` publisher CDN, check JPEG/dimensions, pin received bytes' SHA-256, and retain DVIDS page as the licence/credit origin. Never claim the low-resolution JPEG was independently supplied by the camera operator.
