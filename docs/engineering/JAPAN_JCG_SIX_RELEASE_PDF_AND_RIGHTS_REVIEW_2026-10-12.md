## Rights review of actual page variants (not a legal license grant)

1. The [JCG **Japanese main-site terms**](https://www.kaiho.mlit.go.jp/questions/post-1.html) explicitly state that **JCG-owned** published website material ordinarily falls under **Public Data License 1.0**, unless differently marked. They require **official issuer/source URL attribution**, identification of *who* edited/translated/adapted text, and avoidance of the appearance that adapted material is an unmodified government original. JCG logos and mascot images, specially licensed content and material not owned by the agency are not covered by a simple blanket permission.
2. **Every inspected English `/e/topics_archive/article*.html` page carries the generic footer © 2020 Japan Coast Guard, All rights reserved.** That older footer and the Japanese central default-use policy need an **explicit per-site-version application ruling** before we can confidently license persistent HTML original-body reuse or translation. A generic footer is not itself a documented source-specific exception with a stated ownership analysis; equally, the Japanese default does not prove that every English attachment is cleared. Treat this as **permission/ownership unresolved**, not an automatic prohibition or an automatic PDL grant.
3. **Photo/media and emblem hold:** every linked PDF includes logos or illustrations and most include photographs (including foreign partner personnel). No photo rights, permission for public republication, model image processing or source custody was established. Exclude them entirely from any publication or derived-model text process pending item-level clearance. No OCR is authorized by this review.
4. **HACGAM joint statement is a separate rights issue:** it documents multiple governments/institutions' jointly adopted language. Hosting inside a JCG PDF does not determine ownership, terms or model-usage permission for its constituent statement. **Hold joint-statement full-text capture/republication/model use**, even if the JCG-owned summary prose is later separately permitted.
5. The official terms identify the **JCG General Affairs Department, Policy Evaluation and Public Relations Office** (`海上保安庁総務部政務課政策評価広報室`), reachable through headquarters switchboard **+81-3-3591-6361**, as the use-rule contact. No rights clearance contact has been made; there is **no affirmative publisher reply**. Do not use the separate feedback-only address as a permission mailbox.

**Proposed use disposition, awaiting human signoff:** citation/link-only references to these six official HTML pages and their direct publisher-hosted PDFs may be shown *as external links* with source and PDF-scope notes; JCG-owned, body-only HTML archival/translation/model processing is **pending a source-version permission finding**; all PDF original bytes, attached participant/photographic details as stored text, joint-statement text, logos/photos and full-package completeness claims **held**. Factual observations for independent editorial analysis are not themselves approval to store or redistribute protected originals. Keep all existing production and model gates closed.
