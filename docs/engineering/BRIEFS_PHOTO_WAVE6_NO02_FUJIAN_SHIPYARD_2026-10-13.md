## Alternatives and constraints

There is a [September 2025 Japanese Joint Staff photograph of the carrier *Fujian* itself](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:PLAN_CV-18_Fujian_20250911.jpg), under Japan's government standard-use terms compatible with CC BY 4.0, but the file is an extremely **wide, shallow 1,852×515** frame; cropping it to the catalogue's 3:2 shape risks cutting the ship. It may still be preferable if the source image shows the actual carrier clearly; perform a visual comparison. The China News Service / YouTube carrier-airwing screenshots have unresolved underlying video rights and should **not** be ingested merely because Commons labels them CC BY.
