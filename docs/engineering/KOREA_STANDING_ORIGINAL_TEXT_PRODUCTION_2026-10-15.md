# Korea standing-approved original-text production

**Owner scope, October 15, 2026:** Automatic publication of **future** Korean Ministry of National Defense government *text* independently collected through the Republic of Korea Policy Briefing is authorized **only after** preserved publisher/HWPX source validation, an article-specific KOGL Type 1 text-only notice, current official terms, and independent exception screening pass. The approval does **not** include entire HWPX files, photographs, illustrations, embedded media, English translations, model output, or substantive IPR analysis.

## Evidence and production frontiers

The original frozen 14 MND releases at source Git commit `0595f0fd29b769cbab5dd57c2ef57af174237a67` are a permanent **historical exclusion frontier** for the unattended writer. They retain their existing approved *links*; the one separately owner-approved original `korea-policy:156784297` retains its already published verbatim text. The new standing grant does not silently backfill the other 13 source texts.

The automatic writer uses exactly `kr_policy_mnd_releases`, not the entire Korea Desk, DAPA, other Republic of Korea agencies, or other-country source families. It accepts no unverified new title, content from screenshots, or derived output.

Each successful scheduled shadow collection triggers a new attempt. An independent daily schedule also retries failed or missed collector handoffs. The main-source and Pages writer share the Korea official-link concurrency lock. Prior source-body Git versions can be ancestors of an advanced archival branch, but every previously published original is rechecked against the original text hash, publisher date, and saved HTML capture hash.

## A new original can publish only if

1. It is **not** in the frozen 14, and is not already in the published-text registry.
2. A complete, unchanged, authentic government original is independently re-extracted from its SHA-pinned archived HWPX (without publishing the binary).
3. The exact official article-local **KOGL Type 1 *text-only*** mark and warning about third-party/non-text assets match the preserved publisher HTML.
4. The *current* Korea Policy Briefing and official KOGL terms satisfy the independent first-party policy and attribution contract.
5. The actual text passes the bounded third-party, alternate-terms, sensitive-document and privacy-indicator exception screen, and has no known editorial hold.
6. The full byte-identical original is SHA-pinned, escaped, attributed, and rendered as an original-language official source rather than an IPR-reported fact.
7. Commit scopes contain **only** `research/korea_published_original_texts.json`, registered Korean `.txt` files, `output/korea.html` and `output/korea-originals/*.html`. The separate Pages branch admits only `korea.html` and registered `korea-originals/*.html`. The production SQLite and every other desk's output remain untouched.
8. Source/main/Pages optimistic Git guards and public HTTPS exact-byte readback succeed. Any failure is a hold requiring an inspected retry, never a force push or broad-site upload.

A bounded textual flag scan cannot definitively establish authorship of every expression. Explicit rights changes, broken source capture, previously published text drift and unavailable terms hold release. Any translation or IPR analytical publication must use **separate factual, bilingual and editorial review**.

At rollout, the verified real-source baseline still contained 14 originals, **one** separately approved published text and **zero** new post-frontier candidates. A green no-change run is a wiring/retry success; the desk is not yet proven to have published a subsequent future original unattended.
