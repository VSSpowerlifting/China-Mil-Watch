### Source and rights gates

- All eleven image assets are real archival photography, not AI-generated visuals, graphics, screenshots or unlicensed ministry photographs. Original photograph source, photographer, exact historical date, licensed/public-domain status and derivative treatment are recorded in `site/assets/editorial/manifest.json` and `site/assets/catalogue/admissions.json`.
- Real image-source checks passed in the isolated wave workflows; **Wave 2 #553** also passed [exact-head full offline CI #38501723796](https://github.com/VSSpowerlifting/China-Mil-Watch/actions/runs/38501723796) (6,219 tests). Stacked Waves 3–9 have *not yet run* the full PR CI workflow because the GitHub workflow triggers only on PRs targeting `main`; their full CI is a post-upstream-merge requirement.
- Required DoW/DVIDS non-endorsement appears for relevant U.S. government publisher originals (including originals acquired via Wikimedia).
- Historical context disclaimers are present on each proposed catalogue card. *Photo admission is not evidence of the specific 2026 event.* Existing open-article art is unchanged.
