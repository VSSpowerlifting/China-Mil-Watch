- Compared the observed language with the publisher [official general copyright policy](https://www.korea.kr/guide/copyRight.do) and the [Korea Copyright Commission KOGL Type 1 guidance](https://www.copyright.or.kr/gov/nuri/guide/index.do). Both support attribution-conditional text reuse **where the work carries that mark**. The specific page marking, rather than a host-wide assumption, is the critical evidence.
