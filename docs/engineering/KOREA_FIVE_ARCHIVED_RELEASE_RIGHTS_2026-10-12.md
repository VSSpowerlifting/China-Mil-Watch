## Per-record first-party rights determination (five of five pages)

The actual original publisher pages above were separately opened on October 12 (mobile renditions for four, desktop rendition for `156784196`). **Each of the five pages explicitly displays** the same release-specific notice:

> 이 자료는 텍스트에 한하여 공공누리 제1유형(출처표시)의 조건에 따라 이용할 수 있습니다.

That is **KOGL Type 1 (attribution) for text only**. Each also attributes the release to the Ministry of National Defense through the republication notice and warns that photographs, images, illustrations, video and other nontext works generally are **not** licensed merely because the page shows a text notice. These are five individual observations, not one generic host-level permission inference. See the [publisher's copyright policy](https://www.korea.kr/guide/copyRight.do).

The page notice is positive evidence for **potential source-attributed text-only use**. It does not establish that a complete HWPX package, its tables, logos, figure illustrations or third-party components may be rehosted. The October 6 missile statement's old HWPX filename date, and the October 2 page whose inline body was not exposed to the reader, must not be normalized away. Neither automated original Korean-to-English translation nor a semantic inference from a headline is an editorially approved source claim.
