## Rights determination — narrowed, not final

The [Japanese MOD reuse policy](https://www.mod.go.jp/j/info/contents.html) identifies MOD-owned material, unless specially marked otherwise, as usable under **Public Data License 1.0** and permits copying, public distribution, translation/adaptation and commercial use under its requirements. The [MOD English terms](https://www.mod.go.jp/en/notice.html) likewise require original-source/URL attribution, disclosure of modifications, no suggestion that adaptations are unmodified government originals, and review of **third-party material** and excluded organizational logos/symbols or specially licensed items. Both are publisher-level evidence; neither asserts that all photos, base maps, facility diagrams, extracts and attached items in these exact PDFs are MOD-owned or rights-cleared.

**Safe proposed scope for future individual adjudication:** original-language MOD-authored text **only** (not whole PDF equivalence), keyed to exact official document URL, with publisher/URL attribution, extraction and translation disclosure, and a retained original/capture and version attestation. **No images, logos, third-party quotations or mapped art** without separate page-level ownership/rights decision; especially no six-photo reproduction from `08/28a.pdf`. This is a **proposal**, not authorization to copy raw bodies into production or deliver them to a model.
