## Treatment and delivery specification

- Published photographs must be actual photographs; no AI-generated or generic text plates falsely marked as photos. Current four published photo covers must not change.
- For historical Nos. 1–14, catalogue cards are a **separate surface** from pre-existing historical article art. Wave 2 starts with catalogue cards for Nos. 6 and 14 and does **not** rewrite historical article heroes.
- Originals are retained under `site/assets/editorial/`, a deterministic 1600px photographic crop source under `site/assets/editorial/derivatives/`, and the 720×480 locally served card under `site/assets/catalogue/media/`. Hash each file and pin actual source bytes. Render output only after gating.
- Keep creator, original capture date, original source link, appropriate public-domain or CC BY 4.0 credit, **modification notice**, and contextual-event disclaimer in both the manifest and the visible card credit.
- Owner crop/selection review, catalogue-gate regression tests, full offline CI and controlled Pages release are separate gates. Do not automatically publish once staging succeeds.
- Wave 2 must delete its temporary **networked** ingestion workflow and script before it can merge. Routine production rendering and weekly collection must remain offline.
- Never bypass No. 12 denial, No. 1/10 holds, MINDEF's license limits, or the B6 ShareAlike policy merely to reach 18/18.
