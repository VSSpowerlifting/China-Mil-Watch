## First-party rules

Each of the five Republic of Korea Policy Briefing article pages identifies the document as a **republication of 국방부 (Ministry of National Defense) press material** and labels **text-only** reuse as KOGL **Type 1** (attribution). The pages expressly caution that photographs, illustrations, video and other non-text elements may belong to other parties and require their permission. The official [publisher copyright policy](https://www.korea.kr/guide/copyRight.do) requires specific attribution and advises linking its policy when directly linking publisher works. The [official KOGL Type 1 guide](https://www.kogl.or.kr/info/license.do) permits text reuse, including derivative/commercial uses, under attribution conditions. **Neither source promises that an entire downloaded HWPX, its graphs or embedded assets are Type 1 works.**

## Attribution language (only after human and owner clearance)

`Source: Republic of Korea Ministry of National Defense (issuing institution), republished by Republic of Korea Policy Briefing, [Korean title], [portal publication date], [canonical source link]. Publisher marks source text KOGL Type 1 (attribution). Publisher copyright policy: https://www.korea.kr/guide/copyRight.do. English summary: Indo-Pacific Record, not an official translation.`

When a document's distribution day is distinct from portal posting day, state both. For `156784297`, **October 4 (document distribution) and October 6 (portal posting)** must not be collapsed. Avoid implying ministry endorsement of IPR.

## Outstanding permissions and signoffs

The proposed decisions above identify **scope**, not a final lawyer/owner authorization. For each identity separately, designated reviewer must sign off on the visible notice, the precise excerpt/summary planned, whether embedded elements are excluded, Korean-text semantic accuracy, reviewer identity and timestamp, document/content SHA, and an owner-level editorial decision. A rights notice is not automatically inherited by future records.
