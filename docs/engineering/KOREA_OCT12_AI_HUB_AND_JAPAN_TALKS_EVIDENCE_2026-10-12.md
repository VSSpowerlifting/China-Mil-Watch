   Demand actual pinned `shadow/korea-policy-briefing` Git state, Action

3. **Human language review:** Have an independent Korean-proficient reviewer
   visually compare the archived HWPX with publisher HTML and document
   separately the event date, document-distribution date, portal date,
   ceremony/plan modality, institutional names, and exact quoted claims.
   **No such signature exists in this docket**.

   **KOGL Type 1 text-only** according to previously observed publisher
   pages. October 7 infographic is Type 4; photographs, graphics, and
   attachments can carry separate rights. No full HWPX, third-party image,
   news photograph or news full text is approved for republication.
5. **Publication:** Do not expand the fixed five public Korea references,
   auto-generate an English Brief, include these in production SQLite or
   call other four proposed sites operational. Actual Korean-human, media-
   rights and owner record-level approval remain **HOLD**.
