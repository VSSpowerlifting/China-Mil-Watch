## No. 3 — PLA training context for institutional-implementation gap
- No.3 story: *Institutional Friction and the Gap Between Form and Function*, reporting week ending **23 May 2026**, a retrospective analysis of PLA reforms and the gap between institutional announcements and implementation.
- [DVIDS photo #1745480](https://www.dvidshub.net/image/1745480/us-army-soldiers-work-with-pla-soldiers), **Maj. Lindsey Elder, U.S. Army Pacific PAO**, 18 January 2015. Photograph shows **PLA soldiers and U.S. Army personnel constructing an evacuation camp** during a disaster-relief field-exchange exercise at **Haikou, Hainan, China**. U.S. federal-government original, [DVIDS public-domain terms](https://www.dvidshub.net/about/copyright). Image identity is corroborated by the visible U.S. Army patch and Chinese PLA camouflage; source caption identifies each.
- **Editorial limitation:** The photo documents hands-on practical exchange in **2015**, not the May 2026 PLA Daily reporting, current PLA-wide readiness, command reforms or the specific institutional gaps. Owner may prefer the card's existing non-photographic plate for this abstract story.
- Proposed caption: “PLA and U.S. Army personnel set up an evacuation camp during a 2015 disaster-response field exercise in Hainan. Historical example of hands-on military training, not a photograph or assessment of May 2026 PLA institutional reforms.”
- Publisher image's public **1000w JPEG** is sufficient for 720×480 card; it is *not* the 3216×2136 native camera file. Preserve DVIDS non-endorsement notice.
