Lists the August and March 2026 releases as the newest two as of the check,
then older August 2025 and earlier releases. Thus **no new press release in a
daily window is normal for this source and not evidence of a broken collector**.
Do not transplant Berita's six-day lookback or 40-article cap and describe it
as a comprehensive Siaran Pers monitoring contract.

The individual ministry article pages display the exact original headline,
publication date, the `SIARAN PERS` document class and the numbered release
identifier above the body; the visible statements are attributed to
`Biro Infohan Setjen Kemhan`. The ministry site displays its copyright notice.

These checks are *publisher-page spotchecks by an AI assistant*, **not
independently signed original-language editorial review**. No publisher text
or media is reproduced in the public data. The government publisher's
statements are not independently verified as factual implementation.
