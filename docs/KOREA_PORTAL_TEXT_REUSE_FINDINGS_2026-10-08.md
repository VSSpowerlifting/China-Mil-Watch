## What the publisher actually says

The publisher's standing policy states that text carrying the public-work KOGL **Type 1** notice can be reused with specific source attribution. It distinguishes text from images and other media, warns users to identify the source accurately and cautions about changes that misrepresent the original meaning. Where the page lacks KOGL markings or reuse covers photographs/graphics, separate consultation may be necessary. The policy further recommends linking the publisher's copyright-policy page when directly linking its material.

The publisher's October 6 release page explicitly identifies itself as a **republication of a Ministry of National Defense press release**, and displays a KOGL Type 1 **text-only attribution** notice; it warns that images and non-text media may require permission from their owners. The linked source document is an HWPX attachment; this observation does **not** independently classify every HWPX component (including tables, embedded graphics, logos, or annexes) as Type 1-covered source text.
