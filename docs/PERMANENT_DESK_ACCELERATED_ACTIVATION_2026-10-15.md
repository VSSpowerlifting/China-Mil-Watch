# Permanent source activation — exceptional seven-day evidence path

**Owner direction: October 15, 2026.** The intended public product is a set of permanent IPR country desks, **not** a provisional “beta” with draft records. The standard 30-day collecting duration is an internal maturity preference, not an external legal prerequisite. The owner has authorized designing a shorter route where the real collection, original-language evidence and permissions genuinely support it.

**This policy record is not source-specific publication approval and is not a completed release receipt.** It does not itself change the public registry, admit records, transfer copyrighted originals, approve publisher reuse, authenticate a reviewer or deploy anything.

## Two permanent publication tracks — no temporary labels

**A. Public editorial research desk.** IPR may permanently publish its **own**
approved analysis, verified bibliographic source metadata, first-party links,
a citation trail and appropriate source-version digests **without publicly
rehosting government article bodies, HWPX/PDF files, photographs or other
publisher media**. A source link is not a hosted archival original, and
a hash alone cannot reconstruct a deleted publisher article. The original
publisher retains its own hosting and distribution responsibilities. Public
citation and original IPR text may be preserved as IPR's own publication.

Editorial activation needs actual approved original IPR research, source-version
human verification, a checked link/date/issuer record, narrow source-use
and quotation review, an explicit owner decision and a reviewed rendering and
deployment. **It does not require thirty collection days or even a functioning
original-text collector**, because no assertion of automatic source collection
or public original-text archive is being made. Public claim language must
distinguish a working *editorial research desk* from a production collector;
the registry's `live` status retains its stronger meaning of actual
production collection. Do not change a collector to `live` to describe
editorial publication. A published editorial desk is a **permanent release**,
not a provisional beta.

**B. Production source archive.** IPR may separately admit source records into
the searchable production archive, after the ordinary thirty-day or
owner-authorized seven-day source-specific evidence pathway below. Only
original text/media uses expressly cleared for the requested public output
may be rehosted. A bibliographic-only archive may use approved external
references and internally held original research evidence without copying
publisher originals into public `output/`, but must never claim to expose
full source text, comprehensive source capture, or private custody without
verification.

**Custody classification:** public link/title/date/identity = *bibliographic
preservation only*. A public Git shadow original, where it genuinely exists,
is a byte-bearing copy but **not privately controlled**. The separate
`VSSpowerlifting/ipr-source-evidence-private` repository is currently
**metadata-only and not a completed private source-original transfer**.
An access-controlled original exists only after actual source-byte transfer,
parity check and independently verified encrypted recovery. Do not delete
historical public Git objects or claim old distribution was reversed by a
subsequent private migration. Any local/third-party rights constraints must
be resolved independently of storage location.

**Photographs:** keep links to publisher images or source pages rather than
rehosting photos by default. Do not mirror, hotlink or embed publisher photos,
screenshots or maps as IPR assets without a specific clearance decision;
write and retain IPR-original visualizations when possible. Even federal
government sites can include third-party restricted media.

## The only waiver

For a single explicitly named publisher/source family, the owner may waive:
- the ordinary 30 consecutive-day pre-promotion observation duration;
- pre-promotion timing of Day-14 and Day-30 human operational reviews.

A minimum of **seven consecutive successfully collected real scheduled logical days** is still necessary. A cancelled/missing/failed day breaks that seven-day window; a manually rerun job is never relabeled as an original successful schedule. Run-attempt, job, shadow-state and ledger checks must authenticate the complete visible window. Failed attempt-only evidence must be accounted for without treating checksum parity alone as external GitHub provenance. Coverage limits must be specified rather than claiming whole-of-ministry coverage.

## Nonwaivable pre-publication evidence

1. **C1–C12 all pass** for the actual admitted source subset, on legally and technically accessible official endpoints, under the publisher's robots/access terms. A blocked page or a failed body extraction cannot be made whole by a metadata link or a source from another ministry.
2. **Exact original integrity:** every source record proposed for public admission is bound to original URL, publisher date, byte/capture hashes, immutable Git state and the preserved raw version, with all provenance discrepancies, encoded-text problems and issuer-attribution anomalies dispositioned.
3. **Named substantive human source review:** a real person has inspected the exact original language and publication version, recorded accuracy/reuse findings and documented independent corroboration where the claim needs it. CI, a model or an owner's general statement that someone checked the sources is not an authenticated per-version review.
4. **Source-specific rights and access decision:** record publisher terms, any robots/noindex restrictions, full-text redistribution, translation, short editorial quotation, metadata links, and photographs/attachments **separately**. Admitting a bibliographic link does not permit original-body republication. Restricted or third-party materials remain excluded unless permission or an independently documented lawful basis exists.
5. **Explicit editorial release scope:** named source slug, exact corpus commit, exact admitted record IDs, exclusions/holds, institution and dates, website claim vocabulary, conflict corrections, and production testing receipts. Do not treat three Kemhan articles about the same MTC meeting as three independent corroborating institutions.
6. **Owner release decision:** the owner records an actual signed source-scoped decision in `DECISION_LOG.md` and approves a separate, reviewed source-admission PR containing the exact expected manifest, production DB additions and generated-site diff. Verify the live database, pages, citations, count deltas, noindex/access policy and rollback gate before publication.

## After permanent promotion

Every accelerated source must continue scheduled production observation and record actual Day-14 and Day-30 review decisions after those dates occur, not early or by assumption. If the complete source/rights chain, availability, material coverage failure or data fidelity defect is discovered, stop admission for that source and consider pausing the collector. Preserve and assess those checkpoints without backdating any evidence or reviewer decisions. Retain historic published version identifiers and publish corrections transparently as necessary. The reviewer may not backdate a signoff or silently rewrite the original state. A missing future checkpoint is reported as an unresolved release obligation and triggers an owner assessment; it never retroactively proves historic evidence did not exist.

A **production-collecting** desk can become `live` only when the **admitted production source set** genuinely passes this release, the scheduled production path works, the site renders genuine records accurately, and the guarded deployment succeeds. No permanent-launch flag is inferred from the word “approved” in chat.

## What this does not do

No status tag `beta` or `provisional` is introduced. No new government source, user identity, copyrighted body, media or proprietary algorithm is pulled into public production. No source is declared “fully public” on an unexecuted workflow or merely green synthetic tests. If genuine rights or access decisions remain blocked by October 17, the correct outcome is **not yet admitted**, rather than a falsely live desk.

This is a policy exception pathway, **not** a completed release receipt.
