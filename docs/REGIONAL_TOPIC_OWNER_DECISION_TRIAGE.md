# Owner decisions after the regional topic pilot

**State:** editorial triage, not human adjudication or authority to assign topics. The 19 flagged cases in [`OWNER_DECISION_QUEUE.json`](../research/topic_pilot_v1/OWNER_DECISION_QUEUE.json) are derived from frozen source records and the second model pass merged via PR #128. Their proposed labels and questions remain model judgments; **all 19 `owner_decision` fields are null.**

The queue is intentionally **editor-facing, not blind-reviewer-facing**: it includes the original and skeptical model label sets. Do not share it with a reviewer before that reviewer independently reads the source packet generated by the human-review gate in [PR #142](https://github.com/VSSpowerlifting/China-Mil-Watch/pull/142).

This avoids 19 disconnected debates.

## Adjudication order and safeguards

1. **Complete an independent source-first review**, or explicitly document which selected records could not be fully read. The owner decision queue contains model labels and is *not* a suitable blinded packet. A reviewer must assess original-language full bodies and capture boundaries.
2. After that reviewer has submitted their signed, source-pinned work, open the editor-only queue. Review the disputed scope clusters, comparing the human reading with the original and second model recommendations.
3. Record owner decisions **in a new authorized review record**, naming reviewer, date, exact taxonomy version, and evidence/rationale. The JSON queue is a working artifact; its null decision fields are a safeguard, not an invitation for automated fill-in.
4. Recheck ambiguous records and denied or absent original texts before accepting any classification. Distinguish *taxonomy scope approval* from *particular record topic approval*.
5. Keep all original ledgers unchanged. The model review should remain plainly model-authored. Do not manufacture a human gold set, statistical agreement, or "zero disagreements" through mechanical reconciliation.
6. **Never attach to production or shadow** based on this queue. v2 versioned loader support in [PR #137](https://github.com/VSSpowerlifting/China-Mil-Watch/pull/137) is not a production migration or a record-level authorization. A numbered production schema migration and a separately approved small attachment pilot remain later gates.

## Provenance and quality of the queue

The machine-readable file pins both the merged v1 pilot ledger and skeptical assessment by Git blob SHA, then preserves each disputed record's stable source URL, desk, source-stated date, title and body SHA-256. Its seven groups partition exactly 19 distinct pilot IDs. The queue contains no accepted labels, no human review artifacts and no DB write path. It is safe to maintain as **research documentation** without implying the decisions have been made.
