## Scope and evidence

**Reader's question:** how do Singapore and China place this naval exercise
in their defense relationships, and what does the comparison establish?

**Proposed finding:** MINDEF's opening release puts the exercise within a
deployment involving several partners. Its closing release and China's
ministry answer converge on bilateral trust and cooperation. This is a
comparison of published frames; the different formats and scopes are a
plausible explanation for differences in detail. The starting-date discrepancy
remains unresolved. The editor should assess that interpretation, not just the
mechanical validity of the draft.

five selected statements were published September 5–14. The issue's reporting
window is September 3–19, closing on Saturday so the September 14 ministry
response is included without assigning a Monday week-ending date. It is
explicitly a development-focused retrospective, not a full weekly digest.
