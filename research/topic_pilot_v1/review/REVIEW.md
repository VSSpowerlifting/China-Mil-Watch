## Method and limits

The complete original methodology, 60-record ledger, taxonomy definitions and
pinned original-language bodies were inspected. Source bodies were replayed from
the eight exact Git database blobs in the original ledger into temporary immutable
SQLite copies; existing databases were not opened for writing. Review uses those
preserved bodies, not current web replacements or invented translations. Links
below identify the issuers; the pinned bodies and Unicode offsets in the JSON
are the evidentiary target. Source-stated dates are preserved from the ledger;
this assessment does not certify their chronology or underlying events.

A source-first interpretation was made where possible before comparing proposals,
including inspection beyond the original excerpts. However, the same assistant/chat
produced the earlier pilot, and those proposals remain in context. This is a separate
skeptical model pass, **not a blinded independent reviewer or human adjudication**.
The counts below are descriptive differences, not inter-rater reliability or accuracy.
Native-language owner review is still required; English rationales are interpretations.

A topic needs a concrete subject-specific action/mechanism or developed explanatory
passage. An agenda item, generic readiness slogan, institutional affiliation,
platform, or venue alone is insufficient. Formal relationship evidence can be
short and substantive; there is no paragraph minimum or topic-count cap. Multi-label
recommendations require independently material subjects, not automatic parent/child
or desk-category inheritance. This materiality standard is editorial judgment
within the existing scope notes; disputed applications are explicitly open below.

Evidence statements describe **what the source asserts**. Accepting a topic does
not endorse sovereignty, collision blame, alleged cyber misconduct, claimed performance,
or historical counterfactuals. Reject means insufficient support for that topic at
this threshold, not proof that a relationship or capability does not exist.
Abstention can mean missing evidence, no v1 coverage, or disputed scope; these are
not interchangeable. P36 is evidence-missing; P38 is mission-gap/sustainment ambiguity.

All 30 originally flagged cases were assessed, as were the other 30. Original flags
are retained as `original_flagged` in the separate artifact. New questions include
previously unflagged omissions and boundaries. Original snippets sometimes end
mid-clause: successful offset replay proves authenticity, not semantic sufficiency.
The whole body, especially P19 and P41's multi-part captures, governs adjudication;
new locators supplement rather than overwrite the original evidence.

## Vocabulary recommendations (not decisions or schema changes)

**Military humanitarian assistance/disaster response warrants an owner-reviewed
new-topic proposal.** Its subject is the military contribution to civilian disaster
relief, humanitarian medical/support activity and preparedness for those missions.
Training and diplomatic relationship topics describe modes of activity, not the
mission. The subject has direct support across five desk ownerships:

| Desk / cases | Clear source evidence | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| China P16 | Named humanitarian medical exercise plus medical services and rescue technologies | Military issuer's account; six recurring visits are not six independently verified events |
| Singapore P35 | Multinational disaster-response exercise, medical/public-health/infrastructure support | P30's maritime rescue/HADR exchanges are secondary supporting evidence |
| Japan P38 | Disaster deployment allowances, bathing/water support and rescue transport | Budget/support evidence, not procurement or proof of completed response |
| Philippines P51/P52 | Named earthquake response exercise with rescue/triage/evacuation | Two publications about one exercise |
| Indonesia P57 | Detailed Japanese helicopter disaster assistance under Indonesian taskforce control | Same record also establishes a diplomatic/DCA relationship; Japanese participants do not create a separate Japan Desk record |

These six primary records span five distinct source-described mission contexts,
not six independent incidents. A later controlled-definition proposal should include
actual assistance and dedicated preparedness, allow exercises/diplomacy alongside
it when separately substantive, and exclude civilian hospital speeches (P34),
ordinary healthcare, military casualty care without humanitarian purpose, routine
personnel welfare and remembrance (P60). P41's base flood-prevention facility alone
should not establish a relief mission. No proposed slug is installed or assigned.

**Critical minerals/supply chains is broad, but the pilot supports a general
strategic supply-resilience subject.** Positive full-body cases include P17 chip/sensor
dependence, P33 military industrial/supply resilience, P45 fuel obligations/reserves,
P47 economic-security-linked supporting industry, and P10 historical wartime supply.
P31 is weaker because it merely lists an area. No sampled record develops critical
minerals specifically. Do not narrow this slug to minerals retroactively or manufacture
minerals coverage from P55's mining acreage. Recommend owner-reviewed naming/scope
clarification, preserving its identifier/version history; defer a minerals-versus-supply
split until a targeted minerals sample can test the distinction. Economic security
and supply resilience can overlap when the latter has concrete material content.

**Cyber/information is operationally heterogeneous.** It currently admits network
support (P04), hygiene/AI risks (P07), advisory messaging (P08), electronic warfare
(P18), disinformation/cyberspace capabilities (P33), protection against compromise
(P35), and public-security cyber research (P42). These are not all cyberattacks or
influence operations. The breadth is explicit in v1, so the observations justify
clear inclusion/exclusion examples and a later candidate facet/subtopic evaluation,
not silently rejecting valid EW/hygiene records or immediately splitting sparse
subsets. Routine press communication, IRIS maritime sharing (P30), QR exhibits (P60),
and online-fraud mentions (P44) do not automatically qualify. P31/P33 distinguish
insufficient materiality from too-broad semantics. A tighter public-security versus
defense-technology rule must be applied consistently across P42/P43 and P07/P08.

Space-security/civil-space scope (P22/P23), police security partnerships (P44),
regional-operation geography (P12), emerging remote systems (P18), and sustainment
spending (P38) require explicit owner guidance. A Korean Peninsula topic is a
possible coverage question, not yet an evidence-supported addition from this
three-record Korea sample. No zero-count topic is proven unnecessary by a purposive
sample; there are no positive controls for space security, East China Sea, or
export controls/sanctions. Sample prevalence, general corpus coverage, and event
truth remain unestablished.
