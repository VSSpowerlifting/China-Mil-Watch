**Chronology exception — hold, do not silently harmonize.** The ministry's Oct 14 opening account describes the commission as running **13–15 October**, but its separately dated Oct 14 closing account says Tri Budi Utomo **officially closed** it that day after the agenda and protocol signing. Both are statements attributed to the **same institutional press office**. A changed schedule, early closing, an imprecise projected calendar, or editorial error are possibilities; **none is established** by the documents checked. A publisher correction, independently documented session timetable or the actual protocol record would be needed to resolve this conflict. For now prefer: “Kemhan *reported* that the seventeenth commission closed October 14 and a protocol was signed; an earlier ministry report listed October 13–15 as its planned span.” Do not write unqualified “met October 13–15 and closed October 15,” nor re-date the original.

**Claim-stage exception — distinct from chronology.** A **reported signed protocol** is more concrete than aspirational technology cooperation; this is a valuable finding, but it is **not the same** as an independently checked protocol text or a proven transfer/production contract. Capture the claim's stage as `ministry-reported signed protocol; instrument uninspected`. Separately classify technology transfer, licensed production, MRO and joint research as `ministry priorities or proposed follow-up`.
