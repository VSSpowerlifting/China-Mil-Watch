## Publisher contradiction — reproduce only short source phrases

- **First-party Kemhan original:** https://www.kemhan.go.id/2026/10/02/kunker-ke-kaltara-menhan-sjafrie-tinjau-yonif-tp-880-banuanta-di-bulungan-tegaskan-kemampuan-tempur-dan-teritorial-pembangunan-untuk-kedaulatan-perbatasan.html (dated **October 2, 2026**).
- The headline uses `Kaltara` (Kalimantan Utara / North Kalimantan); the opening identifies **Tanjung Selor, Bulungan, Kalimantan Utara** on **October 2**. The final paragraph instead says `Kunjungan kerja Menhan ke Kalbar tersebut` (Kalbar means West Kalimantan). This is an internal contradiction in the ministry's currently displayed document, not licence to correct the archived original.
- Kemhan's page primarily describes an inspection and ministerial guidance. It does not independently establish quantified combat readiness, newly completed battalion infrastructure, or a distinct West Kalimantan visit.

## New independent first-party institutional corroboration

1. **Indonesian Air Force, Air Base Anang Busra arrival report, published October 5:** https://www.tni-au.mil.id/berita/detail/danlanud-anang-busra-sambut-kedatangan-menhan-ri-di-tarakan . The Air Force reports the minister's **October 2 arrival at Tarakan, Kalimantan Utara**, and states that the onward destination was **Yonif TP 880/Banuanta in Kabupaten Bulungan**. This is a different official publishing institution from Kemhan's Biro Infohan.
2. **Indonesian Air Force, Air Base Anang Busra departure report, published October 5:** https://www.tni-au.mil.id/berita/detail/danlanud-anang-busra-antar-kepulangan-menhan-ri-usai-kunjungan-kerja-di-kalimantan-utara . It describes the minister's **October 3 departure from Tarakan after visits in North Kalimantan**, explicitly placing **Yonif TP 880 in Bulungan, 881 in Nunukan, 921 in Malinau, and 922 in Tana Tidung**. This supports a wider **North Kalimantan** itinerary. This second Air Force report is from the *same* Air Force institutional channel as (1), so it is not a third independent institution or proof of every individual itinerary step.
3. **Outside-government contemporaneous reporting (already noted in issue):** https://www.metrotvnews.com/play/kqYC0724-menhan-kunjungi-yonif-tp-di-bawah-kodam-vi-mulawarman-kalimantan-utara (published **October 2**). Metro TV reports Yonif TP 880/Banuanta in Bulungan and Yonif TP 881/Baratama Dharma in Nunukan. This is an additional publisher account, but it may rely partly on official briefings rather than independent firsthand verification.
