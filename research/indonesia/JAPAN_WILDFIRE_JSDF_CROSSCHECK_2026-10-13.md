## Reconciled claim and timing matrix

- **Service identity matters.** The ship **JS Kunisaki** was **Japan Maritime Self-Defense Force (JMSDF)**; the **CH-47 helicopter crews and aircraft were Japan Ground Self-Defense Force (JGSDF)**. Reporting simply “JMSDF CH-47s” silently changes branch attribution. Treat the relief contingent as **joint JSDF/Japan Disaster Relief** unless reliable roster evidence supports a narrower branch count. The Indonesian page's headline emphasis must not override Japan MOD's explicit branch attribution.
- **361 / 92 / 3 are non-additive.** The Indonesian report says **361 JDR personnel received recognition**, **92** attended the ceremony and **three** were symbolically presented with insignia. Do not report **453 personnel**, call 92 the full deployed contingent, or assume all awards were to a solely naval unit. Japan MOD sources checked here do not independently verify award totals; they verify the mission's components, phases and flight operation counts.
- **Distinct dated events:** **May 4** defense arrangement → **Aug 28** dispatch order → **Sep 16** initial firefighting → **Sep 23** Ketapang operations → **Sep 29** termination order → **Sep 30** award ceremony → **Oct 6** ship's departure. **Sep 29 operational termination does not mean the ship had left Indonesia by Sep 30**. The awards were thus chronologically possible aboard JS Kunisaki before departure.
- **56 missions / 280 tonnes are Japan MOD's Oct 6 end-of-deployment claims** and must not be backdated to Sep 29 or Sep 30. An earlier [29 September ministerial press conference](https://www.mod.go.jp/en/article/2026/09/44474322b2ce8d8467dd8a2ae8a218a23c7ab066.html) cites **more than 40 missions and approximately 220 tonnes as of the prior day**. These are **two dated cumulative snapshots**, not contradictory final counts and not quantities that should be summed.
- **Effectiveness:** Indonesian attribution that Japanese aid helped prevent wider fire spread is a *government assessment*, whereas counts of sorties and discharged water are *Japan MOD operational self-reporting*. Neither proves an independent counterfactual about how many hectares were saved or authorizes a numerical effectiveness estimate.
