    Read the boundaries

    Archive records. Searchable titles and public metadata from the existing archive projection. Captured bodies, machine summaries and source URLs are excluded from bulk search/export. Open the canonical IPR record to inspect its provenance.

    Official links and originals. Approved public loaders define the available fields. A neutral reference label is not a publisher headline. Only separately registered published originals have searchable source text.

    Analysis and dates. Human-controlled Briefs keep their publication identity and citations. A week ending or approval date is not a publication date. Undated items are excluded when date filters are active.
