Kilim, Baybay City, Leyte**. The Eastern Samar row is headed `MEPERU Eastern Samar (Borongan City)` but its associated street address lies in **Brgy. Campoyong, Guiuan, Eastern Samar**. Confirm against signed NTP instructions or acceptance records before geocoding equipment to either city.

The invitation requires provision of **one day of training for at least ten PCG personnel** and stipulates delivery within **90 calendar days after the winning supplier's receipt of an NTP**. Both are conditional **contract requirements**, not evidence that a winning supplier received an NTP, that training happened or that goods arrived.

## 3. Independent process receipt: a post-qualification meeting, not an award

MEPCOM's [January 6, 2026 original post](https://mepcom.coastguard.gov.ph/14091-2/) says its Bids and Awards Committee held a **December 12, 2025** meeting on the *post-qualification report* for Eastern Visayas MARPOL equipment, explicitly the **second posting**.

The narrow verified statement is that **MEPCOM reported conducting a post-qualification review meeting**. The post does **not** identify an awarded bidder, approved bid price, signed contract, notice of award, NTP, acceptance certificate or actual delivery. Those items require their own documents.

**Useful gate revealed by the second original (physical page 73, zero-based 72):** the tender specifies *pre-delivery checks* and *final inspection and acceptance* by multiple PCG, MEPCOM and COA representatives. Even a future genuine NTP is not sufficient for an article claiming that equipment was delivered, inspected and accepted. Seek a signed acceptance/inspection or equivalent attributable completion record.
