Authority tier is a claim about position, not truthfulness

A tier-A source is closer to the institution that speaks. It is not more likely to be accurate, and nothing here treats it as such. Tier describes who is authorized to say a thing, never whether the thing is so.

Enabled is not implemented

A source is enabled when the pipeline is configured to visit it. Whether a working collector exists for it is a separate fact, recorded per run on Coverage — one configured source here has never produced a record and is reported as unimplemented every run rather than hidden.
