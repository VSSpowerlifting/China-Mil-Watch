Declared outside production configuration

This source is declared in a shadow manifest, which is deliberately not discoverable by the production configuration loader. It is not part of the collection contract, it is not enabled, and nothing it reaches enters the record. Fields the shadow manifest does not declare are shown as undeclared rather than filled in.

How this source is positioned

Authority tier — describes how close this publication sits to the institution's authorized public position. It is not a reliability score, and nothing here treats it as one. The source type — — — describes what kind of body publishes it.

What a record from this source can support

That this institution published a specific item on a specific date, and what its original text was at the time it was retrieved. Not that the institution believes it, not that a described capability exists, and not that coverage of this source is complete — see Coverage for what each run actually returned, and Methodology for the limits.
