About this analysis
Summary
A signed essay by Sang Linfeng in a PLA political-work special issue argues that ideological remolding (思想整风) is a lifelong, never-completed obligation for Party members and cadres, grounding the argument in Xi's directives at the senior-cadre training course and the CMC political-work conference, where he listed strengthening ideological remolding as the first of six priorities for 政治建军. The piece documents how the current political consolidation training (政治整训) campaign is being framed doctrinally: the Yan'an Rectification Movement, the Puyang and Danyang pre-campaign consolidations, and figures like Zhou Enlai, Peng Dehuai, and Liu Bocheng's 'three-level illumination' method are marshaled to normalize continuous self-criticism as the institutional baseline, not a crisis response. The framing points to an effort to preempt cadre fatigue with the ongoing rectification cycle by recasting compliance as a permanent communist virtue rather than a bounded campaign.
Translation

Ideological Remolding Has No End

■ Sang Linfeng

A person stands upright by virtue of thought. To lead in thought is the most advantageous form of leading; to lead through thought is the most fundamental form of leadership. Only by thoroughly remolding thought can one successfully remold practice and win victory.

The effectiveness of ideological remolding determines the quality and efficacy of ideological rectification (思想整风). At the opening ceremony of the training course for senior cadres of the entire military, Chairman Xi emphasized conducting ideological rectification, deepening political consolidation training (政治整训), and strengthening the revolutionary tempering of the military's cadre corps, requiring that "cadres at all levels of the military must consciously put themselves in the right position, stand firm on the mass standpoint, and overcome all selfish and impure thoughts that are divorced from the masses."

The depth of ideological remolding bears directly on the depth of building the military through politics (政治建军). At the Central Military Commission's conference on political work, Chairman Xi systematically laid out six priority areas for advancing the building of the military through politics on the new journey, the first of which is to "strengthen the consciousness and thoroughness of ideological remolding."

Ideological remolding is like dusting — it always refreshes one's appearance; like detoxifying — it always restores a healthy body; like curing illness — it always enables rebirth through fire.

The process by which Party members and cadres re-temper their souls, re-forge their Party spirit, re-invigorate their spirit, and elevate their moral realm is one in which ideological remolding accompanies them throughout, without end — Communists learn until old age, study until old age, and remold their thinking until old age.

I

Ideological remolding is of extraordinary significance.

Marx and Engels stated in the Communist Manifesto that the communist revolution "involves the most radical rupture with traditional ideas" in the course of its development.

Ideological remolding means using the Marxist worldview, outlook on life, and values to purify and cleanse the thinking of Party members and cadres — put vividly, it means consolidating and expanding the "Marxist-Leninist base area (马列根据地)" in one's mind. It is a unity of "breaking" and "establishing." What is broken is outdated thinking, habitual modes of thought, and path dependency; what is established is new thinking and new understanding that conforms to the times, shoulders the mission, and can withstand the test of practice.

During the Yan'an period, the Liberation Daily published an account of the Party branch of the Security Department of the Shaanxi-Gansu-Ningxia Border Region discussing the "Decision of the Central Committee of the Chinese Communist Party on Strengthening Party Spirit." That Party branch organized two centralized transmissions and four large-format lectures, with Party small-group discussions totaling 135 hours, of which mutual criticism alone accounted for three-fifths.

This is a vivid example of how our Party at that time relied on intra-Party education to strengthen ideological remolding.

"Pursue the good as if you could never reach it; shun the bad as if you were plunging your hand into boiling water." The process of ideological remolding is simultaneously a process of upholding truth and correcting errors, and a process of strengthening Party spirit and elevating moral conduct.

Looking back at the history of our Party and our military — from the Gutian Conference to the Yan'an Rectification Movement, from the New-Style Army Consolidation Movement to the successive intra-Party concentrated education campaigns after the founding of the People's Republic of China — every one of them achieved correction of deviations and errors through thorough and penetrating ideological remolding, striking down one "powerful ideological enemy" after another, and making the understanding of the cadre corps more unified and their steps more coordinated.

Conversely, when ideological remolding is incomplete, the original aspiration (初心) becomes clouded, convictions waver, and the ideological line of defense falls.

To deepen the building of the military through politics and continuously deepen political consolidation training, we must carry forward the spirit of thorough self-revolution, make every effort to carry ideological remolding through to the end, truly uproot erroneous ideological roots, and establish correct values.

— Ideological remolding is an objective requirement for forging political loyalty.

The military is an armed group that executes the Party's political tasks; it must be ensured that the barrel of the gun is always in the hands of those who are loyal and reliable to the Party. Over the past nearly one hundred years, one important reason our military has been able to advance continuously from victory to victory amid various tests of risk and hardship is that, through conscious and thorough ideological remolding and revolutionary tempering forged through a thousand trials, Party members and cadres have always remained loyal to the Party's faith, the Party's organization, and the Party's cause.

Today, exchanges, fusions, and clashes among various ideological and cultural currents are more frequent than ever, and the struggle in the ideological domain is increasingly sharp and complex. Only by conducting thorough ideological remolding can we better consolidate the soul and unite forces, strengthen the foundation, and uphold the righteous while eliminating the harmful, ensuring that Party members and cadres unwaveringly heed the Party and follow the Party.

— Ideological remolding is an inevitable choice for improving the capacity to win wars.

Ideological remolding and combat effectiveness building are inherently unified; it is the People's Army's secret weapon for winning wars. In the era of revolutionary war, it was precisely through repeated profound ideological tempering that the blood-and-guts courage of fearing neither hardship nor death was cultivated, and the fighting will of being invincible and ever-victorious was forged — enabling our military to repeatedly defeat stronger enemies and compose heroic epics that shake heaven and earth and move ghosts and gods to tears.

It is not difficult to observe that before many of our military's major campaigns, profound ideological remolding was conducted among Party members and cadres — a "revolution in the depths of the soul." Examples include the Puyang consolidation training before the Huaihai Campaign and the Danyang consolidation training before the liberation of Shanghai.

At present, the great changes unseen in a century are accelerating, a new round of technological and military revolutions is advancing rapidly day by day, and the form of warfare, the mechanisms of victory, and the elements of combat are undergoing profound changes.

Faced with a complex and severe national security situation and the arduous and heavy tasks of preparing for and fighting wars, only by continuously deepening ideological remolding — constantly forging the courage to dare to fight, honing the ability to win battles, and stimulating the wisdom to fight well — so that Party members and cadres focus all their thinking on fighting and direct all their work toward fighting, can our military stand at the forefront of global military competition and achieve the ability to respond to the call, fight upon arrival, and win every battle.

— Ideological remolding is a practical necessity for tempering a superior work style.

An excellent work style shapes heroic units; a lax work style destroys an ever-victorious force.

In the long course of revolution, construction, and reform, our Party and our military have not only formed a series of fine work styles but have also accumulated precious experience in work-style building, one important element of which is strengthening ideological remolding.

In every concentrated consolidation and rectification campaign of our Party and our military, ideological education has led the way — through rectifying thought to align steps, discipline the ranks, and correct the work style — ensuring that the entire Party and the entire military always advance in unity.

Ideological deterioration is the most serious pathology. When a Party member or cadre slackens one inch in thought, they will slacken one foot in action, and all manner of transgression and leakage becomes inevitable. Only by persistently strengthening ideological remolding, continuously correcting ideological understanding, and clarifying the boundaries between right and wrong can Party members and cadres effectively fulfill the mission tasks of our military in the new era with an exemplary image of superior work style.

II

Ideological remolding is a required course for Communists.

In this regard, the older generation of revolutionaries and outstanding Party members and cadres have set examples and established benchmarks for us.

Comrade Zhou Enlai cultivated himself throughout his life, constantly reflecting and advancing, persisting in ideological remolding his entire life without ever interrupting it.

On March 18, 1943 — Zhou Enlai's 45th birthday by the lunar calendar — his comrades proposed preparing refreshments to celebrate, but he politely declined, simply eating a bowl of noodles before returning to his office to work. That evening, he wrote the famous "My Principles of Self-Cultivation (我的修养要则)," covering seven aspects including work, study, life, and social interactions, as important content of his lifelong ideological remolding.

During the Yan'an Rectification Movement, Zhou Enlai wrote more than 50,000 characters of rectification reflections and gave five days of exchange speeches — the most detailed self-examination among the senior cadres' rectification meetings.

Ideological remolding that cuts to the bone is not easy; it requires courage, introspection, and self-dissection — sparing no one's feelings and unafraid to expose one's own ugliness.

Comrade Zhang Wentian adhered to the principle of "follow whoever holds the truth" and was a model of daring to turn the blade inward. For a period of time, Zhang Wentian implemented erroneous policies, but he had the courage to uphold truth, correct errors, and proactively assume responsibility. He said sincerely: "To oppose subjectivism, one must make a thorough reckoning — do not conceal, do not fear exposing your own errors, do not fear letting others see your scabby head."

Comrade Xie Juezai was one of the founders of the people's judicial system. He compared ideological remolding to "suing oneself" — that is, waging struggle in one's own mind between communist thought and all manner of incorrect ideas and concepts, insisting on serving simultaneously as defendant, lawyer, and judge, subjecting oneself to strict judgment without the slightest leniency, and polishing the true colors of a Party member through continuous self-"trial."

Xie Juezai pursued "lifelong self-cultivation with no end," never concealing his shortcomings and deficiencies. On New Year's Day 1943, he improvised a poem: "Affairs are already old year by year; conduct must be renewed day by day." Beginning that January, he wrote ten consecutive articles of self-reflection, "laying out the good and the bad together." In his essay "Self-Indictment at Sixty (六十自讼)," he wrote: "'At fifty, one should know the wrongs of the forty-nine years prior' — then at sixty, one should also strive to make up for the shortcomings of the fifty-nine years before." This spirit of examining one's own faults and holding oneself strictly accountable is precisely the essence of ideological remolding.

"The depths of the soul can only be excavated by oneself." On the journey of life, every person is like someone carrying two bundles — one bundle is "the faults of others," and the other is "one's own faults." Most people place the former in front of their chest and the latter behind their back: at a glance downward they see the wrongs of others, while they rarely see their own faults.

Yet for Chinese Communists, self-examination is not an occasional gesture made for appearances. The more consciously one examines and reflects, the more squarely one faces problems; the more resolutely one corrects oneself, the more effective and thorough self-revolution becomes. Many poems, essays, and diaries record the process by which revolutionary predecessors examined and reflected on themselves, strengthening ideological rectification, ideological remolding, and ideological renewal.

Comrade Chen Yi constantly practiced his own motto — "Fullness invites loss, humility receives benefit; be ever diligent throughout the day, strive ceaselessly for self-improvement" — and persisted in the path of ideological remolding. "Do not reach out your hand; if you reach out, you will surely be caught. The Party and the people are watching; with ten thousand eyes upon you, there is no escape." He also wrote large quantities of verse such as "Do Not Reach Out Your Hand (手莫伸)" and "To My Children (示儿女)," warning himself never for a moment to forget ideological Party membership and ideological remolding.

Comrade Wu Yuzhang, on his 81st birthday, solemnly wrote a "Self-Reflective Motto (自省座右铭)": "Past eighty-one, I have yet to resolve my faults. The morning hours are lost, but the evening is not too late. I must painfully reform my past errors and strive to make amends; guard against arrogance and impetuosity, do not be idle or negligent. Inscribed as a reminder."

Countless revolutionary predecessors purified their thinking and rectified their work style through ideological remolding, and thus their original aspirations remained unchanged and their perseverance constant — their Party spirit was strong, their image was towering, and they shine brilliantly in the history of the Party and the military.

Their stories tell us: the more one remolds, the more one feels the need for remolding; the more one strives to advance, the more one thirsts for "daily self-examination on three points." When ideological remolding is conscious and thorough, loyalty to the Party will contain no impurity or dilution; only then can the root problems of thought be resolved, and the political character be preserved forever and purity and honor consolidated.

III

Ideological remolding — the key lies in selecting the right methods. Methods are the bridge and the boat for crossing the river.

Surveying ancient history: Yang Zhen's refusal of a bribe in the dead of night; Sima Guang's "warning pillow" to spur his ambition; Zhu Xi's "investigation of things to extend knowledge (格物致知)"; Wang Yangming's "unity of knowledge and action (知行合一)"; Zhao Gai's "self-reflection by casting beans"; Shen Dianhua's "could I possibly have such faults?"; Zeng Guofan's "twelve daily disciplines (日课十二条)" — these ancient sages and worthies created excellent methods for cultivating moral character and refining thought.

Surveying the history of our Party and our military, methods of ideological remolding have likewise emerged in endless succession, scientific and effective. A few examples are given here:

Method One: Carry out criticism and self-criticism.

The Tan Zheng Report (《谭政报告》) summarized ideological remolding as having three main steps: exposing errors, reflecting on errors, and correcting errors — of which the first and most important is exposing errors. "Without earnest criticism of things that are clearly wrong, one cannot alert cadres, and one cannot mobilize cadres' enthusiasm for examining and criticizing errors."

At the Party's Seventh National Congress, Bo Gu conducted a profound self-criticism and review, expressing his intention to be completely transformed going forward — to "shed the embryo of the petty-bourgeois revolutionary" and "replace the bones of dogmatism."

Only when Party members and cadres carry forward a spirit of seriousness, dare to "flush red-faced and break into a sweat," point out problems in a timely manner, and correct shortcomings and errors, can they ensure that ideological remolding achieves solid results.

Method Two: Forcibly and deliberately examine one's own weaknesses.

Comrade Mao Zedong proposed that leading cadres "repeatedly study their own thinking, their own history, and their current work, and conduct a thorough self-reflection" — even going so far as to "deliberately and forcibly examine one's own weaknesses."

Comrade Li Lisan was an outstanding early leader of the workers' movement in our Party. After leaving an important leadership position due to line errors, he did not sink into despondency but spent his entire life analyzing and correcting his own errors. He would often say openly and frankly, "My name is Li Lisan; I made errors in the past," and he encouraged everyone to offer him criticism and to supervise and help him.

In 1942, to advance the Rectification Movement, the Liberation Daily published an editorial titled "We Must Reflect on Ourselves." It stated: "If we have the resolve to eliminate bad thoughts and habits, and at the same time have the patience to build new thoughts and habits bit by bit, combined with long and unceasing effort, then a complete transformation — discarding the old and establishing the new — is possible for every person." This tells us that if Party members and cadres, in the course of continuously deepening political consolidation training, can all forcibly examine themselves, they will surely be completely renewed in both ideological work style and behavioral habits.

Method Three: Conduct in-depth investigation and research.

Ideological remolding requires deepening understanding on the basis of practice; investigation and research that involves "taking off one's shoes and going down into the fields" is indispensable.

Beginning in January 1942, Zhang Wentian led the "Yan'an Rural Investigation Team" in conducting more than a year of on-site investigation into over twenty villages and towns in northern Shaanxi. This Party theoretician who had once studied in Moscow wrote with deep feeling in his "Record of Departure and Return (出发归来记)": "All work must proceed from objective reality."

He believed that any Communist "once he departs from reality and from the masses, will harden and walk into the museum of old Bolsheviks, becoming a historical exhibit."

Investigation and research is the foundation of planning and the path to accomplishment. Chairman Xi has emphasized the need to cultivate throughout the entire Party a vigorous culture of investigation and research. In recent years, as the pace of strengthening and revitalizing the military has accelerated, our military's grassroots building faces many new situations and changes in terms of mission task requirements, construction content, daily operational conditions, unit organizational forms, the composition and structure of officers and soldiers, and the external social environment. Without conducting in-depth investigation and research, it is impossible to keep pace with the times in thought and thereby make sound decisions.

Method Four: Achieve thorough and transparent "three-level illumination (三层亮)."

The more thorough and transparent ideological remolding is, the more effective and profound it becomes. One must absolutely not be "like the carp spirit in Journey to the West — strike once, spit out one word."

Comrade Deng Xiaoping pointed out: "One must know that the rectification movement relies mainly on one's own hard effort. Only when one harbors a high degree of revolutionary enthusiasm and a spirit of responsibility toward the Party will one be open-hearted … and only then will one be able to rectify one's ideological work style." He believed that those without such resolve could absolutely never rectify themselves.

In 1948, at a conference of cadres at the regimental level and above in the Central Plains Bureau and Central Plains Military Region, Comrade Liu Bocheng proposed the "three-level illumination" approach to ideological remolding — superiors, peers, and subordinates mutually illuminating problems — requiring cadres to say everything they know and say it fully, to profoundly expose problems of lack of organization and discipline, empiricism, and bureaucratism, thereby improving the leadership work style and achieving significant results in army consolidation.

Method Five: Diligently sweep "the dust from one's thinking."

"I examine myself daily on three points, saying I have committed no transgression." Self-reflection is an important method of ideological remolding and elevation of moral realm.

Comrade Peng Dehuai, amid the bustle of military campaigns and the press of ten thousand daily affairs, still emphasized diligently sweeping "the dust from one's thinking." He said with deep feeling: "We are Communists — should we not be more enlightened than the ancients? Can we achieve daily self-reflection, weekly self-reflection, or self-reflection every half-month or month? I reflect on myself once a month. No matter how busy the work, every month I always set aside half a day to earnestly review what I have done — to see what was done correctly and what was done wrongly — so as to make fewer errors or avoid serious errors."

In 1948, during the Western Shaanxi Campaign, the troops recaptured Yan'an but also suffered some losses. In summarizing the lessons of that campaign, Peng Dehuai proactively assumed responsibility and conducted a serious self-criticism: "Peng Dehuai, oh Peng Dehuai — your Marxism-Leninism is simply not thoroughly learned, scattered and fragmentary…"

Carrying the past forward and opening up the future, acting with steadfast commitment. These effective methods of the revolutionary predecessors remain sharp weapons for Party members and cadres in the new era to remold their thinking, temper their souls, and revitalize their spirit.

IV

"A gap the size of a needle's eye is enough to let in the wind." A momentary lapse in ideological vigilance may cause one to fall into an abyss without even knowing it. The value of ideological remolding lies in persisting for a lifetime; the difficulty lies in maintaining consistency throughout.

At present, the world situation, national conditions, Party conditions, and military conditions are all undergoing complex and profound changes, and the political tests our military faces are intricate and complex. From an objective standpoint, the contest between rooting and uprooting has never ceased for a moment; the "four great tests" and "four great dangers" still exist and profoundly affect the building of the military's Party member and cadre corps — the Party's self-revolution is forever on the march. From a subjective standpoint, a person's thinking and behavior can be eroded by "political microorganisms (政治微生物)," and Party members and cadres need to possess sufficiently strong immunity.

Confronted with the situation and tasks, Party organizations at all levels must abandon the notion of "taking a breather and resting one's feet," maintain the tenacity and resolve to see things through to the end, ensure that no errant thoughts within lead to no errant actions without, and continuously strengthen political tempering through ever-deeper ideological remolding.

On the new journey of the new era, doing ideological remolding well requires seizing the root problems of thought and applying precise effort — working hard in the areas of theoretical arming, political education, carrying forward fine traditions, and establishing rules and systems — achieving day-by-day continuity, sustained effort, and long-term results.

— Strengthen theoretical arming to consolidate the foundation of remolding.

The arrow of thought is not for admiration but to strike the center of the target. Strengthening theoretical arming is not for decorating one's façade or embellishing oneself, but for remolding thought and improving work.

To strengthen the consciousness and thoroughness of ideological remolding, we must treat strengthening the arming of the Party's innovative theories as the foremost political task and a long-term strategic task. We must persistently use Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era to consolidate the soul and forge convictions, thoroughly implement Xi Jinping's thought on strengthening the military, and genuinely study thorough theories thoroughly and comprehend vibrant thinking vividly. We must resolutely correct the bad study styles of formalism, superficiality, and utilitarianism, achieve integration of learning, thinking, and application, and unity of knowing, believing, and acting, and truly achieve new results in using learning to forge the soul, using learning to enhance wisdom, using learning to rectify the work style, and using learning to promote action.

— Deepen political education to enhance the practical effectiveness of remolding.

Ideological and political education is construction within the human mind; it is a powerful means and mighty weapon for conducting ideological remolding. Only when educators first receive education themselves, and consciously unify what they say with what they do, can they educate and inspire others through their own exemplary image and role-model conduct. To strengthen the consciousness and thoroughness of ideological remolding, cadres at all levels of the military — especially senior cadres — must receive education first and receive it genuinely.

Persisting in taking the deepening of ideological remolding as the focus, and targeting root ideological problems such as ideals and convictions, Party spirit cultivation, and official virtue and personal character, as well as practical questions such as why one joined the Party, why one joined the military, and what it means to be a commander — we must strengthen the analysis of right and wrong and the clearing of ideological confusion, correct some muddled, distorted, and even subversive concepts, ensure that those who manage souls take the lead in purifying their own souls and those who speak of discipline are the first to strictly observe discipline, and genuinely enhance the persuasive power, influence, and credibility of education.

— Carry forward fine traditions to maintain the normalization of remolding.

Fine traditions are always the spiritual nourishment, precious wealth, and inexhaustible driving force for the ideological remolding of Party members and cadres. Whether we can consistently maintain the glorious traditions and fine work styles of our Party and our military is a major question bearing on military building, and also bears on the quality and character of ideological remolding. With glorious traditions and fine work styles as the foundation, Party members' and cadres' political loyalty, integrity and moral conduct, and moral backbone and character have inner support.

With ideals in the heart, the nation in the chest, and the mission on the shoulders — on the new journey, Party members and cadres must only continue the red genes and inherit the red bloodline, dedicate themselves to the Party and the nation, conduct themselves with integrity, work with solid effort, and serve as officials with clean hands, so as to set the standard and serve as a model with an upright spirit, influencing and driving the troops to uphold the correct path while innovating, and to strive to strengthen the military.

— Improve institutional mechanisms to consolidate the long-term effectiveness of remolding.

Institutions govern the overall situation, the fundamentals, and the long term. To strengthen the consciousness and thoroughness of ideological remolding and ensure that ideological remolding does not become a formality or stop at a single moment, sound institutions are needed as a guarantee.

Strengthen top-level design: by establishing normalized learning systems such as regular theoretical training and special seminars, ensure that the content of ideological education is constantly refreshed and studied deeply and comprehended thoroughly. Optimize the evaluation system: incorporate the results of ideological remolding into the content of cadre assessment, establish reward and punishment mechanisms, commend and reward those with outstanding performance, and urge those who respond passively to rectify. Strengthen institutional implementation: strictly pursue accountability, resolutely eliminate the phenomena of making selective choices, seeking workarounds, and applying discounts, and genuinely uphold the seriousness and authority of institutions.

V

Ideological remolding has no end; the development of the cause has no limit.

It cannot be denied that remolding thought is an arduous and even painful undertaking, but it is the forging of a "political soul (政治灵魂)" commensurate with the Party's nature, purpose, and mission tasks — a systematic project that integrates political character, consciousness, and lifelong commitment.

Ideological remolding — its value lies in consciousness; its difficulty lies in thoroughness. Chairman Xi has pointed out: "Every comrade has the responsibility to remold and improve themselves; sweeping the dust from one's thinking, eliminating bad habits, and correcting erroneous words and deeds have no end and are always in progress."

"A day without a fault to correct is a day without a step forward." Strengthening the consciousness and thoroughness of ideological remolding — returning to the source and consolidating the foundation ideologically — is the intrinsic need of every Party member and cadre for self-revolution, and also the inevitable requirement for implementing the strategy of building the military through politics in the new era and continuously deepening political consolidation training.

Ascending once more to the summit to open new skies, raising the cloud-sail higher to stand at the crest of the tide.

Communists should regard ideological remolding as indispensable as air. Without it, thought will rust and the soul will corrode. As Comrade Zhou Enlai stated in "Passing the 'Five Passes' Well (过好'五关')" — "From the moment one joins the Communist Party, every Party member should have this understanding: be prepared to remold one's thinking, and keep remolding it until old age."

Renew the thinking once, and all things may be hoped for.

Walking on the new Long March road, carrying ideological remolding through to the end, and continuously strengthening the capacity for self-purification, self-improvement, self-renewal, and self-elevation — we will surely be able to "make it new each day, make it new day after day, make it ever new," welcome the centenary of the founding of the military with a completely renewed political bearing, and write a new chapter of strengthening and revitalizing the military that is worthy of our revolutionary predecessors, worthy of the century-old foundation, and worthy of the great era.

思想改造无止境 ■桑林峰 人是靠思想站立的。思想领先是最具优势的领先，思想领导是最根本的领导。只有彻底改造思想，才能成功改造实践，赢得胜利。 思想改造的成效，决定思想整风的质效。在全军高级干部培训班开班式上，习主席强调开展思想整风，深化政治整训，加强我军干部队伍革命性锻造，要求“军队各级干部要自觉摆正位置，站稳群众立场，克服一切脱离群众的私心杂念”。 思想改造的程度，事关政治建军的深度。在中央军委政治工作会议上，习主席体系部署了新征程上推进政治建军要抓好的6个方面重点工作，其中第一个方面就是，“增强思想改造的自觉性和彻底性”。 思想改造如除尘，总能一新面貌；如排毒，总能健康肌体；如祛疾，总能浴火重生。 党员干部灵魂再淬火、党性再锤炼、精神再提振、境界再升华的过程，思想改造伴随左右、永无止境——共产党人活到老，学到老，思想改造到老。 一 思想改造，意义非凡。 马克思、恩格斯在《共产党宣言》中指出，共产主义革命“在自己的发展进程中要同传统的观念实行最彻底的决裂”。 思想改造，就是用马克思主义世界观、人生观、价值观对党员干部思想进行提纯和净化，形象地说就是巩固扩大头脑中的“马列根据地”。它是“破”与“立”的统一。破的是陈旧思想、惯性思维、路径依赖，立的是顺应时代、担当使命、经得起实践检验的新思维新认知。 延安时期，《解放日报》曾刊登陕甘宁边区保安处党支部讨论《中共中央关于增强党性的决定》的情况。该党支部先后组织2次集中传达和4次大课，各党小组讨论共计135小时，单是互相检讨就占到五分之三。 这就是当年我们党依靠党内教育、加强思想改造的一个生动例证。 “见善如不及，见不善如探汤。”思想改造的过程，既是坚持真理、修正错误的过程，又是强化党性、提升操守的过程。 回顾我党我军历史，从古田会议到延安整风，从新式整军运动到新中国成立后的历次党内集中教育活动，无一不是通过深入彻底的思想改造，纠偏正向、纠错正行，打倒一个个“强大的思想敌人”，使党员干部队伍认识更加统一、步调更加整齐。 反之，思想改造不彻底，初心就会蒙尘，信念就会动摇，思想防线就会失守。 深入推进政治建军、持续深化政治整训，必须发扬彻底的自我革命精神，着力将思想改造进行到底，真正把错误的思想根子拔除掉，把正确的价值观念立起来。 ——思想改造是铸牢政治忠诚的客观要求。 军队是执行党的政治任务的武装集团，必须确保枪杆子要始终掌握在对党忠诚可靠的人手中。近百年来，我军之所以能够在各种风险考验和艰难险阻中不断从胜利走向胜利，其中一个重要原因就是通过自觉彻底的思想改造、千锤百炼的革命性锻造，确保党员干部始终忠诚于党的信仰、党的组织、党的事业。 今天，各种思想文化交流交融交锋更加频繁、意识形态领域斗争日益尖锐复杂。唯有进行彻底的思想改造，才能更好地凝魂聚力、强基固本、扶正祛邪，使党员干部坚定不移听党话、跟党走。 ——思想改造是提高胜战能力的必然选择。 思想改造与战斗力建设具有内在统一性，是人民军队的胜战法宝。革命战争年代，正是靠着一次次深刻的思想洗礼，培塑出一不怕苦、二不怕死的血性胆魄，凝聚起无坚不摧、战无不胜的战斗意志，我军才能屡次战胜强敌，谱写惊天地、泣鬼神的英雄史诗。 不难发现，我军在多次大的战役前，都会在党员干部中进行深刻的思想改造，来一场“灵魂深处的革命”。比如淮海战役前的濮阳整训、解放上海前的丹阳整训等。 当前，世界百年未有之大变局加速演进，新一轮科技革命和军事革命日新月异，战争形态、制胜机理、作战要素等发生深刻变化。 面对复杂严峻的国家安全形势和艰巨繁重的备战打仗任务，唯有持续深化思想改造，不断熔铸敢战之勇、砥砺胜战之能、激发善战之智，使党员干部全部心思向打仗聚焦、全部工作向打仗用劲，我军才能在世界军事竞争中挺立潮头，做到召之即来、来之能战、战之必胜。 ——思想改造是锤炼过硬作风的现实需要。 作风优良塑造英雄部队，作风松散搞垮常胜之师。 在长期的革命、建设、改革进程中，我党我军不仅形成了一系列优良作风，而且积累了作风建设的宝贵经验，其中重要一条就是强化思想改造。 我党我军历次集中整训整风，都是以思想教育打头，通过整顿思想来整齐步伐、整肃队伍、整治作风，确保了全党全军始终团结一致向前进。 思想上的滑坡是最严重的病变。党员干部思想上松一寸，行动上就会松一尺，各种出轨越界、跑冒滴漏也就在所难免。唯有坚持不懈强化思想改造，持续匡正思想认识，廓清是非界限，党员干部才能以作风优良的过硬形象，有效履行新时代我军使命任务。 二 思想改造，是共产党人的必修课。 在这方面，老一辈革命家和优秀党员干部为我们作出了表率、立起了标杆。 周恩来同志终身修为、常悟常进，一辈子坚持思想改造，从来没有间断过。 1943年3月18日，是周恩来农历45岁生日。同志们提出为他准备茶点庆贺，他婉言谢绝，只是简单吃了碗面条就回到办公室工作。当晚，他写下著名的《我的修养要则》，涵盖了工作、学习、生活、交往等7个方面，作为其一生思想改造的重要内容。 延安整风期间，周恩来写下5万多字的整改心得，作了5天交流发言，是高级干部整风会议中最详尽的自我剖析。 见筋见骨的思想改造是不容易的，需要勇气，需要内省，需要自我解剖，不留情面，不怕亮丑。 张闻天同志坚持“真理在谁手里，就跟谁走”，是敢于刀刃向内的模范。一段时间内，张闻天执行了错误的政策，但他勇于坚持真理、改正错误，主动承担责任。他诚恳地说：“反对主观主义，要作彻底的清算，不要掩盖，不要怕揭发自己的错误，不要怕自己的瘌痢头给人家看。” 谢觉哉同志是人民司法制度的奠基者之一。他把思想改造比作“自己跟自己打官司”，即在自己的头脑中，用共产主义思想与各种不正确的思想观念作斗争，坚持自己当被告、当律师、当法官，对自己严加评判、毫不手软，在不断自我“审判”中擦亮党员本色。 谢觉哉追求“毕生自修无尽期”，对缺点不足从不掩饰。1943年元旦这天，他口占一诗：“事已年年旧，行须日日新。”从这年1月开始，他连续写了10篇反省文章，“好的坏的，和盘托出”。在《六十自讼》一文中他写道，“‘行年五十，当知四十九年之非’，那么行年六十，也应该设法弥补五十九年以前的缺点”。这种善鉴己过、严于律己的精神，正是思想改造的要义所在。 “灵魂之深处，自掘才可能。”人生旅途上，每个人都好似背着两个包袱，一个包袱是“他人的过失”，另一个包袱是“自己的过失”。大多数人把前者放在胸前，把后者放在背后，一低头就看到他人之非，而对自己之过失却很少看到。 然而，对中国共产党人而言，自我检视并不是偶尔为之的应景之举。检视反思、正视问题越自觉，自省自纠、改正错误越坚决，自我革命才能越有效、越彻底。很多诗词、文章、日记，记录着革命前辈检视自我、反省自我，加强思想整风、思想改造、思想革新的过程。 陈毅同志时刻践行自己的座右铭，“满招损，谦受益；终日乾乾，自强不息”，坚持走思想改造的道路。“手莫伸，伸手必被捉。党与人民在监督，万目睽睽难逃脱。”他还写下《手莫伸》《示儿女》等大量诗句，警醒自己一刻不忘思想入党、思想改造。 吴玉章同志在81岁生日时，郑重写下一篇《自省座右铭》：“年过八一，寡过未解，东隅已失，桑榆未晚。必须痛改前非，力图挽救，戒骄戒躁，毋怠毋荒，谨铭。” 无数革命前辈在改造思想中纯洁思想、纯正作风，因而初心不改、恒心不变，党性坚强、形象高大，在党史军史中熠熠生辉。 他们的故事告诉我们，越改造就越觉得需要改造，越上进就越渴求“吾日三省吾身”。思想改造自觉彻底，对党忠诚才不会掺任何杂质和水分，才能解决思想根子问题，永葆政治本色、巩固纯洁光荣。 三 思想改造，贵在选准方法。方法是过河的桥和船。 纵览古代史，杨震“暮夜却金”、司马光“警枕励志”、朱熹“格物致知”、王阳明“知行合一”、赵概“投豆自省”、沈甸华“吾得毋有是”、曾国藩“日课十二条”……这些古圣先贤创造了修身养性、精进思想的良好方法。 纵观我党我军的历史，思想改造之法，同样层出不穷、科学有效。在此，仅举几例—— 方法之一：开展批评和自我批评。 《谭政报告》总结思想改造主要有3个步骤，揭露错误、反省错误、改正错误，其中首先重要的是揭露错误，“不对明明错了的东西进行认真的批评，就不能警惕干部，就不能发动干部对于检讨错误、批评错误的积极性”。 在党的七大上，博古作了深刻的自我批评和检讨，表示今后将脱胎换骨，“脱小资产阶级革命家之胎”“换教条主义之骨”。 党员干部只有发扬认真精神，敢于“红红脸、出出汗”，及时把问题指出来，把缺点和错误改过来，才能确保思想改造取得扎实成效。 方法之二：强制地省察自己的弱点。 毛泽东同志提出，领导干部“要反复研究自己的思想、自己的历史、自己现在的工作，好好地反省一下”，甚至“要故意地强制地省察自己的弱点”。 李立三同志是我党早期杰出的工人运动领袖，因路线错误离开重要领导岗位后，他没有消沉，而是用一生剖析和纠正自己的错误。他经常开诚布公地说，“我叫李立三，过去犯过错误”，并鼓励大家给自己提意见，监督和帮助自己。 1942年，为推进整风运动，《解放日报》发表社论《一定要反省自己》。文中写道：“假如我们能够有决心来革除不好的思想和习惯，同时又有耐心一点一滴来建设新的思想和习惯，加上长时期不停的努力，则脱胎换骨，除旧布新，每个人都是可能的。”这告诉我们，如果党员干部在持续深化政治整训中，都能强制地省察自我，则必能从思想作风到行为习惯焕然一新。 方法之三：深入开展调查研究。 思想改造，需要在实践的基础上深化认识，“脱鞋下田”的调查研究必不可少。 从1942年1月开始，张闻天率领“延安农村调查团”，深入陕北20多个村镇进行了长达一年多的实地调查。这位曾留学莫斯科的党内理论家，在他的《出发归来记》中深有感触地写道：“一切工作必须从客观的实际出发。” 他认为，任何共产党员“只要他一旦脱离实际、脱离群众，他就会硬化起来，走进老布尔什维克的博物馆，做历史的陈列品”。 调查研究是谋事之基、成事之道。习主席强调，要在全党大兴调查研究之风。这些年，随着强军兴军步伐加快，我军基层建设在使命任务要求、建设内涵、日常运行状态、部队组织形态、官兵成分结构、外部社会环境等方面面临许多新情况新变化，不深入开展调研，思想上就无法做到与时俱进，进而作出科学决策。 方法之四：彻底透明“三层亮”。 思想改造越彻底越有效，越透明越深刻，千万“不要像《西游记》中的鲤鱼精，打一下，吐一字”。 邓小平同志指出：“要知道整风运动主要靠自己下苦功夫。要自己抱有高度的革命热情和对党负责的精神，才会胸怀坦白……也才会把思想作风整好。”他认为，没有这种决心的人，是绝对整不好的。 1948年，在中原局、中原军区团以上干部会议上，刘伯承同志提出思想改造“三层亮”，即上级、同级、下级互相亮问题，要求干部知无不言、言无不尽，深刻揭露无组织无纪律、经验主义、官僚主义等问题，从而改进了领导作风，取得显著整军效果。 方法之五：勤扫“思想上的灰尘”。 “三省吾身，谓予无愆。”自省是思想改造、提升境界的重要方法。 彭德怀同志戎马倥偬，日理万机，仍注重勤扫“思想上的灰尘”。他深有体会地说：“我们是共产党人，应该比古人更高明些吧。我们能不能做到一日一省，一周一省，半月或一月一省呢？我是一月一省吾身，不管工作怎样忙，每月总要抽出半天时间把自己做过的事认真地检讨一番，看哪些做对了，哪些做错了，以便少犯错误或不犯严重错误。” 1948年，在西府战役中，部队收复了延安，但也遭受了一些损失。在总结这次战役经验教训时，彭德怀主动承担了责任，并作了严肃的自我批评：“彭德怀呀彭德怀，你的马列主义就是没学通，七零八碎的……” 继往开来，笃行致远。革命前辈这些管用的方法，依然是新时代党员干部改造思想、淬炼灵魂、焕发精神的锐利武器。 四 “针眼之隙，足以容风。”一时的思想松懈，就可能跌入深渊而不自知。思想改造贵在一辈子、难在一以贯之。 当前，世情、国情、党情、军情都在发生复杂深刻变化，我军在政治上面临的考验错综复杂：从客观情况看，固根与毁根的较量一刻未停，“四大考验”“四种危险”仍然存在，深刻影响着军队党员干部队伍建设，党的自我革命永远在路上；从主观因素讲，人的思想和行为会受到“政治微生物”的侵蚀，党员干部需要具备足够强的免疫力。 形势任务面前，各级党组织必须摒弃“松口气、歇歇脚”的想法，保持一抓到底的韧劲定力，以内无妄思保证外无妄动，以更加深入的思想改造持续加强政治锻造。 新时代新征程，抓好思想改造必须扭住思想根子问题精准发力，在理论武装、政治教育、弘扬传统、建章立制等方面下功夫用气力，做到日日为继、绵绵用力、久久为功。 ——强化理论武装，筑牢改造根基。 思想的利箭不是用来赞美的，而是为了射入靶心。加强理论武装不是为了装点门面、粉饰自我，而是为了改造思想、改进工作。 增强思想改造的自觉性和彻底性，必须把强化党的创新理论武装作为首要政治任务和长期战略任务。坚持不懈用习近平新时代中国特色社会主义思想凝心铸魂，深入贯彻习近平强军思想，切实把彻底的理论学彻底、把鲜活的思想悟鲜活。坚决纠治表态化、浅表化、功利化等不良学风，做到学思用贯通、知信行统一，真正在以学铸魂、以学增智、以学正风、以学促干上取得新成效。 ——深化政治教育，增强改造实效。 思想政治教育是在人的头脑里搞建设，是进行思想改造的有力手段和强大武器。教育者只有先接受教育，自觉把立言与立行统一起来，才能用自己的良好形象和表率作用教育人激励人。增强思想改造的自觉性和彻底性，军队各级干部特别是高级干部必须先受教育、真受教育。 坚持以深化思想改造为重点，针对理想信念、党性修养、官德人品等思想根子问题，针对入党干什么、当兵干什么、为将干什么等现实问题，加强是非辨析和思想清理，把一些模糊的、扭曲的甚至是颠覆的观念正过来，做到管灵魂的带头纯洁灵魂、讲纪律的首先严守纪律，切实增强教育感召力影响力公信力。 ——弘扬优良传统，保持改造常态。 优良传统始终是党员干部思想改造的精神营养、宝贵财富、不竭动力。能否始终如一保持我党我军光荣传统和优良作风，是关系军队建设的大问题，也关系思想改造的质量成色。有了光荣传统和优良作风打底，党员干部的政治忠诚、廉洁操守、气节风骨就有了内在支撑。 心中有理想，胸中有家国，肩上有使命。新征程上，党员干部只有赓续红色基因、传承红色血脉，以身许党许国，干干净净做人，踏踏实实做事，清清白白做官，才能以一身正气树标杆、作表率，影响和带动部队守正创新、奋斗强军。 ——健全制度机制，巩固改造长效。 制度是管全局、管根本、管长远的。增强思想改造的自觉性和彻底性，确保思想改造不流于形式、不止于一时，需要良好的制度作保障。 强化顶层设计，通过建立定期理论培训、专题研讨等常态化学习制度，确保思想教育内容常学常新、学深悟透；优化考评体系，将思想改造成效纳入干部考核内容，设立奖惩机制，对表现突出者表彰奖励，对消极应付者督促整改；强化制度执行，严肃追责问责，坚决杜绝做选择、搞变通、打折扣的现象，切实维护制度的严肃性权威性。 五 思想改造无止境，事业发展无穷期。 不可否认，改造思想是一件艰苦甚至痛苦的事，但它是在锻造与党的性质宗旨和使命任务相匹配的“政治灵魂”，是一项集政治性、自觉性与终身性于一体的系统工程。 思想改造，贵在自觉、难在彻底。习主席指出：“每个同志都有改造自己、提高自己的职责，打扫思想灰尘、祛除不良习气、纠正错误言行永无止境，永远都是进行时。”
