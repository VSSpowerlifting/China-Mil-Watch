Party Branch of Unit 75640's Subunit 72: A Steadfast Fortress Standing on the Banks of the Háo River

75640部队72分队党支部：坚强堡垒矗立濠江之畔
PLA Daily (解放军报) 28 July 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
Unit 75640's Subunit 72, the PLA garrison unit stationed in Macau, is profiled as a national-level advanced grassroots Party organization, with PLA Daily detailing how its Party branch manages ideological discipline in a garrison environment the article describes as uniquely corrosive — barracks abutting casinos, churches, and restaurants. The article documents the branch's use of Xi Jinping Thought on Strengthening the Military, honors-room indoctrination for new arrivals, and a confined-space training regimen developed by deputy Party secretary Wu Shiwei to compensate for Macau's lack of open training ground. This is standard political-work profile content; its value is as a record of how the PLA frames loyalty maintenance and training adaptation for a small, high-visibility garrison under sustained exposure to a non-mainland urban environment.
Translation

National Advanced Grassroots Party Organization, Party Branch of Unit 75640's Subunit 72——

A Steadfast Fortress Standing on the Banks of the Háo River

■ Li Zhongwei, Yan Jiaxin, PLA Daily Reporter Chen Xiaojie

The subunit's Party branch organizes an oath-taking ceremony. Photo by Yan Jiaxin.

At 5 o'clock in the morning, a thin mist still hangs over the Háo River, and the streets and lanes of Macau's urban districts are quiet and still. As the neon lights fade and the morning light spills across the Sai Van Bridge, two sentries stand ramrod straight and motionless at a certain guard post, their camouflaged silhouettes merging into the city's dawn.

This is an ordinary morning on the banks of the Háo River, and it is also the day-after-day vigil kept by the officers and soldiers of Unit 75640's Subunit 72. Since taking up garrison in Macau, faced with the special garrison environment where "the parade ground faces the casino, the assembly hall faces the church, and the guard tower faces the restaurant," the subunit's Party branch has consistently upheld the principles of "build the military soul before building the garrison, raise the Party flag before winning battles, and guard against moral decay before guarding the defense." Confronting the test of diverse ideological currents, it has continuously strengthened the officers' and soldiers' faith and conviction in wholeheartedly following the Party, forging an absolutely loyal and absolutely pure "Háo River Iron Fist."

Ideological redness (思想红) is the most authentic political character of the subunit's Party branch. As a unit personally inspected by Chairman Xi, the subunit's Party branch persists in using Xi Jinping Thought on Strengthening the Military to forge the soul and cultivate personnel, and consistently transforms Chairman Xi's concern into the driving force for unrelenting struggle.

Zhang Long, a Party branch committee member of the subunit and a First-Class Staff Sergeant, has stood guard on the banks of the Háo River for more than ten years. On one occasion, Private Wu Wensheng remarked during a break in training: "Inside the barracks it's the same guard duties and training day after day, while outside is a glittering, bustling metropolis — the contrast across such a short distance is just too great." Upon hearing this, Zhang Long told the new comrade: "The loyalty of soldiers garrisoned in Macau is expressed precisely within this small barracks compound. To fulfill the sacred mission entrusted to us by the Party Central Committee and the Central Military Commission, we must hold fast to our original convictions, endure loneliness, and withstand every test!"

In the subunit, whenever new recruits join the unit or new officers take up their posts, the very first lesson is a visit to the honors room. Inside the honors room, the red memories of the War to Resist U.S. Aggression and Aid Korea, letters of gratitude from flood relief and rescue operations, and certificates of merit from combat readiness training — every physical object represents a period of hard-won glory, and every story is a spiritual baptism.

Macau's territory is small and its buildings densely packed, with no open field training grounds or exercise areas. "Space may be limited, but the enhancement of combat effectiveness cannot be limited; the area may be small, but training standards must absolutely not be lowered!" A subunit officer explained that, in response to the special garrison environment, Wu Shiwei, deputy secretary of the subunit's Party branch, led Party member backbone personnel to work overtime at their combat posts, carving out a distinctive path of military training within the confined barracks compound: on a site just over 100 meters in depth, they set up a continuous series of training events — carrying tires, shouldering ammunition crates, and pushing combat vehicles — to train physical fitness, coordination, and willpower; they first use the "simulated training center" to conduct marksmanship, driving, and communications specialty training, then proceed to field garrison training sites to verify results, forming a training model of "simulation lays the foundation, live equipment builds hard skills."

Respond to command at the sound of the order, dare to charge forward at moments of danger. In the more than 20 years since taking up garrison in Macau, the officers and soldiers of the subunit have consistently carried forward the fine tradition of "dare to charge, fight to the end, strive to be first, and serve as a model." In August 2017, Macau was struck by the strongest typhoon in more than 50 years. The subunit received emergency orders and assisted in rescue operations. During the mission, Party members and officers charged to the front, serving simultaneously as commanders and as fighters. Faced with mountains of foul-smelling debris, Song Haiqiang, then deputy secretary of the subunit's Party branch and a Party member, was the first to climb up and begin clearing it, leading the officers and soldiers to complete the mission to a high standard.

The "lead goose" (头雁) drives the "flock of geese" (群雁) to fly together. The subunit's Party members take the lead and set the example, and every officer and soldier strives to achieve excellence and render new merit. Since taking up garrison in Macau, the subunit has successively been rated "Advanced Unit in Overall Grassroots Development" six times and "Grade-One Unit in Military Training" five times, has been awarded collective first-class merit citations, second-class merit citations, and third-class merit citations once each, and has been commended as a "Model Unit in Practicing the Goal of Strengthening the Military Across the Entire Military."

On the new journey, the subunit's Party branch leads all officers and soldiers, maintaining their vigil on the banks of the Háo River with a spirit of tireless striving, working hard to write a new chapter for the Iron Fist of Macau's defense in the new era.

Original Chinese
全国先进基层党组织、75640部队72分队党支部—— 坚强堡垒矗立濠江之畔 ■李忠伟 鄢家鑫 解放军报记者 陈晓杰 该分队党支部组织宣誓仪式。鄢家鑫摄 凌晨5时，濠江薄雾未散，澳门城区大街小巷静谧安然。当霓虹褪去、晨光漫过西湾大桥，某执勤点位上，两名哨兵身姿挺拔、纹丝不动，迷彩身影融入城市黎明。 这是濠江之畔寻常的清晨，也是75640部队72分队官兵日复一日的坚守。进驻澳门以来，面对“操场对着赌场、礼堂对着教堂、岗楼对着酒楼”的特殊驻防环境，该分队党支部始终坚持“驻军先铸军魂、打赢先亮党旗、防务先防变质”，直面多元思潮考验，不断强化官兵铁心向党的信仰信念，锻造绝对忠诚、绝对纯洁的“濠江铁拳”。 思想红，是该分队党支部最纯正的政治底色。作为习主席视察过的部队，分队党支部坚持用习近平强军思想铸魂育人，始终把习主席的关怀转化为不懈奋斗的动力。 该分队党支部委员、一级上士张龙，在濠江之畔已驻守10余年。一次，列兵吴文生在训练间隙感慨：“营区里面是日复一日的执勤训练，外面是灯红酒绿的繁华都市，咫尺之间落差也太大了。”张龙闻言，告诉这名新战友：“驻澳军人的忠诚，就体现在这方寸营区里。完成党中央、中央军委赋予我们的神圣使命，必须守得住本心、耐得住寂寞、经得住考验！” 在该分队，每逢新兵下队、新干部任职，第一课就是参观荣誉室。荣誉室里，抗美援朝的红色记忆、抗洪抢险的感谢信函、练兵备战的荣誉证书等，每一件实物都是一段峥嵘岁月，每一个故事都是一次精神洗礼。 澳门地域狭小、楼宇密集，没有开阔的野外训练场和演训场地。“场地受限，战斗力提升不能受限；空间虽小，练兵标准绝不降低！”该分队干部介绍，针对驻防特殊环境，分队党支部副书记吴世伟带领党员骨干铆在战位加班加点，在方寸营区中走出一条特色练兵之路：在纵深100多米的场地上，连贯设置搬轮胎、扛弹药箱、推战车等课目，练体能、练协同、练意志；先利用“模拟训练中心”开展射击、驾驶、通信专业训练，再赴野外驻训场检验成效，形成“模拟打基础、实装练硬功”的训练模式。 听从指挥闻令动，危险关头敢冲锋。进驻澳门20多年来，该分队官兵始终传承“敢冲锋、拼到底、争第一、当标杆”的优良作风，出色完成各类大项任务。2017年8月，澳门遭遇50多年来最强台风袭击。分队临危受命，协助救援。任务中，党员、干部冲锋在前，既当指挥员，又当战斗员。面对堆积成山的恶臭垃圾，分队党支部时任副书记宋海强第一个爬上去清理，带领官兵高标准完成任务。 “头雁”带动“群雁”齐飞。该分队党员走在前、当表率，官兵人人争创一流、争建新功。进驻澳门以来，分队先后6次被评为“基层全面建设先进单位”，5次被评为“军事训练一级单位”，荣立集体一等功、二等功、三等功各1次，被表彰为“全军践行强军目标标兵单位”。 新征程上，该分队党支部带领全体官兵，以永不懈怠的奋斗姿态坚守濠江之畔，奋力书写新时代澳门防务铁拳的崭新篇章。
