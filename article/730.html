Make the Grassroots the Fertile Ground for Carrying Forward Fine Traditions

把基层作为赓续优良传统的沃土
PLA Daily (解放军报) 27 May 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
Two researchers from National Defense University's Research Center for Xi Jinping Thought published a political-work essay in response to Xi Jinping's remarks at the PLA and PAP delegation session of the 14th NPC's Fourth Session, calling on grassroots units to institutionalize 'fine traditions' (优良传统) through Party organization strengthening, ideological education, strict discipline, and role-model campaigns. The article is standard political-work content directed at junior officers and soldiers; its value is as a record of how NDU frames the 'three-hard' (三个过硬) grassroots-building standard and the 'red genes' inheritance line in the current political cycle, not as evidence of a new policy or doctrinal shift. The NPC session timing confirms this is a routine post-plenum transmission of Xi's political guidance downward through the force.
Translation

Make the Grassroots the Fertile Ground for Carrying Forward Fine Traditions

■ Sun Wenguang, Zhang Di

Editor's Note

The foundational things must be held fast, and the advantages rooted in tradition must not be lost. The fine traditions of our Party and our military embody a clear political stance, firm ideals and convictions, advanced ways of achieving victory, a lofty revolutionary spirit, and excellent conduct and discipline. They are the concentrated expression of the nature, purpose, and character of the People's Army, and are also a unique political advantage of our military and an important magic weapon (法宝) for defeating the enemy.

Chairman Xi has emphasized: "Whether we can preserve the glorious traditions and fine conduct of our Party and our military bears on the life and death of the armed forces, bears on the rise and fall of the Party's and the nation's cause, and bears on whether the socialist red country will change its color." At present, the cause of building a strong military stands at a critical juncture. As the principal force for carrying forward fine traditions, grassroots officers and soldiers must draw rich political nourishment and powerful spiritual strength from those fine traditions, pass on the red genes well, preserve the character of the old Red Army, and press forward with a fresh bearing—tackling hard problems with courage and advancing with firm resolve.

When attending the plenary session of the delegation of the People's Liberation Army and the People's Armed Police Force at the Fourth Session of the 14th National People's Congress, Chairman Xi emphasized: "Make the grassroots the fertile ground for carrying forward fine traditions; actively explore the laws governing grassroots development and beneficial practices under new circumstances; attach importance to doing work that addresses fundamentals, benefits the long term, and builds lasting strength; and lay a more solid foundation for troop development and combat effectiveness." This important statement profoundly clarifies the intrinsic connection between carrying forward fine traditions and grassroots development under new circumstances, and reveals the special status and role of the grassroots in carrying forward fine traditions. We must deeply comprehend Chairman Xi's important statement, drive the fine traditions to be passed down from generation to generation at the grassroots like a torch that never goes out, and lay a solid foundation for achieving the centenary goal of Army founding on schedule and for advancing the modernization of national defense and the military at high quality.

I

In the great practice of leading the People's Army through revolution, construction, and reform, our Party has accumulated many invaluable experiences and formed a complete set of fine traditions. These fine traditions originated from the creation of officers and soldiers, are rooted in grassroots practice, and are the secret code of victory by which the People's Army has gone from weakness to strength and from victory to victory.

The grassroots is the birthplace and source of fine traditions. The front-line grassroots is the ever-living source and root of our military's spiritual bloodline, and also the rich fertile ground from which fine traditions first emerged. The fundamental principle and system of the Party's absolute leadership over the military originated at the Nanchang Uprising, was laid as a foundation at the Sanwan Reorganization, and took definitive form at the Gutian Conference. The strict discipline of the "Three Main Rules of Discipline and Eight Points for Attention" (三大纪律、八项注意) arose from the practice of disciplinary rectification as the troops of the Autumn Harvest Uprising along the Hunan-Jiangxi border marched toward Jinggang Mountain. The fighting spirit of "fearing neither hardship nor death" (一不怕苦、二不怕死) was embodied in every charge and every bloody battle fought by front-line officers and soldiers. History and practice have proven that fine traditions such as hard struggle, sacrifice and dedication, unity and mutual care, and solidarity between officers and soldiers were all gradually accumulated and formed through practice at the front-line grassroots.

The grassroots is the main arena for inheriting fine traditions. The grassroots is the foundation of all the work and combat effectiveness of the troops, and generation after generation of grassroots officers and soldiers are the most steadfast practitioners and most faithful inheritors of fine traditions. From Zhang Side, who served the people wholeheartedly during the War of Resistance Against Japan, to Dong Cunrui, who sacrificed himself to blow up a concealed bunker during the War of Liberation; from Huang Jiguang, who used his chest to block a gun port during the War to Resist U.S. Aggression and Aid Korea, to Qiu Shaoyun, who remained motionless as flames engulfed his body—countless revolutionary martyrs like them forged loyalty with their blood and shouldered their mission with their lives, manifesting glorious traditions in their revolutionary work and carrying them forward on the battlefield. During the period of peaceful construction, Lei Feng steadfastly upheld his original aspiration of serving the people; Su Ning dedicated himself to national defense modernization; Li Xiangqun charged to the front lines of flood-fighting; Zhang Chao pursued his dream of a strong military across the sea and sky. Countless heroes and model figures like them took root at the grassroots and cultivated it deeply, driving our military's fine traditions to be carried forward from generation to generation without end.

II

To carry forward fine traditions is to plant the red genes into the souls of officers and soldiers, to fully bring into play the political and organizational advantages that the Party has formed in leading our military through long-term practice, and to provide solid support for comprehensively forging a grassroots force that is "hard in three respects" (三个过硬).

Let the fundamental principle and system of the Party commanding the gun take firm root at the grassroots. The Party's absolute leadership over the military is the founding foundation and the soul of a strong military for the People's Army. We must give full play to the unique advantage of building the military through political work; firmly grasp the key link of Party organizations; strengthen the political and organizational functions of grassroots Party organizations; strictly implement the various systems of organizational life; strictly conduct education, management, and supervision of Party members; strengthen the building of the corps of grassroots Party organization secretaries; give full play to the vanguard fortress role of grassroots Party organizations and the pioneering and exemplary role of the broad Party membership; and ensure that the Party's absolute leadership over the military reaches directly to the grassroots and directly to officers and soldiers.

Let lofty ideals and firm convictions take firm root at the grassroots. Lofty ideals and firm convictions are the spiritual pillar of the People's Army and the powerful spiritual force enabling grassroots officers and soldiers to tackle hard problems, resist corruption and prevent degeneration, and fulfill their duties and responsibilities. We must consistently use the great ideal of communism to guide the direction of grassroots development; use the common ideal of socialism with Chinese characteristics to unite the consensus of grassroots officers and soldiers in their struggle; work hard to cultivate new-era revolutionary soldiers who have soul, ability, fighting spirit, and virtue (有灵魂、有本事、有血性、有品德); and guide grassroots officers and soldiers to consciously integrate their personal aspirations into the great practice of building a strong military and revitalizing the military, to hold fast to their original aspirations and strive with dedication in ordinary posts, and to always maintain the political character of revolutionary soldiers.

Let the quality of keeping pace with the times through reform and innovation take firm root at the grassroots. The grassroots contains boundless wisdom and vibrant energy, and is the living source of reform and innovation. We must uphold the principle of putting the grassroots first and soldiers first; respect the principal status and pioneering spirit of officers and soldiers; encourage grassroots units to break through and innovate based on their combat positions and in close connection with actual conditions; integrate innovative thinking into all aspects and the entire process of grassroots development; actively absorb the fresh experience of grassroots innovation and creation; promote practical and effective grassroots measures; and use vigorous innovative vitality to accelerate the advancement of national defense and military modernization.

Let the hard fighting spirit of heroism and fearlessness take firm root at the grassroots. Fighting spirit is an important factor in the combat effectiveness of the military and the key to stimulating the subjective initiative of people and bringing into play the decisive role of people in military practice. We must persist in integrating the cultivation of fighting spirit into the entire process of military training, combat readiness duty, and the execution of major tasks in grassroots units; temper and forge the troops under difficult and strict conditions; hone the fighting spirit and courage of officers and soldiers; forge the will and character of officers and soldiers to fear no strong enemy and dare to fight and be certain of victory; lead officers and soldiers to be full of fighting spirit, and lead the troops to project a powerful and vigorous presence.

Let the strict revolutionary discipline of acting on orders without fail (令行禁止) take firm root at the grassroots. The military is the most disciplined of organizations, and strict discipline is an important source of combat effectiveness. In wartime we won battles through discipline; in peacetime, strengthening troop development is equally inseparable from iron discipline. We must firmly establish a sense of discipline; persist in conducting grassroots development in strict accordance with regulations and rules; strictly implement the provisions of regulations and codes; supervise officers and soldiers to strictly observe disciplinary standards and conform to institutional norms in their words and actions; strengthen supervision and enforcement of discipline; address problems early and when they are small; guard against minor problems before they grow; and use rigid enforcement of discipline to ensure that disciplinary authority takes root at the grassroots.

Let the fundamental purpose of serving the people wholeheartedly take firm root at the grassroots. Coming from the people and serving the people, always remaining flesh-and-blood connected to the people and sharing life and death with them—this is the root of our victory and the source of our strength. We must always stand firmly on the side of the people and firmly establish the concept of putting the people first; focus on the primary responsibility and main task of training for war and preparing for war, and safeguard the people's peace through the ability to fight and win battles; in the face of disasters and emergencies, move at the sound of orders and step forward bravely, vowing to protect the lives and property of the people; and support local economic development and ecological environmental protection, and actively carry out poverty alleviation and assistance work.

III

Driving the carrying forward and inheritance of fine traditions at the grassroots is a systematic project and a long-term task. We must closely integrate the carrying forward of fine traditions with the strengthening of grassroots development, apply comprehensive measures, and work at it persistently over time, so that fine traditions truly permeate the hearts of officers and soldiers and guide their words and actions, and drive high-quality development of grassroots development.

Strengthen theoretical arming, and consolidate the ideological foundation by strengthening the roots and nurturing the vitality (固本培元). Thought is the precursor to action; theory is the guide to practice. We must continuously deepen the arming of officers and soldiers with the Party's innovative theories; organize grassroots officers and soldiers to systematically study the glorious history and fine traditions of our Party and our military; resolve the fundamental questions of for whom we carry rifles, for whom we fight, what we are in the military for, and why we train; fully excavate the red resources of the garrison area and the unit itself; make good use of vivid educational vehicles; innovate forms of study; and use methods that grassroots officers and soldiers enjoy to expound on theory and interpret tradition.

Ground efforts in practical tempering, and firmly establish the orientation of winning battles through training for war and preparing for war. Training for war and preparing for war is the primary responsibility and main task of the troops, and is also the main practical path for carrying forward fine traditions. Grassroots units are on the front line of training for war and preparing for war. We must closely integrate the carrying forward of fine traditions with the practice of training for war and preparing for war; temper the excellent qualities of heeding the Party's command, making sacrifice and dedication, and daring to fight and being certain of victory; and guide grassroots officers and soldiers to practice fine traditions in the execution of tasks and to manifest the powerful strength of fine traditions in tackling hard problems.

Uphold strict management and generous care, and cultivate a pure character in day-to-day work. Carrying forward fine traditions depends primarily on daily cultivation and is most valuable when persisted in over the long term. Strict management can rectify conduct and shape character and temper work style; generous care can unite hearts and inspire morale. The two are dialectically unified and mutually reinforcing. We must unswervingly advance the comprehensive strict governance of the Party and the comprehensive strict governance of the military; do solid work on building a healthy atmosphere at the grassroots; continuously rectify various tendencies and problems; continuously purify the grassroots political ecology; persist in caring for the grassroots and caring for officers and soldiers; get down to work to resolve the urgent, difficult, worrying, and anticipated problems of officers and soldiers; and continuously boost the spirit and bearing of officers and soldiers in doing their work and pursuing their undertakings through the resolution of difficulties and problems.

Emphasize the motivation of exemplary models, and generate intrinsic motivation through the driving force of role models. The deeds of advanced exemplary models embody the fighting spirit of officers and soldiers—they are concrete and vivid, and carry the power to move people. The qualities of advanced exemplary models reflect the value pursuits of the People's Army—they are lofty and plain, and carry the power to inspire. We must emphasize the cultivation and promotion of advanced exemplary models in urgent, difficult, dangerous, and heavy tasks such as training for war and preparing for war, disaster relief, and scientific research breakthroughs; through extensive publicity and in-depth study of their deeds of struggle, guide grassroots officers and soldiers to revere the advanced and strive to be pioneers; and genuinely transform the power of role models into the high-spirited fighting will to sharpen military skills and strengthen capabilities, and into the sense of mission and responsibility to fulfill duties and discharge responsibilities.

(Authors' affiliation: Research Center for Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era, National Defense University)

把基层作为赓续优良传统的沃土 ■孙文广 张 迪 编者按 根子上的东西要守住，传统上的优势不能丢。我党我军的优良传统，蕴含着鲜明的政治立场、坚定的信仰信念、先进的制胜之道、崇高的革命精神和优良的作风纪律，是人民军队性质宗旨本色的集中体现，也是我军特有政治优势和克敌制胜的重要法宝。 习主席强调，“能否保持我党我军的光荣传统和优良作风，关系军队生死存亡，关系党和国家事业兴衰成败，关系社会主义红色江山会不会改变颜色”。当前，强军事业处在一个关键当口，作为赓续优良传统的主体力量，基层官兵要从优良传统中汲取丰厚政治营养、强大精神力量，把红色基因传承好、把老红军本色保持住，以崭新面貌攻坚奋进、勇毅前行。 习主席在出席十四届全国人大四次会议解放军和武警部队代表团全体会议时强调：“要把基层作为赓续优良传统的沃土，积极探索新形势下基层建设规律和有益做法，重视抓好管根本、利长远、增后劲的工作，把部队建设和战斗力基础打得更加牢靠。”这一重要论述，深刻阐明了赓续优良传统与新形势下基层建设的内在联系，揭示了基层在赓续优良传统中的特殊地位和作用。我们要深刻领悟习主席的重要论述，推动优良传统在基层薪火永续、代代相传，为如期实现建军一百年奋斗目标、高质量推进国防和军队现代化打下坚实基础。 一 我们党在领导人民军队进行革命、建设和改革的伟大实践中，积累了许多宝贵经验，形成了一整套优良传统。这些优良传统源自官兵创造，根植基层实践，是人民军队从弱小走向强大、从胜利走向胜利的制胜密码。 基层是优良传统的孕育发源地。基层一线是我军精神血脉生生不息的源头根基，也是优良传统发轫的丰厚沃土。党对军队绝对领导的根本原则和制度，发端于南昌起义，奠基于三湾改编，定型于古田会议；“三大纪律、八项注意”的严明纪律，缘起于湘赣边界秋收起义部队向井冈山进军途中的纪律整顿实践；一不怕苦、二不怕死的战斗精神，体现于一线官兵的每一次冲锋陷阵、每一次浴血奋战……历史和实践证明，艰苦奋斗、牺牲奉献、团结友爱、官兵一致等优良传统，都是在基层一线的实践中逐步积淀形成的。 基层是优良传统的传承主阵地。基层是部队全部工作和战斗力的基础，一代代基层官兵就是优良传统最坚定的践行者、最忠实的传承人。从抗日战争中全心全意为人民服务的张思德，到解放战争中舍身炸暗堡的董存瑞，再到抗美援朝战争中以胸膛堵枪眼的黄继光、烈火焚身岿然不动的邱少云……像他们一样的无数革命先烈以热血铸忠诚、以生命担使命，把光荣传统彰显于革命工作中、弘扬在阵地上。和平建设时期，雷锋坚守为民初心、苏宁献身国防现代化、李向群冲锋抗洪一线、张超逐梦海天强军……像他们一样的无数英雄模范扎根基层、深耕基层，推动我军优良传统代代赓续、绵延不息。 二 赓续优良传统，就要把红色基因植入官兵灵魂，把党领导我军在长期实践中形成的政治优势和组织优势充分发挥出来，为全面锻造“三个过硬”基层提供坚实支撑。 让党指挥枪的根本原则和制度在基层牢牢扎根。党对军队的绝对领导是人民军队的建军之本、强军之魂。要充分发挥政治建军特有优势，紧紧扭住党组织这个关键，强化基层党组织政治功能和组织功能，严格落实各项组织生活制度，严格党员教育管理监督，加强基层党组织书记队伍建设，充分发挥基层党组织战斗堡垒作用和广大党员先锋模范作用，确保党对军队的绝对领导直达基层、直达官兵。 让崇高的理想、坚定的信念在基层牢牢扎根。崇高的理想，坚定的信念，是人民军队的精神支柱，是基层官兵攻坚克难、拒腐防变、履职尽责的强大精神力量。要始终用共产主义远大理想引领基层建设方向，用中国特色社会主义共同理想凝聚基层官兵奋斗共识，着力培养有灵魂、有本事、有血性、有品德的新时代革命军人，引导基层官兵自觉将个人抱负融入强军兴军伟大实践，在平凡岗位上坚守初心、矢志奋斗，始终保持革命军人政治本色。 让与时俱进的改革创新品格在基层牢牢扎根。基层蕴藏着无穷智慧与鲜活力量，是改革创新的源头活水。要坚持基层至上、士兵第一，尊重官兵主体地位和首创精神，鼓励基层单位立足战位、紧贴实际突破革新，让创新思维融入基层建设各方面全过程，积极吸纳基层创新创造的鲜活经验，推广基层务实管用举措，以蓬勃的创新活力加快推进国防和军队现代化建设。 让英勇无畏的过硬战斗精神在基层牢牢扎根。战斗精神，是军队战斗力的重要因素，是军事实践中激发人的主观能动性、发挥人的决定性作用的关键。要坚持把战斗精神培育融入基层部队军事训练、战备执勤、执行重大任务等全过程，从难从严摔打锤炼部队，磨砺官兵血性胆气，锤炼官兵不畏强敌、敢打必胜的意志品质，把官兵带得很有血性，把部队带得虎虎生威。 让令行禁止的严明革命纪律在基层牢牢扎根。军队是最讲纪律的，纪律严明是战斗力的重要源泉。战争年代我们打胜仗靠纪律，和平时期加强部队建设同样离不开铁的纪律。要牢固树立纪律意识，坚持依纲依规抓实基层建设，严格落实条令条例规定，督促官兵言行举止严守纪律准则、符合制度规范；强化监督执纪，抓早抓小，防微杜渐，以刚性执纪确保纪律权威在基层落地生根。 让全心全意为人民服务的根本宗旨在基层牢牢扎根。来自人民、为了人民，始终与人民血肉相连、生死与共，是我们的制胜之本、力量之源。要始终站稳人民立场，牢固树立人民至上理念；聚焦练兵备战主责主业，以能打仗、打胜仗守护人民安宁；在灾害险情面前闻令而动、挺身而出，誓死守护人民群众生命财产安全；支援地方经济建设和生态环境保护，积极开展扶贫帮困等工作。 三 推动优良传统在基层赓续传承，是一项系统工程、长期任务。必须把赓续优良传统与加强基层建设紧密结合，综合施策、久久为功，真正让优良传统浸润官兵心田、指引官兵言行，推动基层建设高质量发展。 加强理论武装，在固本培元中筑牢思想根基。思想是行动的先导，理论是实践的指南。要持续深化党的创新理论武装，组织基层官兵系统学习我党我军光荣历史和优良传统，解决好为谁扛枪、为谁打仗、当兵干什么、练兵为什么等根本性问题；充分挖掘驻地和本部队红色资源，用好鲜活教育载体，创新学习形式，用基层官兵喜闻乐见的方式阐释理论、解读传统。 立足实践砥砺，在练兵备战中立牢胜战指向。练兵备战是部队主责主业，也是赓续优良传统的主要实践路径。基层部队处于练兵备战第一线，要把赓续优良传统与练兵备战实践紧密结合，砥砺听党指挥、牺牲奉献、敢打必胜等优秀品格，引导基层官兵在遂行任务中践行优良传统，在攻坚克难中彰显优良传统的强大力量。 坚持严管厚爱，在日常工作中涵养纯正底色。赓续优良传统，重在日常养成、贵在长期坚持。严管方能正风塑形、砥砺作风，厚爱可以凝聚人心、激发士气，二者辩证统一、相得益彰。要坚定不移推进全面从严治党、全面从严治军，抓实基层风气建设，持续纠治各类倾向性问题，持续纯正基层政治生态；坚持关心基层、关爱官兵，扑下身子解决好官兵急难愁盼问题，在纾难解困中不断提振官兵干事创业精神状态。 注重典型激励，在榜样带动中催生内生动力。先进典型的事迹，体现官兵的奋斗精神，具体而鲜活，具有感染力；先进典型的品质，反映人民军队的价值追求，崇高而质朴，富有号召力。要注重在练兵备战、抢险救灾、科研攻关等急难险重任务中培树先进典型，通过广泛宣传、深入学习其奋斗事迹，引导基层官兵崇尚先进、争当先锋，将榜样力量切实转化为精武强能的昂扬斗志和履职尽责的使命担当。 （作者单位：国防大学习近平新时代中国特色社会主义思想研究中心）
