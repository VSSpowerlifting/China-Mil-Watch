About this analysis
Summary
Three researchers from the PLA Rocket Force Center for the Study and Research of the Party's Innovative Theory published a doctrinal article in military media codifying 'comprehensively strict governance of the Party' as one of the 'Fourteen Upholds' of Xi Jinping's Thought on Party Building, with explicit application to the armed forces under the rubrics of political rectification, system-building, and grassroots enforcement. The article's Rocket Force authorship and its emphasis on purging 'accumulated abuses,' answering foundational questions of loyalty, and driving rectification to the grassroots documents the ongoing institutional response to the corruption crisis that gutted the Rocket Force senior leadership in 2023–2024. This is standard political-work doctrine content, but its value is as a baseline record of how the Rocket Force's own theory apparatus is framing the post-purge rectification campaign to the force's officer corps.

Uphold Comprehensively Strict Governance of the Party

■ Liu Wenyu, Wang Shengnan, Li Yifei

Editor's Note: Governing the country requires first governing the Party; governing the Party demands strictness without exception. "Upholding comprehensively strict governance of the Party (坚持全面从严治党)" is one of the substantive elements of the "Fourteen Upholds" within Xi Jinping's Thought on Party Building. It clearly signals that during this critical period of laying the foundation for and fully advancing the basic realization of socialist modernization, the work of Party building must be advanced as a great project without a moment's relaxation. In the centenary effort to forge a strong military and the drive to build a first-class military, the entire armed forces must consciously apply Xi Jinping's Thought on Party Building to plan and advance Party building in the military, and with higher standards and more substantive measures, carry out all tasks related to comprehensively strict governance of the Party and comprehensively strict governance of the military, continuously consolidating the purity and glory of the People's Army.

At the rally celebrating the 105th anniversary of the founding of the Communist Party of China, Chairman Xi profoundly stated: "To maintain firm confidence and carry forward the struggle, we must persistently advance comprehensively strict governance of the Party." History and practice have fully demonstrated that our Party has been able to overcome one difficulty after another and achieve one victory after another, and the key lies in consistently upholding the principle that the Party must govern itself and never relaxing comprehensively strict governance of the Party. At present, the world situation, national conditions, Party conditions, and military conditions are all undergoing complex and profound changes; the "four great tests" and "four dangers" facing our Party will exist for a long time to come, and some problems affecting the Party's advanced nature and weakening the Party's purity have not been fundamentally resolved. We must advance comprehensively strict governance of the Party and comprehensively strict governance of the military with the tenacity and perseverance of a journey that is never complete, sustain over the long term the keynote of strictness, the measures of strictness, and the atmosphere of strictness, comprehensively raise the quality of Party leadership and Party building work in our military, and provide a strong political guarantee for achieving the centenary goal of army building on schedule and accelerating the building of the People's Army into a world-class military.

I

Looking back on the journey, our Party has continuously and deeply advanced comprehensively strict governance of the Party with a high degree of historical initiative, powerfully demonstrating the great resolve of the Party to permanently preserve its advanced nature and purity through the renewal and transformation of the old, and manifesting the high degree of self-awareness in leading the great social revolution through the great self-revolution.

The great practice of breaking out of the historical cycle of order and chaos, rise and fall. The question of breaking out of the historical cycle is a matter of life and death for the Party and bears on the rise and fall of our country's socialist system. More than 80 years ago, faced with Huang Yanpei's "question of the historical cycle (历史周期率之问)," Comrade Mao Zedong gave the first answer: "Let the people supervise the government." Entering the new era, the Party Central Committee with Comrade Xi Jinping at its core gave the second answer in the great practice of comprehensively strict governance of the Party: self-revolution. Comprehensively strict governance of the Party is the great practice of the Party's self-revolution in the new era, opening up a new realm of self-revolution for a century-old great Party. In the practice of governing the Party in the new era, our Party has carried forward the spirit of thorough self-revolution, resolutely eliminating all factors that damage the Party's advanced nature and purity and all viruses that erode the Party's healthy organism, becoming stronger and more powerful through revolutionary tempering and forging.

The method and path for resolving the unique difficulties of a great Party. At present, our Party has developed into the world's largest governing party with major global influence. While it has accumulated rich experience over more than a century of struggle, it also clearly recognizes the unique difficulties of a great Party under conditions of long-term governance, encapsulated in the "six questions of how to always" (六个如何始终). Since the new era, comprehensively strict governance of the Party has addressed the root causes with long-term effectiveness through the requirement of "comprehensiveness" — with full coverage of content, full coverage of subjects, a full chain of responsibility, and full integration of systems — comprehensively strengthening Party building and continuously improving the Party's level of leadership and capacity for long-term governance; it has precisely tackled problems with the keynote of "strictness," establishing effective mechanisms for identifying problems and correcting deviations, and pinpointing the key focus for resolving the unique difficulties of a great Party; it has solidified responsibility through the concept of "governance," continuously improving the system of responsibility for governing the Party, and continuously driving comprehensively strict governance of the Party to greater depth, ensuring the Party permanently preserves its advanced nature and purity, and providing a strong guarantee for the Party to always win the people's support and navigate the new journey of examination.

The key to driving the new great project of Party building in the new era to develop in depth. Since the 18th National Congress of the Party, the Party Central Committee with Comrade Xi Jinping at its core has incorporated comprehensively strict governance of the Party into the "Four-Pronged Comprehensive Strategy," turned the blade inward, scraped the bone to cure the poison, applied strong medicine to eliminate ailments, and used severe laws to bring order out of chaos. Comprehensively strict governance of the Party has achieved historic and pioneering accomplishments, produced all-round and deep-level influence, and eliminated serious hidden dangers within the Party, the state, and the military. Armies win through discipline; discipline requires strictness above all. Our military is a people's army under the absolute leadership of the Party; doing Party building in the military well is an important component of the new great project of Party building. The People's Army upholds comprehensively strict governance of the Party to advance comprehensively strict governance of the military, with strictness foremost and strictness maintained throughout, purifying the political ecosystem, guarding against major risks, and firmly holding fast to the root and soul through tempering, forging, and the reform of abuses.

II

Comprehensively strict governance of the Party is an important component of Xi Jinping's Thought on Party Building; its rich theoretical innovations and practical achievements compellingly confirm that Xi Jinping's Thought on Party Building carries original contributions and powerful truth-force.

Comprehensively strict governance of the Party has been continuously enriched and developed through theoretical and practical exploration. In December 2014, Chairman Xi first put forward "comprehensively strict governance of the Party" during an inspection in Jiangsu, and it has since been continuously enriched and developed in practice. The Sixth Plenary Session of the 18th Central Committee of the Party devoted special study to comprehensively strict governance of the Party; the 19th National Congress of the Party established "upholding comprehensively strict governance of the Party" as one of the basic strategies for upholding and developing socialism with Chinese characteristics in the new era, and made specific deployments across eight aspects; the 20th National Congress of the Party was the first to put forward "improving the system of comprehensively strict governance of the Party (健全全面从严治党体系)"; the National Conference on Organizational Work held in June 2023, in elaborating Chairman Xi's important thought on Party building, explicitly put forward "highlighting comprehensively strict governance of the Party as the main theme and main thread (突出全面从严治党这个主题主线)"; in June of this year, the National Symposium on Party Building Work explicitly incorporated "upholding comprehensively strict governance of the Party" into the substantive elements of the "Fourteen Upholds" of Xi Jinping's Thought on Party Building. The continuous enrichment and development of comprehensively strict governance of the Party through theoretical and practical exploration marks the continuous deepening of our Party's understanding of the laws governing Party governance, and profoundly embodies the innovative quality of Xi Jinping's Thought on Party Building in keeping pace with the times.

Comprehensively strict governance of the Party transforms the Party's political advantages into advantages for winning. The core of comprehensively strict governance of the Party is strengthening Party leadership. In response to the situation that existed for a period of time within the Party — including the weakening, hollowing out, and dilution of implementation of Party leadership, and the lax and soft governance of the Party — the Party Central Committee with Comrade Xi Jinping at its core adhered to a problem-oriented approach, unswervingly advanced comprehensively strict governance of the Party, strengthened all aspects of Party building under the leadership of the Party's political construction, and through institutionalized, normalized, and long-term governance arrangements, drove an overall improvement in the level of Party governance, continuously enhancing the Party's political leadership, ideological guidance, capacity to organize the masses, and social mobilization power, successfully forging a new path for governing the Party, invigorating the Party, and strengthening the Party. Comprehensively strict governance of the Party transforms the Party's political advantages into advantages for winning, vividly embodying the strong problem orientation and powerful truth-force of Xi Jinping's Thought on Party Building.

Comprehensively strict governance of the Party carries the value orientation of "for whom is the Party governed." The aspirations of the people are the direction of governance. When the Central Eight-Point Regulation was first formulated, Chairman Xi emphasized: "If we are a little less comfortable and a little less at ease, the people will be a little more comfortable, a little more satisfied, and will feel a little better about us." This plain yet profound statement vividly interprets the deep people-centered sentiment embedded in comprehensively strict governance of the Party. The country is the people, and the people are the country. By resolutely rectifying corruption and misconduct around the masses and working hard to resolve the interest problems that the people care about most, most directly, and most realistically, our Party has truly won the wholehearted support of the people. Comprehensively strict governance of the Party upholds the fundamental position of putting the people first, ensuring the Party always stands firmly with the people, thinks together with the people, and works together with the people, fully manifesting the distinctive character of Xi Jinping's Thought on Party Building — that everything is for the people and everything relies on the people.

III

As an armed group that executes the Party's political tasks, the military must maintain higher standards and stricter requirements in continuously advancing comprehensively strict governance of the Party. On the new journey, facing major tests of high winds and rough seas or even violent storms, we must deeply study and implement Xi Jinping's Thought on Party Building, uphold comprehensively strict governance of the Party and comprehensively strict governance of the military, implement the requirements of genuine management and genuine strictness, bold management and bold strictness, and sustained management and sustained strictness throughout the entire process and all domains of army building and military governance, and ensure the forces are absolutely loyal, absolutely pure, and absolutely reliable.

Deeply advance political rectification (政治整训) in the new era. Political rectification is a powerful weapon and vivid practice for the military to carry out comprehensively strict governance of the Party. To deeply advance political rectification, we must deepen ideological transformation, guiding the broad ranks of officers and soldiers to answer well the foundational questions of "why join the Party, why serve as a soldier, why serve as a commander," and purging the poisonous influence and accumulated abuses at the ideological root. We must make rectification and remediation substantive, comprehensively uncovering accumulated problems and abuses, eliminating the soil and conditions for the growth of corruption, and driving a fundamental improvement in the political ecosystem. We must improve systems and mechanisms, refining and codifying the good experiences and practices accumulated during political rectification into institutional regulations, and driving political rectification to be conducted in a normalized, institutionalized, and long-term manner, ensuring the permanent continuation of our military's bloodline, the permanent solidity of its foundation, and the permanent preservation of its advantages.

Improve the system of comprehensively strict governance of the Party. Wherever the cause of the Party and the state extends, Party building must cover; wherever Party building advances, the system of comprehensively strict governance of the Party must be constructed. Further improving a system of comprehensively strict governance of the Party that is complete in its elements, fully functional, scientifically standardized, and operationally efficient is an effective path for continuously advancing the new great project of Party building in the new era. We must adhere to addressing both symptoms and root causes and comprehensive governance, conducting comprehensively strict governance of the Party and comprehensively strict governance of the military in a manner that is holistic rather than partial, systematic rather than piecemeal, sustained rather than temporary, and high-standard rather than perfunctory, responding to new situations and new problems emerging in Party governance, and continuously raising the level of institutionalization, standardization, and scientization of Party governance.

Drive comprehensively strict governance of the Party to extend to the grassroots level. The grassroots is the "last mile" of comprehensively strict governance of the Party; the requirements of comprehensively strict governance of the Party must be implemented all the way through. We must strengthen grassroots organizations, building them in accordance with law and regulations, firmly grasping the tasks of helping build Party branches, helping develop cadres, and helping cultivate backbone personnel with practical measures and seeking practical results, forging every grassroots Party organization into a strong combat fortress. We must transmit pressure and momentum downward, tightening and solidifying the responsibility for comprehensively strict governance of the Party and comprehensively strict governance of the military, further improving accountability mechanisms, ensuring that responsibility is clearly delineated, supervision and inspection are strict, and accountability is carried out in place, making it the norm that dereliction of responsibility is invariably questioned and accountability is invariably strict. We must purify the atmosphere at the grassroots level, firmly establishing the clear orientation of making great efforts to build a sound atmosphere at the grassroots level, regularly analyzing the situation of Party conduct and clean governance, strengthening the ranks of atmosphere supervisors (风气监督员), and continuously driving the building of a positive atmosphere at the grassroots level upward and in a better direction.

(Authors' unit: Rocket Force Center for the Study and Research of the Party's Innovative Theory)

坚持全面从严治党 ■刘文煜 王胜男 李一飞 编者按 治国必先治党，治党务必从严。“坚持全面从严治党”是习近平党建思想“十四个坚持”的内涵要义之一，鲜明昭示出在基本实现社会主义现代化夯实基础、全面发力的关键时期，仍要须臾不可松懈地把党的建设作为一项伟大工程来推进。攻坚建军百年、奋进强军一流，全军必须自觉运用习近平党建思想谋划和推进军队党的建设，以更高标准、更实举措抓好全面从严治党、全面从严治军各项工作，不断巩固人民军队纯洁光荣。 在庆祝中国共产党成立105周年大会上，习主席深刻指出：“坚定信心、接续奋斗，必须持之以恒推进全面从严治党。”历史和实践充分证明，我们党能够战胜一个又一个困难，取得一个又一个胜利，关键在于始终坚持党要管党、全面从严治党不放松。当前，世情、国情、党情、军情都在发生复杂深刻变化，我们党面临的“四大考验”“四种危险”将长期存在，一些影响党的先进性、弱化党的纯洁性的问题还没有根本解决。必须以永远在路上的坚韧和执着推进全面从严治党、全面从严治军，把严的基调、严的措施、严的氛围长期坚持下去，全面提高我军党的领导和党的建设工作质量，为如期实现建军一百年奋斗目标、加快把人民军队建成世界一流军队提供坚强政治保证。 一 回望征程，我们党以高度的历史主动持续深入推进全面从严治党，有力诠释了党在革故鼎新中永葆先进性和纯洁性的强大定力，昭示了以伟大自我革命引领伟大社会革命的高度自觉。 跳出治乱兴衰历史周期率的伟大实践。跳出历史周期率问题关系党的生死存亡，关系我国社会主义制度的兴衰成败。80多年前，面对黄炎培提出的“历史周期率之问”，毛泽东同志给出了第一个答案：“让人民来监督政府”。进入新时代，以习近平同志为核心的党中央在全面从严治党伟大实践中给出了第二个答案，这就是自我革命。全面从严治党是新时代党的自我革命的伟大实践，开辟了百年大党自我革命的新境界。在新时代管党治党的实践中，我们党发扬彻底的自我革命精神，坚决清除一切损害党的先进性和纯洁性的因素、清除一切侵蚀党的健康肌体的病毒，在革命性锻造中更加坚强有力。 解决大党独有难题的方法路径。当前，我们党已经发展成为具有重大全球影响力的世界第一大执政党，既在百余年奋斗中积累了丰富经验，也清醒认识到长期执政条件下面临“六个如何始终”的大党独有难题。新时代以来，全面从严治党以“全”的要求长效治本，通过内容全涵盖、对象全覆盖、责任全链条、制度全贯通，全面加强党的建设，不断提高党的领导水平和长期执政能力；以“严”的基调精准破题，建立起发现问题、纠正偏差的有效机制，找准了解决大党独有难题的着力重点；以“治”的理念压实责任，不断健全管党治党责任体系，将全面从严治党不断引向深入，确保党永葆先进性和纯洁性，为党始终赢得人民拥护、走好新的赶考之路提供坚强保证。 推动新时代党的建设新的伟大工程向纵深发展的关键所在。党的十八大以来，以习近平同志为核心的党中央把全面从严治党纳入“四个全面”战略布局，刀刃向内、刮骨疗毒，猛药祛疴、重典治乱，全面从严治党取得了历史性、开创性成就，产生了全方位、深层次影响，消除了党、国家、军队内部存在的严重隐患。兵以治为胜，治以严为要。我军是党绝对领导下的人民军队，搞好军队党的建设，是党的建设新的伟大工程的重要组成部分。人民军队坚持以全面从严治党推进全面从严治军，严字当头、一严到底，纯正政治生态，防范重大风险，在淬炼锻造、革弊鼎新中牢牢守住了根和魂。 二 全面从严治党，是习近平党建思想的重要组成部分，其丰富的理论创新与实践成果，雄辩印证了习近平党建思想具有原创性贡献与强大真理力量。 全面从严治党在理论和实践探索中不断丰富发展。2014年12月，习主席在江苏考察时首次提出“全面从严治党”，此后在实践中不断丰富发展。党的十八届六中全会专题研究全面从严治党；党的十九大将“坚持全面从严治党”确立为新时代坚持和发展中国特色社会主义的基本方略之一，并从8个方面作出具体部署；党的二十大首次提出“健全全面从严治党体系”；2023年6月召开的全国组织工作会议在阐述习主席关于党的建设的重要思想时，明确提出“突出全面从严治党这个主题主线”；今年6月，全国党建工作座谈会明确将“坚持全面从严治党”纳入习近平党建思想“十四个坚持”的内涵要义。全面从严治党在理论和实践探索中不断丰富发展，标志着我们党对管党治党的规律性认识不断深化，深刻体现了习近平党建思想与时俱进的创新品质。 全面从严治党将党的政治优势转化为制胜优势。全面从严治党，核心是加强党的领导。针对一段时间党内存在的不少落实党的领导弱化、虚化、淡化和管党治党宽松软等情况，以习近平同志为核心的党中央坚持问题导向，坚定不移推进全面从严治党，以党的政治建设为统领加强党的各方面建设，以制度化、常态化、长效化的治理安排，推动管党治党水平整体提升，不断增强党的政治领导力、思想引领力、群众组织力、社会号召力，成功走出了一条管党治党、兴党强党的新路。全面从严治党将党的政治优势转化为制胜优势，生动体现了习近平党建思想强烈的问题导向和强大的真理力量。 全面从严治党承载“治党为了谁”的价值取向。民之所望，政之所向。中央八项规定制定之初，习主席就强调，“我们不舒服一点、不自在一点，老百姓的舒适度就好一点、满意度就高一点，对我们的感觉就好一点”。这一质朴而深刻的论述，生动诠释了全面从严治党蕴含的深厚人民情怀。江山就是人民，人民就是江山。通过坚决整治群众身边的腐败和不正之风，着力解决人民群众最关心、最直接、最现实的利益问题，使我们党真正赢得了人民群众的衷心拥护。全面从严治党秉持人民至上的根本立场，确保党始终坚定地同人民站在一起、想在一起、干在一起，充分彰显了习近平党建思想一切为了人民、一切依靠人民的鲜明底色。 三 作为执行党的政治任务的武装集团，持续推进全面从严治党，军队必须标准更高、要求更严。新征程上，面对风高浪急甚至惊涛骇浪的重大考验，必须深入学习贯彻习近平党建思想，坚持全面从严治党、全面从严治军，把真管真严、敢管敢严、长管长严的要求落实到建军治军全过程和各领域，确保部队绝对忠诚、绝对纯洁、绝对可靠。 深入推进新时代政治整训。政治整训是军队开展全面从严治党的有力武器与生动实践。深入推进政治整训，必须深化思想改造，引导广大官兵回答好“入党干什么、当兵干什么、为将干什么”的初心之问，从思想根子上肃清流毒积弊。抓实整改整治，全面起底问题积弊，铲除腐败滋生的土壤和条件，推动政治生态根本好转。完善制度机制，把政治整训中积累的好经验好做法提炼固化为制度规定，推动政治整训常态化开展、制度化推进、长效化落实，确保我军血脉永续、根基永固、优势永存。 健全全面从严治党体系。党和国家事业拓展到哪里，党的建设就要覆盖到哪里；党的建设推进到哪里，全面从严治党体系就要构建到哪里。进一步健全要素齐全、功能完备、科学规范、运行高效的全面从严治党体系，是不断把新时代党的建设新的伟大工程推向前进的有效途径。要坚持标本兼治、综合治理，做到整体地而不是局部地、系统地而不是零碎地、持久地而不是短暂地、高标准地而不是一般化地全面从严治党、全面从严治军，回应管党治党出现的新情况新问题，不断提升管党治党制度化、规范化、科学化水平。 推动全面从严治党向基层延伸。基层是全面从严治党的“最后一公里”，必须把全面从严治党要求一贯到底。要强固基层组织，依法按纲抓建，扭住帮建支部、帮带干部、帮抓骨干出实策、求实效，把每个基层党组织都锻造成坚强战斗堡垒。传导压力压势，压紧压实全面从严治党、全面从严治军责任，进一步健全问责机制，做到责任分解明确、监督检查严格、责任追究到位，让失责必问、问责必严成为常态。纯正基层风气，牢固树立大抓基层风气建设的鲜明导向，定期分析党风廉政建设形势，建强风气监督员队伍，持续推动基层风气建设向上向好。
