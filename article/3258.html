Military-Civilian Coordination Opens an Aerial Lifeline

军地协作，开辟空中生命通道
PLA Daily (解放军报) 15 August 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
An Eastern Theater Command Air Force unit ran a military-civilian joint emergency rescue exercise simulating a highway mass-casualty incident, deploying two search-and-rescue helicopters with militia medical teams drawn from a local 人武部 (people's armed forces department) to conduct combined landing and hover-and-hoist extractions before transferring casualties to a garrison hospital's prepared landing site. The exercise documents the command link between the combat readiness duty system and civilian agencies — traffic police, fire services, and hospitals — as the specific procedural seam this type of drill is designed to clear. The framing around 军地融合 (military-civilian integration) and the 空中急救走廊 (aerial emergency corridor) points to an institutional effort to routinize these handoff procedures, though the article does not identify the specific Air Force unit, helicopter type, or location.
Translation

In the height of summer, a military-civilian joint rescue exercise organized and led by a unit of the Eastern Theater Command Air Force got fully underway. The exercise simulated a major traffic accident on a highway in a given locality. Garrison forces responded rapidly, conducting a military-civilian joint emergency rescue operation together with local departments including traffic police, fire services, and hospitals, linking the action chain of "response—rescue—evacuation."

After the emergency occurred, roads were severely blocked, ground rescue vehicles could not reach the scene directly, several civilians were trapped inside vehicles, and some critically injured patients were in life-threatening condition. "Requesting air support" — at this critical moment, the military-civilian joint response was immediately activated. Military and civilian sides established a command link through the combat readiness duty system, rapidly exchanged situational information, clarified the division of rescue responsibilities, and launched response operations. An emergency is a combat situation. A unit of the Air Force immediately organized two search-and-rescue helicopters to move into position; a people's armed forces department (人武部) swiftly mobilized militia rescue forces, who boarded the aircraft with professional medical equipment; and the aerial rescue force flew directly to the accident scene. Simultaneously, a garrison hospital organized personnel to clear and secure the hospital's landing site and made ready to receive casualties.

At the rescue scene, emergency and fire personnel cut open the accident vehicles and successively rescued the trapped civilians, while highway traffic police simultaneously carried out traffic control and road clearance to open up the rescue area. When the helicopters arrived over the scene, the search-and-rescue aircraft commander used the advantage of flexible, low-altitude maneuverability to rapidly assess the on-scene situation. After coordinating with personnel on the ground, he decided to conduct the rescue using a combination of "helicopter landing rescue and hover-and-hoist rescue." In a relatively open roadbed area, one helicopter landed precisely; the militia rescue force rapidly disembarked, quickly assessed injuries, applied hemostasis and immobilization, and administered initial treatment to the wounded. At another location, because the position where a critically injured casualty was trapped was too narrow to permit a landing, the search-and-rescue aircraft commander flexibly established an approach route, maintained a stable hover above the casualty, opened the cabin door, and the aerial rescue swimmer descended with a rescue litter and landed steadily, immediately securing the casualty. Hoist! At the aerial rescue swimmer's signal, the winch operator operated the winch to retract the cable, and the casualty was brought safely into the cabin.

Treatment of the casualties brooked no delay. The two-aircraft formation flew to the local hospital. The helicopters opened a landing approach route between the buildings, landed safely at a temporary landing site, and successfully completed the evacuation of the casualties. Medical personnel rapidly moved forward, seamlessly taking over the handoff of casualties, conducting re-assessment of injuries, and administering emergency treatment before immediately transferring them to the emergency department for care — efficiently opening the "aerial emergency corridor (空中急救走廊)."

In this exercise, all military and civilian parties worked in full coordination, effectively clearing the command link and response procedures. Going forward, military-civilian integration (军地融合) will continue to be deepened, laying a solid foundation for safeguarding the lives and property of the people and consolidating the local emergency safety barrier.

Original Chinese
盛夏时节，东部战区空军某部牵头组织的一场军地联合救援演练全面展开。演练模拟某地高速公路突发重大交通事故，驻地部队迅速响应，联合交警、消防、医院等地方部门开展军地联合应急救援，贯通了“响应—救援—后送”的行动链条。 险情发生后，道路严重受阻、地面救援车辆无法直达现场，数名群众被困车内，部分重伤患者生命垂危。“请求空中支援”，危急时刻，立即启动军地联合响应，军地依托战备值班系统建立指挥链路，快速互通情况，明确救援分工，展开处置工作。险情即是战情。空军某部立即组织2架搜救直升机转进，某人武部迅即动员民兵救援力量、携带专业救治设备登机，空中救援力量直飞事故现场。与此同时，驻地某医院组织人员对医院起降点位进行清理警戒，做好接运准备。 救援现场，应急、消防人员破拆事故车辆，陆续救出被困群众，高速交警同步开展交通管制、路面清障，开辟救援场地。直升机抵达现场上空，搜救机长利用灵活机动的低空性能优势，快速评估现场态势，经与地面现场人员协同，决心采取“机降营救+悬停吊救”方式实施救援。在相对开阔的路基区域，1架直升机精准降落，民兵救援力量迅速离机，快速进行伤情判定、止血固定，对伤员进行初步救治。另一处，由于重伤员被困位置狭窄，无法实施机降，搜救机长灵活建立航线，在伤员上空稳定悬停，机舱舱门打开，空中救生员携带救生担架稳定落地，迅即实施绑定。起吊！在空中救生员的示意下，绞车手操纵绞车收回钢索，伤员稳定入舱！ 伤员救治刻不容缓，双机编队飞抵地方医院。直升机在楼宇间开辟起降航线，安全降落临时起降点，顺利完成伤员后送。医护人员迅速前出，无缝衔接开展伤员交接、伤情复检及急救处置，立即送至急诊救治，高效打通“空中急救走廊”。 此次演练，军地各方通力协作，有效畅通了指挥链路和处置流程，下一步将持续深化军地融合，为守护人民群众生命财产安全、筑牢地方应急安全屏障夯实基础。
