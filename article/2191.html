Major Release! Rocket Force Original Music Video "Dongfeng, Dongfeng"

重磅推出！火箭军原创MV《东风 东风》
PLA Daily (解放军报) 7 July 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
The PLA Rocket Force released an original music video titled 'Dongfeng, Dongfeng,' a produced propaganda piece centered on the DF missile series that explicitly invokes nuclear-conventional integration and 'full-domain deterrence and warfighting' (全域慑战) as core institutional identity markers. The lyrics embed operationally significant doctrinal language—'nuclear and conventional capabilities combined, ready for use at any time'—into a mass-audience format, which documents how the Rocket Force is actively normalizing dual-capability readiness as a public-facing posture rather than treating it as a classified or sensitive framing. This fits the post-2023 Rocket Force reorganization pattern of aggressive institutional rebranding following the leadership purges, using cultural products to reassert the force's cohesion and deterrence credibility to both domestic and external audiences.
Translation

In days of peace, we embrace silence. The moment we appear, we are thunder. Though we speak not a single word, we make the world open its eyes wide. Because our name is Dongfeng, Dongfeng.

In the vast deserts and deep mountains I stand ready; across ten thousand miles of open sky I soar. At a single order, wind and thunder are set in motion; mountains and rivers remain unharmed, Dongfeng arcs like a rainbow.

Beneath the banner of a strong military (强军), we forge loyalty. On the journey of tempering the sword (砺剑), we lead the vanguard. Nuclear and conventional capabilities combined, ready for use at any time; full-domain deterrence and warfighting (全域慑战) shakes the heavens. Because our name is Dongfeng, Dongfeng.

In the vast deserts and deep mountains I stand ready; across ten thousand miles of open sky I soar. At a single order, wind and thunder are set in motion; mountains and rivers remain unharmed, Dongfeng arcs like a rainbow!

The piece "Dongfeng, Dongfeng," through its profound and weighty ideological content and mature, full artistic expression, amid the cadence of refined lyrics and bold melody, manifests the sincere devotion and hard-core commitment (硬核担当) of Rocket Force officers and soldiers.
