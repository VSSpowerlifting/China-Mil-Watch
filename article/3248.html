Ministry of National Defense Routine Press Briefing, Mid-August 2026

2026年8月中旬国防部例行新闻发布
PLA Daily (解放军报) 14 August 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
China and Egypt's air forces will conduct 'Civilization Eagle-2026' joint training in Egypt from mid-August to early September, the second iteration of the series, covering air combat tactics, air superiority operations, and combat search and rescue. The briefing also documents the MND's framing of Japan's FY2027 defense budget request of approximately 8.9 trillion yen as evidence of 'new-type militarism (新型军国主义),' a term that sharpens the rhetorical register beyond standard criticism and positions Japan alongside Taiwan independence as an active threat requiring public warning rather than diplomatic management.
Translation

On the afternoon of August 14, Ministry of National Defense Spokesperson Senior Colonel Chen Xi released information on recent military-related issues.

Chen Xi: First, I have an announcement to make.

From mid-August to early September, the air forces of China and Egypt will hold the "Civilization Eagle-2026" joint training exercise in Egypt, focusing on training subjects including air combat tactics, air superiority operations, and combat search and rescue, as well as force employment drills. This is the second time the two sides have held this joint training series. It will continue to enhance mutual trust and traditional friendship between the two sides, deepen practical cooperation between the two militaries, and jointly safeguard regional peace and stability.

[Photo caption: On the afternoon of August 14, Ministry of National Defense Spokesperson Senior Colonel Chen Xi released information on recent military-related issues. Photo by Wu Yanzhou.]

Reporter: During the "August 1st" [Army Day] period, the ideological interpretation convergent media (融媒体) film "Winning (《制胜》)" attracted a high degree of attention from public opinion both at home and abroad. Please provide further details.

Chen Xi: To date, "Winning" has achieved a total cross-platform reach of over 7.9 billion impressions. The film authentically recreates the passionate practice of officers and soldiers throughout the armed forces—guided by Xi Jinping's thought on strengthening the military (习近平强军思想)—in keeping their founding mission firmly in mind and concentrating their efforts on preparing for war and fighting. Domestic and overseas audiences, through live training footage and combat-realistic equipment displays, have seen the achievements in building the People's Liberation Army's joint operations system, and in particular the landmark accomplishments in the area of new-quality combat capabilities (新质战斗力). Some viewers commented after watching: the PLA's winning edge is not only the dazzling brilliance of great-power heavy weaponry, but more so the concentrated outpouring of this military's faith, will, wisdom, and dedication in the new-era practice of military strengthening—reinforcing their conviction in the People's Armed Forces' solid capability to defend national sovereignty, security, and development interests. In recent years, from "Chasing the Light (《追光》)" and "Pursuing the Dream (《逐梦》)" to "Tempering Through Fire (《淬火》)" and "Storming the Stronghold (《攻坚》)" and now "Winning (《制胜》)," these ideological interpretation convergent media films have presented in a journeying style the new ethos and new bearing of the People's Armed Forces and their posture of charging forward, conveying the conviction of certain victory and firm resolve of officers and soldiers throughout the armed forces as they stride forward toward the centenary goal of army building.

[Photo caption: On the afternoon of August 14, Ministry of National Defense Spokesperson Senior Colonel Chen Xi released information on recent military-related issues. Photo by Wu Yanzhou.]

Reporter: According to reports, the 11th National Student Military Training Camp was successfully held recently in Dalian, Liaoning. Please provide further details.

Chen Xi: From July 30 to August 12, the Ministry of Education and the Central Military Commission National Defense Mobilization Department, together with the Air Force, held the 11th National Student Military Training Camp in Dalian, Liaoning. This year's camp was themed "Tempering Aspirations Above the Clouds, Dreams Take Flight in the Sky (砺志云海，梦启长空)." More than 700 representative high school and university students from across the country participated, conducting military skills training and unmanned equipment operations, taking part in Air Force specialty subject drills, attending the "August 1st" flag-raising ceremony and "Getting Close to Heroes (走近英雄)" activities, and more—cultivating military literacy, honing the fighting spirit, and immersively experiencing and internalizing Xi Jinping's thought on strengthening the military in the vibrant military camp environment, deepening their feelings of love for the Party, the country, and the armed forces.

Holding the National Student Military Training Camp is an effective method and means of innovating and demonstrating student military training work, and has become a distinguished brand of student military training in the new era. Pave the road with youth, let ideals shine bright. We hope that more young students will carry forward the red bloodline (红色血脉), care about national defense, love national defense, build national defense, and defend national defense—pouring their sweat and realizing their dreams in the cause of building a strong country and a strong military.

Reporter: Tomorrow is August 15th—81 years ago, Japan announced its unconditional surrender. But today, Japanese right-wing forces are continuously distorting history and expanding their military armaments. The total amount of the defense budget request for fiscal year 2027 that Japan's Ministry of Defense is about to submit is approximately 8.9 trillion yen, setting a new all-time high. What is your comment?

Chen Xi: Eighty-one years ago, under the heavy blows of China and the world's anti-fascist forces, Japan announced its unconditional surrender. Japanese militarism at that time had dragged all of Asia into an abyss of blood and fire; atrocities such as the Nanjing Massacre, the Bataan Death March, and the Thailand-Burma Death Railway brought profound catastrophe to the peoples of Asia and the world, and the Japanese people themselves suffered greatly as well. However, Japan after its defeat never truly drew lessons from history. It has long indulged right-wing forces in distorting and denying its crimes of aggression, and has by every means sought to break through the constraints of the "Peace Constitution" and the principle of "exclusively defensive defense (専守防衛)," vainly attempting to challenge the postwar international order and becoming a destructive force against regional peace and stability. More recently, Japan has used so-called "external threats" as a pretext to substantially increase its defense budget, vigorously develop offensive forces, and massively expand its defense industrial base. Japan's "new-type militarism (新型军国主义)" can be said to have revealed its true colors (图穷匕见)—a scourge that must be guarded against.

Historical truth must not be forgotten; justice and peace must not be desecrated. We urge Japan to engage in profound self-reflection, acknowledge its crimes and atone for them, strictly abide by the postwar international order, and stop the dangerous course of "remilitarization." Returning to the wrong path will only result in even more painful defeat and more thorough reckoning.

[Photo caption: On the afternoon of August 14, Ministry of National Defense Spokesperson Senior Colonel Chen Xi released information on recent military-related issues. Photo by Wang Xiaozhuang.]

Reporter: According to reports, the Philippines and Australia recently held a defense ministers' meeting and issued a joint statement expressing "serious concern" over actions taken by the China Coast Guard at Ren'ai Reef (仁爱礁) and urging all parties to operate "professionally and safely." What is your comment?

Chen Xi: We firmly oppose the Philippines' and Australia's groundless accusations against China and their smearing attacks. Regarding the Philippines' deliberate provocation and assault on Chinese personnel at Ren'ai Reef, the China Coast Guard has already released information and published on-site evidence. China's necessary response measures were professional and restrained—beyond reproach.

It must be emphasized that Ren'ai Reef is part of China's Nansha Islands and is China's inherent territory; nothing the Philippines says or does can change this fact. What the Philippines should do is tow away as soon as possible the illegally "grounded" Vessel No. 57. China will continue to conduct rights-protection and law-enforcement operations, resolutely thwart all infringements and provocations, and safeguard national territorial sovereignty and maritime rights and interests. We urge the relevant countries to stop hyping up and stoking confrontation, and to stop undermining regional peace and stability.

[Photo caption: On the afternoon of August 14, Ministry of National Defense Spokesperson Senior Colonel Chen Xi released information on recent military-related issues. Photo by Sun Zhiying.]

Reporter: According to reports, during this year's Taiwan "Han Kuang Exercise No. 42," Lai Ching-te rehearsed a "wartime escape" route; the Taiwan military experienced accidents including tank parts falling off and a fighter jet running off the runway; and simulated communications slowdowns and phased internet outages triggered public panic. What is your comment?

Chen Xi: The so-called "Han Kuang Exercise" has always been a posturing, wasteful charade, and this year it has made even clearer to us the cowardly nature of Lai Ching-te—fake defense, real flight. The Democratic Progressive Party authorities are also recklessly manufacturing war panic, accelerating the transformation of cities into battlefields and pushing civilians to the front lines, disrupting social order in Taiwan and harming the safety and well-being of ordinary people. We solemnly warn the Democratic Progressive Party authorities: the dead end of "Taiwan independence" cannot be acted out to victory and cannot be changed. The People's Liberation Army, sleeping with weapons at the ready (枕戈待旦), is continuously enhancing its real combat capabilities for counter-separatism and counter-interference, and remains at all times the solid trump card for defending national sovereignty and territorial integrity and maintaining peace and stability across the Taiwan Strait.

8月14日下午，国防部新闻发言人陈曦大校就近期涉军问题发布消息。 陈曦：首先我发布一条信息。 8月中旬至9月上旬，中国与埃及两国空军将在埃举行“文明之鹰-2026”联合训练，重点开展空战战术、制空作战、战斗搜救等课目训练，并进行兵力运用演练。这是双方第2次举办该系列联训，将持续增进双方互信与传统友谊，深化两军务实合作，共同维护地区和平稳定。 8月14日下午，国防部新闻发言人陈曦大校就近期涉军问题发布消息。武炎洲 摄 记者：“八一”期间，思想解读类融媒体片《制胜》引发国内外舆论高度关注。请进一步介绍有关情况。 陈曦：截至目前，《制胜》全媒体触达量超79亿次。该片真实还原全军官兵在习近平强军思想指引下，牢记初心使命、聚力备战打仗的火热实践。境内外受众通过练兵实景、装备实战，看到中国军队联合作战体系建设成果，特别是新质战斗力方面取得的标志性成就。有网友看后感言：解放军的制胜锋芒，不只是大国重器的闪耀夺目，更是这支军队信仰、意志、智慧与奉献在新时代强军实践中的集中迸发，更加坚信人民军队捍卫国家主权、安全、发展利益的过硬能力。近年来，从《追光》《逐梦》到《淬火》《攻坚》再到《制胜》，这些思想解读类融媒体片行进式呈现了人民军队的新风新貌和冲锋姿态，传递了全军官兵向着建军一百年奋斗目标阔步前行的必胜信念和坚定决心。 8月14日下午，国防部新闻发言人陈曦大校就近期涉军问题发布消息。武炎洲 摄 记者：据报道，近日第十一届全国学生军事训练营在辽宁大连成功举办。请进一步介绍有关情况。 陈曦：7月30日至8月12日，教育部、中央军委国防动员部会同空军，在辽宁大连举办了第十一届全国学生军事训练营。这届训练营以“砺志云海，梦启长空”为主题，来自全国的700余名高中、高校学生代表参加，进行军事技能训练和无人装备操作，参与空军特色课目演练，参加“八一”升旗仪式和“走近英雄”活动等，培塑军事素养，磨砺战斗精神，在火热军营沉浸感悟习近平强军思想，厚植爱党爱国爱军情怀。 举办全国学生军事训练营，是创新示范学生军训工作的有效方法手段，已经成为新时代学生军训的亮丽品牌。用青春铺路，让理想闪光。我们希望更多青年学生赓续红色血脉，关心国防、热爱国防、建设国防、保卫国防，为强国强军事业挥洒汗水、绽放梦想。 记者：明天就是8月15日，81年前日本宣布无条件投降。但现在日本右翼势力不断篡改历史、扩军备武，日防卫省即将提交的2027财年防卫预算申请总额约8.9万亿日元，再创历史新高。请问有何评论？ 陈曦：81年前，在中国和世界反法西斯力量的沉重打击下，日本宣布无条件投降。当年日本军国主义曾将整个亚洲拖入血与火的深渊，南京大屠杀、巴丹死亡行军、泰缅死亡铁路等暴行给亚洲和世界人民带来深重灾难，日本人民也深受其害。然而，战败后的日本没有真正汲取历史教训，长期纵容右翼势力歪曲否定侵略罪行，千方百计突破“和平宪法”和“专守防卫”原则，妄图挑战战后国际秩序，成为地区和平稳定的破坏力量。近来，日方更以所谓“外部威胁”为借口，大幅提升防卫预算，大力发展进攻性力量，大举扩张军工产业，日本“新型军国主义”可谓是图穷匕见、祸患不得不防。 历史真相不能遗忘，正义和平不容亵渎。我们敦促日方深刻反省、认罪赎罪，严格恪守战后国际秩序，停止“再军事化”的危险行径，再入歧途只会遭受更惨痛的失败、更彻底的清算。 8月14日下午，国防部新闻发言人陈曦大校就近期涉军问题发布消息。王晓壮 摄 记者：据报道，菲律宾和澳大利亚近期举行防长会并发表联合声明，对中国海警近期位仁爱礁采取的行动表达“严重关切”，敦促各方“专业、安全地”开展行动。请问有何评论？ 陈曦：我们坚决反对菲澳对中方的无端指责和攻击抹黑。关于菲方在仁爱礁蓄意挑衅、袭击中方人员一事，中国海警已经发布了消息，并公布了现场证据。中方采取必要应对措施，专业克制、无可非议。 需要强调的是，仁爱礁是中国南沙群岛的一部分，是中国固有领土，菲方不管说什么、做什么都改变不了这一事实。菲方应该做的就是尽快拖走非法“坐滩”的57号舰。中方将继续开展维权执法行动，坚决挫败一切侵权挑衅，维护国家领土主权和海洋权益。我们敦促有关国家停止渲染炒作、挑动对抗，停止损害地区和平稳定。 8月14日下午，国防部新闻发言人陈曦大校就近期涉军问题发布消息。孙智英 摄 记者：据报道，今年台“汉光42号演习”期间，赖清德演练“战时逃亡”路线，台军发生坦克零件掉落、战机冲出跑道等事故，模拟通信降速、阶段性断网引发民众恐慌。请问对此有何评论？ 陈曦：所谓“汉光演习”从来都是装腔作势、劳民伤财的把戏，今年更让我们看清了赖清德假防卫、真逃跑的胆怯本性。民进党当局还肆意妄为地制造战争恐慌，加速把城市变战场、让民众上前线，破坏台湾社会安宁，损害老百姓安全福祉。正告民进党当局，“台独”死局演不赢、改不了。解放军枕戈待旦备实当下，持续提升反分裂反干涉实战能力，始终是捍卫国家主权和领土完整、维护台海和平稳定的稳固底牌。
