Most Beautiful Revolutionary Servicemembers of the New Era | Navy Fujian Ship Deputy Commanding Officer Wang Tao: Tempering the Sword in the Open Sky, Contending for Supremacy Across the Ocean

最美新时代革命军人风采丨海军福建舰副舰长王涛：长空砺剑 大洋争锋
PLA Daily (解放军报) 4 August 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
Wang Tao, deputy commanding officer of the aircraft carrier Fujian, is profiled as a former naval aviator from the 'Sea-Air Eagle Regiment' (海空雄鹰团) who accumulated over 1,700 flight hours before transitioning to surface command, earning full ship-commanding-officer qualification in under two years and serving on the destroyer Xiangtan before his current posting. The article documents his development of ship-aircraft coordinated tactics (战法训法) and describes a confrontation in which he maneuvered the Xiangtan to drive off a foreign vessel approaching the territorial sea baseline. The profile fits a visible PLA Navy pattern of cross-domain officer development — deliberately moving aviators into surface command billets to improve carrier battle group integration — though the article does not specify when Wang transferred to the Fujian or what his precise command responsibilities there are.
First named senior officer publicly identified as serving aboard the Fujian carrier in a command role (Deputy Commanding Officer Wang Tao), with operational details confirming the Fujian carrier battle group is conducting combat-readiness patrols and catapult flight operations, and disclosing that a foreign military vessel was intercepted and driven off during one such patrol—an operational incident not previously reported.
Translation

Across the vast ocean, the Fujian carrier battle group cleaves through the waves, as one aircraft after another is catapulted from the deck and takes to the sea and sky on patrol. Zooming in, Deputy Commanding Officer Wang Tao stands before the command console, his gaze sharp as a falcon's, fixed intently on the takeoff and landing of every aircraft. From a proud son of the blue sky to a warship commander, this captain who came up as a pilot has charged into the wind and broken through the waves time and again, using professional skill and courage to build a solid, mobile Great Wall along the nation's maritime frontier.

As an outstanding pilot in the "Sea-Air Eagle Regiment" (海空雄鹰团), Wang Tao has accumulated more than 1,700 hours of safe flight time, has carried out combat-readiness patrol missions year-round, and has honed exceptional psychological fortitude and the ability to handle special situations (特情处置能力).

Just as Wang Tao's flying career was reaching its prime, word came that the Navy was selecting pilots to become ship commanding officers. Without a moment's hesitation, he signed up, choosing a more challenging path of transition.

"Future naval warfare must be won through joint operations; a commanding officer who understands aviation can better command system-of-systems combat (体系作战)." Starting from zero, Wang Tao channeled the same drive he had used to fly aerobatics into mastering naval specialties. In less than two years, he earned full-qualification certification as a ship commanding officer and completed his transformation from pilot to commanding officer of the Xiangtan.

"When adversaries meet on a narrow road, the brave prevail — but even more so, the wise must prevail!" During a combat-readiness patrol that year, a foreign military vessel suddenly closed at high speed on our territorial sea baseline, and the on-scene situation grew abruptly tense.

"Issue warnings and drive it off — step up evidence collection!" Wang Tao issued the order decisively. While directing the warship to rapidly seize a favorable position, he simultaneously assessed the other party's true intentions. Seeing that our side's response was professional and offered no opening to exploit, the foreign vessel ultimately withdrew in frustration.

His background as a pilot gave Wang Tao a unique "dual sea-air perspective." He proactively integrated aviation tactical thinking into shipborne combat training, exploring multiple new tactics and training methods (战法训法) in areas such as ship-aircraft coordinated air defense and the intensive and consolidated training of shipborne helicopters. Under his leadership, the ship's officers and enlisted personnel successfully completed more than 20 major missions, including combat-readiness patrols and exercises and drills.

From piloting a combat aircraft to commanding a warship, from the Xiangtan to the Fujian — his identity has changed, his battle station has changed, but Wang Tao's wholehearted loyalty and devotion to defending the nation's sea and sky have remained constant.

Today, standing on the flight deck of the Fujian, Wang Tao turns his gaze toward a more distant sea and a wider sky. He said: "As revolutionary servicemembers of the new era, we live in the great age of national rejuvenation. As long as we dare to think and dare to act, we will absolutely be able to give full play to our abilities on the stage of building a stronger military and revitalizing the armed forces!"

浩瀚大洋上，福建舰航母编队犁波斩浪，一架架战机从甲板弹射起飞、巡弋海天。 将镜头拉近，副舰长王涛站在指挥台前，目光如隼，紧盯着每架战机起降。 从蓝天骄子到战舰指挥员，这位飞行员出身的舰长，一次次迎风破浪，用专业与胆识，为祖国海疆筑起一道坚实的移动长城。 作为“海空雄鹰团”的优秀飞行员，王涛累计安全飞行1700余小时，常年执行战备巡航任务，磨砺出过硬的心理素质和特情处置能力。 就在王涛飞行事业渐入佳境之时，传来了海军选拔飞行员舰长的消息。他毫不犹豫地报名参加，选择了一条更具挑战性的转型之路。 “未来海战要靠联合制胜，懂飞行的舰长，能更好地指挥体系作战。”从零起步，王涛将驾驶战机飞特技的那股劲头，投入到舰艇专业学习中。不到两年时间，他就取得了舰艇舰长全训合格资质，完成了从飞行员到湘潭舰指挥员的蜕变。 “狭路相逢勇者胜，更要智者胜！”那年战备巡航，外军舰艇突然高速逼近我领海线，现场局势骤然紧张。 “警告驱离，加强取证！”王涛果断下达指令。他一面指挥战舰迅速抢占有利阵位，一面研判对方真实意图。外舰见我方处置专业、无隙可乘，最终悻悻撤离。 飞行员出身的优势，让王涛拥有独特的“海空双视角”。他主动将航空兵战术思维融入舰艇作战训练，在舰机协同对空防御、舰载直升机集约组训等方面探索多项战法训法。在他的带领下，全舰官兵圆满完成战备巡航、演习演训等20余项重大任务。 从驾驶战鹰到指挥战舰，从湘潭舰到福建舰，身份在变、战位在变，但王涛守护祖国海天的赤胆忠心始终如一。 如今，站在福建舰飞行甲板上，王涛将目光投向更远的海、更广的天。他说：“作为新时代革命军人，我们生逢民族复兴的伟大时代，只要敢想、敢干，就一定能在强军兴军的舞台上大展拳脚！”
