"Sword-Forgers" Deep in the Grasslands — An Investigative Report from an Army Unit

草原深处“铸剑人”——来自陆军某部的调查报告

View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
A PLA Daily feature profiles the officers and soldiers of an unidentified Army ordnance test range located in a grassland region — established in 1953 as China's first military ordnance test range — documenting the work of senior engineers Leng Xuebing and Wang Chang'an and operators Yang Caiyong and Wang Ayue in testing and evaluating small arms, precision-guided rocket projectiles, tank systems, and ammunition under extreme environmental conditions. The article documents the institutional culture and generational continuity of the range's 'range people' (靶场人), framing their work around the 'testing–training–combat' (试验—训练—作战) capability generation chain and the unit's sixteen-character spirit (艰苦奋斗、科学求实、团结协作、默默奉献). Its value is as a record of how PLA military media frames the test-and-evaluation community's professional identity and its relationship to combat readiness, not as evidence of a new capability or changed posture.
Translation

"Sword-Forgers" Deep in the Grasslands — An Investigative Report from an Army Unit

■ Wei Qingwei, Gong Ming, PLA Daily Reporter Qian Xiaohu

A fierce wind swept across the deep grasslands, and the gravel it kicked up beat urgently against the car windows. Leng Xuebing, a senior engineer with a certain Army unit, straightened the test-participant armband on his sleeve, gripped his walkie-talkie, and fixed his gaze on the test range — his expression like that of a warrior about to leap from a trench.

"3, 2, 1 — fire!" At the command, the earth gave a violent shudder, and a certain type of rocket projectile pierced the sky like a sharp sword. Leng Xuebing held his breath, eyes locked on the ballistic curve dancing across the screen… Only when the final set of data appeared did the furrow in his brow finally ease.

"Hard work is the only path to real results. For us 'range people' (靶场人), testing and evaluating weapons and equipment to be practical, effective, and durable — that is the most brilliant battle honor!" In a discussion the unit subsequently organized on the theme of "what constitutes genuine achievement," Leng Xuebing's remarks struck a chord with everyone present.

Facing the great test of the era — achieving the centenary goal of the People's Army on schedule — generation after generation of "range people" have taken root at their posts, carrying forward the spirit of "hard struggle, scientific rigor, solidarity and cooperation, and selfless dedication" (艰苦奋斗、科学求实、团结协作、默默奉献), and fixing their sights on three functional objectives: the main battleground for equipment testing and evaluation, the incubator for combat capability generation in the forces, and the whetstone for building a new-type Army. They strictly guard the nation's standards and devote themselves wholeheartedly to forging the sword for war.

[Caption: A certain type of tank undergoes a performance evaluation test at the unit. Pictured: the moment of firing. Photo by Li Haifeng.]

The Battlefield in Their Chests, Mud on Their Boots

"Conducting tests cannot be done on paper — you must aim at real combat requirements, sink your heart into it, and get your legs moving!" Wang Chang'an, a senior engineer with the unit, keeps this saying constantly on his lips. In his view, genuine achievement is not the fine words written in summary reports, but rather providing the forces with weapons and equipment capable of winning battles.

The battlefield in their chests, mud on their boots. On one occasion, in order to compile a certain type of ammunition effectiveness manual, Wang Chang'an traveled thousands of li, going from the test range to the exercise ground. Some officers and soldiers had reported that a certain type of ammunition was limited in effectiveness under complex terrain conditions. Upon learning of this, Wang Chang'an immediately organized verification and confirmed it was indeed the case. With support from higher authorities, he led his team in a concentrated effort and developed a fire command assistance system that effectively resolved the problems with that type of ammunition.

War never follows a script, and testing must dare to "get tough and push hard" (较真碰硬). During the evaluation test of a certain new-type weapon, Wang Chang'an and his comrades keenly identified a fatal vulnerability: under strong electromagnetic interference, the weapon's firing accuracy dropped markedly. After the situation was reported back to the developing unit, the other party maintained that it "met the basic requirements of the outline," and given the enormous difficulty of making improvements, they dragged their feet and took no action.

"If we encounter a strong magnetic environment on the battlefield, are we supposed to just sit and wait for death?" Wang Chang'an and his comrades knew full well that allowing equipment with defects to go to the battlefield would inevitably exact a price in blood. They worked through the night researching and demonstrating the problem, and put forward a certain dynamic self-alignment method. This determined spirit of seriousness influenced and drove the developing unit. Through joint efforts, the firing accuracy of that weapon type under strong magnetic interference was substantially improved.

In everyday life, Wang Chang'an is an easygoing person, but the moment he steps onto the test range, he instantly becomes a "stern-faced judge." During a test of a certain precision-guided rocket projectile, he found that the target differed from real combat requirements and immediately called a halt: "Equipment testing must answer to the battlefield! If we are always content to just clear the 'passing line,' what is the point of testing?" In the end, the relevant unit adjusted its plan.

"Testing cannot be satisfied with mere 'qualification' — it must also connect with the training needs of the forces." Beyond equipment testing, he led his team in sorting through and analyzing large volumes of data, summarizing a new method for rapid fire planning that provided strong support for combat units in shortening their emergency response times. At the same time, they collected feedback on equipment use, continuously optimized algorithm models, and further cleared the pathway of the "testing — training — combat" (试验—训练—作战) combat capability generation chain. This style of seeking no empty fame and doing no empty work is a vivid footnote to their hard and earnest toil and the results they have created that can withstand the test of real combat.

"The fierce wind reveals the sturdy grass. The grass here has backbone — after wind and rain, it grows thicker and more upright." These words were written on the flyleaf of a notebook by a veteran of the range. That stretch of grassland is where their dreams began, and it has become the spiritual homeland that the "Wang Chang'ans" guard with their sense of mission.

Willingly Staying Behind the Scenes Year-Round, Forging Sharp Weapons Through Testing

"I want to be a frontline fighter holding a steel rifle and galloping across the battlefield!" More than ten years ago, young Wu Zongliang watched the National Day parade on television and felt a fire ignite in his chest. Two years later, he put on a military uniform as he had wished.

He and his new comrades rode a military train deep into the grasslands. The vast and sparsely populated landscape made their hearts grow colder with every passing kilometer. After being assigned to his squad, Wu Zongliang sat in a corner in a daze, and squad leader Hu Aijun saw through what was on his mind. "Squad leader, what's the point of us being so obscure and unknown? Who even knows we exist?" Hu Aijun listened, patted him on the shoulder, and said earnestly: "Endure the cold bench for ten years, and the sword will be sharpened in a single morning (板凳甘坐十年冷，砺剑方能一朝成). The rifles that pass through our testing and evaluation become sharp weapons for defending the homeland — isn't that meaningful enough?" The words were a wake-up call. A light appeared in Wu Zongliang's eyes, and he understood the mission of the "range people": to forge one fine "rifle" after another for the motherland!

The challenges arrived on schedule. During one dust test, a sandstorm suddenly erupted inside the environmental simulation chamber, reducing visibility to less than one meter. Wu Zongliang, wearing a gas mask, held his breath and pulled the trigger. The gunfire left his ears ringing, and he was required to fire continuously for several hours under such conditions. This was just one ordinary hurdle in small-arms testing; there were also torrential rain, salt fog, high temperatures approaching 50 degrees Celsius, and severe cold reaching minus 45 degrees Celsius… Only by withstanding all of these trials could a weapon be considered provisionally qualified. Senior engineer Huang Xueying said: "This is both a test of the equipment and, even more so, a test of the shooter. You must achieve 'unity of person and weapon' (人枪合一) before you can serve as a qualified 'examiner.'"

Ballistic measurement is a process of being "meticulous to the last detail" (锱铢必较) with data measured in meters, seconds, and pascals. Years ago, during a measurement mission for a certain type of target drone, Leng Xuebing discovered that the ballistic velocity curve, which should have been smooth, showed extremely faint fluctuations. At the time, some said these minor variations did not affect the test results and could be disregarded.

"Victory or defeat may hinge on precisely these fractions of a second and millimeter." With few reference materials available, Leng Xuebing and his comrades set out on a path of exploration into the unknown. At that moment, a cutting-edge research topic in the field of radar studies entered their field of view.

"This topic has had many experts scratching their heads — can they really crack it?" In the face of doubt, Leng Xuebing and his comrades never wavered. The test conditions were rudimentary, so they built their own facilities independently; there were no training instruments, so they developed simulation systems… Through unrelenting effort, they ultimately overcame the difficulty and applied the results to testing and evaluation missions for long-range suppression, air defense and anti-missile, and armored assault-type weapons.

"I don't need you to recognize me, I don't yearn for you to know me, I have poured my youth in, poured it into the rivers and mountains of the motherland…" The song "The Motherland Will Not Forget" (《祖国不会忘记》) is the one most beloved by the officers and soldiers of this unit. When the weapons and equipment they have tested and evaluated are delivered into the hands of the forces, those years of willingly staying behind the scenes and quietly working the fields bloom into the most brilliant flowers of "visible achievement" (显绩).

Credit Need Not Go to Me — Running the Relay for a Strong Military Well

"In 1953, the Party Central Committee, the Central Military Commission, and Chairman Mao, with profound strategic vision, made the decision to build our country's first military ordnance test range. Warriors who had just returned from the battlefields of the War to Resist U.S. Aggression and Aid Korea, their battle dust not yet washed away, launched a great undertaking on a grassland ravaged by wind and sand…"

In the military history museum, that history of burning passion produces a different feeling with every listening. "Our predecessors used wind vanes, stopwatches, and calipers, relying on sheer force of will to reduce error to the minimum. Given the conditions we have now, what reason do we have to be slack or to complain?" The revolutionary spirit of the range's pioneers in battling heaven and earth has inspired Yang Caiyong to grow from a new recruit into a tank squad leader.

On one occasion, he was responsible for the driving component of a firing finalization test for a certain new-type tank. Just as the tank was traveling at 60 kilometers per hour over a rutted road surface, the left-side track suddenly broke. "Oh no!" New recruit Wang Hongjian's heart leapt straight to his throat. Inside the driver's compartment, Yang Caiyong gripped the steering wheel, held his breath, and used the "intermittent tap-braking method" (间歇性点刹法) to bring the tank to a smooth stop…

"Only by achieving zero errors in operation can the hidden problems of equipment under evaluation be precisely exposed, and only then can the evaluation results be reliable and valid." This is the creed that Yang Caiyong and his comrades have always upheld. Beyond that, whenever they had free time, they would climb into tanks and repeatedly study the internal structure; during holidays they would often spend long hours in the workshop, researching possible dangerous situations and how to respond to them. Later, when Yang Caiyong completed his service and was demobilized, he left behind a notebook filled with data and experience for Wang Hongjian. Today, Wang Hongjian has also grown into a squad leader, and he regards that notebook as a "family heirloom."

In the unit's plaza, the slogan "hard struggle, scientific rigor, solidarity and cooperation, and selfless dedication" (艰苦奋斗、科学求实、团结协作、默默奉献) is prominently displayed — these sixteen characters are also the personal motto of operator Wang Ayue. During a finalization test of a certain new-type ammunition, Wang Ayue and his comrades were required to fire continuously inside a sealed tank, collecting air data from within the cabin to provide critical support for improving ammunition quality.

"Preparations complete — ready to fire!" As the test proceeded, the cramped cabin rapidly filled with thick smoke, and the high temperatures generated by equipment operation and the violent concussive roar of rounds leaving the barrel challenged Wang Ayue's physical limits again and again. When the test ended, the other end of the walkie-talkie waited and waited for Wang Ayue's reply, but none came. When his comrades opened the hatch, they found Wang Ayue collapsed at his post, unable to speak. Afterward, when comrades praised him, Wang Ayue said simply: "The older generation of 'range people' dedicated their entire lives to this place. Now the baton has been passed to our hands — we have no reason not to run our leg well."

Strengthening and revitalizing the military is a relay race: "credit need not go to me, but credit will certainly include me" (功成不必在我，功成必定有我). Standing on the test range of this unit and looking out across it, on the journey of sharpening the sword for war, one young and vigorous figure after another presses boldly forward.

草原深处“铸剑人” ——来自陆军某部的调查报告 ■魏庆伟 宫 铭 解放军报记者 钱晓虎 狂风掠过草原深处，卷起的砾石急促地敲打着车窗。陆军某部高级工程师冷雪冰整理了一下参试袖标，握紧对讲机，目光投向试验场——那神情，像一名即将跃出战壕的勇士。 “3、2、1，发射！”一声令下，大地猛地一颤，某型火箭弹如同利剑一般刺破苍穹。冷雪冰屏住呼吸，紧盯屏幕上跃动的弹道曲线……当最后一组数据映入眼帘，他紧锁的眉头才舒展开来。 “实干才能出真业绩。对我们‘靶场人’而言，试验鉴定出实用、好用、耐用的武器装备，就是最闪亮的军功章！”在该部随后组织的一场关于“什么是真正的政绩”讨论中，冷雪冰的发言引发大家共鸣。 面对如期实现建军一百年奋斗目标的时代大考，一批批“靶场人”扎根战位，发扬“艰苦奋斗、科学求实、团结协作、默默奉献”精神，瞄准“装备试验鉴定主阵地、部队战斗力生成孵化器、新型陆军建设磨刀石”三大职能目标，严格为国把关、潜心为战铸剑。 某型坦克在该部接受性能鉴定试验。图为射击瞬间。李海峰摄 胸膛装着战场，脚下沾满泥土 “搞试验不能纸上谈兵，要瞄准实战需求，把心沉下去，让腿跑起来！”该部高级工程师王长安常把这句话挂在嘴边。在他看来，真正的业绩不是写在总结材料里的漂亮话，而是为部队提供能打胜仗的武器装备。 胸膛装着战场，脚下沾满泥土。一次，为编研某型弹药效能手册，王长安辗转千里，从试验场奔赴演练场。有官兵反映，某型弹药在复杂地形条件下效能发挥受限。王长安得知后立即组织验证，发现确有其事。在上级支持下，他带领团队展开攻关，研发出火力指挥辅助系统，有效破解了该型弹药存在的问题。 战争从不按剧本演进，试验就要敢于“较真碰硬”。在某新型武器鉴定试验中，王长安和战友们敏锐捕捉到一个致命隐患：强电磁干扰下，该型武器射击精度明显下降。向承研单位反馈情况后，对方却认为“符合大纲基本要求”，加之改进难度极大，迟迟按兵不动。 “若在战场遭遇强磁环境，难道要坐以待毙？”王长安和战友深知，如果让装备带着缺陷上战场，必将付出血的代价。他们连夜研究论证，提出某动态自对准方法。这股认真的劲头，影响带动了承研单位。经联合攻关，该型武器在强磁干扰下的射击精度得到大幅提升。 平时生活中，王长安为人随和，可一到试验场，就秒变“黑脸判官”。某型精确制导火箭弹试验，他发现靶标与实战要求有差距，当即叫停：“装备试验要对战场负责！总满足于‘及格线’，试验的意义何在？”最终，相关单位调整了方案。 “试验不能只满足于‘合格’，更要对接部队训练需求。”装备试验之余，他带领团队梳理分析大量数据，总结出快速火力规划新方法，为作战部队缩短应急应战反应时间提供了有力支撑。同时，收集装备使用反馈意见，不断优化算法模型，进一步畅通“试验—训练—作战”战斗力生成链路。这种不慕虚名、不务虚功的作风，正是他们苦干实干、创造经得起实战检验实绩的生动注脚。 “疾风知劲草。这里的草有风骨，风雨过后，反而更粗壮，更挺拔。”这是一位靶场前辈写在笔记本扉页上的一句话。那片草原，是他们梦想开始的地方，也成了“王长安们”用使命守护的精神家园。 常年甘居幕后，试验锻造利器 “我要当手握钢枪、驰骋沙场的尖兵！”10多年前，在电视屏幕前观看国庆阅兵的青年吴宗良，胸中燃起一团火。两年后，他如愿穿上了军装。 他和新战友乘坐军列驶向草原深处，地广人稀的景象，让他们越走心越凉。分到班里后，吴宗良躲在角落里发呆，被班长胡爱军看穿心事。“班长，我们这样默默无闻，有啥意义？谁知道我们？”胡爱军听后拍了拍他的肩膀，语重心长地说：“板凳甘坐十年冷，砺剑方能一朝成。经过我们试验鉴定的枪，成了保家卫国的利器，还不够有意义？”一语点醒梦中人。吴宗良眼里透着光亮，读懂了“靶场人”的使命：为祖国锻造一把把“好枪”！ 挑战如期而至。一次扬尘试验，环境模拟室内沙尘暴骤起，能见度不足一米。头戴防毒面罩的吴宗良屏住呼吸，扣动扳机。枪声震得耳朵嗡嗡响，而他需要在这样的条件下连续射击数小时。这只是轻武器试验的普通一关，还有暴雨、盐雾、近50摄氏度的高温、零下45摄氏度的严寒……只有经受住这些考验，一款枪才算初步合格。高级工程师黄雪鹰说：“这既是对装备的考验，更是对射手的考验，必须练就‘人枪合一’，才能当好这名‘考官’！” 弹道测量，是一个以米、秒、帕为计量单位对数据“锱铢必较”的过程。多年前，在某型靶弹测量任务中，冷雪冰发现，本应平滑的弹道速度曲线有极其微弱的起伏。当时有人说，这些细小波动不影响试验结果，可以忽略不计。 “输赢可能就在这分秒毫厘之间。”缺少参考资料，冷雪冰和战友们踏上了充满未知的探索之路。此时，一个雷达研究领域的前沿课题进入他们的视野。 “这个课题让很多专家挠头，他们能拿下吗？”面对质疑，冷雪冰和战友们从未动摇。试验条件简陋，就自主搭建设施；没有训练器材，就开发模拟系统……经过不懈努力，他们最终攻克难关，并将成果应用于远程压制、防空反导和装甲突击类武器的试验测试任务中。 “不需要你认识我，不渴望你知道我，我把青春融进，融进祖国的江河……”《祖国不会忘记》是该部官兵最爱唱的歌。当他们试验鉴定的武器装备交付官兵手中时，那些甘于幕后、默默耕耘的岁月，便开出了最绚烂的“显绩”之花。 功成不必在我，跑好强军接力 “1953年，党中央、中央军委和毛主席以深邃的战略视野，决策建设我国第一座军械试验靶场。从抗美援朝战场凯旋的勇士们征尘未洗，在风沙肆虐的草原上，开启了一场伟大的创业……” 军史馆里，那段激情燃烧的历史，让每一次聆听都有不同感受。“前辈们用风向仪、秒表和卡尺，靠顽强意志将误差降到最低。我们现在条件这么好，还有什么理由懈怠和抱怨？”靶场拓荒者战天斗地的革命精神，激励着杨才涌从一名新兵成长为坦克班班长。 一次，他负责某新型坦克射击定型试验的驾驶课目。就在坦克以60公里时速驶过坑洼路面时，左侧履带突然断裂。“糟了！”新兵王洪舰的心一下子提到了嗓子眼。驾驶舱内，杨才涌紧握方向盘，屏气凝神，运用“间歇性点刹法”将坦克平稳停下…… “只有做到操作‘零差错’，待试装备隐藏的问题才能精准暴露，鉴定结果才能可靠有效。”这是杨才涌和战友一直坚守的信条。不仅如此，一有空闲时间，他们便钻进坦克，反复钻研内部结构；节假日里更是经常“泡”在工房，研究可能出现的险情及其应对方法。后来，杨才涌服役期满退伍，将写满数据和经验的笔记本留给了王洪舰。如今，王洪舰也成长为班长，这个笔记本被他视为“传家宝”。 在该部广场，“艰苦奋斗、科学求实、团结协作、默默奉献”的标语十分醒目，这16个字也是操作手王阿越的座右铭。在某新型弹药定型试验中，王阿越与战友需要在密闭的坦克里连续射击，采集舱内空气数据，为改进弹药质量提供关键支撑。 “准备完毕，可以射击！”随着试验的展开，狭小的舱内迅速充满浓烟，设备运转产生的高温、炮弹出膛的强震轰鸣，一次次挑战着王阿越的生理极限。试验结束，对讲机的另一端却迟迟没有等来王阿越的回复。当战友打开舱门，发现王阿越瘫倒在战位上说不出话。事后，面对战友的点赞，王阿越淡淡地说：“老一辈‘靶场人’把一生都奉献给了这里。如今，接力棒传到了我们手里，我们没有理由不跑好。” 强军兴军，是一场接力赛，“功成不必在我，功成必定有我”。置身该部靶场放眼望去，在向战砺剑的征途上，一个个年轻而矫健的身影奋勇向前。
