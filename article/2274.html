About this analysis
Summary
A July 8 Xinhua feature, bylined by three reporters and timed to the PLA's approaching centenary, synthesizes Xi Jinping's military-building framework across the post-2012 period, covering the CMC Chairman Responsibility System, the 2016 theater command restructuring, and the September 3, 2025 WWII anniversary review as the capstone demonstration of results. The article's institutional value is as a record of how the PLA's political apparatus frames the entire reform era—political rectification, force structure overhaul, equipment modernization, and combat readiness—as a unified, Xi-directed project rather than a sequence of discrete policy decisions. It documents the current official narrative baseline against which future PLA political work content and leadership speeches can be measured for deviation or emphasis shift.
Translation

Writing the New-Era Answer Sheet for Strengthening and Revitalizing the Military

Xinhua News Agency reporters Ding Zengyi, Zhang Yongjian, and Li Minghui

In Gutian, western Fujian, the hills are lush and green, and the eight characters reading "The Gutian Conference Shines Forever" stand solemn and striking. Step inside the main hall of the Gutian Conference site, and traces of charcoal fires remain—on a winter's day 97 years ago, the delegates gathered here lit charcoal fires, and also kindled the spark of building the Party through ideology and building the military through politics.

In the golden autumn of 2014, General Secretary Xi Jinping personally decided to convene the All-Army Political Work Conference here, leading the People's Army to trace its roots, renew its resolve, and set out again.

Since the 18th Party Congress, General Secretary Xi Jinping has clearly put forward the goal of military strengthening (强军目标) of "building a people's army that obeys the Party's command, is capable of winning battles, and has an excellent work style." The Party's goal of military strengthening in the new era plans for the great cause of national rejuvenation, lays out the overall situation of a strong nation and strong military, establishes the foundation for development and security, and defines what the People's Army should look like as the military of a major power and a strong nation.

Around this goal, General Secretary Xi Jinping has led the entire military in conducting in-depth theoretical exploration and practical innovation, forming Xi Jinping Thought on Strengthening the Military (习近平强军思想), which profoundly answers the major questions of our era regarding what kind of powerful people's army to build in the new era and how to build it. It has made clear that strengthening the nation requires strengthening the military, that consolidating national defense and building a powerful people's army are the strategic underpinning for upholding and developing socialism with Chinese characteristics and achieving the great rejuvenation of the Chinese nation in the new era, and has established the strategic layout of building the military through politics (政治建军), strengthening the military through reform (改革强军), strengthening the military through science and technology (科技强军), strengthening the military through talent (人才强军), and governing the military according to law (依法治军). It has opened up new horizons in Marxist military theory and the development of contemporary Chinese military practice.

Obeying the Party's command is the soul, determining the political direction of military development.

General Secretary Xi Jinping has profoundly stated: "Upholding the Party's absolute leadership over the military is the soul of military strengthening; forging the military soul (铸牢军魂) is the core task of our military's political work, and must never waver at any time."

Since the 18th Party Congress, the Party has led the People's Army in advancing political rectification (政治整训) with the spirit of the rectification movement (整风精神), resolutely and comprehensively enforcing strict Party governance and strict military governance, with a determination and intensity unprecedented in scale. The building of the military through politics in the new era has achieved historic results.

The entire military treats the comprehensive and thorough implementation of the Chairman of the Central Military Commission Responsibility System (军委主席负责制) as the highest political requirement to observe and the highest political discipline to uphold. It has improved the institutional mechanisms for implementing the Chairman of the CMC Responsibility System and ensured the implementation of working mechanisms for reporting requests for instructions, supervision and inspection, and information services, genuinely achieving "listening to Chairman Xi's command, being accountable to Chairman Xi, and giving Chairman Xi peace of mind."

The 19th Party Congress elevated "upholding the Party's absolute leadership over the people's army" to a basic strategy for upholding and developing socialism with Chinese characteristics in the new era, and enshrined in the Party Constitution the leadership system of the Central Military Commission operating under the Chairman Responsibility System.

"Golden stars shine on the military flag, our principle is the Party commands the gun..." The song echoes through military barracks, expressing the officers' and soldiers' political consciousness of absolute loyalty, absolute purity, and absolute reliability.

Being capable of winning battles is the core, reflecting the fundamental function of the military and the fundamental orientation of military development.

"When the Party and the people need it, can this military of ours always uphold the Party's absolute leadership? Can it be called up and win battles? Can commanders at all levels lead troops into battle and command battles?" General Secretary Xi Jinping's "question of victory in war (胜战之问)" was a thunderclap, and a "great discussion on the standard of combat effectiveness" swept through the entire military. The entire military firmly established combat effectiveness as the sole and fundamental standard, focusing all thinking on fighting and directing all work toward fighting.

The deepening of national defense and military reform advanced steadily and swiftly. The "three major campaigns" of reform of the leadership and command system, reform of scale, structure, and force composition, and reform of military policy systems were pushed forward in overlapping succession, constructing a new pattern in which the CMC exercises overall management, theater commands focus on warfighting, and the services focus on force building. The People's Army has achieved comprehensive renewal in its institutional structure, organizational structure, overall pattern, and appearance.

Domestically produced aircraft carriers, the J-20, the Y-20, and other advanced equipment have entered service, and the Dongfeng series missiles have formed a powerful deterrent. Exercises and training in the East China Sea, South China Sea, and Taiwan Strait directions are conducted on a routine basis, and escort missions in the Gulf of Aden and overseas evacuation of nationals demonstrate the responsibility of a major power. From live-force confrontation exercises at the Zhurihe Joint Training Base to combat exercises on the frontline border, the People's Army has enhanced its joint operations and all-domain operations capabilities through tempering in the smoke of battle, safeguarding national sovereignty, unity, and territorial integrity with stronger deterrence and warfighting power.

An excellent work style is the guarantee, relating to the nature, purpose, and character of the military.

General Secretary Xi Jinping has earnestly admonished: "An excellent work style can forge heroic units; a lax work style can destroy an ever-victorious army." The entire military has studied and implemented the spirit of the Central Eight-Point Regulation and the CMC Ten-Point Regulation and their implementing rules, conducted in-depth "eight special rectification campaigns (八个专项清理整治)," comprehensively halted paid services by the military, and issued disciplinary prohibitions strictly banning unauthorized banquets and drinking and strictly banning unauthorized business operations for profit.

At the same time, the red gene (红色基因) is passed down from generation to generation, with the exemplary and leading role of advanced models such as the "Great Merit Third Company (大功三连)" given full play, and the fighting spirit of "fearing neither hardship nor death (一不怕苦、二不怕死)" continuously cultivated. From guarding the border in the Karakoram to the frontlines of disaster relief, officers and soldiers have put into action "pure love, only for China."

On September 3, 2025, commemorative activities marking the 80th anniversary of the victory of the Chinese People's War of Resistance Against Japanese Aggression and the World Anti-Fascist War were held. The People's Army, with the new bearing of political rectification, the new layout of force structure, the new progress of modernization, and the new results of combat readiness and warfighting, received with honor the review of General Secretary Xi Jinping and the review of the Party and the people.

At present, we are closer to the goal of the great rejuvenation of the Chinese nation than at any point in history, and we also need to build a powerful people's army more than at any point in history. The People's Army focuses on striving toward the centenary of the founding of the military, anchors its goals and tasks, and follows the blueprint for military strengthening charted by General Secretary Xi Jinping, advancing toward world-class status with a more vigorous posture and providing stronger strategic support for achieving the great rejuvenation of the Chinese nation.

The military flag flutters; the war drums urge the march forward. The People's Army, having traversed a glorious journey of nearly one hundred years, will, in the bugle call of the new era, assuredly advance from victory to new victory.

(Xinhua News Agency, Beijing, July 8)

Original Chinese
书写强军兴军的新时代答卷 新华社记者丁增义、张勇健、李明辉 闽西古田，漫山青翠，“古田会议永放光芒”八个大字庄严夺目。走进古田会议会址大厅，炭火痕迹犹存——97年前的冬日里，与会代表们在这里燃起炭火，也点亮了思想建党、政治建军的星星之火。 2014年金秋，习近平总书记亲自决策在这里召开全军政治工作会议，引领人民军队寻根溯源、重整行装再出发。 党的十八大以来，习近平总书记鲜明提出“建设一支听党指挥、能打胜仗、作风优良的人民军队”的强军目标。党在新时代的强军目标，谋的是民族复兴伟业，布的是强国强军大局，立的是发展安全之基，标定了人民军队作为大国军队、强国军队的样子。 围绕这一目标，习近平总书记带领全军深入进行理论探索和实践创造，形成了习近平强军思想，深刻回答了新时代建设一支什么样的强大人民军队、怎样建设强大人民军队的重大时代课题，明确了强国必须强军，巩固国防和强大人民军队是新时代坚持和发展中国特色社会主义、实现中华民族伟大复兴的战略支撑，确立了政治建军、改革强军、科技强军、人才强军、依法治军的战略布局，开辟了马克思主义军事理论和当代中国军事实践发展新境界。 听党指挥是灵魂，决定军队建设的政治方向。 习近平总书记深刻指出：“坚持党对军队绝对领导是强军之魂，铸牢军魂是我军政治工作的核心任务，任何时候都不能动摇。” 党的十八大以来，党领导人民军队以整风精神推进政治整训，坚定不移全面从严治党、全面从严治军，决心之大前所未有，力度之大前所未有，新时代政治建军取得历史性成就。 全军把全面深入贯彻军委主席负责制，作为最高的政治要求来遵守、最高的政治纪律来维护，健全贯彻军委主席负责制体制机制，抓好请示报告、督促检查、信息服务工作机制落实，切实做到“听习主席指挥、对习主席负责、让习主席放心”。 党的十九大把“坚持党对人民军队的绝对领导”上升为新时代坚持和发展中国特色社会主义的一条基本方略，把中央军事委员会实行主席负责制这一领导体制在党章中确立下来。 “金星闪耀在军旗上，我们的原则是党指挥枪……”歌声回荡军营，唱出了官兵绝对忠诚、绝对纯洁、绝对可靠的政治自觉。 能打胜仗是核心，反映军队的根本职能和军队建设的根本指向。 “在党和人民需要的时候，我们这支军队能不能始终坚持住党的绝对领导，能不能拉得上去、打胜仗，各级指挥员能不能带兵打仗、指挥打仗？”习近平总书记的“胜战之问”振聋发聩，一场“战斗力标准大讨论”席卷全军。全军牢固确立战斗力这个唯一的根本的标准，全部心思向打仗聚焦、各项工作向打仗用劲。 深化国防和军队改革蹄疾步稳，领导指挥体制改革、规模结构和力量编成改革、军事政策制度改革“三大战役”压茬推进，构建起军委管总、战区主战、军种主建新格局，人民军队体制一新、结构一新、格局一新、面貌一新。 国产航母、歼-20、运-20等先进装备列装，“东风”系列导弹形成强大威慑。东海、南海、台海方向演训常态开展，亚丁湾护航、海外撤侨彰显大国担当。从朱日和联合训练基地的实兵对抗，到边防一线的实战演训，人民军队在硝烟淬炼中提升联合作战与全域作战能力，以更强大的威慑和实战力量捍卫国家主权、统一和领土完整。 作风优良是保证，关系军队的性质、宗旨、本色。 习近平总书记谆谆告诫：“作风优良才能塑造英雄部队，作风松散可以搞垮常胜之师。”全军深入学习贯彻中央八项规定精神、军委十项规定及其实施细则精神，深入开展“八个专项清理整治”，全面停止军队有偿服务，出台严禁违规宴请喝酒、严禁违规经营牟利等纪律禁令。 与此同时，红色基因代代相传，充分发挥“大功三连”等先进典型示范引领作用，“一不怕苦、二不怕死”的战斗精神持续涵养。从喀喇昆仑卫国戍边到抢险救灾一线，官兵用行动践行“清澈的爱、只为中国”。 2025年9月3日，中国人民抗日战争暨世界反法西斯战争胜利80周年纪念活动举行。人民军队以政治整训新风貌、力量结构新布局、现代化建设新进展、备战打仗新成效，光荣接受习近平总书记检阅，接受党和人民检阅。 当前，我们比历史上任何时期都更接近中华民族伟大复兴的目标，也比历史上任何时期都更需要建设一支强大的人民军队。人民军队聚焦奋斗建军百年，锚定目标任务，沿着习近平总书记擘画的强军蓝图，以更加昂扬的姿态迈向世界一流，为实现中华民族伟大复兴提供更为坚强的战略支撑。 军旗猎猎，战鼓催征。走过近百年辉煌征程的人民军队，在新时代的号角声中，必将从胜利走向新的胜利。 （新华社北京7月8日电）
