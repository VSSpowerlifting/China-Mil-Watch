Riding the Vessel Toward the Heavenly Palace, Chasing Dreams to Grasp the Stellar River — A Firsthand Account of the Shenzhou-23 Crewed Mission Crew's Departure

乘舟向天宫 逐梦揽星河——神舟二十三号载人飞行任务乘组出征侧记
PLA Daily (解放军报) 25 May 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
On May 24, the Shenzhou-23 crew — commander Zhu Yangzhu (on his second spaceflight), pilot Zhang Zhiyuan, and Hong Kong payload specialist Li Jiaying — launched aboard a Long March-2F Yao-23 rocket from Jiuquan at 23:08, with one crew member designated for a one-year extended on-orbit stay that the article describes as the start of an 'annual mode' for Chinese crewed missions. The piece is PLA Daily ceremonial launch coverage; its narrow documentary value is the confirmation that China is normalizing year-long crew rotations aboard Tiangong, extending the station's operational tempo beyond the six-month cycles that characterized earlier missions.
Translation

Riding the Vessel Toward the Heavenly Palace, Chasing Dreams to Grasp the Stellar River — A Firsthand Account of the Shenzhou-23 Crewed Mission Crew's Departure

■ Yang Zifan, PLA Daily Reporter Zhao Qian

Astronauts Zhu Yangzhu (center), Zhang Zhiyuan (right), and Li Jiaying at the departure ceremony. Photo by Xinhua News Agency reporter Cai Yang.

"Her rainbow garments trail their broad sashes, floating and drifting as she ascends to heaven."

On May 24, deep in the desert, on the banks of the Ruoshui River, the milk-white Long March-2F Yao-23 carrier rocket stood ready to launch. On the mission insignia for Shenzhou-23, the flowing ribbons raised at the arms of the "Flying Apsara goddess" once again drew the world's gaze to yet another magnificent new chapter of the "dream of flight."

This is a dream-chase surging with the energy of youth——

At approximately 20:18, beginning from the Wentian Pavilion, the three astronauts of the Shenzhou-23 crewed mission crew set out together. Commander Zhu Yangzhu strode forward with bold and resolute steps. From his first spaceflight in May 2023 to his return to "Tiangong" in May 2026, he once again stepped to the rhythm of his dreams, flying toward the brilliant starry sky.

Astronaut Zhang Zhiyuan smiled and waved to the people who had come to see them off on both sides of the passageway. Once he piloted fighter jets over deserts and patrolled the sea and sky; now he pilots "Shenzhou" to knock on the vault of heaven and roam the stellar river. Making his first spaceflight, his eyes were filled with boundless longing for the vast universe.

On the chest of astronaut Li Jiaying, who comes from Hong Kong, the beautiful bauhinia flower on her mission insignia shone in harmony with her radiant smile. As a payload specialist, she is primarily responsible for various space science experiment tasks during this space journey.

This is a dream of incomparable splendor——

"5, 4, 3, 2, 1, ignition!" At 23:08, Shenzhou rose and pierced the heavens. The brilliance of the flames lit up the night sky, resonating with the heartbeats of hundreds of millions of Chinese people.

In the crowd, science fiction writer Liu Cixin gazed up at the starry sky and offered his finest wishes to the astronauts racing toward the universe: "Our technology can build a very good living environment for humanity in space — this is even better than what I imagined when I was writing. I believe that China's space program in the future will bring us even more stunning science fiction scenes."

In the stellar river, time flows quietly. The stories of China's space workers breaking through the boundaries of imagination time and again are more vivid and more romantic than any work of science fiction.

Seventy years ago, Qian Xuesen submitted to the Central Committee the "Proposal for Establishing China's National Defense Aviation Industry." In October 1956, China's first rocket and missile research institution — the Fifth Research Academy of the Ministry of National Defense — was established. From that moment, China's space program took its first step of historic significance.

Forty years ago, amid the surging tide of the world's scientific and technological revolution, China's "863 Program" was born, and space technology was designated as one of the key fields for China's development of high technology. China's space workers carried on and accelerated their advance.

Ten years ago, the Tiangong-2 space laboratory and the Shenzhou-11 crewed spacecraft were launched in succession, achieving a perfect conclusion to the second step of China's crewed spaceflight program's "three-step" strategy.

On this night, among the crew of the Shenzhou-23 crewed mission embarking on their space journey, one astronaut will conduct a one-year on-orbit extended stay experiment. The duration of Chinese astronauts' "space business trips" has begun to enter an "annual mode."

This is a shared journey to the stars to realize a dream——

The tail flame of the rocket gradually became a small point of light, finally merging into the vast darkness of the night. Inside Dongfeng Space City, countless eager gazes remained fixed on the center of the "dream of flight."

In the command-and-control hall, Guo Liguo, a medical monitoring and healthcare physician with the China Astronaut Research and Training Center test team, carefully observed the various physiological signals on the screen, ensuring the health and safety of the three astronauts, and put into action the pledge: "The astronauts are in my heart; the astronauts' safety is in my hands."

In the meteorological consultation hall, Kang Lin, the primary person in charge of the meteorological system for this launch mission, kept his eyes fixed on the radar imagery on the screen, "conversing" with heaven and earth in the silent dark of night, making precise assessments of every shift in wind and cloud.

Beside the launch tower, platform commander Gao Fei monitored the launch process in real time. This veteran space worker, who has been rooted in his post for 19 years, said: "It is the era that has given us the precious opportunity to lift rockets to soar high."

In the Dashu tracking and control zone, upon hearing the report of "optical and radar tracking normal," tracking and control operator Yin Shenggang felt a spontaneous surge of joy. From Shenzhou-1 to Shenzhou-23, he and his team members have successfully completed every tracking and control mission, and have personally witnessed one brilliant moment after another in the history of China's crewed spaceflight development……

Approximately ten minutes later, the spacecraft and rocket successfully separated and entered the predetermined orbit, and the launch mission was a complete success.

The stellar river blazes bright; the journey is not yet done. Brave in exploration, pressing ardently forward. Tonight, the Shenzhou-23 crew, bearing the great force of national rejuvenation and the hopes of hundreds of millions of people, rides "Shenzhou" toward "Tiangong," taking ever more resolute steps into the depths of the universe.

(PLA Daily, Jiuquan, May 24)

Original Chinese
乘舟向天宫 逐梦揽星河 ——神舟二十三号载人飞行任务乘组出征侧记 ■杨子凡 解放军报记者 赵倩 航天员朱杨柱（中）、张志远（右）、黎家盈在出征仪式上。新华社记者 才扬 摄 “霓裳曳广带，飘拂升天行。” 5月24日，大漠深处，弱水河畔，乳白色的长征二号F遥二十三运载火箭蓄势待发。神舟二十三号飞行任务标识上，“飞天女神”臂间扬起的飘带，让世界的目光再度聚焦于“飞天梦”的又一壮丽新篇。 这是一场青春澎湃的逐梦—— 20时18分许，以问天阁为起点，神舟二十三号载人飞行任务乘组3名航天员携手出征。指令长朱杨柱步伐豪迈而坚定，从2023年5月首次飞天，到2026年5月再赴“天宫”，他又一次踏着梦想节拍，飞向璀璨星空。 航天员张志远微笑着向通道两侧前来欢送的人们挥手致意。曾经驾驶战机飞越沙漠、巡弋海天，如今驾乘“神舟”叩问苍穹、遨游星河，首次飞天的他，眼眸中饱含着对浩瀚宇宙的无限向往。 来自香港的航天员黎家盈胸前的任务标识上，美丽的紫荆花与她灿烂的笑容交相辉映。作为载荷专家，此次太空之旅，她主要负责各类空间科学实验任务。 这是一个绚烂无比的绮梦—— “5、4、3、2、1，点火！”23时08分，神舟起，破苍穹。烈焰的光芒点亮夜空，澎湃着亿万中国人的心跳。 人群中，科幻作家刘慈欣仰望星空，为奔向宇宙的航天员们送上最美好的祝福：“我们的技术可以在太空中为人类建造出很好的生存环境，这比我此前写作时想象的还要好！相信未来的中国航天，会给我们带来更震撼的科幻场景。” 星河里，时光静静流淌。中国航天人一次次突破想象力边界的故事，比科幻作品更加生动、更加浪漫。 70年前，钱学森向中央提交《建立我国国防航空工业意见书》。1956年10月，中国第一个火箭、导弹研究机构——国防部第五研究院成立。从此，中国航天迈出了具有历史意义的第一步。 40年前，世界科技革命浪潮风起云涌，我国“863计划”诞生，航天技术被列为我国发展高科技的重点领域之一，中国航天人接续前行、加速奔跑。 10年前，“天宫二号”空间实验室、神舟十一号载人飞船接连发射，实现了中国载人航天工程“三步走”中第二步的完美收官。 这一夜，开启太空之旅的神舟二十三号载人飞行任务乘组中，一名航天员将开展1年期在轨驻留试验，中国航天员的“太空出差”时间开始迈入“年度模式”。 这是一次共赴星辰的圆梦—— 火箭的尾焰逐渐变成小小的光点，最终融入茫茫夜色。东风航天城内，无数热切的目光依旧聚焦“飞天梦”的圆心。 指控大厅中，中国航天员科研训练中心试验队的医监医保医生郭立国，仔细观察屏幕上的各项生理信号，确保3名航天员的生命健康，用实际行动践行“航天员在我心中、航天员安全在我手中”的誓言。 气象会商大厅里，此次发射任务气象系统主要负责人康林，紧盯着屏幕上的雷达图，在无声暗夜与天地“对话”，精准研判每一丝风云变幻。 发射塔架旁，平台指挥高飞实时监控发射过程。这位扎根岗位19年的老航天人说：“是时代为我们提供了托举火箭高飞的宝贵机遇。” 大树里测控区中，听到“光学、雷达跟踪正常”的报告声，测控人员尹胜刚的喜悦之情油然而生。从神舟一号到神舟二十三号，他和团队成员圆满完成每一次测控任务，也亲历了中国载人航天发展史上一个个精彩瞬间…… 大约10分钟后，飞船与火箭成功分离并进入预定轨道，发射任务取得圆满成功。 星河璀璨，征途未已；勇敢探索，热烈向前。今夜，神舟二十三号乘组承载着民族复兴的伟力与亿万人民的期盼，乘“神舟”向“天宫”，向着宇宙深处迈出愈加坚实的步伐。 （解放军报酒泉5月24日电）
