PLA Daily Commentator: Firm Confidence, Sustained Struggle

解放军报评论员：坚定信心 接续奋斗
PLA Daily (解放军报) 4 July 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
PLA Daily's third consecutive commentator article on Xi Jinping's July 1 speech ties the PLA's 15th Five-Year Plan obligations directly to the CCP's 105th anniversary ideological framework, explicitly invoking the 2027 centenary founding objectives and the 'new three-step' modernization arrangement as the military's near-term benchmarks. The article documents how the PLA's political apparatus is operationalizing Xi's 'five musts' formulation as a force-wide compliance standard, linking party loyalty rhetoric to concrete modernization timelines in a way that confirms the 2027 deadline remains the organizing pressure point for capability generation. The explicit pairing of 'strategic capabilities acceleration' with the 15th Five-Year Plan period raises the question of whether this commentary series is laying ideological groundwork for a forthcoming institutional directive on readiness or force structure.
Translation

Firm Confidence, Sustained Struggle — Third Commentary on Earnestly Studying and Implementing Chairman Xi's Important Speech at the Celebration of the 105th Anniversary of the Founding of the Communist Party of China

■ PLA Daily Commentator

All great achievements are the result of sustained struggle, and all great undertakings must be advanced by carrying forward the past and opening up the future.

"Fulfilling the Party's mission and tasks on the new journey of the new era requires all members of the Communist Party of China to firm up their confidence, sustain their struggle, and continuously create new achievements worthy of the era and the people." In his important speech at the celebration of the 105th anniversary of the founding of the Communist Party of China, Chairman Xi profoundly expounded where the Communist Party of China came from, why it has been able to succeed, and where it is going. He set forth the explicit requirement of "five musts" for all Party members to firm up their confidence and sustain their struggle on the new journey, and issued a great call to the entire Party, the people of all ethnic groups across the country, to strive to create new historical glory. Chairman Xi's important speech penetratingly reveals that confidence and struggle are the keys to realizing dreams and winning victory. It is inspiring and rousing, and will surely motivate us to firm up our conviction of certain victory, maintain a high fighting spirit, and forge ahead with courage and determination toward our grand objectives.

Firming up confidence and sustaining struggle is a defining quality of the Communist Party of China. Over the past 105 years, all the achievements we have made are the result of generation after generation of Communist Party of China members uniting and leading the Chinese people in tireless struggle. Having passed through the revolutionary years of bloody battle, the construction period of self-reliance, and the reform era of forging ahead, and having strode into the new era of building a strong country, our Party has always kept the people in its heart and carried the mission on its shoulders. Moving forward in accordance with the logic of historical progress, shouldering responsibilities with courage and determination amid the tide of the times, struggling tirelessly and forever, persisting with constancy and working toward long-term results, it has opened up a great path, established great achievements, forged a great spirit, accumulated precious experience, and written a magnificent epic in the development of the Chinese nation.

Great merit rests on great aspirations; great undertakings rest on great diligence. The new Long March to advance the cause of socialism with Chinese characteristics must be carried forward in continuous relay over the long term, and each generation must walk its own Long March road well. In his important speech, Chairman Xi set forth the explicit requirement of "five musts": "must adhere to the Party's basic theory, basic line, and basic policy"; "must rely closely on the people to create great historical achievements"; "must actively respond to risks and challenges on the road ahead"; "must continuously promote the building of a community with a shared future for mankind"; "must persistently advance comprehensive and strict Party governance." The explicit requirements of the "five musts" are closely connected and mutually integrated. They are the profound lessons of our Party's 105 years of tireless struggle, and the scientific guidance for advancing the great undertaking of building a strong country and national rejuvenation on the new journey. They inspire and encourage the entire Party and the people of all ethnic groups across the country to firm up their confidence, unite in struggle, and overcome difficulties, continuously opening up a new landscape in the construction of Chinese-style modernization.

"The 105 years of glorious history of the Communist Party of China are a source of pride, but we must absolutely not become arrogant and complacent or halt our advance." The 20th National Congress of the Party explicitly set forth the mission and tasks of the Communist Party of China on the new journey of the new era, emphasizing the need to "comprehensively advance the great rejuvenation of the Chinese nation through Chinese-style modernization." Realizing socialist modernization is a historical process of step-by-step progression and continuous development and progress. The mission we face is more glorious, the tasks more arduous, the challenges more severe, and the work more momentous. Only if the entire Party maintains the steadiness of an unwavering path and unaltered aspirations, adheres to the Party's overall leadership and the centralized and unified leadership of the Party Central Committee, continuously strengthens the Party's political leadership, ideological guidance, mass organizational capacity, and social mobilizing power, fully stimulates the enthusiasm, initiative, and creativity of hundreds of millions of people, reinforces awareness of potential dangers, adheres to bottom-line thinking, firms up resolve and determination, and buries itself in hard and earnest work, can it consolidate the immense force driving Chinese-style modernization forward and ensure that the great vessel of China's rejuvenation cleaves through the waves and proceeds steadily toward a distant destination.

Grand ambitions soar to the clouds; with courage and determination we embark on a new journey. Our military is a people's army created and led by the Party; the Party's mission and tasks determine the mission and tasks of the people's army. During the 15th Five-Year Plan period, our military must achieve the centenary of the founding of the People's Liberation Army struggle objectives on schedule and drive decisive progress toward the basic realization of national defense and military modernization. The entire military must earnestly study and implement the spirit of Chairman Xi's important speech, earnestly measure itself against the explicit requirements of the "five musts" for firming up confidence and sustaining struggle, coordinate development and security and national prosperity and military strength, ensure that the pace of national defense and military modernization is commensurate with the pace of national modernization and that military capabilities are commensurate with national strategic requirements, drive the accelerated generation of our military's strategic capabilities, and better support the great undertaking of building a strong country and national rejuvenation through a strong military. We must resolutely implement the new "three-step" strategic arrangement for national defense and military modernization, accelerate the modernization of military theory, military organizational structure, military personnel, and weapons and equipment, consolidate and enhance the integrated national strategic system and capabilities, go all out to fight and win the decisive battle to achieve the centenary of the founding of the People's Liberation Army struggle objectives, continuously open up a new landscape in national defense and military modernization, and provide strong strategic support for comprehensively advancing the great undertaking of building a strong country and national rejuvenation through Chinese-style modernization.

坚定信心 接续奋斗 ——三谈认真学习贯彻习主席在庆祝中国共产党成立105周年大会上的重要讲话 ■解放军报评论员 一切伟大成就都是接续奋斗的结果，一切伟大事业都需要在继往开来中推进。 “实现新时代新征程党的使命任务，要求全体中国共产党人坚定信心、接续奋斗，不断创造无愧于时代和人民的新业绩。”习主席在庆祝中国共产党成立105周年大会上的重要讲话，深刻阐明中国共产党从哪里来、为什么能、向何处去，对新征程上全体中国共产党人坚定信心、接续奋斗提出了“五个必须”的明确要求，向全党全国各族人民发出了奋力创造新的历史辉煌的伟大号召。习主席的重要讲话深入揭示了信心与奋斗是成就梦想、赢得胜利的关键所在，鼓舞人心、催人奋发，必将激励我们坚定必胜信念、保持昂扬斗志，向着宏伟目标开拓进取、勇毅前行。 坚定信心、接续奋斗，是中国共产党的鲜明品质。105年来，我们取得的一切成就，是一代又一代中国共产党人团结带领中国人民不懈奋斗的结果。历经浴血奋战的革命岁月、自力更生的建设时期、开拓进取的改革年代，阔步迈入强国建设新时代，我们党始终把人民放在心中，把使命扛在肩头，在历史前进逻辑中开拓前行，在时代发展潮流中勇毅担当，不懈奋斗、永远奋斗，持之以恒、久久为功，开辟了伟大道路，建立了伟大功业，铸就了伟大精神，积累了宝贵经验，书写了中华民族发展的壮阔史诗。 功崇惟志，业广惟勤。推进中国特色社会主义事业的新长征要持续接力、长期进行，我们每代人都要走好自己的长征路。习主席在重要讲话中提出“五个必须”的明确要求：“必须坚持党的基本理论、基本路线、基本方略”“必须紧紧依靠人民创造历史伟业”“必须积极应对前进道路上的风险挑战”“必须持续推动构建人类命运共同体”“必须持之以恒推进全面从严治党”。“五个必须”的明确要求紧密联系、相互贯通，是我们党105年不懈奋斗历程的深刻启示，是新征程上推进强国建设、民族复兴伟业的科学指引，激励和鼓舞全党全国各族人民坚定信心、团结奋斗、攻坚克难，不断开创中国式现代化建设新局面。 “中国共产党105年的辉煌历史令人自豪，但我们决不能骄傲自满、止步不前。”党的二十大明确提出新时代新征程中国共产党的使命任务，强调要“以中国式现代化全面推进中华民族伟大复兴”。实现社会主义现代化是一个阶梯式递进、不断发展进步的历史过程，我们面临的使命更光荣、任务更艰巨、挑战更严峻、工作更伟大。全党只有保持道不变、志不改的定力，坚持党的全面领导和党中央集中统一领导，不断增强党的政治领导力、思想引领力、群众组织力、社会号召力，充分激发亿万人民的积极性主动性创造性，强化忧患意识、坚持底线思维，坚定决心意志、埋头苦干实干，才能凝聚起推进中国式现代化的磅礴力量，确保中华复兴号巨轮劈波斩浪、行稳致远。 宏图凌云志，勇毅启新程。我军是党缔造和领导的人民军队，党的使命任务决定了人民军队的使命任务。“十五五”时期，我军要如期实现建军一百年奋斗目标，推动基本实现国防和军队现代化取得决定性进展。全军要认真学习贯彻习主席重要讲话精神，认真对照坚定信心、接续奋斗“五个必须”的明确要求，统筹发展和安全、富国和强军，确保国防和军队现代化进程同国家现代化进程相适应，军事能力同国家战略需求相适应，推动我军战略能力加速生成，更好地以强军支撑强国复兴伟业。要坚决贯彻国防和军队现代化新“三步走”战略安排，加快军事理论、军队组织形态、军事人员、武器装备现代化，巩固提高一体化国家战略体系和能力，全力以赴打好实现建军一百年奋斗目标攻坚战，不断开创国防和军队现代化新局面，为以中国式现代化全面推进强国建设、民族复兴伟业提供坚强战略支撑。
