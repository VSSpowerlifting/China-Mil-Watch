Doctrine

Operational Planning Must Make Good Use of Time Resources to Seize the Initiative and Gain First-Mover Advantage on the Future Battlefield

作战筹划要利用好时间资源，在未来战场上占据主动、赢得先机
PLA Daily (解放军报) 19 August 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
A PLA theoretical article, published without byline or institutional attribution, lays out a four-part framework for treating time as an active operational resource across the full planning cycle, organized around four distinct constructs: time windows (fleeting opportunity intervals), time tempo (campaign rhythm), time precision (cross-domain synchronization), and time potential energy (时间势能, the latent combat power accumulated through long-term force building). The article is doctrinal-educational in register rather than operational reporting, and its value is as a record of how PLA planning theory formalizes time competition—particularly the concept of 时间势能, which extends the logic of 'generation-gap advantage' (装备代差优势) into a broader accumulative framework that encompasses training, organizational structure, and theory, not just hardware. What remains unknown is whether this framing originates from a specific PLA research institution, staff college, or individual author, which would sharpen its weight as evidence of emerging doctrine versus individual theorizing.
Translation

War is a contest of strength, and even more a game of time. Within the grand system of operational planning, time is a core resource that can be actively cognized, systematically allocated, and dynamically employed. It runs through the entire process of strategic judgment, campaign planning, and tactical execution, and permeates every link of force organization, battlefield maneuver, and fire strikes. The depth of one's understanding of time resources determines the precision of operational planning; the level of skill in employing time resources affects the realization of operational effectiveness. As the form of modern warfare accelerates its transformation, the tempo of operations quickens unprecedentedly, and competition in the time dimension grows ever more intense. Whoever can more profoundly grasp the inherent laws of time resources and more skillfully apply methods for allocating time resources will be able to seize the initiative and gain first-mover advantage on the future battlefield.

Time resources in operational planning refer to the totality of all time elements that an operational subject can control, regulate, and utilize throughout the entire operational process in order to achieve specific military objectives. They possess such distinct characteristics as irreversibility, uniformity, multi-dimensionality, and composites. In the perspective of operational planning, time resources present a complex structure that is multi-tiered, multi-form, and multi-valued. From time windows, to time tempo, to time precision, and further to time potential energy (时间势能), each dimension of time resources has its unique mode of existence and mechanism of action. It is essential to grasp the essential attributes of these time resources and systematically construct the methodological pathways for employing them, so as to continuously improve the quality and effectiveness of operational planning.

Make good use of the time window as an opportunity-type resource. The so-called time window is the interval of favorable posture formed under specific spatial and temporal conditions by the contradictory movement of war—a product of the interaction of multiple factors such as the balance of forces between the enemy and ourselves and battlefield environmental conditions. It is objective, not subject to human will; it is scarce, not existing at all times; and it is fleeting—once missed, it is difficult to reproduce. Whether operational planning can acutely identify and firmly seize the time window is directly related to the gain or loss of strategic initiative and the direction of the overall war situation. Grasping the time window requires establishing a systematic mechanism for identification, assessment, and exploitation. First, build a time-window assessment system that fuses multi-source intelligence, integrating multi-dimensional information to dynamically monitor the evolution of the strategic situation and precisely anticipate the opening node, duration, and closing conditions of time windows. Second, establish a value assessment model for time windows, conducting quantitative assessments of the value of different time windows from multiple angles—including the balance of forces between the enemy and ourselves, battlefield environmental suitability, and the attainability of operational objectives—to provide a scientific basis for planning decisions. Third, formulate strategies for actively shaping time windows: through strategic deception, force deployment adjustments, and other means, actively create time windows favorable to ourselves, compress the enemy's time windows, and maximize strategic benefit.

Make good use of time tempo as a regulatory resource. Time tempo is the overall "rhythm" formed by the alternation of fast and slow, tension and relaxation in operational actions along the time dimension—the inner "pulse" of the campaign process. It does not advance at a uniform, linear pace, but is a complex system organically composed of multiple tempo forms such as fast and slow, urgent and gradual, advance and halt. Different tempo forms have different operational effectiveness: a fast tempo can generate surprise and disrupt enemy deployments; a slow tempo can accumulate strength and await the opportunity to break the enemy; tempo transitions can create illusions in the enemy's mind and generate combat opportunities. The level of skill in regulating time tempo is an important standard for measuring the quality and effectiveness of operational planning. Regulating time tempo requires establishing scientific mechanisms for tempo design, tempo control, and tempo transition. On one hand, top-level design of tempo must be conducted around the overall operational objective: based on factors such as the division of operational phases, the sequence of force employment, and changes in the battlefield environment, the tempo form for each phase is planned in advance, forming an overall tempo scheme that is orderly in its fast and slow elements and measured in its tension and relaxation. On the other hand, a dynamic regulatory mechanism for tempo must be established: through adjustments to the scale of troop commitment, the intensity of fire strikes, and the rate of logistical support, precise control over operational tempo is achieved, ensuring that the actual tempo can be adjusted in a timely manner in response to battlefield changes, thereby firmly maintaining battlefield initiative.

Make good use of time precision as a coordinative resource. Time precision is the consistency and accuracy of various operational units along the time dimension, and is an important support for tactical coordination. It manifests as the precise alignment of different actions at time nodes, the seamless connection between different forces in time sequence, and the high degree of synchronization of different systems along the time dimension. In informatized and intelligentized (信息化智能化) warfare, the composition of operational forces is increasingly diverse, the space of operational actions continues to expand, and the transition between operational modes becomes more frequent, placing ever-higher demands on time precision. Even minor deviations in time precision can lead to coordination failure and may even cause the overall failure of operational actions. Improving time precision requires building a time synchronization management and control system covering all elements and the full process. First, establish a unified time reference system: relying on high-precision timekeeping means, ensure that all operational units, weapons platforms, and command systems use the same time standard. Second, refine the time-node design for operational actions: decompose operational tasks to specific time points and time periods, and clarify the action sequence and time requirements for each element. Third, improve the verification mechanism for time coordination: when changes in the battlefield situation cause time deviations, rapidly adjust the time parameters of relevant actions to maintain the time precision of overall coordination and ensure that the operational system operates efficiently in accordance with a unified time beat.

Make good use of time potential energy (时间势能) as an accumulative resource. Time potential energy is the latent energy accumulated by operational forces through long-term construction, training, and preparation. The accumulation of time potential energy is the process by which operational forces undergo quantitative change leading to qualitative change (从量变到质变), manifested in the improvement of personnel capabilities and quality, the iterative development of weapons and equipment, the continuous optimization of organizational structure, and the unceasing innovation of operational theory. It is concealed—difficult to directly perceive in peacetime; it is long-term—requiring sustained investment to form; and it can even be decisive—once operational capability achieves a qualitative change, it may exert a major influence on the course of war. Therefore, time potential energy is an important time resource that operational planning must not neglect. At the practical level, accumulating time potential energy requires, first, formulating a scientific long-range plan for force construction: based on national security strategic requirements and the developmental trends in the form of warfare, clarify the direction, priorities, and pathways for accumulating time potential energy, ensuring that all construction efforts advance in the correct direction. Second, establish a normalized training mechanism: through continuous military training and combat-realistic exercises (实战化演练), improve the military quality of officers and soldiers and the combat capability of units, converting time investment into genuine combat power. Third, improve the research, development, and stockpiling system for weapons and equipment: maintain sustained investment in key technological domains, form technological reserves and equipment generation-gap advantages (装备代差优势), and lay the material foundation for releasing time potential energy.

Original Chinese
战争是力量的对抗，更是时间的博弈。在作战筹划的宏大体系中，时间是一种可以被主动认知、系统配置、能动运用的核心资源。它贯穿于战略判断、战役谋划、战术执行的全过程，渗透于力量编组、战场机动、火力打击的各环节。对时间资源的认知深度，决定着作战筹划的精度；对时间资源的运用水平，影响着作战效能的发挥。现代战争形态加速嬗变，作战节奏空前加快，时间维度的竞争日趋激烈，谁能更深刻地把握时间资源的内在规律，更娴熟地运用时间资源的配置方法，谁就能在未来战场上占据主动、赢得先机。 作战筹划中的时间资源，是指作战主体为达成特定军事目的，在作战全流程中可以支配、调控和利用的一切时间要素的总和，它具有不可逆性、均等性、多维性和复合性等鲜明特征。在作战筹划视野中，时间资源呈现出多层级、多形态、多价值的复杂结构。从时间窗口，到时间节奏，再到时间精度，乃至时间势能，每一维度的时间资源都有其独特的存在形态和作用机理。必须把握这些时间资源的本质属性，系统构建运用时间资源的方法路径，从而不断提高作战筹划质效。 利用好时间窗口这一机遇性资源。所谓时间窗口，是战争矛盾运动在特定时空条件下形成的有利态势区间，是敌我力量对比、战场环境条件等多重因素交互作用的产物。它具有客观性，不以人的意志为转移；具有稀缺性，并非随时存在；具有易逝性，一旦错过便难以复现。作战筹划能否敏锐识别并牢牢把握时间窗口，直接关系到战略主动的得失和战争全局的走向。把握时间窗口需要建立系统的识别、评估和利用机制。一是构建多源情报融合的时间窗口研判体系，综合多维度信息，动态监测战略态势演变，精准预判时间窗口的开启节点、持续时长和关闭条件。二是建立时间窗口的价值评估模型，从敌我力量对比、战场环境适配、作战目标可达成等多个角度，对不同时间窗口的价值进行量化评估，为筹划决策提供科学依据。三是制定时间窗口的主动塑造策略，通过战略欺骗、力量部署调整等多种手段，主动创造有利于我的时间窗口，压缩敌方的时间窗口，实现战略效益的最大化。 利用好时间节奏这一调控性资源。时间节奏是作战行动在时间维度上的快慢交替、张弛变化所形成的整体“韵律”，是战役进程的内在“脉搏”。它不是匀速线性地推进，而是由快与慢、急与缓、进与停等多种节奏形态有机组合而成的复杂系统。不同的节奏形态有着不同的作战效能：快节奏能够形成突然性，打乱敌方部署；慢节奏能够积蓄力量，待机破敌；节奏转换能够制造敌方错觉，创造战机。时间节奏的调控水平，是衡量作战筹划质效的重要标准。调控时间节奏需要建立科学的节奏设计、节奏控制和节奏转换机制。一方面要围绕作战总目标进行节奏的顶层设计，根据作战阶段划分、力量使用顺序、战场环境变化等因素，预先规划各阶段的节奏形态，形成快慢有序、张弛有度的整体节奏方案。另一方面要建立节奏的动态调控机制，通过对兵力投入规模、火力打击强度、后勤保障速率等要素的调节，实现对作战节奏的精准控制，确保实际节奏能够根据战场变化及时调整，从而牢牢掌握战场主动权。 利用好时间精度这一协同性资源。时间精度是各作战单元在时间维度上的一致性和准确性，是战术协同的重要支撑。它体现为不同行动在时间节点上的精确吻合，不同力量之间在时间序列上的无缝衔接，不同体系在时间维度上的高度同步。在信息化智能化战争中，作战力量构成日益多元、作战行动空间不断拓展、作战样式转换更加频繁，对时间精度的要求越来越高。时间精度的微小偏差，都可能导致协同失效，甚至造成作战行动的整体失败。提升时间精度需要构建全要素、全流程的时间同步管控体系。一是要建立统一的时间基准系统，依托高精度授时手段，确保所有作战单元、武器平台、指挥系统使用同一时间标准。二是要细化作战行动的时间节点设计，将作战任务分解到具体的时间点和时间段，明确各要素的行动时序和时间要求。三是要完善时间协同的校验机制，当战场情况变化导致时间偏差时，能够迅速调整相关行动的时间参数，保持整体协同的时间精度，确保作战体系按照统一的时间节拍高效运转。 利用好时间势能这一积累性资源。时间势能是作战力量经过长期建设、训练和准备所积累形成的潜在能量。时间势能的积累是作战力量从量变到质变的过程，体现在人员能力素质的提升、武器装备的迭代发展、体制编制的持续优化和作战理论的不断创新等方面。它具有隐蔽性，平时难以直接感知；具有长期性，需要持续投入才能形成；甚至具有决定性，一旦作战能力达成质变，就可能对战争进程产生重大影响。因此，时间势能是作战筹划不容忽视的重要时间资源。在实践层面，积累时间势能首先要制定科学的力量建设长远规划，根据国家安全战略需求和战争形态发展趋势，明确时间势能积累的方向、重点和路径，确保各项建设沿着正确方向推进。其次要建立常态化的训练机制，通过持续不断的军事训练和实战化演练，提升官兵的军事素养和部队的作战能力，将时间投入转化为实实在在的战斗力。再次要完善武器装备的研发和储备体系，在关键技术领域保持持续投入，形成技术储备和装备代差优势，为释放时间势能奠定物质基础。
