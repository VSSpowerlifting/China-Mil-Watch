Chinese Military on the World Stage | Twenty Years in Lebanon: China's Blue Helmets Guard Peace Through Hard Work

走向世界舞台的中国军队丨赴黎20载，中国蓝盔以实干守卫和平
PLA Daily (解放军报) 6 August 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
The multi-functional engineer detachment of China's 24th peacekeeping contingent to Lebanon held a themed event marking the 20th anniversary of PLA deployments to UNIFIL, at which UNIFIL Force Commander Aroldo Lázaro Sáenz praised Chinese contributions; over 24 rotations since 2006, more than 8,800 Chinese personnel have cleared over two million square meters of minefields, destroyed more than 18,000 mines and unexploded ordnance, repaired 320 kilometers of roads, and provided medical treatment to over 90,000 people. This is a commemorative feature in official military media; its value is as a record of how the PLA frames its UNIFIL mission to domestic and international audiences—emphasizing operational metrics, sacrifice (military observer Du Zhaoyu, killed July 25, 2006), and host-nation goodwill—rather than as evidence of a changed force posture or new capability.

Twenty Years in Lebanon: China's Blue Helmets Guard Peace Through Hard Work

■ Ma Bo, Qu Fatong

Soldiers of the multi-functional engineer detachment of China's 24th peacekeeping contingent to Lebanon exchange mine-clearance and explosive ordnance disposal experience with Italian peacekeeping counterparts. Photo by Qu Fatong

This year marks the 20th anniversary of China's Blue Helmets deploying to Lebanon for peacekeeping operations. The smoke of the Lebanon-Israel conflict has yet to fully clear, and risks remain. In the mission area of southern Lebanon, Chinese peacekeeping officers and soldiers have held the line for peace without wavering, faithfully fulfilling their responsibilities and obligations.

Since the first Chinese peacekeeping contingent deployed to Lebanon in 2006, more than 8,800 Chinese peacekeeping officers and soldiers have taken up the mission in succession. Over 20 years, China's Blue Helmets have faced the threat of combat without fear, ventured into life-and-death minefields, cumulatively cleared more than 2 million square meters of minefields, destroyed more than 18,000 mines and various types of unexploded ordnance, and repaired 320 kilometers of roads. They have been praised by the United Nations Mine Action Service as "the best demining team." Officers and soldiers have gone deep into local military camps, communities, and schools to carry out humanitarian assistance, cumulatively providing medical treatment to more than 90,000 people, performing more than 2,500 surgical procedures, and donating more than 100,000 items of supplies to protect lives and health. They have been warmly called "the most beloved Eastern friends" by the Lebanese people.

Shebli, principal of Qurela Middle School in Tyre, said: "The Chinese peacekeeping force has taken root in southern Lebanon, protecting the safety of local residents year-round, and has brought stability, security, and hope to this land that has endured the ravages of war."

At the themed event recently held by China's 24th peacekeeping contingent to Lebanon to celebrate the 99th founding anniversary of the People's Liberation Army and the 20th anniversary of peacekeeping deployment to Lebanon, UNIFIL Force Commander Aroldo Lázaro Sáenz highly affirmed the contributions made by China's peacekeeping forces. He stated that over 20 years, Chinese peacekeeping officers and soldiers have built roads, cleared mines and explosive ordnance, assisted communities, and served the people, earning the trust and respect of the people of southern Lebanon through hard work, and are an indispensable pillar of UNIFIL.

Lebanon has long been known as the "powder keg of the Middle East." The situation in the mission area is volatile and conflicts occur frequently, and peacekeeping missions are often a matter of life and death. Casting our minds back 20 years—on July 25, 2006, Chinese military observer Du Zhaoyu was killed in an airstrike while carrying out his mission at a military observation post at the tri-border junction of Lebanon, Israel, and Syria. Four United Nations military observers, including him, perished, their young lives forever inscribed on this land scarred by war. Twenty years of wind and rain have passed, and the smoke of war has cycled through several times. The oath written in blood and life by Martyr Du Zhaoyu has inspired generation after generation of peacekeeping officers and soldiers to press forward without fear of sacrifice, shouldering their duty with dedication.

On March 30 of this year, an emergency suddenly arose in the mission area: an Indonesian peacekeeping convoy was attacked by an unidentified explosive device, leaving two peacekeeping personnel critically wounded with life-threatening injuries. Upon receiving the rescue order, Zeng Lihong, a military physician with the medical detachment of China's 24th peacekeeping contingent to Lebanon, rushed to the scene at once. The accident site was a scene of mangled flesh and blood, permeated with the smell of blood. Zeng Lihong steadied her nerves, working with her team to rapidly measure vital signs, establish intravenous access, debride wounds and stop bleeding, while bending down to comfort the wounded. After emergency treatment, both critically injured personnel ultimately pulled through. When she removed her protective gloves, Zeng Lihong's fingertips trembled uncontrollably, and her uniform was soaked through with sweat.

Beyond fulfilling their peacekeeping duties, the officers and soldiers have also taken the initiative to build cultural bridges between China and other countries, bringing outstanding traditional Chinese culture—including guqin (古筝), martial arts, and traditional dance—into local schools and allied military camps, using the warmth of culture to dispel the shadow of war.

During one external duty patrol, a Chinese peacekeeping convoy was making its way through narrow streets and alleys when it was blocked by vehicles parked on the roadside. Upon seeing the Five-Starred Red Flag, local residents came forward of their own accord to move their vehicles and clear the road. Some residents stood to the side waving and calling out "China, China!" in Chinese. That recognition made the officers and soldiers feel the responsibility and honor of peacekeeping.

Li Gang, commander of China's 24th peacekeeping contingent to Lebanon, stated that since the beginning of this year, conflicts in the mission area have been unceasing and the situation has remained persistently tense, yet all officers and soldiers have consistently risen to the challenge and held their posts, standing shoulder to shoulder with peacekeeping counterparts from various countries to fulfill their duties and jointly safeguard peace. "Over 20 years, generation after generation of Chinese peacekeeping officers and soldiers have set out for southern Lebanon, far from their homeland, charging at the front lines, and practicing the original aspiration of peacekeeping through steadfast dedication."

赴黎20载，中国蓝盔以实干守卫和平 ■马 波 瞿发统 中国第24批赴黎维和部队多功能工兵分队官兵与意大利维和友军进行扫雷排爆经验交流。瞿发统 摄 今年是中国蓝盔赴黎巴嫩维和20周年。当前黎以冲突硝烟未散，风险仍存。在黎南部任务区，中国维和官兵始终为和平坚守，忠实履行责任担当。 自2006年首批赴黎巴嫩维和部队部署以来，8800余名中国维和官兵接力出征。20年来，中国蓝盔不惧战火威胁、勇闯生死雷场，累计清排雷场200余万平方米，销毁地雷及各类未爆物1.8万余枚，修复道路320公里，被联合国地雷行动中心称赞为“最好的扫雷队伍”。官兵们深入当地军营、社区、学校开展人道主义援助，累计实施医疗救治9万余人次，实施手术2500余台次，捐赠物资10万余件，守护生命健康，被黎民众亲切称为“最可爱的东方朋友”。 提尔市库雷拉中学校长谢卜利说：“中国维和部队扎根黎巴嫩南部，常年守护当地民众安全，为这片历经战火的土地带来了稳定、安全与希望。” 在日前举办的中国第24批赴黎巴嫩维和部队庆祝中国人民解放军建军99周年暨赴黎维和20周年主题活动中，联黎部队司令迪奥达托·阿巴尼亚拉高度肯定中国维和力量作出的贡献。他表示，20年来，中国维和官兵修建道路、扫雷排爆、援助社区、服务民众，用实干赢得黎南部人民的信任与尊重，是联黎部队不可或缺的支柱力量。 黎巴嫩素有“中东火药桶”之称，任务区局势动荡、冲突频发，维和任务时常面临生死考验。时间回溯到20年前——2006年7月25日，中国军事观察员杜照宇在黎巴嫩、以色列、叙利亚三国交界的一处军事观察哨所执行任务时遭到空袭，包括他在内的4名联合国军事观察员不幸牺牲，年轻的生命永远镌刻在这片饱经战火的土地上。20载风雨流转，几度硝烟轮回。杜照宇烈士用鲜血和生命写下的誓言，激励着一批又一批维和官兵不畏牺牲、担当奉献。 今年3月30日，任务区突发险情，印度尼西亚维和部队车队遭不明爆炸物袭击，2名维和人员身负重伤，生命垂危。接到救援指令后，中国第24批赴黎维和部队医疗分队军医曾丽红火速驰援。事故现场血肉模糊、血腥味弥漫，曾丽红稳住心神，配合团队快速测量生命体征、建立静脉通路、清创止血，同时俯身安抚伤员。经过抢救，2名重伤员最终脱险。脱下防护手套时，曾丽红指尖止不住地颤抖，制服也被汗水浸透。 履行维和职责之外，官兵们还主动搭建中外文化桥梁，将古筝、武术、传统舞蹈等中华优秀传统文化带入当地校园、友军营地，用文化的温度驱散战争阴霾。 一次外出执勤时，中方维和车队穿行在狭窄街巷，被路边占道车辆阻碍通行。当地民众看到五星红旗，主动上前挪车、疏通道路。还有民众在一旁挥手，用中文呼喊着“中国、中国！”，这份认可让官兵们感受到维和的责任与荣光。 中国第24批赴黎维和部队指挥长李岗表示，今年以来任务区冲突不断、局势持续紧张，全体官兵始终迎难而上、坚守战位，与各国维和友军并肩履职、共护安宁。“20年来，一代代中国维和官兵奔赴黎南，远离故土，冲锋在一线，用坚守与奉献践行维和初心。”
