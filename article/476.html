Summary
Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin issued a joint statement in Beijing on May 20, 2026, committing to expand joint military exercises, joint maritime and air patrols, and cooperation on military AI within bilateral and multilateral frameworks, while renewing the 2001 Treaty of Good-Neighborliness and Friendly Cooperation under Article 25. The statement documents the breadth of institutionalized coordination the two sides now claim: from BeiDou-GLONASS interoperability and satellite internet cooperation to shared positions on the U.S. 'Golden Dome' missile defense program, nuclear arms control, and the Korean Peninsula. The framing on Ukraine — Russia 'positively evaluates' China's 'objective and impartial position' — stops well short of endorsing Russian military operations and leaves unresolved how far China's 'constructive role' in any settlement process extends in practice.
The joint statement contains an explicit, named Chinese endorsement of Russia's 'Eurasian security architecture' initiative, a formal Chinese condemnation of the U.S. 'Golden Dome' missile defense program by name, a commitment to coordinate military AI applications within CCW frameworks, and Russian support for Chinese positions on the South China Sea and Taiwan—collectively representing a documented elevation of strategic alignment with direct implications for U.S. force posture and arms control policy.

Xinhua News Agency, Beijing, May 20 — Joint Statement of the People's Republic of China and the Russian Federation on Further Strengthening Comprehensive Strategic Coordination and Deepening Good-Neighborly Friendship and Cooperation

At the invitation of President Xi Jinping of the People's Republic of China, President Vladimir Putin of the Russian Federation paid a state visit to the People's Republic of China from May 19 to 20, 2026. The two heads of state held formal talks in Beijing and jointly attended the opening ceremony of the 2026–2027 China-Russia Year of Education. Premier Li Qiang of the State Council of the People's Republic of China met with President Putin of Russia.

The People's Republic of China and the Russian Federation (hereinafter referred to as "the two sides") declare as follows:

I

This year marks the 30th anniversary of the two sides' declaration of their resolve to develop a strategic cooperative partnership of equality and trust oriented toward the 21st century, and the 25th anniversary of the signing of the Treaty of Good-Neighborliness and Friendly Cooperation Between the People's Republic of China and the Russian Federation.

The Treaty of Good-Neighborliness and Friendly Cooperation Between China and Russia inherits and carries forward the long-standing tradition of Sino-Russian exchanges, adheres to universally recognized principles and norms of international law, and lays a long-term legal foundation for the development of contemporary China-Russia relations. It not only fully embodies the time-honored good-neighborly and friendly relations between the two peoples and their desire to develop friendship from generation to generation, but is also a concrete manifestation of the common values of all humanity — peace, development, fairness, justice, democracy, and freedom.

The fundamental principles of bilateral cooperation enshrined in the Treaty of Good-Neighborliness and Friendly Cooperation have stood the test of time and remain of great practical significance today. On the basis of the principles of mutual respect for sovereignty and territorial integrity, mutual non-aggression, mutual non-interference in internal affairs, equality and mutual benefit, and peaceful coexistence, China and Russia support each other's right to independently choose their social systems and development paths, mutually support each other in safeguarding historical and cultural identity and traditional values, and the bilateral relationship is characterized by non-alliance, non-confrontation, and non-targeting of third countries.

The model of bilateral relations development established in the China-Russia Joint Statement of April 25, 1996, and in the Treaty of Good-Neighborliness and Friendly Cooperation of July 16, 2001, has become the cornerstone of the China-Russia Comprehensive Strategic Partnership of Coordination for a New Era. Through the unremitting efforts of both sides, this relationship has reached its highest historical level and continues to advance, demonstrating mature characteristics, possessing endogenous momentum, free from any external influence; its stability and comprehensiveness fully serve the fundamental interests of the two countries and their peoples, serve the respective overall national development objectives of China and Russia, and make an important contribution to promoting a just multipolar world and the democratization of international relations.

The two sides agree to extend the Treaty of Good-Neighborliness and Friendly Cooperation in accordance with its Article 25, and will continue to abide by the principles and spirit of the Treaty and of the Joint Statement of the People's Republic of China and the Russian Federation on the 20th Anniversary of the Signing of the Treaty of Good-Neighborliness and Friendly Cooperation Between China and Russia issued on June 28, 2021, and will promote the continued development of bilateral relations to a higher level and of higher quality.

II

The two sides will take head-of-state diplomacy as the guide, comprehensively implement the important consensus reached by the two heads of state, continue to maintain close high-level exchanges, and ensure the efficient and smooth operation of mechanisms for exchanges between governments, legislative bodies, and political parties.

The two sides will continue to consolidate the traditional friendship between the two militaries, enhance mutual trust in the military sphere, improve cooperation mechanisms, expand joint exercises and training and joint maritime and air patrols, strengthen coordination and cooperation within bilateral and multilateral frameworks, jointly address various risks and challenges, and jointly safeguard global and regional security and stability.

The two sides will jointly combat the smuggling of narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances and their precursors and weapons, cybercrime, transnational organized crime, economic crime, cross-border corruption, and illegal immigration, and will strengthen cooperation in law enforcement capacity building.

The two sides will continue to strengthen bilateral cooperation in combating money laundering and terrorist financing through the exchange of financial intelligence, and will maintain cooperation within multilateral anti-money laundering frameworks. They will strengthen young people's awareness of and vigilance against the risks of money laundering and its predicate offenses, safeguarding the future security of both countries.

The two sides are willing to deepen comprehensive exchanges and cooperation in the field of emergency management, strengthen cooperation in disaster prevention, mitigation, and relief, and carry out cooperation in the prevention and control of work safety risks.

The two sides emphasize the need to protect memorial facilities for martyrs who died in the Second World War on each other's territory, and will continue to cooperate on the protection and management of memorial facilities, jointly advancing the search and excavation of the remains of martyrs and missing persons, commemorating and honoring heroes and martyrs, and improving the relevant legal and regulatory foundations.

The two sides highly commend the high-quality development maintained in bilateral trade and economic cooperation in recent years and its positive contribution to improving the well-being of the two peoples. The two sides will continue to strengthen communication and exchanges on trade and economic policies, consolidate cooperation in key areas, explore new growth points, promote the upgrading of goods trade and services trade, and resolutely defend the right to independently develop bilateral trade and economic partnership.

The two sides will vigorously develop cooperation in the fields of automobiles, shipbuilding, and civil aviation industry, enhance the level of cooperation, and, in the spirit of inclusiveness, and taking into account their respective domestic laws and national development priorities, will continuously expand cooperation in digital economy, artificial intelligence and other information and communications technologies, cross-border e-commerce, and the extraction of relevant minerals, jointly promote the implementation of key cooperation projects, and further unleash the potential of China-Russia cooperation.

The two sides support the implementation of projects in the chemical and metallurgical sectors, and have agreed to actively promote cooperation in areas such as joint mineral development and green standards.

The two sides note that investment cooperation between the two countries is conducted on the basis of equality and mutual benefit, facilitating the implementation of major investment cooperation projects, safeguarding the legitimate rights and interests of investors, and ensuring a fair investment environment. Bilateral investment cooperation is showing a positive trend, achieving high-level development and continuing to advance. The two sides are willing to continue to promote the improvement of quality and efficiency of investment cooperation, support the work of the China-Russia Investment Cooperation Committee, and better leverage the role of the Committee's Secretariat in implementing the various consensuses reached.

The two sides will fulfill the obligations stipulated in the Agreement Between the Government of the People's Republic of China and the Government of the Russian Federation on the Promotion and Mutual Protection of Investments signed on May 8, 2025, continuously optimize the business environment, enhance the level of investment facilitation and protection, and safeguard the security and stability of industrial and supply chains.

The two sides note the important significance of the China-Russia Investment Cooperation Planning Outline. In order to enhance the level of bilateral investment cooperation, the two sides are willing to increase efforts to support investment cooperation in the key areas listed in the Outline.

The two sides highly commend the fruitful results achieved in China-Russia energy cooperation, and have agreed to continue to consolidate and develop a comprehensive energy cooperation partnership, support enterprises in deepening mutually beneficial cooperation in oil and gas, coal, civilian nuclear energy, and renewable energy including green electricity certificates, ensure the safe and stable operation of cross-border energy infrastructure, facilitate the smooth transportation of energy, promote expanded dialogue between energy-producing and energy-consuming countries, jointly maintain the stability of global energy markets, and enhance energy security on the basis of the principle of energy justice.

The two sides will continue to advance the construction projects of Tianwan Nuclear Power Plant and Xudabao Nuclear Power Plant, ensuring their timely completion and commissioning. On this basis, they will deepen cooperation in the peaceful use of nuclear energy, progressively advance cooperation in thermonuclear fusion, fast reactors, and closed nuclear fuel cycles, and, on the basis of the principles of mutual benefit and balanced interests, explore cooperation in the front end of the nuclear fuel cycle and in jointly building nuclear power projects through a "package" approach.

The two sides will continue to develop cooperation in the financial sector between the two countries, leverage the potential of the Financial Cooperation Subcommittee of the China-Russia Prime Ministers' Regular Meeting Committee, the China-Russia Finance Ministers' Dialogue, and other relevant sectoral cooperation mechanisms, maintain the progress achieved in local currency settlement, steadily advance practical cooperation between China and Russia in the banking sector and in capital markets, better serve the healthy and stable development of the two countries' economies and capital markets, and deepen cooperation in customs, taxation, and other areas.

The two sides will continue to advance bilateral and multilateral financial cooperation processes, strengthen communication and coordination on macroeconomic policies, achieve sustainable economic development, and effectively respond to global economic challenges.

The two sides are willing to comprehensively strengthen customs cooperation, actively carry out exchanges and mutual learning in smart customs cooperation, continuously enhance customs governance capacity, and continuously optimize regulatory services; deepen cooperation on "single window" interconnection and interoperability, facilitate bilateral trade, and continue to actively cooperate on bilateral information exchange issues.

The two sides are willing to take multiple measures to ensure the smooth operation of China-Russia border ports, actively carry out smart port cooperation, continuously accelerate the modernization of port infrastructure, simultaneously enhance the efficiency of two-way border crossing and customs clearance at ports, and comprehensively improve port interconnectivity. They will accelerate the construction of the road passenger and cargo port on Heixiazi Island (Bolshoy Ussuriysky Island).

The two sides are willing to continue to strengthen cooperation in the field of air transport between the two countries, and encourage airlines of both sides to increase more passenger and cargo routes and flights in accordance with the aviation rights arrangements of the two countries and based on market demand.

The two sides reaffirm that, in the spirit of Article 9 of the Agreement Between the People's Republic of China and the Union of Soviet Socialist Republics on the Eastern Section of the Sino-Soviet State Boundary of May 16, 1991, they will continue to carry out trilateral consultations with the Democratic People's Republic of Korea on the issue of access to the sea via the Tumen River; and, in the spirit of the Protocol Between the Government of the People's Republic of China and the Government of the Russian Federation on the Navigation of Chinese and Russian Vessels in the Waters Surrounding the Heixiazi Island Area (Tarabarov Island and Bolshoy Ussuriysky Island Area) of October 14, 2004, they will expedite bilateral negotiations on the draft navigation agreement for the relevant waters.

The two sides are willing to strengthen the construction of China-Russia cross-border transportation infrastructure and improve the efficiency of cross-border passenger and cargo transport. They will continue to organize cross-border cargo transport using unmanned driving technology. The two sides emphasize the important role of cooperation in the transportation sector, and will exchange experience on the legal regulation of the use of unmanned technologies in air, land, and maritime autonomous navigation.

The two sides will ensure the unobstructed operation of China-Russia cross-border corridor routes, strengthen mutually beneficial cooperation on China-Europe freight trains, maintain stable freight rates, and continue to support China-Europe rail, road, and maritime cargo transport transiting Russia. They will continuously advance practical cooperation in the development and utilization of the Northern Sea Route aimed at increasing cargo volumes and its overall development.

The two sides are willing to expand rail freight volumes for imports and exports between China and Russia and transit to third countries. The two sides will simultaneously strengthen the construction of railway border station infrastructure and rear corridor routes, build smooth, stable, and sustainably healthy railway logistics corridors, and jointly ensure the safety and efficiency of cargo transport.

The two sides are willing to deepen cooperation in the construction of cross-border toll road bridges, such as the Heihe-Blagoveshchensk road bridge. The two sides are willing to continue to develop the Europe-Western China international transport corridor and promote road interconnectivity among Russia, China, and Kazakhstan.

The two sides highly commend the prospects for further deepening the overall coordination of the trilateral mechanism with the participation of Mongolia and the China-Mongolia-Russia Economic Corridor, and will actively promote the implementation of key jointly-built projects and cooperation under the framework of this corridor. The two sides agree to provide assistance for Mongolia's accession to regional integration processes including the Shanghai Cooperation Organization.

The two sides are willing to deepen cooperation in agriculture and forestry, further strengthen investment cooperation in the agricultural sector, and accelerate the cooperative establishment of a China-Russia Agricultural Cooperation Experimental Demonstration Zone in the Russian Far East, as a pilot project for the implementation of Russia's International Priority Development Area policy.

Deepening cooperation in the agricultural sector serves the economic interests of China and Russia and helps enhance food security in both countries. On the premise of ensuring safety and on the basis of risk assessment, the two sides will jointly commit to comprehensively expanding the categories and quantities of meat products supplied from disease-free zones in Russia to China, including cattle and pork by-products, jointly promote the resumption of exports to China of products from qualified Russian poultry enterprises, and deepen two-way trade in pet food between China and Russia. The two sides look forward to China providing a richer variety of animal and plant products to Russia, jointly promoting the export to Russia of products from qualified Chinese poultry enterprises, and further expanding the number of registered Chinese aquatic product enterprises exporting to Russia.

The two sides will further strengthen cooperation on the protection of wild animals such as Siberian tigers, Amur leopards, giant pandas, golden monkeys, and migratory birds, and strengthen exchanges on experience in nature reserve management.

The two sides have agreed to continue implementing major projects under national space programs that serve the common interests of China and Russia, actively advance cooperation in key areas of space including the International Lunar Research Station and lunar and deep-space exploration, and comprehensively enhance the level and quality of cooperation.

The two sides have decided to continuously deepen mutually beneficial exchanges in the field of satellite navigation, actively promote the implementation of the China-Russia Satellite Navigation Cooperation Roadmap for 2026–2030, and ensure the complementarity of the BeiDou Navigation Satellite System and the GLONASS Global Navigation Satellite System for the benefit of users worldwide.

The two sides are willing to continuously strengthen cooperation in the coordination and use of radio frequencies and satellite frequency-orbit resources between China and Russia, and continuously deepen exchanges and cooperation between China and Russia in the fields of satellite internet and the Internet of Things.

The two sides have agreed to explore the establishment of a software cooperation mechanism to jointly empower digital infrastructure construction. They will deepen cooperation and exchanges in the open-source field, promote the industrialization of high-quality open-source achievements in key industries, and achieve common development in the open-source field of the two countries.

The two sides have agreed to strengthen close cooperation in the fields of environmental protection and water resources. They will continuously deepen cooperation in hydrological flood reporting and flood control for transboundary waters, and ensure flood control security in the border areas of the two countries. They will continue to deepen practical cooperation in the protection of transboundary water quality, maintain cooperation on emergency liaison for sudden ecological and environmental incidents, promote the construction of a transboundary nature reserve network, protect biodiversity, strengthen cooperation in waste management, and continue to promote nature conservation cooperation under the frameworks of the G20, BRICS, the Shanghai Cooperation Organization, and the Northeast Asia Sub-regional Environmental Cooperation Program.

The two sides recognize the important role of standardization cooperation in promoting technological progress and trade facilitation between the two countries, and will further deepen standardization cooperation in key areas, including promoting mutual recognition of standards in automobiles, children's products, organic products, and other fields, and establishing a long-term information exchange and cooperation mechanism in the field of conformity assessment.

The two sides have agreed to continuously deepen exchanges and cooperation in intellectual property, provide higher-quality services to applicants and rights holders in both countries, and jointly create a favorable business and rule-of-law environment. They will promote the exchange of experience and sharing of practices in intellectual property protection in the context of the development of the digital economy and artificial intelligence technologies.

The two sides emphasize the importance of strengthening cooperation between China and Russia in the field of anti-monopoly policy. Under the practical cooperation mechanism in the law enforcement field, they will effectively expose and stop violations of anti-monopoly laws with cross-border characteristics. The two sides are willing to promote the implementation of the intergovernmental agreement between the two countries on fair competition and anti-monopoly, safeguarding the interests of the peoples of both countries.

The two sides are willing to deepen cooperation in the field of consumer rights protection, and jointly take measures to ensure an appropriate level thereof, including in the areas of cross-border procurement services, e-commerce, and tourism services.

The two sides consider metrology to be an important foundation for safeguarding international trade exchanges. Through the annual China-Russia Metrology Cooperation Working Group meetings, the two sides have achieved practical cooperation results in metrology fields such as energy and medical health. The two sides are willing to continue to carry out experience exchanges and mutual visits of technical professionals in the field of metrology, and have agreed to continue to carry out joint comparisons of national metrology standards in areas of common interest. The two sides are willing to continue to deepen exchanges and cooperation in the field of metrology.

The two sides are willing to promote the deepening of cooperation in the field of housing and urban-rural development, carry out joint research on "good housing" in cold regions, and strengthen mutual learning in professional fields such as "urban infrastructure lifeline safety engineering," urban renewal, and the application of digital and artificial intelligence technologies in the construction industry.

The two sides attach great importance to and welcome the positive development of cooperation between China's northeastern region and Russia's Far East, and between China's middle and upper Yangtze River region and Russia's Volga Federal District, in the fields of trade and economics, investment, and people-to-people exchanges, and support the strengthening of exchanges and expansion of cooperation between local governments and border areas of the two countries. The Chinese side welcomes the Russian side's policy promotion of International Priority Development Areas.

The two sides have agreed to deepen practical cooperation between China and the Eurasian Economic Union under the framework of the Trade and Economic Cooperation Agreement Between China and the Eurasian Economic Union, and to continuously advance the alignment of the Belt and Road Initiative with the development plans of the Eurasian Economic Union in the fields of transportation, logistics, transit, digitalization, e-commerce, policy coordination and elimination of trade barriers, food, and agricultural product trade. The two sides consider this dialogue mechanism to be crucial for promoting trade cooperation, conducive to deepening economic integration and connectivity in the Asia-Pacific and Eurasian regions, and to promoting the common development of the Belt and Road Initiative and the Greater Eurasian Partnership. The two sides are willing to further upgrade and expand the institutional arrangements for trade and economic cooperation between the Chinese side and the Eurasian Economic Union and its member states, and raise the level of trade liberalization and facilitation.

III

The two sides uphold cultural and civilizational diversity, support each other in protecting cultural and historical identity and traditional moral values, firmly safeguard cultural sovereignty, do not impose their own values and models on others, and do not engage in ideological confrontation.

The two sides believe that the role of UNESCO as a universal intergovernmental platform for people-to-people exchanges should be further strengthened, to promote mutually respectful professional dialogue, facilitate efficient communication among member states, and enhance solidarity.

The two sides unanimously agree that people-to-people cooperation is an important foundation of public opinion and a spiritual bond in China-Russia bilateral relations, and that through educational connectivity, cultural integration, and youth interaction, the mutual identification of the peoples of both sides is deepened, laying a solid social foundation for political mutual trust and trade and economic cooperation.

The two sides fully affirm the results of cooperation in the field of education, and have agreed to further promote the establishment of partnerships between Chinese and Russian educational institutions, jointly hold activities, and promote exchanges of experience and good practices among educators of the two countries.

The two sides will hold the "China-Russia Year of Education" with high quality, advance practical cooperation in various fields, and deepen people-to-people exchanges. They will further promote the establishment of cooperative partnerships between Chinese and Russian universities and scientific research institutions, and facilitate the exchange of experience and practices in the educational field between the two countries. They will continue to expand the scale of two-way student exchanges, and strengthen the joint cultivation of high-level talent at universities of both countries. They will continue to build the China-Russia Institute of Basic Science Research, and support the joint establishment of a China-Russia Innovation Research Institute by universities of both sides. They will support the construction of the Alliance of Similar Universities of China and Russia, the China-Russia Middle School Alliance, and the China-Russia Vocational Education Alliance. They will continue to promote the teaching of each other's national language in their own countries, promote student exchanges between China and Russia, carry out activities such as China-Russia student summer schools and exchanges between Chinese and Russian primary and secondary school students in basic academic disciplines, enhance mutual understanding and trust among young students, strengthen communication and cooperation in the field of education within multilateral frameworks, and jointly strengthen international influence in the field of education.

The two sides believe that deepening cooperation in scientific and technological innovation is of important strategic significance for the two countries to enhance their development momentum and international competitiveness. The two sides will further consolidate the foundation of cooperation in basic and applied sciences and frontier technology fields, and promote high-level cooperative research. They will regularly carry out the selection of joint scientific research projects and continuously improve funding mechanisms. They will actively raise the level of mutual openness of major scientific facilities and carry out collaborative research. They will continue to strengthen scientific and technological exchanges and exchanges and joint cultivation of young scientists. They will deepen scientific and technological cooperation within multilateral frameworks such as BRICS, the Shanghai Cooperation Organization, APEC, and the G20, and jointly enhance and tap the potential of scientific and technological innovation in promoting the economic and social development of the two countries.

The two sides highly commend the fruitful results achieved by the two countries in the fields of performing arts, museums, libraries, radio and television, and arts education under the framework of the 2024–2025 China-Russia Year of Culture, and will continuously expand the coverage of cooperation. They support the holding of the 14th China "Russian Culture Festival" and the 17th Russia "Chinese Culture Festival," the 4th China-Russia Library Forum, the 16th China-Russia Cultural Fair, the "Happy Chinese New Year" events in Moscow and other Russian cities, and the "Maslenitsa" (farewell to winter festival) events in Beijing and other Chinese cities. They will give full play to the platform role of the cultural centers established by the two countries in each other's territory, and carry out practical cooperation in exhibitions and performances, language teaching, cultural and tourism promotion, and youth and local exchanges.

The two sides have agreed to further deepen China-Russia film exchanges and cooperation. Under the framework of the Film Cooperation Subcommittee of the China-Russia Humanities Cooperation Committee, they will support the annual holding of film festivals in each other's countries, make full use of platforms such as the Shanghai International Film Festival, the Beijing International Film Festival, the Silk Road International Film Festival, and Russia's Moscow International Film Festival, "Message to Man," and "Soul of Fire" international film festivals, and strengthen exchanges of films and personnel. The two sides will commit to expanding the scale of co-productions, promote the establishment and deepening of ties between film institutions and personnel of the two countries, and continue to implement the Action Plan for 2030 on Cooperative Film Production Between the National Film Administration of the People's Republic of China and the Ministry of Culture of the Russian Federation, jointly approved by the China National Film Administration and the Russian Ministry of Culture.

The two sides fully affirm the results of cooperation in the field of health between the two countries, and express satisfaction with the China-Russia Disaster Medicine Tactical Special Exercise and the International Symposium on Disaster Medicine held in 2026. The two sides will continue to advance cooperation in disaster medicine, biosafety, infectious disease prevention and control and cross-border transmission, medical education, the China-Russia Medical Universities Alliance, medical science and innovation; oncology and nuclear medicine, ophthalmology, psychiatry, maternal and child health, digital health, traditional medicine, and the safety of pharmaceuticals and medical devices, and other health fields.

The two sides will continue to strengthen close cooperation on international platforms in the field of health, jointly safeguard global public health security, and enhance the health and well-being of the peoples of both countries. The two sides have agreed to expand cooperation in the fields of biosafety, infectious disease prevention and control, and cross-border transmission through disease control departments.

The two sides highly commend the positive results of sports exchanges between the two countries and look forward to further strengthening sports exchange and cooperation.

The two sides affirm the positive results of the 4th China-Russia Winter Youth Games and the 10th China-Russia Summer Youth Games held in Russia. The two sides agree to continue the tradition of alternately hosting winter and summer youth games in China and Russia, and have determined that the 5th China-Russia Winter Youth Games and the 11th China-Russia Summer Youth Games will be held in China.

The two sides note the necessity of further developing exchanges between the two countries in traditional ethnic sports events of greatest mutual interest, strengthening inter-regional sports cooperation, and agree to use sports venues in China and Russia to conduct joint training and sports competitions including winter sports events. They emphasize the need to further expand cooperation in sports education and scientific research, positively evaluate the role of the China-Russia Sports Colleges Alliance, and support sports education institutions of the two countries in establishing ties through the cooperative holding of forums, conferences, and online courses.

The two sides plan to carry out cooperation in the field of anti-doping. The two sides advocate further promoting equal international sports cooperation and carrying forward the Olympic spirit and ideals. The two sides agree to continue to coordinate positions on sports cooperation within the frameworks of multilateral organizations such as the Shanghai Cooperation Organization, BRICS, UNESCO, and the Asia Cooperation Dialogue.

The two sides affirm the effectiveness of the visa-free policy and its positive role in promoting trade and economic exchanges and people-to-people exchanges, and agree to further simplify visa procedures and continuously improve the level of facilitation of personnel exchanges between the two countries.

The two sides have agreed to further deepen cooperation in the field of youth policy, and carry out cooperative projects in areas such as patriotic and ideological-moral education for children and youth, youth entrepreneurship, volunteer service, creative industries, and new media. They will expand practical exchanges between local youth affairs departments and non-profit organizations, and jointly lead the youth agenda and advocate youth action within multilateral mechanisms such as the United Nations, the Shanghai Cooperation Organization, and BRICS.

The two sides are willing to coordinate information support for major international events, promote coverage of the political, economic, social, and cultural development of the two countries and of bilateral relations, jointly respond to unfriendly actions by third parties that negatively affect media cooperation between the two countries, resist interference in internal affairs in the information sphere and the spread of false information that undermines cooperation between the two countries, and expand the information dissemination channels of both sides.

The two sides will deepen media exchanges and cooperation, jointly hold the China-Russia Media Forum well, promote mutual visits between mainstream media, strengthen joint talent cultivation, provide convenience for media personnel of the two countries to work in each other's countries, and carry out experience exchanges in the field of digital diplomacy. They will expand the practical application of new technologies in the media field and deepen practical cooperation between local media of the two countries.

The two sides support exchanges and cooperation between China and Russia in the fields of joint production in online media, content co-creation by online influencers, cultivation of online and new media talent, and the holding of major online media forums.

The two sides will deepen cooperation in the publishing field, actively carry out cooperative projects for the mutual translation and distribution of classic and contemporary works of the two countries, continue to participate in book fairs held in China and Russia, support the mutual holding of guest-of-honor country events, and will strengthen exchanges and sharing of experience and practices in copyright protection and utilization.

The two sides are willing to deepen cooperation in the archival field between the two countries, exchange experience in archival management, exchange copies of archives of mutual interest, jointly compile and publish collections of archival documents, and jointly hold historical archive exhibitions.

The two sides emphasize the importance of people-to-people diplomacy in multi-field cooperation between China and Russia, and positively evaluate the role of the China-Russia Friendship, Peace and Development Committee and the friendship associations of the two countries in strengthening social ties between the two countries and enhancing mutual understanding between the two peoples.

IV

The two sides note that today's world is marked by intertwined turbulence and transformation, intensifying geopolitical competition, and frequent local conflicts and instability. The global deficits in peace, development, security, and governance are growing, and international relations management is lacking. The two sides reaffirm their resolute opposition to hegemonism and unilateralism, and their opposition to the world reverting to power politics, and firmly safeguard the authority of the United Nations and its central role in international affairs. The two sides resolutely oppose the imposition of unilateral sanctions in trade, the discriminatory use of tariffs and other restrictive measures, and call on all parties to genuinely safeguard the multilateral trading system with the WTO at its core and maintain the stability and smooth functioning of global industrial and supply chains.

The two sides note that certain countries pursue hegemonism and cling to a neo-colonialist mentality, and that their aggressive policies are leading to increasingly intense international competition and a continuously rising level of tension in global affairs. These countries attempt to infringe upon the sovereignty of other countries, contain the economic and technological development of other countries, and create obstacles to impede the building of a multipolar world.

The two sides condemn unilateral coercive, punitive, and discriminatory protectionist measures taken by any country, bloc, or coalition of states that violate international law, the principle of sovereign equality, and the principle of non-interference in internal affairs. The two sides call for the cancellation of illegal unilateral coercive measures that undermine the purposes and principles of international law and the UN Charter, and reaffirm that neither side will impose nor support any sanctions inconsistent with international law that have not been approved by the UN Security Council.

The two sides note that, in accordance with the principle of sovereign equality of states established by the UN Charter, all states must strictly comply with international obligations regarding the immunity of states and their property (including sovereign reserves). The two sides strongly condemn any illegal acts of freezing, seizing, misappropriating, or otherwise confiscating the state property and assets of other countries, and emphasize that the victimized states have the right to take countermeasures in accordance with international law.

The two sides emphasize the important positive significance of the concept of building a community with a shared future for mankind and a series of global initiatives put forward by the Chinese side. The two sides will closely cooperate on issues of maintaining international peace, stability, and security, and will accordingly fully support and promote each other's initiatives in international forums.

The two sides emphasize the important significance of the Global Security Initiative put forward by the Chinese side for reforming and improving global security governance. They advocate that all countries fully, completely, and comprehensively observe the purposes and principles of the UN Charter, adhere to respecting the sovereignty and territorial integrity of all countries, and oppose actions that infringe upon the fundamental interests of other countries, especially in the security field. On the basis of equal and indivisible security, they advocate resolving differences and eliminating the root causes of conflicts through peaceful means via dialogue and consultation, and improving global governance and the management of international relations.

The two sides resolutely condemn any external military coercion and actions aimed at undermining the unity and equal cooperation of the Eurasian continent. The Chinese side highly commends the Russian side's initiative to build an equal and indivisible "Eurasian security architecture," which aims to achieve long-term peace and stability in the region and create a development environment for regional countries free from the threat of external interference. The two sides call on all countries to adhere to the principle of equal and indivisible global and regional security, and to build common, comprehensive, cooperative, and sustainable security.

The two sides are willing to explore cooperation around the Global Security Initiative and the Eurasian security architecture initiative, promote the exchange and alignment of security concepts, strengthen strategic coordination in the security field, jointly benefit the peoples of the region, and contribute to the maintenance of world peace and tranquility.

The Russian side attaches great importance to the Global Civilization Initiative, and is willing to jointly hold relevant commemorative events with the Chinese side in conjunction with the UN General Assembly resolution on the International Day for Dialogue among Civilizations, and to jointly promote exchanges and mutual learning among civilizations and enhance people-to-people bonds.

The two sides will continue to resolutely defend the correct historical view of the Second World War, firmly safeguard the fruits of victory in the Second World War as enshrined in the UN Charter and the charters and judgments of the Nuremberg and Far East International Military Tribunals, and resolutely oppose any attempt to deny, distort, or falsify the history of the Second World War.

The two sides are willing to continue to strengthen cooperation in combating the glorification of Nazism, fascism, and militarism, as well as attempts to revive these non-constructive ideologies and deny the facts of genocide. The two sides note the importance of conducting correct historical education, protecting memorial facilities for the peoples of the two countries and martyrs who fought side by side and died during the Second World War, and opposing the desecration and damage of such memorial facilities. 中华人民共和国和俄罗斯联邦（以下称“双方”），声明如下： 一 今年是双方宣布决心发展平等信任的、面向二十一世纪的战略协作伙伴关系30周年、《中俄睦邻友好合作条约》签署25周年。 《中俄睦邻友好合作条约》继承弘扬了历史悠久的中俄交往传统，遵循公认的国际法原则和准则，为当代中俄关系发展奠定长期法律基础，不仅充分彰显两国人民源远流长的睦邻友好关系、发展世代友好的意愿，也是和平、发展、公平、正义、民主、自由的全人类共同价值的具体体现。 《中俄睦邻友好合作条约》中所固定下来的双边协作基本原则历经时间考验，时至今日仍具有重要现实意义。中俄在互相尊重主权和领土完整、互不侵犯、互不干涉内政、平等互利、和平共处原则基础上，支持彼此独立自主选择社会制度和发展道路权利，相互支持维护历史文化认同和传统价值观，两国关系具有不结盟、不对抗、不针对第三国的性质。 1996年4月25日的中俄联合声明以及2001年7月16日《中俄睦邻友好合作条约》中确立的两国关系发展模式成为中俄新时代全面战略协作伙伴关系的基石。在双方不懈努力下，这一关系达到历史最高水平并不断向前发展，展现成熟特质，具有内生动力，不受任何外部影响，其稳定性和全面性完全符合两国和两国人民根本利益，符合中俄各自总体国家发展任务，也为促进公正的世界多极化和国际关系民主化作出重要贡献。 双方同意根据《中俄睦邻友好合作条约》第二十五条对其予以延期，将继续恪守该条约及2021年6月28日发表的《中华人民共和国和俄罗斯联邦关于〈中俄睦邻友好合作条约〉签署20周年的联合声明》各项原则和精神，推动双边关系继续向更高水平、更高质量发展。 二 双方将以元首外交为引领，全面贯彻落实两国元首达成的重要共识，继续保持密切高层交往，保障政府、立法机构及党际间交往机制高效畅通运行。 双方将继续巩固两军传统友好，增进军事领域互信，完善合作机制，拓展联合演训和海空联合巡航，加强双边及多边框架下协调配合，携手应对各种风险与挑战，共同维护全球和地区安全稳定。 双方将共同打击走私麻醉药品、精神药物及其前体和武器，网络犯罪、跨国有组织犯罪、经济犯罪、跨境腐败和非法移民等活动，并加强执法能力建设合作。 双方将通过金融情报交流继续在打击洗钱和恐怖融资领域加强双边协作，在反洗钱多边框架下保持协作。加强青少年对洗钱及其上游犯罪的风险认识和防范意识，守护两国未来安全。 双方愿深化在应急管理领域全面交流与合作，加强防灾减灾救灾领域合作，并在安全生产风险防控方面开展协作。 双方强调应保护在对方境内第二次世界大战中牺牲烈士的纪念设施，并将继续就纪念设施保护管理、共同推进搜寻挖掘烈士和失踪人员遗骸、纪念缅怀英烈以及完善相关法律法规基础等方面开展合作。 双方高度评价近年来两国经贸合作保持高质量发展，为改善两国人民福祉作出积极贡献。双方将继续加强经贸政策沟通交流，巩固重点领域合作，挖掘新的增长点，推动货物贸易和服务贸易提质升级，坚定捍卫自主发展双边经贸伙伴关系的权利。 双方将在汽车、船舶、民用航空工业等领域大力发展合作，提升合作水平，本着兼容并蓄原则，并考虑各自国内法律及国家发展重点，持续拓展数字经济、人工智能等信息通信技术、跨境电商、开采相关矿产等领域合作，共同促进重点合作项目实施，进一步释放中俄合作潜力。 双方支持在化工和冶金领域实施项目，商定积极推动在联合矿产开发、绿色标准等领域合作。 双方指出，两国在平等互利基础上开展投资合作，助力实施重大投资合作项目，保障投资者合法权益，确保公正的投资环境。双方投资合作展现积极态势，实现高水平发展并不断推进。双方愿继续推动投资合作提质增效，支持中俄投资合作委员会工作，更好发挥委员会秘书处在落实各项共识方面的作用。 双方将履行2025年5月8日签署的《中华人民共和国政府和俄罗斯联邦政府关于促进和相互保护投资的协定》规定的义务，持续优化营商环境，提升投资便利化和保护水平，保障产业链供应链安全稳定。 双方指出，《中俄投资合作规划纲要》具有重要意义。为提升两国投资合作水平，双方愿加大力度支持规划中所列重点领域投资合作。 双方高度评价中俄能源合作取得的丰硕成果，商定继续巩固发展全面能源合作伙伴关系，支持企业深化油气、煤炭、民用核能、可再生能源包括绿色电力证书等领域互利合作，保障能源跨境基础设施安全稳定运营，促进能源运输畅通，推动能源生产国与能源消费国扩大对话，共同维护全球能源市场稳定，基于能源公正原则提升能源安全保障水平。 双方将继续推进田湾核电站和徐大堡核电站建设项目，确保按时完成建设并投入运行。在此基础上深化和平利用核能领域合作，逐步推进热核聚变、快堆和闭式核燃料循环领域合作，基于互利共赢和利益均衡原则，探讨通过“一揽子”方式开展核燃料循环前端和共建核电项目合作。 双方将继续发展两国金融领域合作，发挥中俄总理定期会晤委员会金融合作分委会、中俄财长对话和其他有关领域合作机制潜力，保持本币结算方面取得的进展，稳步推进中俄在银行领域和在资本市场务实合作，更好服务两国经济和资本市场健康稳定发展，深化关税、税收等领域合作。 双方将继续推进双多边财金合作进程，加强宏观经济政策沟通与协调，实现经济可持续发展并有效应对全球经济挑战。 双方愿全面加强海关合作，积极开展智慧海关合作交流互鉴，不断提升海关治理能力，持续优化监管服务；深入推进“单一窗口”互联互通合作，促进双边贸易便利，在双边信息交流问题上继续积极协作。 双方愿多措并举保障中俄边境口岸畅通，积极开展智慧口岸合作，持续加快口岸基础设施现代化改造，同步提升口岸双向往来通关效率，全面提升口岸互联互通水平。加快推进黑瞎子岛（大乌苏里岛）客货运公路口岸建设。 双方愿继续加强两国航空运输领域合作，鼓励双方航空公司按照两国航权安排，根据市场需求增加更多客货运航线航班。 双方重申将按照1991年5月16日《中华人民共和国和苏维埃社会主义共和国联盟关于中苏国界东段的协定》第九条精神，同朝鲜一道继续就图们江出海问题做好三方协商工作；按照2004年10月14日《中华人民共和国政府和俄罗斯联邦政府关于中俄船只在黑瞎子岛地区（塔拉巴罗夫岛和博利绍伊乌苏里斯基岛地区）周围水域航行的议定书》精神，加紧推进有关水域航行协定草案的双边谈判。 双方愿加强中俄跨境交通基础设施建设，提高跨境客货运输效率。继续组织开展使用无人驾驶技术的跨境货物运输。双方强调交通领域协作的重要作用，交流在空运、陆运和船舶自主航行领域使用无人技术的法律规制经验。 双方将保障中俄跨境通道走廊无障碍运行，加强中欧班列互利合作，保持运价稳定，继续支持中欧过境俄罗斯开展铁路、公路和海上货物运输。持续推进旨在增加北极航道货运量及其整体发展的建设利用领域务实合作。 双方愿扩大中国、俄罗斯之间进出口以及过境第三国的铁路货运量。双方将同步加强铁路国境站基础设施及后方通道建设，构建畅通稳定、可持续健康发展的铁路物流通道，共同保障货物运输安全和效率。 双方愿加深跨境收费公路大桥建设领域协作，例如黑河－布拉戈维申斯克公路大桥。双方愿继续发展“欧洲－中国西部”国际交通走廊，推动实现俄罗斯、中国、哈萨克斯坦公路互联。 双方高度评价进一步深化由蒙古国参与的三方机制统筹协作及中蒙俄经济走廊的前景，将积极推动该走廊框架下关键共建项目落实及协作。双方同意为蒙古国加入包括上海合作组织在内的地区一体化进程提供协助。 双方愿深化农林领域合作，进一步加强农业领域投资合作，加快在俄罗斯远东地区合作建立中俄农业合作试验示范区，将其作为俄罗斯国际跨越式发展区政策落地见效的试点项目。 深化农业领域协作符合中俄经济利益，有助于提升两国粮食安全。在确保安全前提下，在风险评估基础上，双方将共同致力于全面扩大从俄罗斯无疫区向中国提供的肉类产品的品类和数量，包括牛、猪副产品，携手推动俄罗斯符合要求的禽肉企业产品恢复输华，深化中俄宠物食品双向贸易。双方期待由中国向俄罗斯提供更加丰富的动植物产品，携手推动中国符合要求的禽肉企业产品输俄，并进一步扩大中国输俄水产品企业注册数量。 双方将进一步加强东北虎、东北豹、大熊猫、金丝猴、候鸟等野生动物保护合作，加强自然保护地管理经验交流。 双方商定继续实施符合中俄共同利益的国家航天计划大项目，积极推进包括国际月球科研站、月球与深空探测在内航天各重点领域合作，综合提升合作层次和水平。 双方决定持续深化卫星导航领域互利交流，积极推动落实《2026至2030年中俄卫星导航领域合作路线图》，保障北斗卫星导航系统和格洛纳斯全球卫星导航系统的互补性，造福世界各国用户。 双方愿持续加强中俄无线电频率协调和使用、卫星频轨资源协调和使用领域的合作，不断深化中俄在卫星互联网、物联网领域的经验交流与合作。 双方商定探索建立软件合作机制，共同赋能数字基础设施建设。深化开源领域合作交流，推动优质开源成果在重点行业的产业化落实，实现两国开源领域的共同发展。 双方商定加强在环保、水资源领域的密切协作。持续深化跨界水水文报汛和防洪领域合作，保障两国边境地区防洪安全。继续深化跨界水体水质保护务实合作，就突发生态环境事件应急联络保持协作，推动建设跨界自然保护地网络，保护生物多样性，加强废物管理领域合作，继续推动二十国集团、金砖国家、上海合作组织、东北亚次区域环保合作方案框架下的自然保护合作。 双方认同标准化合作对促进两国技术进步和贸易便利化的重要作用，将进一步深化重点领域标准化合作，包括推进汽车、儿童用品、有机产品等领域标准互认，并且在合格评定领域建立长期的信息交流合作机制。 双方商定持续深化知识产权交流与合作，为两国申请人及权利人提供更优质的服务，共同营造良好的营商与法治环境。在数字经济、人工智能技术发展的背景下推进知识产权保护的经验交流与实践分享。 双方强调加强中俄反垄断政策领域合作的重要性。双方在执法领域务实协作机制下，有效揭露和制止具有跨境特点的违反反垄断法的行为。双方愿推动落实两国关于公平竞争和反垄断领域的政府间协议，保障两国人民利益。 双方愿深化消费者权益保护领域协作，共同采取措施保障其应有水平，包括跨境采购服务、电子商务和旅游服务领域。 双方认为计量是保障国际贸易交往的重要基础。双方通过每年召开的中俄计量合作工作组会议，在能源、医疗健康等计量领域取得务实合作成果。双方愿继续开展经验交流和计量领域技术专业人才互访，并且商定在双方共同关心的领域继续开展国家计量单位基准联合比对。双方愿继续深化在计量领域的交流与合作。 双方愿推动深化住房城乡建设领域合作，开展寒冷地区“好房子”联合研究，加强“城市基础设施生命线安全工程”、城市更新、建设行业数字与人工智能技术应用等专业领域相关方向互学互鉴。 双方高度重视并欢迎中国东北地区和俄罗斯远东地区以及中国长江中上游地区和俄罗斯伏尔加河沿岸联邦区在经贸、投资和人文领域合作的积极发展，支持两国地方间和边境地区加强交流，扩大合作。中方欢迎俄方就国际跨越式发展区进行政策宣介。 双方商定在《中国与欧亚经济联盟经贸合作协定》框架下深化中国同欧亚经济联盟的务实合作，持续推进共建“一带一路”倡议与欧亚经济联盟发展规划在交通、物流、运输、数字化、电子商务、政策协调和消除贸易壁垒、食品、农产品贸易等领域对接。双方认为该对话机制对于促进贸易合作至关重要，有利于深化亚太与欧亚地区经济一体化及互联互通，推动共建“一带一路”倡议与构建“大欧亚伙伴关系”共同发展。双方愿进一步升级拓展中方与欧亚经济联盟及其成员国之间的经贸合作制度性安排，提高贸易自由化和便利化水平。 三 双方坚持文化和文明多样性，支持彼此保护文化历史认同和传统道德价值观，坚定维护文化主权，不将自己的价值观和模式强加于人，不搞意识形态对抗。 双方认为，应进一步加强联合国教科文组织作为政府间人文交流普遍性平台的作用，推动开展相互尊重的专业对话，促进成员国间高效沟通，增进团结。 双方一致同意，人文合作是中俄双边关系中重要的民意根基与精神纽带，通过教育互通、文化互融、青年互动，深化双方民众认同，为政治互信、经贸协作筑牢社会基础。 双方充分肯定教育领域合作成果，商定进一步推动中俄教育机构建立伙伴关系，共同举办活动，促进两国教育工作者交流经验和良好实践。
