Revelations from Wang Ji's Advanced Deeds | Choose What Is Urgently Needed and Hold Fast; Choose What Is Long-Term and Cultivate Deeply

王戟先进事迹启示录丨选刚需而坚守 择长远而深耕
PLA Daily (解放军报) 9 August 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
PLA political work media profiles Wang Ji, a military software researcher awarded a First Prize of the Military Science and Technology Progress Award, framing his decades-long focus on high-assurance software (高可信软件技术)—the foundational code embedded in combat aircraft, missiles, and naval vessels—as the model of a correct 政绩观 (political achievement outlook) for defense scientists. The piece documents an institutional messaging effort aimed at redirecting PLA research culture away from 'short, flat, and fast' (短平快) projects toward unglamorous but operationally critical foundational work, explicitly naming the absence of platform-level software talent as a systemic vulnerability. The framing points to leadership concern that incentive structures in military S&T are producing capability gaps at the software foundation layer, though the article does not specify which programs or timelines are affected.
Translation

Choose What Is Urgently Needed and Hold Fast; Choose What Is Long-Term and Cultivate Deeply

— Interpreting the Pursuit of Political Achievement (政绩追求) of "Model of the Era" Wang Ji

■ Yu Jinhu, Niu Jianguang

What does the character "guan" (观) in "political achievement outlook" (政绩观) mean? The Shuowen Jiezi states: "Guan means to examine carefully." Some annotators interpret it as deep scrutiny, discernment, and selection. An artificial intelligence program answers that the "guan" here refers to an overall view of and value-based choices regarding political achievements.

One ancient, one modern — two interpretations converge on the word "choice." Reflected in Wang Ji, this means answering a series of real-world multiple-choice questions — "lie flat or charge forward," "popular or unpopular" — in order to answer the mandatory questions every Party member and cadre must face: "For whom are political achievements built," "what kind of political achievements should be built," and "on what basis should political achievements be built."

Choice implies trade-offs and reflects one's pursuits. Wang Ji was admitted to university before the age of 14 and was promoted to full professor at 33. He clearly had many options, yet he chose to pursue research in high-assurance software technology (高可信软件技术): boldly venturing into "uncharted territory," willingly enduring the cold bench (坐冷板凳), conducting research that the nation needs and that supports warfighting.

In every trade-off, Wang Ji was guided by an internal scale: the three characters "high-assurance" (高可信) may seem "cold" to others, but they are "right" for the nation — fighter aircraft, missiles, naval vessels, and other national defense and military-industrial systems all depend on high-assurance software. This may not be a research "hot topic," but it is absolutely a vital pressure point (命门) in defense security that researchers must hold fast.

On the scales of political achievement pursuit, if the "weight" of research accomplishments is placed on the individual side, it brings at most personal merit; but if placed on the side of the Party's and nation's needs and national defense and military construction, it brings great achievements for the cause and victories on the battlefield.

Moved by her father's selfless dedication, Wang Ji's daughter painted a picture for him titled "Staircase," with a note in the lower right corner that the staircase "symbolizes Father's self-respect and ideals." Wang Ji treasures this painting deeply. The child's innocent words struck at his true inner feelings: the "self-respect and ideals" of a military scientific researcher are simple — to work and build for the nation, and to concentrate strength and overcome difficulties for the sake of war.

Chairman Xi has repeatedly emphasized that "in undertaking work and building achievements, one must establish a correct political achievement outlook." High-assurance software belongs to foundational core technology: its fault-tolerance standards are stringent, its research breakthroughs are extremely difficult, and it rarely produces visible results — for a long time, few researchers have cultivated it deeply. If scientific researchers gravitate toward the easy and avoid the difficult, chasing "short, flat, and fast" (短平快) projects, it will inevitably cause the absence of foundational platform technologies and bury security risks in the development of equipment construction. The confidence of the cause of strengthening the military cannot be separated from the foundational support that consolidates the base and covers the bottom. Wang Ji's perseverance is precisely a pragmatic act of filling gaps in the system and laying a solid foundation for military strengthening — holding fast to a critical position in national defense research.

A correct political achievement outlook stems from a correct value system. Only by placing the cause of the Party, the nation, and the people above all else can one achieve the resolute determination to charge forward without hesitation, always and forever. High-level amputation could not defeat him; years of dialysis could not crush him. Wang Ji has been "nailed" (钉) to a single specialized field for decades, presiding over and participating in more than 30 major research projects, winning a First Prize of the Military Science and Technology Progress Award and a Second Prize of the National Science and Technology Progress Award, and supporting — through one research achievement after another — the takeoff and landing of combat aircraft, the roar of missiles, and the long voyages of naval vessels.

Credit for success need not go to oneself, yet credit for success must come from oneself (功成不必在我、功成必定有我). As the leader of a research team, Wang Ji actively guides everyone to correct their pursuit of political achievements: be "silent" like high-assurance software — quietly standing guard in ordinary times, willing to serve as an invisible guardian, yet capable of bearing a weight of a thousand jun (千钧之重) when danger arrives, and thereby manifesting glory.

"Pursue substance and results will endure; chase reputation and achievements will be shallow (征实则效存，徇名则功浅)." Facts have proven that all deviations and misalignments in the pursuit of political achievements originate from a single character: "self" (私). Communists serve the great public good, uphold great righteousness, and seek the greater self (大我). Only by holding the cause higher, taking responsibility more seriously, and placing less weight on personal honor, status, and interests can one draw clear lines and maintain proper standards on what should and should not be done — and only then can one's political achievement outlook remain impartial and well-calibrated.

Wang Ji's life choices serve as a clear mirror, reflecting the most precious spiritual character of national defense research: not chasing trends, not clinging to visible achievements — choosing what is urgently needed and holding fast, choosing what is long-term and cultivating deeply. Scientific and technological workers of the new era should, like Wang Ji, identify where their knowledge can be put to use, make down-to-earth efforts, find at the intersection of national need and personal strength a direction worth pursuing over the long term, and shoulder the historical responsibility entrusted by the Party and the people.

选刚需而坚守 择长远而深耕 ——解读“时代楷模”王戟的政绩追求 ■余金虎 牛剑光 政绩观的“观”为何意？《说文解字》云：“观，谛视也。”有人注释为深度审视、辨析选择。人工智能软件回答说，这里的“观”，是对政绩的整体看法和价值选择。 一古一今，两种释义在“选择”二字上交汇，映射在王戟身上，就是通过解答“躺平还是冲锋”“热门还是冷门”这一道道现实选择题，来回答党员干部“政绩为谁而树”“树什么样的政绩”“靠什么树政绩”这一道道必答题。 选择，意味着取舍，体现着追求。不满14岁考入大学，33岁晋升教授……对王戟来说，他明明有很多选择，可他却偏偏选择高可信软件技术研究：勇闯“无人区”、甘坐冷板凳，做国家需要的科研、支撑打仗的科研。 取舍之间，皆因王戟内心有杆秤：高可信，这3个字对旁人来说是“冷的”，但对国家来说是“对的”，战斗机、导弹、舰船等国防军工，哪一样都离不开高可信软件。这也许不是科研“热门”，但一定是科研工作者必须坚守的国防安全“命门”。 在政绩追求的天平上，科研成绩这个“砝码”如果放在个人这一端，带来的至多是个人业绩，但如果放在党和国家需要、国防和军队建设这一端，带来的就是事业伟绩、战场胜绩。 感动于父亲的忘我工作，女儿为王戟画了一幅名为“台阶”的画，右下角写道，台阶“象征着父亲的自尊和理想”。这幅画被王戟视若珍宝，孩童纯真之言戳中了他的真实内心：军队科研工作者的“自尊和理想”很简单，就是为国干事创业、为战聚力攻坚。 习主席反复强调，“干事创业一定要树立正确政绩观”。高可信软件属于保障性核心技术，容错标准严苛、攻关难度极大、难出显性成果，长期鲜有研究者深耕。若科研工作者趋易避难，追逐“短平快”项目，势必造成基础底盘技术缺位，埋下装备建设发展安全隐患。强军事业的底气，离不开固本兜底的基础支撑。王戟的坚守，正是补齐体系短板、筑牢强军根基的务实之举，守住了国防科研的关键阵地。 正确的政绩观来源于正确的价值观，视党、国家和人民事业高于一切，才会有义无反顾、永远冲锋的执着坚定。高位截肢打不倒他、常年透析压不垮他，王戟几十年如一日“钉”在专一领域，主持和参与重要科研项目30余项，捧回了军事科学技术进步奖一等奖、国家科学技术进步奖二等奖，用一项项科研实绩支撑战机起落、导弹呼啸、舰船远航。 功成不必在我、功成必定有我。作为科研团队带头人，王戟积极引导大家端正政绩追求：像高可信软件那样“沉默”，平时默默守护，甘当隐形卫士，一旦险情降临，能扛得住千钧之重，彰显荣耀。 “征实则效存，徇名则功浅。”事实证明，政绩追求的偏差和错位皆源于一个“私”字。共产党人为的是大公、守的是大义、求的是大我。把事业看得高一些、把责任看得重一些，把个人的名誉、地位、利益看得淡一些，才会在该干什么、不该干什么上界限分明、尺度得当，才能做到政绩观不偏不倚。 王戟的人生选择如同一面明镜，照见了国防科研最可贵的精神底色：不蹭热度、不恋显绩，选刚需而坚守、择长远而深耕。新时代的科技工作者应像王戟那样，认准所学知识的用武之地，付出脚踏实地的努力，在国家需要与个人所长的交汇处，找到值得长期奔赴的方向，扛起党和人民赋予的历史重任。
