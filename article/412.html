Rocket Force Steadily Advances Construction of a Training Venue System

火箭军扎实推进训练场地体系建设
PLA Daily (解放军报) 18 May 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
Rocket Force units are conducting tiered training across geographically dispersed venues — local mountain ranges for individual-soldier and equipment drills, desert wastelands for combined-arms training (合成训练), and multi-branch grounds for joint exercises (联演联训) — under a venue-sharing system built around the 'basic—combined-arms—joint' capability generation pathway (基础—合成—联合战斗力生成路径). A Rocket Force training department official describes the shift away from unit-siloed, linearly expanding infrastructure toward centralized unified construction and cross-theater, cross-service open sharing, with newly formed missile brigades now drawing on pooled regional venues rather than maintaining dedicated organic facilities. The article documents an institutional effort to solve a known PLA structural problem — training venue fragmentation by unit establishment — but does not specify which missile brigades, which theater commands, or what timeline governs the cross-theater sharing arrangements.
Translation

Tiered and Interconnected, Functionally Complementary, Jointly Linked and Shared — Rocket Force Steadily Advances Construction of a Training Venue System

PLA Daily report by special correspondent Li Bingfeng: Relying on layered mountain ranges to conduct nearby basic training such as individual-soldier and individual-equipment operations; charging across desert wastelands to carry out combined-arms training (合成训练) with all elements and all processes, including fire assault; transferring to multi-branch training grounds to integrate into joint exercises and training (联演联训) across the "three services" (三军)… In early summer, units subordinate to the Rocket Force have unfurled vivid "training scenes" one after another. This is the new landscape brought about by the Rocket Force's exploration and construction of a training venue system that is tiered and interconnected (层次衔接), functionally complementary (功能互补), and jointly linked and shared (联动共享).

Training venue construction is the foundational project of the training domain and a guarantee for combat capability generation. In recent years, the Rocket Force, based on the "basic—combined-arms—joint" combat capability generation pathway (基础—合成—联合战斗力生成路径), has sorted and compiled training venue requirements. Following the approach of building on existing resources, leveraging the entire military, designing systemically, integrating combat and training (战训融合), and achieving intensive sharing, it has categorically integrated various resources including traditional common training grounds, specialized technical training grounds, and tactical training grounds, and has pressed ahead with improving the support conditions for combat-training venues, providing a solid foundation for units at all levels to carry out realistic combat training (实战化练兵) in a thorough manner.

"In the process of constructing, managing, and using training venues, we have abandoned the simple and extensive approach of linear growth and are actively exploring a high-quality development model of intensive optimization and general-purpose sharing (集约集优、通用共享)." A leader from the Rocket Force's training department introduced that, on one hand, they have broken down barriers between unit establishments, reinforced the concepts of centralized unified construction, regional joint construction, and functional integrated construction (集中统建、区域合建、功能套建), and promoted practices such as one venue for multiple uses, one room for multiple uses, and one depot for multiple uses, substantially increasing the utilization rate of training venues. On the other hand, they have complementarily improved support conditions for combined-arms training, live-force confrontation (实兵对抗), and other combat-training activities; scientifically demonstrated and newly built several regional-level training grounds that complement the advantages of existing resources; and have progressively opened up a path of cross-theater command and cross-service open sharing, powerfully supporting various types of training including large-scale unit maneuver and realistic combat field operations (实战化野战化), and maximizing the comprehensive support effectiveness of training venues.

New concepts for construction, management, and use have generated new changes on the training ground. A certain newly formed missile brigade previously had its training progress affected by insufficient training venues. Now, they need only submit phased requirements to higher-level organs and can "take the field unburdened" (轻装上阵). Looking through the annual training plan of another missile brigade, the reporter found that they not only share training venues with neighboring units to conduct training, but also dispatch forces along multiple routes to travel far to the northern forest sea (北国林海) and plateau wastelands to be tempered and hardened, forging the "great-power swordsmanship" (大国剑法) to defeat the enemy in environments of different regions and different weather conditions.

Today's training grounds forge elite troops; tomorrow's battlefields will showcase their prowess. From the vast desert wastelands to the deep mountain forests, from the snow-covered plateau to the seaside, the reporter, visiting the Rocket Force's training grounds one by one, observed that the broad ranks of officers and soldiers, oriented toward winning future wars, are conducting training and exercises in depth, and the units' realistic combat capability level (实战化水平) continues to climb.

Original Chinese
层次衔接 功能互补 联动共享 火箭军扎实推进训练场地体系建设 解放军报讯 特约记者李兵峰报道：依托叠嶂山峦，就近开展单兵单装操作等基础训练；驰骋荒漠戈壁，全要素全流程实施火力突击等合成训练；转进多军兵种练兵场，融入“三军”展开联演联训……初夏，火箭军所属部队铺展开一幅幅生动“练兵图”。这是火箭军探索构建层次衔接、功能互补、联动共享的训练场地体系带来的新景象。 训练场地建设是训练领域的基础工程，也是战斗力生成的保障。近年来，火箭军基于“基础—合成—联合”战斗力生成路径，梳理汇总训练场地需求，按照立足现有、借力全军、体系设计、战训融合、集约共享的思路，分类整合传统共同训练场、专业技术训练场、战术训练场等各类资源，加紧完善战训场地支撑条件，为部队各级扎实开展实战化练兵提供坚实基础。 “在训练场地建设、管理和使用过程中，我们摒弃简单粗放的线性增长做法，积极探索集约集优、通用共享的高质量发展模式。”火箭军训练部门领导介绍，他们一方面打破单位建制壁垒，强化集中统建、区域合建、功能套建理念，推行一场多用、一室多用、一库多用等经验做法，大幅提升训练场地使用率；另一方面配套完善合成训练、实兵对抗等战训保障条件，科学论证新建数个区域级训练场，与原有资源优势互补，逐步走开跨战区、跨军种开放共享路子，有力支撑部队大范围机动、实战化野战化等各类训练，最大限度发挥训练场地综合保障效益。 建管用新理念，催生练兵场上新变化。某新组建导弹旅，此前因训练场地不足问题影响训练进程。如今，他们只需向上级机关提报阶段性需求，就可“轻装上阵”。记者翻看另一个导弹旅的年度训练计划发现，他们不仅和友邻单位共用训练场地开展训练，还兵分多路远赴北国林海、高原戈壁加钢淬火，在不同地域、不同天候的环境中，锤炼克敌制胜的“大国剑法”。 今朝校场砺精兵，未来战场显身手。从大漠戈壁到深山密林，从雪域高原到大海之滨，记者探访火箭军座座训练场看到，广大官兵立足打赢未来战争，深入开展训练演练，部队实战化水平不断攀升。
