Finest Faces of Revolutionary Soldiers in the New Era | Wang Wenbin of a Unit Under the Information Support Force: Winning the Contest on the Invisible Battlefield

最美新时代革命军人风采丨信息支援部队某部王文彬：博弈制胜无形战场
PLA Daily (解放军报) 8 August 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
Wang Wenbin, a senior engineer in an unspecified unit under the Information Support Force (ISF), is profiled as a cyber and information defense specialist who has spent over a decade developing network protection methods, earning three second-class military science and technology progress awards and one first-class merit citation. The article documents his team's growth from two to more than twenty personnel and credits him with innovating more than ten 战法技法 (tactical and technical methods) for cyber defense, including an in-exercise system upgrade following a command-link compromise during a joint exercise. As a profile in the ISF's 'Finest Faces' series, this is institutional recognition content; its analytical value is as a record of how the ISF frames cyber defense work — emphasizing continuous in-operation innovation (边作战边创新边优化) and talent development — rather than as evidence of a specific capability shift, with the unit's identity, location, and the exercises referenced left unspecified.
Translation

"Anomalous data flow!" Suddenly, an alert message shattered the silence. Senior Engineer Wang Wenbin immediately assessed and responded — comparing signatures, locking onto the target, tracing the source back to its origin… Within a short time, a covert probing attack had been successfully neutralized.

Winning the contest on the invisible battlefield (博弈制胜无形战场) and safeguarding cybersecurity and information security (网络信息安全) is a distillation of Wang Wenbin's work over more than a decade.

In 2011, Wang Wenbin enlisted after completing his doctoral degree at a civilian university. Years of deep study in cyber and information technology sharpened his awareness: "Cyberspace has become an important battlefield in modern warfare — we must seize the technological high ground."

Rooted at the front lines of the cyber and information domain (网信疆场), Wang Wenbin has actively explored new models of cyber and information defense, innovated more than ten tactical and technical methods (战法技法), and effectively advanced the enhancement of combat capability. He has received three second-class awards for military science and technology progress, one first-class merit citation, and one third-class merit citation.

"Maintaining cyber and information security is a contest without gunsmoke — only by achieving absolute certainty can we prevent a single failure from becoming a catastrophic one (万无一失，方能杜绝一失万无)!" This is a phrase Wang Wenbin often has on his lips. Facing threats such as covert infiltration and malicious attacks, he adheres to the approach of "fighting, innovating, and optimizing simultaneously" (边作战边创新边优化), working to connect the chain from "technological innovation" to "the capability to win," and leading his team in building an impregnable cyber and information security barrier.

During one joint exercise, the layering of complex network environments rendered conventional protective measures ineffective, and alarms on the command link sounded repeatedly. Wang Wenbin led his team through an all-night investigation, ultimately eliminating the threat and stabilizing the situation. Once the mission concluded, he led the team in reviewing the data line by line, breaking down signatures, and running through scenarios, successfully iterating and upgrading the protection system and substantially improving operational effectiveness.

"One person can run fast; only a group of people can run far." As the leader of his team, Wang Wenbin has consistently regarded mentoring core technical personnel as a critical task in team building. He has implemented a "mentorship-plus-tasking" (导师制+任务制) development model, proactively assigning tasks to young engineers and building platforms for them. Today, his team has grown from an initial two people to more than twenty, producing dozens of innovative technical results.

From the snowfields of the northern frontier to the dense forests of the southern lands, from the northwestern Gobi to the shores of the East China Sea… Over the years, Wang Wenbin has led his team to frontline units across the services and arms and to the scenes of major military operations. "This is a race against time — every second bears on victory or defeat!" he said.

“数据流异常！”突然，一条告警消息打破宁静。高级工程师王文彬立即研判处置，对比特征、锁定目标、追踪溯源……很快，一次隐蔽试探攻击被成功处置。 博弈制胜无形战场、保障网络信息安全，是王文彬十余年如一日工作的缩影。 2011年，王文彬从地方高校博士毕业后参军入伍。长期对网络信息技术的钻研，让他敏锐意识到：“网络空间已成为现代战争的重要战场，必须抢占技术制高点。” 扎根网信疆场前沿阵地，王文彬积极探索网络信息防御新模式，创新战法技法10余项，有效助推战斗力提升，获军队科技进步二等奖3项，荣立一等功1次、三等功1次。 “维护网信安全是没有硝烟的博弈，只有做到万无一失，方能杜绝一失万无！”这是王文彬时常挂在嘴边的一句话。面对隐蔽渗透、恶意攻击等威胁，他坚持“边作战边创新边优化”思路，着力打通“技术创新”到“打赢能力”链路，带领团队筑起坚不可摧的网络信息安全屏障。 一次联合演习中，由于复杂网络环境叠加，常规防护手段失效，指挥链路警报频繁响起，王文彬带队通宵排查，最终消除威胁、稳住态势。任务结束，他带队逐条复盘数据、拆解特征、推演场景，成功迭代升级防护系统，大幅提升了作战效能。 “一个人跑得快，一群人才能跑得远。”作为团队领头人，王文彬始终把帮带业务骨干作为团队建设的重要任务，推行“导师制+任务制”培养模式，主动给年轻工程师压任务、搭平台。如今，他的团队已从最初的2人发展到20余人，取得数十项革新技术成果。 从北疆雪原到南国密林，从西北戈壁到东海之滨……这些年，王文彬带领团队奔走于各军兵种一线部队、重大军事行动现场。“这是一场与时间的赛跑，每一秒都关乎胜负！”他说。
