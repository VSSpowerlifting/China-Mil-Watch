From Fear of the Waves to Charging Toward the Blue: One Soldier's Transformation on the Maritime Training Course

从畏惧海浪到奔赴蔚蓝：一名战士的海训蜕变之路
PLA Daily (解放军报) 3 August 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
At a maritime training site on the eastern Shandong coast, PLA soldier Lin Yuan completes his first open-water sea-swimming progression under squad leader Wu Bin, moving from shore-based dry drills (磨肚皮, 'belly grinding' on folding stools) through a failed first ocean entry to an extended open-water swim. The article is a first-person narrative feature in official military media; its value is as a record of how the PLA frames individual fear, failure, and perseverance for junior enlisted audiences, not as evidence of a new training program or changed maritime readiness posture. The unit and formation are not identified, leaving the institutional context of this maritime training cycle unknown.
Translation

On the eastern Shandong coast, the air was thick with the smell of salt and damp. Only at night, lying in his tent listening to the roar of the waves, did Lin Yuan truly feel that he was at a maritime training site. For the past week, his daily training had consisted of "belly grinding" (磨肚皮). "Belly grinding" means one person, one folding stool: lined up in a row on ground scorched by the sun, everyone lying prone on their stools, simulating swimming strokes in the water, letting the stool dig into their chest and abdomen. "Pull in, flip, kick, squeeze—the movements must be fluid, stretch out like a frog!" Squad leader Wu Bin stood at the front, his commands ringing out. Lin Yuan followed the commands, working through the key points of each movement over and over, sweat dripping from his chin. Life at maritime training was nothing like the wave-cleaving adventure he had imagined—only the tedium of day-after-day practice and the aching soreness in his limbs. He had already dreamed several times of swimming freely in the open sea. Deep blue water churning with snow-white surf, fish playing around him……

A few days later, Lin Yuan went ahead with the squad leaders to set up the maritime training site. Along the way, Lin Yuan quietly listened as the squad leaders swapped stories about amusing incidents from past maritime training. At some point, the conversation turned to Wu Bin.

"The first time the whole company went into the sea, Wu Bin had barely set out when he swallowed water. Fortunately the company commander spotted him in time and pulled him onto the assault boat," said the second-squad leader.

The fourth-squad leader continued: "After that, he trained even harder. He eventually became the battalion's swimming champion."

They reached their destination, and the sight before him left Lin Yuan dumbstruck: how was this sea nothing like what he had imagined? The nearshore water was a murky yellow-brown, like an overturned bucket of muddy water. Only by straining his gaze to the horizon could he make out a ribbon of deep blue where sea met sky.

Lin Yuan froze on the spot. Every instinct in him recoiled, but he steeled himself and followed the squad leader into the water.

The icy seawater closed around him; a salty, bitter taste shot straight into his nostrils and forced its way into his mouth. In the instant his mind went blank, a wave struck without warning. Lin Yuan felt everything go dark before his eyes as he was slammed violently down into the murky water below. He had no memory of how he got back to shore—he later learned the squad leader had dragged him back. That time, he had managed to swim barely a hundred meters.

On the way back, Lin Yuan felt his body strangely heavy, his mind mechanically repeating the basic swimming movements. But that suffocating terror, mixed with the salt and bitterness of the seawater, surged over him again and again.

Back in the squad bay, his eyes darted away, unable to meet the squad leader's concerned gaze. That evening, the squad leader came to find him. Lin Yuan lowered his head and said quietly: "Squad leader, I trained so long, and I still couldn't hold out past a hundred meters."

To his surprise, the squad leader laughed: "Everyone is like this the first time they go into the sea. But even after swallowing enough bitter water, we have to press forward with courage and challenge the sea again!"

Wu Bin led Lin Yuan through a final warm-up on the beach. The thunder of waves breaking on shore came in one surge after another. Wu Bin turned and reminded him: "In a moment I'll still be out front breaking the waves—stay close behind me."

Stepping back into the water, the cold, murky surge came rolling in, and Lin Yuan's heart clenched tight. He fixed his gaze on Wu Bin's back cutting through the waves ahead and forced his rigid body to keep pace.

One stroke, two strokes…… in the struggle, his body quietly found its rhythm. He felt the current sliding across his skin, felt the thrust generated by each kick and squeeze.

Suddenly, a few blurred shapes flashed at the corner of his eye. A school of small fish! He did not know how long he had been swimming when he lifted his head again to breathe and looked hard ahead—Wu Bin's powerful figure was cresting a wave. Beyond that wave, the churning water revealed a deep, profound blue.

Lin Yuan put his head down, his arms pulling faster, his legs kicking with greater force. He swam on and on, swimming into that expanse of blue that grew ever wider, ever more pure.

Original Chinese
鲁东海滨，空气中弥漫着咸湿的味道。只有夜晚躺在帐篷里听着海浪滔滔，林远才真切地意识到，自己正身处海训场。一周以来，他每天的训练内容都是“磨肚皮”。所谓“磨肚皮”，就是一人、一马扎，在被太阳炙烤的地面上排成一排，大家趴在马扎上，模拟在水中的游泳动作，任由马扎硌着胸腹。 “收、翻、蹬、夹，动作要连贯，要像青蛙一样舒展！”班长吴彬站在前方，口令洪亮。林远跟着口令，反复体会动作要领，汗珠顺着下颌滴落。海训的生活没有想象中的劈波斩浪，只有日复一日的枯燥练习和四肢肌肉的酸痛。 他已经有好几次梦见自己在大海里恣意遨游。湛蓝的海水翻卷着雪白的浪涛，鱼儿围在身边嬉戏…… 过了几天，林远跟着班长们前出设置海训场地。一路上，林远静静地听班长们讲起往年海训的趣事。不知什么时候，话题转到了吴彬身上。 “当时第一次组织全连下海，吴彬刚出发一会儿就呛了水，幸好被连长及时发现，将他拉上冲锋舟。”二班班长说。 四班班长接着说：“自那以后，他训练更刻苦了，后来成为全营游泳冠军。” 目的地到了，眼前的景象让林远傻了眼：这大海怎么跟想象中不一样？近海一片浑黄，简直像打翻了的泥水缸。只有极目远眺，海天交界处才横着一条深蓝色的飘带。 林远立时怔住了，心里本能地抗拒，但还是硬着头皮跟着班长下了水。 冰凉的海水裹上来，咸涩的味道直冲鼻腔、钻进嘴里。就在他心神恍惚的刹那，一个浪头毫无征兆地打来。林远只觉眼前一黑，整个人被狠狠扣进了浑浊的水下。至于怎么上的岸，他毫无印象，后来得知是班长将他拖回去的。这一次，他仅仅游出去百余米。 回去的路上，林远感觉身子异常沉重，脑海里机械地重复着游泳的基本动作。可那窒息的恐惧感，混合着海水的咸涩，一次次袭上心头。 回到班里，他眼神闪躲，始终不敢触碰班长关切的目光。直到晚上，班长主动找到了他。林远低着头小声说：“班长，我练了那么久，没想到连百余米都撑不住。” 出乎他的意料，班长笑了：“第一次下海都是这样，可哪怕呛够了苦水，我们还要勇往直前，再次向大海发出挑战！” 吴彬带着林远在沙滩上做着最后的热身。海浪拍岸的轰鸣一阵紧过一阵。吴彬转头叮嘱：“一会儿还是我在前开浪，你跟紧我。” 再次踏入海水，冰凉浑浊的浪涌上来，林远的心猛地揪紧。他紧紧盯住前方吴彬破浪的背影，强迫僵硬的身体跟上节奏。 一下，两下……身体在对抗中竟悄然地找到了韵律。他感受着水流划过皮肤，感受着每一次蹬夹带来的推力。 忽然，眼角掠过几道模糊的影子。是一群小鱼！不知游了多久，当他再次抬头换气，奋力前望，只见吴彬矫健的身影正破开一道浪峰。浪峰之后，那翻滚的海水，竟透出一种深邃的蓝色。 林远埋下头，手臂划得更快，双腿蹬得更有力。他一直游，一直游，游进了那片越来越广阔、越来越纯粹的蔚蓝之中。
