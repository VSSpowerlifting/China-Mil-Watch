Modernization Doctrine

Military Service Organs Nationwide Focus on Combat Training and War Preparedness, Strictly Control Military Service Checkpoints, and Improve the Quality of Recruits

全国兵役机关聚焦练兵备战，严把兵役关口，提高兵员质量
PLA Daily (解放军报) 14 August 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
The Ministry of National Defense Conscription Office issued a new 'Professional Ethics Code for Conscription Workers' and, per PLA Daily, college graduates now make up over 70% of annual recruits nationally—a share that has held for three consecutive years after first exceeding 50% in 2022, with localities such as Xiangcheng District, Suzhou reaching roughly 90%. The article documents the institutional infrastructure behind that shift: the 'two-induction, two-discharge' (两征两退) scheduling reform, the 'five-dimensional, four-type' (五维四型) recruit-quality framework, AI-assisted physical assessment in Linyi City, cross-provincial online political-review processing in Sichuan and Chongqing, and 'sunshine conscription' (阳光征兵) transparency measures in Shandong. Taken together, the piece records a sustained, multi-year effort to reorient conscription toward technically educated recruits—the framing points to institutional acknowledgment that the PLA's shift toward informatized and intelligentized warfare has outpaced what a traditional conscription pipeline can supply.
Translation

Military Service Organs Nationwide Focus on Combat Training and War Preparedness, Strictly Control Military Service Checkpoints, and Improve the Quality of Recruits—— Sending More Outstanding Young People to the Vibrant Military Barracks

■ PLA Daily Reporters Zhu Hongbo, Zhang Lei

"Strive to gather more outstanding young people under the Party's banner and deliver them to the people's armed forces, and genuinely fulfill the sacred mission of selecting talent for the military and serving the goal of a strong military." Recently, the Ministry of National Defense Conscription Office issued the "Professional Ethics Code for Conscription Workers," drawing widespread attention. Requirements such as "broadly recruiting outstanding talent from all corners of the country," "selecting talent on the basis of merit," and "choosing the best of the best" regarding the responsibility to select recruits not only allow people to see the increasingly standardized and law-based (法治化) trend of conscription work in the new era, but also convey the resolve and effort that military service organs have demonstrated in recruiting high-quality personnel.

The foundation of the military lies in its soldiers, the source of soldiers lies in conscription, and conscription work directly affects the generation of combat power and the development and construction of the armed forces. From the implementation of the "two-induction, two-discharge" (两征两退) reform to the revision and improvement of laws and regulations such as the Regulations on Conscription Work and the Provisions on Military Service Registration; from innovating the pre-conscription and pre-reserve model to promoting the use of the auxiliary soldier-assignment system; from the "five-rate" (五率) evaluation to building the "five-dimensional, four-type" (五维四型) framework—in recent years, conscription work has undergone profound changes, and all of these changes point to a single goal: "focus on combat training and war preparedness, strictly control the conscription checkpoint, improve the quality of recruits, and continuously provide strong talent support for a strong military capable of winning wars."

"Looking at national conscription data, the number of college graduates conscripted and the proportion of high-quality recruits have increased significantly compared to the past and have been maintained at a high level in recent years. In 2022, the proportion of college graduates conscripted exceeded 50% for the first time, and has remained stable above 70% for the past three years." A staff member of the Ministry of National Defense Conscription Office introduced that "focusing on college students and emphasizing the conscription of graduates from schools at all levels and of all types" has been a consistent requirement of national conscription work in recent years. In particular, young people of eligible age who possess urgently needed professional skills or relevant skills certificates have been designated by military service organs in many localities as priority targets for "preferential conscription and preferential assignment."

Conscription data from the first half of this year shows: the proportion of college students conscripted in Yucheng City, Shandong Province has remained above 85% for the past three consecutive years, and the proportion in Xiangcheng District, Suzhou City, Jiangsu Province has been maintained at around 90% for the past two years. As the main front for conscription work, universities are also working hard to improve the effectiveness of mobilization and the rate of recruit enlistment. Student enthusiasm for enlisting at "double first-class" (双一流) universities such as Tsinghua University and Nankai University continues to run high.

In early July, five themed conscription publicity bus lines in Lu'an City, Anhui Province officially began operations, becoming mobile conscription classrooms shuttling through the city's streets and alleys. "City buses are a publicity window directly facing the public, and also a new vehicle for us to create a 'mobile exhibition platform' for conscription publicity," the head of the city's conscription office introduced. To select quality recruits, one must first expand the base of pre-conscription candidates. Conscription publicity that is novel in form and broad in coverage is critically important. After the second-half conscription work was launched, they joined with the city's public transportation company to carefully select five routes running through the urban area and covering multiple schools, integrating conscription information and national defense news into residents' daily commutes, thereby broadening the coverage of conscription publicity.

At the beginning of the year, the three northeastern provinces created new conscription publicity scenarios in conjunction with the ice and snow tourism season. Beside the ice sculpture of the "Yang Jingyu Detachment Battle Flag" on Xinmin Street in Changchun City, Jilin Province, a night market conscription publicity point was packed with people, and a giant ice-and-snow painting of the Fujian carrier and three types of carrier-based aircraft created by teachers and students of Harbin Engineering University went viral. Fujian Province on the eastern coast, as well as Guizhou and Chongqing in the southwest, leveraged popular sporting events such as "Village Super" (村超) and "Village BA" (村BA), as well as drone light shows, to ignite conscription publicity and stoke the enthusiasm of eligible young people to sign up.

"Which aspect of our city's conscription work are you most satisfied with?" At the beginning of the year, the Linyi City Conscription Office in Shandong Province organized a conscription work questionnaire survey, and the "intelligentized (智能化) physical fitness and physical capability assessment model for prospective recruits" received widespread praise from pre-conscription youth. The physical fitness and physical capability assessment of prospective recruits is a critical checkpoint for ensuring the quality of recruits. In the past, this work was typically carried out using manual stopwatches and verbal counting. This year, Linyi City fully rolled out the use of intelligent equipment to precisely read and assess prospective recruits' body mass index, lung capacity, balance ability, and physical fitness levels in events such as jump rope, standing long jump, and the 1,000-meter run, accurately reflecting the capabilities and qualities of prospective recruits and effectively eliminating human interference.

"Every measure of fairness and impartiality in conscription work deepens the popular foundation of national defense and military construction; every breath of clean and upright conduct bolsters the enthusiasm of society as a whole to care about national defense and love the military." A staff member of the Shandong Province Conscription Office introduced that the province has continued to advance the implementation of "sunshine conscription" (阳光征兵) through measures such as reinforcing the conscription physical examination responsibilities at all levels, openly selecting incorrupt conscription supervisors from across society, organizing parents to participate in soldier-assignment meetings, and improving the military-civilian joint political review system, ensuring that the conscription process is open and transparent, that recruit quality is precisely assessed, and that recruit assignments are scientifically allocated.

More than ten service windows—covering civil affairs, social security, taxation, and other functions—are lined up in a row, with members of the public who have come to handle business waiting in orderly fashion according to their appointment times. Inside the government services hall of Guangfeng District, Shangrao City, Jiangxi Province, a military service station filled with military-themed elements stands out prominently. To make it more convenient for prospective recruits and their parents to seek consultation, the district relocated the military service station from the district people's armed forces department into the services hall at the beginning of the year.

"Doing military service work well and attracting ambitious young people to sign up and join the military requires joint efforts from both the military and local authorities." A conscription worker introduced that in recent years, military service organs and relevant local departments have continuously strengthened coordination and pooled their efforts. Anhui Province is advancing the integrated construction of grassroots armed forces departments and veterans service stations, building a service and support platform covering "enlistment—active service—discharge"; the two localities of Sichuan and Chongqing have introduced online processing of political reviews conducted in locations other than one's place of registration, establishing a cross-provincial conscription political review channel so that data "does more of the running" and prospective recruits "do less of the running"; Guangdong Province, Shaanxi Province, and other localities have optimized employment placement and preferential treatment policies for military family members of college student soldiers, encouraging large numbers of young people to sign up enthusiastically.

Short Commentary

Select the Best of the Best and Send Elite Soldiers

■ Zhang Lei

To strengthen the nation, one must first strengthen the military; to strengthen the military, one must first strengthen its soldiers. At present, the new round of technological revolution and military revolution is advancing with each passing day, the form of warfare is accelerating its evolution toward informatization and intelligentization (信息化、智能化), and the organizational form, force structure, and operational methods of the armed forces are undergoing profound transformation. The requirements for the specialization and composite capability (专业化、复合化) of recruits are growing ever higher, and the demand for high-quality recruits is growing ever more urgent.

To do well the great and important task of recruit conscription, one must continuously expand the "reservoir" of high-quality recruits. In conscription work, military service organs must deeply tap the potential of quality recruits, actively mobilize outstanding young people to sign up and join the military enthusiastically, and, through a scientific and rigorous selection mechanism, guard the checkpoints of political reliability, physical condition, psychological fitness, and educational background, selecting the best of the best, so that every new soldier delivered can withstand the test.

On the basis of "selecting well," one must also "deliver with precision." To drive the precise alignment of the recruit "supply side" with the military's "demand side," military service organs must also strengthen communication with the troops, scientifically formulate conscription plans, clear the fast lane for precise delivery, send quality recruits to the most suitable posts, and better bring into play the role of high-quality recruits.

Deliver good soldiers to the military, select elite soldiers for war, let the headwaters of high-quality recruits surge forth in abundance, and provide solid talent support for the goal of a strong and prosperous military.

Original Chinese
全国兵役机关聚焦练兵备战，严把兵役关口，提高兵员质量—— 把更多优秀青年送到火热军营 ■解放军报记者 朱宏博 张 蕾 “着力把更多优秀青年汇聚到党的旗帜下、输送到人民军队中，切实担负起为军选才、服务强军的神圣使命。”近日，国防部征兵办公室印发《征兵工作人员职业道德准则》，引发广泛关注。其中“广纳五湖四海优秀人才”“以事业取才”“优中选优”等关于选拔兵员的责任要求，不仅让人们看到了新时代征兵工作日益规范化、法治化的趋势，也感受到了兵役机关为征集高素质兵员所展现出的决心与努力。 军队的基础在士兵，士兵的源头在征兵，征兵工作直接影响战斗力生成和军队建设发展。从“两征两退”改革落地到《征兵工作条例》《兵役登记工作规定》等法规制度的修订完善；从创新预征预储模式到推广使用辅助定兵系统；从“五率”考评到构建“五维四型”……近年来，征兵工作发生深刻变化，所有变化指向一点——“聚焦练兵备战，严把征集关口，提高兵员质量，不断为强军胜战提供有力人才支撑”。 “从全国征兵数据看，近些年大学毕业生征集人数和征优比例较过去明显提高，并保持较高水准。2022年大学毕业生征集比例首次突破50%，近3年稳定在70%以上。”国防部征兵办公室工作人员介绍，“以大学生为重点，突出各级各类学校毕业生征集”是近年来全国征兵工作的一贯要求，特别是部队急需专业人才以及拥有相关技能证书的适龄青年，被多地兵役机关列为“优先征集、优先定兵”对象。 今年上半年征兵数据显示：山东省禹城市近3年大学生征集比例一直保持在85%以上，江苏省苏州市相城区近两年大学生征集比例保持在90%左右。作为征兵工作主阵地，高校也在着力提升动员质效和兵员征集率。清华大学、南开大学等“双一流”大学学生入伍热情持续高涨。 7月上旬，安徽省六安市5条征兵宣传主题公交专线正式投入运营，成为穿梭于城市大街小巷的流动征兵课堂。“城市公交是直接面向群众的宣传窗口，也是我们打造征兵宣传‘移动展台’的新载体。”该市征兵办负责人介绍，优选兵员，先要扩大预征基数。形式新颖、覆盖面广的征兵宣传至关重要。下半年征兵工作启动后，他们联合市公共交通公司，精心选取贯通城区、覆盖多所院校的5条线路，将征兵信息和国防资讯融入市民日常出行，拓宽征兵宣传的覆盖面。 年初，东北三省结合冰雪旅游季打造征兵宣传新场景，吉林省长春市新民大街上的“杨靖宇支队战旗”冰雕旁，夜市征兵宣传点人头攒动，哈尔滨工程大学师生绘制的巨幅福建舰和三型舰载机冰雪画火爆“出圈”。东部沿海的福建省，以及西南地区的贵州、重庆等省（市），借助“村超”“村BA”等大众体育赛事，以及无人机灯光秀表演，带火了征兵宣传，也燃旺了适龄青年的报名热情。 “你对我市征兵工作的哪个方面最满意？”年初，山东省临沂市征兵办组织征兵工作问卷调查，“智能化应征青年体质体能评估模式”受到预征青年广泛点赞。应征青年体质体能评估是确保兵员质量的关键关口。以往，该项工作通常采取人工掐表、口头计数等方式进行。今年，临沂市全面推开使用智能设备，精准判读和评估应征青年体重指数、肺活量、平衡能力，以及跳绳、立定跳远、1000米跑等体能水平，精准体现应征青年能力素质，有效杜绝人为干预。 “征兵工作的每一份公平公正，都在厚植国防和军队建设的群众根基；每一缕清风正气，都在提振全社会关心国防、热爱军队的拥军热情。”山东省征兵办工作人员介绍，该省通过压实各级征兵体检责任、面向全社会选聘廉洁征兵监督员、组织家长参与定兵会、完善军地联合政治考核制度等举措，持续推进“阳光征兵”落地落实，确保征集过程公开透明、兵员素质精准评估、兵员去向科学分配。 民政、社保、税务等10多个业务窗口一字排开，前来办理业务的群众按照预约时间，有序等待办理业务。江西省上饶市广丰区政务服务大厅内，布满军旅元素的兵役服务站格外醒目。为方便应征青年和家长咨询，年初该区将兵役服务站从区人武部搬进了服务大厅。 “做好兵役服务，吸引有志青年报名参军，需要军地共同努力。”一名征兵工作人员介绍，近年来，兵役机关与地方有关部门不断加强协调、凝聚合力。安徽省推进基层武装部与退役军人服务站融合建设，打造“入伍—在伍—退伍”服务保障平台；川渝两地推出异地政治考核网上办理，建立跨省征兵政治考核通道，让数据“多跑路”、应征青年“少跑腿”；广东省、陕西省等地优化大学生士兵就业安置、军属优待政策，激励广大青年踊跃报名。 短 评 优中选优送精兵 ■张 蕾 强国必先强军，强军必先强兵。当前，新一轮科技革命和军事革命日新月异，战争形态加速向信息化、智能化演进，军队组织形态、力量结构、作战方式正在发生深刻变革，对兵员的专业化、复合化要求越来越高，对高素质兵员的需求越来越迫切。 做好兵员征集这项大事、要事，就要不断扩容高素质兵员的“蓄水池”。在征兵工作中，兵役机关要深挖优质兵员潜力，积极动员优秀青年踊跃报名参军，以科学严格的选拔机制，守好政治、身体、心理、文化等关口，优中选优，让输送的每一名新兵都经得起考验。 在“选得好”的基础上，更要“送得准”。推动兵员“供给侧”精准对接部队“需求侧”，兵役机关还需加强与部队沟通，科学制订征集计划，畅通精准输送的快车道，将优质兵员送到最合适的岗位上，更好发挥高素质兵员的作用。 为军送好兵、为战点精兵，让高质量兵员的源头活水竞相奔涌，为强军兴军提供坚实的人才支撑。
