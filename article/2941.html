About this analysis
Summary
A camouflage detachment of an unidentified Army brigade conducted a 33-hour field exercise in which personnel concealed a command post and vehicle column against infrared and satellite reconnaissance, with Sergeant First Class Liu Meng also serving as an instructor at a group army engineering support backbone training course (集团军工程保障骨干集训) earlier this year. The article documents a specific institutional problem the detachment has been working through: traditional smoke-screen and visual camouflage methods failed against infrared reconnaissance in a previous exercise, exposing vehicle heat signatures and personnel through the smoke screen, and the unit has since pursued cross-branch study, model-building for equipment recognition, and integration of unmanned systems to address that gap. The framing—emphasizing that new equipment raises efficiency but cannot replace craft knowledge—points to ongoing tension between technology fielding and the foundational skills needed to exploit it.

**Editor's Note: The True Color of Youth**

■ Yang Yue

How many forms should youth take? How many colors?

In the blazing years of military service, some people's youth means galloping across the battlefield, facing the smoke of gunfire, charging forward in the eyes of thousands; others' youth means hiding in the wilderness, keeping company with grass and breeze, quietly taking root in a corner where no one notices.

For the officers and soldiers of the camouflage specialty (伪装专业), who spend every day manufacturing illusions, the color of youth seems to resemble that unremarkable camouflage net. Yet they interpret, in their own way, a sincere and pure true color of youth (青春底色)——

Laying camouflage and correcting details requires an enormous amount of painstaking, foundational work; they fulfill every duty of their post with a down-to-earth "authenticity" (真实). Without the hot-blooded excitement of cannon fire, far from the spotlight of cameras, they hone their camouflage skills with a single-minded "sincerity" (真心). For the sake of victory, they are willing to serve as the cornerstone; with a "genuine devotion" (真情) to strengthening the military, they build a barrier that protects the battlefield safety of their comrades……

Youth has never had a single template. Making a resounding impact is struggle; silent perseverance is also struggle. For the broad ranks of young officers and soldiers at different posts with different responsibilities, as long as they "integrate personal pursuits into the cause of the Party and the nation, seizing every moment and not squandering their prime years," every posture and every choice will be transformed into the most moving true color of youth.

Pave the road with youth; let ideals shine. At their posts, these officers and soldiers who conceal the real and display the false tell us through their perseverance and choices: as long as one holds belief in one's heart and keeps running, even an ordinary youth can write an extraordinary chapter of army-building (强军篇章).

**Feature**

**The Heartfelt Account of a Group of "Camouflage Soldiers"**

■ Zhu Jun, PLA Daily Special Correspondent Liao Xiaobin, Ni Xinlei

*A camouflage detachment of an Army brigade conducts smoke-screen camouflage training. Photo by Yuan Dekun.*

In the early summer, the sun blazes like fire. At a field training site of an Army brigade, the main force has yet to assemble, but the battle belonging to the "camouflage soldiers" has already quietly begun.

The camouflage detachment has a tradition: every year during field training and exercises, they are the first to set out and the last to return to camp. Because of the special nature of camouflage missions, the detachment's officers and soldiers keep company with wilderness and mountain forests year-round—"sweating all over on sunny days, covered in mud on rainy days" is their normal state.

When the reporter asked "isn't this kind of life exhausting?" they simply grinned: "We are the protective barrier for our comrades. Sweat more in peacetime, and there will be less bloodshed in wartime!"

As "battlefield magicians" (战地魔术师) who conceal the real and display the false, the camouflage soldiers' mission is not to create brilliance but to hide targets and deceive the enemy. On the smoke-filled battlefield, these officers and soldiers who are skilled at "fabricating falsehoods" use the most sincere emotions and the most steadfast efforts to build a line of defense that their comrades trust, and to make ordinary youth shine with dazzling light.

**"Set the false image before the adversary sees the truth, and you can outrun time and seize the initiative"**

"Faster, faster!" The thing the officers and soldiers of an Army brigade's camouflage detachment are most familiar with is the sound of urging that frequently rings in their ears.

"Set the false image before the adversary sees the truth, and you can outrun time and seize the initiative." Staff Sergeant First Class (一级上士) Feng Junlin still remembers how, when he first transferred into the camouflage specialty, he found it very difficult to adapt to this fast pace. The first time he executed a false-target emplacement mission, he was still waiting for assignment instructions when he discovered that his fellow soldiers had already launched into action at full speed. "Every minute earlier we get the false position set up, the real position gains one more minute of safety," an old soldier, seeing Feng Junlin's confusion, told him.

It was the first time Feng Junlin realized that he was not completing a task—he was racing against an invisible adversary. An exercise not long afterward gave him an even deeper appreciation of this "race."

That day, the detachment had just arrived at the target area when an urgent report came over the radio: "Enemy reconnaissance satellite is about to pass overhead!"

The difficulty of completing concealment camouflage on all vehicles in the shortest possible time went without saying. Feng Junlin watched every comrade running and working frantically: one person had worn blisters on his hands but gritted his teeth and kept gripping the camouflage rope; another, heedless of being covered in mud, lay flat on a dirt mound tending to camouflage details…By the time the satellite passed overhead, the vehicle column had long since merged with the mountain forest.

Feng Junlin sat on the ground, exhausted, gasping for breath, yet his inner understanding of the urgency inherent to this post had grown ever deeper—for a "camouflage soldier," every race against the clock is a matter of buying a survival window for comrades and seizing the initiative for the battlefield.

From that point on, he began training with a stopwatch—from fabrication to emplacement, he repeatedly refined every step, working to compress the time to the absolute minimum. Through repeated humble consultations and optimization of details, Feng Junlin quickly worked out a method of operation that was both fast and reliable. "But this doesn't mean I've already done my best. To guarantee 'outrunning' the adversary, I must always keep the string of saving time taut," he said.

Private (列兵) Ji Poke Zi also has his own understanding of "racing against time."

The first time Ji Poke Zi participated in a command post camouflage mission, he ran straight into a typhoon. At the mission site, the squad leader's urging mixed with the sound of lashing rain and hurried footsteps, leaving him not a moment to relax.

In the heavy rain, Ji Poke Zi went back and forth again and again to reinforce the fixing poles of the camouflage net. The rain blurred his vision; the camouflage net rose and fell with the howling wind; his heart could not help but tighten along with it.

"As the advance force for the exercise, only after we finish can the follow-on detachments enter the area. We must buy time for them!" Squad Leader Liu Yifan told Ji Poke Zi. In that moment, Ji Poke Zi suddenly understood: their race against the clock was not to respond to the squad leader's urging, but to shoulder that weighty responsibility.

After the rain cleared, the mission was accomplished perfectly. On the return journey, every comrade's face showed exhaustion, yet Ji Poke Zi saw in it a sense of pride—an emotion rooted in a "camouflage soldier's" recognition of the value of his own post.

Very soon, Liu Yifan noticed that Ji Poke Zi seemed to have "become a different person": training no longer needed urging; he voluntarily put in extra practice during rest time; he even proactively sought out veteran cadre to ask professional questions. Not long ago, higher authorities organized a "all-round top performer" (全能尖子) competition, and Ji Poke Zi stood out in company- and battalion-level assessments, becoming the only conscript soldier to earn an "entry ticket."

**"Only when every detail is taken to the utmost in authenticity can a more precise camouflage be achieved"**

For the camouflage detachment, precision is the "high standard" (高标) that must be upheld in camouflage operations.

Every time a camouflage net is laid, Staff Sergeant Second Class (二级上士) Ji Hei Wa Tie steps back a distance and carefully observes from different angles. He patiently adjusts every detail, as if tending to a work of art nearing completion. His apprentice, Sergeant (中士) Shangguan Zijun, assists at his side while also offering his own suggestions for deeper camouflage.

When Shangguan Zijun first enlisted, he did not understand Ji Hei Wa Tie's "pickiness": "The net is laid and the colors roughly match—isn't that enough?"

Then one training day, after painstakingly completing the camouflage of a simulated observation post, Shangguan Zijun's face already showed fatigue. Ji Hei Wa Tie flew a drone up into the sky, then handed him the remote control: "Help me check the camouflage effect." Looking at the image on the screen, Shangguan Zijun was suddenly stunned—the area they had been busy with for several hours had now completely merged with the surrounding mountain forest; the outline of the observation post had vanished entirely from view.

"Beyond astonishment, what I felt more was the power that comes from striving for perfection (精益求精)," Shangguan Zijun reflected. "Only when every detail is taken to the utmost in authenticity can a more precise camouflage be achieved." From that day on, he too learned from his "master" Ji Hei Wa Tie, carefully scrutinizing the surrounding environment and becoming exacting about every detail.

For Staff Sergeant First Class Cui Penghui, the meaning of "precision" is embodied in the study of weather conditions.

Smoke-screen obscuration (发烟遮障) is an important means of camouflage operations, with very high requirements for the timing, angle, and density of release. Cui Penghui still remembers a mission in which, due to an error in predicting wind direction and speed, the smoke screen he and his comrades had set was blown open at a critical moment. Although they quickly closed the gap, that brief exposure still allowed the adversary to capture key information.

This setback sounded an alarm for Cui Penghui. From then on, he kept meteorological observation instruments with him at all times, recording data on wind direction, wind speed, humidity, and temperature at every training session, repeatedly studying the patterns of smoke behavior under different weather conditions.

Dedicated effort pays off. The following year, Cui Penghui accurately predicted a wind direction change during an exercise, seamlessly connecting the smoke screen with the morning mist, and the target was never detected throughout the entire operation.

"Precision on the battlefield is won through countless rigorous repetitions on the training ground," Company Commander Chen Qi told the reporter. To calibrate an error of one centimeter or one degree of angle, the "camouflage soldiers" study diligently and refine repeatedly. Though they carry no guns or artillery, they have placed an invisible safety lock on combat effectiveness through precision and meticulousness.

In recent years, as technology has been updated, various new types of equipment have been progressively fielded to the camouflage detachment, and the efficiency of camouflage operations has improved substantially. In Chen Qi's view, the tools have been upgraded, but that relentless pursuit of detail can never be replaced.

"New equipment has improved our camouflage efficiency, but the difficult problems in camouflage operations still require the most sincere craftsmanship (匠心) to resolve," he said.

*Detachment officers and soldiers carry camouflage nets. Photo by Yuan Dekun.*

**"Persisting in studying the craft of 'fabricating falsehoods' is for the purpose of forging genuine skills that will not be eliminated by the times"**

In the early part of this year, Sergeant First Class (三级军士长) Liu Meng was unusually busy.

It was the period of a group army engineering support backbone training course (集团军工程保障骨干集训), and as an instructor for the training class, he incorporated all the tactical case studies he had personally experienced into his lesson plans, imparting his professional knowledge without reservation.

In the classroom, an unexpected question sent Liu Meng into reflection: "Can traditional military camouflage methods cope with modern high-technology reconnaissance means?"

Looking out at the young faces below the podium, he proactively shared with the students a personal experience.

That year, Liu Meng and his comrades received a camouflage mission. Using dozens of old-style smoke canisters, they built a dense white smoke screen that quickly made the position "disappear" into the haze.

Just as they thought the mission had been accomplished brilliantly, the exercise control group (导调组) projected onto the large screen the footage captured by an infrared reconnaissance aircraft—through these "eyes" capable of seeing heat, the outline of the command post, the not-yet-cooled engines of vehicles, and even the heat-radiating human bodies were all clearly visible to the camera.

"We blocked the naked eye, but we could not block the eye of technology." That image lodged itself in Liu Meng's heart like a sharp thorn. It coldly announced a fact: old methods cannot cope with the new battlefield.

From that point on, Liu Meng and his comrades actively coordinated with reconnaissance detachments to conduct joint training, proactively studied the principles of their advanced equipment, and attempted to optimize camouflage effects by borrowing the reconnaissance perspective, researching corresponding countermeasures.

"Persisting in studying the craft of 'fabricating falsehoods' is for the purpose of forging genuine skills that will not be eliminated by the times." At the end of his lecture, Liu Meng said this solemnly. His words were like a stone cast into water, sending ripples through the hearts of the students.

Sergeant Tan Xuhu recalled an exercise several years ago in which higher authorities ordered them to set up a false artillery position. Tan Xuhu, then a Private First Class, worked with his comrades through great hardship to fabricate a series of false targets, but because they did not understand the configuration patterns of a real artillery position—the spacing between guns, the location of ammunition sites, the layout of personnel shelters—the "position" they set up was seen through by the exercise control group at a glance.

"That lesson made me understand that there are still many gaps in the knowledge we possess. To deceive the adversary, we must manufacture plausible false appearances according to the logic of the real battlefield," Tan Xuhu reflected.

After that, he and his comrades began systematically studying the knowledge of various arms and services in their spare time beyond daily training. They came to understand not only the tactical-level "order of battle" (布阵图) but also deeply researched the dimensions, external appearance, and radar signatures of different types of equipment—all so that every detail they fabricated could withstand the test of actual combat.

It was also because of this experience that this group of "camouflage soldiers" began to cultivate a shared hobby: handcrafting military models.

"Once you've worked through it with your hands, you have it in your heart. On the battlefield, you can do it both fast and accurately." Tan Xuhu picked up a finely detailed model and said earnestly to the reporter.

Day after day and night after night of persistent study transformed into solid confidence on the battlefield. In recent years, the "camouflage soldiers" have converted newly learned technologies and knowledge into a series of practical innovations. In a recent exercise, the brigade's camouflage detachment employed unmanned technology means to conduct camouflage operations within the prescribed time, successfully countering multiple "enemy" reconnaissance methods and accomplishing the mission with distinction. On the journey of army-building (强军征程), this group of "camouflage soldiers" writes, with running strides and tireless struggle, a chapter of youth that honors their mission and does not squander their prime years.

**Eyewitness Account**

**From "Finding the Adversary" to "Concealing Oneself"**

■ Lieutenant Colonel Zhao Lu

That year, I came from the headquarters reconnaissance section to serve in the camouflage detachment. With the change of post, my understanding also underwent an iteration.

Previously, my mission was to study how to find the adversary by following the faintest of clues. In the face of the ever-proliferating modern reconnaissance means, many traditional methods of concealment are difficult to bring into play—heat "speaks," color "informs," and radar waves can easily see through simple false appearances. As technological means have been revolutionized, the battlefield has become increasingly transparent.

Arriving at the camouflage detachment with this understanding, my sense of urgency was intense. Simply "covering" the target clearly does not work; we must think about how to make it blend into the environment, to make something that does not naturally belong there appear reasonable.

So we began attempting to give traditional camouflage methods the wings of technology, incorporating various new types of equipment into operations. This was a process full of challenges, like learning an entirely new language.

In one exercise, we leveraged new technology to successfully create the false impression of "heavy troop concentration" (重兵集结) in a secondary direction. When I heard the report that the "enemy" reconnaissance forces had been effectively tied down, what surged in my heart was not only excitement, but also a calmer, more solid sense of achievement.

In modern warfare, the value of "camouflage soldiers" extends far beyond "hiding"—they must also learn to "weave a fog of war" (编织迷雾). In this cognitive iteration from "offense" to "defense," my post has changed, but my mission has become clearer: in the dialectical thinking of spear and shield, to forge battlefield armor that leaves the enemy "unable to see clearly, unable to strike accurately, unable to penetrate" (看不清、打不准、攻不透).

**First-Hand Scene**

**Thirty-Three Hours Hidden in the Mountain Forest**

■ PLA Daily Special Correspondents Liao Xiaobin and Ni Xinlei

The night was dark as ink; one after another, armored vehicles threaded through the silent mountain forest. As the exercise got underway, a camouflage detachment of an Army brigade received its mission orders and set out first, heading under cover of darkness toward the designated position.

"The camouflage detachment bears the heavy responsibility of protecting key targets and deceiving enemy reconnaissance. From static camouflage to dynamic protection, from single backgrounds to complex environments, we must constantly challenge the limits of vision and engage in sustained contest with enemy reconnaissance means," the detachment commander told the reporter.

For "camouflage soldiers," camouflage is an art filled with wisdom—they must conceal the real within nature and dissolve targets into the environment. At the exercise work site, the reporter directly experienced the complexity and precision of this "art."

After arriving at the designated area, Sergeant First Class Liu Meng and his comrades immediately began constructing the command post. The sound of metal frames colliding rang out continuously; everyone was busy with their tasks. By the time the command post framework had taken initial shape, the first light of dawn was already showing on the horizon. Liu Meng told the reporter that the real test had just begun—they had to make the entire command post and several vehicles and equipment disappear from the "enemy's" field of vision within the prescribed time.

Very soon, the detachment's officers and soldiers once again threw themselves into a race against time. They rapidly unloaded camouflage nets and various equipment, completing measurement, erection, and splicing operations in succession.

Private First Class Du Zuodong followed Squad Leader Liu Meng in carefully dressing the camouflage net; his fingertips were cut with fine marks by grass blades. "Even one centimeter off could expose us!" Liu Meng earnestly cautioned. The reporter crouched down to observe their work: the orientation of every leaf, the bend of every tuft of dry grass, every point of connection with the vegetation—all of it reflected a pursuit of battlefield precision.

As night fell again, to ensure the concealment effect, camouflage operations had to continue. Liu Meng divided the team into two groups working in rotation; while one group was active, the other seized the scattered intervals to rest. After the officers and soldiers had worked through the night, the command post and vehicles and equipment finally merged completely into the mountain forest, becoming difficult to distinguish. Immediately, Liu Meng used a drone equipped with an infrared lens to conduct an all-around inspection of the camouflaged area. On the screen, the target area was completely consistent in characteristics with the surrounding environment.

"Camouflage effect outstanding!" Hearing the final result, smiles broke out on the faces of the officers and soldiers. Du Zuodong wiped a smear of mud from his face; his eyes were full of pride. After thirty-three consecutive hours of hard fighting, they had completed the camouflage mission to a high standard with wisdom and sweat.

In that moment, a phrase rose in the reporter's mind: to manifest value within concealment; to accumulate the force of thunder in silence.

For this group of "camouflage soldiers," mountains, rivers, grass, and trees bear witness to their wisdom and tenacity; camouflage coatings speak of their focus and dedication. Their existence is like that camouflage net that blends into the environment—unassuming, yet indispensable……

“作为演训的先行部队，只有我们干完了，后续分队的战友才能进场。我们必须为他们争取时间！”班长刘一帆对吉坡科子说。那一刻，吉坡科子忽然明白：他们的争分夺秒，不是为了应对班长的催促，而是为了担起那份沉甸甸的责任。 雨过天晴，任务圆满完成。返程时，每名战友脸上都挂着疲惫，吉坡科子却从中看到了一种自豪感——那份情感，源于一名“伪装兵”对自身岗位价值的认同。 很快，刘一帆发现吉坡科子好像“变了一个人”：训练不再需要催促，休息时间也自觉加练，甚至主动向老骨干请教专业问题。前不久，上级组织“全能尖子”比武，吉坡科子在营连两级考核中脱颖而出，成为唯一一名获得“入场券”的义务兵。 “只有每个细节都极尽真实，才能实现更加精密的伪装” 对伪装分队来说，精准，是伪装作业中必须恪守的“高标”。 每次伪装网铺设完成，二级上士吉黑挖铁都会向后退出一段距离，从不同角度仔细观察。他耐心调整着每一处细节，仿佛对待一件即将完成的艺术品。他的徒弟、中士上官子俊在一旁协助，同时对深度伪装提出自己的建议。 刚入伍时，上官子俊并不理解吉黑挖铁的“较真”：“网铺好了，颜色差不多不就行了？” 直到一次训练日，在辛苦完成一处模拟观察哨的伪装后，上官子俊脸上已露出疲惫。吉黑挖铁操纵着无人机飞上天空，随后把遥控器递给了他：“你帮我看看伪装效果。”看到屏幕上的画面，上官子俊一下子愣住了——那片他们忙活了数小时的区域，此刻和周围山林完全融为一体，观察哨的轮廓在视野中消隐无踪。 “除了惊叹，更多的是感受到精益求精带来的力量。”上官子俊感慨道，“只有每个细节都极尽真实，才能实现更加精密的伪装。”从那天起，他也学着“师傅”吉黑挖铁的样子，认真审视周围环境，对所有细节较真起来。 对一级上士崔鹏辉来说，“精准”的意义，体现在对天候的钻研。 发烟遮障是伪装作业的重要手段，对释放时机、角度和浓度的要求都很高。崔鹏辉还记得曾经一次任务，由于对风向风速预判偏差，他和战友设置的烟幕在关键时刻被吹出缺口。虽然他们迅速将缺口补上，但那短暂的暴露，依然让对手捕捉到关键信息。 这次失利，为崔鹏辉敲响了警钟。自此，他将气象观测器材带在身边，每次训练都要把风向、风速、湿度、温度等数据记录下来，反复琢磨不同天候条件下的发烟规律。 功夫不负有心人。次年，崔鹏辉在演练中精准预判风向变化，将烟幕与晨雾衔接得天衣无缝，整个行动期间目标始终未被发现。 “战场上的精准，是训练场上无数次严苛练习换来的。”某连连长陈琪告诉记者。为了校准一厘米、一度角的误差，“伪装兵”们刻苦钻研、反复打磨。虽然手中没有枪炮，但他们用精准和细致，为战斗力上了一道无形的安全锁。 近年来，随着技术更新，各型新装备陆续列装伪装分队，伪装作业的效率也大幅提高。在陈琪看来，工具升级了，那份对细节的极致追求却永远无法被取代。 “新装备为我们提升了伪装效率，但伪装作业中的难题，仍然需要用最真诚的匠心化解。”他说。 分队官兵搬运伪装网。袁德坤摄 “坚持研究‘造假’的学问，是为了淬炼不被时代淘汰的真本领” 今年初那段时间，三级军士长刘盟异常忙碌。 时值集团军工程保障骨干集训，作为集训队教员，他把亲历过的战法案例全部融入教案，将专业知识倾囊相授。 课堂上，一个意料之外的提问，让刘盟陷入回忆——“传统的军事伪装手段，能否应对现代高科技侦察手段？” 望着台下一张张年轻的面庞，他主动向学员分享了自己的一段经历。 那年，刘盟和战友们受领伪装任务。他们用几十个老式发烟罐，筑起一道浓重的白色烟幕，很快让阵地“消失”在烟雾中。 就在他们以为任务完成得很出色时，导调组把红外侦察机拍摄的画面投到大屏幕上——透过这双能看到热量的“眼睛”，指挥所的轮廓、车辆尚未冷却的引擎，乃至散发着温度的人体，在镜头之下全都清晰可见。 “我们挡住了肉眼，却挡不住科技的眼睛。”那个画面像一根尖锐的刺，深深扎进刘盟心里。它冰冷地宣告了一个事实：老办法，应对不了新战场。 自那以后，刘盟和战友们积极协调侦察分队开展协同训练，主动学习他们的先进装备原理，并尝试借侦察视角优化伪装效果，研究对应的反制措施。 “坚持研究‘造假’的学问，是为了淬炼不被时代淘汰的真本领。”授课最后，刘盟郑重地说。他的话像一颗石子，在学员们心底激荡起阵阵涟漪。 中士谭旭虎想起数年前一次演训中，上级命令他们设置假炮兵阵地。当时还是上等兵的谭旭虎和战友一起，费尽千辛万苦制作了一系列假目标，却因不了解真实炮兵阵地的火炮间距、弹药所位置、人员掩体布局等配置规律，导致设置的“阵地”被导调组一眼识破。 “那次教训让我明白，我们掌握的知识还有很多空缺。要想骗过对手，必须按真实战场的逻辑来制造合理假象。”谭旭虎感慨地说。 此后，他和战友开始在日常训练之余，系统学习各兵种知识。他们既了解战术层面的“布阵图”，还深入研究不同装备的尺寸、外形与雷达特征，只为让营造的每一个细节都经得起实战检验。 也是因为这次经历，这群“伪装兵”开始培养出一个共同的业余爱好——手工制作军事模型。 “手上过一遍，心里才有底。上了战场，才能做得又快又像。”谭旭虎拿起一架细节精致的模型，认真地对记者说。 一个个坚持学习的日夜，变成了战场上实实在在的底气。近年来，“伪装兵”们把学习到的新技术新知识，转化成一项项实用的创新成果。前不久一场演训中，该旅伪装分队在规定时间内运用无人技术手段进行伪装作业，成功防范“敌人”多种侦察手段，圆满完成任务。强军征程上，这群“伪装兵”用奔跑的脚步、不懈的奋斗，努力书写着不负使命、不负韶华的青春篇章。 亲历者说 从“找对手”到“藏自己” ■中校 赵路 那年，我从机关侦察科来到伪装分队任职。随着岗位调整，我的认知也经历了一次迭代。 过去，我的任务是研究怎么依靠蛛丝马迹找到对手。在层出不穷的现代侦察手段面前，许多传统的隐蔽方式都难以发挥作用——热量会“说话”，颜色会“告密”，雷达波能轻易识破简单的假象。随着技术手段的革新，战场变得越来越透明。 带着这样的认知来到伪装分队，我的紧迫感十分强烈。光是把目标“盖住”显然不管用，我们得思考如何让它融入环境，让原本不存在于此的事物变得合理。 于是，我们开始尝试为传统的伪装手段插上科技的翅膀，将各种新式装备融入作业中。这是一个充满挑战的过程，就像在学习一门全新的语言。 一次演练，我们借助新技术，在次要方向成功营造了“重兵集结”的假象。听到“敌”侦察力量被有效牵制的通报时，我心中涌起的不仅是兴奋，还是一种更沉静、扎实的成就感。 现代战争中，“伪装兵”的价值远不止于“藏”，更要学会“编织迷雾”。在这场由“攻”到“防”的认知迭代中，我的岗位虽然变了，使命却更加清晰：在矛与盾的辩证思考中，锻造出让敌人“看不清、打不准、攻不透”的战场护甲。 （解放军报特约记者廖晓彬整理） 第一现场 隐入山林的三十三小时 ■解放军报特约记者 廖晓彬 倪鑫磊 夜色如墨，一辆辆战车穿梭于寂静的山林。随着演训拉开序幕，陆军某旅伪装分队接到任务指令后率先出发，在夜色掩映中赶往预定阵地。 “伪装分队肩负着保护关键目标、迷惑敌方侦察的重任。从静态伪装到动态防护，从单一背景到复杂环境，我们必须不断挑战视觉的极限，与敌侦察手段持续博弈。”该分队分队长告诉记者。 对“伪装兵”来说，伪装是一门充满智慧的艺术——他们需要将真实隐匿于自然，让目标消融于环境。在演训的作业现场，记者切实感受到了这门“艺术”的繁琐与精细。 到达预定地域后，三级军士长刘盟和战友第一时间开始构筑指挥所。金属支架的碰撞声不断响起，每个人都忙于操作。当指挥所框架初步成型，天边已是晨曦微露。刘盟告诉记者，真正的考验刚刚开始——他们要在规定时间内，让整个指挥所及数台车辆装备从“敌人”视野中消失。 很快，分队官兵再次投入与时间的赛跑。他们快速卸下伪装网和各类器材，连续完成测量、架设、拼接等作业。 上等兵杜作东跟随班长刘盟仔细修饰着伪装网，指尖被草叶划出道道细痕。“差一厘米都可能暴露！”刘盟认真叮嘱道。记者蹲下身观察他们的作业，每一片树叶的朝向、每一束枯草的弯度、与植被的每一处衔接，都呈现出对战场精度的追求。
