The Finest Revolutionary Soldiers of the New Era | Running One's Own Leg of the Race on the Road to Joint Operations — A Profile of Li Can, "Finest New-Era Revolutionary Soldier"

最美新时代革命军人风采丨在联合作战征程上跑好自己这一棒——记“最美新时代革命军人”李灿
PLA Daily (解放军报) 2 August 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
Li Can, a Central Theater Command joint operations staff officer and former Army battalion commander, was profiled by Xinhua on August 2 as a 2026 '最美新时代革命军人' (Finest New-Era Revolutionary Soldier) honoree, credited with developing a multi-echelon synchronous coordination work mode (多级同步统筹工作模式), a tactical map-marking integrated work ruler, and a multi-dimensional dynamic calibration mechanism, and with leading a theater-wide all-element combat readiness baseline survey over three years. The profile documents a persistent institutional problem the PLA has named but not resolved: Army officers transferred into joint headquarters arrive with deep single-service expertise and acute 'capability panic' (his term) when confronted with multi-domain situational data, requiring years of remedial cross-branch immersion before they can function as genuine joint planners. The framing of Li Can's manual-review insistence at key planning junctures as a virtue, rather than a limitation of intelligent tools, points to ongoing command-system reliability concerns that the theater is managing through individual discipline rather than structural solutions.
Translation

In the height of summer, July, deep in the night. The clock hand passes midnight, yet a certain center of the Central Theater Command remains ablaze with light. On the electronic situation display screen, red and blue markers surge and interweave across the map, like waves of battle rolling through the dark. Staff officer Li Can fixes his gaze on the screen, fingertips sliding across it, freezing on a key node of a typical combat case, extracting a segment of on-the-spot decision-making of particular reference value and filing it into his combat case database…… This is an ordinary day for him, and the normal state of his work.

In 2023, through successive rounds of selection, Li Can was transferred from an Army battalion commander position into the Central Theater Command headquarters.

Yet standing in the Theater Command's joint operations command hall, watching screens filled with multi-service situational data, Li Can — well known within the Army as a top-ranked "martial champion (武状元)" — was seized by an acute sense of capability panic: "Before, it was enough to master knowledge within one's own service domain. Coming to the Theater Command, you have to understand and grasp all the knowledge involved in joint operations."

The "knowledge gaps" kept multiplying. Driven by the urgency of feeling that "one night's sleep might leave you behind," he adapted in the shortest possible time to the Theater Command's rhythm of "running to get up to speed" and "joining the fight on the move."

He became a "regular" at the joint lecture hall, and a "primary school student" chasing down the backbone personnel of every service branch to ask questions. His notebooks were filled, densely packed, with equipment parameters of every type, tactical logic, and key points for coordination.

Memorizing parameters alone was not enough. He flew alongside pilots in simulated training, crouched with armored companies running tactical deductions, and ran with signal troops through communications link debugging…… After that, he had traveled to nearly every front-line unit of every service branch within the theater's jurisdiction, rapidly updating his "brain" through learning. Over three years, he took the lead in conducting a comprehensive baseline survey of all-element combat readiness, gaining thorough familiarity with the principal terrain characteristics, equipment parameters, and coordination links.

"Studying warfare is not about flipping through military history or memorizing procedures." Having worked deeply in the field of warfare study, Li Can holds his own creed: "The traditional approach of studying combat cases in their entirety lets you learn the 'complete process,' but you cannot truly comprehend 'breaking through a desperate situation (绝境破局)'; you become familiar with 'how things go when they go smoothly,' but find it difficult to 'respond to changing circumstances (应对变局).'"

He broke down dozens of typical engagements and combat cases into hundreds of micro-scenarios of key decision-making: a split-second difference in situational judgment, the hairsbreadth trade-offs in resource allocation, second-level errors in coordination adjustments, and the design of contingency plans as a risk backstop — all were extracted individually and examined repeatedly.

For the same battlefield scenario, he repeatedly compared the different directions a battle could take under multiple decision options, mapping out with complete clarity the dividing line between victory and defeat, and the branching points of logic.

To keep his research from floating in the air, he investigated real conditions, solicited opinions, and embedded all research findings into actual plans, using the battlefield as the measuring stick for every detail.

During the preparation for a major mission in 2025, the first-round plan was nearly perfect under conventional conditions, yet Li Can furrowed his brow: "Of course there's no problem if nothing unexpected happens — but what you have to guard against is exactly the unexpected."

He stepped outside the assumptions of ideal conditions and smooth-running situations, sorted out more than a dozen categories of extreme contingencies, broke down the timeline for each category, pinpointed the risk nodes, and reverse-engineered the plan's architecture.

To verify the plan's reliability, he took the lead in organizing stress testing: stripping out key elements, disrupting the pace of execution, layering in successive special situations, forcing the system to expose its weaknesses and reverse-tracing the process to identify and fill gaps.

On the day of the mission, there were zero errors and zero omissions from start to finish, with coordination handoffs precise to the second.

The more solidly one plants one's feet in the direction of war-fighting, the faster new capabilities are generated——

He innovatively proposed a certain auxiliary aiming method that effectively resolved the problem of artillery night-aiming points being easily exposed, improving the efficiency of artillery night fire strikes.

The "tactical map-marking integrated work ruler (战术标图综合作业尺)" he independently developed integrates the commonly used map-marking tools of every service branch, substantially improving the speed and precision of manual map marking.

The multi-dimensional dynamic calibration mechanism he pioneered gives commanders a more reliable basis for coordinating forces and allocating resources.

After years of deep work in joint operations, Li Can has identified a pattern: the winning formula (制胜密码) of joint operations lies in "synchronization, connectivity, and integration (同步、贯通、一体)."

To break through the problems of vertical desynchronization and loose lateral coordination, Li Can traveled to headquarters at every level, grassroots units, and duty posts, collecting practical operational suggestions, and innovatively introduced a certain multi-echelon synchronous coordination work mode (多级同步统筹工作模式), enabling higher-level intent to reach the front line directly and front-line conditions to be fed back in real time, improving the quality and efficiency of operational planning and command.

Facing the tide of intelligentized warfare (智能化战争), Li Can has always maintained a clear-headed awareness.

"Intelligent tools are an aid; they cannot replace the human brain's on-the-spot judgment." Li Can said. In the development of joint operations command, he not only uses intelligent simulation for verification but also insists on manual review at key junctures, correcting deviations and loopholes on multiple occasions.

Li Can's insistence on getting plans exactly right is well known throughout the Theater Command.

During one intensive plan-drafting period, he worked through hundreds of thousands of characters of tactical method case studies, restructured the contingency plan system, deleted multiple redundant sections, added multiple practical combat-handling measures, and produced a practical blueprint for grassroots officers and soldiers in combat readiness preparation.

As a backbone professional, Li Can not only studies and plans for war himself but has also proactively taken on the role of "guide" for new staff officers, frequently taking the initiative to approach them, teaching hands-on and mentoring face-to-face.

In April of this year, the Theater Command organized a joint operations staff officer skills competition and assessment. Li Can proactively took the lead in organizing preparation for the competition, and in the end, four members of his group were commended as "Top Ten Outstanding Staff Officers."

Over 21 years of military service, Li Can has participated in nearly 30 major exercises and training events, been commended as an "Outstanding Staff Officer of the Whole Army (全军优秀参谋)," and awarded first-class merit once, second-class merit twice, and third-class merit twice. This year, Li Can was selected as a 2026 New-Era Youth Vanguard (新时代青年先锋) and commended as a 2026 "Finest New-Era Revolutionary Soldier (最美新时代革命军人)."

Faced with these honors, he has always maintained the purity and clear-headedness he had when he first entered the military.

"The joint staff officer is a critical link in achieving joint victory." He said. "As joint staff officers, we must strive to run our own leg of the race on the road to joint operations."

(Xinhua News Agency, Beijing, August 2)

Original Chinese
盛夏7月，夜已深沉。时针跳过零点，中部战区某中心依然灯火通明。电子态势屏上，红蓝标识在地图上交错奔涌，宛如暗夜里翻涌的战潮。 参谋李灿紧盯屏幕，指尖滑动，定格一场典型战例的关键节点，将其中极具参考价值的临机决策片段单独截取，归入他的战例资料库…… 这是他寻常的一天，也是他工作的常态。 2023年，通过层层选拔，李灿从陆军营长岗位选调进入中部战区机关。 然而，站在战区联指大厅，看着满屏多军种态势数据，作为陆军有名的“武状元”，李灿却产生了强烈的本领恐慌：“以前掌握军种领域相关知识就行。来到战区，联合作战涉及的知识都要了解掌握。” “知识缺口”越来越多。他带着“睡一觉醒来就可能落后”的紧迫感，以最短时间适应了“跑步进入情况”“行进间加入战斗”的战区节奏。 他成了联合讲堂里的“常客”，成了追着各军兵种骨干问问题的“小学生”。他的笔记本上，密密麻麻记满了各型装备参数、战术逻辑、协同要点。 光背参数还不够。伴着飞行员飞模拟训练、蹲在装甲连搞战术推演、跟着通信兵跑链路调试……此后，他几乎跑遍了辖区内军兵种一线部队，通过学习快速更新“大脑”。3年时间，他牵头摸底全要素战备底数，对于主要地形特质、装备参数和协同链路，都了如指掌。 “研战不是翻战史、背流程。”深耕研战领域，李灿有着自己的信条，“传统整段式学战例，学得到‘完整流程’，却悟不透‘绝境破局’；熟悉‘顺利打法’，却难以‘应对变局’。” 他把数十场典型战局战例，拆解成上百个关键决策微场景：态势判断的一念之差、资源调配的毫厘取舍、协同调整的秒级误差、风险兜底的预案设计，都被单独拎出来反复琢磨。 同一个战场场景，他反复对比过多种决策带来的不同战局走向，把胜负的分界点、逻辑的分叉点摸得明明白白。 为了不让研究悬浮在空中，他摸实情、听意见，研究成果全部嵌入实案，拿战场标尺卡每一个细节。 2025年某次大项任务筹备，首轮方案在常规态势下几近完美，李灿却皱起了眉：“没有意外当然没问题，但要防的就是意外。” 他跳出理想条件、顺利态势的预设，梳理出十几类极端突发情况，逐类拆解时间线、抠准风险点，反向倒推方案架构。 为了验明方案的可靠性，他牵头组织压力测试：抽离关键要素、打乱推进节奏、叠加连续特情，逼着体系暴露短板、倒推流程查漏补缺。 任务当天，全程零差错、零疏漏，协同衔接分秒不差。 向战为战的脚步踩得有多实，新的能力生成就有多快—— 他创新提出某辅助瞄准法，较好解决炮兵夜间瞄准点容易暴露的问题，提升了炮兵夜间火力打击效率。 他自主研制的“战术标图综合作业尺”，集成各军兵种标图常用工具，让手工标图的速度和精度大幅提升。 他首创的多维动态校准机制，让指挥员统筹兵力、调配资源有了更可靠的支撑依据。 深耕联战这些年，李灿摸出一个规律：联合作战的制胜密码，藏在“同步、贯通、一体”里。 为破解上下不同步、左右衔接散的问题，李灿跑遍各级机关、基层分队、执勤点位，收集实操建议，创新推出某多级同步统筹工作模式，让上级意图直达一线、一线情况实时反馈，提升了作战筹划指挥质效。 面对智能化战争的浪潮，李灿始终保持着清醒的认知。 “智能工具是辅助，替代不了人脑的临机判断。”李灿说。在联合作战指挥建设中，他不仅用智能仿真验证，还在关键环节坚持人工复核，多次修正偏差漏洞。 李灿对方案的较真，在战区也是出了名的。 一次方案攻坚期，他啃了数十万字的战法案例，重塑预案体系，删掉多处冗余内容，新增多项实战处置举措，成了基层官兵备战的实用蓝本。 作为业务骨干，李灿不仅自己研战谋战，还主动当起了新参谋的“引路人”，经常主动靠上去，手把手教、面对面带。 今年4月，战区组织联合作战参谋比武考核，李灿主动牵头负责备赛，最终所在组有4人荣获“十佳优秀参谋”。 入伍21年，李灿先后参加近30场重大演训，被表彰为“全军优秀参谋”，荣立一等功1次、二等功2次、三等功2次。今年，李灿获评2026年度新时代青年先锋，被表彰为2026年度“最美新时代革命军人”。 面对荣誉，他却始终保持着初入军营时的那份纯粹和清醒。 “联合参谋是联合制胜的关键一环。”他说，“作为联合参谋，我们要在联合作战征程上奋力跑好属于自己的这一棒。” （新华社北京8月2日电）
