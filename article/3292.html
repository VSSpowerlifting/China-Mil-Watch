Political Work

Knowledge and Action Forum | Let Youth Shine Through Striving and Hard Work

知行论坛丨让青春在拼搏奋斗中闪光
PLA Daily (解放军报) 16 August 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
Standard political education content directed at junior PLA officers and soldiers, published in conjunction with the 105th anniversary of the CCP's founding, urging loyalty to the Party, combat-oriented training, and personal integrity. The article invokes several established PLA political-work formulations — including 杀手锏 (trump-card skills), 卡脖子 (chokepoint technologies), and 和平积弊 (chronic ailments of peacetime) — and cites 'Model of the Era' Wang Ji as a model of sustained technical contribution. Its value is as a record of how the PLA frames ideological and professional expectations for young personnel in the current 'decisive victory' phase of the centenary army-building goal, not as evidence of a policy shift or changed operational posture.
Translation

● Integrate personal ideals into the great cause of strengthening the military, strive to run well the critical leg of the relay race that belongs to our generation, and let youth shine through striving and hard work.

In his important speech at the rally celebrating the 105th anniversary of the founding of the Communist Party of China, Chairman Xi offered these words to Chinese youth in the new era: "Integrate personal pursuits into the cause of the Party and the country, seize every moment and not squander your prime years, run well the historical relay race on the new journey, pave the road with youth, and let ideals shine!" At present, the effort to achieve the centenary goal of army-building has entered a critical stage of decisive victory and hard fighting, with tight timelines and heavy tasks. Young officers and soldiers of the military must have the courage to take responsibility and the boldness to act, integrate personal ideals into the great cause of strengthening the military, strive to run well the critical leg of the relay race that belongs to our generation, and let youth shine through striving and hard work.

Temper the character of loyalty. Absolute loyalty to the Party is the foremost political quality of a revolutionary soldier and the political soul of a revolutionary soldier. Looking back at history — from the gunshot that opened the Nanchang Uprising, to the red flags unfurling across Jinggang Mountain; from the arduous trek of the Long March of ten thousand li, to the resistance against foreign aggression in the War of Resistance Against Japan; from the north-and-south campaigns of the War of Liberation, to defending the homeland in the War to Resist U.S. Aggression and Aid Korea — generation after generation of young officers and soldiers wrote, through sacrifice and dedication, a hymn of absolute loyalty to the Party, and created immortal achievements worthy of the era, worthy of the Party, and worthy of the people. Loyalty to the Party stems from theoretical clarity and firmness of faith. Young officers and soldiers must persistently use Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era to forge unity of mind and soul, with emphasis on studying Xi Jinping Thought on Strengthening the Military, mastering the Marxist positions, viewpoints, and methods contained therein, solidly laying the foundation of faith, replenishing the calcium of the spirit, and steadying the helm of thought — ensuring that loyalty to the Party is never adulterated, that following the Party's command admits no discounting, and continuously strengthening the consciousness of dedicating oneself to the Party without reservation. Loyalty to the Party is not a momentary declaration but a lifelong commitment. Young officers and soldiers must internalize listening to the Party and following the Party in their hearts and externalize it in their actions — going wherever the Party directs, doing whatever the Party calls for — and embody loyalty to the Party in concrete actions of sharing the Party's concerns and fulfilling one's duties to the Party.

Strengthen the sense of mission and responsibility. Each generation bears its own historical responsibilities; each generation carries its own mission and responsibility. At present, the great changes unseen in a century are accelerating, our country's security situation faces numerous challenges, and various foreseeable and unforeseeable risks and challenges have increased markedly. Time waits for no one; history waits for no one. Only by tempering strength of will through enduring hardship, forging war-winning capabilities through overcoming difficulties, and strengthening the sense of mission and responsibility by bearing heavy burdens with full awareness can young officers and soldiers shoulder the mission tasks entrusted by the era. They must always keep the enemy situation in mind, always keep their eyes on the adversary, firmly establish the thinking of soldiering for war, training for war, and leading troops for war, focus all thoughts on fighting, and direct all work toward fighting. Adhere to the principle that troops train the way wars are fought and train hard in whatever fighting requires; on the training ground, dare to challenge limits and break through bottlenecks, undergo repeated tempering and repeated forging, and develop "trump card" skills (杀手锏) capable of defeating the enemy with a single blow. Keep a close eye on cutting-edge technologies and disruptive technologies, boldly venture into "no-man's land" (无人区), dare to gnaw on "hard bones" (硬骨头), continuously break through critical "chokepoint" (卡脖子) technologies, and use science and technology empowerment to raise the contribution rate to winning. Thoroughly rectify the chronic ailments of peacetime (和平积弊), develop a strong combat style, and with the fighting spirit of "fearing neither hardship nor death" (一不怕苦、二不怕死) and the blood and courage of "seeking nothing but victory, sparing nothing for victory" (除了胜利一无所求、为了胜利一无所惜), ensure that at any time and under any circumstances, upon a single order, the mission is not dishonored and all tasks assigned by the Party and the people are resolutely completed.

Commit to sustained struggle. Advancing on the journey of military strengthening, the broad ranks of young officers and soldiers need not only lofty aspirations but, even more, diligent and earnest practice. Struggle is not a slogan spoken aloud but the gritting of teeth on the training ground, the pursuit of excellence in tackling tasks, and the quiet perseverance at ordinary posts. "Model of the Era" (时代楷模) Wang Ji, through long-term commitment, cultivated deeply in a "niche track" (冷门赛道): when key algorithms were lacking, he developed them himself; when core tools were absent, he built them himself; using high-level scientific research achievements, he installed one reliable "safety lock" after another on the nation's critical weapons. The spirit of Wang Ji — grounded in his post, feet planted firmly on the ground, quietly plowing forward — is worthy of our emulation. Yet in reality, some comrades aim too high while their hands fall short; they are unwilling to keep their feet on the ground and work hard with their heads down, habitually taking shortcuts, and in the end waste their prime years and accomplish nothing. The cause of military strengthening is fought and forged through hard work. One must firmly believe that "daily progress fears not a journey of ten million li" (日日行不怕千万里), focus on primary responsibilities and principal duties, and stand grounded in the realities of one's post — whether stationed at a sentry post on the snow-covered frontier, or at a position deep in mountains and deserts; whether operating high-precision advanced weapons and equipment, or engaged in day-after-day logistical support — always maintain enthusiasm and pursue excellence, with the dedication of "work in one field, love one field, master one field" (干一行、爱一行、钻一行), refining through repetition and breaking through in perseverance. Be willing to serve as "paving stones" (铺路石) and "tree planters" (栽树人); do more work that has long cycles and slow results but lays foundations and benefits the long term; with the breadth of mind that "success need not be achieved by me" (功成不必在我) and the responsibility that "success will certainly have my part" (功成必定有我), carry on the struggle, strive with full force, and write the youthful answer sheet of serving the country through military service.

Discipline oneself, uphold integrity, and guard against corruption. Young officers and soldiers have grown up in an era of information explosion and pluralistic values — broad in vision and active in thinking, but still limited in experience and relatively lacking in the resolve to resist various temptations and tests. On the road ahead, there are flowers and applause, but also hidden reefs and dangerous shoals. Temptations of fame and gain and the erosion of unhealthy tendencies exist at all times; if the ideological line of defense relaxes even slightly, it becomes very difficult to resist various temptations and tests. Young officers and soldiers must engrave Party discipline and national law upon their hearts and build a solid defensive line against corruption and degeneration deep within. Always maintain a sense of reverence (敬畏之心) — reverence for the organization, reverence for responsibility, reverence for law and discipline — never disturbed by external things, never moved by temptation, ensuring upright conduct through inner clarity. Frequently engage in self-warning and self-reflection. Regularly ask oneself: Are ideals and convictions firm? Is the fulfillment of duties in place? Is adherence to discipline resolute? Is clean and honest self-discipline conscious? Only by daring to self-reflect and being diligent in self-reflection can one promptly brush away the dust of thought and remove the grime of behavior. Pay attention to small matters and minor conduct. "Neglecting small actions will ultimately harm great virtue" (不矜细行，终累大德). If gaps in small matters and minor conduct are not promptly repaired, over time quantitative change will bring about qualitative change, causing the line of defense of clean and honest self-discipline to fall. Young officers and soldiers must always maintain a clear head — not relaxing self-discipline on the grounds that "minor matters are of no consequence," not excusing themselves with "this will be the last time," not indulging themselves on the grounds that "no one will know" — and strive and struggle on the journey of building a strong country and a strong military with the qualities of loyalty, integrity, and responsibility, letting youthful dreams bloom and achieving a brilliant life.

Original Chinese
●把个人理想融入强军伟业，努力跑好属于我们这代人的关键一棒，让青春在拼搏奋斗中闪光 习主席在庆祝中国共产党成立105周年大会上的重要讲话中寄语新时代中国青年：“把个人追求融入党和国家事业，只争朝夕、不负韶华，在新征程上跑好历史接力赛，用青春铺路、让理想闪光！”当前，实现建军一百年奋斗目标进入决胜攻坚的关键阶段，时间紧、任务重，军队青年官兵必须勇于担当、敢于作为，把个人理想融入强军伟业，努力跑好属于我们这代人的关键一棒，让青春在拼搏奋斗中闪光。 淬炼忠诚品格。对党忠诚是革命军人的首要政治品质，是革命军人的政治灵魂。回望历史，从南昌起义的一声枪响，到井冈山上的红旗漫卷；从万里长征的艰难跋涉，到抗日战争的抵御外侮；从解放战争的南征北战，到抗美援朝的保家卫国，一代代青年官兵用牺牲奉献谱写了对党绝对忠诚的赞歌，创造了无愧于时代、无愧于党、无愧于人民的不朽业绩。对党忠诚，源于理论上的清醒和信仰上的坚定。青年官兵要坚持不懈用习近平新时代中国特色社会主义思想凝心铸魂，突出学好习近平强军思想，掌握蕴含其中的马克思主义立场观点方法，筑牢信仰之基、补足精神之钙、把稳思想之舵，做到对党忠诚绝不掺假，听党指挥不打折扣，不断强化为党毫无保留奉献的自觉。对党忠诚不是一阵子的表态，而是一辈子的坚持。青年官兵要把听党话、跟党走内化于心、外化于行，做到党让去哪就去哪、党叫干啥就干啥，把对党忠诚体现在为党分忧、为党尽职的实际行动上。 强化使命担当。一代人有一代人的历史责任，一代人有一代人的使命担当。当前，世界百年未有之大变局加速演进，我国安全形势面临诸多挑战，各种可以预料和难以预料的风险挑战明显增多。时间不等人，历史不等人。青年官兵唯有在吃苦耐劳中砥砺意志品质，在攻坚克难中锻造胜战本领，在知重负重中强化使命担当，方能肩负起时代赋予的使命任务。要做到心中始终有敌情，眼中始终有对手，树牢当兵打仗、练兵打仗、带兵打仗的思想，全部心思向打仗聚焦，全部工作向打仗用劲。坚持仗怎么打兵就怎么练，打仗需要什么就苦练什么，在训练场上勇于挑战极限、敢于突破瓶颈，反复摔打、反复淬炼，练就一招制敌的“杀手锏”。紧盯前沿技术、颠覆性技术，勇闯“无人区”、敢啃“硬骨头”，不断突破“卡脖子”关键技术，以科技赋能提升打赢贡献率。深入纠治和平积弊，练就过硬战斗作风，以“一不怕苦、二不怕死”的战斗精神和“除了胜利一无所求、为了胜利一无所惜”的血性胆气，确保任何时候任何情况下，都能做到一声令下、不辱使命，坚决完成党和人民交给的各项任务。 矢志接续奋斗。奋进强军征程，广大青年官兵既需要志存高远，更需要勤勉躬行。奋斗不是喊在嘴上的口号，而是训练场上的咬牙坚持、任务攻关中的精益求精、平凡岗位上的默默坚守。“时代楷模”王戟以长期主义的坚守深耕“冷门赛道”，没有关键算法就自己开发，没有核心工具就自己研制，用高水平科研成果为国之重器加装一道道可靠“安全锁”。王戟身上这种立足岗位脚踏实地、默默耕耘的精神值得我们学习。然而现实中，有的同志好高骛远，眼高手低，不愿脚踏实地、埋头苦干，习惯抄近道，最终蹉跎年华，一事无成。强军事业是靠实干拼出来、闯出来的。要笃信“日日行不怕千万里”，聚焦主责主业，立足岗位实际，无论是身处雪域边关的哨所，还是深山大漠的阵地，无论是操作高精尖的武器装备，还是从事日复一日的后勤保障，始终保持热忱、追求极致，用“干一行、爱一行、钻一行”的执着，于重复中精进，在坚守中突破。要甘做“铺路石”，愿当“栽树人”，多做那些周期长、见效慢但打基础、利长远的工作，以“功成不必在我”的胸怀和“功成必定有我”的担当，接续奋斗，奋勇拼搏，书写从军报国的青春答卷。 律己正身守廉。青年官兵成长于信息爆炸、价值多元的时代，眼界开阔，思维活跃，但经历阅历尚浅，抵御各种诱惑考验的定力相对不足。前行路上，既有鲜花掌声相伴，也有暗礁险滩潜藏。名利诱惑、歪风侵蚀时刻存在，如果思想防线稍有松懈，就很难抵御各种诱惑考验。青年官兵必须把党纪国法刻印于心，在内心深处筑牢拒腐防变的坚固防线。常怀敬畏之心。敬畏组织，敬畏责任，敬畏法纪，始终不为外物所扰、不为诱惑所动，以内心的澄澈确保行动的刚正。时常自警自省。经常叩问自己，理想信念是否坚定？履职尽责是否到位？遵守纪律是否坚决？廉洁自律是否自觉？只有敢于自省、勤于自省，才能及时掸去思想之尘、祛除行为之垢。注重小事小节。“不矜细行，终累大德。”小事小节上的漏洞如果不及时加以修补，久而久之，就会由量变引发质变，导致廉洁自律防线失守。青年官兵当始终保持清醒头脑，不以“小节无碍”放松自我，不以“下不为例”开脱自我，不以“无人知晓”放纵自我，以忠诚、干净、担当的品质在强国强军征程上拼搏奋斗，绽放青春梦想、成就出彩人生。
