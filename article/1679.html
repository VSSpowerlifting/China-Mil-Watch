About this analysis
Summary
A senior official from the CMC Training and Management Department's Military Education Bureau outlined the 2026 PLA military academy enrollment plan: 22 academies will take roughly 17,500 high school graduates, while 23 academies will enroll about 3,000 officer-track undergraduates and 29 academies about 12,000 NCO-track vocational cadets from the soldier pool, with dedicated seats under the Cyberspace Force, Information Support Force, and Space Systems Department academies. The policy document introduces 贯通培养 (through-train cultivation) as the framing for officer undergraduate education, explicitly linking the undergraduate phase to pre-assignment force training rather than treating it as a standalone credential. The emphasis on targeted political officer cultivation "from the source" and the expansion of the associate-to-bachelor's NCO pathway for 新域新质力量 (new-domain, new-quality forces) positions documents the institutional priority of building those corps through the pipeline rather than lateral assignment.

Interpretation of 2026 Military Academy Enrollment Policies

— A Senior Official from the Military Education Bureau of the CMC Training and Management Department Answers Reporters' Questions

■ Wang Xiao, PLA Daily Reporter Li Lin, Zhang Lin, and Special Correspondent Wu Xu

I. Military Academy Enrollment of Ordinary Senior High School Graduates

1. What are the characteristics of this year's military academy enrollment of ordinary senior high school graduates?

Answer: In 2026, 22 military academies plan to enroll approximately 17,500 ordinary senior high school graduates from all 31 provinces and municipalities nationwide. The main characteristics are as follows: First, the through-train cultivation (贯通培养) of growing-cadre officers (生长军官) is being deployed — during the undergraduate education phase, cadets will consolidate their scientific, cultural, military-political, and professional foundations, with emphasis on cultivating command literacy (指挥素养) and strengthening long-term development potential, laying a solid foundation for pre-assignment training in the force; second, targeted cultivation of political officers is being strengthened, consolidating and building the political officer corps of our military from the source; third, relying on 3 military medical universities, an integrated "5+3" cultivation program in clinical medicine is being conducted to cultivate and forge a high-quality, specialized medical support force. The number of enrolling institutions this year remains consistent with last year at 22, comprising: 1 directly under the Central Military Commission (CMC), 5 under the Army, 5 under the Navy, 4 under the Air Force, 1 under the Rocket Force, 1 under the PLA Strategic Support Force Space Systems Department (军事航天部队), 1 under the Cyberspace Force (网络空间部队), 1 under the Information Support Force (信息支援部队), 1 under the Joint Logistic Support Force, and 2 under the People's Armed Police Force. At the same time, relying on the distinctive and advantageous disciplines and specializations of military academies, talent cultivation in emerging technology fields will continue.

For details, click → 2026 Military Academy Enrollment Plan

2. What are the basic requirements for applying to military academies?

Answer: In accordance with the Measures for Military Academies to Enroll Ordinary Senior High School Graduates jointly issued by the Ministry of Education and relevant CMC departments in December 2023, ordinary senior high school graduates applying to military academies must meet the following conditions: unmarried; as of August 31 of this year, no younger than 16 years of age and no older than 20 years of age; physical fitness test scores during the senior high school phase must reach a passing grade or above; must have taken this year's National College Entrance Examination (普通高等学校招生全国统一考试), and the gaokao score must reach the province's special-category enrollment control line or the first-tier (一本) line. Both current-year and prior-year senior high school graduates may apply.

3. What is the application process for military academies?

Answer: There are five main steps: ① Political review (政治考核) — applicants participate in political review in accordance with the requirements of the military service organ (兵役机关) at their place of household registration, habitual residence, or place of application; ② Filling in preferences (填报志愿) — applicants fill in their preferences in accordance with the gaokao enrollment requirements of their respective province, autonomous region, or directly administered municipality; ③ Setting the military examination threshold (划设军检线) — the provincial military district (省军区) enrollment work office, in conjunction with the provincial enrollment committee office, sets the military examination threshold at 3 to 5 times the enrollment plan quota and publishes it to the public; ④ Interview and physical examination — applicants who have filled in military academy preferences and have reached the military examination threshold participate in interviews and physical examinations in accordance with the requirements of the local provincial military district enrollment work office; ⑤ File submission and enrollment (投档录取) — applicants who have passed the political review, interview, and physical examination, and whose scores have reached the file-submission score line (提档分数线) of their preferred institution, will have their files submitted by the provincial enrollment committee office in the early-batch (提前批次) in descending order of scores; enrollment is confirmed after the institution reviews and approves the files. Those whose files are not submitted or who are not enrolled may participate in subsequent regular-batch (普通批次) file submission.

4. What does the political review primarily assess?

Answer: The political review primarily assesses the applicant's own political and ideological conduct, as well as the political background and criminal record of family members and siblings not living together. The main procedure is as follows: after the gaokao, applicants fill out a political review form, which is submitted by the school through the local county, district, or municipal education department to the local military service organ; the military service organ, in conjunction with the public security organ, organizes the political review and renders a conclusion, which is reported up through successive levels to the provincial military district enrollment work office, which then provides it to the provincial enrollment committee office. For detailed content and requirements, applicants should follow information released by local relevant departments.

5. What aspects does the interview generally assess?

Answer: The interview is specifically organized and conducted by the provincial military district enrollment work office, and primarily assesses the applicant's motivation for applying, value orientation, personal growth experience, social support and communication, adaptability to the environment, as well as logical thinking and verbal expression abilities. After the interview is completed, the interview conclusion is communicated to the interviewee on the spot by the interview staff. If the interviewee has objections to the interview conclusion, they may request a review on the spot; the review conclusion is the final conclusion.

6. What are the requirements regarding vision, height, weight, and similar factors for applying to military academies?

Answer: Military academy enrollment of ordinary senior high school graduates is governed by the Physical Examination Standards for the Selection of Officers and Civilian Personnel by the Military. The general standards for vision, height, and weight are: ① Vision — bare-eye visual acuity of no less than 4.5 in either eye; ② Height — males 162 cm or above, females 158 cm or above; ③ Weight — body mass index between 17.5 and 30 for males, and between 17 and 24 for females.

For details, click → Physical Examination Standards for Military Academy Enrollment of Ordinary Senior High School Graduates

7. What are the main differences between the "5+3" integrated program and the eight-year program at the 3 military medical universities?

Answer: Cadets in the "5+3" integrated program who qualify at the undergraduate stage are directly advanced to the master's degree cultivation stage, simultaneously completing standardized residency training (住院医师规范化培训) while enrolled; upon graduation they are awarded a master's degree and the rank of second lieutenant (中尉). Cadets in the eight-year program who qualify at the undergraduate stage are directly advanced to the doctoral degree cultivation stage; upon graduation they are awarded a doctoral degree and the rank of captain (上尉), but must still complete standardized residency training after graduation.

8. How can one inquire about military academy enrollment information?

Answer: In order to allow all applicants and their parents to obtain comprehensive and accurate information about military academy enrollment, the CMC's enrollment business department has established the "军校招生" (Military Academy Enrollment) WeChat official account to release and update all types of enrollment information in a timely manner. Applicants may also obtain relevant information through the official websites or WeChat official accounts of provincial enrollment committee offices, military academy enrollment institutions, and provincial military district enrollment work offices. Military academy enrollment strictly implements the Ministry of Education's "Sunshine Enrollment" (阳光招生) policy; there are no so-called out-of-plan quotas or internal quotas. Applicants and parents must absolutely not trust anyone who promises to guarantee enrollment, in order to prevent financial losses and, more importantly, to avoid affecting applicants' normal application process.

II. Military Academy Enrollment of Soldier Candidates (士兵学员)

1. Please provide a general overview of this year's military academy enrollment plan quotas for soldier candidates.

Answer: In order to adapt to developments and changes in the construction of new-type combat forces (新型作战力量) and the composition and structure of personnel, and based on the needs for selecting and supplementing officers (警官) and for training non-commissioned officers (军士/警士), this year 23 military academies plan to enroll approximately 3,000 full-time undergraduate education cadets for growing-cadre officers (警官), and 29 military academies plan to enroll approximately 12,000 vocational and technical education cadets for non-commissioned officers (军士/警士).

2. Please provide a general overview of this year's associate-degree-to-bachelor's-degree non-commissioned officer (军士/警士) vocational and technical education program.

Answer: In order to meet the demand for non-commissioned officer talent in combat readiness and warfighting and in key positions in new-domain, new-quality forces (新域新质力量), the pilot associate-degree-to-bachelor's-degree non-commissioned officer (军士/警士) vocational and technical education program will continue this year, with 23 military academies arranging 43 specializations to enroll 1,333 cadets.

3. How can academic subject examination scores be queried?

Answer: Examination scores are expected to be released at the end of June or early July. At that time, soldier candidates may log in to the "Military Academy Enrollment Information Platform" on the Qiangjun Network (强军网) using their ID number and candidate number to query their scores. If a soldier candidate has doubts about their queried score, they may submit a review application to the enrollment business department of their division- or regiment-level unit on the day the scores are released; applications are consolidated at successive levels and the review is organized by the CMC enrollment business department. Score review primarily checks whether the soldier candidate's information is consistent with the relevant information on the answer sheet, whether the scanned image of the answer sheet is clear and complete, whether there are any missed evaluations, and whether there are any errors in score tabulation and calculation. The reviewed score is the final score.

4. How do soldier candidates fill in their preferences?

Answer: After scores are released, preferences are filled in through the "Military Academy Enrollment Information Platform" on the Qiangjun Network, combining individual choice with organizational arrangement. Candidates applying for full-time undergraduate education for growing-cadre officers (警官) may fill in 3 preferences, except for the military cultural work specialization and the option to switch to non-commissioned officer (军士/警士) vocational and technical education (associate degree). Candidates applying for non-commissioned officer (军士/警士) vocational and technical education (associate degree) may fill in 1 preference, which must be the same as or similar to their current specialization, in addition to a transfer preference (调剂志愿). Candidates applying for associate-degree-to-bachelor's-degree non-commissioned officer (军士/警士) vocational and technical education may fill in 1 preference and may not be transferred (调剂). All soldier candidates should fully understand the methods and requirements for filling in preferences, complete the process on time, and be responsible for the authenticity and accuracy of the content they submit.

5. How is the file-submission plan formulated for soldier candidates applying for full-time undergraduate education for growing-cadre officers (警官)?

Answer: The file-submission plan for soldier candidates applying for full-time undergraduate education for growing-cadre officers (警官) (excluding military cultural work-related specializations) is formulated by each department and unit based on enrollment plan quotas, candidate preferences, and physical examination conclusions, ranking candidates from highest to lowest by the combined total of three items — military common subject (军事共同科目) examination scores, academic subject examination scores, and policy bonus points — using a parallel preferences (平行志愿) method. If candidates have the same total score, comparison is made first by military common subject examination score, then by academic subject examination score, and then successively by scores in Chinese, mathematics, English, comprehensive military-political knowledge (军政知识综合), and comprehensive scientific knowledge (科学知识综合). Those who cannot be submitted according to their preferences but who agree to be transferred may be transferred; those who do not agree to be transferred will not have their files submitted. Each department and unit submits files to institutions at 100% of the enrollment plan quota. For candidates applying to military cultural work-related specializations at the National Defense University's College of Arts (国防大学军事文化学院), candidates must go to the institution to take a specialization examination; for specific examination arrangements and enrollment rules, see the enrollment prospectus and work plan issued by the National Defense University's College of Arts.

6. What are the requirements for file submission for candidates applying for non-commissioned officer (军士/警士) vocational and technical education?

Answer: The file-submission rules for candidates applying for non-commissioned officer (军士/警士) vocational and technical education are determined separately by the enrollment business departments of each department and unit. Among these: those who switch from applying for full-time undergraduate education for growing-cadre officers (警官) to non-commissioned officer (军士/警士) vocational and technical education (associate degree), and those recommended for non-commissioned officer (军士/警士) vocational and technical education (associate degree) with exemption from academic subject examinations, are counted within the non-commissioned officer (军士/警士) vocational and technical education enrollment plan quota; those directly enrolled and those in targeted cultivation (定向培养) as non-commissioned officers (军士/警士) who apply for full-time undergraduate education for growing-cadre officers (警官) may not switch to non-commissioned officer (军士/警士) vocational and technical education of equivalent academic standing (同等学力); those applying for full-time undergraduate education for growing-cadre officers (警官) may not switch to associate-degree-to-bachelor's-degree non-commissioned officer (军士/警士) vocational and technical education.

7. Under what circumstances may bonus points be applied?

Answer: Soldier candidates applying for full-time undergraduate education for growing-cadre officers (警官) may receive bonus points if they personally have received a third-class combat merit citation (三等战功), a second-class merit citation (二等功), or a third-level commendation (三级表彰) or higher; have served for a specified number of years in units stationed in special regions (驻特殊地区部队); or if their parents are martyrs (烈士) or died in the line of duty (因公牺牲), have received a second-class combat merit citation (二等战功) or higher, have been publicized as a major model (重大典型), have served for a specified number of years in units stationed in special regions or in special positions (特殊岗位), or are currently active-duty military personnel.

Soldier candidates applying for non-commissioned officer (军士/警士) vocational and technical education may receive bonus points if they personally have received a fourth-class combat merit citation (四等战功), a third-class merit citation (三等功), or a third-level commendation (三级表彰); have served for a specified number of years in units stationed in special regions; have obtained a corresponding national or military vocational skills level or professional technical title certification; have served as squad leader (班长) or deputy squad leader (副班长) for a specified number of years; or if their parents have served for a specified number of years in units stationed in special regions or in special positions, or are currently active-duty military personnel.

Candidates applying for associate-degree-to-bachelor's-degree non-commissioned officer (军士/警士) vocational and technical education are not subject to policy bonus points.

8. What aspects does the file review for soldier candidates primarily cover?

Answer: File review for soldier candidates is conducted by the enrollment business department in conjunction with the file management department. Review content includes candidate materials such as the soldier candidate examination approval report form (士兵考学审批报告表), service materials such as the conscript induction approval document (应征公民入伍批准书), and other materials such as graduation certificates. The file review primarily examines whether the candidate's qualifications meet the conditions, whether the candidate's file is complete, whether the candidate's information is internally consistent, and whether the file materials are authentic and valid.

9. How is the fairness and impartiality of soldier candidate enrollment subject to oversight?

Answer: The Measures for Military Academies to Enroll Soldier Candidates requires units at the regimental level and above to improve and refine the oversight mechanism for soldier candidate enrollment work, strengthen oversight and inspection of key links in enrollment work, and voluntarily accept oversight by officers and soldiers. At the same time, in order to implement the Sunshine Enrollment requirements, safeguard the legitimate interests of all candidates, rectify the professional atmosphere in the field of military education, and focus on sensitive matters and key areas in soldier candidate enrollment work, the entire military has established from top to bottom institutional mechanisms for information disclosure, power restraint, and early warning and notification, exercising oversight over all areas and every link of enrollment work. During the enrollment period, a military-wide soldier enrollment supervision and reporting hotline has been established at 010-66827155; individual units have also established their own supervision and reporting hotlines to receive reports of relevant situations.

答：2026年22所军队院校面向全国31个省市，计划招收普通高中毕业生约1.75万名。主要有以下特点：一是部署生长军官贯通培养，本科教育阶段打牢学员科学文化、军政素质和专业基础，注重指挥素养培育，夯实长远发展潜力，为部队岗前培训奠定扎实基础；二是加强政治军官针对性培养，从源头上巩固建强我军政治军官队伍；三是依托3所军医大学，开展临床医学“5+3”一体化培养，培育锻造高素质专业化卫勤医疗队伍。今年招生院校与去年保持一致，为22所，其中，军委直属1所、陆军5所、海军5所、空军4所、火箭军1所、军事航天部队1所、网络空间部队1所、信息支援部队1所、联勤保障部队1所、武警部队2所。同时，依托军队院校特色优势学科专业，继续开展新兴技术领域人才培养。 详情请点击→2026年军校招生计划 2.报考军队院校的基本条件是什么？ 答：根据2023年12月教育部和军委机关有关部门联合颁发的《军队院校招收普通高中毕业生工作办法》，普通高中毕业生报考军队院校须符合下列条件：未婚；截至今年8月31日，年龄不低于16周岁、不超过20周岁；高中阶段体质测试成绩达到及格以上；参加今年普通高等学校招生全国统一考试，高考成绩须达到本省特殊类型招生控制线或一本线。普通高中应届毕业生、往届毕业生均可报考。 3.报考军队院校的流程是什么？ 答：主要有5个步骤：①政治考核，考生按照户籍所在地、经常居住地或报考所在地兵役机关要求参加政治考核；②填报志愿，考生根据所在省、自治区、直辖市高考招生工作要求填报志愿；③划设军检线，省军区招生工作办公室按照招生计划数的3倍～5倍，会同省级招委会办公室划设军检线并向社会公布；④面试体检，填报军队院校志愿且达到军检线的考生，按照当地省军区招生工作办公室要求参加面试和体格检查；⑤投档录取，通过政治考核、面试和体格检查，且成绩达到志愿院校提档分数线的考生，由省级招委会办公室在提前批次按照成绩由高到低进行投档，院校审档通过后录取。未被投档或未被录取的，可以参加后续普通批次投档。 4.政治考核主要考核什么内容？ 答：政治考核主要考核考生本人政治思想表现，以及家庭成员、未共同生活的兄弟姐妹的政治背景和违法犯罪等情况。其主要程序：高考结束后考生填写政治考核表，由学校通过所在县、区、市教育部门报送当地兵役机关，兵役机关会同公安机关组织政治考核、作出结论，逐级上报至省军区招生工作办公室，由其向省级招委会办公室提供。详细内容和要求，考生可关注当地有关部门发布的信息。 5.面试一般考察哪些方面？ 答：面试由省军区招生工作办公室具体组织实施，主要考察考生的报考动机、价值取向、成长经历、社会支持与沟通、环境适应性，以及逻辑思维和语言表达能力等。面试完成后，面试结论由面试工作人员当场告知面试对象。面试对象对面试结论有异议的，可以当场申请复核，复核结论为最终结论 6.报考军队院校对视力、身高、体重等方面有哪些要求？ 答：军队院校招收普通高中毕业生执行《军队选拔军官和文职人员体检标准》，视力、身高、体重的通用标准是：①视力，任何一眼裸眼视力不低于4.5；②身高，男性162cm以上、女性158cm以上；③体重，男性体重指数在17.5至30、女性体重指数在17至24之间。 详情请点击→军队院校招收普通高中毕业生体检标准 7.3所军医大学“5+3”一体化和八年制专业主要区别是什么？ 答：“5+3”一体化专业学员，本科阶段合格者直接转入硕士研究生培养阶段，在校同步完成住院医师规范化培训，毕业授予硕士学位、中尉军衔；八年制专业学员，本科阶段合格者直接转入博士研究生培养阶段，毕业授予博士学位、上尉军衔，但毕业后还需参加住院医师规范化培训。 8.如何查询了解军队院校招生信息？ 答：为了让广大考生和家长全面准确了解军校招生信息，军委机关招生业务部门已开设了“军校招生”微信公众号，及时发布更新招生工作各类信息。同时，考生也可通过省级招委会办公室、军队招生院校、省军区招生工作办公室的官方网站或微信公众号了解相关信息。军队院校招生严格执行教育部“阳光招生”政策，没有所谓计划外指标和内部指标，任何人许诺保证录取的，考生和家长切不可轻信，防止财物损失，更重要的是避免影响考生正常报考。 二、军队院校招收士兵学员 1.请介绍一下今年军队院校招收士兵学员计划指标的大概情况？ 答：为适应新型作战力量建设、兵员成分结构等方面发展变化，根据军官（警官）选拔补充需求和军士（警士）培训需求，今年，23所军队院校计划招收生长军官（警官）全日制本科教育学员约0.3万名，29所军队院校计划招收军士（警士）职业技术教育学员约1.2万名。 2.请介绍一下今年组织专科起点本科军士（警士）职业技术教育的大概情况。 答：为满足备战打仗和新域新质力量重要岗位军士人才需求，今年继续试点开展专科起点本科军士（警士）职业技术教育，安排23所军队院校43个专业招收1333名学员。 3.文化科目考试成绩怎样查询？ 答：考试成绩预计于6月底或7月初公布，届时士兵考生可凭身份证号、考生号，登录强军网“军队院校招生信息平台”查询。如对所查询成绩存疑，士兵考生可在成绩发布当日向所在师团级单位招生业务部门提出复核申请，逐级汇总后由军委机关招生业务部门组织复核。成绩复核主要核查士兵考生信息与答题卡相关信息是否一致，答题卡扫描图像是否清晰、完整，是否存在漏评，成绩统计核分是否有误等事项。复核成绩为最终成绩。 4.士兵考生怎样填报志愿？ 答：成绩公布后，采取个人选择与组织安排相结合方式，依托强军网“军队院校招生信息平台”填报志愿。报考生长军官（警官）全日制本科教育考生，除军队文化工作专业和改录军士（警士）职业技术教育（专科）志愿外，可以填报3个志愿；报考军士（警士）职业技术教育（专科）考生，除调剂志愿外，可以填报1个志愿且与所从事专业相同或相近；报考专科起点本科军士（警士）职业技术教育考生，可以填报1个志愿且不得调剂。广大士兵考生应充分了解志愿填报方法要求，按时完成填报并对个人所填报内容的真实性、准确性负责。 5.报考生长军官（警官）全日制本科教育士兵考生投档方案是怎样制定的？ 答：报考生长军官（警官）全日制本科教育（军队文化工作相关专业除外）士兵考生投档方案，是由各部门各单位根据招生计划、考生志愿、体检结论等情况，按照军事共同科目、文化科目考试成绩及政策加分3项总分由高到低排序，采取平行志愿方式制定。如考生总分相同，先依次比较军事共同科目考试成绩、文化科目考试成绩，再依次比较语文、数学、英语、军政知识综合、科学知识综合科目考试成绩。对不能按志愿投档但服从调剂的，可以进行调剂；不服从调剂的，不予投档。各部门各单位按照招生计划数的100%向院校投档。报考国防大学军事文化学院军队文化工作相关专业的，考生需到校参加专业考试，具体考试安排和录取规则，见国防大学军事文化学院发布的招生简章和工作方案。 6.报考军士（警士）职业技术教育考生投档有什么要求？ 答：报考军士（警士）职业技术教育考生的投档规则，由各部门各单位招生业务部门分别确定。其中，由报考生长军官（警官）全日制本科教育改投军士（警士）职业技术教育（专科）、免文化科目考试保送军士（警士）职业技术教育（专科）的，纳入军士（警士）职业技术教育招生计划指标；直接招收和定向培养军士（警士），报考生长军官（警官）全日制本科教育的，不能改投同等学力军士（警士）职业技术教育；报考生长军官（警官）全日制本科教育的，不能改投专科起点本科军士（警士）职业技术教育。 7.哪些情形可以享受加分政策？ 答：报考生长军官（警官）全日制本科教育的士兵考生，如本人获得三等战功、二等功、三级表彰以上奖励表彰，在驻特殊地区部队服役满一定年限；父母为烈士、因公牺牲，获得二等战功以上奖励，被作为重大典型宣传，在驻特殊地区部队、特殊岗位任职满一定年限或为现役军人的，可以享受加分政策。报考军士（警士）职业技术教育的考生，如本人获得四等战功、三等功、三级表彰奖励，在驻特殊地区部队服役满一定年限，通过国家、军队相应职业技能等级、专业技术职称认定，担任班长、副班长满一定年限，或父母在驻特殊地区部队、特殊岗位任职满一定年限或为现役军人，可以享受加分政策。报考专科起点本科军士（警士）职业技术教育的考生，不执行政策加分。 8.士兵考生档案审查主要涉及哪些方面？ 答：士兵考生档案审查由招生业务部门会同档案管理部门进行，审查内容包括士兵考学审批报告表等考生材料、应征公民入伍批准书等服役材料、毕业证书等其他材料。档案审查主要看考生资格是否符合条件、考生档案是否完整齐全、考生信息是否前后一致、档案材料是否真实有效。 9.士兵考学招生的公平公正如何接受监督？ 答：《军队院校招收士兵学员工作办法》要求团级以上单位健全完善招收士兵学员工作监督机制，加大对招生工作关键环节监督检查力度，自觉接受官兵监督。同时，为落实阳光招生要求，维护广大考生切身利益，纯正军事教育领域行业风气，聚焦士兵考学招生工作中的敏感事务和重点部位，全军自上而下建立信息公开、权力制约、预警预告等制度机制，对招生工作全领域各环节进行监管。招生录取期间，设立全军士兵招生监督举报电话010-66827155，各单位也分别设立监督举报电话，受理反映情况。
