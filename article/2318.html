About this analysis
Summary
On July 8–9, Southern Theater Command Army units — specifically elements of the 75th Group Army — conducted flood rescue operations at Xijiang Education Park in Guigang City, Guangxi, evacuating tens of thousands of stranded teachers and students from multiple schools using assault boats amid deep standing water and submerged debris; PAP Guigang Detachment personnel simultaneously cleared silt in Da'an Town, and Nanning Garrison militia evacuated residents in Hengzhou City. The article is standard disaster-relief political work content, foregrounding named Party-member NCOs (First Sergeant Liang Binbin, Staff Sergeant First Class Xing Jingzhou) to frame military service as selfless duty — its value is as a record of how PLA Daily constructs the civil-military bond narrative at the unit level during domestic emergency response, not as evidence of changed operational posture or capability.
Translation

Guardians in Wind and Rain

■ PLA Daily Reporter Guo Shudong

"Look, the PLA is here!" Hearing the voice from the crowd, teachers and students trapped at Guangxi Logistics Vocational and Technical College rushed to windows and corridors, waving to the soldiers who had come to rescue them… Early on July 8, this reporter boarded an assault boat and followed officers and soldiers of the Southern Theater Command Army into the Xijiang Education Park in Guigang City, Guangxi, to evacuate the trapped teachers and students.

Battered by continuous heavy rainfall, the park suffered severe flooding, leaving tens of thousands of teachers and students from multiple schools—including Guangxi Logistics Vocational and Technical College and Guigang Senior High School—stranded.

Hearing the cries of the teachers and students, assault boat helmsman, First Sergeant, and Party member Liang Binbin increased his speed.

"Don't panic—watch your step!" Rescue soldiers carefully helped teachers and students board the assault boat. Once everyone was seated, Liang Binbin, guided by a school teacher, steered the assault boat around obstacles and made precise turns.

The education park sits on low-lying ground with deep standing water; the surface was strewn with debris, while submerged branches and other hazards lurked beneath—dangers at every turn. Liang Binbin, who has served for 26 years, had already been fighting on the rescue front line for several days. Though somewhat exhausted, he kept his eyes fixed ahead and drove with full concentration. He said: "As long as I can help the teachers and students escape danger and get home sooner, a little fatigue is nothing."

On the shore, the families of teachers and students waited anxiously. The rescued teachers and students stepped off the assault boats one after another, offering thanks to the rescue soldiers, and many shed tears of gratitude.

The scene filled Staff Sergeant First Class and Party member Xing Jingzhou with deep satisfaction. At noon on the 7th, Xing Jingzhou had already arrived at the park to carry out rescue operations, transporting urgently needed supplies into the park again and again with his comrades. "The people are in our hearts, and the mission is on our shoulders. As long as the floodwaters do not recede, my comrades and I will keep fighting," Xing Jingzhou said. As long as the disaster-stricken people need us, we will hold our ground to the end—that is a soldier's duty.

(PLA Daily, Guigang, Guangxi, July 9 dispatch)

Original Chinese
风雨中的守护 ■解放军报记者 郭澍东 “看，解放军来了！”听到人群中传来的声音，广西物流职业技术学院被困师生纷纷来到窗户边、走廊上，向前来救援的官兵招手……7月8日一早，记者乘冲锋艇，跟随南部战区陆军官兵进入广西贵港市西江教育园区，转移被困师生。 受连续强降雨影响，这个园区受灾严重，广西物流职业技术学院、贵港市高级中学等多所学校上万名师生被困。 听见师生们的呼喊声，冲锋艇操舵手、一级军士长、党员梁彬彬加快了速度。 “不要慌，注意脚下！”救援官兵小心翼翼地搀扶师生登上冲锋艇。待师生坐稳后，梁彬彬在学校老师指引下，驾驶冲锋艇避开障碍物，准确转向。 教育园区地势低、积水深，水面杂物遍布，水下则暗藏树枝等，危险重重。入伍26年的梁彬彬已在救援一线奋战多日，尽管有些疲惫，他仍双眼紧盯前方、全神贯注驾驶。他说：“只要能帮助师生脱离险境、早点回家，这点累不算啥。” 岸边，师生的家人们焦急等候。获救师生陆续走下冲锋艇，纷纷向救援官兵道谢，不少人流下了感动的泪水。 此情此景让一级上士、党员幸经周倍感欣慰。7日中午，幸经周就已赶到园区开展救援，和战友一次次将急需物资运进园区。“群众在心间，使命在肩头。洪水不退，我和战友就坚持战斗。”幸经周说，只要受灾群众需要，就坚守到底，这是一名军人的职责。 （解放军报广西贵港7月9日电） 7月9日，第75集团军某部官兵在广西贵港市转移群众。黄远利摄 7月9日，武警贵港支队官兵在贵港市大安镇清淤。孙 跃摄 7月9日，南宁警备区民兵在广西横州市转移群众。黄 榕摄
