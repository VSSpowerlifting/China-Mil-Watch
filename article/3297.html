Commemorating the 90th Anniversary of the Victory of the Workers' and Peasants' Red Army's Long March | The Wisdom of Victory in Adversity

纪念工农红军长征胜利90周年 | 逆境突围的制胜智慧

About this analysis
Summary
On the 90th anniversary of the Long March's completion, PLA official media published a doctrinal retrospective drawing explicit operational lessons from the 1934–1936 campaigns of the First, Second, and Fourth Front Armies and the 25th Army, covering mobile warfare, strategic initiative, logistics without a rear area, and mass-line political work. The piece frames each historical lesson as a direct prescription for current force development, invoking the CMC Chairman Responsibility System, the 'Two Establishes' (两个确立), and the 'Two Upholds' (两个维护) as the institutional expression of Party command over the gun. This is standard anniversary political-education content; its analytical value is as a record of how the PLA currently packages Long March mythology to reinforce CMC centralization and Xi Jinping's personal authority over the force, not as evidence of a doctrinal shift.
Translation

This year marks the 90th anniversary of the victory of the Chinese Workers' and Peasants' Red Army's Long March. From October 1934 to October 1936, under the resolute leadership of the Communist Party of China, the First, Second, and Fourth Front Armies and the 25th Army of the Red Army carried out the great Long March. The officers and soldiers of the Red Army, with extraordinary wisdom and dauntless heroism, overcame countless hardships and made tremendous sacrifices to successfully complete the Long March—a feat that shook the world and will shine forever in the annals of history—creating a human miracle of earth-shattering magnitude. To commemorate this great undertaking, carry forward and promote the great Long March spirit, and draw deeply on the military wisdom embedded within it, this section will publish a series of related articles to revisit and interpret this world-shaking revolutionary feat and great victory, providing intellectual inspiration for the great practice of strengthening and revitalizing the military.

The Red Army's Long March is a great feat in human history—a military textbook forged in extreme adversity that shines with the brilliance of defeating the strong with the weak. Confronting the manifold difficulties of a stronger enemy, treacherous terrain, and scarce supplies, the officers and soldiers of the Red Army, with iron will and outstanding wisdom, fought more than 600 campaigns and battles, crossed nearly 100 rivers, and scaled more than 40 high mountains and dangerous peaks. Through the baptism of blood and fire, they corrected the military line, innovated strategic and tactical approaches, and forged a treasury of military wisdom that transcends time and remains ever fresh.

"Devote one's heart to the Party; cast the soul of the military forever." The Party's absolute leadership over the military was the fundamental guarantee of the Red Army's victory in perilous circumstances during the Long March.

The most core and profound military lesson of the Long March lies in the fact that it demonstrated through ironclad evidence that the Party's absolute leadership over the military is the founding principle and the soul of strengthening the people's army—an unshakable fundamental principle for defeating the enemy. In the early stages of the Long March, the errors of "Left" opportunist dogmatism weakened the Party's collective leadership; individual arbitrariness caused military decision-making to become divorced from reality, resulting in heavy losses for the Red Army in battles such as the Xiangjiang River campaign. The Zunyi Conference was a turning point of life-and-death significance in the history of our Party. This conference began to establish the leading position of the correct Marxist line represented primarily by Comrade Mao Zedong within the Party Central Committee, restored the centralized, unified, scientifically sound, and efficient command system of the Party over the military, and at an extremely critical historical juncture, saved the Party, saved the Red Army, and saved the Chinese revolution.

"Upholding the principle that the Party commands the gun and building our own people's army is an irrefutable truth the Party arrived at through struggle in blood and fire." The supreme leadership and command authority over the military must be unconditionally and absolutely concentrated in the Party Central Committee and the Central Military Commission (CMC). Major strategic decisions and campaign deployments must strictly follow the principle of democratic centralism, ensuring scientific authority and strict compliance with orders. The more severe and complex the situation, the more the Party's centralized and unified leadership must be emphasized, ensuring the entire military maintains unified will, unified command, and unified action. On the new-era journey of building a strong country and a strong military, we must profoundly understand the decisive significance of the "Two Establishes" (两个确立), strengthen the "Four Consciousnesses" (四个意识), firm up the "Four Confidences" (四个自信), achieve the "Two Upholds" (两个维护), implement the CMC Chairman Responsibility System, and ensure that the barrel of the gun forever obeys the Party's command.

"Follow the ink [i.e., the plan] and adapt to the enemy to decide the outcome of battle." Seeking truth from facts in military guidance was the solid foundation on which the Red Army turned danger into safety during the Long March.

The Long March demonstrated through hardship and victory that war is an art full of uncertainty, and that the soul of military guidance lies in adhering to seeking truth from facts and proceeding from reality in all things. In the early stages of the Long March, due to the erroneous leadership of dogmatism within the Party, "Left" adventurism was practiced militarily: disregarding the Red Army's actual conditions, mechanically executing positional defense and short, sharp attacks, and engaging in attrition warfare against a stronger enemy, falling into a passive position. After the Zunyi Conference, the Red Army returned to the essence of war guidance—"you fight your way, I fight my way" (你打你的，我打我的)—discarding dogma and giving full play to the strengths of mobile warfare and guerrilla warfare. Crossing the Chishui River four times with a surprise force, cleverly crossing the Jinsha River to avoid the enemy's sharp edge, and seizing the Luding Bridge in a flying assault to create a miracle—all were exemplary demonstrations of accurately grasping enemy and friendly conditions, terrain, and weather, and flexibly applying strategy and tactics.

"Proceeding from reality in all things is the starting point and the ultimate goal of how we think about problems, make decisions, and handle affairs." Military construction and operational guidance must be grounded in the realities of national and military conditions, rooted in the country's own political, economic, cultural, and geographic characteristics and the military's own strengths. We must absolutely never copy foreign models or blindly trust textbook dogma. Strategy and tactics must change with the situation, change with the enemy, and change with the terrain. We must keenly observe the battlefield situation, accurately capture the opponent's weaknesses, make full use of environmental conditions, persist in flexible maneuvering and striking the enemy where he is weak while avoiding where he is strong, and firmly grasp the battlefield initiative in dynamic confrontation.

"Contending for military advantage brings benefit; contending for military advantage brings danger." Doing everything possible to seize battlefield initiative was the key to the Red Army's breaking out of adversity during the Long March.

The entire Long March was an epic of seizing, creating, and maintaining strategic initiative while surrounded by a powerful enemy. The Nationalist Army, relying on absolute superiority, attempted to annihilate the Red Army on a predetermined battlefield. The Red Army, with extraordinary strategic vision and maneuver capability, repeatedly turned danger into safety and transformed passivity into initiative through decisively adjusting the direction of advance and executing large-scale strategic envelopments. The Tongdao turn brought a sudden reversal; feinting east while striking west across Sichuan, Guizhou, and Yunnan; and ultimately moving north to take root in Shaanxi-Gansu—each critical turning point was the Red Army seizing the initiative at the strategic level, winning both military victory and strategic initiative by changing routes of march, timing of operations, and strike objectives.

"When strategic judgments are accurate, strategic planning is scientific, and strategic initiative is won, the cause of the Party and the people holds great promise." Strategic planning must possess a global perspective and forward-looking vision, accurately anticipate the enemy's strategic intentions, and avoid falling into preset traps. When the balance of forces is unfavorable and the situation is disadvantageous, hard attrition warfare should be avoided. Strategic maneuver must be executed decisively, trading space for time and seeking opportunities to build momentum in movement. We must be adept at exploiting and widening the enemy's internal contradictions, command errors, and gaps in deployment, and actively creating a posture favorable to ourselves.

"Military forces have no constant disposition; water has no constant form." Flexible and mobile warfare was the trump card by which the Red Army defeated the strong with the weak during the Long March.

The Long March honed mobile warfare—the people's army's signature skill—to a state of consummate mastery. Through large-scale, high-intensity maneuver, the Red Army mobilized, exhausted, and dispersed the enemy; created local superiority in movement; concentrated forces to fight battles of annihilation; and sought quick, decisive victory. The four crossings of the Chishui River employed highly deceptive feints and repeated envelopments, threading through hundreds of thousands of enemy troops to actively seek opportunities for battle. Throughout the entire Long March, all units implemented the principle of flexible maneuvering—"fight when you can win, withdraw when you cannot"—skillfully unifying strategic withdrawal with the destruction of the enemy's effective strength.

"The highest realm of the art of war guidance is: you fight your way, I fight my way." Battlefield competition cannot be a matter of "competing with the Dragon King for treasures." The weaker side must not be bound to deploying imposing formations on the frontal battlefield or engaging in tit-for-tat confrontation; instead, it must leverage strengths and avoid weaknesses through flexible battlefield maneuver to create and form local battlefield superiority. High importance must be attached to strategic, campaign, and tactical deception; we must be adept at using feints, camouflage, information concealment, and other means to confuse and disrupt the enemy. We must strive to create opportunities for battle in movement, concentrate absolutely superior forces, fight quick-decision battles and battles of annihilation, and achieve the effect of "better to cut off one finger than to wound ten" (伤其十指不如断其一指).

"Those resolved to die will live; those hoping merely to survive will die." Strict discipline and tenacious fighting style were the strong pillars sustaining the Red Army's combat effectiveness during the Long March.

Throughout the Long March, the entire army obeyed orders without exception; officers and soldiers shared hardships and depended on each other through life and death. In the extreme conditions of crossing snow mountains, traversing grasslands, suffering hunger and cold, and facing relentless pursuit, the Red Army could not be worn down, could not be scattered, and grew more courageous with each setback—this was due to iron discipline, a tenacious fighting style, and firm ideals and convictions. In the dense fire of the flying seizure of Luding Bridge, on the sheer cliffs of the assault on Lazikou Pass, and in the arduous trek across the vast grasslands, the officers and soldiers of the Red Army, relying on "the heroism and revolutionary spirit of overcoming all enemies without being overcome by any enemy, and conquering all difficulties without being conquered by any difficulty," repeatedly surpassed the limits of physical endurance and created miracles in the history of warfare.

"Strengthen discipline, and revolution is invincible." Strict discipline is the lifeline of the military and the fundamental guarantee of unified will and unified action. "Military orders are as immovable as a mountain" and "strict compliance with orders" must be infused into the lifeblood of the military. Harsh environments are the furnace for tempering the fighting spirit; troops must be tested under complex and difficult conditions to forge officers and soldiers with the solid fighting style of not fearing a strong enemy, daring to sacrifice, overcoming fortified positions and difficulties, and sustaining continuous operations. Powerful spiritual strength is the deep-level support for defeating the enemy; we must continuously strengthen the education of officers and soldiers in ideals and convictions, consolidate the foundation of faith, and inspire an indomitable fighting will.

"Water can carry a boat, and it can also capsize it." The support and backing of the people was the source of strength that sustained the Red Army's ten-thousand-li march during the Long March.

Wherever the Long March went, the Party's mass work took root there—connecting with the masses, propagating among the masses, arming the masses, uniting the masses, and relying on the masses. The Red Army strictly implemented the "Three Main Rules of Discipline and Eight Points for Attention" (三大纪律八项注意), respected the customs of all ethnic groups, and sincerely worked for the benefit of the masses. Discipline that left not a single needle or thread untouched and actions serving the people won the wholehearted support of the peoples of all ethnic groups along the route. The masses proactively served as guides for the Red Army, provided intelligence, gathered food and fodder, rescued the wounded, and enthusiastically joined the army. The "Yi Sea Alliance" (彝海结盟) successfully applied the Party's ethnic policy, transforming potential resistance into powerful assistance, vividly interpreting the high degree of unity between political work and military action.

"The military wins battles; the people are the backing." The deepest source of the mighty force of war lies among the masses. We must always adhere to the strategic thinking of people's war and unswervingly follow the mass line. Rock-solid unity between the military and the government and between the military and the people is our army's unique strategic advantage. We must strictly observe mass discipline, safeguard the interests of the masses, always maintain a flesh-and-blood connection with the people, and continuously consolidate and enhance the integrated national strategic system and capabilities. We must be adept at wielding the magic weapon of the united front, uniting all forces that can be united, isolating the enemy to the maximum extent, and strengthening ourselves.

Under conditions of extreme hardship, the officers and soldiers of the Red Army, with extraordinary wisdom and tenacity, creatively explored a set of logistical and survival modes adapted to operations without a rear area and with large-scale maneuver: relying on "local resupply" (就地补给) through methods such as expropriating from local despots and battlefield capture; implementing strict material management, practicing strict economy, and traveling light; and adapting to local conditions—in extreme environments such as snow mountains and grasslands, working out invaluable survival skills such as cold prevention during marches, disease prevention, field camping, and identifying edible plants. This logistical model became the solid support for completing the great strategic transfer.

"In a certain sense, fighting a war means fighting logistics." The ancient maxim that "an army without baggage trains will perish, without food will perish, without accumulated stores will perish" remains applicable under the conditions of informatized and intelligentized warfare, and the requirements are even higher. We must build a modernized logistics and equipment support system with the capabilities of all-domain maneuver, rapid response, precise efficiency, and survivability under attack. We must, based on complex and harsh battlefield environments, strengthen training in troops' sustained operational capabilities including field survival, emergency resupply, treatment of casualties and illness, and equipment maintenance. We must optimize troop organization and equipment systems, emphasizing modularity, lightweight design, and multi-functionality, and enhance strategic and campaign maneuver and rapid response capabilities.

"The passes are iron-strong, yet we stride forward from the start." The precious experiences forged by the Red Army in adversity profoundly reveal the internal laws by which the people's army grew from small to large, from weak to strong, and overcame all hardships and obstacles. They will forever be the fertile spiritual soil and source of wisdom nourishing the people's army as it advances from victory to victory, inspiring us to continuously win new and greater victories on the new great journey.

今年是中国工农红军长征胜利90周年。从1934年10月至1936年10月，在中国共产党的坚强领导下，红军第一、第二、第四方面军和第二十五军进行了伟大的长征。红军将士以非凡的智慧和大无畏的英雄气概，战胜千难万险，付出巨大牺牲，胜利完成震撼世界、彪炳史册的长征，创造了气吞山河的人间奇迹。为纪念这一伟大壮举，传承弘扬伟大的长征精神，深入汲取蕴含其中的军事智慧，本版将陆续刊发相关文章，重温和解读这一惊天动地的革命壮举和伟大胜利，为强军兴军伟大实践提供智慧启迪。 红军长征是人类历史上的伟大壮举，是一部在极端困境中淬炼而成、闪耀着以弱胜强光辉的军事教科书。面对敌强我弱、环境险恶、补给匮乏的重重困境，红军将士以钢铁意志和卓越智慧，同敌人进行了600余次战役战斗，跨越近百条江河，攀越40余座高山险峰，在血与火的洗礼中校正了军事路线、创新了战略战术，铸就了一座跨越时空、历久弥新的军事智慧宝库。 “铁心向党，军魂永铸。”党对军队绝对领导是红军长征险境制胜的根本保证。 长征最核心、最深刻的军事启示，在于以的事实昭示党对军队绝对领导是人民军队的建军之本、强军之魂，是克敌制胜不可动摇的根本原则。长征初期，“左”倾教条主义错误削弱党的集体领导，个人专断导致军事决策脱离实际，致使红军在湘江战役等战斗中损失惨重。遵义会议是我们党历史上一个生死攸关的转折点。这次会议开始确立以毛泽东同志为主要代表的马克思主义正确路线在党中央的领导地位，恢复党对军队集中统一、科学高效的指挥体系，在极端危急的历史关头，挽救了党、挽救了红军、挽救了中国革命。 “坚持党指挥枪、建设自己的人民军队，是党在血与火的斗争中得出的颠扑不破的真理。”军队的最高领导权和指挥权必须无条件、绝对地集中于党中央、中央军委。重大战略决策、战役部署必须严格遵循民主集中制原则，确保科学权威、令行禁止。越是形势严峻复杂，越要强调党的集中统一领导，确保全军统一意志、统一指挥、统一行动。新时代强国强军征程上，我们必须深刻领悟“两个确立”的决定性意义，增强“四个意识”、坚定“四个自信”、做到“两个维护”，贯彻军委主席负责制，确保枪杆子永远听党指挥。 “践墨随敌，以决战事。”实事求是的军事指导是红军长征转危为安的坚实依托。 长征用艰辛和胜利表明，战争是充满不确定性的艺术，军事指导的灵魂在于坚持实事求是，一切从实际出发。长征初期，由于党内教条主义的错误领导，军事上实行“左”倾冒险主义，不顾红军实际情况，机械执行阵地防御、短促突击，与强敌硬拼消耗，陷于被动局面。遵义会议后，红军回归“你打你的，我打我的”战争指导精髓，摒弃教条，发挥运动战、游击战优长。四渡赤水出奇兵、巧渡金沙江避锋芒、飞夺泸定桥创奇迹，无不是精准把握敌情我情、地形天候，灵活运用战略战术的典范。 “坚持一切从实际出发，是我们想问题、作决策、办事情的出发点和落脚点。”军队建设和作战指导必须立足国情军情实际，植根本国政治、经济、文化、地理特点和军队自身优势，决不能照搬外国模式、迷信书本教条。战略战术要因势而变、因敌而变、因地而变，敏锐洞察战场态势，精准捕捉对手弱点，充分利用环境条件，坚持灵活机动、避实击虚，在动态对抗中牢牢掌握战场主动权。 “军争为利，军争为危。”千方百计争夺战场主动权是红军长征逆境破局的关键所在。 长征全过程，就是一部在强敌环伺下，争夺、创造和保持战略主动权的史诗。国民党军队凭借绝对优势，妄图将红军聚歼于预设战场。红军则以超凡的战略眼光和机动能力，通过果断调整进军方向、实施大范围战略迂回，一次次化险为夷，变被动为主动。通道转兵峰回路转、声东击西转战川黔滇，最终北上陕甘落脚生根，每一次关键转折都是红军在战略层面掌握主动，通过改变行军路线、作战时机和打击目标，既赢得了战争胜利，也赢得了战略主动。 “战略上判断得准确，战略上谋划得科学，战略上赢得主动，党和人民事业就大有希望。”战略筹划必须具有全局视野和前瞻眼光，准确预判敌战略意图，避免陷入预设陷阱。在力量悬殊、形势不利时，应避免硬拼消耗。要果断实施战略机动，以空间换取时间，在运动中寻机造势。要善于利用和扩大对手内部矛盾、指挥失误、部署间隙，积极营造有利于我的态势。 “兵无常势，水无常形。”灵活机动的运动战是红军长征以弱胜强的制胜法宝。 长征将运动战这一人民军队的看家本领锤炼至炉火纯青。红军通过大范围、高强度机动，调动、疲惫、分散敌人，在运动中创造局部优势，集中兵力打歼灭战，速战速决。四渡赤水以高度佯动、反复迂回，穿插于敌人数十万重兵之间，积极寻求战机。整个长征过程中，各部队贯彻灵活机动原则，“打得赢就打，打不赢就走”，巧妙地将战略转移与消灭敌人有生力量统一起来。 “战争指导艺术的最高境界，就是你打你的、我打我的。”战场比拼不能“与龙王比宝”，处于弱势一方要不拘泥于在正面战场摆堂堂之阵、搞针锋相对，而是要通过灵活的战场机动扬长避短，制造形成局部战场优势。要高度重视战略、战役和战术欺骗，善于运用佯动、伪装、信息遮蔽等手段惑敌乱敌。力求在运动中创造战机，集中绝对优势兵力，打速决战、歼灭战，达成“伤其十指不如断其一指”的效果。 “必死则生，幸生则死。”严明纪律与顽强作风是红军长征保持战力的坚强支撑。 长征途中，全军令行禁止，官兵同甘共苦，生死相依。在爬雪山、过草地、饥寒交迫、追兵不断的极端环境下，红军拖不垮、打不散、愈挫愈勇，靠的是钢铁纪律、顽强战斗作风和坚定理想信念。在飞夺泸定桥的密集火力中，在强攻腊子口的悬崖峭壁上，在茫茫草地的艰难跋涉里，红军将士凭借“压倒一切敌人而不被任何敌人所压倒、征服一切困难而不被任何困难所征服的英雄气概和革命精神”，一次次超越生理极限，创造了战争史上的奇迹。 “加强纪律性，革命无不胜。”严明纪律是军队的生命线，是统一意志、统一行动的根本保证。“军令如山”“令行禁止”必须融入军队血脉。艰苦环境是锤炼战斗精神的熔炉，必须在复杂困难条件下摔打部队，锻造官兵不畏强敌、敢于牺牲、攻坚克难、连续作战的过硬作风。强大的精神力量是克敌制胜的深层支撑，必须持续强化官兵理想信念教育，筑牢信仰之基，激发无坚不摧的战斗意志。 “水能载舟，亦能覆舟。”人民群众拥护和支持是红军长征转战万里的力量源泉。 长征走到哪里，党的群众工作就扎根到哪里，联系群众、宣传群众、武装群众、团结群众、依靠群众。红军严格执行“三大纪律八项注意”，尊重各民族风俗，真心实意为群众谋利益。秋毫无犯的纪律和为民服务的行动，赢得了沿途各族人民的衷心拥护。群众主动为红军当向导、送情报、筹粮秣、救伤员、踊跃参军。“彝海结盟”成功运用党的民族政策，将潜在阻力转化为强大助力，生动诠释了政治工作与军事行动的高度统一。 “军队打胜仗，人民是靠山。”战争的伟力之最深厚的根源，存在于民众之中。必须始终坚持人民战争的战略思想，坚定不移走群众路线。坚如磐石的军政军民团结，是我军独有的战略优势。必须严守群众纪律，维护群众利益，始终保持同人民群众的血肉联系，不断巩固提升一体化国家战略体系和能力。要善于运用统一战线法宝，团结一切可以团结的力量，最大限度孤立敌人、壮大自身。
