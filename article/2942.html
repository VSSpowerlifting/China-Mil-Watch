Video | Multiple Firsts Experienced, Multiple Breakthroughs Achieved! Shenzhou-21 Crew Makes First Public Appearance After Returning Home

视频丨经历多个首次 实现多项突破！神二十一乘组回家后首次公开亮相
PLA Daily (解放军报) 5 August 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
Astronauts Zhang Lu, Wu Fei, and Zhang Hongzhang made their first post-quarantine public appearance at Beijing Aerospace City on June 12, recounting a 210-day Shenzhou-21 mission that included three EVAs, China's first on-orbit aeroponic cultivation of cherry tomatoes and wheat, and the first closed-environment housing of mice in space. The crew also personally managed the program's first space-debris-triggered emergency response sequence — spacecraft impact, extended stay, cross-vehicle crew return, and emergency launch of a replacement craft — which Zhang Lu described as a real-world validation of China's ground-space emergency response system. The article is a post-mission public affairs event; its analytical value lies in documenting how CMSA frames the emergency response episode as a systems-level success story rather than a safety failure, and in confirming Zhang Lu now holds the national record for individual EVAs across two missions.
Translation

Recently, the China Astronaut Research and Training Center held a press meet-and-greet for the Shenzhou-21 crew at Beijing Aerospace City. This marked the first public appearance before media and the general public by the three astronauts — Zhang Lu, Wu Fei, and Zhang Hongzhang — following their return to Earth and completion of quarantine and recuperation.

210 Days! The Shenzhou-21 Crew Sets a New Record for Single-Mission On-Orbit Stay Duration

This mission set a new record for a single crew's on-orbit stay duration of 210 days. During the mission:

The crew successfully completed 3 extravehicular operations;

Completed tasks including inspection photography of the Shenzhou-20 spacecraft's reentry capsule porthole and installation of space debris protection devices on the space station;

Conducted multiple cargo ingress and egress missions;

And, in close coordination with ground-based researchers, completed a large volume of space science experiments and tests in fields including microgravity fundamental physics, space materials science, space life sciences, aerospace medicine, and aerospace technology.

In addition, the crew personally experienced and witnessed several historic moments in the history of China's crewed spaceflight program: the first-ever postponement of a spacecraft's return due to a space debris impact, the first-ever crew return via a different spacecraft, and the first-ever emergency spacecraft launch. They experienced and witnessed the unforgettable moment when the entire crewed spaceflight program — ground and space united in one heart (天地同心、万众一心) — launched an emergency response.

At the press event, the three astronauts recalled their precious experiences during their stay in space and shared their individual unique experiences.

The "Space Apple" Returns Safely! Zhang Lu Brings Back a Special "Gift"

As one of the firsthand participants in China's first space emergency operation, Commander Zhang Lu candidly stated that what struck them most deeply about this space emergency experience was the composure and confidence of Chinese spaceflight — embodying the values of life first and mission success first (生命至上、成功至上), demonstrating the scientific work ethic of being rigorous, careful, meticulous, and precise (严慎细实、精益求精), and exemplifying the spirit of tackling hard challenges together and forging unity of purpose (攻坚克难、众志成城) in a great collaborative effort. Zhang Lu said: "The ground-space emergency response system of China's crewed spaceflight program has been tested in real operations and has passed review by the motherland and its people." Whether in handling the spacecraft's space debris impact, dynamically adjusting the stay duration, executing the crew's return via a different spacecraft, or organizing and implementing the emergency launch, the entire system was interlocked link by link with seamless coordination, constructing an effective safety guarantee for the astronauts on orbit.

Zhang Lu has carried out two flight missions — Shenzhou-15 and Shenzhou-21 — completing a cumulative total of 7 extravehicular operations, breaking the national record for the number of extravehicular activities by an individual Chinese astronaut. He candidly stated that every successful extravehicular excursion is the result of the silent support of tens of millions of aerospace workers, including: underwater training instructors who accompanied them throughout high-intensity water tank simulation training; extravehicular activity support teams who repeatedly rehearsed work procedures and refined contingency plans; and two extravehicular suits that were constant companions day and night — special "comrades-in-arms" guarding their lives.

At the event, Zhang Lu shared the story of the space apple. The apple is the fruit that has been stored the longest on the space station. During the Shenzhou-15 mission, Zhang Lu gave the last apple he had preserved to a crewmate as a birthday gift, bearing witness to the warm camaraderie between crews — to him, the apple is a sweet memory. In this mission, the apple carried more of a meaning of "safe return" (平安). Upon returning, he deliberately brought back an apple he had personally inscribed with the characters "平安" (safe return), expressing the shared wish of all aerospace workers that every mission proceed smoothly and safely.

Ground Developer + On-Orbit Operator: Wu Fei's Special Connection to Spaceflight

Spaceflight engineer Wu Fei has a special connection to spaceflight. Before becoming an astronaut, he participated in the development of the Tianhe core module and the Wentian experiment module, and was one of the earliest researchers to test and maintain the module equipment before the space station went to orbit. Now, operating on orbit with his own hands the equipment he had repeatedly debugged and serviced years ago, he was filled with the warm familiarity of "coming home." "The experience accumulated on the ground has all been transformed into the confidence and assurance for my precise on-orbit operations and calm judgment." Wu Fei expressed with deep feeling that this experience has also filled him with reverence for the aerospace endeavor, because behind every composed operation in space lies the round-the-clock rehearsals, experiments, and vigilance of ground-based researchers.

For his 210-day extended stay, Wu Fei summed up his personal gains with the word "tempering" (淬炼). At the level of individual capability, he unlocked three valuable "space skills": the ability for fine manipulation in a weightless environment, the ability for high-load ground-space coordinated communication, and the ability for rapid on-orbit troubleshooting. In his view, astronauts carry a threefold mission: they are the "scientist's hands" completing space experiments on behalf of scientists; they are the "engineer's eyes" inspecting and maintaining the space station; and they are, even more so, the "pioneer's feet" venturing forth into deep space exploration.

Zhang Hongzhang: Cultivating a "Space Peach Blossom Spring" — Shenzhou-21 Grows Tomatoes and Sweet Potatoes On Orbit

Payload specialist Zhang Hongzhang explained that the microgravity environment of space fundamentally alters the physical laws governing gravity, buoyancy, and fluid convection, differing vastly from ground laboratory conditions and presenting many special challenges for conducting various experiments. The research team completed in advance a full set of refined contingency plan designs. During the mission, the on-orbit experiments and tests achieved multiple domestic technological breakthroughs:

China's first on-orbit aeroponic cultivation of cherry tomatoes and wheat was achieved, validating key technologies including efficient utilization of light spectrum and water-fertilizer management;

China's first on-orbit closed-environment housing of mice in space was achieved, laying a technical foundation for subsequent space mammal experiments.

Beyond the heavy scientific research workload, the crew cultivated on orbit more than ten varieties of crops including naked oats, wheat, sunflowers, tomatoes, sweet potatoes, mint, and lettuce, tending a "Space Peach Blossom Spring" (太空桃花源) above the orbit, imbued with classical Chinese poetic sensibility. These not only validated and refined on-orbit cultivation techniques but also added considerable enjoyment to the high-intensity life in space.

How does one maintain vigorous energy over a long on-orbit stay? Zhang Hongzhang believes that ground support is the foundation, mutual crew assistance is the key, and self-adjustment is the secret. In particular, the profound depth of China's outstanding traditional culture offers many unique insights into regulating body and mind. Before resting each day, Zhang Hongzhang would softly recite classical poetry and practice Tai Chi — through the interplay of stillness and movement, he would calm the nerves wound tight throughout the day, quickly readjust his state, and ensure he could devote himself to work with full energy the following day.

The Shenzhou-21 Crew Encourages Youth Who Have Just Taken the Gaokao: Study Diligently and Act with Conviction, Serve the Motherland

Addressing the younger generation who have just completed the college entrance examination (gaokao) and aspire to serve the country through scientific research, Zhang Hongzhang encouraged them: "As long as you hold in your heart 'the great affairs of the nation' (国之大者), study diligently and act with conviction (勤学笃行), advance steadily, and continuously hone your own abilities, you will ultimately forge a broad path that belongs to you and through which you can serve the motherland, shining and contributing in your own field." The atmosphere throughout the press event was enthusiastic, with all present expressing that they will not forget their original aspirations, will continue to strive, will meet new missions with an even fuller spirit, and will press forward with resolve toward the deeper reaches of space.

According to a relevant official from the China Astronaut Research and Training Center, under the careful care of the research support team at the China Astronaut Center, the Shenzhou-21 crew is in good physical and mental condition; muscle strength, endurance, and cardiopulmonary fitness have basically recovered to pre-flight levels, and they have currently entered the recovery observation phase. After completing the post-mission return recovery health evaluation and summary, they will transition to normal training and work.

(Central Broadcasting Corporation reporters: Tan Lin, Ma Yuchen, Kang Pengcheng, Wang Xiaozun)

近日，中国航天员科研训练中心在北京航天城举办神舟二十一号航天员乘组与记者见面会。这是张陆、武飞、张洪章三名航天员返回地球并完成隔离和疗养恢复后，首次面向媒体与社会公众公开亮相。 210天！ 神二十一乘组创下单次在轨驻留时长新纪录 此次任务创下单次乘组210天在轨驻留时长新纪录。在轨期间： 乘组圆满完成3次出舱作业， 完成了神舟二十号飞船返回舱舷窗巡检拍照、空间站空间碎片防护装置安装等任务， 开展了多次货物进出舱任务， 并在地面科研人员密切配合下，完成了涉及微重力基础物理、空间材料科学、空间生命科学、航天医学、航天技术等领域大量空间科学实（试）验。 此外，乘组在轨亲身经历和见证了我国载人航天工程史上首次飞船受空间碎片撞击推迟返回、首次航天员乘组换船返回、首次飞船应急发射等历史时刻，经历和见证了载人航天工程全线天地同心、万众一心开展应急行动的难忘瞬间。 见面会上，三名航天员回忆了太空驻留的宝贵经历，分享了各自的独特体验。 “太空苹果”平安归来！ 张陆带回一份特殊“礼物” 作为我国首次太空应急行动的亲历者之一，指令长张陆坦言，这次太空应急行动的经历，让他们感受最深的就是中国航天的从容自信，体现的是生命至上、成功至上的价值追求，展示的是严慎细实、精益求精的科研作风，还有攻坚克难、众志成城的大协作精神。张陆说：“我国载人航天工程的天地应急体系，经历了实战考验，接受了祖国和人民的检阅。”无论是飞船遭受空间碎片撞击的处置、驻留周期动态调整、乘组换船返回，还是应急发射组织实施，整套体系环环相扣、无缝衔接，为在轨航天员构建起有效的安全保障。 张陆先后执行神舟十五号、神舟二十一号两次飞行任务，累计完成7次舱外作业，刷新了我国航天员个人出舱活动次数纪录。他坦言，每一次成功出舱，都是千万航天人的默默托举，其中包括：水下训练教员全程相伴开展高强度水槽模拟训练，出舱活动支持团队反复推演工作流程、打磨应急方案，两套舱外服更是朝夕相伴、守护生命安全的特殊“战友”。 现场，张陆分享了太空苹果故事。苹果是空间站在轨储备最久的水果，神舟十五号任务时，张陆将他保存的最后一枚苹果当作生日礼物送给队友，见证乘组间温暖情谊，对他来说，苹果是甜蜜的回忆；而本次任务中，苹果更多承载的是“平安”的寓意，返航之际，他还特意带回一枚亲手标注 “平安”的苹果，寄托全体航天人期盼每次任务顺遂平安的共同心愿。 地面研制者+在轨操作者 武飞的特别航天缘分 航天飞行工程师武飞拥有一段特别的航天缘分。成为航天员前，他曾参与天和核心舱、问天实验舱研制工作，是空间站上天前最早测试、守护舱体设备的科研人员之一。如今，在轨亲手操作当年反复调试检修的设备，心中满是“回家”的亲切感。“地面工作积累的经验，都化作了我在轨精准操作、沉着判断的底气和信心。”武飞深情流露，这段经历也使得他对航天事业充满敬畏，因为太空里每一次从容操作，背后都是地面科研人员日夜不休的推演、试验与守护。 210天超长驻留，武飞以“淬炼”二字概括自身收获。个人能力层面，他解锁了三项宝贵的“太空技能”：失重环境下的精细操作能力、高负荷下的天地协同沟通能力和在轨快速排故能力。在他眼中，航天员身负三重使命：是替科学家完成太空实验的 “科学家之手”，是巡检维护空间站的“工程师之眼”，更是奔赴深空探索的“开拓者之足”。 张洪章：耕耘出一片“太空桃花源” 神二十一在轨种番茄、红薯 载荷专家张洪章介绍，太空微重力环境彻底改变重力、浮力、流体对流等物理规律，与地面实验室环境差异悬殊，给各类试验开展带来诸多特殊挑战，科研团队提前完成全套精细化预案设计。任务期间，在轨实（试）验实现了多项国内技术突破： 国内首次实现了樱桃番茄和小麦在轨气雾培养，验证了光谱和水肥的高效利用等关键技术； 国内首次实现了小鼠的空间密闭在轨饲养，为后续开展空间哺乳动物实验奠定了技术基础。 繁重科研任务之余，乘组在轨栽种莜麦、小麦、向日葵、番茄、红薯、薄荷、生菜等十余种作物，在轨道之上耕耘出一片满含中式诗意的“太空桃花源”。它们不仅验证和完善了在轨种植技术，也给高强度的太空生活增添了不少乐趣。 如何能够在轨长时间保持旺盛的精力？张洪章认为，地面保障是基础，乘组互助是关键，自我调适是秘诀。特别是中华优秀传统文化博大精深，在调适身心方面有很多独到之处。每天休息前，张洪章会轻声诵读古典诗词，练习太极拳，一静一动之间平复全天紧绷的神经，快速调整状态，保障次日能够精力充沛投入工作。 神舟二十一号乘组勉励高考青年：勤学笃行，报效家国 面向刚结束高考、立志科研报国的青年一代，张洪章勉励大家：“只要心怀‘国之大者’，勤学笃行、稳步向前，不断打磨自身本领，终能走出属于自己、也能报效家国的宽阔道路，在属于自己的领域发光发热。”整场见面会气氛热烈，大家一致表示，将不忘初心，接续奋斗，以更饱满的状态迎接新任务，向着更深远的太空勇毅前行。 据中国航天员科研训练中心相关负责人介绍，在中国航天员中心科研保障团队精心照料下，神舟二十一号乘组身心状态良好，肌肉力量、耐力和运动心肺功能基本恢复至飞行前水平，目前转入恢复观察阶段。待完成任务返回后恢复期健康评估总结后，转入正常训练和工作。 （总台记者 谭琳 马宇辰 康鹏程 王孝尊）
