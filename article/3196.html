Profiles in "Two Excellence, One Vanguard" (两优一先) | The "Cloud Sentinel Post" Stands Fast atop the Snow-Capped Mountains

“两优一先”风采录丨“云中哨位”坚守在雪山之巅
PLA Daily (解放军报) 13 August 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
The Party branch of Detachment 66, Unit 95662, stationed at roughly 5,000 meters elevation on the plateau, has received the PLA's 'Nationally Advanced Grassroots Party Organization' and 'All-Army Combat Readiness Model Unit' designations, with branch secretary Long Wanlun describing efforts to compress air-ground intelligence and fire-unit coordinated kill-chain (空地情报与火力单元协同杀伤链) coordination from the minute level to the second level. This is standard political work content in the 'Two Excellence, One Vanguard' (两优一先) awards series; its value is as a record of how the institution frames loyalty and combat readiness to junior officers at a high-altitude early-warning and detection post, not as evidence of a new capability or posture shift. The kill-chain compression claim is the one operationally specific detail, but the article provides no independent verification of the result, and the unit's precise mission set—beyond air situation reporting and early-warning detection—remains unspecified.
Translation

Nationally Advanced Grassroots Party Organization, Party Branch of Unit 95662, Detachment 66 — The "Cloud Sentinel Post" Stands Fast atop the Snow-Capped Mountains

■ PLA Daily Correspondents Zhang Leifeng and Wang Yue; Special Correspondent Qu Xiyang

Photo: Party members of the detachment reaffirm their Party oath before the position. Photo by Zhu Yuanqiang

In the height of summer, the snow-covered plateau at nearly 5,000 meters above sea level remains bitterly cold. At a certain position of Unit 95662, Detachment 66, as the clouds and mist slowly parted, all Party member cadres faced the Party flag, raised their right fists, and solemnly swore their oath — their resounding vow echoing across the mountaintop.

Stepping into the detachment's honor room, plaques reading "Nationally Advanced Grassroots Party Organization" and "All-Army Combat Readiness Model Unit" stand quietly on display, like the outstanding answer sheets submitted by this combat fortress in the clouds. The detachment's Party branch secretary, Long Wanlun, told reporters that over the years the Party branch has consistently placed forging steadfast loyalty and faith (铸牢忠诚信仰) at the top of its agenda, continuously pressing toward the goal of "building the branch at the company level — and making it strong at the company level" (支部建在连上、更要强在连上), achieving a leap-forward development (跨越式发展) across all areas of construction.

"More than 60 years ago, a single order sent our revolutionary forebears pressing without hesitation into this forbidden zone for life. Oxygen deprivation, water shortages, lack of supplies — no hardship could stop their resolute steps. Taking root at the cloud sentinel post on the snow-covered plateau was sustained by the flame of faith burning in their chests." Long Wanlun said: times have changed and conditions have improved, but the Party branch's standard for carrying forward the red gene (红色基因) and consolidating the ideological foundation has never been lowered by half a measure.

In recent years, they have built an offline main classroom at the position, an online MOOC (massive open online course) lecture hall, and a new-media mobile classroom, allowing theoretical study to truly "come alive" and "move"; they have abandoned rote recitation and transformed the Party's innovative theories into "soldier-speak" (兵言兵语) that officers and soldiers can understand and put into practice; they have persisted in raising political requirements when taking up a post, studying the current situation when powering on equipment, and reminding personnel of handling regulations when logging air situation reports — embedding "obeying the Party's command" (听党指挥) into every shift of duty and every operation, and engraving "loyalty to the Party" (对党忠诚) onto every high-altitude combat position. The thematic practice activity "Remember the Leader's Entrustment, Forge Elite Troops of the Snow Region" (牢记领袖重托、锻造雪域精兵), conducted as a regular practice, works like a spring breeze and rain, allowing the ideal and conviction of "taking root on the plateau and guarding the air and space" (扎根高原、守护空天) to fuse deeply into the blood of officers and soldiers.

Faith internalized in the heart, responsibility externalized in action (信仰内化于心，担当外化于行). The branch has rigorously pursued concentrated intra-Party education, driving basic education and routine ideological education to resonate at the same frequency. "Position organizational life" (阵地组织生活) and "tent Party small-group meetings" (帐篷党小组会) are conducted as routine activities; Party member cadres rectify their conduct through the collision of ideas and temper their loyalty on the front lines of their combat positions.

"Enduring hardship is not the same as making a contribution — winning is the contribution." When it comes to training for combat readiness, the branch leadership has a clear-eyed understanding: being stationed on the plateau does not mean holding the plateau; standing above the clouds means one must achieve merit above the clouds.

In the past, some officers and soldiers relied on old experience when on duty, and their thinking about training trended toward conservatism. The branch leadership took the lead in breaking through this status-quo mentality (守成心态). Targeting the difficulties of heavy clutter interference in high-altitude mountain terrain and blind spots in low-altitude detection, they drove combat readiness to achieve three transformations: from passively waiting for tasks to actively making forward-looking assessments; from single-station independent handling to system-integrated coordinated linkage (体系协同联动); and from relying on experience to judge air situation reports to using data for precise support.

The branch explored and established a "problem-posting challenge" mechanism (难题揭榜机制), posting one by one on the wall the shortcomings exposed during combat readiness duty and exercise-and-training confrontations. Facing the longstanding, stubborn problem of intelligence links between services being unsmooth and data synchronization being obstructed — causing coordinated handling to lag by half a beat — branch leadership personally organized a technical assault, debugging interface by interface, running through process by process, compressing time limits second by second, and exploring and innovating an information-data-sharing mechanism that compressed the construction of the air-ground intelligence and fire-unit coordinated kill chain (空地情报与火力单元协同杀伤链) from the "minute level" to the "second level."

Superior combat capability originates from the silent inspiration of Party member cadres charging at the front. The detachment has an unwritten rule: the most arduous duty shifts — Party members go first; the most difficult specialist subjects — Party members train first; the highest-risk emergency tasks — Party members step forward first.

Staff Sergeant First Class and Party member cadre Zeng Honglin vividly remembers the emergency from last year: late one night, a violent wind carrying a blizzard struck, and the communications antenna faced the danger of damage and fracture. The Party member assault team moved on the order, and under conditions of more than minus 20 degrees Celsius, braved the wind and snow to climb more than ten meters up the antenna mast to remove ice and clear the obstruction. After officers and soldiers fought continuously, the communications antenna emerged unscathed.

Standing at the very front in moments of crisis, taking the lead as a model in high-difficulty training, grabbing the heaviest work in daily tasks — the branch routinely carries out joint-construction and joint-innovation activities (联建联创活动), organizes the practice activity "One Post, One Responsibility; One Party Member, One Flag" (一个岗位一份担当、一个党员一面旗帜), and sets the hard standard of "discipline first, capability first, hard work first, dedication first" (纪律第一、能力第一、实干第一、奉献第一), driving all personnel to forge the ability to be proficient in multiple specialties (一专多能).

Thanks to the mentoring and guidance of Party member cadres, every officer and soldier in the detachment is proficient in multiple posts and capable of working independently; the air situation report qualification rate (空情合格率) has been maintained at one hundred percent year after year; post skills competitions and special assessments have repeatedly produced outstanding results, giving rise to a cohort of advanced models including "All-Army Network-Electronic Warfare and Early Warning Detection Advanced Individual" (全军网电作战和预警探测先进个人) and "Air and Space Vanguard" (空天先锋).

As the sun sets in the west, golden afterglow spreads across the position, and the Party flag that has witnessed countless storms and glories grows ever more vivid and striking. Long Wanlun pointed toward the distant, unbroken snow peaks and told reporters in a firm tone: "Standing fast at the cloud sentinel post atop the snow-capped mountains — we will absolutely never fail the entrustment of the Party and the people!"

全国先进基层党组织、95662部队66分队党支部—— “云中哨位”坚守在雪山之巅 ■解放军报记者 张磊峰 王 越 特约记者 曲喜洋 该分队党员在阵地前重温入党誓词。朱元强 摄 盛夏时节，海拔近5000米的雪域高原依旧充满寒意。云雾渐渐散开的95662部队66分队某阵地上，全体党员骨干面向党旗，举起右拳庄严宣誓，铮铮誓言响彻山巅。 走进该分队荣誉室，“全国先进基层党组织”“全军备战标兵单位”……一块块牌匾静静陈列，恰似这个云端战斗堡垒交出的优秀答卷。该分队党支部书记龙万伦告诉记者，多年来，党支部始终坚持把铸牢忠诚信仰摆在首位，瞄准“支部建在连上、更要强在连上”的目标持续发力，全面建设实现跨越式发展。 “60多年前一纸命令，革命先辈义无反顾挺进这片生命禁区。缺氧、缺水、缺物资，再大的困难也挡不住他们坚定的脚步。在雪域高原的云中哨位扎根，靠的是胸膛里那团信仰的火。”龙万伦说，时代变了，条件好了，但党支部传承红色基因、夯实思想根基的标准从未降低半分。 近年来，他们搭建阵地线下主课堂、线上慕课大课堂、新媒体移动新课堂，让理论学习真正“活”起来、“动”起来；摒弃照本宣科，把党的创新理论化为官兵听得懂、能践行的“兵言兵语”；坚持上岗必讲政治要求，开机必研当前形势，录空情必嘱处置规定，把“听党指挥”融入每一次执勤、每一次操作，将“对党忠诚”镌刻在每一个高原战位。常态化开展的“牢记领袖重托、锻造雪域精兵”主题实践活动如春风化雨，让“扎根高原、守护空天”的理想信念深深融入官兵血脉。 信仰内化于心，担当外化于行。该支部狠抓党内集中教育，推动基础教育与经常性思想教育同频共振。“阵地组织生活”“帐篷党小组会”常态开展，党员干部在思想碰撞中整肃作风，在战位一线磨砺忠诚。 “艰苦不算奉献，打赢才是贡献。”谈及练兵备战，该支部一班人有着清醒认识：守在高原不等于守住高原，站上云端更要建功云端。 过去，部分官兵依赖老经验执勤，练兵思维趋于保守。该支部一班人带头破除守成心态，针对高原山地杂波干扰大、低空探测存在盲区的难点，推动备战打仗实现三重转变：由被动等任务转为主动前瞻研判，由单站独立处置转为体系协同联动，由靠经验判断空情转为以数据精准支撑。 该支部探索建立“难题揭榜”机制，把战备值班和演训对抗中暴露的短板一一挂到墙上。面对各军种间情报链路不畅、数据同步受阻导致协同处置慢半拍的“老大难”问题，支部领导带头组织攻关，逐接口调试、逐流程磨合、逐秒压缩时限，探索创新信息数据共享机制，将空地情报与火力单元协同杀伤链的构建从“分钟级”压缩到“秒级”。 过硬的战斗力，源自党员骨干冲锋在前的无声感召。该分队有一条不成文的规矩：最艰苦的值班班次，党员先值；难度最大的专业课目，党员先练；风险最高的突发任务，党员先上。 三级军士长、党员骨干曾洪林对去年那次险情记忆犹新——一天深夜，狂风裹挟暴雪袭来，通信天线面临受损断裂的危险。党员突击队闻令而动，在零下20多摄氏度的严寒条件下，冒着风雪攀上10余米高的天线架除冰排障。经过官兵连续鏖战，通信天线安然无恙。 危急关头挺在最前面，险难训练率先作示范，日常工作抢最重的干……该支部常态化开展联建联创活动，组织“一个岗位一份担当、一个党员一面旗帜”实践活动，立起“纪律第一、能力第一、实干第一、奉献第一”硬标准，带动全员锤炼一专多能本领。 得益于党员骨干的传帮带，分队官兵人人精通多岗、个个独当一面，空情合格率常年保持百分之百；岗位比武、专项考核屡创佳绩，涌现出“全军网电作战和预警探测先进个人”“空天先锋”等一批先进典型。 夕阳西下，金色的余晖洒满阵地，那面见证了无数风雪与荣光的党旗愈发鲜艳夺目。龙万伦遥指远方连绵的雪峰，语气坚定地告诉记者：“坚守雪山之巅的云中哨位，我们绝不辜负党和人民的重托！”
