Correct Outlook on Political Achievement, Practiced in the Barracks | Highland Wind and Snow Bite Cold, Well Water Nourishes the Heart

正确政绩观 践行在军营丨高原风雪寒 井水润心田

View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
The Hongshanhe Maintenance Station, a high-altitude signals facility of what is now the Information Support Force located deep in the Karakoram on the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau, completed a decade-long relay effort across multiple unit reorganizations to drill a 150-meter well, build a water purification station, and solve frozen-pipe failures, ending more than 40 years of water hardship for its garrison. The article is standard political work content — a PLA Daily case study illustrating the concept of 正确政绩观 (correct outlook on political achievement) and the paired slogans 功成不必在我 / 功成必定有我 — and its value is as a record of how the institution frames institutional continuity and soldier welfare to mid-level Party committee leaders, not as evidence of a new policy line or changed posture at the station.
Translation

Highland Wind and Snow Bite Cold, Well Water Nourishes the Heart

■ Pu Jiehong, Li Zihan, PLA Daily Reporter Sun Xingwei

Deep in the Karakoram, amid the ancient permafrost of the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau, there is a maintenance station called Hongshanhe.

It is a place name that cannot be found on any map, and it is one of our military's high-altitude garrison posts. More than 40 years ago, a group of signal soldiers started from nothing here and built what became the highest-altitude maintenance station in the entire military, shouldering the responsibility of maintaining and ensuring communications line connectivity.

Hongshanhe is named after a "river," yet there is no river. The problem of obtaining water has plagued the station's officers and soldiers since it was first established. Due to the lack of water, the signal soldiers at Hongshanhe had no choice but to haul water from a lake nearly a hundred kilometers away, making the long trek back to the garrison. In extreme weather, they could only find ways to melt snow for emergency use.

Sinking a well in the garrison and drinking clean water became the dream of generation after generation of officers and soldiers.

The hardships of the officers and soldiers weighed on the hearts of leadership organs at every level. But sinking a well on the plateau — easier said than done.

Hongshanhe Maintenance Station is located deep in the Karakoram. The garrison is frigid, oxygen-deprived, and snow-covered year-round, with a harsh natural environment and no civilian support infrastructure. High altitude, saline-alkali soil, permafrost — one obstacle after another stood in the way, making the road to sinking a well long and arduous.

Illustration: Fang Han

From a certain unit of the Xinjiang Military District to a certain unit of the Information Support Force, over the years Hongshanhe Maintenance Station has been transferred through several reorganizations, spanning multiple service branch units, yet the higher-level units to which it belonged never abandoned the effort to sink a well.

Six years ago, the then-political commissar of a certain unit learned of this situation during an investigation and research visit and resolved to solve the longstanding water problem for the officers and soldiers.

After descending from the plateau, he immediately organized the staff to research and initiate a project. At the Party committee meeting, the well-drilling plan was studied and approved. However, before the project could get underway, the unit underwent restructuring and reform, and the plan was temporarily shelved.

This political commissar was reassigned due to the reform. When his successor took office, many people assumed that "when the leadership changes, everything gets overturned," and that the well-drilling matter would come to nothing. However, the new Party committee made its position clear: "The well-drilling effort must not be abandoned — each cohort must carry it forward from the last."

When the project first went to tender, many local enterprises took one look at the drilling conditions and walked away. After great difficulty, an agreement was reached with one company, but the construction crew had not been working long before they discovered that the project was far more difficult than imagined:

The plateau is nothing like the plains — the rock strata are numerous and the geology is hard, posing an enormous challenge to drilling equipment. By the time they had drilled just 80 meters down, two drill bits had already been destroyed. Officers and soldiers leaned over the edge of the wellhead, craning their necks to look down — only mud came up, no water — and the disappointment on every face was impossible to hide.

"The geology here is truly hard!" The construction crew leader went to the political commissar to complain and began to waver. The political commissar communicated and coordinated repeatedly, again and again requesting that they "keep drilling."

At the end of 2021, two drilling vehicles worked in shifts around the clock for four months and finally succeeded in striking water. "We finally have water — this was truly no easy feat!" That day, as clear spring water gushed up from 150 meters below ground, the construction workers and the station's officers and soldiers could not hold back tears of joy.

However, when the water quality inspection report came out, the officers and soldiers were stunned: testing showed that the mineral content of the well water severely exceeded standards and could not be drunk directly.

The problem was once again placed before the Party committee. When the discussion turned to whether to build a separate water purification station to treat the water quality, opinions were divided. Some felt that "the maintenance station doesn't have many people — building another water purification station would require too large an investment, with a low cost-effectiveness ratio." Some suggested "using the funds for headquarters infrastructure construction, so there would be something to 'show' during higher-level inspections." Some thought that "building a water purification station on the plateau is time-consuming and labor-intensive, and its feasibility remains to be demonstrated"…

"Officers and soldiers give their all to fulfill the mission — the organization cannot let them down. Funds are meant to be spent on solving the urgent difficulties and pressing concerns (急难愁盼) of officers and soldiers!" In the end, consensus was reached: "We will carry this 'heart-warming project' (暖心工程) forward in relay — we must ensure officers and soldiers drink safe, high-quality water!"

After three years of arduous construction, the water purification station was finally completed. Everyone thought they would finally be able to use "safe water," but unexpectedly, as autumn gave way to winter, disaster struck again — the water pipes froze and burst. Although heating facilities had been installed during construction of the purification station, they still could not withstand the extreme nighttime low temperatures of -30°C at Hongshanhe in winter.

The pipes were buried 1.5 meters underground. Because of the hard geology, officers and soldiers dug from early morning until late at night for a long time without even catching a glimpse of the pipes. With no other option, the maintenance station's officers and soldiers went back to hauling water from the frozen lake.

Before long, the unit's Party committee underwent a change of leadership. Upon learning of Hongshanhe Maintenance Station's predicament, the newly appointed political commissar made the station his very first grassroots destination, traveling 2,000 kilometers by a roundabout route to come to the station in person and work out solutions together with the officers and soldiers.

After a period of research and demonstration, the approach of using "movement" to prevent "freezing" (以"动"防"冻") emerged: a water pump was added to the drainage pipes to keep both the supply and drainage pipes flowing at all times. At the same time, the previous method of direct-burial piping was changed, and a new underground maintenance tunnel was constructed to facilitate routine maintenance by officers and soldiers. Considering that the permafrost would thaw in summer, they also specifically used reinforced concrete construction to prevent the tunnel from collapsing and causing danger.

"Carrying the well-drilling effort forward in relay, clean water nourishes Hongshanhe." Day by day, the renovation project was completed amid the eager anticipation of officers and soldiers, and clear well water finally achieved round-the-clock stable supply. Hongshanhe Maintenance Station thus completely bid farewell to more than 40 years of water hardship.

"Difficulty is precisely where responsibility is shown; hardship is precisely where character is revealed. The pool of well water at Hongshanhe reflects the spirit of several successive Party committee teams — the bearing of 'success need not come on my watch' (功成不必在我) and the responsibility of 'success will surely come with my contribution' (功成必定有我)." Looking at the clear well water before them, the maintenance station's officers and soldiers always recall the pledge made by the unit's leadership at the Party committee meeting: "We must ensure officers and soldiers drink safe, high-quality water!"

Genuine Care Is the Sweetest Spring

■ Liu Weijiao

From shoveling snow and chipping ice to sinking a deep well, from purifying water quality to solving frozen pipes, the complete resolution of Hongshanhe Maintenance Station's more than 40 years of water hardship is both a moving story of concern for the grassroots and warmth toward officers and soldiers, and a vivid educational lesson in how to establish and practice a correct outlook on political achievement (正确政绩观).

What is a correct outlook on political achievement? One of its essential points is to wholeheartedly do good things, handle practical matters, and resolve difficult problems for officers and soldiers. Faced with challenges including extreme cold and oxygen deprivation, drilling through permafrost, substandard water quality, and frozen burst pipes, the successive Party committee teams of the unit to which Hongshanhe Maintenance Station belongs did not shirk responsibility because of unit transfers, did not allow continuity to break because of changes in principal officers, and did not give up because the effort was "large in investment, slow to show results, and unlikely to shine." Instead, they kept the grassroots' hopes and the officers' and soldiers' needs constantly in mind, carrying the work forward cohort after cohort, striking blow after blow, and ultimately made this heart-warming project warm the hearts of soldiers and rally their morale.

Chairman Xi has repeatedly emphasized: "Creating prosperity for the people is the greatest political achievement." Yet in reality, some units and leading cadres, though highly enthusiastic about doing practical things for soldiers, do not actually do the things soldiers need most. At its root, this reflects a lack of a people-centered philosophy and a correct outlook on political achievement. When leading cadres and staff organs remain committed to caring for grassroots officers and soldiers — treating the voices of officers and soldiers as the primary signal, keeping their urgent difficulties, pressing concerns, safety, and well-being constantly in mind and firmly in hand — the political achievements they produce will be like the deep well at Hongshanhe Maintenance Station: flowing with the clearest and sweetest spring water.

高原风雪寒 井水润心田 ■蒲杰鸿 李子涵 解放军报记者 孙兴维 冰封雪裹的喀喇昆仑腹地，青藏高原亘古冻土之上，有一个机务站，名叫红山河。 这是一个在地图上找不到的地名，也是我军的一个高原驻兵点。40多年前，一群通信兵在这里白手起家，建起这座全军海拔最高的机务站，担起保障联通通信线路的重任。 红山河以“河”命名，却没有河。用水难题自建站以来就一直困扰着全站官兵。因为缺水，红山河的通信兵不得不从近百公里外的湖里取水，长途跋涉拉回营区。遇到极端天气，只能想方设法融化雪水应急。 在营区打一口井，喝上纯净的饮用水，成为一代代官兵心中的梦想。 官兵的困难，牵动着各级领导机关的心。可是在高原上打井，谈何容易？ 红山河机务站位于喀喇昆仑深处，驻地高寒缺氧、四季飘雪，自然环境恶劣，没有社会依托。海拔高、盐碱地、永冻层……一个个难题横亘在面前，让打井之路变得漫长而艰辛。 插图：方 汉 从新疆军区某部到信息支援部队某部，多年来，红山河机务站几经转隶，跨越了几个军兵种单位，但其所在上级单位一直没有放弃打井的探索。 6年前，时任某部政委在调研中得知这一情况，决心为官兵解决用水“老大难”。 下山后，他立即组织机关研究立项，党委会上，研究通过了打井方案。然而，工程尚未启动，部队便迎来调整改革，方案暂时搁置。 这名政委因改革转岗，继任的新政委上任之初，不少人以为“领导一换兜底翻”，打井一事将不了了之。然而，新一届党委班子态度鲜明：“打井的事不能断，一茬接着一茬干。” 招标之初，不少地方企业一看打井条件，摆摆手就走了。好不容易与一家企业达成协议，施工队开工不久就发现，这项工程比想象中难了不是一星半点—— 高原不同于平原，岩层多、地质硬，对钻井设备是一个巨大的考验。刚打到80米深，钻头就钻坏了两根。官兵们趴在井沿，伸着头往下看，只有泥浆不出水，一个个脸上藏不住的失落。 “这地质真硬！”施工队领导找到政委诉苦，打起了“退堂鼓”。政委反复沟通协调做工作，一再请求“继续打下去”。 2021年底，2台钻井车历经4个月昼夜轮流作业，终于成功打出井水。“总算有水了，真是太不容易了！”那天，当汩汩清泉从150米深的井中喷涌而出，施工队工人和全站官兵忍不住喜极而泣。 然而，水质检验报告出炉，官兵们傻了眼：经检测，井水矿物质含量严重超标，不能直接饮用。 难题再次摆上党委会。讨论研究是否另建一座净水站净化水质时，大家意见不一。有人认为，“机务站人不多，再建一座净水站投入太大，效费比低”；有人建议，“把经费用在机关基础设施建设上，上级检查时有‘看点’”；有人觉得，“高原建净水站耗时费力，可行性有待论证”…… “官兵为履行使命尽心，组织不能让官兵寒心。经费就是要花在解决官兵急难愁盼上！”最后，大家形成共识，“把这项‘暖心工程’接力干下去，一定要让官兵喝上放心水、优质水！” 历经3年艰苦施工，净水站终于竣工。原以为可以用上“放心水”了，没想到秋去冬至，意外再次发生——水管冻裂。尽管净水站建设时安装了加热设施，还是没扛住红山河冬日夜间-30℃的极端低温。 管道埋在地下1.5米深。因地质坚硬，官兵起早摸黑挖了许久，连水管的影子都没见到。无奈之下，机务站官兵又过上冰湖拉水的日子。 没多久，该部党委班子换届。得知红山河机务站的困境，新任政委下基层的第一站，就辗转2000公里专程赶到站里，与官兵一起想办法。 经过一番研究论证，以“动”防“冻”的思路应运而生：在下水管道上加装一台水泵，让上水和下水管道全时流动。同时，改变以往直埋管道的方法，在地下新建一条维修通道，方便官兵日常维护。考虑到夏季冻土会融化，他们还专门采用钢混结构建设，防止通道坍塌造成危险。 “接力打好一口井，净水滋润红山河。”日子一天天过去，改造工程在官兵翘首期盼中完成，清澈的井水终于实现全天候稳定供应，红山河机务站从此彻底告别长达40多年的“用水难”问题。 “难，方显担当；苦，更见境界。红山河的一汪井水，映照出几届党委班子‘功成不必在我’的境界、‘功成必定有我’的担当。”看着眼前的清澈井水，机务站官兵总会想起该部领导在党委会上作出的承诺：“一定要让官兵喝上放心水、优质水！” 真情如泉最甘甜 ■刘维姣 从铲雪凿冰到打出深井，从净化水质到解决冻管，红山河机务站40多年用水之困的彻底解决，既是一个心系基层、情暖官兵的感人故事，也是一堂如何树立和践行正确政绩观的生动教育课。 什么是正确的政绩观？要义之一就是全心全意为官兵做好事、办实事、解难事。面对高寒缺氧、冻土钻井、水质不达标、水管冻裂等挑战，红山河机务站所在单位几届党委班子没有因单位转隶而推责，没有因主官更换而断档，也没有因“投入大、见效慢、难出彩”而放弃，而是始终把基层所盼、官兵所需记挂在心，一茬接着一茬干、一锤接着一锤敲，最终让这项暖心工程暖了兵心、聚了士气。 习主席反复强调：“为民造福是最大政绩”。然而，现实中一些单位和领导干部，为兵办实事的工作热情虽高，但所办之事并不是官兵最需要的。究其根本，是缺乏以人为本的理念和正确的政绩观。领导干部和机关坚持情系基层官兵，把官兵的呼声作为第一信号，把官兵的急难愁盼、安危冷暖时刻放在心上、抓在手上，做出的政绩就会像红山河机务站的深井一样，流淌出最清澈、最甘甜的泉水。
