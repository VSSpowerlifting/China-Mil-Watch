A Glimpse of Officers and Soldiers of the "Ren Changlun Company" Carrying Forward the Spirit of Heroes

“任常伦连”官兵赓续英雄精神掠影
PLA Daily (解放军报) 26 July 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
The 'Ren Changlun Company' of an unspecified brigade under the Southern Theater Command Army uses daily singing of its 1945 company song — commemorating Eighth Route Army hero Ren Changlun, killed at Haiyang Changshabo in November 1944 — as a structured political-work practice, illustrated here by platoon leader Luogulari's account of recovering from a 2023 loss at the 'Southern · Cutting Edge' (南部·锋刃) sniper course to win first place the following year. This is standard heroic-lineage unit (荣誉连队) political education content; its value is as a record of how the PLA frames individual resilience and competitive failure through hero-unit identity rather than as evidence of any capability or posture change. The brigade designation is withheld, so the article cannot be used to track the unit's location or order of battle.
Translation

Songs Temper the Warrior's Heart — A Glimpse of Officers and Soldiers of the "Ren Changlun Company" Carrying Forward the Spirit of Heroes

■ Wang Shaoxing, Wang Mingcheng, Wang Zemin — Photo Report

On July 9, 2026, company officers and soldiers evacuate residents trapped by floodwaters.

Officers and soldiers sing the company song in full voice.

Conducting tactical training.

"Combat hero Ren Changlun, he is a man of Sun Huzhuang in Huangxian County, at nineteen he joined the Eighth Route Army. In battle he was fierce as a tiger, charging at the head of the assault…"

Eighty years ago, this song rang out again and again in the villages of the Jiaodong region and among marching troops. Eighty years later, inside the garrison of the "Ren Changlun Company" of a certain brigade of the Southern Theater Command Army, officers and soldiers still sing this song every day. The singing crosses time and space, fusing the spirit and sentiments of an anti-Japanese war hero deep into the bloodline of officers and soldiers of the new era.

Looking back to the years of the War of Resistance Against Japan: Ren Changlun joined the anti-Japanese self-defense corps at age 17, enlisted in the Eighth Route Army at 19, experienced more than 120 engagements large and small, was wounded nine times yet continued to charge forward, and was awarded the title of "First-Class Combat Hero" by the Shandong Military District. In November 1944, during the Battle of Haiyang Changshabo — the final counter-"mopping-up" (反"扫荡") operation by the military and civilians of Jiaodong — then-Deputy Platoon Leader Ren Changlun fought with all his blood. When his ammunition ran out, he resolutely engaged the enemy in bayonet combat. In the end, after killing five enemy soldiers with his bayonet, he fell heroically in battle at only 23 years of age.

The hero fell; his spirit endures. After the Battle of Changshabo, the company in which Ren Changlun had served was named the "Ren Changlun Company." The following year, his comrades-in-arms composed the song "Combat Hero Ren Changlun." From that point on, this song became the company song, sung without cease, inspiring generation after generation of officers and soldiers to shoulder heavy responsibilities and press forward with courage.

The company's current platoon leader, Luogulari, is a heroic successor who grew up listening to this song. In 2023, he led a team to participate in the "Southern · Cutting Edge" (南部·锋刃) sniper training course; due to insufficient preparation, they lost in the competition, leaving him both dejected and remorseful. The following year, when he again heard news of the training competition, Luogulari was for a time hesitant and undecided. It was at that moment that a familiar melody rose from the garrison. "Compared to the heroic predecessors, what do the setbacks I've encountered amount to?" Luogulari suddenly came to his senses and once again volunteered to serve as an instructor. During the training course, he and his team members kept their bows fully drawn and gave everything they had: they stacked cartridge cases on the barrel and held a firing position for three hours at a stretch; they ran 15 kilometers under a 25-kilogram load and immediately dropped down to practice breath control; their notebooks were filled, line after line, with data on wind speed, humidity, and ballistics…

On the day of the assessment, a torrential rain suddenly arrived. Luogulari did not hesitate for a moment, leading his team members charging into the curtain of rain. Hold breath, aim, fire… In the end, they achieved the outstanding result of first place in the overall evaluation and won the honor of "Gold Medal Sniper Team."

Standing on the awards platform, Luogulari sang the company song at the top of his voice. His comrades praised him as the "Eagle of the Yi Mountains" (彝家山鹰), but he said: "It was the hero's anthem of praise that inspired me to fly over the most treacherous peaks."

歌声激荡砺兵心 ——“任常伦连”官兵赓续英雄精神掠影 ■王绍兴 王明城 王泽民 摄影报道 2026年7月9日，连队官兵转移被洪水围困的群众。 官兵高唱连歌。 开展战术训练。 “战斗英雄任常伦，他是黄县孙胡庄的人，十九岁参加了八路军。打仗赛猛虎，冲锋在头阵……” 80年前，这首歌在胶东大地的乡间村落和行军队伍中一次次唱响。80年后，南部战区陆军某旅“任常伦连”营区内，官兵们每日依旧高唱这首歌曲。歌声穿越时空，将一位抗日英雄的精神情怀，深深融入新时代官兵的血脉。 时光回溯至抗战岁月。任常伦17岁加入抗日自卫团，19岁参加八路军，经历大小战斗120余次，9次负伤仍冲锋在前，被山东军区授予“一等战斗英雄”称号。1944年11月，在胶东军民最后一次反“扫荡”的海阳长沙堡战斗中，时任副排长的任常伦浴血奋战，子弹打光后毅然与敌人拼刺刀。最终，在刺死5名敌人后，他不幸壮烈牺牲，年仅23岁。 英雄陨落，精神永存。长沙堡战斗后，任常伦生前所在连被命名为“任常伦连”。次年，他的战友谱写出歌曲《战斗英雄任常伦》。这首歌从此成为连队的连歌，传唱不息，激励着一代代官兵担当重任、奋勇争先。 连队现任排长洛古拉日，就是听着这首歌成长起来的英雄传人。2023年，他带队参加“南部·锋刃”狙击手集训，由于准备不充分，在比武中失利，令他既失落又愧疚。次年，再次听到集训比武消息时，洛古拉日一度踌躇不决。就在那时，营区响起了熟悉的旋律。“和英雄前辈相比，我遇到的这点挫折算什么？”洛古拉日猛然醒悟，再次请缨担任教官。集训期间，他和队员们满弓紧弦，全力以赴投入训练：枪管上垒弹壳，据枪一蹲就是3小时；负重25公斤跑完15公里，立刻趴下练呼吸控制；笔记本上，密密麻麻记满风速、湿度、弹道数据…… 考核那天，暴雨突至。洛古拉日毫不犹豫，带领队员冲进雨幕。屏息、瞄准、击发……最终，他们获得总评第一的优异成绩，赢得“金牌狙击小队”荣誉。 站在领奖台上，洛古拉日高声唱起连歌。战友们称赞他是“彝家山鹰”，他却说：“是英雄的赞歌，激励我飞过了最险的山峰。”
