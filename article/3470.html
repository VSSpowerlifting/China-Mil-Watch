Establishing and Practicing the Correct Outlook on Political Achievement | A Highland Garrison Training Site Now Has a Military-Civilian "Joint Clinic"

树立和践行正确政绩观丨高原驻训场有了军地“联合诊疗室”
MOD China (国防部) 24 August 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
An unidentified Army brigade conducting highland garrison training established a military-civilian 'Joint Clinic' (联合门诊) by coordinating with local hospitals that implement military medical support policies, embedding civilian specialists in internal medicine, orthopedics, and dentistry alongside military doctors for the full training period. The article documents a concrete gap in PLA organic medical capacity at high-altitude training sites: brigade-level medical assets cannot handle complex altitude illnesses, and system hospitals (体系医院) cannot sustain long-term personnel detachments, leaving units dependent on improvised civil-military arrangements. The framing around correcting the 'outlook on political achievement' (政绩观) points to institutional resistance — the brigade's own account treats the previous inaction as a leadership failure requiring ideological correction, not merely a resource problem.
Translation

An Army Brigade Transforms Its Concept of Serving the Grassroots to Improve the Quality and Effectiveness of Accompanying Support ——

A Highland Garrison Training Site Now Has a Military-Civilian "Joint Clinic"

"I never expected that even at a highland garrison training point, a sudden acute illness could be quickly treated by a specialist." Walking out of the military-civilian "Joint Clinic," Yang Shuai, a soldier of an Army brigade, had his face written with gratitude. A few days earlier, he had suffered a sudden onset of high-altitude pulmonary edema during training, and his condition was critical. A respiratory specialist providing accompanying support administered treatment immediately, carrying out high-altitude specialized nursing care, and brought Yang Shuai's condition rapidly under control.

This emergency treatment race against time reflects the changes brought about by the brigade party committee's conscious correction of deviations in its outlook on political achievement (政绩观) and its close alignment with actual combat conditions to improve the quality and effectiveness of accompanying support. In the past, when the brigade conducted highland garrison training, medical support relied mainly on the brigade's own organic medical forces, which often felt inadequate when confronted with certain complex high-altitude illnesses and training injuries. Meanwhile, the system hospitals (体系医院), burdened with their own heavy support missions, found it very difficult to detach backbone personnel for extended periods to provide accompanying support during highland garrison training.

"Insufficient existing capacity is absolutely not an excuse for inaction! We must proactively break through the impasse." At the party committee meeting, the brigade's leadership engaged in deep reflection, focusing discussion and analysis on the questions of "for whom political achievement is built, what kind of political achievement to build, and what to rely on to build political achievement." After research and deliberation, they decided to coordinate with nearby local hospitals that implement military medical support policies, organize backbone medical and nursing personnel, and establish a "Joint Clinic" together with the brigade to provide full-course accompanying support throughout the garrison training period.

Subsequently, the brigade's support department dispatched personnel to the local hospital to negotiate and coordinate backbone medical and nursing personnel from multiple departments to join the "Joint Clinic"; the transportation and delivery section developed transportation plans to ensure the rapid delivery of large oxygen-generating equipment and other supplies supported by the hospital; and the medical company organized military doctors to study alongside the specialists, improving their capacity to handle high-altitude acute conditions.

This reporter entered the brigade's garrison training camp to find a row of field prefabricated rooms (野战板房) marked with the words "Joint Clinic," standing out prominently. Oxygen generators, portable color ultrasound units, electrocardiographic monitors, and other equipment were all present and complete. Specialists in internal medicine, orthopedics, dentistry, and other fields from the local hospital sat in consultation and administered treatment alongside military doctors, ensuring that common high-altitude illnesses troubling officers and soldiers received timely and professional medical care. Staff Sergeant Second Class (二级上士) Chen Bo suffered from gingival recession; his condition had worsened due to the high-altitude environment, and if left untreated could lead to tooth loss. After being examined by a dental specialist at the clinic, he took the appropriate medication on schedule, and his symptoms were quickly relieved.

With the health of officers and soldiers guaranteed, the odds of battlefield victory increase. The brigade's leadership introduced that not long ago, a soldier from a neighboring unit suffered a sudden high-altitude acute illness, and by the time he was transferred to the "Joint Clinic," his condition was already extremely critical. Through timely diagnosis and treatment by specialists, combined with comprehensive pharmaceutical and equipment support, the soldier quickly moved from danger to safety. Data show that during this year's garrison training period, the incidence rates of high-altitude illness and training injuries among the brigade's officers and soldiers both declined significantly.

Original Chinese
陆军某旅转变服务基层理念提升伴随保障质效—— 高原驻训场有了军地“联合诊疗室” “没想到在高原驻训点，突发急症也能快速得到专家救治。”走出军地“联合诊疗室”，陆军某旅战士杨帅的脸上写满感激。前几天，他在训练中突发高原肺水肿，病情危急。伴随保障的呼吸科专家第一时间实施救治，进行高原特需护理，让杨帅的病情迅速得到控制。 这场与时间赛跑的紧急救治，折射出该旅党委自觉校正政绩观偏差、紧贴实战提升伴随保障质效带来的变化。以往该旅高原驻训，医疗保障主要靠旅本级卫生力量，面对部分复杂高原病和训练伤，常感力不从心。而体系医院因自身保障任务重，很难长期抽调骨干力量伴随保障高原驻训。 “现有力量不足，绝不是无所作为的借口！必须主动破局。”党委会上，该旅领导深刻反思，聚焦“政绩为谁而树、树什么样的政绩、靠什么树政绩”展开讨论辨析。经过调研论证，他们决定就近协调驻地执行军人医疗保障政策的地方医院，组织骨干医护力量，与该旅成立“联合诊疗室”，全程伴随保障驻训。 随后，该旅保障部派专人赴驻地医院磋商，协调多科室骨干医护力量加入“联合诊疗室”；运投科做好运输方案，保障医院支援的大型制氧机等设备快速投送；卫生连组织军医随专家跟学，提升高原急症处置能力。 笔者走进该旅驻训营地，只见一排标有“联合诊疗室”字样的野战板房格外醒目。制氧机、便携彩超仪、心电监护仪等一应俱全，来自地方医院的内科、骨科、牙科等专家与部队军医同台坐诊、协同施治，让困扰官兵的高原常见病得到及时专业诊疗。二级上士陈博患有牙龈萎缩，受高原环境影响病情有所加重，如不及时医治可能导致牙齿脱落，在诊疗室接受牙科专家检查后，他按时服用对症药物，症状很快得到缓解。 官兵健康有保障，战场打赢添胜算。该旅领导介绍，前不久，友邻单位一名战士突发高原急症，转运至“联合诊疗室”时，病情已十分危急。经过专家及时诊治，加之完备的药品、设备保障，这名战士很快转危为安。数据显示，今年驻训期间，该旅官兵高原病发病率、训练伤发生率均大幅下降。
