Only by "Breaking Down and Kneading Apart" the Reasoning Can You Speak It into the Hearts of New Comrades

只有把道理“掰开揉碎”，才能讲进新战友心坎里
PLA Daily (解放军报) 24 August 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
A political work article in PLA official media lays out a four-method framework for conducting ideological and political education (思想政治教育) among new recruits drawn primarily from the post-2000 and post-2005 cohorts: distilling doctrine into pithy formulations (短实新), grounding reasoning in concrete scenarios, targeting specific personal problems rather than generic themes, and leading by behavioral example. The article is standard political work guidance aimed at grassroots unit leaders during the recruit intake period; its value is as a record of how the PLA currently frames the challenge of ideological penetration among younger enlistees who are described as intellectually active but prone to "confusion and fluctuation." The framing points to institutional concern that surface-level compliance—knowing what without knowing why—is insufficient to produce the internalized commitment the PLA considers a prerequisite for combat effectiveness.
Translation

Enlisting in the military is the starting point of a person's military career, and even more so a critical stage for shaping thought and laying the foundation of belief. At present, the new recruits—composed mainly of those born after 2000 and after 2005—are full of vitality, intellectually active, broad in outlook, and bold in forging ahead. They are an important force in building the combat effectiveness of the troops. But at the same time, confronted with entirely new mission responsibilities, disciplinary requirements, and ways of life, their thinking is prone to confusion and fluctuation, making it all the more necessary to conduct ideological and political education well. This not only concerns individual growth but also bears directly on unit stability and the generation of combat effectiveness. Practice has repeatedly proven: the root cause of many problems in managing new recruits lies in their thinking, and the effectiveness of ideological and political education work depends on whether it enters the mind and the heart. If education merely skims the surface—knowing what without knowing why—then over time, new comrades will find it difficult to act consciously, let alone achieve genuine identification and internalization. Only by "breaking down and kneading apart" the reasoning, expressing it in language they can absorb and remember, and corroborating it with real and tangible stories, can the reasoning be spoken into the hearts of new comrades—resolving their confusion, steadying their minds, and settling their spirits—thereby solidly building the ideological foundation of an unwavering commitment to strengthening the military.

Make good use of concise summation so that reasoning is "short, solid, and fresh" (短实新) and enters the ears and the mind. Summarizing and distilling is an important method for clarifying complex problems and systematically deepening scattered understanding. Only by being good at summarizing can one transform weighty reasoning into a cognitive guide that soldiers are willing to hear, can understand, and can use. Grassroots troop leaders should transform profound theory into vivid language, making weighty reasoning feel light. For example, when speaking of the importance of organization, one might say: "Soldiers scattered a thousand li never lose the thread—holding fast to the organization is the key"; when speaking of generating combat effectiveness, emphasize: "Being a soldier means fighting, and fighting means being able to win"; when speaking of striving and pressing forward, quote: "Struggle for your dreams, fight hard for your happiness"… Some of these formulations originate from classic documents, some are drawn from folk proverbs, and some come from actual combat experience. Though brief and pithy, they carry deep meaning, and new recruits can remember them the moment they hear them. This mode of expression lowers the cognitive threshold, enhances the effectiveness of transmission, and allows reasoning to dissolve like salt in water—present in flavor but leaving no trace—helping to achieve the transformation from "entering the ears" to "entering the heart."

Integrate closely with practice so that reasoning "gets dirt on it" (沾泥土) and can be felt and understood. The vitality of reasoning lies in its practical character. Education divorced from reality is like a tree without roots or water without a source—it is difficult to truly move people. For example, many new recruits at the beginning of their enlistment have a shallow understanding of the significance of "emergency assembly," viewing it simply as the mechanical execution of orders. In response, one can organize discussion and deduction around "the value of one minute": if an enemy escapes for one minute, a danger spreads for one minute, or a combat opportunity is delayed for one minute, what are the consequences? Through this kind of concrete situational immersion, new recruits are helped to grasp the profound meaning of emergency assembly and to treat every assembly as a real combat sortie. Reasoning is like a seed: only when sown in the fertile soil of practice can it take root, sprout, blossom, and bear fruit. Grassroots troop leaders should closely combine the fundamental principles of conducting oneself, serving in the military, and repaying the nation with daily work and specific affairs, so that reasoning has a living source of water, thereby avoiding the "two-skin" phenomenon (两张皮) in which theory and practice are separated.

Adhere to a problem-oriented approach so that reasoning "strikes the heart" (戳心坎) and resolves doubts. Ideological and political education work must not evade the real and grasp at the empty, or beat around the bush. It must prescribe the right remedy for the illness, letting reasoning strike directly at the pain point. Years ago, when this author was leading troops, a new recruit confided that he wanted to leave the military, solely because his younger brother at home was unruly and his parents were unable to restrain him. At the time, this author did not speak empty words but instead helped him analyze the pros and cons of his specific situation: even if he rushed home, he might not necessarily solve the problem, and might instead provoke new conflicts through impulsive action; if he achieved results in the unit and won commendations, his own growth and progress could serve as a vivid example for his younger brother. The new recruit was deeply moved upon hearing this, trained with greater enthusiasm from that point on, often used his own growth experience to counsel his younger brother, and family relations gradually eased. Looking back afterward, the reason this instance of education was effective was precisely because it was aimed at the specific problem. Had it merely skirted the issue and scratched the surface, no amount of words would have been anything more than a fist striking cotton. Therefore, in ideological and political education, only by confronting problems directly and prescribing the right remedy can reasoning be delivered fully and get close to the heart, stirring ripples in the minds of officers and soldiers and winning their heartfelt conviction.

Emphasize behavioral demonstration so that reasoning "is seen in action" (见行动) and leads and inspires. Beyond verbal instruction, teaching by example is even more essential. Action is the most powerful language; a role model is the most vivid form of reasoning. Sometimes a troop leader need not say much—conducting oneself uprightly and standing tall is itself a form of silent, leading demonstration. Years ago, a colleague of this author participated fully in training throughout the entire new recruit training period, investing wholeheartedly, completing every new training task assigned by superiors without exception, and even persisting through injury. This produced a strong motivating effect among the new recruits. The recruits were deeply inspired and consciously intensified their training one after another, thereby forming a healthy atmosphere of comparing, learning, catching up, and surpassing, with training results improving markedly. The ancients said: "When he himself is upright, things get done without orders; when he himself is not upright, orders are not followed." Every word and action of a troop leader is itself the most vivid teaching material. Therefore, grassroots troop leaders must make good use of behavioral demonstration to convey reasoning—daring to take the lead and stand at the front in the face of urgent, difficult, dangerous, and arduous tasks; setting an example and serving as a model in observing rules and maintaining discipline; taking the initiative and acting first in training tasks—thereby establishing a standard and firmly setting the orientation, and by so doing motivating new recruits to shoulder heavy responsibilities and strive to be pioneers.

Original Chinese
参军入伍，是一个人军旅生涯的起点，更是思想塑形、信仰奠基的关键阶段。当前，以00后、05后为主体的新兵朝气蓬勃、思想活跃、视野开阔、敢闯敢拼，是部队战斗力建设的重要力量。但与此同时，面对全新的使命责任、纪律要求、生活方式，他们的思想也易生困惑和波动，尤须抓好思想政治教育。这不仅关系到个体成长，更关乎部队稳定与战斗力生成。实践反复证明：许多新兵管理问题的根源在思想，而思想政治教育工作的成效在入脑入心。如果教育仅浮于表面，知其然而不知其所以然，长此以往，新战友行动上就难以自觉，更谈不上真正的认同与内化转化。只有把道理“掰开揉碎”，用他们听得进、记得住的语言讲出来，用真实可感的故事加以佐证，才能把道理讲进新战友心坎里，在解其惑、稳其心、定其神中筑牢矢志强军的思想根基。 善用凝练总结，让道理“短实新”、入耳入脑。总结提炼，是将复杂问题梳理清晰、零散认识系统深化的重要方法。善于总结，才能把厚重的道理转化为战士愿听、能懂、可用的认知指南。基层带兵人应将深邃的理论转化为鲜活的语言，让厚重的道理变得轻盈。比如，谈及组织重要性时，可以说“兵撒千里不断线，扭住组织是关键”；谈及战斗力生成时，强调“当兵就要打仗，打仗就要能赢”；谈及奋进拼搏，引用“为梦想奋斗、为幸福打拼”……这些表述，有的源自经典文献，有的取自民间谚语，有的来自实战经验，虽短小精悍，却意蕴深远，新兵们一听就能记住。这种表达方式，降低了认知门槛，提升了传播效能，使道理如盐在水、有味无痕，有助于实现从“入耳”到“入心”的转变。 紧密融入实践，让道理“沾泥土”、可感可悟。道理的生命力，在于其实践品格。脱离实际的教育如无根之木、无源之水，难以真正打动人心。例如，许多新兵在入伍之初都曾对“紧急集合”的意义理解不深，简单视作机械执行命令。对此，可以围绕“一分钟的价值”组织讨论推演：如果敌人脱逃一分钟、险情蔓延一分钟、战机延误一分钟，会有什么后果？通过这种具象化的情景代入，帮助新兵读懂紧急集合的深刻内涵，将每一次集合都视为实战出动。道理如同种子，唯有播撒在实践沃土，方能生根发芽、开花结果。基层带兵人应把立身为人、从军报国的基本道理与日常工作、具体事务紧密结合，让道理拥有源头活水，从而避免理论与实践脱离的“两张皮”现象。 坚持问题导向，让道理“戳心坎”、解疑释惑。思想政治教育工作不能避实就虚、顾左右而言他，必须对症下药，让道理直击痛点。当年，笔者在带兵时，曾有一名新兵吐露想退伍，只因弟弟在家不服管教、父母无力约束。笔者当时没有空讲大话，而是帮他分析具体情况利弊：即使仓促回家也未必能解决问题，反而可能因冲动酿成新的矛盾；如果在部队干出成绩、立功受奖，自身的成长进步恰恰能够给弟弟树立鲜活榜样。这名新兵听后深受触动，从此训练更加积极，还常以自身成长经历劝导弟弟，家庭关系也逐渐缓和。事后复盘，这次教育之所以有效，正是因为瞄准了具体问题，如果当时仅仅避重就轻、隔靴搔痒，说再多也只是拳头打在棉花上。因此，在思想政治教育中，只有直面问题、对症下药，道理才能讲到位、贴近心，才能在官兵心田中激起涟漪，让人心悦诚服。 突出行为示范，让道理“见行动”、引领感召。言传之外，更需身教。行动是最有力的语言，榜样是最鲜活的道理。有时带兵人不必多言，自身做得正、站得直，就是一种无声的带动示范。当年，笔者的一位同事在新训期间全程跟训、倾情投入，面对上级部署的新训任务一项不落，甚至带伤坚持，在新兵中产生了很好的带动作用。新兵们深受鼓舞，纷纷自觉加强训练，由此形成了比学赶超的良好氛围，训练成效显著提升。古人云：“其身正，不令而行；其身不正，虽令不从。”带兵人的一言一行，本身就是最生动的教材。因此，基层带兵人要善用行为示范来讲道理，在急难险重任务面前敢打头阵站排头，在遵规守纪方面立身为旗作表率，在训练任务中以身作则干在先，立起标杆、树牢导向，由此带动新兵们勇挑重担、争当先锋。
