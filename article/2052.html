Strengthen the Capacity of Grassroots Party Organizations for Independent Development and Construction

增强基层党组织自主抓建能力
PLA Daily (解放军报) 3 July 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
Writing under their names in PLA media, Zou Yonghang and Wang Qian lay out a doctrinal framework for strengthening grassroots Party organization capacity for independent development, citing Xi Jinping's directive at the Fourth Session of the 14th NPC delegation meeting as the authoritative prompt, and centering the argument on three lines of effort: political guidance, combat readiness, and regulation-based governance. The article documents a persistent institutional problem the PLA has named explicitly — that routine dispersed deployment (常态动散条件) degrades the Party committee's ability to supervise and develop subordinate units, creating governance gaps that erode discipline, ideological cohesion, and combat readiness at the unit level. The emphasis on eliminating 'local policies' and 'local rules,' cutting 'stubborn chronic ailments,' and preventing officers and soldiers from being pulled away from primary missions records the PLA's ongoing effort to reassert centralized Party control over grassroots units whose physical dispersion has outpaced the institutional mechanisms designed to manage them.
Translation

Strengthen the Capacity of Grassroots Party Organizations for Independent Development and Construction

■ Zou Yonghang, Wang Qian

Grassroots Party organizations are the strong fighting fortresses (战斗堡垒) that unite and consolidate the troops and accomplish all tasks. When attending the plenary session of the PLA and People's Armed Police Forces delegation to the Fourth Session of the 14th National People's Congress, Chairman Xi emphasized the need to "strengthen the capacity of grassroots Party organizations for independent development and construction (增强基层党组织自主抓建能力)." Under the new situation, forces are diverse in type and varied in mission. Particularly under conditions of routine dispersed deployment (常态动散条件), grassroots development faces many new circumstances and new problems. There is an urgent need to build the frontline fighting fortresses stronger, to drive new improvements in grassroots development through newly strengthened independent development and construction capacity of grassroots Party organizations, and to ensure the Party's banner always flies high at the frontlines of mission execution.

Emphasize political guidance. Our military is a people's army under the absolute leadership of the Party; for grassroots units to be strong, political strength must come first. At present, the world situation, national conditions, Party conditions, and military conditions are all undergoing complex and profound changes, and the political tests our military faces are intricate and complicated. Strengthening the capacity of grassroots Party organizations for independent development and construction requires always placing political development in the foremost position—genuinely establishing correct political orientation, grasping political tempering, and tightening political discipline—and implementing the requirements of building the military through political means (政治建军) into every aspect and the entire process of grassroots development, ensuring that the Party's absolute leadership over the military reaches directly to the grassroots and directly to officers and soldiers. In the work of correcting the ideological bearing of officers and soldiers, cultivate deeply and work meticulously: closely attuned to the new ideological changes and new cognitive characteristics of officers and soldiers in the new era, persistently use Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era to forge unity of mind and temper the soul, thoroughly implement Xi Jinping's Thought on Strengthening the Military (习近平强军思想), and continuously use the sunlight of truth to dispel confusion, genuine emotion to nourish hearts, and sincere faith and steadfast action to inspire and call forth, so as to solidly lay the ideological foundation for officers and soldiers to heed the Party and follow the Party. In the work of setting the correct direction for development objectives, apply precise effort: be adept at planning grassroots development from the overall perspective of the cause of military strengthening, focus on precisely translating the "three-hardness" (三个过硬) standards into a "construction blueprint" for independent development and construction, effectively integrating the "four-haves" (四有) standards into the "roadmap" for the growth of officers and soldiers, strengthen goal guidance and process forging, and drive the Party's goal of military strengthening in the new era to take root at the grassroots. In the work of establishing the correct governance orientation and rectifying discipline, apply comprehensive measures: resolutely cut into the stubborn chronic ailments (顽瘴痼疾) that erode the purity and honor of the troops, and draw the sword against the unhealthy tendencies that pollute the political ecosystem; persist in inspiring officers and soldiers with fine traditions and advanced models, and in warning hearts and guiding conduct with red-line boundaries and cases of disciplinary violations; ensure that consolidating the foundation and purifying the source are unified, and that upholding the upright and eliminating the deviant are grasped with both hands, so as to ensure that grassroots units maintain strict discipline, a clean ecosystem, and an atmosphere full of integrity.

Focus on overcoming difficulties and winning battles. Whether a fighting fortress is strong or not ultimately depends on whether combat effectiveness is solid. Grassroots units are at the frontline of training and war preparation and at the forefront of military struggle preparation. Strengthening the capacity of grassroots Party organizations for independent development and construction requires firmly grasping combat effectiveness as the sole and fundamental standard, focusing all work on being capable of fighting and winning battles, and forging a sharp-edged vanguard (尖刀利刃) that dares to fight and is certain to win. Attention and energy must be concentrated on the primary mission of war preparation and combat: focus the planning starting point, the emphasis of effort, and the practical landing point of all grassroots work toward war and for war; use rigid measures to rectify the empty spinning and wasteful consumption that interferes with primary responsibilities and primary missions, so that officers and soldiers can concentrate more fully on planning for and studying warfare. Combat preparation must always be aimed at genuine fighting and real readiness: truly open up every link and activate every node in the emergency response and combat chain, ensuring that the troops always maintain a state of readiness with arrows nocked and drawn. Tempering troops through rigorous training must be oriented toward forging the hard skills needed to win: closely follow the development trends of warfare to analyze mechanisms, closely align with actual combat requirements to refine military skills and practice for battle, maximally excavate the combat potential of every officer and soldier and the operational potential of every piece of equipment, and form the hard-core strength to come when called, fight upon arrival, and win every battle.

Strictly develop and build in accordance with regulations (按纲抓建). Chairman Xi has pointed out the need to strengthen grassroots Party organization development, enhance the capacity to develop and build in accordance with regulations and to lead officers and soldiers in executing missions, and consolidate the foundation of force development and combat effectiveness. Developing and building in accordance with regulations is an important handle for improving the level of grassroots governance. Practice has proven that for grassroots development to have a stable base, a solid foundation, strong resilience, and good progress, it is inseparable from grassroots Party organizations earnestly developing and building in accordance with regulations and building themselves in accordance with law. Emphasize "threading the needle" (穿针引线) in coordinating the overall effort: precisely categorize and integrate various tasks, scientifically handle the relationships between large and small, primary and secondary, urgent and deferred, prevent "grabbing everything at once" and "fighting a confused battle," and truly use the "needle" of laws and regulations to thread the "thousand threads" of grassroots affairs. Persist in "dripping water wears through stone" (滴水穿石) in grasping the long-term effort: sediment the habit of grasping the routine and laying the foundation into habit and internalize it as conscious action; persist in managing according to regulations, building according to outlines, and training according to syllabi, one drop at a time, accumulating bit by bit; thoroughly eliminate all "local policies" (土政策) and "local rules" (土规定) that interfere with normal order, so that personnel at all levels cultivate the behavioral habit of handling affairs according to systems, according to rules, and according to responsibilities. Solidly walk the mass line: educate and guide officers and soldiers to firmly shoulder responsibility and tightly grasp it in hand, drive all levels to work together to grasp development, and mobilize all personnel comprehensively and with full effort to build, so that collective wisdom and effort drive comprehensive progress and across-the-board strength in grassroots development.

增强基层党组织自主抓建能力 ■邹咏航 王 倩 基层党组织是团结巩固部队、完成各项任务的坚强战斗堡垒。习主席在出席十四届全国人大四次会议解放军和武警部队代表团全体会议时强调，“增强基层党组织自主抓建能力”。新形势下，部队类型多样、担负任务多元，特别是常态动散条件下，基层建设面临许多新情况新问题，迫切需要将一线战斗堡垒建设得更加坚强，以基层党组织自主抓建能力新加强带动基层建设新提升，让党的旗帜始终高扬在任务一线。 突出政治引领。我军是党绝对领导下的人民军队，基层过硬，首先是政治上要过硬。当前，世情、国情、党情、军情都在发生复杂深刻变化，我军在政治上面临的考验错综复杂。增强基层党组织自主抓建能力，必须始终把政治建设摆在首位，真正把政治导向立起来、政治历练抓起来、政治规矩严起来，把政治建军要求落实到基层建设各方面和全过程，确保党对军队绝对领导直达基层、直达官兵。在导正官兵思想航向上深耕细作，紧扣新时代官兵思想新变化、认知新特点，坚持不懈用习近平新时代中国特色社会主义思想凝心铸魂，深入贯彻习近平强军思想，持续用真理阳光廓清迷雾、真情实感浸润心田、真信笃行示范感召，打牢官兵听党话、跟党走的思想根基。在把正建设目标指向上精准发力，善于立足强军事业全局谋划基层建设发展，注重将“三个过硬”标准精准转化为自主抓建的“施工图”，把“四有”标准有效融入官兵成长的“路线图”，强化目标引领和过程锻造，推动党在新时代的强军目标在基层落地生根。在树正整肃治理风向上综合施策，坚决向侵蚀部队纯洁光荣的顽瘴痼疾开刀、对污染政治生态的不良风气亮剑，坚持用优良传统、先进典型感召官兵，用红线边界、违纪案例警心策行，做到固本与清源相统一、扶正与祛邪两手抓，确保基层纲纪严明、生态清朗、正气充盈。 锚定攻坚打赢。战斗堡垒强不强，关键是看战斗力是否过硬。基层部队处于练兵备战第一线、军事斗争准备最前沿，增强基层党组织自主抓建能力，必须紧紧扭住战斗力这个唯一的根本的标准，一切工作向能打仗、打胜仗聚焦用力，带出敢打必胜、克敌制胜的尖刀利刃。心思精力要聚焦备战打仗主业，把基层各项工作的谋划起点、着力重点、实践落点聚焦向战为战，以刚性举措整治干扰主责主业的空转虚耗，让官兵更加聚精会神谋战研战。作战准备要始终瞄准真打实备，切实将应急应战链路上各个环节打通、每个节点激活，确保部队时刻保持箭在弦上、引而待发的备战状态。砺兵强训要着眼锤炼胜战硬功，紧跟战争发展趋势研析机理、紧贴实战需求精武练战，最大限度挖掘每名官兵的战斗潜能、每个装备的作战潜力，形成召之即来、来之能战、战之必胜的硬核实力。 严格按纲抓建。习主席指出，要加强基层党组织建设，增强按纲抓建和带领官兵遂行任务能力，夯实部队建设和战斗力基础。按纲抓建是提升基层治理水平的重要抓手。实践证明，基层建设要想底盘稳、基础牢，韧性强、发展好，离不开基层党组织扎扎实实按纲抓建、依法自建。注重“穿针引线”统筹抓，精准分类整合各项任务，科学处理大与小、主与次、急与缓等关系，防止“眉毛胡子一把抓”和“打乱仗”，真正用法规制度这根“针”，穿好基层事务“千条线”。坚持“滴水穿石”长线抓，把抓经常打基础沉淀为习惯、内化成自觉，坚持一点一滴、铢积寸累地按条令管、按纲要建、按大纲训，彻底清除各类干扰正常秩序的“土政策”“土规定”，使各级养成按制度办事、按规矩办事、按职责办事的行为习惯。扎实走好群众路线，教育引导官兵把责任牢牢扛在肩上、紧紧抓在手里，推动上下齐心动手抓、全员全面全力建，群策群力推动基层建设全面进步、全面过硬。
