Revelations from Wang Ji's Advanced Deeds | Only Love Can Drive One to the Ends of the Earth

王戟先进事迹启示录丨唯有热爱，可赴山海
PLA Daily (解放军报) 7 August 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
PLA political work media profiles Wang Ji, a wheelchair-bound researcher at the National University of Defense Technology's 'Galaxy' (银河) supercomputing program, framing his decades of work on high-assurance software (高可信软件) through dialysis and disability as the model of Party loyalty for the current force-building era. The piece is standard political education content — its value is as a record of how PLA institutions construct the 'Model of the Era' (时代楷模) exemplar to reinforce loyalty as behavioral practice rather than declarative allegiance, aimed at junior officers and cadres. The repeated invocation of Xi Jinping and the closing call to establish 'high-assurance' loyalty documents the current campaign's framing but does not indicate a new policy line.
Translation

Only Love Can Drive One to the Ends of the Earth

— Interpreting the Loyal Character of "Model of the Era" Wang Ji

■ Zhang Di, Yang Heng

"A person's greatest happiness is nothing more than having one's entire life bound together with one's country."

Savoring this personal reflection from Wang Ji, we cannot help but ask many searching questions of the soul: Why is Wang Ji's steadfast commitment to and practice of loyalty as clear and pure as the smile on his face — free of even a trace of self-interest? As resolute and tenacious as his charge forward from a wheelchair — undaunted by any storm or trial? And as enduring and lasting as the cause he so deeply loves — never wavering from the course set by his lifelong original aspiration (初心)?

Loyalty to the Party begins with love and is rooted in faith. Wang Ji often said: "Putting on the uniform is a lifelong matter — once you put it on, it is hard to take off." This love, etched into his very bones, runs through the entire arc of his life. Pursuing his dream of national defense, studying and joining the military, taking root in the fertile soil of "Galaxy" (银河) and cultivating the front lines of scientific research — he regarded wearing the uniform as a lifelong honor and the cause of strengthening the military as his life's pursuit. In Wang Ji's heart, the uniform is the mark of faith, and scientific research is the battlefield for serving the nation. Through decades of unceasing effort, he proved that loyalty is not a momentary impulse, but a firm conviction and unwavering pursuit — once in uniform, forever pledged to the Party and the nation.

Wang Ji's loyalty was never a slogan. It was a constant remembrance throughout the long years of military service, and an unwavering constancy even after injury and disability plunged him into his darkest hour. An old professor who knew and worked alongside Wang Ji for more than forty years said: "Because of his original aspiration, he always remembered the road he came from; because of his steadfastness, he was able to firmly 'stand up' after falling down; because of his persistence, he held on longer than others. In Wang Ji, he turned faith into daily life." Wang Ji's loyalty is the focused concentration with which he raced to read papers between dialysis sessions; the enthusiasm with which he revised his students' papers word by word from a hospital bed; the rushing about to catch the earliest flight at five in the morning for a business trip… Countless netizens have reflected that while legs can hold a body upright, loyalty and faith can keep a person's spiritual backbone straight. Wang Ji's deeds instruct us: the loyalty of Party members and cadres is measured not only by what they say, but even more by what they do; not only by how they press forward in favorable circumstances, but even more by whether they can remain steadfast in adversity. The small contributions of ordinary daily life, the quiet, day-after-day cultivation — this is the most plain and most pure form of loyalty.

Thirty-five years ago, Wang Ji wrote in his application to join the Party: "Only by linking one's entire life to the Party's cause, to the nation's future, and to the people's interests can one's life be more fulfilling and more meaningful." Faced with national strategic requirements and national defense security needs, Wang Ji plunged headlong into the niche track of high-assurance software (高可信软件) research, using his wheelchair as a battle vehicle and code as a forged shield, running a breathtaking acceleration on the journey of strengthening the military through science and technology. "As long as my mind works and my hands can still be used, I will not stop." His actions confirm that loyalty depends on the sustained resolve of long-term effort (久久为功); only by merging personal ideals into the needs of the Party and the nation, and watering one's original aspiration with a lifetime of love, can one transcend the storms of the years and fulfill the mission of strengthening the military.

An original aspiration is easy to hold at the start but hard to keep to the end; a mission is easy to understand but arduous to carry through to the distance. Chairman Xi has profoundly pointed out: "Our Party has come a long way, experiencing countless dangers and hardships, yet no difficulty has crushed us and no enemy has defeated us — this is because of the loyalty of tens of millions of Party members." Loyalty to the Party is the foremost political quality of a Communist, and the fundamental cornerstone upon which a revolutionary soldier stands and performs his duties. Disease took Wang Ji's legs, but it could not take his faith in charging toward the heights of scientific research. Wang Ji has, through his actions, erected for us a benchmark of the era for tempering political character and upholding the true colors of loyalty.

On the journey of building a strong nation and a strong military, why are there always those willing to do what others are unwilling to do, and daring to do what others dare not do? Only because their love for this land and for this military runs deep. For them, undertaking work and building a cause is both contribution and dedication, and also love and belonging. Where the will tends, no distance is too far; only love can drive one to the ends of the earth (志之所趋，无远弗届；唯有热爱，可赴山海). We should, like Wang Ji, establish a "high-assurance" (高可信) loyalty and faith, shoulder our mission and responsibility, and ensure that when the Party has a need, we answer the call; when the nation has an expectation, we must act; when the military has a hope, I will move forward.

Original Chinese
唯有热爱，可赴山海 ——解读“时代楷模”王戟的忠诚品格 ■张 迪 阳 恒 “一个人最大的幸福，莫过于他的一生，是和他的国家绑定在一起的。” 品味王戟这句人生感言，我们不由生发许多灵魂拷问：为何王戟对忠诚的坚守与践行，就如同他脸上的笑容一般澄澈纯粹，不含一丝功利杂质？如同他轮椅上的冲锋一般笃定坚韧，不惧一切风雨洗礼？又如同他深爱的事业一般恒久绵长，不改一生初心航向？ 对党忠诚始于热爱、源于信仰。王戟常说：“穿上军装是一辈子的事情，穿上了就难以脱下。”这份刻入骨髓的热爱，贯穿他整个人生轨迹。逐梦国防、求学从军，扎根“银河”沃土、深耕科研一线，他把身着军装当作终身荣光，把强军事业当作毕生追求。在王戟心中，军装是信仰的标识，科研是报国的战场。他用数十年不辍攻关证明，忠诚不是一时的心血来潮，而是一朝戎装在身、终生许党报国的坚定信念与无悔追求。 王戟的忠诚，从来不是口号，而是漫长军旅岁月里的念念不忘、因伤残跌入至暗时刻后的始终如一。与王戟相识共事40余年的老教授说：“因为初心，所以始终记得来时的路；因为坚守，所以倒下后能坚定‘站’起来；因为执着，所以撑得比别人更久。在王戟身上，他把信仰活成了日常。”王戟的忠诚，是透析间隙争分夺秒读论文的专注，是病床上逐字逐句修改学生论文的热情，是凌晨五点赶最早航班出差的奔波……无数网友感慨，双腿能支撑起身形的挺立，而忠诚与信仰则能让一个人挺直精神脊梁。王戟的事迹启示我们：党员干部的忠诚，不仅看如何说，更要看如何做；不仅看顺境中如何奔赴，更要看逆境中能否坚定。平凡日常的点滴付出、日复一日的默默耕耘，就是最质朴、最纯粹的忠诚。 35年前，王戟在入党志愿书中这样写道：“只有把自己的一生同党的事业，同国家的前途、人民的利益联系起来，才能更加充实、更有意义。”面对国家战略需求和国防安全需要，王戟一头扎进高可信软件研究这一冷门赛道，以轮椅作战车、以代码铸坚盾，在科技强军的征途上跑出了震撼人心的加速度。“只要我的脑子好使，手还能用，我就不会停下来。”他用行动印证，忠诚靠的是久久为功的定力，只有把个人理想融入党和国家需要，以毕生热爱浇灌初心，方能跨越岁月风雨、践行强军使命。 初心易得，始终难守；使命易晓，致远惟艰。习主席深刻指出：“我们党一路走来，经历了无数艰险和磨难，但任何困难都没有压垮我们，任何敌人都没能打倒我们，靠的就是千千万万党员的忠诚。”对党忠诚，是共产党人首要的政治品质，是革命军人立身履职的根本基石。病魔夺走了王戟的双腿，却夺不走他冲锋科研高地的信仰。王戟用行动为我们立起了淬炼政治品格、坚守忠诚本色的时代标杆。 在强国强军的征程上，为什么总有人愿做他人不愿做、敢为他人不敢为？只因对这片土地、对这支军队爱得深沉。于他们而言，干事创业是付出与奉献，更是热爱与归属。志之所趋，无远弗届；唯有热爱，可赴山海。我们应像王戟一样，树立起“高可信”的忠诚与信仰，扛起使命担当，做到党有所需、召之即来，国有所期、必有所为，军有所盼、我必前行。
