From "Commander" to "Old Xu": General Xu Shiyou Joins a Company as a Common Soldier to Shed His "Official Airs"

从“首长”到“老许”， 将军许世友下连当兵打掉“官架子”
PLA Daily (解放军报) 23 May 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
In October 1958, General Xu Shiyou — then a senior commander — spent thirty days as a private first class in the 7th Squad, 6th Infantry Company at a coastal defense frontline position, eating, drilling, and standing guard alongside enlisted men as part of Mao Zedong's directive that all military cadres serve one month annually as common soldiers. The article is a commemorative profile drawn from the Chinese People's Revolution Military Museum's holdings; its value is as a record of how PLA political education frames the officer-soldier relationship and the ideal of 去官气 (shedding official airs) for contemporary junior personnel, not as evidence of any current policy or institutional change.
Translation

The Chinese People's Revolution Military Museum holds a set of military uniforms worn and used by General Xu Shiyou — the clothing, equipment, and kit indistinguishable in every respect from those of an ordinary soldier of the time. Why would a senior cadre put on the uniform of a private first class? The story behind this must be traced back to 1958.

The military uniform, straw sandals, and shoulder bag Xu Shiyou used when he went down to the company.

In August 1958, Comrade Mao Zedong advocated that "all military cadres go down and serve as soldiers for one month each year" — training and living alongside the enlisted men. Xu Shiyou responded actively, becoming one of the first generals to voluntarily sign up for the program.

Xu Shiyou.

On October 17 of that same year, Xu Shiyou — wearing a forage cap and soldier's uniform — arrived at the 7th Squad of the 6th Infantry Company at a coastal defense frontline position, where he served as a "private first class."

On the first day at mealtime, the squad leader and soldiers competed to ladle water and dish out food for Xu Shiyou. Xu Shiyou declined, saying: "Comrade Squad Leader, I am serving as a soldier in your squad — you are my superior. You must manage and correct me boldly. Don't stand on ceremony. You must let me shed my official airs."

A photograph taken of Xu Shiyou during his time with the company.

The company conducted cliff-climbing training. Seizing a moment when no one was paying attention, Xu Shiyou grabbed a rope and began to climb as well. He ultimately reached the top successfully. At the squad affairs meeting that evening, Squad Leader Zhang Jisheng criticized Xu Shiyou, saying: "You are getting on in years — you should rest." Xu Shiyou laughed and replied: "If I wanted to rest, why would I come to serve as a soldier? I came to serve as a soldier precisely to eat, live, and drill together with everyone. I will not be a special-case soldier."

On another occasion, Xu Shiyou was hoeing weeds and sweeping up rubbish together with the soldiers in his squad. A cadre holding a camera stood to one side and asked Xu Shiyou to stop for a moment to have his photograph taken. Xu Shiyou did not stop. He kept sweeping as he said: "The old commune members in the people's communes, fifty or sixty years old, work from morning to night, and no one takes their photograph. We do a little bit of work, and someone wants to take a photograph — I think we'd better not."

A photograph taken of Xu Shiyou during his time with the company.

During breaks in training, the soldiers learned that Xu Shiyou had practiced martial arts in his youth and had a deep foundation in the martial arts. They unanimously asked him to give a demonstration. Xu Shiyou stood up, clasped his fists together, and launched into a performance with fierce, vigorous energy. Many soldiers watched and then pestered him to teach them kung fu. In the end, every soldier in the entire company became his "disciple." The "bench calisthenics" (板凳操) he refined became a fitness activity that swept through the company for a time.

He also used spare moments to have heart-to-heart talks with the soldiers. The men in the squad had initially been somewhat reserved, but seeing how sincere and approachable he was, they quickly set aside their misgivings. At first the soldiers addressed him as "Commander" (首长); later they called him "Old Comrade Xu" (老许同志); and finally they simply called him "Old Xu" (老许). From this shift in forms of address, Xu Shiyou knew he had become one of the soldiers.

At night, Xu Shiyou stood guard together with the soldiers.

In the blink of an eye, thirty days had passed. Xu Shiyou had come to understand the soldiers' living conditions, training, and state of mind, and had forged a deep bond with them. When the time came to leave the company, neither side could bear to part. Squad Leader Zhang Jisheng, on behalf of the squad, prepared a detailed assessment of Xu Shiyou's performance. Xu Shiyou was named an honorary soldier of the company.

General Xu Shiyou, at the age of destiny (天命之年), once again became a soldier. Strict with himself and yet able to become one with the enlisted men, he demonstrated through his actions that true political achievement (政绩) lies not in how high the rank on one's shoulders, but in how much mud is on one's boots — and in whether one has truly won the wholehearted support of soldiers and the people.

(Sources: Chinese People's Revolution Military Museum, Xinhua News Agency, People's Daily Online)

中国人民革命军事博物馆收藏了一套 许世友将军穿戴使用过的上等兵军服 衣物、装具都与当时一名普通士兵别无二致 为什么一名高级干部会穿上等兵的军装？ 这背后的故事得追溯到1958年 许世友穿过的军装、草鞋和下连队使用的挎包。 1958年8月 毛泽东同志倡导 “所有军队干部每年下去当一个月兵” 与士兵们一同训练、生活 许世友积极响应 成为首批主动报名参加此活动的将军之一 许世友。 同年10月17日 许世友头戴船形帽 身穿士兵服 来到海防前线某步兵六连七班 当了一名“上等兵” 第一天吃饭时 班长和战士争着替许世友舀水盛饭 许世友谢绝道： “班长同志， 我在你们班当兵， 你就是我的上级， 就要大胆管教我， 不要太客气了， 要让我拿下官架子。” 许世友下连队期间的留影。 连队进行攀登绝壁训练 趁大家不注意 许世友也抓起绳子攀登起来 最终他顺利登顶 晚上的班务会上 班长张吉生批评了许世友，说： “你年纪大， 应该休息休息。” 许世友笑答： “要休息何必来当兵？ 我来当兵就是要和大家同吃同住同操练， 我不当特殊兵。” 还有一次 许世友和班里的战士一起锄草、扫垃圾 有个干部拿着照相机 在一旁请求许世友停下来照个相 许世友没有停 他边扫边说： “人民公社五六十岁的老社员 一天干到晚， 也没有人给他照相。 我们干了一点活， 就要照相， 我看还是不照。” 许世友下连队期间的留影。 训练间隙 知道许世友年轻时习过武 武术功底深厚 战士们就一致要求他表演武术 许世友站起来双拳一抱 就虎虎生风地打了起来 许多战士看后缠着他学功夫 最后全连战士都成了他的“徒弟” 被他改进的“板凳操” 成了连队里风靡一时的运动项目 他还利用空隙时间和战士们谈心 班里的战士原来还有点拘束 但看到他那样诚恳且平易近人 很快消除了顾虑 开始战士们称他为“首长” 后来称“老许同志” 最后索性直接叫“老许”了 从这些称谓的变化上 许世友知道自己像个兵了 夜间，许世友和战士一起站岗。 转眼间30天过去 许世友了解了战士们的 生活、训练和思想状况 与战士们建立了深厚的感情 要离开连队时 彼此间都恋恋不舍 班长张吉生代表班里 给许世友作了详细的鉴定 许世友成了连里的荣誉战士 许世友将军于天命之年再为兵 严于律己又能和战士打成一片 用实际行动证明 真正的政绩 不在于肩上的军衔有多高 而在于脚下的泥土有多厚 在于是否真正赢得了 战士和群众的真心拥护 （资料来源：中国人民军事革命博物馆、新华社、人民网）
