About

Dylan Tanzi studies International Politics at Georgetown University's Walsh School of Foreign Service. As Lead Research Analyst at Indo-Pacific Record, he has helped establish the Vietnam Research Desk and expand the publication's Southeast Asian coverage. His work focuses on primary-source research, translation verification, and editorial review of regional security and foreign policy analysis, with particular interests in Southeast Asian affairs, international security, and historical memory.
