Editorial team

Research, editorial review, and communications behind the record.

Benjamin Yang

Founder & Director of Research and Editorial

Benjamin Yang studies International Affairs at George Washington University's Elliott School, with a focus on Indo-Pacific security, U.S.–China relations, and regional strategic affairs. He founded Indo-Pacific Record to make official defense and security publications more accessible and to examine what those sources reveal—and leave unresolved—about regional developments. As Director of Research and Editorial, he guides the publication's research direction and editorial standards, emphasizing primary-source evidence, transparent documentation, and careful distinctions between official claims and independent analysis.

Dylan Tanzi

Lead Research Analyst

Dylan Tanzi studies International Politics at Georgetown University's Walsh School of Foreign Service. As Lead Research Analyst at Indo-Pacific Record, he has helped establish the Vietnam Research Desk and expand the publication's Southeast Asian coverage. His work focuses on primary-source research, translation verification, and editorial review of regional security and foreign policy analysis, with particular interests in Southeast Asian affairs, international security, and historical memory.

Christin Sawyer

Communications Editor

Christin Sawyer studies International Affairs at George Washington University. As Communications Editor at Indo-Pacific Record, she supports the presentation and distribution of source-grounded research through visual explainers, editorial updates, and public-facing communications. Fluent in Korean and English, she brings language skills and a cross-cultural perspective to the publication's coverage of Indo-Pacific affairs.
