The PLA Watch

The weekly analytical series of the China Desk, published from 2026 under the predecessor masthead China Mil Watch. It is a legacy series in the archival sense — it was published under a name this project no longer uses — not a discontinued one.

These issues are preserved as published

Every issue keeps the issue number, title, dek, byline and date it carried when it was published, and keeps the path it was published at: the full archive. Those issues were published by China Mil Watch, and China Mil Watch's own domain redirects to them rather than dropping them. Nothing here is rewritten to look as though it appeared under Indo-Pacific Record, because it did not.
