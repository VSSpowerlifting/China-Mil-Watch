The PLA Watch

The PLA Watch is now part of Indo-Pacific Record Briefs

The China Desk's weekly series The PLA Watch spanned the project's publication rename: 13 earlier editions were published under China Mil Watch, and 1 later edition was published by Indo-Pacific Record. Its issues are now earlier Briefs in Indo-Pacific Record Briefs, listed with every other Brief in one place.

Read the Briefs

These issues are preserved as published

Every issue keeps the issue number, title, dek, byline, date and publication identity it carried when it was published, and keeps the path it was published at: the series archive. China Mil Watch's own domain redirects to the 13 editions published under that masthead rather than dropping them. No issue is rewritten to look as though it appeared under a masthead it did not carry, in either direction.
