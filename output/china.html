Limits of this desk, stated plainly

  • Coverage is selective and heavily concentrated in one source.
  • Not every Chinese official publication surface is implemented. One configured source has no working collector and is reported as such on every run rather than hidden.
  • Collection was interrupted between 2026-07-17 and 2026-07-24. The gap is preserved, not backfilled.

These are properties of the desk a reader needs before citing it — see Coverage for what each collection run actually returned.
