Summary

Machine summary

Chen Xi, an Army Senior Colonel born in 1983 and most recently Deputy Director of the Press Bureau of the CMC Office for International Military Cooperation, debuted as the new Ministry of National Defense spokesperson at the June 16 regular press conference. His career path — PLA University of International Relations, then staff and officer roles before moving into the international cooperation apparatus — fits the pattern of selecting MND spokespersons with foreign-affairs and English-language backgrounds rather than operational ones. His prepared remarks foregrounded two specific phrases worth tracking as messaging anchors: 新时代强军故事 ('telling the story of military strengthening in the new era') and 可靠的中国力量 ('China's reliable strength').
