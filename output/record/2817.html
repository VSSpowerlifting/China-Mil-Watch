MND: Illegitimate Attempts of the Philippine Side are Doomed to Fail 7月30日下午，国防部举行例行记者会，国防部新闻发言人蒋斌大校答记者问。 记者：据报道，近期菲律宾公务船多次侵闯中国黄岩岛管辖海域，中国海警依法予以驱离。菲美日位相关海域开展所谓“联合巡航”。请问对此有何评论？ Question:It was reported that recently, Philippine public vessels intruded into the waters under China's jurisdiction at Huangyan Dao multiple times. The China Coast Guard (CCG) took necessary countermeasures in accordance with the law to expel them. Later, the Philippines, the US, and Japan conducted so-called joint patrols in the relevant waters and airspace. What's your comment on this? 蒋斌：有关国家的防务安全合作不应针对第三方，不得损害地区和平稳定。黄岩岛是中国固有领土，菲律宾领土范围由一系列国际条约确定，黄岩岛在其领土范围之外。无论菲方搞什么小动作、拉谁站台，都改变不了这一客观事实，非法图谋也不可能得逞。中国军队保持高度戒备，坚决反制任何侵权挑衅行径，以实际行动捍卫国家领土主权和海洋权益，维护南海和平稳定。 Jiang Bin:Defense and security cooperation among relevant countries should not target at any third party, nor undermine regional peace and stability. Huangyan Dao is China's inherent territory. The Philippines' territorial scope is defined by a number of international treaties, and Huangyan Dao lies beyond that scope. This fact cannot be altered no matter what tricks the Philippine side plays, or whomever it courts for support. Its illegitimate attempts are doomed to fail. The Chinese military will always remain on high alert, resolutely counter any rights-violation and provocative acts, take concrete actions to safeguard national territorial sovereignty and maritime rights and interests, and uphold peace and stability in the South China Sea.
