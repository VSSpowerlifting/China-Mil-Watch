People's Daily ran a front-page retrospective on September 12, 2026 marking the tenth anniversary of the CMC Joint Logistic Support Force (JLSF), highlighting the Wuhan Joint Logistic Support Base and the five Joint Logistic Support Centers, and showcasing capabilities including UAV swarm high-altitude delivery, unmanned vehicle accompanying support, telemedicine, and an automated three-dimensional warehouse system enabling what the article calls 'issuance at the touch of a button.' The piece documents the JLSF's self-described transition from 'logistics' (后勤) to 'joint logistics' (联勤) and from 'support team' to 'combat team,' framing the three interconnected chains — military logistics, joint delivery, and battlefield medical treatment — as the operational core of the reformed system. The article's value is as a record of how the institution presents its decade of development to a mass audience, including the specific named exercises ('Joint Logistic Mission' and 'Jingwu Joint Logistics' series) and individual exemplars; what remains unknown is the actual readiness status and throughput capacity of the systems described.
