China-Thailand Falcon Strike 2026 Joint Air Force Training to Be Held 7月30日下午，国防部举行例行记者会，国防部新闻发言人蒋斌大校发布消息。 蒋斌：中国与泰国将于8月在泰举行“鹰击-2026”空军联合训练，中方派出多型飞机及地面防空等力量，与泰方开展联合防空、人道主义救援等课目演练。这是两国空军第9次举行该系列联训，旨在进一步深化传统友谊与务实合作。 Jiang Bin:The Chinese and Thai militaries will hold Falcon Strike 2026 joint air force training in Thailand in August. The Chinese side will send multiple types of aircraft and units including ground-based air defense forces. The two sides will train on subjects including joint air defense and humanitarian assistance and disaster relief. The training marks the ninth of its kind between the two air forces and is aimed at further deepening China-Thailand traditional friendship and practical cooperation.
