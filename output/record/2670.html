Summary

Machine summary

The PAP Guizhou General Corps is running its spring 2026 new-recruit training cycle, covering formations, physical fitness, basic tactics, grenade throwing, battlefield first aid, and close-quarters baton and shield work under a phased curriculum the corps describes as 按纲施训、科学组训 ('training per regulations, organizing scientifically'). This is standard new-recruit onboarding content; its value is as a baseline record of the PAP's current recruit training syllabus and the specific subjects — notably emergency baton techniques and police baton-and-shield drills — that the Guizhou corps treats as foundational for internal-security forces.

近日，武警贵州总队严密组织开展2026年度春季入伍新兵教育训练。该总队坚持“按纲施训、科学组训、稳中求进、循序渐进”的原则，创新制定新兵成长路线图，科学划分训练阶段，精心设置组训内容，组织开展队列、军事体育、战术基础动作、手榴弹投掷和战场救护等实战化训练课目，着力锤炼新兵战斗作风，夯实军事素质根基。 示范教学。黄俊 摄 刺杀训练。黄俊 摄 应急棍术训练。黄俊 摄 警棍盾牌训练。黄俊 摄
