On July 25–26, the Southern Theater Command Navy deployed the hospital ship Youhao and the destroyer Rizhao to waters roughly 38 nautical miles north of Fiery Cross Reef after a Vietnamese vessel foundered and sank, rescuing five survivors and providing medical treatment to two crew members with hypothermia aboard the Youhao. The operation documents the Southern Theater Command Navy's use of Fiery Cross Reef as a de facto response hub, with the Youhao's medical capacity playing a direct operational role rather than a symbolic one. The prominent publication of Vietnamese crew members expressing gratitude points to a deliberate framing of the PLA Navy as a benign regional presence in the South China Sea.
