At the September 24 MND press conference, spokesperson Senior Colonel Jiang Bin framed unification as eliminating the 'root cause and source of chaos that is Taiwan independence' and removing the 'hidden danger of war and fire across the Taiwan Strait,' invoking the standard formulations 和平盛世 ('peaceful and flourishing era') and 民族复兴 ('national rejuvenation'). The statement is standard political work content prompted by a reporter question about rising pro-unification voices on the island; its value is as a record of how the MND publicly packages coercive framing within the 'peaceful reunification, one country, two systems' formula, not as evidence of a policy shift. What remains unknown is whether the reporter's premise—that pro-unification sentiment on Taiwan is meaningfully rising—reflects actual polling or is itself part of the framing.
