CCG vessels Changshan and Wuhu returned to port on September 9 after completing the 11th North Pacific high seas fisheries enforcement patrol — a 45-day, 19,800-nautical-mile mission during which the formation boarded and inspected 20 fishing vessels from multiple countries and flew 7 helicopter aerial enforcement sorties. The patrol documents the CCG's sustained, now-routine presence in North Pacific high seas fisheries enforcement under the Convention on the Conservation and Management of High Seas Fisheries Resources in the North Pacific Ocean, extending a pattern of incrementally institutionalized operations that began with the first such patrol and now includes aerial enforcement as a standard component. The article provides a baseline record of operational tempo and inspection scale but does not indicate changed posture or expanded geographic scope.

新华社北京9月9日电（孙崇峰、许富泽）中国海警长山舰和五虎舰组成的舰艇编队9日上午圆满完成2026年北太平洋公海渔业执法巡航任务，顺利返港。这是中国海警在北太平洋公海开展的第11次巡航。 巡航期间，中国海警舰艇编队根据联合国大会相关决议、《北太平洋公海渔业资源养护和管理公约》等，加大公海渔业法律法规和养护管理措施宣传，加强作业渔船巡查监管，做好区域渔业管理注册渔船信息核对，共观察记录各国渔船95艘，登临检查20艘，直升机公海飞行执法7架次，有效履行了公海执法职责，维护了北太平洋渔业生产秩序。 此次巡航历时45天，航程19800余海里，航时2100余小时。下一步，中国海警将持续深入开展公海渔业执法巡航，积极履行公约义务，为有效维护公海渔业生产秩序发挥应有作用。
