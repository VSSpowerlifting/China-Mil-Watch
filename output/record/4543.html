Summary

Machine summary

The People's Armed Police Qiandongnan Detachment held its 2026 Military Sports Meet and Mass Troop Training Competition, fielding over one hundred competitors from grassroots units across events ranging from the 5-kilometer armed cross-country and bayonet fighting (刺杀对抗) to logistics specialties including weapon disassembly and field cooking. This is standard annual unit-level competition content; its value is as a baseline record of how a PAP prefecture-level detachment in Guizhou structures internal readiness assessment, including the specific procedural controls — on-site scoring, signature confirmation, full-process video recording, and a dedicated 风气监督员 (atmosphere supervisory officer) — used to enforce judging integrity.
