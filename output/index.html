Record and analysis are not the same thing

  • The record

    Every item is stored as its institution published it: original language, original date, original URL, and a content fingerprint taken at capture. Nothing in the record asserts that what an institution said is true.

    Source record

    Explore the record

  • Machine output

    English titles, summaries, screening and the model flag are produced by software, and the daily pipeline publishes them without human review. They can be wrong. A model flag is a triage cue, not an editorial judgment.

  • The analysis

    Analysis is human-controlled, cites the records it rests on, and is labeled as interpretation.
