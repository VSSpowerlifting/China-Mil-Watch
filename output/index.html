Scope and coverage

This snapshot contains public records from China Desk, Singapore Desk. Coverage is selective and concentrated in Chinese sources. Other desks contribute no public records to this snapshot; their scope and limitations are described in coverage detail.

Evidence first. Interpretation labeled.
