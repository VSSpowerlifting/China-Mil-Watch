Remaining barriers to source admission

Broader Korean source interpretation and coverage remain incomplete.
Three specifically reviewed English translations and source-grounded analytical releases are publicly registered, but no machine-only assessment can be represented as independently verified research. Future drafts require editor approval, and DAPA-specific article evidence is not qualified.
Original files, third-party media and unreviewed sources remain excluded.
Separately approved KOGL Type 1 Korean source text may be reproduced with attribution. That scope does not clear the HWPX file itself, photographs, graphics, videos, third-party works, or any later unreviewed source; such material is not mirrored.
Production collector promotion is separate.
An operational MND metadata feed and item-specific source-text/English approvals do not promote the entire country desk or DAPA feed, assert countrywide completeness, or confer model-generated editorial authority. Sustained reliability and separate DAPA verification remain required.

Limits of this desk, stated plainly

  • Five original plus nine additional bibliographic links are not counted as independently archived originals. Separately governed production MND link metadata, licensed text and reviewed English are explicitly listed elsewhere; their existence does not establish comprehensive Korean defense coverage.
  • The admitted MND source family identifies 국방부 as issuer and Korea Policy Briefing as republisher; it is not direct MND.gov.kr collection. DAPA is a separate unadmitted issuer with a read-only first-party listing observation and a different prospective source-access and rights decision.
  • Bibliography records retain links and dates; separately qualified Korean original-text pages and independently approved English translations/analyses are public. HWPX, PDF binaries, photographs, diagrams and videos are not mirrored merely because article text qualifies for reuse.
  • Official source claims remain attributed, not independently verified facts. Portal posting and ministry distribution dates remain distinct. No DAPA production or automatic source-to-editorial approval is authorized.
