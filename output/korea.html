Remaining barriers to source admission

Substantive Korean-language analysis not admitted.
The five original HWPX files have passed machine parity but no independent Korean-language semantic and rendering signoff. The public directory provides official-source links and titles only, without IPR analytical summaries.
Original documents, images and future sources excluded.
Only source links and factual bibliographic metadata are shown. KOGL Type 1 source-text attribution does not cover all embedded photographs, media or HWPX components. No such material is reproduced.
Production collector promotion is separate.
Public reference links do not enable a production source, claim countrywide coverage, populate the archive or make raw Korean text eligible for AI-generated Briefs. Full production promotion requires the separate reliability and admission process.

Limits of this desk, stated plainly

  • Public scope is five frozen bibliographic links, not 5 production archive records, 5 separately corroborated sources or live Korea military coverage.
  • Issuing institution: Republic of Korea Ministry of National Defense (국방부); public publisher: Republic of Korea Policy Briefing (대한민국 정책브리핑). No direct MND.gov.kr ingest, DAPA or other-agency collection; the DAPA publisher index is a navigation link only.
  • The directory reproduces Korean headline, canonical official link and source dates only. No IPR translation, full original Korean article body, HWPX, photo, PDF, diagram or video is published.
  • Source-author claims are not independently verified by IPR; original distribution and portal posting dates are shown separately. No automatic Sunday Brief or production collector admission.
