US Indo-Pacific Reference Desk

Declared as a reference desk, deliberately bounded: the minimum US institutional output needed to read other desks' framing against a US framing of the same events. It is not a feed of US defense releases.

Official congressional and Treasury metadata feeds live — PACOM collection blocked

The U.S. Desk now automatically collects verified official URL, date and issuer metadata from two distinct sources: the U.S.-China Economic and Security Review Commission (congressional) and U.S. Department of the Treasury (executive). Direct PACOM collection remains access-blocked and DVIDS paused; this is not full military-source coverage and no publisher bodies, headlines, translations or analysis are automatically republished.

Limits of this desk, stated plainly

  • Automatic collection currently covers the official USCC press listing only, not the entire U.S. military or government.
  • Direct PACOM site access is blocked; Navy fleet sites also returned access restrictions. DVIDS is a distinct media service and its collector is paused.
  • Admitted USCC entries preserve publisher URL, publication date and issuer metadata, not original documents, photographs, summaries or IPR analysis.
  • A government statement is an attributable source, not independent verification of the statement's claims.

These are properties of the desk a reader needs before citing it — see Coverage for what each collection run actually returned.
