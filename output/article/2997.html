Strong Military Forum | Defining the Beauty of a Soldier Through Martial Excellence (精武)

强军论坛丨用精武定义军人之美
PLA Daily (解放军报) 7 August 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
The Central Propaganda Department and the CMC Political Work Department jointly named 12 'Most Beautiful Revolutionary Soldiers of the New Era,' including Central Theater Command Joint Staff Department staff officer Li Can, cyberspace forces researcher Gu Qiaoyun, and cyber-information domain (网信疆场) operator Wang Wenbin, with the announcement framed entirely around 精武 (martial excellence) rather than appearance or political virtue. The article documents how the PLA's official model-soldier campaign has shifted its evaluative criteria toward technical warfighting competence in informatized and intelligentized warfare, new-domain capabilities, and systems thinking — a framing that extends the pattern of PLA political work instrumentalizing hero-model propaganda to reinforce capability-building priorities rather than ideological loyalty alone. What remains unknown is the specific unit affiliations of most honorees and whether the selection criteria represent a formal policy change or editorial emphasis by military media.
Translation

In this era when everyone pursues beauty, there is a kind of beauty that radiates loyalty, valor, courage, and strength — this is the beauty of a soldier. This beauty begins with faith, is forged through victory in war, and is revealed through steadfast dedication.

Not long ago, the Central Propaganda Department and the Political Work Department of the Central Military Commission (CMC) jointly announced 12 "Most Beautiful Revolutionary Soldiers of the New Era." The beauty of a soldier is not defined by appearance or dress, but by martial excellence (精武).

"As long as one wears the uniform, one must bear the mission." The 12 officers and soldiers — Li Can, Wang Xiaoming, Wang Tao, Bu Jiawen, Zhou Hongchao, and others — are advanced models who have emerged from the armed forces' great practice of strengthening political revolutionary tempering (政治上的革命性锻造) under the Party's banner and advancing the realization of the centenary goal of army building.

Among them are political instructors who forge the soul and cultivate people with the Party's innovative theories, and troop-leading cadres who carry on the bloodline and build strong companies; there are staff officers capable of planning and deliberating on warfare, and special operations elite soldiers who sharpen their skills through realistic combat training; there are outstanding commanders who chase their dreams in the deep blue and sail the oceans, and model pilots who train hard and soar through the skies; there are research pioneers who devote themselves to achieving victory in the cyber and information domain (网信疆场), and research experts who focus on frontier breakthroughs in new domains and new-quality capabilities (新域新质); there are also skilled craftsmen riveted to their combat positions and devoted to equipment support, and pioneers in national defense mobilization fighting on the front lines of coastal defense… Examining their common characteristics, without exception all are top performers in their specialties, training experts, and martial excellence models — and this is precisely where their greatest beauty lies.

In literature there is no first place; in martial affairs there is no second place. Soldiers are born to win in battle, and the battlefield has never had a runner-up. For a soldier, only by having solid capabilities and first-rate qualities can one defeat the adversary and win glory.

The conduct of operations concerns thousands of troops and horses; the key to strategic planning determines the outcome of war. Li Can, a staff officer at a certain bureau of the Central Theater Command Joint Staff Department, approaches every plan drafted and every piece of data verified with the mindset of "going to the battlefield tomorrow," "always keeping in mind the game of shifting circumstances, always rehearsing future battles in the mind." He keeps a close eye on global military development trends, conducts in-depth research on the mechanisms for achieving victory in informatized and intelligentized warfare (信息化智能化战争), proactively tackles difficult and key problems such as joint operations command and the employment of new-type combat forces, and devotes all his energy to studying and planning for war. A staff officer talent who integrates command and technical skills in this way is naturally "most beautiful."

Gu Qiaoyun is a researcher at a unit of the cyberspace forces. She takes the needs of the state and the military as her research direction, has long engaged in the analysis and research of highly difficult technologies, tackles the invisible battlefield, and seizes the forefront of the struggle. She has achieved multiple key technological breakthroughs in the generation of new-domain and new-quality combat capabilities (新域新质战斗力). This heroic woman (巾帼英雄) who boldly blazes new trails is, of course, "most beautiful."

Chernyshevsky once said: "Whatever life we can see that, according to our understanding, ought to be so — that is beauty." For an ordinary person, the standards of beauty may number in the thousands. But soldiers must go to the battlefield, and can only let their strength do the talking. Only through martial excellence and enhanced capabilities — able to deploy, advance, and win — without disgracing the mission or failing under the burden, can one be called "most beautiful" without reservation.

Today, science and technology have become the core combat capability; new modes and means of combat are accelerating in their evolution, and unmanned warfare, information warfare, and intelligentized warfare (智能作战) have become the primary forms of war. "When times change, methods must change accordingly (世易时移，变法宜矣)." The "face" of war keeps changing, and the standards of martial excellence must also keep pace with the times. To be worthy of the title "most beautiful," one must have not only physical fitness and technical skills, but also intelligence; not only a fighting spirit, but also a scientific spirit; not only individual excellence, but also systems thinking (体系思维). That is to say, the hard-core strength of the "most beautiful" soldier must encompass both the fearless courage of revolution and all-out effort, and the scientific literacy of mental victory (心胜) and intellectual victory (智胜).

Wang Wenbin, a "Most Beautiful Revolutionary Soldier of the New Era," is rooted at the frontline positions of the cyber and information domain (网信疆场), contests the high-end invisible battlefield, keeps a close eye on changes in technology, changes in warfare, and changes in the adversary, explores new models of cyber and information defense, leads his team in overcoming multiple key technical challenges, successfully completes multiple major tasks, has innovated more than ten new operational methods and techniques, and effectively drives the enhancement of combat capability. His martial excellence reflects the demands of the era and increases the odds of victory in war.

The beauty of a soldier is defined by martial excellence and tested by victory in war. If one cannot win on the battlefield, everything amounts to zero. The battlefield is the most rigorous auditor, and also the best testing ground for "most beautiful." Every officer and soldier should consciously learn from the "Most Beautiful Revolutionary Soldiers of the New Era," strive to become training top performers, martial excellence stars, and warfare experts, train their warfighting skills to be strong, build up sufficient confidence in achieving victory, genuinely forge the solid capabilities to overcome and defeat the strong, and ensure that when the moment comes, they can achieve decisive victory on the battlefield and effectively defend the state's sovereignty, security, and development interests.

Original Chinese
在这个人人都追求美的年代，有一种美透着赤胆、威武、果敢和刚强，这就是军人之美。这份美，始于信仰、铸于胜战、显于坚守。 前不久，中央宣传部、中央军委政治工作部联合发布12名“最美新时代革命军人”。军人之美，不靠颜值装扮，而靠精武定义。 “只要身披军装，就必须肩负使命。”李灿、王晓明、王涛、步佳文、周宏潮等12名官兵，是全军部队在党的旗帜下，加强政治上的革命性锻造、推动实现建军一百年奋斗目标的伟大实践中涌现出的先进典型。 他们中有用党的创新理论铸魂育人的政治教员、赓续血脉建设过硬连队的带兵骨干，有能参善谋研战务战的机关参谋、实战实训精武强能的特战尖兵，有逐梦深蓝驰骋大洋的优秀指挥员、苦练精飞搏击长空的飞行员标兵，有潜心为战制胜网信疆场的科研先锋、紧盯前沿攻关新域新质的研究专家，还有铆定战位潜心装备保障的工匠能手、奋战在海防一线的国防动员先锋……探究他们的共同特点，无一例外皆是业务尖子、训练能手、精武标兵，这也正是他们最美的地方。 文无第一，武无第二。军人生来为战胜，战场从来无亚军。对军人来说，只有能力过硬、素质一流，才能战胜对手、赢得荣耀。 作战之事，关乎千军万马；运筹之要，定夺战争胜败。中部战区联合参谋部某局参谋李灿，以“明天就要上战场”的心态对待每一次方案拟制、数据核对，“胸中常怀变局博弈，脑中常演未来战事”。他紧盯世界军事发展潮流，深入研究信息化智能化战争制胜机理，主动攻关联合作战指挥、新型作战力量运用等重难点问题，将全部精力投入谋战研战。这样指技合一的参谋人才，自然是“最美”。 顾巧云，网络空间部队某部研究员。她以国家和军队需要为科研方向，长期从事高难技术分析研究，攻坚无形战场、抢占斗争前沿，在新域新质战斗力生成方面取得多项关键技术突破。这样勇蹚新路的巾帼英雄，当然是“最美”。 车尔尼雪夫斯基说过：“凡是我们看得见依照我们的理解应当如此的生活，就是美。”作为一名普通人，美的标准可能有千万条。但军人是要上战场的，只能靠实力说话。唯有精武强能，拉得出、上得去、打得赢，不辱使命、不负重担，才能当之无愧地称为“最美”。 今天，科技已成为核心战斗力，新的作战模式、作战手段加速演进，无人作战、信息作战、智能作战等成为主要战争形态。“世易时移，变法宜矣。”战争的“脸”不停地在改变，精武的标准也必须与时俱进。要想称得上“最美”，不仅要有体能、技能，还要有智能；不仅要有战斗精神，还要有科学精神；不仅要个体过硬，还要有体系思维。也就是说，“最美”军人的硬核实力，既要有革命加拼命的无畏勇气，还要有心胜加智胜的科学素养。 “最美新时代革命军人”王文彬，扎根网信疆场前沿阵地，博弈高端制胜无形战场，紧盯科技之变、战争之变、对手之变，探索网络信息防御新模式，带领团队攻克多项关键技术难题，圆满完成多项重大任务，创新战法技法10余项，有效助推战斗力提升。他的精武，体现了时代的要求，增加了战争的胜算。 军人之美，用精武定义，靠胜战检验。战场打不赢，一切等于零。战场是最严苛的审计师，也是对“最美”最好的检验场。每名官兵都应自觉向“最美新时代革命军人”学习，争当训练尖子、精武之星、打仗行家，把打仗的本领练强，把制胜的底气蓄足，切实锻造出制强胜强的过硬能力，确保一旦有事能决胜疆场，有效捍卫国家主权、安全、发展利益。
