For More Than 80 Years, the Same Name Has Bound a Hero Company to the Hero's Hometown

从80多年前起，同一个名字就将英雄连队与英雄故里紧紧相连
PLA Daily (解放军报) 16 August 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
A company-level unit of an unspecified brigade of the Southern Theater Command Army, designated the 'Ren Changlun Company' after an Eighth Route Army martyr killed in Shandong in 1944, conducted flood rescue operations in Guigang, Guangxi in July 2025, patrolling 45 kilometers of embankments, evacuating more than 1,000 civilians, and clearing over 200 tons of debris; villagers from Changlun Village and Fengyi Shop Village in Longkou City, Shandong—the martyr's hometown—sent care packages 2,350 kilometers south, prompting a formal reply letter from the company's political instructor (指导员) Wang Shaoxing. The article is standard political work content documenting the PLA's 'honoring heroes' (英烈) institutional culture and the military-civilian bond narrative; its value is as a record of how the Southern Theater Command Army frames unit identity and disaster-relief legitimacy through martyr lineage, not as evidence of any operational or structural development.
Translation

The "Ren Changlun Company" of a certain brigade of the Southern Theater Command Army participates in emergency rescue operations; villagers from Changlun Village, Longkou City, Shandong Province, send their regards from thousands of miles away——

The Same Name Binds Us Tightly Together

■ Zhang Daqi, PLA Daily Special Correspondents Kong Qingshan and Jia Yusheng

In July of this year, the "Ren Changlun Company" participated in emergency flood rescue operations in Guigang, Guangxi, following heavy rainfall. Pictured are officers and soldiers searching for and evacuating trapped civilians. Photo by Wang Mingcheng

Changlun Village and Fengyi Shop Village in Longkou City, Shandong Province, prepare care packages to send to the officers and soldiers of the "Ren Changlun Company." Villagers affix messages of condolence to the parcels. Photo by Zhang Daqi

The reply letter from the "Ren Changlun Company" to the villagers of Changlun Village and Fengyi Shop Village. Photo by Zhang Daqi

"We have received your care packages. This deep affection that has traveled more than two thousand kilometers is deeply imprinted in the heart of every officer and soldier…"

Not long ago, two letters spanning 2,350 kilometers were sent from the "Ren Changlun Company" of a certain brigade of the Southern Theater Command Army to Changlun Village in Qijia Town and Fengyi Shop Village in Shiliang Town, Longkou City, Shandong Province.

This past summer, Guangxi was struck by sudden heavy rainfall and flooding, and the officers and soldiers of the "Ren Changlun Company" rushed to the rescue through the night. After the people of the hero's hometown saw the related news, they packed local Jiaodong specialties and heat-relief supplies and sent them to the unit.

Care crosses mountains and seas; letters carry deep feeling. For more than 80 years, the same name has bound the hero company tightly to the hero's hometown.

The Martyr Returns to His Hometown; the Hero Has Successors

At the Jiaodong Revolutionary Martyrs Cemetery on Yingling Mountain in Qixia City, Shandong Province, the bronze statue of Martyr Ren Changlun has stood on the cliff for 81 years.

Ren Changlun was born in 1921 in Sun Huzhuang Village, Huangxian County (present-day Changlun Village, Longkou City, Shandong Province). He joined the Anti-Japanese Self-Defense Corps at age 17, enlisted in the Eighth Route Army at 19, participated in more than 120 engagements of varying scale, was wounded nine times in succession, and was awarded the title of "First-Class Combat Hero" by the Shandong Military District for his valor in battle. In November 1944, during the blocking battle at Changshabo in Haiyang, Ren Changlun—serving as deputy platoon leader of the 3rd Platoon, 5th Company, 1st Battalion, 14th Regiment, 5th Brigade, Jiaodong Military District of the Eighth Route Army—led his soldiers in close-quarters bayonet fighting against Japanese troops, consecutively bayoneting five enemy soldiers before being struck by a bullet and killed in action at the age of only 23.

After the hero gave his life for the nation, the Jiaodong Military District named the company in which he had served the "Ren Changlun Company," and the former Huangxian County People's Government renamed his hometown of Sun Huzhuang as Changlun Village. In the spring of 1945, the people of Jiaodong built the Jiaodong Anti-Japanese Martyrs Memorial Tower on Yingling Mountain and had Martyr Ren Changlun reinterred in the cemetery. As a further tribute, the relevant authorities also decided to cast a bronze statue of the martyr within the cemetery.

Wartime material resources were scarce, but when the military and civilians of the base area heard that a bronze statue of the martyr was to be cast, they spread the word and competed to donate copper. Young members of the Children's Corps turned in copper shell casings discarded by Japanese troops during "mopping-up" operations (扫荡) and copper coins saved at home; newlywed couples donated newly purchased copper washbasins; elderly people donated their cherished copper pipe bowls; others brought out copper ladles, copper locks, and copper handles from their chests and cabinets… Relying on the "copper from a hundred families" collected from the people, the bronze statue of Ren Changlun was completed in July 1945.

In 1958, Huangxian County built the Wangwu Reservoir, and Changlun Village was relocated in its entirety. As the People's Army maneuvered across many fronts and underwent several reorganizations and restructurings, the "Ren Changlun Company" eventually took up permanent station in Guangxi. Though separated by a thousand miles, the same name still connects the hero unit to the hero's hometown.

On the 70th anniversary of the victory of the War of Resistance Against Japan in 2015, the Longkou Farmers' Military Band traveled to Guilin, Guangxi, to pay tribute to the hero's successors with stirring music; on the eve of the 80th anniversary of the victory of the War of Resistance in 2025, the company organized representatives of its officers and soldiers to come to Longkou to visit red historical sites. At the parade marking the 80th anniversary of the victory of the Chinese People's War of Resistance Against Japanese Aggression and the World Anti-Fascist War, the battle flag of the "Ren Changlun Company" was honored in the review, and the villagers of Changlun Village watched the livestream together with exceptional pride.

"The company is the successor of the hero; we are the descendants of the hero's hometown. Our roots and veins are connected." Wang Zhongjin, Party branch secretary of Changlun Village, told reporters that over the years, Changlun Village has continuously relayed good news about the development of the old revolutionary base area to the company, while the company has regularly reported its achievements in training for war to the villagers, and the ties between the two sides have grown ever closer.

Fight Like a Fierce Tiger in Battle; Charge at the Vanguard

This summer, Guigang, Guangxi, was struck by sustained heavy rainfall, causing river levels to surge, widespread waterlogging in urban and rural areas, and frequent emergencies at multiple embankments. Facing the disaster, the "Ren Changlun Company" moved upon receiving orders and rushed to the affected areas.

"Fight like a fierce tiger in battle; charge at the vanguard." On the south bank of the Yu River, officers and soldiers began uninterrupted patrols and reinforcement of earthen embankments from deep in the night, assigning personnel to fixed posts at the most dangerous sections, vowing to hold the safety line at all costs. Upon receiving word that a certain middle school in Guigang City was surrounded by floodwaters, the company immediately drew on its backbone personnel to form a rescue team, drove assault boats through the swift currents into the school grounds, and safely evacuated teachers and students.

Due to the heavy rainfall, water in some low-lying areas of Guigang exceeded one meter in depth. In front of one residential building, water had already submerged the first floor, completely blocking the stairwell. Officers and soldiers erected a bamboo ladder at a second-floor window, and residents inside passed children out through the window. Platoon Leader Luogulari stood on the bamboo ladder, one hand bracing against the slippery wall, the other steadily receiving the children. He then worked with his comrades to help the residents evacuate safely. "As successors of the hero, we will fight with our lives to protect every single person," said Luogulari.

"Learn from Ren Changlun; the steel blade never dulls." Chanting their slogan and carrying the company flag, the "Ren Changlun Company" appeared wherever the disaster was most severe and the people most in need.

After the floodwaters receded, silt and debris accumulated across many parts of Guigang, and the officers and soldiers immediately turned to dredging operations. Under the scorching sun and amid swarms of insects, few officers and soldiers took extended rest, all in order to restore road access as quickly as possible and ensure the people could travel. The images of officers and soldiers fighting hard in the disaster zone were not only faithfully captured and recorded by journalists and broadcast by local news media, but were also photographed and shared by ordinary citizens. One citizen captured this photograph: a young soldier, clutching a half-eaten compressed biscuit, had fallen asleep leaning against a sandbag, his clothes covered in the cracked patterns left by dried mud.

During this emergency rescue operation, the company cumulatively patrolled and inspected 45 kilometers of embankments, evacuated more than 1,000 trapped civilians, and cleared more than 200 tons of silt and debris. Images of assault boats cleaving through the waves and camouflaged figures wading against the current flooded the internet, and countless netizens praised the soldiers. And a thousand miles away, another group of loved ones was also following their news at every moment.

Warmth Sent Across a Thousand Miles; United Hearts Battle the Flood Crest

Turbid waves surged across Guangxi; longing stretched long across Jiaodong. From the beginning of July, someone in the WeChat group of Changlun Village residents was always forwarding news about the "Ren Changlun Company" officers and soldiers participating in the emergency rescue operations.

Village elder Wang Baode is over 80 years old. Whenever he heard there was news about the "Ren Changlun Company," he would put on his reading glasses and have family members play the video, watching it over and over again. The sunburned faces and mud-stained clothing of the officers and soldiers in the videos made the villagers feel both heartache and pride: "That's the unit that came out of our place—still fighting with their lives for the common people!"

"Watching them is truly like watching my own children," one villager said in the group, and another immediately replied: "They are the children of our whole village."

A simple idea took root in the hearts of the villagers: to send the soldiers on the flood-fighting front line a token of remembrance from home. Changlun Village and Fengyi Shop Village in Shiliang Town—a village with a deep red cultural heritage—immediately agreed, deciding to jointly send care packages to the "Ren Changlun Company." Once the news spread, villagers spontaneously donated money to purchase heat-relief and epidemic-prevention medicines, and the heads of local township enterprises contributed local specialties—pine mushroom drinks and pine mushroom vermicelli—as gifts. Auntie Sun, a Changlun Village resident in her seventies with limited mobility, came leaning on her cane carrying a bag of dried dates she had sun-dried herself, insisting they be put in the package: "These are sweet—let the children have a taste."

Villagers of all ages, men and women, pitched in together to pack the care items, affixed the slogan "Warmth Sent Across a Thousand Miles; United Hearts Battle the Flood Crest" (千里寄温情 同心战洪峰), and sent them southward along with their care for the flood-fighting soldiers.

2,350 kilometers—ten boxes of care packages traveled from the Jiaodong Peninsula across the Yellow River, the Yangtze River, and the Nanling Mountains, heading south all the way to the land of Guangxi.

Wang Shaoxing, the political instructor (指导员) of the "Ren Changlun Company," recalls that the day the care packages arrived at the rescue front line, it happened to fall during a lull between rainstorms. The company's officers and soldiers gathered together and opened the parcels sent from the hero's hometown. The remembrance from a thousand miles away brought tears to the eyes of many soldiers: "We never expected that, separated by such a great distance, the elders of the hero's hometown would still be thinking of us."

Moved by the gesture, the company Party branch decided to write a reply letter in the name of all the company's officers and soldiers, to express gratitude to all the villagers of Changlun Village and Fengyi Shop Village.

"Mountains and seas separate us, yet warmth connects us; a thousand miles of watching over one another, great love warms the heart. We will transform the deep affection and expectations of the people of our hometown into an inexhaustible driving force for moving forward, and with steadfast dedication and hard work continue to write the glory of the heroes…" The impassioned words conveyed the emotion of all the officers and soldiers upon receiving the packages, reported to the elders on the progress of the rescue operations, and solemnly expressed their resolve to carry forward the spirit of the martyrs and do everything in their power to safeguard the lives and property of the people.

Several days later, the two reply letters arrived in Longkou. Reading to the end of the letters, the villagers discovered that Ren Changlun's name had been written at the very center of all the officers' and soldiers' signatures.

In the hearts of the officers and soldiers, the hero has never gone far—he is still at the head of the formation, at the front of every mission, and in every one of these letters filled with friendship and promise.

南部战区陆军某旅“任常伦连”参与抢险救援任务，山东省龙口市常伦庄村村民不远千里寄去问候—— 同一个名字 将我们紧紧相连 ■张大琪 解放军报特约记者 孔庆珊 贾玉省 今年7月，“任常伦连”参与广西贵港暴雨洪涝抢险救援任务，图为官兵搜救转移被困群众。王明城 摄 山东省龙口市常伦庄村、丰仪店村筹备慰问物资寄送给“任常伦连”官兵，乡亲们在慰问包裹上张贴慰问词。张大琪 摄 “任常伦连”给常伦庄村、丰仪店村乡亲的回信。张大琪 摄 “你们的慰问物资，我们收到了。这份跨越两千多公里的深情厚爱，深深烙印在每一名官兵心底……” 前不久，两封跨越2350公里的书信，从南部战区陆军某旅“任常伦连”寄达山东省龙口市七甲镇常伦庄村、石良镇丰仪店村。 今年夏季，广西突发暴雨洪涝灾害，“任常伦连”官兵星夜驰援。英雄故里乡亲看到相关新闻后，打包胶东特产、防暑用品寄到部队。 关爱跨越山海，信笺承载深情。从80多年前起，同一个名字就将英雄连队与英雄故里紧紧相连。 烈士归故里 英雄有传人 山东省栖霞市英灵山胶东革命烈士陵园内，任常伦烈士铜像已在山崖上矗立了81年。 任常伦1921年生于黄县孙胡庄村（今山东省龙口市常伦庄村），17岁加入抗日自卫团，19岁参加八路军，经历大小战斗120余次，先后9次负伤，因作战英勇被山东军区授予“一等战斗英雄”称号。1944年11月，在海阳长沙堡阻击战中，担任八路军胶东军区第5旅14团1营5连3排副排长的任常伦，带领战士与日军展开白刃格斗，接连刺倒5名敌人后，不幸中弹牺牲，年仅23岁。 英雄殉国后，胶东军区将他生前所在连队命名为“任常伦连”，原黄县人民政府将他的家乡孙胡庄更名为常伦庄。1945年春，胶东人民在英灵山修建胶东抗日烈士纪念塔，将任常伦烈士迁入陵园安葬。为表纪念，有关部门还决定在陵园内铸造一座烈士铜像。 战争年代物资紧缺，根据地军民听说要铸造烈士铜像，奔走相告，争相捐铜。儿童团员交上日军“扫荡”时丢弃的铜弹壳和家里积攒的铜钱，新婚夫妇捐出刚买的铜脸盆，老人捐出自己爱不释手的铜烟袋锅，还有的拿出了家里的铜勺、铜锁和箱柜上的铜把手……靠着群众募集的“百家铜”，任常伦铜像于1945年7月落成。 1958年，黄县修建王屋水库，常伦庄整体搬迁。随人民军队多方转战、历经数次编制体制调整，“任常伦连”后来常驻广西。纵然相隔千里，同一个名字仍将英雄部队与英雄故里连在一起。 2015年抗战胜利70周年之际，龙口农民军乐团赴广西桂林慰问，用铿锵旋律致敬英雄传人；2025年抗战胜利80周年前夕，连队组织官兵代表来到龙口，寻访红色旧址。在纪念中国人民抗日战争暨世界反法西斯战争胜利80周年阅兵式上，“任常伦连”战旗光荣受阅，常伦庄村村民集体观看直播，分外自豪。 “连队是英雄传人，我们是英雄家乡的后人，我们根脉相连。”常伦庄村党支部书记王忠金告诉记者，这些年来，常伦庄村持续向连队传递老区发展喜讯，连队时常向乡亲汇报练兵备战实绩，双方联系愈加紧密。 打仗赛猛虎 冲锋在头阵 今夏，广西贵港遭遇持续性强降雨，江河水位暴涨，城区、乡村大面积内涝，多处堤防险情频发。灾情当前，“任常伦连”闻令而动，奔赴受灾区域。 “打仗赛猛虎，冲锋在头阵。”郁江南岸，官兵从深夜开始不间断巡查加固土堤，在最危险的堤段定人定岗，誓死守住安全防线。接到贵港市某中学被洪水围困的消息，连队立即抽调骨干组成救援队，驾驶冲锋舟穿过激流挺进校园，将师生安全转移。 受强降雨影响，贵港部分地势低洼地段积水超过一米。在一处居民楼前，积水已漫过一楼，楼道被彻底封堵。官兵在二楼窗口架起竹梯，屋内群众将孩子从窗口递出。排长洛古拉日站在竹梯上，一手扶住湿滑的墙壁，一手稳稳接住孩子。随后，他又与战友帮助群众安全转移。“作为英雄传人，我们拼了命也要护好每一个人。”洛古拉日说。 “学习任常伦，钢刀不卷刃。”喊着口号、打着连旗，“任常伦连”出现在灾情最严重、人民最需要的地方。 积水退去后，贵港多地淤泥杂物堆积，官兵转身又投入清淤任务。烈日炙烤、蚊虫侵扰，却少有官兵长时间休整，只为早一刻恢复道路畅通，保障群众出行。官兵奋力救灾的身影，不仅被记者真实拍摄记录，在当地新闻媒体播发，也被市民群众拍摄传播。一位市民就拍下这样一张照片：一名年轻战士攥着没吃完的压缩饼干、靠着沙袋睡着了，衣裤上满是泥浆干涸后的裂纹。 此次抢险救援任务中，连队累计巡堤查险45公里，转移受困群众千余名，清理淤泥杂物200余吨。冲锋舟破浪前行、迷彩身影涉水逆行的画面刷屏网络，众多网友为子弟兵点赞。而在千里之外，还有一群亲人也时刻关注着他们的消息。 千里寄温情 同心战洪峰 八桂浊浪奔涌，胶东牵挂绵长。从7月初开始，常伦庄村村民的微信群里，就总有人转发“任常伦连”官兵参加抢险救援任务的新闻。 村民王宝德老人80多岁了，听说有“任常伦连”的新闻，就戴上老花镜让家人播放视频，并一遍遍回放。视频里官兵晒得发红的脸庞、沾满泥浆的衣襟，让村民们既心疼又自豪：“这就是咱们这里走出去的部队呀，还是在为了老百姓拼命！” “看着他们，真像看着自己的孩子。”一名村民在群里说道，马上就有另一名村民回复：“他们就是咱全村的孩子。” 一个朴素的念头在乡亲们的心中萌发：要给抗洪一线的子弟兵送上一份家乡的惦念。常伦庄村与有着深厚红色文化底蕴的石良镇丰仪店村一拍即合，决定共同为“任常伦连”寄送慰问品。消息一传开，村民纷纷自发捐款购买防暑防疫药品，乡镇企业负责人拿出特产松茸饮、松茸粉丝作为慰问品。70多岁的常伦庄村村民孙大娘腿脚不便，拄着拐杖送来一包自己晒的干枣，非要塞进包裹：“这个甜，让孩子们尝尝。” 村民们男女老少齐上阵，将慰问品打包，贴上“千里寄温情 同心战洪峰”的标语，与对抗洪官兵的关怀一起寄往远方。 2350公里——10箱慰问物资从胶东半岛跨越黄河、长江、南岭，一路向南抵达八桂大地。 “任常伦连”指导员王绍兴记得，慰问物资抵达救援一线那天，正逢一场暴雨间隙。连队官兵围在一起，拆开英雄故里寄来的包裹。来自千里之外的记挂，让不少战士红了眼眶：“没想到相隔这么远，英雄家乡的父老还惦记着我们。” 激动之余，连队党支部决定以全连官兵名义撰写回信，答谢常伦庄村、丰仪店村全体乡亲。 “山海相隔，温情相连；千里守望，大爱暖心。我们会把家乡人民的厚爱与期盼化作前行的不竭动力，用坚守与实干续写英雄荣光……”滚烫的文字诉说全体官兵收到物资的感动，向父老乡亲汇报抢险进展，郑重表达传承英烈精神、全力守护人民群众生命财产安全的决心。 数日后，两封回信抵达龙口。读至信尾，乡亲们发现，任常伦的名字被写在全连官兵签名的正中央。 在官兵心中，英雄从未远去——他依然在队列排头，在任务一线，在这一封封载满情谊与承诺的信件里。 本版制图：扈 硕
