Reporter's Notes | "Dissipating Heat" — Fang Zhiyuan

记者手记｜“散热”方致远
PLA Daily (解放军报) 9 August 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
Wang Ji, a double-amputee researcher on dialysis at the National University of Defense Technology's High-Confidence Software Laboratory, voluntarily withdrew his team from international software verification competitions after three consecutive top-prize wins, redirecting effort toward what he frames as direct operational requirements and equipment-development chokepoints (卡脖子难题). The article is a political-work profile in the 'reporter's notes' genre; its analytical value is as a record of how NUDT's institutional messaging frames the tension between academic prestige and combat-oriented research priorities, using Wang's personal sacrifice to reinforce the 'combat first, victory paramount' (打仗为先、打赢为要) standard for military scientists. The framing points to ongoing institutional concern that defense researchers are drawn toward internationally legible metrics — competition rankings, conference appearances, peer recognition — at the expense of classified or operationally sensitive work that produces no comparable public credential.
Translation

Walking into the High-Confidence Software Laboratory at the National University of Defense Technology, I came to understand the most moving dialectic of struggle embodied by Wang Ji: true striving to strengthen the military is not about blindly chasing heat and fame, but about giving everything in the charge forward, and actively cooling down and dissipating heat (散热) when it comes to making choices.

Previously, Wang Ji led his team to win top prizes at international software verification competitions for three consecutive years, drawing the attention of the industry and the admiration of academia, with everyone expecting him to reach the summit again and create another legend. At the very peak of that acclaim, he resolutely and on his own initiative called a halt to competition participation, in order to "reduce hollow fever and strip away superficiality (降虚热、祛浮华)" — for his team, for his research, and for his own academic pursuits.

Faced with students who could not understand, Wang Ji said firmly: "In military scientific research, mission comes first — not rankings. No matter how impressive a trophy looks, it cannot buy equipment safety, and it cannot buy battlefield victory." In his view, international competitions lean toward general academic evaluation and are removed from the actual combat needs of the troops. Rather than expending enormous energy chasing the glory of the competition arena, it is better to actively "dissipate heat" from a restless mindset, withdrawing all one's energy from the pursuit of honors and popularity and directing it toward what national defense urgently needs and what the battlefield immediately demands. In that single act of choosing, the supreme pursuit of the military researcher — "combat first, victory paramount (打仗为先、打赢为要)" — is fully revealed.

Both legs amputated at a high level, dialysis three times a week — fate gave him the lowest physical configuration, yet it could not stop him from upholding the highest standard of devotion to his country. Others urged him to rest more and travel less; he always replied with a smile: "As long as my mind can still think and my hands can still move, I will not stop charging forward."

Year after year he moved between project front lines, review sites, and teaching classrooms. After dialysis, with only a brief rest, he would immediately bend over his desk to revise diagrams, work through models, and guide students, his schedule perpetually running at overload. The more physically and mentally depleted he became, the more he understood the need to actively dissipate heat and make precise choices. Faced with a steady stream of requests for academic appearances, pointless social engagements, and redundant reviews, he declined them all and cleared them away entirely. Instead, he used the time freed up in the laboratory, and in accompanying his students.

He said candidly: "This life that the organization fought to save does not belong to me personally — it belongs to the cause of national defense. Limited energy must be used where it matters most."

Under the overall support of the institute's Party organization, he streamlined ineffective travel, optimized the rhythm of field work, and retained only those tasks with direct operational necessity. Last year he traveled on assignment across eleven cities, not to build a reputation or add luster to a halo, but solely to break through the chokepoint problems (卡脖子难题) in equipment development. In the choices made, the wasteful exhaustion of constant travel was shed, so that every unit of life's energy could be precisely applied to the cutting edge of military strengthening.

Carrying the triple mission of soldier, discipline leader, and doctoral supervisor, Wang Ji holds to this principle: "The body may have limitations; the mission absolutely has no shortcomings."

He long subjected himself to self-imposed pressure, but later grew ever more clear-minded: true responsibility is not burning oneself out in solitary struggle, but building a strong team, cultivating a strong succession echelon (梯队), and keeping the cause evergreen.

So he relied on the Party organization and the collective strength of his team, building platforms, assigning burdens, and letting go to develop talent. When honors were recommended for him, he twice voluntarily deferred: "My physical condition is limited; young people have greater potential — the stage should be left for those who come after." When students came seeking to train under him by reputation, he proactively engaged them and encouraged them to pursue national defense research: "Every step of your growth is paving the road toward the goal of strengthening the military."

From burning alone to relay succession, from individual breakthroughs to collective concentration of effort — this "dissipation of heat" at the level of responsibility is not a weakening of commitment, but an elevation of pursuit. He shed the weight of his own obsessions, let the young take up the baton of hard struggle, and allowed the cause of military strengthening to continue without end, achieving results that endure.

Half a lifetime of burning perseverance, a lifetime of clear-headed choices. Wang Ji's "dissipating heat" is a three-level self-purification: cooling the hollow fever of awards, to guard the true heart of research; reducing the overload of travel, to focus on the main business of achieving victory; releasing the pressure of responsibility's obsessions, to lift up the future of the cause.

He made me truly understand: the best kind of striving is knowing how to dispel hollow fire (祛虚火), guard one's original heart, and know what to choose and what to relinquish. Measured "dissipation of heat" — shedding superficiality, settling and concentrating strength — reveals the sincere original aspiration of a soldier, burning always and only for the strengthening of the military and service to the country.

Original Chinese
走进国防科技大学高可信软件实验室，我读懂了王戟最动人的奋斗辩证法：真正的强军拼搏，不是盲目追逐热度，而是冲锋时倾尽全力，取舍时主动降温散热。 此前，王戟带领团队连续三年斩获国际软件验证顶级赛事大奖，行业瞩目、学界追捧，所有人都期待他登顶再创传奇。就在荣誉热度最高的时候，他毅然主动叫停参赛，为团队、为科研、为自己的学术追求“降虚热、祛浮华”。 面对学生不解，王戟语气坚定：“军队科研，第一位是使命，不是名次。奖杯再好看，换不来装备安全、换不来战场打赢。”在他眼里，国际竞赛偏重通用学术评价，和部队实战需求存在差距。与其耗费大量精力追逐赛场光环，不如主动给浮躁心态“散热”，把全部心力从追荣誉、博热度中抽离，投向国防急需、战场刚需。一句取舍，尽显军人科研者“打仗为先、打赢为要”的至高追求。 双腿高位截肢、每周三次透析，命运给了他最低的身体配置，却挡不住他最高标准的报国坚守。旁人劝他多休养、少奔波，他总笑着说：“只要脑子还能思考、手还能动，我就不会停下冲锋的脚步。” 常年辗转项目一线、评审现场、教学课堂，透析结束稍作休整，他便立刻伏案改图、推演模型、指导学生，行程常年超负荷运转。越是身心透支，他越懂得主动散热、精准取舍。面对纷至沓来的学术站台、无效应酬、冗余评审，他一概婉拒清零。相反，他把空出的时间，用在实验室，用在陪伴学生身上。 他坦言：“组织抢回来的这条生命，不属于我个人，属于国防事业。有限的精力，必须用在最关键的地方。” 在学院党组织统筹保障下，他精简无效行程、优化外勤节奏，只保留实战刚需任务。去年辗转十一城出差攻坚，不为名气造势、不为光环添热，只为破解装备卡脖子难题。取舍之间，褪去奔波虚耗，让每一份生命热能都精准用在强军刀刃上。 军人、学科带头人、博导三重使命在肩，王戟恪守：“身体可以有局限，使命绝对无短板。” 他长期自我加压，后来愈发通透：真正的担当，不是单打独斗耗尽自己，而是带强团队、育强梯队、让事业长青。 于是他依托党组织、依靠团队集体，搭台压担、放手育才。面对荣誉推荐，他两次主动谦让：“我身体受限，年轻人更有潜力，舞台该留给后来者。” 面对慕名而来的代培学生，他主动对接、鼓励他们从事国防科研：“你们的每一步成长，都是在为强军目标铺路。” 从独自燃烧到梯队接续、从个人攻坚到集体聚力，这份责任层面的“散热”，不是弱化担当，而是升华追求。他让自己卸下执念重压，让青年接过攻坚接力棒，让强军事业生生不息、久久为功。 半生滚烫坚守，一生清醒取舍。王戟的“散热”，是三次高级别的自我提纯：为奖项虚热降温，守住科研本心；为行程过载减负，聚焦打赢主业；为责任执念松压，托举事业未来。 他让我真正明白：最好的奋斗，是懂得祛虚火、守本心、知取舍。适度“散热”，褪去浮华、沉淀力量，彰显一名军人的赤诚初心，始终只为强军报国滚烫燃烧。
