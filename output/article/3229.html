About this analysis
Summary
The CMC Political Work Department, the Cyberspace Administration of China, and China Media Group jointly released a six-episode convergent media documentary series titled 'Achieving Victory' (制胜), broadcast on CCTV's General Channel beginning July 30, covering the Army, Navy, Air Force, Rocket Force, and joint operations with footage described as all-real, no-special-effects filming from frontline units including electromagnetic catapult launches on the Fujian and a Rocket Force single-operator launch sequence by Commander Xue Wei. The article is a production retrospective by a credited series creator, not a news report; its value is as a record of how the CMC frames 'thought on strengthening the military' for domestic and international audiences through story-based narrative and theory visualization (理论可视化), a genre the author traces across at least four prior series. The explicit goal of breaking down audience segment barriers (圈层壁垒) and reaching youth communities through short-video platforms documents the institutional logic behind the PLA's convergent media political work strategy, but the article cannot support conclusions about actual unit capabilities or readiness.

Let the Light of Thought Illuminate the Journey of Military Strengthening — Creative Insights from the Ideological Interpretation Convergent Media Series "Achieving Victory" (制胜)

■ Liu Xiaowei

Poster for the ideological interpretation convergent media series "Achieving Victory" (制胜). Provided by the author.

Recently, the Political Work Department of the Central Military Commission (CMC), in conjunction with the Cyberspace Administration of China and China Media Group, released the six-episode ideological interpretation convergent media series "Achieving Victory" (制胜). Since its broadcast on CCTV's General Channel beginning July 30, the series has rapidly generated enthusiastic responses across all sectors of society and has also attracted attention from international opinion. Audiences have widely praised the series, saying it is both a vivid elucidation of Xi Jinping's thought on strengthening the military and a vibrant presentation of the achievements of military strengthening and revitalization in the new era, while also conveying to the world the resolve and capability of the Chinese military to defend sovereignty and safeguard peace.

At present, the academic community has yet to arrive at a unified definition of "ideological interpretation convergent media series" (思想解读类融媒体片). Drawing on my practical experience and reflection from participating in the creation of "Chasing the Dream" (逐梦), "Forged in Fire" (淬火), "Storming the Fortress" (攻坚), and "Achieving Victory" (制胜) in recent years, I would define it as follows: an ideological interpretation convergent media series is a new type of audiovisual work that emerged against the backdrop of the all-media era. It integrates multiple media forms and technological means, with the systematic elucidation and dissemination of the Party's innovative theories as its central purpose, "thought + art + technology" integrated communication as its creative philosophy, and "story-based narrative" (故事化叙事) and "theory visualization" (理论可视化) as its primary methods. By transforming abstract theory into concrete, perceptible audiovisual content, it continuously enhances the appeal and receptivity of ideological communication.

"Achieving Victory" (制胜) has achieved new breakthroughs in precisely these areas. It is no longer content with telling audiences "what the thought on strengthening the military is," but instead allows audiences to genuinely feel "why the thought on strengthening the military is truth" and "how it has profoundly influenced and transformed this military."

I.

The greatest challenge in ideological interpretation film and television works is none other than handling the relationship between "going deep" (深入) and "coming out clearly" (浅出). Too deep, and the work becomes inaccessible to the masses, with limited communicative effect; too shallow, and it lapses into didacticism, losing the weight of thought. "Achieving Victory" (制胜) consists of six episodes: "Where the Battle Flag Points" (战旗所向), "The 'Land' Force Sheds Its Feathers" (换羽之"陆"), "Advancing into the Deep Blue" (挺进深蓝), "Tempering the Sword in Air and Space" (空天砺剑), "The East Wind Surges Mightily" (东风浩荡), and "Joint Operations, Joint Victory" (联战联胜). The series focuses on the theme of "being capable of fighting and winning battles" (能打仗、打胜仗), closely following the fervent practice of officers and soldiers across the entire military as they concentrate on preparing for war under the guidance of Xi Jinping's thought on strengthening the military. It progressively reflects the new spirit and new conduct of officers and soldiers in achieving victory and striving to strengthen the military.

"Achieving Victory" (制胜) approaches ideological interpretation by expressing thought through stories and conveying stories through characters. The series does not deliberately embellish achievements or one-sidedly showcase equipment; instead, through actual combat exercise and training scenarios, the logic of system-of-systems operations (体系作战), and the personal growth experiences of officers and soldiers, it deeply interprets the fundamental reasons for "achieving victory" (制胜): first, victory of thought (思想之胜) — important statements such as "a strong nation must have a strong military, and only with a strong military can the nation be secure" and "only by being capable of war can war be stopped" are vividly presented through one story after another. Second, victory of spirit (精神之胜) — the inheritance of the red gene (红色基因), the founding mission and original aspiration, and the blood-and-iron commitment of revolutionary servicemen and women in the new era have forged the soul of victory. Third, victory of the system (体系之胜) — joint operations have truly realized the transformation of scattered fingers into a clenched fist, achieving victory through jointness. At the same time, the series' artistic expression consistently serves the communication of values; amid stunning audiovisual experiences and nuanced story narration, it imperceptibly conveys the thought on strengthening the military and consolidates national confidence, achieving a high degree of unity among ideological content, artistic quality, and educational value, and demonstrating the responsibility and commitment of mainstream convergent media film and television works.

II.

In terms of narrative approach, "Achieving Victory" (制胜) has also undertaken some bold innovations, constructing a narrative system that progressively advances through imagery narrative (意象叙事), scene narrative (场景叙事), and character narrative (人物叙事), combining aesthetic appeal with depth. For example, it achieves a poetic expression of service-branch narrative. The series uses four great natural images — "spring thunder, ocean waves, swift wind, and fireworks" — to precisely correspond to the actual combat exercise and training scenarios of the four major services — the Army, Navy, Air Force, and Rocket Force — merging imagery of military training with the magnificent artistic conception of the natural world, endowing military-themed film and television works with the poetic aesthetic beauty of narrative and the artistic conception of Eastern aesthetics, making the narrative more vivid and tangible.

The series draws on and employs literary narrative techniques. For example, in showcasing the Navy officers' and soldiers' capability to win battles, the series does so by telling a story of "finding life in a desperate situation" (绝境逢生). While carrying out a long-range mission, a submarine encountered a sudden and life-threatening "depth drop" (掉深) emergency even as enemy anti-submarine forces continued to patrol. The crew had to simultaneously cope with the crisis of potentially being crushed by water pressure at any moment while maintaining silent concealment. In the end, within a mere ten-plus seconds, five precise commands were issued, successfully resolving the emergency, and the mission to conduct reconnaissance and simulated strike against an enemy formation was accomplished in full. The Rocket Force's victory story focuses on a realistic combat exercise. After the Blue Force conducted a precise strike, all the operators of one Red Force launch vehicle were adjudicated as "killed in action," leaving only Commander Xue Wei and one security operator. Under these extreme circumstances, Xue Wei sat down at the control console alone and single-handedly completed the entire launch sequence, successfully launching the missile into the air. The excavation of details about typical characters in these typical scenarios gives the work greater narrative appeal and emotional tension.

The series also explores and constructs a hierarchically progressive narrative logic of "individual soldier — service branch — system" (单兵—军种—体系). The six episodes of the full series adopt a "general — specific — general" (总—分—总) narrative structure, building layer upon layer and elevating step by step: from frontline officers' and soldiers' extreme combat training and breakthroughs in adversarial exercises, to the display of each service branch's specialized operational capabilities, and finally arriving at the all-around leap of the joint operations system (联合作战体系), completing the logical construction from "victory through individual equipment and individual valor" to "victory through system integration and full-domain systemic operations" (体系融合制胜、系统全域制胜). The series adheres to the principle of documentary narrative, does not shy away from shortcomings, does not conceal difficulties, and does not avoid speaking of failure, authentically presenting the breakthroughs and growth of officers and soldiers in complex battlefield environments, giving the story of military strengthening a more genuine texture.

III.

Each era has its own literature and art; each era has its own aesthetics. "Achieving Victory" (制胜) strives to create a military aesthetics belonging to the new era, with authenticity as its most important artistic foundation. The series upholds the creative principle of "documentary as the foundation, real locations as the priority" (纪实为本、实景为要): all combat scenarios, exercise and training footage, and equipment operation shots are filmed on location with frontline units, without the use of any virtual cinematic special effects. From live-fire launches of long-range rocket artillery to electromagnetic catapult launches on the Fujian, from carrier-based aircraft takeoffs and landings to the handling of sudden air-sea situations, all footage originates from real exercises, training, and mission scenarios. This creative approach of "zero special effects, all real footage" (零特效、全实拍) conveys the confidence and self-assurance of a major-power military through an authentic cinematic language. Compared with artistic processing, the documentary aesthetics of "Achieving Victory" (制胜) carry greater impact. Every thundering cannon, every high-speed assault, every Red-versus-Blue confrontation allows audiences to directly touch the authentic state of combat training and war preparation, achieving the aesthetic effect of "authenticity is shock, simplicity is grandeur" (真实即震撼、质朴即磅礴).

In audiovisual expression, the series constructs an immersive audiovisual experience, creating an aesthetic effect that fuses the grand with the minute. In camera work, the series achieves a perfect combination of sweeping panoramic shots and nuanced close-ups. Aerial long takes present the battlefield landscape and magnificent momentum of the Gobi Desert, the deep sea, and the vast sky; tracking shots follow officers and soldiers through exercises and equipment operations, recreating the tense atmosphere of realistic training; close-up shots focus on the eyes of officers and soldiers and the details of their operations, capturing the martial spirit (血性) and steadfast commitment of servicemen and women, grounding the grand theme of military strengthening in vivid imagery.

In terms of pacing, the series is measured in its tension and release, interweaving fast and slow. High-intensity live-fire confrontations and rapid-cut joint operations sequences highlight the fast tempo of modern warfare; slow-motion and lyrical segments of officers and soldiers reviewing and reflecting, conducting daily war preparations, and persevering in harsh environments ease the narrative pace, organically fusing the hard iron-and-blood with soft warmth.

The series also strives for precision and depth in distilling "soldiers' words in soldiers' language" (兵言兵语). When speaking of the inheritance of the victory-winning gene (胜战基因), 98-year-old Korean War veteran Wei Diren, seated in a wheelchair, cried out to the officers and soldiers of the "Dadu River Company" (大渡河连): "Charge forward, charge forward toward that bright red flag of your company, and strive for even greater glory." A single utterance surpasses a thousand words. In addition, golden phrases in the series such as "with the courage that once crossed the river, march forth to today's crossing of the sea" (以当年渡河之勇，赴今朝跨海之征) achieve a high degree of unity among image, sound, and emotion.

IV.

As a "convergent media series" (融媒体片), "Achieving Victory" (制胜) bears the distinct characteristics of cross-screen convergent media dissemination, providing the industry with a replicable model in terms of communication. It breaks through the single dissemination format of traditional television documentaries, achieving multiple innovations in creation, dissemination, and interaction.

In terms of dissemination platforms, it achieves two-way linkage between large screens and small screens. The film is broadcast during prime time on CCTV's General Channel, ensuring the authority of its dissemination; it is also adapted to the dissemination characteristics of short-video platforms and new-media clients, with "highlight segments" (高光片段) extracted and edited into lightweight content for easy viewing by mobile users, achieving full-channel, full-coverage dissemination and breaking down barriers between audience segments (圈层壁垒).

In terms of dissemination register, it achieves a youthful transformation of mainstream narrative. The series abandons a didactic register, tells stories through authentic footage and conveys thought through realistic combat scenarios, and uses authentic, direct forms of expression that young people enjoy, bringing mainstream values into youth communities and achieving efficient dissemination and deep immersion of the Party's innovative theories.

In summary, "Achieving Victory" (制胜) forges its soul through ideological value, breaks new ground through narrative innovation, sets a new standard through documentary aesthetics, and expands its boundaries through convergent media form. It is a documentary masterwork showcasing the great journey of military strengthening and revitalization of the People's Army in the new era, as well as a fine convergent media artistic work that integrates Eastern aesthetics, modern register, and mainstream values. It provides reference value for military-themed works and mainstream convergent media content creation in the new era.

Reflecting on the creative practice of "Achieving Victory" (制胜), I feel deeply that it has answered an important proposition for the creation of ideological interpretation convergent media series in the new era: how can the Party's innovative theories reach people's hearts in a lively and vivid artistic manner? Its answer is — stand on the high ground of thought to study and reflect, immerse oneself in the fervent practice of officers and soldiers, and make every screen a window for telling the story of military strengthening. Literary and artistic works often mark the spiritual height of an era. I look forward to seeing more outstanding works, illuminated by the light of thought, use the camera to write the epic of military strengthening that belongs to this great era.

Selected Synchronized Dialogue from the Ideological Interpretation Convergent Media Series "Achieving Victory" (制胜)

The warplanes that participated in the founding ceremony of the nation went to the Korean War battlefield alongside our predecessors just one year later. Today, we fly more advanced fighter jets in a glorious review. Will we be able, tomorrow, to step forward like our predecessors and achieve victory on the battlefield?

— Episode 1: "Where the Battle Flag Points" (战旗所向)

Some ask: where exactly does the new-type Army's newness lie? Its newness lies in our ability to reach any place where we are needed. The ground, the sky, the ocean — the boundaries of the battlefield are the boundaries of the Army.

— Episode 2: "The 'Land' Force Sheds Its Feathers" (换羽之"陆")

Only by being able to hold the line can we win battles; only by winning battles can we repel the enemy in distant seas. Those who face the sea flourish; those who turn their backs to the sea decline. Those who cannot control the sea will inevitably be controlled by the sea. Chairman Xi has urged us to accelerate the formation of real combat capability in new equipment and new forces. Over these years, we have not stopped for a single moment. From Tsinghua University to the military, from coastal waters to the deep blue, over 21 years I have served on 7 warships of 5 types. I understand that venturing into distant seas is not merely romance and dreams, but rather dozens of combat stations and hundreds of officers and soldiers — silent wakes in the dark of night breaking through the "impossible," using wisdom-based victory (智胜) to underpin achieved victory (制胜), and using the backbone of commitment to hold up the shield of distant-sea defense.

— Episode 3: "Advancing into the Deep Blue" (挺进深蓝)

Accelerating the building of a powerful People's Air Force that is integrated across air and space and capable of both offense and defense is the strategic objective for which every one of us Air Force officers and soldiers strives. On the plateau, in the Gobi, over distant seas, we soar and achieve victory under the same sky, building an impregnable Great Wall of Blue Sky (蓝天长城).

— Episode 4: "Tempering the Sword in Air and Space" (空天砺剑)

Is it lonely, guarding a mountain valley? If you have ever touched a missile with your own hands and felt the weight of that responsibility, you would not think so. Every missile that passes through our hands is like our own child. We tend to it carefully, keeping it in good health, and then send it off to carry out the mission that belongs to it. The farther it flies, the more precisely it strikes, the more meaningful our concealment becomes. This is not loneliness — it is glory.

— Episode 5: "The East Wind Surges Mightily" (东风浩荡)

During a shooting exercise, I suddenly had an inspiration. Every combat unit in joint operations is like a different type of gun. The reason a sharpshooter can hit the target with every gun is that he is thoroughly familiar with every type of firearm. We must become joint sharpshooters (联合的神枪手), materializing and integrating the experience and achievements of each service branch and arm into a single system.

— Episode 6: "Joint Operations, Joint Victory" (联战联胜)

让思想之光照亮强军征程 ——思想解读类融媒体片《制胜》创作启示 ■刘笑伟 思想解读类融媒体片《制胜》海报。作者提供 日前，中央军委政治工作部联合中央网信办、中央广播电视总台，推出6集思想解读类融媒体片《制胜》。该片自7月30日起在中央电视台综合频道播出后，迅速在社会各界引起热烈反响，也获得国际舆论关注。广大观众纷纷称赞，该片既是习近平强军思想的生动阐释，又是新时代强军兴军成果的鲜活呈现，也向世界传递出中国军队捍卫主权、守护和平的决心和能力。 目前，“思想解读类融媒体片”在学术界尚无统一的定义。根据近年来参与创作《逐梦》《淬火》《攻坚》及《制胜》的实践与思考，我认为：思想解读类融媒体片，是在全媒体时代背景下产生的一种融合多种媒介形式与技术手段，以系统阐释和传播党的创新理论为主旨、以“思想+艺术+技术”的融合传播为创作理念、以“故事化叙事”及“理论可视化”等为主要手段，通过将抽象理论转化为具象、可感知的视听内容，不断增强思想传播的感染力与接受度的新型影视作品。 《制胜》正是在这些方面上实现了新的突破。它不再满足于告诉观众“强军思想是什么”，而是让观众真切感受到“强军思想为什么是真理”以及“它如何深刻影响和改变了这支军队”。 一 思想解读类影视作品最大的难题，莫过于处理好“深入”与“浅出”的关系。太深，则曲高和寡，传播效果有限；太浅，则流于说教，失去思想的重量。《制胜》共6集，分别为《战旗所向》《换羽之“陆”》《挺进深蓝》《空天砺剑》《东风浩荡》《联战联胜》。该片聚焦“能打仗、打胜仗”主题，紧扣全军官兵在习近平强军思想指引下聚力备战打仗火热实践，行进式反映了部队官兵制胜打赢、奋斗强军的新气象新作为。 《制胜》在思想解读方面的做法是，把思想通过故事表达出来，把故事通过人物讲述出来。该片不刻意渲染成绩、不片面展示装备，而是透过实战演训场景、体系作战逻辑、官兵成长经历，深度诠释“制胜”的根本原因：一是思想之胜，“强国必须强军，军强才能国安”“能战方能止战”等重要论述，通过一个个故事得以生动呈现。二是精神之胜，新时代革命军人的红色基因传承和初心使命、血性担当铸就了胜战之魂。三是体系之胜，联合作战真正实现了攥指成拳、联合制胜。同时，该片的艺术表达始终服务于价值传播，在震撼的视听体验、细腻的故事叙事中，潜移默化传递强军思想、凝聚民族自信，实现了思想性、艺术性、教育性的高度统一，彰显了主流融媒体影视作品的责任与担当。 二 在叙事方式上，《制胜》也进行了一些大胆的革新，构建起了意象叙事、场景叙事与人物叙事层层递进的叙事体系，兼具美感与深度。比如，它实现了军种叙事的诗意表达。该片以“春雷、海浪、疾风、焰火”四大自然意象精准对应陆军、海军、空军、火箭军四大军种实战演训场景，将军事训练的画面与自然天地的磅礴意境相融，赋予军事题材影视作品叙事诗意美感与东方美学意蕴，让叙事更加形象可感。 该片借鉴使用了文学化的叙事手段。比如，在展现海军官兵胜战能力时，该片是通过讲述“绝境逢生”的故事来实现的。某潜艇在执行远航任务中，在敌方反潜兵力持续巡弋的情况下，又突发致命的“掉深”险情。官兵一边要应对随时可能被水压碾碎的危机，一边要保持静默隐蔽。最终在短短十几秒内精准下达5道口令，成功化解险情，并圆满完成了对敌编队的侦察和模拟打击任务。火箭军的胜战故事则聚焦于一场实战化演练。蓝方精准破袭后，红方一辆发射车的号手被全部裁定“阵亡”，仅剩下指挥长薛伟和一名警戒号手。在极端情况下，薛伟独自坐上控制台，一人完成了全套发射流程，成功将导弹发射升空。这些典型场景中典型人物的细节挖掘，让作品更加富有叙事魅力与情感张力。 该片还探索构建了“单兵—军种—体系”的层级递进叙事逻辑。全片6集采取“总—分—总”的叙事结构，层层铺垫、步步升华，从一线官兵的极限练兵、对抗突破，到各军种专项作战能力展示，最终落脚于联合作战体系的全方位跃升，完成从“单一装备制胜、个体英勇制胜”到“体系融合制胜、系统全域制胜”的逻辑构建。该片秉持纪实叙事原则，不回避短板、不遮掩困境、不讳言失败，真实呈现官兵在复杂战场环境中的突破与成长，让强军故事更具真实质感。 三 一个时代有一个时代的文艺，一个时代有一个时代的美学。《制胜》努力创造了属于新时代的军事美学，真实是它最重要的艺术底色。该片坚守“纪实为本、实景为要”的创作原则，所有作战场景、演训画面、装备实操镜头均为一线部队实景实拍，不使用任何影视虚拟特效。从远箱火箭炮实弹发射到福建舰电磁弹射，从舰载机起降到海空突发态势处置，所有画面均源自真实演训、任务场景。这种“零特效、全实拍”的创作方式，以真实的镜头语言传递大国军队的底气与自信。相较于艺术加工，《制胜》的纪实美学更具冲击力。每一个炮火轰鸣、每一次极速突击、每一场红蓝对抗，都让观众直观触摸到练兵备战的真实状态，实现了“真实即震撼、质朴即磅礴”的美学效果。 在视听表达上，该片通过沉浸式视听建构，营造宏大与精微相融合的美学效果。在镜头运用上，该片实现全景宏阔与特写细腻的完美结合。航拍长镜头，呈现戈壁、深海、长空的战场格局与磅礴气势；运动镜头跟拍官兵演训、装备操作，还原实战实训紧张氛围；特写镜头聚焦官兵眼神、操作细节，捕捉军人的血性与坚守，让宏大的强军主题落地于鲜活的画面。 节奏把控上，该片张弛有度、快慢交织。高强度的实弹对抗、联合作战快剪镜头，凸显现代战争的快节奏；官兵复盘反思、日常备战、坚守艰苦环境的慢镜头与抒情片段，舒缓了叙事节奏，让坚硬的铁血与柔软的温情有机融合。
