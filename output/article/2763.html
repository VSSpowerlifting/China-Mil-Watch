Political Work

Strong Military Forum | Be a Person Who Can Awaken Others

强军论坛丨做一个能够唤醒他人的人
PLA Daily (解放军报) 2 August 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
A commentary in the PLA's 'Strong Military Forum' column holds up Wang Ji, a wheelchair-bound computer scientist at the National University of Defense Technology's College of Computer Science and a designated 时代楷模 ('Model of the Era'), alongside nuclear-weapons researcher Lin Junde and July 1st Medal recipient Chen Junwu as moral exemplars for the force, framing their careers in highly reliable software and nuclear-shield development as the standard officers and soldiers should emulate during the push toward the centenary army-building goal. This is standard political-work content; its value is as a record of how PLA media links defense-scientist hagiography to current campaign themes—specifically the 建军一百年奋斗目标 deadline pressure and the 15th Five-Year Plan cycle—rather than as evidence of any new policy line or capability development. The piece does confirm that Wang Ji's work on 高可信软件 (highly reliable software) for informatized warfare retains enough institutional prestige to anchor a force-wide motivational campaign.
Translation

As a journalist, although I have never interviewed Wang Ji, a researcher at the College of Computer Science of the National University of Defense Technology, in person, his noble soul and selfless dedication have left me deeply moved.

Einstein, in his eulogy for Marie Curie, said: "The measure of the greatness of a first-rate person for the age and the course of history lies perhaps even more in their moral character than in their purely intellectual achievements." As a "Model of the Era" (时代楷模), Wang Ji carries his own stories, his own character, and his own power to move people — he is a person who can awaken others. This is the power of a role model, the magnetism of an exemplar.

Wang Ji is known as the "wheelchair scientist." What makes him precious is that he holds his spear in defense of the nation (执戟以卫社稷). He has devoted himself to research on highly reliable software (高可信软件), forging a "security weapon" for informatized warfare (信息化战争), training more than 120 master's and doctoral students for the national defense science and technology enterprise, and at the age of 55 receiving the First Prize of the Military Science and Technology Progress Award. His resolve to serve the nation through scientific research has never wavered, and its results have continued to manifest.

Wang Ji has been commended as a "National Model of Self-Improvement" (全国自强模范). His strength lies in never giving up. Though he tragically lost both legs and was then diagnosed with uremia at the age of 51, his mind never stopped thinking creatively and his hands never stopped writing code.

Having achieved such abundant results, and facing such physical conditions, Wang Ji did not "lie flat" (躺平), nor did he "enjoy life" — instead, he chose to be a scientist in the mold of Luo Jianfu, chose to carry forward the "Galaxy Spirit" (银河精神), and chose to honor his commitments to the Party, the nation, and the military with "highly reliable faith" (高可信的信仰).

This inevitably brings to mind another resolute soldier — Lin Junde, the "Outstanding Scientist Who Dedicated Himself to the National Defense Science and Technology Enterprise."

Lin Junde was both a general and a soldier. He was content with solitude and found joy in hardship, standing guard at Lop Nur for 52 years, leading research personnel in breaking through multiple core and critical technologies, and making outstanding contributions to forging the Republic's nuclear shield.

In May 2012, Lin Junde was diagnosed with late-stage cholangiocarcinoma. During his hospitalization, the words he repeated most often were "I want to work." When people urged him repeatedly to rest for a while, he shook his head again and again, saying: "I cannot rest — once I lie down, I won't be able to get up again." In the final eight days of his life, he still raced against time, working selflessly. After Lin Junde's death, officers and soldiers of a certain base offered this couplet in his memory: "A life of fortitude, toiling in obscurity to accomplish earth-shaking deeds; a lifetime of simplicity, willing to be a person who buries their name and hides their identity."

Lin Junde and Wang Ji are both persons written in capital letters. Comrade Mao Zedong pointed out that "a model itself is a kind of political force." Scientists like Wang Ji — their political force lies in awakening and inspiring others through noble character, concrete action, and outstanding contributions. It can be said that they themselves are visible philosophy, living guides to values.

Chen Junwu, a recipient of the "July 1st Medal" (七一勋章), is also a scientist. He never enjoyed a moment of leisure throughout his life, and he has never regretted serving the nation through science and technology. Chen Junwu has a formula for measuring his own worth: "If in one's lifetime contribution exceeds what one takes, life is brilliant; if contribution equals what one takes, life is ordinary; if contribution is less than what one takes, life is dim."

I believe that scientists like Wang Ji deeply understand this formula for measuring one's own worth. Throughout their lives they have held fast to their original aspirations, kept their mission firmly in mind, and devoted themselves with unwavering dedication — and so their lives shine with dazzling brilliance, forging themselves into persons who can awaken others.

"A good role model is like a bell that calls people together." At present, the effort to achieve the centenary goal of army-building (建军一百年奋斗目标) has entered a critical period; the layout of the 15th Five-Year Plan is advancing and its construction is fully underway; tasks of shaping the situation through struggle (斗争塑势), capability building, and training and war preparation are intertwined and advancing in parallel — making it all the more necessary for models to demonstrate and exemplars to lead, continuously igniting the enthusiasm and drive of officers and soldiers to strive to build a centenary army and advance toward a first-class strong military. Let us learn from scientists like Wang Ji, use their exemplary deeds to awaken our own souls, passion, and fighting spirit, reinvigorate the mental energy (精气神) to get things done and forge new ground, actively and proactively contribute our youth, wisdom, and strength to the cause of building a strong military, and write a brilliant chapter in our military lives.

Original Chinese
作为一名记者，我虽没有当面采访过国防科技大学计算机学院研究员王戟，但他高尚的灵魂、无私的奉献，让我深深折服。 爱因斯坦在悼念居里夫人时说：“第一流人物对于时代和历史进程的意义，在其道德品质方面，也许比单纯的才智成就还要大。”作为“时代楷模”，王戟自带故事、自带品格、自带震撼力，是一个能够唤醒他人的人。这就是榜样的力量、楷模的魅力。 王戟被称为“轮椅科学家”，他的可贵在于执戟以卫社稷。他深耕高可信软件研究，打造信息化战争的“安全利器”，为国防科研事业培养120多名硕士博士，55岁荣获军事科学技术进步奖一等奖……科研报国的决心始终没有动摇、成效不断显现。 王戟被表彰为“全国自强模范”，他的强大在于永不放弃。虽不幸失去双腿，51岁时又患上尿毒症，但他的大脑从未停止创新思考、双手从未停止编写代码。 取得如此丰硕的成绩，面对这样的身体条件，王戟没有“躺平”，更没有“享受人生”，而是选择当罗健夫式的科学家，选择让“银河精神”发扬光大，选择用“高可信的信仰”兑现对党、国家和军队的承诺。 这不禁让我想起了另一位坚强的军人——“献身国防科技事业杰出科学家”林俊德。 林俊德既是将军，也是战士，他甘于寂寞、以苦为乐，坚守罗布泊52年，带领科研人员突破多项核心关键技术，为铸就共和国核盾牌作出卓越贡献。 2012年5月，林俊德被确诊为胆管癌晚期。住院期间，他重复最多的一句话就是“我要工作”。大家多次劝他休息一会儿，他一再摇头说：“不能休息，一躺下就起不来了。”在生命的最后8天里，他仍旧争分夺秒地忘我工作。林俊德去世后，某基地官兵送上这样一副挽联：“铿锵一生，苦干惊天动地事；淡泊一世，甘做隐姓埋名人。” 林俊德和王戟，都是大写的人。毛泽东同志指出，“典型本身就是一种政治力量”。像王戟那样的科学家，他们的政治力量就在于以崇高品格、实际行动、突出贡献唤醒和激励他人。可以说，他们本身就是看得见的哲理，是鲜活的价值引领。 “七一勋章”获得者陈俊武，也是一位科学家。他一生未得片刻闲，科技报国终不悔。陈俊武有一个考量自身价值的公式，即“一生中奉献大于索取，人生就灿烂；奉献等于索取，人生就平淡；奉献小于索取，人生就黯淡”。 我想，像王戟那样的科学家，深深懂得这个考量自身价值的公式。他们一生坚守初心、牢记使命、执着奉献，故其人生闪烁夺目的光彩，把自己锻造成能够唤醒他人的人。 “好榜样就像把人们召集到一起的钟声。”当前，实现建军一百年奋斗目标进入关键期，“十五五”规划布局推进、建设全面展开，部队斗争塑势、能力建设、练兵备战等任务交织并进，更加需要典型的示范、榜样的引领，持续激发官兵奋斗建军百年、奋进强军一流的热情干劲。让我们向王戟那样的科学家学习，用他们的先进事迹唤醒自己的灵魂、激情和斗志，提振干事创业的精气神，积极主动为强军事业贡献青春、智慧和力量，书写出彩的军旅人生。
