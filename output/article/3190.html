PLA and People's Armed Police Officers and Soldiers, and Militia Go All Out in Emergency Rescue Operations

解放军和武警部队官兵、民兵全力开展抢险救援
PLA Daily (解放军报) 13 August 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
Following Typhoon Bebinca (No. 13), PAP and PLA garrison commands, people's armed forces departments (人武部), and militia conducted flood relief operations across Shanghai, Jiangsu, Zhejiang, Hubei, and Hebei between August 10–12, evacuating nearly 10,000 residents in Hebei alone, deploying over 5,000 militia members province-wide, and pre-positioning more than 40,000 units of flood-control materials; in Zhejiang's Yuhuan City, militia used dragnet-style (拉网式) inspections to extract 120 trapped residents from Chumen Town overnight. The article documents the standard civil-military disaster response architecture — garrison commands activating militia through 人武部 channels, coordinating with local governments on evacuation and levee work — and its editorial commentary, framing the operation as a 大考 (major test) requiring cadres to embody both vigilance and technical competence, is evidence of how PLA Daily uses disaster response to reinforce political-work themes around Party member responsibility, not evidence of a capability or posture shift.
Translation

PLA Daily Report — Affected by Typhoon "Bebinca" (No. 13 of this year), our country has experienced heavy rainfall in multiple locations over consecutive days, with some areas suffering varying degrees of disaster. To safeguard the lives and property of the people, PLA and People's Armed Police (PAP) officers and soldiers, and militia have gone all out in emergency rescue operations.

On the evening of August 10, waterlogging occurred in Zhujiajiao Town, Qingpu District, Shanghai, trapping some residents in their homes. Officers and soldiers from a brigade of the PAP Shanghai General Corps rushed to the scene, navigating assault boats through the streets, conducting door-to-door inspections, and helping trapped residents evacuate safely.

From the 10th to the 11th, southern Jiangsu experienced sustained heavy rainfall. The garrison commands (军分区) and people's armed forces departments (人武部) of Suzhou and Wuxi rapidly activated emergency plans, organizing militia to quickly clear drainage channels and remove debris from roads. Faced with rapidly rising water levels in some areas, local militia used sandbags to construct water-blocking cofferdams to seal breaches, and with the aid of professional drainage equipment, successfully eliminated the danger.

Affected by the typhoon, from the night of the 11th to the morning of the 12th, Yuhuan City, Zhejiang Province, experienced widespread torrential rain, with localized extreme torrential rain. Serious waterlogging accumulated in multiple low-lying areas of the city, trapping some residents. The Yuhuan City People's Armed Forces Department organized militia emergency forces for an urgent deployment, rushing overnight to the severely affected Chumen Town to conduct a dragnet-style (拉网式) inspection, evacuate trapped residents, and deliver emergency food supplies. After several hours of hard work, the militia evacuated more than 120 trapped residents.

As the remnant circulation of Typhoon "Bebinca" moved northward and westward, Hubei, Hebei, and other provinces experienced heavy precipitation. On the evening of the 11th, the water level of Guanshan River in Guanshan Town, Danjiangkou City, Hubei Province, rose rapidly, inundating multiple road sections along Provincial Road 280. The Danjiangkou City People's Armed Forces Department organized militia forces overnight to assist the local government in road control and blockade operations, helping evacuate more than 400 residents from Guanshan Town. To ensure safe passage through the flood season (安全度汛), the Yicheng City People's Armed Forces Department organized militia forces to move into dangerous sections of the Han River and flood-prone areas nearby, conducting 24-hour levee patrols and hazard inspections and rushing to build secondary levees (子堤) in coordination with professional rescue forces.

The Hebei Provincial Military District closely monitored rainfall and flood conditions, called on militia throughout the province to actively participate on the front lines of flood prevention, and guided Shijiazhuang, Langfang, Xingtai, Handan, and other localities to mobilize more than 5,000 militia members. They conducted safety hazard inspections at key points including low-lying flood-prone areas, riverbanks, and pond dams; pre-positioned more than 40,000 units (pieces/sets) of flood-control materials; and assisted local governments in evacuating and relocating nearly 10,000 residents.

(Compiled from contributions by Chen Zeyang, Li Yang, Fang Fang, Chen Yichen, and Zhou Xin; reporters Lü Xiaobo, Guo Zhongzheng, Xiang Liming, and Lu Dongfang)

Currently, our country is in the critical period of flood prevention known as "the latter part of July and the first part of August" (七下八上), and flood prevention work faces complex and severe challenges. Confronting this "major test" (大考) bearing on the lives and property of the people, the broad ranks of Party member cadres in the military must both maintain the sense of responsibility of "never being able to set one's mind at ease at any moment" (时时放心不下) and possess the solid capability of "having a firm grasp of every matter" (事事心中有底).

"Never being able to set one's mind at ease at any moment" manifests a sincere commitment to putting the people first and life first, and an ever-vigilant sense of hardship awareness (忧患意识) that never relaxes for an instant; it demands that Party member cadres, in the face of complex and ever-changing flood conditions, resolutely overcome complacency and carelessness. "Having a firm grasp of every matter" embodies a pragmatic work style of responding scientifically and the professional competence of fulfilling one's duties and responsibilities — anticipating risks ahead of time and doing work down to the finest details. Only when both thinking and capability are solid can a firm defensive line be built to protect the lives and property of the people.

Flood prevention and disaster relief is a hard battle. Units assigned to the mission must, on the one hand, estimate difficulties more fully, think through risks more deeply, and prepare response measures more comprehensively; on the other hand, they must have a thorough command of the hydrological terrain and risk points within their area of operations, be proficient in emergency plans and rescue procedures, and precisely allocate material reserves and rescue forces — ensuring that at the first order they can deploy immediately, respond scientifically, and conduct rescue operations efficiently, so as to live up to the trust placed in them and not fail in their mission.

Original Chinese
解放军报讯 受今年第13号台风“白海豚”影响，连日来，我国多地遭遇强降雨，部分地区出现不同程度受灾情况。为保障人民群众生命财产安全，解放军和武警部队官兵、民兵全力开展抢险救援。 8月10日晚，上海市青浦区朱家角镇出现内涝，部分群众被困家中。武警上海总队某支队官兵迅速赶赴现场，驾驶冲锋舟穿梭于各条街道，逐门逐户巡查，帮助被困群众顺利转移。 10日至11日，江苏南部地区遭遇持续强降雨。苏州、无锡两地军分区和人武部迅速启动应急预案，组织民兵快速抢通排水渠道，清理道路杂物。面对部分地区水位急剧上涨的情况，当地民兵利用沙袋筑起挡水围堰封堵缺口，并借助专业排涝设备，成功排除险情。 受台风影响，11日夜至12日清晨，浙江省玉环市普降大暴雨，局部出现特大暴雨。当地多处低洼地带积水严重，部分群众被困。玉环市人武部组织民兵应急力量紧急出动，连夜奔赴受灾严重的楚门镇开展拉网式排查，转移受困群众，并送来应急食品。经过数小时奋战，民兵共转移被困群众120余人。 随着台风“白海豚”残余环流北上西进，湖北、河北等省份出现强降水过程。11日晚，湖北省丹江口市官山镇官山河水位快速上涨，280省道沿线多处路段被河水淹没。丹江口市人武部连夜组织民兵力量，协助地方政府开展道路封控等工作，帮助官山镇转移群众400余人。为确保安全度汛，宜城市人武部组织民兵力量，就近进驻汉江险段、易涝区域，联合专业救援力量24小时巡堤查险、抢筑子堤。 河北省军区紧盯雨情汛情，号召全省民兵踊跃投身防汛一线，指导石家庄、廊坊、邢台、邯郸等地动员5000余名民兵，对低洼易涝地段、河道沿岸、塘坝等关键点位开展安全隐患排查，预置防汛物资4万余台（件、套），协助地方政府转移疏散群众近万人。 （综合陈泽阳、李阳、方芳、陈一琛、周鑫，记者吕晓博、郭中正、向黎鸣、卢东方来稿） 当前，我国正值“七下八上”防汛关键期，防汛工作面临复杂严峻的考验。面对这场关乎人民群众生命财产安全的“大考”，军队广大党员干部既要保持“时时放心不下”的责任感，还要具备“事事心中有底”的过硬本领。 “时时放心不下”，彰显的是人民至上、生命至上的真挚情怀和须臾不松懈的忧患意识，要求党员干部面对复杂多变的汛情，坚决克服麻痹大意思想；“事事心中有底”，体现的是科学应对的务实作风和履职尽责的专业素养，把风险想在前头、把工作做在细处。只有思想与能力都过硬，才能筑牢守护人民生命财产安全的坚实防线。 防汛救灾是一场硬仗，任务部队一方面要把困难估计得更充分一些，把风险思考得更深入一些，把应对措施准备得更周全一些；另一方面，要对任务区内水文地貌、风险点位了然于胸，对应急预案、抢险流程熟练掌握，对物资储备、救援力量精准调配，确保一声令下能够迅即出动、科学应对、高效救援，做到不负重托、不辱使命。
