PLA Daily Commentator: Advance the Study and Education Campaign with a Higher Standpoint and More Concrete Measures

解放军报评论员：以更高站位、更实举措推动学习教育走深走实
PLA Daily (解放军报) 27 May 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
PLA Daily's commentator column calls on Party organizations at all levels throughout the military to deepen an ongoing campaign to establish a correct 政绩观 (outlook on political performance), explicitly naming four behavioral deviations to be rectified: blind recklessness, fraud and falsification, passive 躺平 (lying flat), and new officials refusing to inherit predecessors' unfinished work. The column's acknowledgment that a 'small number of units' have shown insufficient effort and insufficiently concrete measures documents internal compliance problems with the campaign rather than its success. The framing—tying the campaign explicitly to 政治整训 (political rectification and training) and the 建军一百年 centenary goal—points to continued institutional pressure on officer conduct as a priority for the 14th Five-Year Plan opening year, though which units or echelons are underperforming is not identified.
Translation

Advance the Study and Education Campaign with a Higher Standpoint and More Concrete Measures

■ PLA Daily Commentator

In undertaking work and building achievements, one's outlook on political performance (政绩观) is of paramount importance.

At present, the entire Party and the entire military are conducting in-depth study and education on establishing and practicing a correct outlook on political performance (树立和践行正确政绩观). This study and education campaign is an important task for Party building this year. Chairman Xi has attached great importance to conducting this study and education campaign well, issuing special important instructions that have pointed the direction and provided the fundamental guidance for carrying it out. Party organizations at all levels in the military and the broad ranks of Party member cadres must develop a profound theoretical and practical understanding of the importance of the outlook on political performance to the development of the Party's, the state's, and the military's cause, and genuinely strengthen their ideological and action-level consciousness in establishing and practicing a correct outlook on political performance.

The higher the standpoint, the stronger the sense of mission and responsibility, and the more proactively one engages in the study and education campaign; the more concrete the measures, the more vigorous and forceful the work posture, and the more solid the results the study and education campaign can achieve. Deploying and conducting this study and education campaign in the opening year of the 14th Five-Year Plan (十五五) is a major decision made by the Party Central Committee and Chairman Xi with an eye toward the overall work of the Party, the state, and the military, based on the evolving situation and tasks. It carries special political significance, a clear contemporary meaning, and far-reaching strategic considerations. Party organizations at all levels and the broad ranks of Party member cadres must fully recognize the important practical significance and far-reaching historical significance of conducting this study and education campaign for: completely, accurately, and comprehensively implementing the new development philosophy and rallying efforts to advance high-quality development of national defense and military building; for continuously advancing the political approach to military building (政治建军) and deepening political rectification and training (政治整训); and for making solid efforts to fight and win the decisive battle of achieving the centenary goal of military building (建军一百年奋斗目标) and continuously opening up new prospects for strengthening and revitalizing the military. They must consciously advance the study and education campaign to greater depth and more solid results with a higher standpoint and more concrete measures.

Since the study and education campaign was launched, units at all levels throughout the military have consciously taken up a high political position and grasped the work firmly and concretely, strengthening research and planning, carefully organizing implementation, and earnestly and solidly raising ideological understanding, rectifying prominent problems, and establishing sound institutional rules and regulations, so as to thoroughly advance all aspects of the study and education campaign. At the same time, we must soberly recognize that a small number of units still have problems in conducting the study and education campaign, such as insufficient investment of effort, insufficient focus on problems, and insufficiently concrete work measures. There is an urgent need for Party organizations at all levels to further deepen their ideological understanding, resolutely and forcefully implement the major decisions and deployments of the Party Central Committee, the Central Military Commission (中央军委), and Chairman Xi, conduct the study and education campaign with strict and solid standards, and guide and motivate the broad ranks of Party member cadres to work hard and get real results with a more spirited fighting will and a more pragmatic work style—producing political performance for the people and producing political performance through hard work—so as to concentrate efforts on improving the military's level of modernization and warfighting capability.

To advance the study and education campaign to greater depth and more solid results, the key is to closely align with the objectives and requirements and implement them in detail. "Establishing the Party for the public good, creating benefits for the people, making decisions scientifically, and working hard to get real results" (立党为公、为民造福、科学决策、真抓实干) is the general requirement for this study and education campaign. It must be deeply understood, accurately grasped, and comprehensively implemented, running through every aspect and the entire process of the study and education campaign, so that in the course of studying, reflecting, practicing, and internalizing (学思践悟), and in careful and steadfast action (细照笃行), real work is done, real results are sought, and real achievements are created. We must uphold the political stance of "establishing the Party for the public good," treat Party character as the cornerstone of one's conduct, career, speech, and virtue, constantly reflect on why one joined the Party, why one serves in the military, and what it means to be a commander, and fulfill duties and responsibilities and undertake work and build achievements with the sense of responsibility that success need not be attributed to oneself, yet success must certainly be achieved through one's own efforts (功成不必在我、功成必定有我). We must uphold the fundamental purpose of "creating benefits for the people," genuinely keep the original aspiration firmly in mind, carry the mission firmly on the shoulders, and hold the purpose firmly in place, faithfully fulfill the sacred duty of bearing arms for the people and fighting for the people, and forever be a resolute defender of the people's interests. We must uphold the important benchmark of "making decisions scientifically," strengthen investigation and research, gain a clear understanding of actual conditions and a thorough grasp of objective laws, so that all decisions more closely conform to reality and to objective laws. We must uphold the practical orientation of "working hard to get real results," bringing forth the real drive to work hard, the bold drive to dare to act, the skillful drive to act wisely, and the persistent drive to act consistently—working hard and striving to strengthen the military, and endeavoring to create achievements that can withstand the tests of practice, the people, and history.

The study and education campaign and the conduct of ideological rectification (思想整风) and the deepening of political rectification and training (政治整训) are consistent in their objectives, content arrangements, and focus of investigation and correction. To advance the study and education campaign to greater depth and more solid results, it is necessary to press forward and tighten up with the spirit of rectification (整风精神) and the standards of rectification and training (整训标准), conscientiously conduct study and discussion, problem investigation and correction, and the establishment of rules and regulations, ensuring that study is of quality, investigation has force, and correction is effective. We must make good use of the "two mirrors" of positive and negative model cases to strengthen education and draw lessons, urging Party member cadres to place themselves in the picture, place their duties in the picture, and place their work in the picture, and resolutely rectify deviations in the outlook on political performance (政绩观偏差) such as blind recklessness, fraud and falsification, passive lying flat (消极躺平), and new officials refusing to deal with old accounts (新官不理旧账). We must clarify which "red lines" must not be crossed, which "bottom lines" must not be breached, and which "minefields" must not be entered. Party organizations at all levels and the broad ranks of Party member cadres must persist in genuinely finding problems and finding genuine problems, carefully sort out and refine the list of problems reflecting deviations in the outlook on political performance, and focus on advancing rectification in an integrated manner together with problems identified through various types of disciplinary inspections and governance campaigns, achieving integrated and grounded results.

Strong organizational leadership is the prerequisite and guarantee for conducting the study and education campaign well. At all levels, the study and education campaign must be placed in an important position and grasped as a major matter. Party committees at the regimental level and above must shoulder primary responsibility; Party committee secretaries and deputy secretaries must fulfill their responsibilities as the first person responsible, and genuinely grasp the study and education campaign tightly and solidly. Precise and categorized guidance must be strengthened, identifying the focus points of the study and education campaign in accordance with the actual characteristics of different levels, types, and sectors, refining specific standards and requirements, and avoiding a "one-size-fits-all" approach. Practical transformation must be advanced, combining the resolution of ideological problems with the resolution of practical problems, and integrating the conduct of the study and education campaign with the work of making a good start to the 14th Five-Year Plan (十五五), concentrating efforts on advancing the hard battle of combat readiness (备战攻坚), breaking through bottlenecks and shortfalls in combat capability building, and strengthening the grassroots and laying foundations—using the solid results of the force's high-quality development to test the effectiveness of the study and education campaign.

Original Chinese
以更高站位、更实举措推动学习教育走深走实 ■解放军报评论员 干事创业，政绩观至关重要。 当前，全党全军正在深入开展树立和践行正确政绩观学习教育。这次学习教育，是今年党的建设的重要任务。习主席对抓好学习教育高度重视，专门作出重要指示，为开展学习教育指明了方向、提供了根本遵循。军队各级党组织和广大党员干部要从理论上和实践上深刻认识政绩观对党、国家和军队事业发展的重要性，切实增强树立和践行正确政绩观的思想自觉和行动自觉。 站位越高，使命感责任感越强，投身学习教育越主动；措施越实，工作态势越强劲有力，学习教育越能取得扎实成果。在“十五五”开局起步之年部署开展学习教育，是党中央和习主席着眼党、国家和军队工作全局，基于形势任务发展变化作出的重大决策，具有特殊政治承载、鲜明时代意蕴、深远战略考量。各级党组织和广大党员干部要充分认清开展学习教育，对于完整准确全面贯彻新发展理念、凝心聚力推进国防和军队建设高质量发展，对于持续推进政治建军、深化政治整训，对于真抓实干打好实现建军一百年奋斗目标攻坚战、不断开创强军兴军新局面，具有重要现实意义和深远历史意义，自觉以更高站位、更实举措推动学习教育走深走实。 学习教育启动以来，全军各级自觉站上政治高位紧抓实抓，加强研究谋划，精心组织实施，认认真真、扎扎实实提高思想认识、整改突出问题、立好制度规矩，深入抓好学习教育各项工作。同时也要清醒看到，少数单位开展学习教育还存在精力投入不够、聚焦问题不深、工作措施不实等问题，迫切需要各级党组织进一步深化思想认识，坚决有力贯彻落实党中央、中央军委和习主席重大决策部署，从严从实抓好学习教育，引导和激励广大党员干部以更加昂扬的斗志、更加务实的作风真抓实干，为人民出政绩、以实干出政绩，聚力提升军队现代化水平和实战能力。 推动学习教育走深走实，关键要紧扣目标要求落细落实。“立党为公、为民造福、科学决策、真抓实干”，是这次学习教育的总要求，必须深刻理解、准确把握、全面落实，将其贯穿学习教育全过程各方面，在学思践悟、细照笃行中干实事、求实效、创实绩。要坚持“立党为公”的政治立场，把党性作为立身、立业、立言、立德的基石，常思常想入党干什么、当兵干什么、为将干什么，以功成不必在我、功成必定有我的责任担当，履职尽责、干事创业；坚持“为民造福”的根本目的，切实把初心记牢、使命扛牢、宗旨守牢，忠实履行为人民扛枪、为人民打仗的神圣职责，永远做人民利益的坚定捍卫者；坚持“科学决策”的重要标尺，加强调查研究，把实际情况摸清、把客观规律摸透，使各项决策更加符合实际、符合规律；坚持“真抓实干”的实践导向，拿出真抓的实劲、敢抓的狠劲、善抓的巧劲、常抓的韧劲，苦干实干、奋斗强军，努力创造经得起实践、人民、历史检验的实绩。 学习教育与开展思想整风、深化政治整训在目标指向、内容安排、查纠重点上是一致的。推动学习教育走深走实，要以整风精神、整训标准抓下去严起来，认真搞好学习研讨、问题查纠、建章立制，确保学有质量、查有力度、改有成效。要用好正反面典型案例“两面镜子”加强教育镜鉴，督促党员干部把自己摆进去、把职责摆进去、把工作摆进去，坚决纠治盲目蛮干、弄虚作假、消极躺平、新官不理旧账等政绩观偏差，搞清楚哪些“红线”不能踩、哪些“底线”不能越、哪些“雷区”不能闯。各级党组织和广大党员干部要坚持真找问题、找真问题，认真梳理细化政绩观偏差问题清单，注重与各类整肃整治发现的问题一体推进整改、一体落地见效。 坚强的组织领导，是搞好学习教育的前提和保证。各级要坚持把学习教育摆上重要位置大事大抓，团级以上党委扛起主体责任，党委书记、副书记履行好第一责任人责任，切实把学习教育抓紧抓实。要加强精准指导、分类指导，针对不同层次、不同类型、不同行业特点实际，找准学习教育着力点，细化具体标准要求，不搞“一刀切”。要推动实践转化，将解决思想问题与解决实际问题结合起来，把开展学习教育与抓好“十五五”开局起步、聚力推动备战攻坚、破解战斗力建设瓶颈短板、抓基层打基础等工作融合起来，以部队高质量发展的实绩检验学习教育成效。
