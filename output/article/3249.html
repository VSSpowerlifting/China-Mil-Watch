About this analysis
Summary
Wang Ji, designated a PLA 'Model of the Era' (时代楷模), is profiled here as a high-assurance software technology (高可信软件技术) researcher who continued working on defense systems after a 2013 accident required high-level amputation and left him wheelchair-bound. The article frames his continued productivity — narrowing his focus to technical breakthroughs and talent cultivation after leaving administrative roles — as the ideological ideal of subordinating personal loss to Party mission and national defense. This is standard political work content; its value is as a record of how PLA institutional media constructs the model-soldier narrative for defense scientists, linking individual sacrifice to 大国重器 (major strategic weapons systems), not as evidence of a specific program or capability development.
Translation

Treating Loss as Gain, Transforming "Subtraction" into "Addition"

— Interpreting "Model of the Era" Wang Ji's View on Gain and Loss

■ Xiao Shuangjian, Li Zhe

"My second life was retrieved from the hands of death by everyone around me. What appears to be the loss of a calamity is in fact the gain of rebirth." This inner monologue of Wang Ji's reflects his view on gain and loss.

The year 2013 was a watershed in Wang Ji's life. Before it, he had been precocious and tireless, with a bright road ahead strewn with flowers; after the calamity, a high-level amputation left him able to move only by wheelchair. A life-and-death ordeal placed "gain" and "loss" in sharp contrast, compelling him to reexamine fate and take the measure of life.

In all things there is both gain and loss. How much is gained? How much is lost? The reckoning between gain and loss depends on a person's faith, convictions, and moral cultivation. Ordinary people weigh gain and loss by advantage and disadvantage, calculating meticulously over immediate fortune and misfortune, honor and disgrace; Communist Party members weigh what matters by Party character (党性), keeping their original aspiration and mission always in mind. What the worldly eye sees as "loss," Wang Ji, through the transformation of ideals and convictions, converts into abundant "gain."

He lost the use of his running legs, yet gained an upright spirit. For an ordinary person, physical impairment often leads to a sinking of the will, but for Wang Ji it became an opportunity for the spirit to grow taller. At the darkest moment of his life, when others lamented and grieved on his behalf, Wang Ji saw "another side": "From now on I can free up more time for research." From that point on, his steps never ceased on the road of scientific research; his mind ranged freely across the field of high-assurance software technology (高可信软件技术). Just as a green pine, though it loses a branch, does not change its uprightness, Wang Ji, though he lost his legs, has kept his soul standing upright on the road of the charge.

He shed the complexity of administrative affairs and gained the focused concentration of deep academic cultivation. In former days, balancing administrative management with scientific research meant his energy was constantly divided among multiple demands. After his grave illness, Wang Ji directed all his energy toward technical breakthroughs and talent cultivation, settling his mind to gnaw on the hardest bones. He even continually applied "subtraction" to his life, reducing the complexity of living to its minimum, solely to obtain purer academic time — reserving all his energy for academic pursuit, forging a shield for the nation, and cultivating talent for war.

He lost the comfort of ordinary life and gained a sense of mission that brooks no delay. A life-and-death ordeal taught Wang Ji to understand the preciousness of life and the value of time. "Since this life was retrieved, I have no right to waste it." Wang Ji does not avoid pain, nor does he beautify suffering, but he refuses to be defined by his deficiencies. He treats every day as a countdown and wrings the maximum value from every minute. The words "as long as my mind works and my hands can still be used, I will not stop" are his most stubborn declaration of war against time — the charge from a wheelchair is equally worthy of acclaim.

Chairman Xi has pointed out that one must correctly regard momentary success and failure, gain and loss: do not become complacent in favorable circumstances, do not lose one's ambition in the face of setbacks, and make both favorable and adverse circumstances into assets of life rather than burdens. On the road of life, there is the joy of success, but difficulties and pressures may also arrive unexpectedly. Whether in favorable or adverse circumstances, both are scenery along the way of life and can be transformed into assets of life. The key lies in what kind of mindset one brings to the world. It is precisely because Wang Ji carries within him a world far larger than himself that those sufferings did not swallow him. In prosperity, no reckless joy; in adversity, no timid despondency. Such understanding comes from identification with one's mission, and also from the vicissitudes of life. Such a spirit has always shone in generation after generation of national defense science and technology workers.

One media outlet offered this assessment: Wang Ji is one person, but he is also a group of people on the military's scientific research front, and even more so, he represents several generations of people dedicated to strengthening and revitalizing the military. Among them, some have fallen in computer rooms, some have come to rest forever on the Gobi. What they lost was personal comfort and even their precious lives; what they won in return was the absolute security of the nation's great strategic weapons (大国重器) and the solid foundation of national rejuvenation.

The charge from the wheelchair continues. Those wheel tracks imprinted in the ground record not only a physical trajectory, but also the most vivid annotation of the word "gain and loss" by a Communist Party member.

把失去当拥有，化“减法”为“加法” ——解读“时代楷模”王戟的得失观 ■肖双剑 李 哲 “我的第二次生命，是大家从死神手里帮我捡回来的。看似劫难之失，实则重生之得。”王戟这句内心独白，折射出他的得失观。 2013年，是王戟人生的一道分水岭。在此之前，少年早慧、步履不停，前路鲜花满径；劫难过后，高位截肢，只能以轮椅代步。一场生死考验，将“得”与“失”置于尖锐对比之中，让他重新审视命运、丈量人生。 凡事皆有得有失。得多少？失几何？得失之间的衡量，取决于个人的信仰信念和道德修养。常人以利弊权衡得失，斤斤计较于眼前的福祸荣辱；共产党人用党性称量轻重，念兹在兹的是初心与使命。世俗眼中的“失”，在王戟这里，经由理想信念转化为丰盈的“得”。 失去奔跑的双腿，却收获了精神的挺立。对于常人而言，身体的残缺，往往会导致意志消沉，但在王戟身上，却是精神拔节的契机。人生至暗时刻，众人替他惋惜扼腕时，王戟却看到了“另一面”：“以后可以腾出更多时间做研究了。”从此，脚步，在科研的道路上从未停歇；思想，在高可信软件技术的领域里纵横驰骋。正如青松虽断枝，却不改其直，王戟虽失去双腿，灵魂却始终挺立在冲锋路上。 卸下行政事务的繁杂，换来深耕学术的专注。昔日兼顾行政管理与科研，精力常常被多方分摊，重疾之后，王戟把全部精力投向技术攻关与人才培育，沉下心啃硬骨头。他甚至不断为人生做“减法”，把生活的复杂度降到最低，只为得到更纯粹的学术时间，把所有精力都留给学术求索、为国铸盾、为战育才。 失去寻常生活的安逸，得到时不我待的使命感。一场生死劫难，让王戟读懂生命的珍贵、时光的宝贵。“既然这条命是捡回来的，就没有资格虚度。”王戟不回避痛苦，更不美化苦难，但他拒绝被缺憾定义。他把每一天都当作倒计时，把每一分钟都用出最大值。那句“只要我的脑子好使，手还能用，我就不会停下来”，是他对时间最倔强的宣战，轮椅上的冲锋同样让人喝彩。 习主席指出，要正确对待一时的成败得失，处优而不养尊，受挫而不短志，使顺境逆境都成为人生的财富而不是人生的包袱。人生路上，有成功的喜悦，困难和压力也可能不期而至。无论顺境逆境，都是生活的风景，都可转化为人生的财富。这里的关键是，以什么样的心态去处世。正是王戟心里装着一个远比自己大的世界，那些苦难才没有吞没他。顺不妄喜，逆不惶馁。这样的感悟，来自使命认同，也来自人生起伏。这样的精神，始终在一代代国防科技工作者身上闪耀。 有媒体这样评价：王戟是一个人，也是军队科研战线的一群人，更是矢志强军兴军的几代人。在他们中间，有人倒在机房，有人长眠于戈壁。他们失去的，是个人安逸乃至宝贵的生命；他们换来的，是大国重器的绝对安全和民族复兴的坚实底座。 轮椅上的冲锋仍在继续，那一道道车辙，印刻下的不仅是物理的轨迹，更是一名共产党员对“得失”二字最生动的注解。
