Forum on Strengthening the Military | Use Every Possible Means to Improve the Quality of Weapons and Equipment

强军论坛丨千方百计提高武器装备质量
PLA Daily (解放军报) 30 May 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
An opinion piece by an author from Unit 63936, published under the CMC Equipment Development Department's professional ethics framework, argues that quality failures in weapons and equipment—illustrated by Argentina's deteriorated munitions in the Falklands War—directly determine battlefield outcomes and must be addressed through full-life-cycle management (全寿命管理), combat-realistic assessment (实战化考核鉴定), and lifelong personal accountability for quality defects. The piece documents how the CMC Equipment Development Department is using a named ethics code—built around six slogans including 廉洁治装 (administering equipment with integrity)—to institutionalize quality culture across the defense industrial workforce ahead of the 2027 centenary army-building target. Its value is as a record of how the PLA frames equipment quality accountability to mid-level acquisition and maintenance personnel, not as evidence of a new procurement policy or a specific capability problem.
Translation

Use Every Possible Means to Improve the Quality of Weapons and Equipment

■ Yang Huan

Without refined implements, one cannot speak of warfare. Chairman Xi has emphasized: "Weapons and equipment are an important hallmark of military modernization, an important foundation for military struggle preparedness, and an important pillar of national security and national rejuvenation." This important statement highlights the profound significance of weapons and equipment, and also makes clear to us that winning future wars cannot be separated from high-quality weapons and equipment.

The professional ethics and conduct standards for the military's equipment domain, promulgated by the Central Military Commission Equipment Development Department, require adherence to common principles of professional conduct: wholehearted dedication to equipment (赤诚为装) and selfless contribution; working hard to advance equipment (实干兴装) and uniting in struggle; pursuing innovation in equipment development (创新谋装) and boldly scaling new heights; strengthening equipment through systems (体系强装) with quality paramount; managing equipment with precision (精细管装) and serving the troops; and administering equipment with integrity (廉洁治装) and conducting oneself with clean hands. The purpose of formulating and practicing these standards is to firmly establish a sense of mission and a consciousness of quality, and to use every possible means to improve the quality of weapons and equipment.

"On the day of battle, if a single implement is not refined, a soldier's life is forfeit." The word "refined" here refers primarily to the quality of equipment. Quality is life; quality is the odds of victory. Quality problems in weapons and equipment exact a price paid in blood, and lead to defeat in battle. In the Falklands War, although Argentine forces had made careful dispositions that drew British warships into an ambush, no one anticipated that Argentine stockpiled ammunition had deteriorated due to poor management and was sent to the battlefield without inspection. As a result, more than half of the bombs and missiles that struck British warships failed to detonate, causing Argentina to lose the initiative and allowing British forces to turn defeat into victory. History has repeatedly proven that quality problems in weapons and equipment bear on the lives of officers and soldiers and on the outcome of wars. In modern warfare in particular, combat is to a great extent a contest of weapons and equipment, and high-technology weapons and equipment are increasingly becoming the decisive factor in achieving victory. We must, with an attitude of responsibility for winning battles, responsibility for combat effectiveness (战斗力), and responsibility for the lives of officers and soldiers, ensure that the quality baseline and the technical and tactical performance baseline of weapons and equipment are upheld.

Comrade Deng Xiaoping once said: "We must absolutely insist on quality first. This issue is very important, especially for military-industrial products. If a few artillery shells fail to fire at a critical moment on the battlefield, it could affect the entire engagement." Modern warfare is a contest of system against system (体系与体系的对抗), a competition of system against system (系统与系统的较量); combat operations place ever greater emphasis on jointness, coordination, and integration. Whether the system as a whole can function normally depends on whether each component can perform its function on time and as intended. Any quality defect in any weapon or piece of equipment may be amplified through coupling effects (耦合效应) within the system, causing serious consequences. We must under no circumstances be careless about quality problems in weapons and equipment, and must absolutely never allow weapons and equipment with quality problems to be fielded by the troops or taken onto the battlefield.

Scientifically sound and efficient management is not only the foundation for weapons and equipment to generate combat effectiveness, but also a powerful means of improving the quality of weapons and equipment and enhancing their effectiveness. We should make sustained efforts to build a modernized management system for weapons and equipment, carry quality requirements through every link in the full-life-cycle management (全寿命管理) of weapons and equipment, and continuously raise the level of specialized, refined, and scientific management in weapons and equipment development. We should work to improve the quality management system, strengthen combat-realistic assessment and evaluation (实战化考核鉴定) of weapons and equipment, do a good job of quality rectification for in-service weapons and equipment, ensure that the quality baseline of weapons and equipment is upheld, and strive to provide an advanced, reliable, practical, and complete national defense science and technology and weapons and equipment system.

"If the method of manufacture is not refined, hold the craftsman accountable." To improve equipment quality, we must improve the accountability system, establish a system of lifelong accountability for quality responsibility, and ensure that authority entails responsibility, that the exercise of authority is subject to supervision, and that violations of law are invariably investigated. The glorious history of the People's Army and the national defense science and technology industry has nurtured a rich quality culture (质量文化). We must give full play to this unique advantage, establish the concept of pursuing excellence, uphold the spirit of scientific truth-seeking, carry forward the work style of being strict, cautious, meticulous, and grounded (严慎细实), inherit the character of selfless dedication, press down quality responsibility at every level and in every area, promote the inheritance and development of quality culture, rectify the ethos of the industry, and provide the critical material support for achieving the centenary goal of the founding of the People's Army (建军一百年奋斗目标).

(Author's unit: Unit 63936)

Original Chinese
千方百计提高武器装备质量 ■杨 欢 器械不精，不可言兵。习主席强调：“武器装备是军队现代化的重要标志，是军事斗争准备的重要基础，是国家安全和民族复兴的重要支撑。”这一重要论述，凸显了武器装备的重大意义，也深刻昭示我们，打赢未来战争离不开高质量的武器装备。 中央军委装备发展部推行的军队装备领域职业道德行为规范要求，要坚守职业行为共性准则，赤诚为装、无私奉献，实干兴装、团结奋斗，创新谋装、勇攀高峰，体系强装、质量至上，精细管装、服务部队，廉洁治装、干净做事。制定和践行这一规范，目的就是树牢使命意识、质量意识，千方百计提高武器装备质量。 “用兵之日，一器不精，即戕一卒之命。”这个“精”，主要指装备的质量。质量就是生命，质量就是胜算。武器装备出了质量问题是要付出血的代价的，是要打败仗的。马岛战争中，尽管阿根廷军队经过周密部署，使英国军舰进入了伏击圈。可谁承想，由于阿军库存的弹药管理不善而变质，又没经检查就运往战场，结果，阿军击中英舰的炸弹、导弹半数以上没有爆炸，从而失去战机，使英军反败为胜。历史一再证明，武器装备的质量问题关系官兵生命、关乎战争胜负。特别是现代战争，很大程度上打的就是武器装备，高技术武器装备越来越成为制胜的关键因素。我们必须以对打赢负责、对战斗力负责、对官兵生命负责的态度，确保武器装备的质量托底、技战术性能托底。 邓小平同志说过：“一定要坚持质量第一。这个问题很重要，特别是军工产品。在战场上关键时刻有几发炮弹打不响，就可能影响整个战斗。”现代战争是体系与体系的对抗、系统与系统的较量，作战行动更加讲究联合性、协调性、整体性，体系能否正常运转，取决于每一个要件能否按时正常发挥作用，任何武器装备的质量缺陷都可能通过系统内部的耦合效应变大，造成严重后果。我们对武器装备质量问题万不可掉以轻心，绝不能让武器装备带着质量问题列装部队、走上战场。 科学高效的管理不仅是武器装备形成战斗力的基础，也是提高武器装备质量、增强武器装备效能的有力手段。我们应在构建武器装备现代化管理体系上下功夫，把质量要求贯彻到武器装备全寿命管理各个环节，不断提高武器装备建设专业化、精细化、科学化管理水平。着力完善质量管理体系，加强武器装备实战化考核鉴定，抓好现役武器装备质量整治，确保武器装备质量托底，努力提供先进、可靠、管用、完备的国防科技和武器装备体系。 “造法不精，责其匠。”提高装备质量，必须健全责任体系，建立质量责任终身追究制度，确保有权必有责、用权受监督、违法必追究。人民军队和国防科技工业的光辉历史，孕育了厚重的质量文化。我们要充分发挥这一独特优势，树立追求卓越的理念，秉持科学求是的精神，弘扬严慎细实的作风，传承无私奉献的品格，压实各方面各层级质量责任，促进质量文化传承发展，纯正行业风气，为实现建军一百年奋斗目标提供关键物质支撑。 （作者单位：63936部队）
