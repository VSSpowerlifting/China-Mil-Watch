Correct Outlook on Political Achievement, Practiced in the Barracks | The Commissar Has an Electric Bicycle

About this analysis
Summary
Yuan Fei, commissar of a support detachment under the Shenyang Joint Logistic Support Center, has logged over 2,000 kilometers on a personal electric bicycle since October 2024, making more than one hundred unannounced visits to dispersed mountain sub-detachments to surface problems — clogged bathhouse showerheads, extreme temperature swings in cave ammunition depots causing rheumatism, and rote political education — and driving Party committee decisions to renovate bathrooms, build physical therapy rooms, and restructure self-directed education (自主教育) into discussion-based formats. The article is standard political work content, its value as a record of how the PLA frames the correct outlook on political achievement (正确政绩观) and the mass line (群众路线) for commissar-level cadres — specifically, that unannounced, unaccompanied grassroots inspection is the institutional ideal against which formal vehicle-and-entourage visits are explicitly criticized as producing official airs (官架子).

The Commissar Has an Electric Bicycle

■ Tao Yongjun, Guo Bing, and PLA Daily Special Correspondent Wang Xuechao

The sun had just crept above the hilltops, and the morning mist had yet to lift from the land of Qi and Lu. Yuan Fei, commissar of a certain support detachment under the Shenyang Joint Logistic Support Center, finished morning exercises and rode his electric bicycle straight toward the No. 1 Sub-detachment's garrison.

No advance notice. No accompanying personnel. Yuan Fei rode alone down a country lane. The air carried the fresh scent of earth and vegetation, and riding into the light breeze, Yuan Fei felt as though he had melted into this beautiful stretch of hills and wilderness.

Before long, Yuan Fei arrived at the building of the No. 1 Sub-detachment. He parked his bicycle, kicked down the stand, and walked straight inside. The sound of rushing water came from the bathroom — officers and soldiers who had just finished a 5-kilometer morning run were washing up. Yuan Fei asked: "Is the water hot enough?"

Recognizing Yuan Fei's voice, several soldiers at the sinks replied: "Good morning, Commissar! The water temperature is just right — and there's hot water around the clock!" Their answer left Yuan Fei feeling quite gratified, and he could not help but recall the scene from his very first visit to the No. 1 Sub-detachment for an investigation.

Illustration: Fang Han

In October 2024, Yuan Fei took up his new post as commissar of the support detachment. The multiple sub-detachments under the detachment are dispersed across the mountains, each located between 3 and 10 kilometers from detachment headquarters; making a full circuit of all the sub-detachments means traveling more than 20 kilometers of mountain roads.

At the outset of his tenure, eager to familiarize himself with each sub-detachment as quickly as possible, Yuan Fei decided to go down to the front line for firsthand investigation, with the No. 1 Sub-detachment as his first stop. That day, before the vehicle carrying Yuan Fei had even entered the No. 1 Sub-detachment's compound, the sub-detachment's backbone cadres — who had been organizing patrol and warehouse inspections — heard the sound of a car and figured that "a leader has arrived," and quickly rushed over to organize the officers and soldiers into a formation to receive him.

During the subsequent investigation, Yuan Fei noticed that the soldiers seemed somewhat ill at ease. He understood: arriving by car had given everyone the impression that he put on official airs (官架子).

The first investigation had not produced the expected results, so Yuan Fei decided to change his approach. In the days that followed, he used the time before breakfast or after dinner to run all the way to the No. 3 Sub-detachment — the nearest one — for his investigations. Inside the garrison, Yuan Fei encountered many officers and soldiers on their morning or evening runs, and he would proactively join their groups, chatting with everyone as they ran together — from running tips to personal development to the sub-detachment's current situation. Before long, he had a clear picture of that sub-detachment's actual conditions.

The other sub-detachments, however, were not only farther from headquarters but also accessible only by rugged mountain roads; running there and back took far too long. Just as Yuan Fei was fretting over this, the sight of local villagers riding electric bicycles gave him a flash of inspiration: electric bicycles are light and fast, allowing him to come and go at will and save a considerable amount of time. And so, on the country lanes around the garrison, there appeared one more camouflage-clad figure on two wheels.

One weekend evening, Yuan Fei rode his newly purchased electric bicycle to the No. 1 Sub-detachment again. This time, no cadres came to meet him, and the garrison was entirely in its "natural state" (原生态). But he noticed something: although it was supposed to be the sub-detachment bathhouse's open hours, hardly anyone had come to bathe. Upon asking, he learned that the bathhouse was some distance from the sub-detachment's building, and every time officers and soldiers went to bathe they had to run back and forth — working up a sweat on the way there in summer, and ending up with ice crystals in their hair in winter.

Moreover, officers and soldiers reported that the hard water at the garrison caused the showerheads to clog frequently, and people would rather fill a washbasin in the bathroom and pour water over themselves than make the trip to the bathhouse. Yuan Fei got back on his bicycle and made a circuit of the other sub-detachments, finding similar situations everywhere.

"The bathhouse was built, yet the officers and soldiers don't want to go bathe — you really can't discover the underlying problem without seeing it for yourself on the ground." After getting a clear picture of the situation, Yuan Fei and the Party committee leadership studied the matter and decided to renovate the bathrooms in each sub-detachment's building into wet-dry separated bathroom suites (干湿分离的卫浴间). At the same time, they adopted suggestions from officers and soldiers and installed filters, motion-sensor lights, and other equipment in the bathrooms.

"Commissar Yuan's down-to-earth work style has given us a deep appreciation that going down to the troops for investigation means not only walking into the grassroots barracks, but also walking into the hearts of officers and soldiers," said the detachment commander. Since Commissar Yuan took office more than a year ago, he has not lit "three fires" (烧"三把火") or thrown "the first three kicks" (踢"头三脚") — instead, he has persisted in riding his bicycle deep into each sub-detachment's front line to find out the real situation, listen to the soldiers' voices, and work out countermeasures.

Last summer, Yuan Fei rode to a mountain warehouse for an investigation. Outside, the sun blazed and the temperature was approaching 40°C, yet inside the cave depot the temperature was only 13°C. Although he had changed into a heavy coat before entering, Yuan Fei still felt a bone-chilling cold. "Going in and out is like switching between fire and ice — it's extremely uncomfortable." Yuan Fei learned that when ammunition receiving and dispatching tasks arose, officers and soldiers going in and out to work simply had no time to change clothes, and over time many had developed rheumatism and arthritis.

Returning from the investigation, Yuan Fei proposed building a physical therapy room for each sub-detachment. The Party committee studied and refined his proposal, deciding to equip the therapy rooms with moxibustion, electrotherapy, cupping, and other devices. After the therapy rooms were built, the support detachment's medics went from sub-detachment to sub-detachment to demonstrate and teach; many soldiers learned common traditional Chinese medicine therapy techniques, enabling them to promptly relieve discomfort and reduce pain.

On one occasion, Yuan Fei rode to a sub-detachment to "push open the door and sit in on a class" (推门听课). Below the podium, the soldiers sat ramrod straight; on the podium, the sub-detachment commander had his head down, "reading" from the lesson plan. After class, Yuan Fei asked the soldiers what they had learned, and many could not answer.

When he then asked the sub-detachment commander, the commander also felt aggrieved: the sub-detachment's support tasks were heavy, and when he organized political education on his own he could only find lesson plans online and read from a script in class — naturally it was hard to resonate with anyone.

After Yuan Fei led the sub-detachment's backbone cadres in studying the problem, he suggested that when sub-detachments subsequently organized self-directed education (自主教育), they should conduct interactive exchanges and topic-based discussions (互动式交流、话题式讨论) around issues that officers and soldiers cared about, with the sub-detachment commander serving as moderator and guiding soldiers to come up to the front and speak their minds and share their reflections. This would both improve the quality and effectiveness of education and reduce the burden on sub-detachment commanders. "This approach works quite well and has already been rolled out across the support detachment. We place no time limit on education sessions, and topics are set independently by each sub-detachment," said Yuan Fei.

Today, in the time outside his office work — after morning exercises, after dinner — Yuan Fei persists in seizing every available moment to ride down to the sub-detachments, take a look around the garrison, have a casual chat, and experience daily life firsthand.

The light bicycle moves silently, reaching straight to the soldiers' hearts (轻骑无声，直抵兵心). Conducting on-site inspections of each sub-detachment's study rooms, gyms, and gymnasiums; experiencing the accommodation conditions in the family-visit quarters (来队家属房)… Yuan Fei brings the problems and difficulties he collects from the front line to Party committee meetings, driving the timely resolution of problems — renovating family-visit quarters, study rooms, and gyms; refurbishing the gymnasium and cinema; helping more than ten officers and soldiers resolve practical difficulties in marriage, relationships, and family matters. The officers and soldiers' drive and spirit for getting things done have grown ever stronger, and they have completed major tasks — including warehouse renovation and multi-service-branch joint storage and supply (多军兵种联储联供) — to a high standard. Last year, the support detachment was commended by the Shenyang Joint Logistic Support Center as an "Advanced Unit in Training Preparation for War" (练兵备战先进单位).

The bicycle frame has grown worn — and the sub-detachments have grown familiar. The tires have worn down — and the officers and soldiers have grown close. Over more than a year in post, Yuan Fei's electric bicycle has logged more than 2,000 kilometers. Calculated at 20 kilometers per round trip, he has made more than one hundred circuits among the sub-detachments.

A Hundred Rides on a Light Bicycle to Resolve Soldiers' Hardships

■ Han Wei

One electric bicycle. More than 2,000 kilometers of mountain roads. More than one hundred passages through deep-mountain garrisons. Yuan Fei, commissar of a certain support detachment under the Shenyang Joint Logistic Support Center, has measured the grassroots reality with his own footsteps and listened to officers' and soldiers' needs with genuine sincerity — a vivid embodiment of what it should look like for Party member cadres to establish and practice a correct outlook on political achievement (正确政绩观).

The reason Commissar Yuan's story commands admiration is that he has cast aside official airs and formulaic routines. When he first took office and conducted investigations by car, what he heard were briefings from written materials and what he saw was "formation reception for inspection" — officers and soldiers were ill at ease, and the real situation was hard to find. When he switched to running and cycling, without the "entourage and fanfare" (前呼后拥), what he got his hands on was the grassroots reality: soldiers' difficulty bathing, the extreme temperature swings in the cave depot, the tedium of political education. One change of "vehicle" stripped away the official airs and brought back genuine feeling; it discarded the preset path and put the mass line (群众路线) into real practice.

A hundred rides on a light bicycle to resolve soldiers' hardships. This news story offers us an insight: a correct outlook on political achievement is not measured by how loudly slogans are shouted or how resolutely determination is declared, but is reflected in "down-to-earth" (接地气) investigation and in the hard work of "resolving soldiers' worries" (解兵忧). It is our hope that more leading cadres will follow Commissar Yuan's example — going in "in body" and also "in heart" (身入又心入), finding real problems and producing real solutions amid the "smell of earth and sweat" (泥土味和汗水味), and truly writing their political achievements into every brick and tile of grassroots construction and into the solid results of preparation for war and combat.

政委有辆电动自行车 ■陶永俊 郭 冰 解放军报特约记者 汪学潮 太阳刚爬上山头，齐鲁大地晨雾还未散尽。出完早操的沈阳联勤保障中心某保障队政委原飞，骑着一辆电动自行车，直奔一分队营区。 没有预先通知，没有随行人员，原飞独自一人骑行在乡间小路上。空气中混合着泥土和草木的清新气息，迎着微风，原飞感觉自己仿佛融入了这片美丽的山野。 不一会儿，原飞来到一分队楼前。他把车子一停、脚撑一支，径直进了楼。卫浴间传来哗哗的水流声，刚结束5公里晨跑的官兵正在洗漱，原飞问道：“水热不热？” 听出原飞的声音，正在洗漱的几名战士说：“政委好！水温很合适，而且24小时都有热水！”战士们的回答，让原飞颇感欣慰，他不禁想起自己第一次来一分队调研时的情景。 插图：方 汉 2024年10月，原飞履新该保障队政委。保障队下设的多个分队分散驻扎在群山间，各分队与队部相距3到10公里不等，去这几个分队转一圈，要走20余公里的山路。 上任之初，为尽快熟悉各分队情况，原飞决定沉到一线调研，第一站就是一分队。那天，原飞乘坐的车子还没进一分队大院，原本正在组织巡逻查库的分队骨干听到有汽车的声音，估摸着“有领导来了”，就迅速赶过来组织官兵列队迎接。 随后调研中，看到战士们有些拘谨，原飞明白因为坐着汽车来调研，让大家觉得他有官架子。 第一次调研没收到预期效果，原飞决定换个方式。随后一段日子，他利用早饭前或晚饭后的时间，一路跑步去距离最近的三分队调研。营区内，原飞遇到不少晨跑或夜跑的官兵，便主动加入他们的队伍，与大家边跑边聊，从跑步经验聊到个人发展、分队现状。很快，他便摸清了这个分队的实际情况。 然而，其他几个分队不仅距离队部较远，而且山路崎岖，跑步往返用时太长。就在原飞为此犯愁时，驻地村民骑电动自行车的身影，让他眼前一亮：电动自行车轻便快捷，可随去随返，能节约不少时间。于是，驻地乡间小路上，便多了一个骑行的迷彩身影。 一个周末的傍晚，原飞骑着新买的电动自行车，再次来到一分队。这次，没有干部迎接，营区一片“原生态”。可他发现，本是分队澡堂开放时间，来洗澡的人却寥寥无几。一问才得知，澡堂离分队楼有点远，官兵每次洗澡都要来回跑，夏天往返一身汗，冬天头发结冰碴。 不仅如此，官兵反映，驻地水质偏硬导致花洒经常出水不畅，大家宁愿在卫生间用脸盆接水冲澡也不愿去澡堂。原飞骑上车，到其他几个分队转了一圈，发现都有类似情况。 “澡堂建好了，官兵却不愿去洗澡，不实地查看还真发现不了背后的问题。”摸清情况后，原飞与党委一班人研究决定，将各分队楼里的卫生间改成干湿分离的卫浴间。同时，他们还采纳官兵建议，在卫浴间加装了过滤器、感应灯等设备。 “原政委的务实作风，让我们深刻认识到，下部队调研，既要走进基层营房，更要走入官兵心房。”该保障队队长说，原政委上任一年多来，没烧“三把火”，也没踢“头三脚”，而是坚持骑车深入各分队一线摸实情、听兵声、研对策。 去年夏天，原飞骑车来到一座山间仓库调研。室外烈日炎炎，气温逼近40℃，可洞库内的温度仅有13℃。虽然进库时换上了大衣，原飞仍感觉寒意刺骨。“一进一出就像冰火两重天，让人非常难受。”原飞了解到，遇到弹药收发任务时，官兵进出作业根本来不及换衣服，时间一长，不少人患上了风湿病和关节炎。 调研归队，原飞提议为每个分队建一个理疗室。党委研究完善了他的提议，决定在理疗室配备艾灸、烤电、拔罐等设备。理疗室建好后，保障队卫生员逐个分队示范教学，不少战士学会了常用中医理疗手法，得以及时消除不适、减轻病痛。 一次，原飞骑车去一个分队“推门听课”。台下，战士们坐得板板正正；台上，分队长埋头“读”教案。课后，原飞问战士们学到了啥，很多人答不上来。 再一问分队长，他也很委屈：分队保障任务重，自己搞教育只能从网上找教案、上课念稿子，自然难以引发共鸣。 原飞带领分队骨干研究后，建议分队后续组织自主教育时，针对官兵关注的问题展开互动式交流、话题式讨论，分队长当主持人，引导战士们上台谈心声、话感悟，既能提升教育质效，也给分队长减了负。“这个办法挺好用，目前已在保障队推广，我们对教育时间不做限制，话题也由各分队自主设置。”原飞说。 如今，办公之余，早操出完，晚饭过后，原飞坚持见缝插针骑车下队，看看营区，唠唠家常，体验日常。 轻骑无声，直抵兵心。到各分队学习室、健身房、体育馆实地查看，在来队家属房体验住宿保障……原飞把从一线收集到的问题和困难带到党委会上，推动问题及时解决——改造家属房、学习室、健身房，翻新体育馆、电影院，为10余名官兵解决婚恋、家庭等方面现实困难。官兵干事创业的精气神也越来越足，高标准完成库房改造、多军兵种联储联供等大项任务。去年，该保障队被沈阳联勤保障中心表彰为“练兵备战先进单位”。 车身变旧了，分队熟悉了；轮胎磨损了，官兵熟络了。履职一年多来，原飞这辆电动自行车的骑行里程已超过2000公里。按单程20公里算，他已经把几个分队转了百余个来回。 轻骑百趟解兵难 ■韩 炜 一辆电动自行车，2000多公里山路，百余次穿行深山营区。沈阳联勤保障中心某保障队政委原飞，用脚步丈量基层实情，用真心倾听官兵诉求，生动诠释了党员干部树立和践行正确政绩观应有的模样。 原政委的故事之所以令人敬佩，在于他摒弃了官气与套路。初上任时，乘车调研，听到的是材料汇报，看到的是“列队迎检”，官兵拘谨、实情难寻；换作跑步、骑车去，没了“前呼后拥”，摸到的是战士洗澡难、洞库温差大、教育枯燥的基层实情。一“车”之变，去掉的是官气，换来的是真情；改掉的是预设路径，走实的是群众路线。 轻骑百趟解兵难。这则新闻启示我们：正确的政绩观，不看口号喊得多响亮、决心表得多坚决，而体现在“接地气”的调研里、“解兵忧”的实干中。希望更多领导干部能像原政委那样，“身入”又“心入”，在“泥土味”和“汗水味”中找到真问题、拿出真办法，把政绩真正写在基层建设的一砖一瓦上，写在备战打仗的扎实成效中。
