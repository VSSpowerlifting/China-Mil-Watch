Military Units at All Levels Advance Concurrent Learning, Inspection, and Rectification from a Higher Standpoint and with More Substantive Measures

军队各级以更高站位更实举措推进边学边查边改
PLA Daily (解放军报) 20 June 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
Units across the PLA and PAP — including formations under the Cyberspace Force, Southern Theater Command Army, Northern Theater Command, Rocket Force, Military Space Force, Information Support Force, Joint Logistic Support Force, Academy of Military Sciences, National University of Defense Technology, Air Force, and a naval frigate — are conducting a campaign on the 正确政绩观 (correct outlook on political achievement), combining ideological study, self-criticism, and concrete rectification measures. The campaign targets a specific institutional problem: cadre incentive distortion, explicitly naming the attitude that 'doing more means more mistakes, doing nothing means no mistakes' as a deviation requiring correction. This is standard political education content; its value is as a cross-institutional record of how the PLA is framing the campaign's scope and the behavioral norms it is trying to enforce, not as evidence of a new policy line or changed operational posture.
Translation

Military Units at All Levels Conduct In-Depth Study and Education on Establishing and Practicing the Correct Outlook on Political Achievement——

Advancing Concurrent Learning, Inspection, and Rectification from a Higher Standpoint and with More Substantive Measures

■ PLA Daily Reporter Yang Lun

Since the launch of the study and education campaign on establishing and practicing the correct outlook on political achievement (正确政绩观), military units at all levels have conducted in-depth study and implementation of the spirit of Chairman Xi's important speeches and important instructions, closely adhering to the goals and requirements of the study and education campaign. They have sustained efforts in study and discussion, tackled problems with genuine resolve in inspection and rectification, and pursued practical results in translating learning into practice, working to convert the fruits of study and education into tangible outcomes that drive the high-quality development of the force.

Conducting In-Depth Study and Comprehension to Correct Ideological Deviations in Values

The party committee of a unit under the Cyberspace Force (网络空间部队) has established a group study and instruction model of "lead-study guidance + two-way exchange + delivering courses to the grassroots." The party committee standing committee's study and discussion findings are distributed to grassroots units; heads of directly subordinate units are arranged to sit in on the party committee's theoretical study center group to exchange learning insights; and outstanding political instructors are organized to prepare and deliver high-quality courses for grassroots units, with focused efforts to enhance the quality and effectiveness of study and discussion.

The party committee of a unit under the Southern Theater Command Army (南部战区陆军) has emphasized leveraging the exemplary and leading role of positive models. It organized party member cadres to watch a documentary on the advanced deeds of Gu Wenchang, arranged visits in batches to the Gu Wenchang Memorial Hall, and invited experts and professors from the Gu Wenchang Cadre Academy to come to the unit to give lectures and guidance. This educated and guided personnel at all levels to identify their own shortcomings by comparing themselves against advanced models, and to consciously establish and practice the correct outlook on political achievement with a strong sense of responsibility and mission.

The party committee of a college under the National University of Defense Technology (国防科技大学) has broadly carried out activities of "ideological analysis and right-versus-wrong discernment (思想剖析、是非辨析)." It organized the broad ranks of party member cadres to conduct ideological analysis around the theme of "establishing and practicing the correct outlook on political achievement and comprehensively advancing the college's high-quality development," and to conduct right-versus-wrong discernment around the notion that "doing more means more mistakes, doing less means fewer mistakes, doing nothing means no mistakes (多干多错、少干少错、不干不错)," investigating and correcting ideological deviations incompatible with duties and mission.

The party committee of a general corps (总队) under the People's Armed Police Force (武警部队) has used both positive and negative model cases to strengthen education and cautionary warnings. It organized party member cadres to earnestly study the correct outlook on political achievement embodied in positive models, and to deeply analyze the nature, harms, causes, and lessons of the deviations in the outlook on political achievement found in negative models, achieving self-examination and self-correction through admonition and awakening.

Upholding Genuine Correction and Real Rectification to Win the Trust of Officers and Soldiers

The party committee of a directly subordinate unit under the Northern Theater Command (北部战区) has, in the course of sorting out and rectifying problems, proactively sought advice, needs, and assessments from officers and soldiers. It established a commanding officer hotline and suggestion box; committee members conducted on-site investigations at duty posts and held face-to-face discussions with officers and soldiers to hear their thoughts and concerns and inquire about their hardships; and it examined and reflected on deviations in the party committee organ's work guidance.

The party committee of a naval frigate (海军某护卫舰) has taken officer and soldier satisfaction as the standard for evaluating the effectiveness of study and education. It convened grassroots opinion consultation meetings, collected messages and replies from officers and soldiers' online mailboxes, understood and grasped the concerns and expectations of officers and soldiers, organized organ departments to study and respond to each item one by one, and drove the resolution of pressing real-world difficulties, making the results of study and education perceptible and tangible to officers and soldiers.

The party committee of a unit under the Joint Logistic Support Force (联勤保障部队) has devoted itself to helping the grassroots resolve difficulties. It formulated a plan for the party committee organ to do practical things for the grassroots, responded to the concerns of officers and soldiers, issued practical handbooks on non-commissioned officer selection and promotion assessments and the implementation of welfare benefits, and made concentrated efforts to resolve practical difficulties such as apartment housing support, schooling for officers' and soldiers' children, and cooking support at small and dispersed posts, stimulating everyone's motivation and vitality to work and forge ahead.

The party committee of a unit under the Academy of Military Sciences (军事科学院) has advanced the rectification and remediation of study and education in a thorough and solid manner. In conjunction with the rectification and discipline campaign (整训整肃), it improved the party committee's deliberation and decision-making rules based on problems identified, formulated and implemented a "moving the timber to establish trust (徙木立信)" checklist for the party committee organ, and drove the resolution of real-world difficulties such as optimizing scientific research conditions and improving living support for grassroots units, using officer and soldier satisfaction to evaluate the results of rectification and remediation.

Emphasizing the Application of Learning to Break Through Development Difficulties

The party committee of an Air Force regiment (空军某团) has continuously realigned the focus of work on combat readiness and warfighting. Centered on "preventing and overcoming work that deviates from actual combat requirements and strays from the combat effectiveness standard (战斗力标准)," it conducted discussions and exchanges, deeply excavated the chronic maladies of peacetime (和平积弊), and strictly corrected problems such as dispersed attention and energy in planning for and attending to war, and unsubstantial training and exercise conduct, driving the conversion of study and education results into quality and effectiveness in military training and combat readiness.

The party committee of a unit under the Rocket Force (火箭军) has persisted in integrating the deep advancement of study and education, the rectification of development concepts, and the promotion of planning and construction. Focused on urgent combat readiness needs, construction priorities, and long-term development, it convened multiple party committee standing committee meetings to study the execution of the "15th Five-Year Plan (十五五)," organized multiple rounds of assessment and demonstration by experts and professional unit backbones, clarified construction tasks in key areas, and charted a development blueprint.

The party committee of a unit under the Military Space Force (军事航天部队) has focused on strengthening the "three front lines (三个一线)" to build the grassroots and lay foundations. It organized training in batches for grassroots party organization secretaries around basic party knowledge, basic party-building regulations, and basic party affairs skills; conducted collective training for front-line troop leaders with emphasis on strengthening the basic skills of understanding the situation, explaining reasoning, providing guidance, and resolving difficulties; and made concentrated efforts to do work that manages fundamentals, benefits the long term, and builds lasting strength.

The party committee of a unit under the Information Support Force (信息支援部队) has kept a close eye on the weak links and shortcomings in grassroots construction to strengthen and improve work guidance. Committee members went deep to the front lines to conduct investigations around the laws governing grassroots unit construction, development goals, and construction approaches; the party committee conducted centralized analysis and research on the state of grassroots construction; formulated and implemented opinions and measures for systematically building the grassroots; and drove the building of strong grassroots units.

军队各级深入开展树立和践行正确政绩观学习教育—— 以更高站位更实举措推进边学边查边改 ■解放军报记者 杨 伦 树立和践行正确政绩观学习教育开展以来，军队各级深入学习贯彻习主席重要讲话和重要指示精神，紧扣学习教育目标要求，持续用力抓学习研讨、动真碰硬抓问题查改、务求实效抓实践转化，着力将学习教育成果转化为推动部队高质量发展的成效。 深入学习领悟 校正价值偏差 网络空间部队某部党委走开“领学辅导+双向交流+送课基层”组学施教模式，党委班子学习研讨成果印发基层部队，安排直属单位主官列席党委理论学习中心组交流学习感悟，组织优秀政治教员备课备讲为基层部队提供精品课程，着力增强学习研讨质效。 南部战区陆军某部党委注重发挥正面典型的示范引领作用，组织党员干部观看谷文昌先进事迹纪录片，分批前往谷文昌纪念馆参观见学，邀请谷文昌干部学院专家教授来队授课辅导，教育引导各级对照先进典型查找差距不足，自觉以强烈的责任感使命感树立和践行正确政绩观。 国防科技大学某学院党委广泛开展“思想剖析、是非辨析”活动，组织广大党员干部围绕“树立和践行正确政绩观、全面推进学院高质量发展”进行思想剖析，围绕“多干多错、少干少错、不干不错”进行是非辨析，查纠与职责使命不相适应的思想偏差。 武警部队某总队党委运用正反面典型案例加强教育警示，组织党员干部认真学习正面典型蕴含的正确政绩观，深刻剖析反面典型政绩观偏差的性质、危害、原因、教训等，在警策警醒中自我省察、自我修正。 坚持真纠实改 务求取信官兵 北部战区某直属单位党委梳理查纠问题主动问计、问需、问效于官兵，开设主官热线和意见箱，班子成员赴值勤点位实地调研、与官兵面对面座谈交流，听所思所想、问冷暖疾苦，检视反思党委机关工作指导上的偏差。 海军某护卫舰党委把官兵满意度作为检验学习教育成效的标准，召开基层意见恳谈会，收集官兵网上信箱留言跟帖，了解掌握官兵关注期盼，组织机关部门逐项研究、逐个答复，推动解决现实急难愁盼，让官兵对学习教育成效可感可及。 联勤保障部队某部党委倾心为基层解难帮困，制订党委机关为基层办实事计划，回应官兵关切，制发军士选晋考核、福利待遇落实实用手册，下力解决公寓住房保障、官兵子女入学、小散点位炊事保障等现实困难，激发大家干事创业的动力活力。 军事科学院某部党委深入扎实推进学习教育整改整治，结合整训整肃发现问题跟进完善党委议事决策规则，制订落实党委机关徙木立信清单，推动解决科研条件优化、基层部队生活保障等现实难题，用官兵满意度检验整改整治成效。 注重学以致用 破解发展难题 空军某团党委不断归正备战打仗工作重心，围绕“防止和克服工作背离实战需求、偏离战斗力标准”开展讨论交流、深挖和平积弊，从严纠治谋战务战心思精力分散、训风演风不实等问题，推动学习教育成效转化为练兵备战质效。 火箭军某部党委坚持把深抓学习教育、端正发展理念、推进规划建设贯通起来，聚焦备战急需、建设重点、长远发展，多次召开党委常委会研究“十五五”规划执行，组织专家和部队专业骨干多轮评估论证，明确重点领域建设任务，绘就发展蓝图。 军事航天部队某部党委聚焦建强“三个一线”抓基层打基础，围绕党的基本知识、党建基本法规、党务基本技能分批组织基层党组织书记培训，突出练强知情、说理、疏导、解难基本功开展一线带兵人集训，下力做好管根本、利长远、增后劲的工作。 信息支援部队某部党委紧盯基层建设短板弱项加强和改进工作指导，班子成员深入一线，围绕基层部队建设规律、发展目标、抓建思路等开展调研，党委集中分析研究基层建设形势，制订落实体系抓建基层的意见措施，推动建设过硬基层。
