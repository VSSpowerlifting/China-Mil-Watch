Political Work

Series Discussion on Studying and Implementing Chairman Xi's Important Expositions on Grassroots Work Methods ① | Making the Strengthening of the Grassroots and Laying Solid Foundations a Long-Term Plan and a Fundamental Strategy for Consolidating the Root

学习贯彻习主席关于基层工作方法的重要论述系列谈①｜把抓基层打基础作为长远之计和固本之策
PLA Daily (解放军报) 4 August 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
The Central Leading Group for Party Building has directed all PLA Party organizations to use two new Xi Jinping study compilations on grassroots work methods as required materials for education on the 正确政绩观 (correct view of political achievement), framing grassroots strengthening as a precondition for meeting the 2027 centenary army-building deadline. The article introduces a named series and rehearses the 'Four Persisting Grasps' framework alongside legacy governance concepts — the Fengqiao Experience, Four Going Down to the Grassroots, and Four Iron/Four Haves standards — as the institutional vocabulary for this campaign. This is standard political work content; its value is as a record of how the CMC is packaging Xi's governance philosophy as a military discipline campaign ahead of the 2027 milestone, not as evidence of a structural change in grassroots policy.
Translation

Recently, the Central Leading Group for Party Building issued a notice requiring Party organizations at all levels to use the Excerpts from Xi Jinping's Expositions on Grassroots Work Methods and the Study Reader on Xi Jinping's Important Expositions on Grassroots Work Methods as important study materials for education on establishing and practicing a correct view of political achievement (正确政绩观). This requires the entire military to earnestly study and implement Chairman Xi's important expositions on grassroots work methods, continuously strengthen grassroots work capabilities, and strive to open up a new landscape for grassroots work in the new era and on the new journey.

Chairman Xi is a leader of a great Party and a great nation who grew up from the grassroots and from among the people. He has always harbored deep affection for the people and possesses profound insight into the laws governing grassroots work. Whether during his years working at the local level or after coming to work at the center, Chairman Xi has consistently valued the grassroots, cared for the grassroots, gone deep into the grassroots, and supported the grassroots. He has persisted in making the strengthening of the grassroots and laying solid foundations a long-term plan and a fundamental strategy for consolidating the root, and has explored and developed a series of grassroots work methods rich in theoretical significance and practical value. The series of important expositions Chairman Xi has delivered on grassroots work methods are far-reaching in their aspirations, rich in content, and profound in thought. They scientifically answer major theoretical and practical questions such as what the grassroots is, how to go deep into the grassroots, and how to serve the grassroots. They have deepened the understanding of the laws governing grassroots work and have pointed the way forward and provided important guidance for doing grassroots work well in the new era and on the new journey.

Whether it is the "Fengqiao Experience" (枫桥经验) of "mass prevention and mass governance, with minor matters not leaving the village, major matters not leaving the township, and contradictions not being escalated upward," or the "Four Entering Ten Thousand Households" (四个万家) activities for maintaining close ties with the people; whether it is the "Eight-Eight Strategy" (八八战略) of "using planning to lead economic and social development," or the comprehensive implementation of the "Four Going Down to the Grassroots" (四下基层) system — over the years, the reason these vibrant practices of grassroots governance have become golden calling cards of "China's Governance" (中国之治) lies precisely in firmly grasping the grassroots foundation, preventing and resolving contradictions and risks at the grassroots level to the greatest extent possible, and forming a work pattern in which each level takes charge of the level below it, doing one's own level well to lead the level below, and placing great emphasis on the grassroots to strengthen the foundation.

Governing the country and securing the nation depends critically on the grassroots; so too does strengthening and building the military. Chairman Xi has emphasized: "No matter how the situation changes, the principle of relying on the grassroots and building the grassroots strong must never be abandoned." Strengthening grassroots building in the new era is an inherent requirement for achieving the centenary goal of army building on schedule and for accelerating the building of the people's military into a world-class military. All work of the armed forces must be implemented by the grassroots; charging into battle on the front line also depends entirely on the grassroots. Without grassroots officers and soldiers, no matter how grand a strategy may be, it cannot be realized, and no matter how advanced the weapons and equipment may be, they cannot be brought into play. Practice has proven that the state of development and the pace of progress of the armed forces are closely linked to the implementation of routine and foundational work. Only by doing solid work on strengthening the grassroots and laying foundations can we consolidate the foundation for strengthening and building the military and expand the forces capable of achieving superiority and victory.

The new journey calls for new accomplishments. In realizing the Party's goal of building a strong military in the new era, the foundation lies in the grassroots and the vitality lies in the grassroots. At present, the deadline for achieving the centenary goal of army building is drawing ever closer, the transformation and development of the armed forces is advancing in depth, and grassroots building in our military is facing many new situations and new changes. From mission and task requirements to the substance of building, from the state of daily operations to the organizational form of the forces, from the composition and structure of officers and soldiers to the external social environment — many new changes have occurred, many new characteristics have emerged, and many new challenges must be confronted. This requires Party organizations at all levels to earnestly study and implement Chairman Xi's important expositions on grassroots work methods, to deeply comprehend the great significance, fundamental requirements, scientific concepts, effective approaches, and fundamental guarantees for doing grassroots work well, to better understand the grassroots, go deep into the grassroots, and serve the grassroots, and to strive to drive comprehensive progress and across-the-board excellence in grassroots building.

Strengthening grassroots building in our military in the new era is where the foundation and the strength for building a strong military lie. Since the 18th National Congress of the Party, all units across the military have resolutely implemented the decisions and deployments of the Party Central Committee, the Central Military Commission, and Chairman Xi, persisting in grasping Party organization to strengthen the grassroots, grasping combat readiness and training to strengthen the grassroots, grasping officers and soldiers as the main body to strengthen the grassroots, and grasping the strict rule of law to strengthen the grassroots, driving substantial progress in grassroots building across our military. The "Four Persisting Grasps" (四个坚持扭住) accurately grasp the characteristics and laws of grassroots building and have seized the key to strengthening grassroots building in our military in the new era in a programmatic way. They should be cherished greatly and adhered to over the long term, and continuously enriched and developed in practice. Party organizations at all levels should strengthen the thinking of strengthening the foundation and consolidating the root, firmly establish the clear orientation of placing great emphasis on the grassroots, persist in placing the center of gravity of work at the grassroots level, lay foundations in a solid manner, repeatedly follow through on implementation, and consolidate the cornerstone of military governance. They should uphold the primacy of the grassroots and the soldier first, fully respect the principal status and pioneering spirit of officers and soldiers, and carry out work with deep feelings for officers and soldiers. They should strengthen and improve the work of guiding and helping to build the grassroots, placing the focus of guidance and assistance on improving the grassroots' capacity for self-building, enhancing the internal driving force and work initiative of the grassroots, and forming a smooth and efficient work pattern for strengthening the grassroots.

Making the strengthening of the grassroots and laying solid foundations a long-term plan and a fundamental strategy for consolidating the root must ultimately be judged by the results of grassroots building. This requires Party organizations at all levels to focus on comprehensively forging a strong grassroots that listens to the Party and follows the Party, a strong grassroots capable of fighting and winning battles, and a strong grassroots with strict discipline and a healthy atmosphere, driving the realization of the Party's goal of building a strong military in the new era down to the grassroots level. They should earnestly implement the Outline for Grassroots Building in the Military (《军队基层建设纲要》), conduct in-depth activities to strive to create "Four Iron" (四铁) advanced units and strive to become "Four Haves" (四有) outstanding individuals, and inspire the broad masses of officers and soldiers to forge ahead on the new journey and render meritorious service in the new era.

Original Chinese
近日，中央党的建设工作领导小组发出通知，要求各级党组织把《习近平关于基层工作方法论述摘编》和《习近平关于基层工作方法的重要论述学习读本》作为树立和践行正确政绩观学习教育的重要学习内容。这要求全军认真学习贯彻习主席关于基层工作方法的重要论述，不断增强基层工作本领，奋力开创新时代新征程基层工作新局面。 习主席是从基层、从群众中成长起来的大党大国领袖，对人民群众始终饱含深情，对基层工作规律洞察深刻。无论是在地方工作期间还是到中央工作以后，习主席始终重视基层、关心基层、深入基层、支持基层，坚持把抓基层打基础作为长远之计和固本之策，探索形成了一系列富有理论意义和实践价值的基层工作方法。习主席围绕基层工作方法发表的一系列重要论述，立意高远，内涵丰富，思想深刻，科学回答了什么是基层、如何深入基层、如何服务基层等重大理论和实践问题，深化了对基层工作的规律性认识，为做好新时代新征程基层工作指明了方向、提供了重要遵循。 无论是“群防群治和小事不出村、大事不出镇、矛盾不上交”的“枫桥经验”，还是密切联系群众的“四个万家”活动；无论是“以规划引领经济社会发展”的“八八战略”，还是全面落实“四下基层”制度……多年来，这些基层治理的火热实践之所以成为“中国之治”的一张张金色名片，就在于牢牢抓住基层基础，最大限度把矛盾风险防范化解在基层，形成一级抓一级、抓好本级带下级、大抓基层强基础的工作格局。 治国安邦，重在基层；强军兴军，同样重在基层。习主席强调：“无论形势如何变化，依靠基层、建强基层这一条永远不能丢。”加强新时代基层建设，是如期实现建军一百年奋斗目标、加快把人民军队建成世界一流军队的必然要求。部队所有工作都要靠基层去落实，在第一线冲锋陷阵也全靠基层，离开基层官兵，再宏伟的战略也不能实现，再先进的武器装备也发挥不了作用。实践证明，部队发展状态、进步幅度都与经常性基础性工作落实息息相关。只有扎实做好抓基层打基础工作，才能夯实强军兴军的根基、壮大制强胜强的力量。 新征程要有新作为。实现党在新时代的强军目标，基础在基层、活力在基层。当前，实现建军一百年奋斗目标后墙节点日益临近，部队转型建设向纵深推进，我军基层建设正面临很多新情况新变化。从使命任务要求到建设内涵，从日常运行状态到部队组织形态，从官兵成分结构到社会外部环境，都发生许多新的变化，呈现许多新的特点，面对许多新的挑战，需要各级党组织认真学习贯彻习主席关于基层工作方法的重要论述，深刻领悟做好基层工作的重大意义、根本要求、科学理念、有效方式、根本保证，更好地认识基层、深入基层、服务基层，努力推动基层建设全面进步、全面过硬。 加强新时代我军基层建设，是强军兴军的根基所在、力量所在。党的十八大以来，全军各单位坚决贯彻党中央、中央军委和习主席决策部署，坚持扭住党的组织抓基层、扭住战备训练抓基层、扭住官兵主体抓基层、扭住厉行法治抓基层，推动我军基层建设取得长足进步。“四个坚持扭住”，准确把握了基层建设的特点规律，提纲挈领地抓住了加强新时代我军基层建设的关键，应当倍加珍惜、长期坚持，在实践中不断丰富和发展。各级党组织应强化强基固本思想，牢固树立大抓基层的鲜明导向，坚持把工作重心放在基层，扎实打基础，反复抓落实，夯实军事治理基石。应坚持基层至上、士兵第一，充分尊重官兵主体地位和首创精神，带着对官兵深厚的感情做工作。应加强和改进指导帮建基层工作，把指导帮建的着力点放在提高基层自建能力上，增强基层内生动力和工作主动性，形成顺畅高效的抓基层工作格局。 把抓基层打基础作为长远之计和固本之策，最终要看基层建设的成效。这就要求各级党组织围绕全面锻造听党话、跟党走的过硬基层，能打仗、打胜仗的过硬基层，法纪严、风气正的过硬基层，推动党在新时代的强军目标落实到基层末端。应认真贯彻《军队基层建设纲要》，深入开展争创“四铁”先进单位、争当“四有”优秀个人活动，激励广大官兵奋进新征程、建功新时代。
