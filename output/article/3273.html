Sharp Words | Denying Historical Guilt Will Bring Complete Reckoning

锐言丨否认历史罪责 必遭彻底清算
PLA Daily (解放军报) 15 August 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
PLA media outlet ran a commentary by Pei Yongzhen on the 81st anniversary of Japan's surrender, cataloguing Imperial Japan's wartime atrocities — the Nanjing Massacre, the 'Three Alls' policy (三光政策), comfort women (慰安妇), and forced labor — and framing Japan's current defense buildup, including its participation in the U.S.-Philippines 'Balikatan' exercise, as 'new-type militarism' (新型军国主义). The piece documents how PLA-affiliated media is using the anniversary to link Japan's wartime record directly to its present rearmament and alliance activities, framing the Balikatan deployment as the first firing of offensive missiles on foreign soil since WWII and casting it as a threat to regional stability. The framing points to a deliberate effort to build a historical-guilt narrative that delegitimizes Japan's security role in the U.S.-led alliance structure, though the article itself is opinion content and does not indicate any change in PLA posture or operational planning.
Translation

Denying Historical Guilt Will Bring Complete Reckoning

■ Pei Yongzhen

Eighty-one years ago, on August 15, Japan announced its acceptance of the Potsdam Declaration and surrendered unconditionally. Today, Japanese right-wing politicians are incessantly distorting settled historical verdicts and attempting to break through the postwar order, while "new-type militarism (新型军国主义)" has grown into a serious menace that gravely threatens the peace and stability of the Asia-Pacific region and the world.

Using rhetorical sleight of hand to beautify acts of aggression is a despicable trick of Japanese militarism. In the first half of the twentieth century, Japanese militarist forces, under the pretext of "liberating the peoples of Asia," wrapped their ambitions in shameless lies such as the "Greater East Asia Holy War" and "co-existence and co-prosperity," and carried out savage aggression against many countries in the Asia-Pacific, committing atrocity upon atrocity.

In China, the Japanese aggressors perpetrated the Nanjing Massacre, in which more than 300,000 people perished; implemented the brutal "Three Alls" policy (三光政策); and openly conducted bacteriological and chemical warfare in violation of international law. In Southeast Asia, they carried out the Manila Massacre and the Sook Ching massacre in Singapore — approximately 100,000 Filipinos were killed within a single month, and tens of thousands of Singapore civilians were murdered. As recorded in the judgment of the International Military Tribunal for the Far East, Japan carried out more than 100 large-scale massacres in Malaysia, Indonesia, Burma, Thailand, and elsewhere.

Beyond the bloody slaughter, the Japanese aggressors also engaged in the mass conscription of forced laborers and the plundering of resources. Japan organized forced labor on a massive scale during the Second World War: tens of thousands of Southeast Asian laborers and Allied prisoners of war were abused and killed during the construction of the Thailand-Burma Railway, and more than four million Chinese laborers conscripted by the Japanese military were killed or injured due to brutal conditions and extreme overwork. The Japanese military even forced women from China, the Korean Peninsula, Southeast Asia, the Netherlands, and other countries and regions to serve as "comfort women" (慰安妇). At the same time, under the banner of the "Greater East Asia Co-Prosperity Sphere," Japan rampaged through Southeast Asian nations, plundering rice, petroleum, coal, tin, rubber, and other resources. The disasters that Japanese militarism inflicted upon Asia-Pacific nations are documented case by case, verifiable item by item.

Yet for the monstrous crimes it committed, Japan has never shown the slightest remorse. Japan's right-wing forces have repeatedly reversed course on historical understanding, scheming with deliberate intent to whitewash the crimes of aggression. They arbitrarily falsify history textbooks, deliberately amplify narratives of victims of the Hiroshima and Nagasaki atomic bombings, deny the legitimacy of the Tokyo Trials, make frequent visits to Yasukuni Shrine, and even use artificial intelligence to fabricate false historical materials. These acts not only deeply wound the feelings of the peoples of the nations that were invaded — they are also a brazen provocation against and serious undermining of the postwar international order and the just outcomes of the Tokyo Trials.

Proceeding in lockstep with the denial of guilt is Japan's headlong rush down the road of military expansion and rearmament. In recent years, Japan has dramatically increased its defense budget, lifted the ban on the exercise of collective self-defense, relaxed restrictions on the export of lethal weapons, pushed to restore the old military rank system, and right-wing politicians have even floated the argument for "independent nuclear possession." The new edition of Japan's Defense White Paper, released earlier this month, emphasizes the need to fundamentally strengthen "defense capabilities" and pursue "new-type operational methods (新型作战方式)" — plans that have long since exceeded what self-defense requires. Japan's dangerous scheme to break free from the constraints of the postwar peace framework is plain for all to see.

What must be vigilantly watched is that Japan now seeks to exploit the alliance system to break the postwar taboo on the use of military force — an intention that is extremely sinister. This year, Japan dispatched Self-Defense Forces to the Philippines to participate in the U.S.-Philippines "Balikatan" joint military exercise — the first large-scale dispatch of combat forces to join a live-action joint exercise since the Second World War, and the first time offensive missiles were fired on foreign soil. The Japanese aggressors who once incurred a blood debt in the Philippines are now, through carefully crafted packaging, attempting to turn the exercise area into a "forward position (前沿阵地)" for stirring up regional tensions. This is a grave insult and threat to the peoples of all nations that were invaded.

Today, more and more people of insight are seeing through Japan's hypocritical face with ever greater clarity. A Japan that refuses to reflect on its crimes and is accelerating its military buildup has become a major hidden danger to regional security. Indonesia this year released a new edition of its history textbooks, changing Japan's wartime "military occupation" to the more critically charged term "colonial rule"; not long ago, Singapore media produced and broadcast the documentary Uncovering Unit 731: Japan's Secret Human Experiments, restoring to view the monstrous crimes of the Japanese military and spreading historical truth.

History must not be distorted; guilt cannot be evaded. All nations and peoples who love peace must maintain the highest vigilance against the reckless actions of Japan's "new-type militarism." This year marks the eightieth anniversary of the opening of the Tokyo Trials. Only by squarely confronting its history of aggression and sincerely reflecting on and repenting for its wrongs can Japan win the trust of its neighbors through concrete actions. If Japan returns to the evil path of militarism, the Chinese people will never accept it, the international community will never permit it, and it can only end in failure — subjected to a complete and just trial and historical reckoning.

否认历史罪责 必遭彻底清算 ■裴永桢 81年前的8月15日，日本宣布接受《波茨坦公告》，无条件投降。而今，日本右翼政客不断篡改历史定论、企图突破战后秩序，“新型军国主义”更是成势为患，已严重威胁亚太地区和世界的和平稳定。 靠话术美化侵略行径是日本军国主义的卑劣伎俩。20世纪上半叶，日本军国主义势力打着“解放亚洲各民族”的幌子，用“大东亚圣战”“共存共荣”等无耻谎言进行包装，对亚太多国实施野蛮侵略，犯下累累暴行。 在中国，日本侵略者制造了遇难者超30万人的南京大屠杀，推行残酷的“三光”政策，还公然实施违反国际公法的细菌战和化学战；在东南亚，制造了马尼拉惨案和新加坡大屠杀，约10万名菲律宾人在一个月内丧生，数以万计的新加坡民众遇害。《远东国际军事法庭判决书》记载，日本在马来西亚、印尼、缅甸、泰国等地制造的大规模屠杀高达100多起。 除了血腥屠戮之外，日本侵略者还大肆强征劳工、掠夺资源。日本在二战中大搞强迫劳动，数万名东南亚劳工和盟军战俘在修建泰缅铁路中遭虐待身亡，逾400万名被日军强征的中国劳工因恶劣条件和超强度劳动伤亡。日军甚至强迫中国、朝鲜半岛、东南亚、荷兰等国家和地区的女性充当“慰安妇”。同时，日本以“大东亚共荣圈”为名，疯狂掠夺东南亚国家的大米、石油、煤、锡、橡胶等物资。日本军国主义带给亚太国家的灾难桩桩有据、件件可考。 对于自身犯下的滔天罪行，日本却始终没有悔改之意。日本右翼势力在历史认知上频开倒车，处心积虑洗白侵略罪行。他们肆意篡改历史教材，刻意渲染广岛、长崎核爆受害者叙事，否认东京审判正当性，频繁参拜靖国神社，甚至利用AI炮制虚假史料……这些行径不仅深深刺痛了被侵略国民众的情感，更是对战后国际秩序和东京审判正义成果的公然挑衅和严重破坏。 与否认罪责一体谋划推动的，是日本在扩军备武道路上的狂飙突进。近年来，日本大幅提升防卫预算，解禁集体自卫权，放宽杀伤性武器出口限制，推动恢复旧时军衔体系，右翼政客甚至抛出“自主拥核”论调。本月初日本公布的新版《防卫白皮书》强调，要从根本上强化“防卫力量”，推行“新型作战方式”，种种规划早已超出自卫所需。日本突破战后和平体制约束的危险图谋昭然若揭。 必须警惕的是，日本如今欲借同盟体系打破战后用兵禁忌，用心极为险恶。日本今年派遣自卫队赴菲律宾参加美菲“肩并肩”联合军演，是二战后首次大规模派遣作战部队加入实战联演，并首次在境外发射进攻型导弹。曾在菲律宾欠下血债的日本侵略者，如今却通过精心包装，妄图将演习地域打造成搅动地区局势的“前沿阵地”。这是对所有被侵略国民众的严重侮辱和威胁。 如今，越来越多的有识之士愈加看清日本的伪善面目，一个拒不反省罪责、加速重整军备的日本，已成为地区安全的重大隐患。印尼今年发布新版历史教科书，将日本二战期间的“军事占领”改为更具批判意义的“殖民统治”；不久前，新加坡媒体制作播出《揭秘731部队：日本的秘密人体实验》纪录片，还原日军滔天罪行，传播历史真相。 历史不容篡改，罪责不容逃避。所有爱好和平的国家及民众都需对日本“新型军国主义”的妄动保持高度警惕。今年是东京审判开庭80周年，日本只有正视侵略历史，真诚反省悔过，才能以实际行动取信邻国。日本若重走军国主义邪路，中国人民不会答应，国际社会不会允许，最终只能以失败告终，遭到彻底的正义审判和历史清算。
