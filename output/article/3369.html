Ensuring High-Quality National Defense and Military Modernization Through High-Quality Political Army-Building

以政治建军高质量保证国防和军队现代化建设高质量
PLA Daily (解放军报) 19 August 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
The Research Center on Xi Jinping's Thought on Strengthening the Military published a full-page doctrinal essay in People's Daily on August 19, 2026, elaborating on Xi's July 30 remarks at the 20th Politburo's 27th collective study session, which foregrounded 政治建军 (political army-building) as the organizing principle for the 15th Five-Year Plan defense modernization cycle and the PLA's self-described 'centenary goal' year. The essay is standard political work content — its value is as a record of how the CMC frames the relationship between Party control, anti-corruption enforcement, and capability development for the officer corps, not as evidence of a new policy line. The explicit acknowledgment that 'operational efficiency of the military system is still not high enough' and that key core technologies face chokepoint risks documents the institutional framing of persistent structural problems, which the essay attributes to insufficient political consciousness rather than organizational or resource failures.

Ensuring High-Quality National Defense and Military Modernization Through High-Quality Political Army-Building (In-Depth Study and Implementation of Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era)

Research Center on Xi Jinping's Thought on Strengthening the Military

On July 30, General Secretary Xi Jinping emphasized at the 27th collective study session of the 20th Central Political Bureau of the Communist Party of China: "Political army-building (政治建军) is the founding principle of the people's army and bears on the direction and fundamental character of national defense and military modernization. We must uphold and strengthen the Party's absolute leadership over the military, comprehensively implement the new-era strategy for political army-building, strengthen theoretical arming, deepen ideological rectification (思想整风), consolidate the ideological foundation that makes officers and soldiers heed the Party and follow the Party, and ensure that the barrel of the gun forever obeys the Party's command." This important formulation profoundly elucidates the special status and role of political army-building, reveals the basic principles by which political army-building leads national defense and military modernization, and elevates our understanding of the laws governing national defense and military modernization to a new height. This year is the inaugural year of the 15th Five-Year Plan and also a year of decisive breakthroughs in achieving the centenary goal of army-building; our military's development is at a critical stage of improving quality and increasing efficiency. The deeper national defense and military modernization advances, the more firmly we must uphold the correct political orientation; the heavier the tasks and the more intertwined the risks, the more we must consolidate the political foundation, brighten the political character, and deepen the political advantage.

Political Army-Building Bears on the Direction and Fundamental Character of National Defense and Military Modernization

Only when one is politically indestructible can development proceed with sure and steady strides. The quality of political army-building is bound up with the nature and purpose of the people's army, with the rise or fall of the cause of military strengthening, and with the long-term stability of the Party and the state. The Party Central Committee, the Central Military Commission (CMC), and Chairman Xi have planned far ahead and made scientific arrangements, advancing national defense and military modernization through political deployment and leading the way onto the path of military strengthening with Chinese characteristics. We must deeply comprehend and fully implement the new-era strategy for political army-building, and establish scientific guidance for high-quality advancement of national defense and military modernization.

The political requirement for ensuring that national defense and military modernization proceeds steadily and reaches far. Chairman Xi has pointed out: "The question of the road is the foremost question bearing on the rise or fall of the Party's cause; the road is the Party's lifeblood." The foundation of political army-building lies in the Party's absolute leadership over the military, which determines the political character and the direction of advance of national defense and military modernization. Over nearly a hundred years, the Party has led the people's army from the "millet plus rifles" of the past, step by step developing into a powerful military that is accelerating the integrated development of mechanization, informatization (信息化), and intelligentization (智能化). It is precisely because our Party has consistently built and controlled the military ideologically and politically that national defense and military modernization has never deviated from its path, changed its course, or altered its character. Today, our military's development is shifting from "how many" in quantity to "how good" in quality, and from "how large" in scale to "how strong" in capability. Whether in setting planning objectives or advancing strategic tasks, we must continue to leverage the unique advantages of political army-building, implement all requirements with high quality, and ensure that national defense and military modernization always strides forward along the correct political direction.

An important treasure (法宝) for responding to the risks and challenges of national defense and military modernization. History has proven that political army-building has always been the distinctive advantage that sets the Party-led people's army apart from other militaries, and is the "ballast stone" with which our military effectively responds to risks and challenges. At present, the once-in-a-century transformation of the world is accelerating, the international situation is turbulent and chaotic, and the instability and uncertainty of our country's security environment are increasing. Faced with external encirclement, containment, and suppression, national defense and military modernization may face the risk of having its progress interrupted; faced with the rapid development of a new round of technological and military revolutions and the profound transformation of the form of warfare, national defense and military modernization confronts the predicament of key core technologies being "choked at the neck," "cut off at the chain," and "walled off." To respond to and resolve these risks and challenges, we must rely on political army-building to grasp the overall situation and trend, boost confidence and morale, and strengthen the spirit of struggle, ensuring that we maintain strategic resolve in a treacherous environment, make sound decisions under conditions of multiple overlapping security threats, seize the window of opportunity amid a multitude of construction tasks, accelerate the pace, and achieve high-quality results.

The fundamental guarantee for resolving prominent problems in national defense and military modernization. Political character is the essential attribute of the military; many problems in military development are connected to political problems and can only be fundamentally resolved by addressing them politically. Since the 18th National Congress of the Party, our military's modernization level and combat readiness capability have improved significantly, and the cause of military strengthening has achieved historic accomplishments and undergone historic transformations. At the same time, we must recognize that the operational efficiency of the military system is still not high enough, and the dynamism and vitality for innovative development are still not sufficient enough—these deep-seated contradictions and problems constraining our military's high-quality development still exist in reality. Many of these contradictions and problems are connected to political problems; if they are not recognized and resolved politically, we will fall into the passive situation of treating symptoms rather than root causes. Only by strengthening political consciousness, enhancing political self-awareness, and improving political judgment (政治判断力), political comprehension (政治领悟力), and political execution (政治执行力) can we fundamentally and at the source resolve problems, making political army-building truly the "golden key" for overcoming difficulties and the "booster" for promoting high-quality development.

Political Army-Building Embodies the Objective Laws of National Defense and Military Modernization

Politics governs direction, governs the soul, and governs fundamentals. Political army-building is our Party's application of its own excellent qualities—qualities unmatched by any other party or political force—to infuse and transform the military, endowing our military with a lofty political mission, advanced political consciousness, and a pure political character, enabling it to withstand any test of wind and waves and to achieve victory after victory. This is the key code to why the Communist Party of China can succeed and why the people's army can win. To advance national defense and military modernization with high quality, we must firmly grasp political army-building as the fundamental, study the principles, and master the laws.

Uphold political guidance to set the course and direction for national defense and military modernization. The people's army is an armed group that executes the Party's political tasks; the foremost task of national defense and military modernization is to answer the fundamental questions of "what banner to raise, what road to take, whose command to follow, and for whom to fight." If the political direction is not firm, the foundation of national defense and military modernization will be unstable. The stronger the political guidance, the more robust national defense and military modernization will be; the more powerful the political guarantee, the more efficient national defense and military modernization will be. At present, great-power competition is intensifying, the struggle in the ideological domain is sharp and complex, and various currents of thought can easily impact officers' and soldiers' cognition and interfere with the layout of development. The more arduous the tasks and the more complex the situation, the more we must uphold, apply, and develop political army-building as an important treasure, ensuring that national defense and military modernization always advances in the direction the Party points.

Uphold ideological soul-forging (铸魂) to unite hearts and pool strength for national defense and military modernization. National defense and military modernization is a systems engineering project, encompassing all aspects including combat systems, military personnel, logistical support, weapons and equipment, and national defense mobilization. It involves many challenges, large investments, long cycles, and severe tests; relying solely on equipment, technology, and institutions cannot resolve problems of value identification, spiritual motivation, and organizational mobilization—it is necessary to rely on strong ideological leadership to unite hearts and pool strength. National defense and military modernization is a contest of technology and even more a contest of hearts and minds; a contest of hardware and even more a contest of faith; a contest of material resources and even more a contest of spirit. High-quality political army-building means forging the soul and cultivating people, strengthening foundations and consolidating fundamentals, and uniting hearts and rallying spirits—using the Party's innovative theories of the new era to unify thinking, using the red bloodline and the culture of military strengthening to inspire the fighting spirit, and using powerful ideological mobilization, model leadership, and mass work to form a powerful combined force of military and civilian hearts united in advancing national defense and military modernization.

Uphold organizational consolidation to lay foundations and empower national defense and military modernization. Chairman Xi has emphasized: "The Party's strength comes from organization; when organization is strong, the military is strong." National defense and military modernization is vast in scale and complex in system; ultimately, achieving all objectives and tasks depends on Party organizations at all levels to lead and advance. In those years, under the centralized and unified leadership of the Party Central Committee, all relevant departments across the country coordinated and cooperated, pooled efforts to tackle key problems, leveraged the advantage of the whole-nation system (举国体制), and maximized the concentration of human, material, and financial resources, successfully developing the "Two Bombs, One Satellite" (两弹一星). This major achievement is a model example of transforming political advantages, organizational advantages, and institutional advantages into advantages for national defense and military modernization under the Party's strong leadership. The facts tell us that the reason the Party's organization has strength is that it possesses political leadership capacity, ideological guidance capacity, mass organizational capacity, and social mobilization capacity, enabling it to make meticulous decisions, broadly mobilize forces, and effectively overcome difficulties. High-quality advancement of national defense and military modernization especially requires using strong organizational leadership to run through the entire development chain, transforming the political capacity of Party organizations at all levels into the capacity to tackle key development challenges.

Uphold the emergence of talent to provide support for national defense and military modernization. People are the core element of combat power and also the decisive factor in national defense and military modernization. Advanced equipment and advanced systems must be mastered by "brave hearts and powerful hands" and driven by a revolutionized and professionalized talent force. Looking back at history, our military's growth from small to large and from weak to strong, and the ever-broadening path of national defense and military modernization, have been made possible by the support of a large contingent of cadres and personnel who are politically strong and professionally excellent. Entering the new era, strictly selecting and appointing the worthy according to the standard of good military cadres, and concentrating efforts on forging a high-quality, specialized new type of military talent capable of shouldering the heavy responsibility of military strengthening, supports national defense and military modernization in advancing triumphantly. High-quality advancement of political army-building must firmly grasp talent as the decisive factor, strengthen the development of a revolutionized and professionalized talent force, and achieve simultaneous and coordinated improvement of political quality and scientific and technological quality.

Uphold discipline rectification (风纪整肃) to purify the ecosystem for national defense and military modernization. Corruption is a stumbling block and obstacle in the development of the Party's and the state's undertakings, and is a malignant tumor that must be excised from national defense and military modernization. There must absolutely be no one in the military who harbors disloyalty toward the Party, and absolutely no place for corrupt elements to hide. Today, the volume of funding invested in national defense and military modernization is growing ever larger, and major engineering projects and high-tech equipment procurement programs are increasing ever more; risks of corruption and security risks are simultaneously compounding. Only when political army-building leads the way and standards are strictly maintained can key links be controlled, ensuring that every yuan of military expenditure, every development project, and every piece of equipment can withstand the test of practice and history. High-quality political army-building requires comprehensively and strictly governing the Party and comprehensively and strictly governing the military, clearing accumulated malpractices through political rectification, regulating the exercise of power through intra-Party oversight, discovering problems through mass oversight, and building a solid defensive line for high-quality advancement of national defense and military modernization.

Political Army-Building Manifests the Distinctive Features and Advantages of National Defense and Military Modernization

Political army-building is the principle and activity by which our Party builds and controls the military ideologically and politically; its starting point is politics, and its implementation also depends on politics—this is our military's distinctive characteristic and unique advantage. Now, the new "three-step" approach to national defense and military modernization is approaching a historic juncture of strategic transition. We must advance political army-building without pause, deeply study and explore practical paths for transforming distinctive features and advantages into development advantages, and continuously open up new vistas for national defense and military modernization.

Strengthen the ideological positioning of political guidance. Ideological soul-forging, as the foundational project of political army-building, is in essence about consolidating officers' and soldiers' faith and conviction and cultivating political loyalty through systematic theoretical arming and value shaping, providing lasting endogenous motivation for national defense and military modernization. In advancing national defense and military modernization, we must strengthen political guidance, more actively and proactively integrate into the great process of Chinese-style modernization (中国式现代化), and ensure that military development goals remain consistently aligned with the Party's goals of struggle; continuously deepen theoretical arming, study and apply well Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era, especially Xi Jinping's Thought on Strengthening the Military (习近平强军思想), and guide officers and soldiers to deeply understand and grasp the Party's basic theories, basic line, and basic strategies, genuinely using the major decisions and deployments of the Party Central Committee and Chairman Xi on advancing modernization to unify thinking and action; strengthen ideological and political education, inspire the drive and boldness to strive for excellence, temper the will and courage to pioneer and innovate, form a rich atmosphere of solidarity and cooperation, and contribute wisdom and strength to high-quality advancement of national defense and military modernization.

Strengthen political self-awareness of the Party leading everything. Chairman Xi has emphasized: "Party, government, military, civilian, and academic; east, west, south, north, and center—the Party leads everything." As reflected in military development, this means ensuring that all work is placed under the unified leadership of the Party committee, and that all important questions are studied and decided by the Party committee. High-quality advancement of national defense and military modernization must leverage the key role of Party organizations at all levels in tackling major tasks, resolving prominent contradictions, and solving development difficulties. Uphold Party control over military affairs, run Party leadership through all aspects of training and war preparation, deeply advance the transformation and upgrading of military training, accelerate the development of advanced combat power, focus on new-domain and new-quality (新域新质) combat forces, progressively build an intelligentized military system, and accelerate national defense science and technology innovation and the conversion of advanced technologies. Uphold Party control over cadres, implement the standard for good military cadres, systematically improve the work of selecting and employing personnel, improve the capacity and level of supply of all types of talent, and achieve a virtuous interaction between talent force development and national defense and military modernization. Uphold Party control over sectors, improve the oversight system for major projects, strengthen civil-military integrated oversight, regulate sector order, consolidate and improve the integrated national strategic system and capabilities, and genuinely transform the advantage of Party leadership into an advantage for national defense and military modernization.

Strengthen rigid constraints for quality improvement and efficiency enhancement. The key to fully leveraging the role of political army-building in high-quality advancement of national defense and military modernization lies in transforming its principles and requirements into institutional mechanisms that ensure high quality in national defense and military modernization. Always treat implementing the system of CMC Chairman responsibility as the highest political requirement to be observed and the highest political discipline to be upheld; strengthen inspection and supervision (巡视巡察), audit oversight, and rectification follow-through, ensuring that wherever Chairman Xi's decisions and deployments on high-quality advancement of national defense and military modernization reach, political oversight follows. Always uphold combat effectiveness as the fundamental orientation and standard of evaluation; improve and refine the indicator system, evaluation system, and oversight system oriented toward actual combat, and use whether it is conducive to improving combat effectiveness to evaluate the quality and effectiveness of national defense and military modernization. Resolutely rectify work styles, enforce discipline, and combat corruption; continuously and deeply advance political rectification and strengthen the rectification and governance of key sectors and fields; strengthen awareness of laws and regulations and strictly advance development according to plans; carry forward fine traditions and uphold the principle of building the military through diligence and thrift; create a sound ecosystem for high-quality advancement of national defense and military modernization; and ensure that all development meets the baseline of quality, capability, and integrity.

Strengthen the sense of responsibility for achieving success through hard work. Hard work is the only path to accomplishing great undertakings and is also an intrinsic requirement for high-quality advancement of national defense and military modernization. National defense and military modernization is an undertaking without precedent; it cannot for a single moment be separated from hard and solid work. We must enhance the sense of responsibility, establish and practice a correct view of political achievements (政绩观), and persist in the spiritual realm of "success need not be achieved in my time" and the historical responsibility of "success will certainly be achieved with my participation," resolutely shouldering the heavy responsibilities entrusted by the Party and the people. Improve the capacity for struggle; strengthen ideological tempering, political training, practical exercise, and professional training; continuously enhance the courage to dare to struggle and the ability to struggle skillfully; be willing to gnaw on hard bones and handle hot potatoes; and through severe and complex struggles, weather storms, broaden experience, strengthen sinews and bones, and grow in ability. Practice a pragmatic work style; carry forward the spirit of hammering nails; persist in seeing one blueprint through to the end; resolutely oppose formalism and bureaucratism; and be resolute actors and hard workers in advancing national defense and military modernization.

以政治建军高质量保证国防和军队现代化建设高质量（深入学习贯彻习近平新时代中国特色社会主义思想） 习近平强军思想研究中心 7月30日，习近平总书记在二十届中共中央政治局第二十七次集体学习时强调：“政治建军是人民军队立军之本，关乎国防和军队现代化建设方向和底色。要坚持和加强党对军队的绝对领导，全面落实新时代政治建军方略，加强理论武装，深化思想整风，打牢官兵听党话、跟党走的思想根基，确保枪杆子永远听党指挥。”这一重要论断，深刻阐明政治建军的特殊地位作用，揭示政治建军引领国防和军队现代化的基本原理，把我们对国防和军队现代化建设的规律性认识提升到新高度。今年是“十五五”开局之年，也是实现建军一百年奋斗目标的攻坚之年，我军建设正处在提质增效的关键阶段。国防和军队现代化建设越是深入发展，越要坚持坚定正确的政治方向；越是任务繁重、风险交织，越要夯实政治根基、擦亮政治底色、厚植政治优势。 政治建军关乎国防和军队现代化建设方向和底色 政治上坚不可摧，建设上才能蹄疾步稳。政治建军质量如何，关系人民军队性质宗旨，关系强军事业兴衰成败，关系党和国家长治久安。党中央、中央军委和习主席深谋远虑、科学运筹，从政治上布局推进国防和军队现代化建设，引领走出中国特色强军之路。我们要深刻领悟、全面贯彻新时代政治建军方略，立起高质量推进国防和军队现代化建设的科学指导。 确保国防和军队现代化行稳致远的政治要求。习主席指出：“道路问题是关系党的事业兴衰成败第一位的问题，道路就是党的生命。”政治建军的根本在于党对军队的绝对领导，这决定了国防和军队现代化建设的政治底色和前进方向。近百年来，党领导人民军队由过去的“小米加步枪”，一步步发展成为加快机械化信息化智能化融合发展的强大军队。正因为我们党始终坚持从思想上政治上建设和掌握军队，国防和军队现代化建设才始终道不偏、路不改、色不变。现在，我军建设发展正从数量“多不多”转向质量“好不好”、从体量“大不大”转向力量“强不强”，无论是规划目标制定，还是战略任务推进，都需要继续发挥政治建军特有优势，高质量落实各项要求，确保国防和军队现代化始终沿着正确政治方向阔步前进。 应对国防和军队现代化风险挑战的重要法宝。历史证明，政治建军始终是党领导的人民军队区别于其他军队的显著优势，是我军有效应对风险挑战的“压舱石”。当前，世界百年变局加速演进，国际形势变乱交织，我国安全形势不稳定性不确定性增大。面对外部的围堵、遏制、打压，国防和军队现代化可能面临进程被打断的风险；面对新一轮科技革命和军事革命迅猛发展、战争形态深刻演变，国防和军队现代化面临关键核心技术被“卡脖子”“断链路”“筑高墙”的困境。应对化解这些风险挑战，要靠政治建军把握大局大势、提振信心士气、增强斗争精神，确保在波谲云诡的环境中保持战略定力，在多种安全威胁叠加的态势下作出科学决策，在千头万绪的建设任务中抢抓窗口期，跑出加速度、建出高质量。 解决国防和军队现代化突出问题的根本保证。政治性是军队的本质属性，军队建设的很多问题都同政治问题相关联，都需要从政治上抓才能得到根本解决。党的十八大以来，我军现代化水平和实战能力显著提升，强军事业取得历史性成就、发生历史性变革。同时要看到，军事系统运行效能还不够高，创新发展的动力活力还不够足等，这些制约我军高质量发展的深层次矛盾和问题还现实存在。这些矛盾问题，很多都同政治问题相关联，如果不从政治上认识和解决，就会陷入头痛医头、脚痛医脚的被动局面。只有强化政治意识、增强政治自觉，提高政治判断力、政治领悟力、政治执行力，才能从根本上源头上解决问题，使政治建军真正成为攻坚克难的“金钥匙”和促进高质量发展的“助推器”。 政治建军蕴含国防和军队现代化建设的客观规律 政治管方向、管灵魂、管根本，政治建军是我们党运用自身具有的其他政党和政治力量无可比拟的优秀特质，对军队进行的贯注和改造，使我军拥有崇高的政治使命、先进的政治觉悟、纯洁的政治本色，能够经受住任何风浪考验、取得一个又一个胜利。这是中国共产党为什么能、人民军队为什么胜的关键密码。高质量推进国防和军队现代化，必须紧紧扭住政治建军这个根本，研机析理、把握规律。 坚持以政治引领为国防和军队现代化领航定向。人民军队是执行党的政治任务的武装集团，国防和军队现代化首要是回答“举什么旗、走什么路、听谁指挥、为谁打仗”等根本问题。政治方向不牢，国防和军队现代化根基就不稳。政治引领越坚强，国防和军队现代化建设越稳健；政治保证越有力，国防和军队现代化建设越高效。当前，大国博弈日趋激烈，意识形态领域斗争尖锐复杂，各种思潮容易冲击官兵认知、干扰建设布局。越是任务艰巨、形势复杂，越要坚持好、运用好、发展好政治建军这个重要法宝，确保国防和军队现代化建设始终沿着党指引的方向前进。 坚持以思想铸魂为国防和军队现代化凝心聚力。国防和军队现代化建设是一个系统工程，涵盖作战体系、军事人才、后勤保障、武器装备、国防动员等各个方面，挑战多、投入大、周期长、考验严，单靠装备、技术、制度无法解决价值认同、精神动力、组织动员等问题，必须靠强有力的思想领导凝心聚力。国防和军队现代化拼技术更拼人心，拼硬件更拼信仰，拼物质更拼精神。高质量政治建军，就是要铸魂育人、强基固本、凝心聚气，用新时代党的创新理论统一思想，用红色血脉、强军文化激发战斗精神，用强有力的思想动员、典型引领、群众工作形成军民一心推进国防和军队现代化建设的强大合力。
