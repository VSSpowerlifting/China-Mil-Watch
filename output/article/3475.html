An Air Force Base Takes Multiple Measures to Accelerate the Rapid Growth of Its Young Technical Cadre Force

空军某基地多措并举助推年轻技术干部队伍快速成长
PLA Daily (解放军报) 24 August 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
An unidentified PLA Air Force base in the Gobi Desert — described as integrating test and evaluation, combat training, scientific and technological innovation, and operational assessment — has built more than 30 science and technology innovation teams, including a post-2000s-led group that independently developed an informatized war-game deduction system (信息化兵棋推演系统) now fielded force-wide. The base's talent pipeline runs from 双一流 doctoral recruits through a structured 'one outline, seven objectives' (一纲七目) mentorship and tiered-cultivation framework, with a research fault-tolerance (科研容错) mechanism designed to lower the career risk of failed experiments. The article documents how the Air Force is institutionalizing the conversion of civilian academic talent into operational R&D capacity at a test-and-evaluation installation, though the base's designation, its parent command, and the specific systems it evaluates remain unidentified.
Translation

An Air Force Base Takes Multiple Measures to Accelerate the Rapid Growth of Its Young Technical Cadre Force — A Contingent of Young Science and Technology Talent Rises in the Desert

PLA Daily report by Tang Jun and reporter Li Jianwen: Not long ago, a reporter entered the training research office of a Navy unit and saw an informatized war-game deduction system (信息化兵棋推演系统) running on an electronic screen. The officer on the podium explaining the system's operating procedures was Chen Pengwei, a post-2000s assistant engineer from an Air Force base stationed in the desert Gobi.

"This system was independently developed by our team and has substantially improved deduction efficiency," Chen Pengwei explained. The team's members have an average age of 32 and include both senior engineers and newly commissioned officers; post-1990s and post-2000s personnel have already become the main force leading key research breakthroughs.

As an important platform through which the Air Force integrates the functions of "test and evaluation, combat training, scientific and technological innovation, and operational assessment," the base has continuously attracted talent from all quarters. One young science and technology cadre voluntarily applied for reassignment and set off for the desert Gobi; Li Zefang, a doctoral graduate from a "double first-class" (双一流) university, gave up a high-paying position in a coastal city to take root on the front lines of equipment testing. "Devote oneself to national defense, forge the sword to serve the country, and let youth shine through shouldering responsibility" — this has become the shared value pursuit of the base's young scientific research personnel.

"Standing at the forefront of combat-capability generation, we focus on forging a contingent of young science and technology talent capable of 'fighting and winning battles,' and we are doing everything in our power to resolve the era's pressing questions of transitioning weapons and equipment toward actual-combat and systems-based employment (实战化、体系化转型)." A base leader explained that, to accelerate the growth of young talent, they have scientifically formulated talent cultivation plans, built a "one outline, seven objectives" (一纲七目) growth-planning pathway covering all categories of talent, and improved mechanisms for mentorship pairing, tiered cultivation, and research fault tolerance (科研容错). The base has also engaged in in-depth cooperation with military academies and scientific research institutes, hosted professional forums, and carried out commendation activities such as selections for "Outstanding Science and Technology Worker" and "Advanced Individual in Digital and Intelligent Development," fully stimulating the innovative vitality of science and technology talent.

After doctoral graduate Ruan Chengwei was assigned to work at the base upon graduation, the base precisely matched his posting to his area of expertise and designated an expert to pair with him as a mentor. With organizational support, he led the development of a certain type of assessment system; after years of sustained effort the work yielded results, and the system has now been promoted for use across the force. In recognition of his outstanding performance, Ruan Chengwei was named an "Individual Model in Practicing the Goal of Strengthening the Military Across the Entire Military" (全军践行强军目标标兵个人) in 2023.

The desert forges the sword and cultivates talent; digital and intelligent (数智) vanguard soldiers bravely shoulder their responsibilities. It is understood that in recent years, with its sights fixed on the demands of future high-end warfare and seizing the opportunity of digital and intelligent (数智化) transformation, the base has incubated more than 30 science and technology innovation teams and high-end talent groups, and has achieved a number of independently innovative results.

Original Chinese
空军某基地多措并举助推年轻技术干部队伍快速成长 大漠崛起青年科技人才方阵 解放军报讯 唐浚、记者李建文报道：前不久，记者走进海军某部训练研究室看到，电子屏幕上，正运行着一套信息化兵棋推演系统，而在台上讲解系统操作流程的军官，是来自驻守在大漠戈壁的空军某基地“00后”助理工程师陈鹏威。 “这套系统是我们团队自主研发的，大幅提升了推演效率。”陈鹏威介绍，他们团队成员平均年龄32岁，既有高级工程师，也有新毕业干部，“90后”“00后”已成为领衔攻关的主力军。 作为空军集“试验鉴定、实战训练、科技创新、作战评估”功能于一体的重要平台，该基地持续吸引各方英才。一名年轻科技干部主动申请选调，奔赴大漠戈壁；某“双一流”高校博士李泽放弃沿海城市高薪职位，扎根装备试验一线。“献身国防、砺剑报国，让青春在挺膺担当中闪光。”这已成为该基地年轻科研人员共同的价值追求。 “身处战斗力生成前沿，我们聚焦锻造‘能打仗、打胜仗’的青年科技人才方阵，全力破解武器装备实战化、体系化转型的时代课题。”该基地领导介绍，为加速青年人才成长，他们科学制订人才培养方案，构建覆盖各类人才的“一纲七目”成长规划路径，健全导师帮带、梯次培养、科研容错等机制。该基地还与军队院校、科研院所深入合作，举办专业论坛，并开展“优秀科技工作者”“数字智能建设先进个人”等评选表彰活动，充分激发科技人才创新活力。 博士阮铖巍毕业后被分配到该基地工作，基地依据其专长精准定岗，并指派专家结对帮带。在组织支持下，他牵头研制某型评估系统，历经数年攻关取得成果，如今该系统已在部队推广使用。因业绩突出，阮铖巍于2023年被评为“全军践行强军目标标兵个人”。 大漠砺剑育英才，数智尖兵勇担当。据了解，近年来，该基地紧盯未来高端战争需求，抢抓数智化转型机遇，孵化出30余个科技创新团队和高端人才群体，取得一批自主创新成果。
