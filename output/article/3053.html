Summary
Senior Sergeant Second Class Lu Lihu, nicknamed 'Lu Ban,' serves as a squad leader in a transport company of an unspecified brigade of the 73rd Group Army and has been designated as the concentrated-training instructor for an upcoming brigade-level transport specialty skills competition. He structured his instruction around an open challenge format, using a live defeat of Private First Class Huang Rong in a jackless tire-change drill as a teaching text to model the bi-xue-gan-bang-chao (比学赶帮超) competitive-learning ethos. Translation

"Lu Ban" Sets Up the Stage — Who Will "Wield the Axe"?

■ Yang Qiran

Lu Lihu (center) explains equipment operating techniques to fellow soldiers. Photo by Ren Zhen.

In a certain transport company of a certain brigade of the 73rd Group Army, Senior Sergeant Second Class Lu Lihu has a resounding nickname — "Lu Ban." For one thing, his surname is Lu and he serves as a squad leader (班长); for another, his mastery of transport support is genuinely something to behold. His comrades started calling him that, and the name spread.

This "Lu Ban" has one defining trait: the bigger the crowd, the more boldly he steps to the front.

One day during field driving drills, the company commander suddenly raised the difficulty: "Today we're running a 'full gauntlet' — driving through a combined obstacle course. Who goes first?"

Before the words had settled, "Lu Ban" stepped forward: "I will!"

"Lu Ban" turned and called back to the group: "Watch my details closely — don't come crying to me if you miss something!" His comrades smiled knowingly. Then the vehicle started moving, and their expressions quickly turned to admiration. The S-curve, the figure-eight, the circular disc section — the transport vehicle threaded through each obstacle section like a butterfly weaving through flowers. "A transport truck driven like a sports car."

"Lu Ban's" role as a model and motivator extends beyond the exercise and training ground.

When the new recruits arrived at the company, many of them found folding their quilts into a perfect block a daunting task. One day, "Lu Ban" spotted a few new soldiers huddled around their quilts, puzzling it out. Without a word, he walked over: "Call everyone together — I'll show you how it's done."

Within minutes, a sharp-edged, perfectly square "block of tofu" had taken shape. More and more new soldiers gathered to watch. "Lu Ban" grew even more energized and went on to share two small tricks for squaring the corners. Everyone crowded around him with questions, and his eyes shone.

Some wondered whether this squad leader was just too fond of showing off. But more people felt that "Lu Ban" charging ahead in everything was a good thing — and that he was genuinely wholehearted toward his comrades.

Not long ago, the brigade organized preparations for a transport specialty skills competition, and the company designated "Lu Ban" as the concentrated-training instructor. On his very first day in the role, he set up a "challenge stage": "You're welcome to challenge me at any time. Whoever beats me can have the instructor's seat!"

The words landed like a thunderclap — soldiers were taken aback: "Isn't he just inviting everyone to 'wield a great axe before Lu Ban' (鲁班门前弄大斧)?" But Private First Class Huang Rong was tempted. Young Huang's training scores were solid, and with the boldness of youth, he figured: we're all carrying one head on two shoulders — who's afraid of whom? He immediately stepped up to challenge "tire change without a jack" (无千斤顶更换轮胎), and onlooking soldiers surged into a circle around them.

The contest began. "Lu Ban's" hands moved fast and steady, clean and decisive, drawing a round of cheers. Young Huang pushed with everything he had to match the speed, but the more he rushed, the more he fumbled, and several critical details were missed.

Young Huang couldn't keep his composure; his ears burned red. "Lu Ban," however, dusted off his hands, pulled Huang over to his side, and waved the crowd in: "Everyone, come here."

Using the just-completed exercise as his text, "Lu Ban" walked through his own operational details: "See that? This way of doing it is better — both efficiency and precision are high." He then went through Young Huang's mistakes one by one: "Look — in the rush for speed, you skipped over these few details, and that's where problems will come from." With that, he ran through the standard movements again from the beginning, breaking down every step and key point until it was thoroughly digested.

Afterward, when Huang Rong spoke of Lu Lihu again, he was completely convinced: Squad Leader Lu may look like he loves the spotlight, but he's genuinely warm-hearted — he can stir up in us the drive to compare, learn, catch up, help each other, and surpass (比学赶帮超).

Over the years, encouraged by Lu Lihu and several other squad leader cadre, more and more soldiers have dared to "wield the axe before Lu Ban," and a cohort of "little Lu Bans" in transport support has been cultivated. Recently, this "Lu Ban" has picked up a new lesson plan, thinking through how to hone his own professional skills to an even sharper edge.

“鲁班”摆擂，谁来“弄斧” ■杨麒冉 鲁立虎（中）给战友讲解器械使用技巧。任震先 摄 在第73集团军某旅某运输连，二级上士鲁立虎有个响当当的外号——“鲁班”。一来他姓鲁，担任班长；二来运输保障这手绝活确实有两下子，战友们叫着叫着就传开了。 这位“鲁班”有个特点：越是人多的地方，越敢冲在前面。 那天场地驾驶训练，连长临时加了难度：“今天咱们搞个‘一条龙’，驾驶通过组合障碍路段，谁先来？” 话音刚落，“鲁班”站了出来：“我来！” “鲁班”回头冲大伙喊，“注意看我细节，错过了可别后悔！”战友们会心一笑，直到车子动起来，大家的神情很快变成了赞叹。S路、8字路、圆饼路……运输车在各个障碍路段之间像穿花蝴蝶，“运输车开出了跑车的感觉”。 “鲁班”的表率带动作用，不只在演训场上。 新兵下连那阵子，很多新兵觉得叠被子是个坎。一天，“鲁班”看见几个新兵围着被子琢磨，二话不说走过去：“把人都叫来，我给你们打个样。” 没几分钟，一块棱角分明的“豆腐块”就成了。围观新兵越来越多，“鲁班”更来劲了，又传授了两个修被角的小技巧。大家围着他问问题，他的眼神里闪着光。 有人觉得这班长是不是太爱显摆了？可更多人觉得，“鲁班”事事冲在前是好事，而且对战友也是真掏心窝子。 前不久，旅里筹备运输专业比武，连队指定“鲁班”担任集训教员。他走马上任头一天就摆了“擂台”：“欢迎随时挑战，谁赢了我，教员的位子就让给他！” 此话一出，战士们都吓了一跳：“这不是让大家‘鲁班门前弄大斧’吗？”可是，上等兵黄荣动了心思。小黄平时训练成绩不错，年轻气盛，心想都是两个肩膀扛一个脑袋，谁怕谁。当即站出来要比“无千斤顶更换轮胎”，看热闹的战友呼啦一下围了一圈。 比试开始。“鲁班”手上动作又快又稳，干净利落，赢来一片叫好。小黄拼尽全力追速度，可越急越乱，好几处关键细节有所疏漏。 小黄脸上挂不住了，耳朵根烧得通红。“鲁班”却拍拍手上的灰，一把把他拽到身边，冲大伙招手：“所有人都过来。” 就着刚才的课目，“鲁班”复述自己的操作细节：“瞧见没，这样操作更好，效率准度都很高。”随即又帮小黄逐个指出问题：“你看，为了追求速度，这几处细节跳过了，容易出问题。”说完，他又把标准动作重新示范一遍，每一个步骤要点都掰开揉碎。 后来再说起鲁立虎，小黄心服口服：鲁班长看似爱出风头，实际上是热心肠，能把我们比学赶帮超的劲头调动起来。 这些年，在鲁立虎等几名班长骨干鼓励下，敢于“班门弄斧”的战士越来越多，带出了一批运输保障的“小鲁班”。最近这位“鲁班”又捧起了新教案，琢磨着怎么把自己的专业技术练得更精湛。
