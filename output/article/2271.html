Officers and Soldiers of the Entire Military Conduct In-Depth Study and Implementation of the Spirit of President Xi Jinping's Important Speech at the Rally Celebrating the 105th Anniversary of the Founding of the Communist Party of China

全军官兵深入学习贯彻习近平主席在庆祝中国共产党成立105周年大会上的重要讲话精神
PLA Daily (解放军报) 9 July 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
MND spokesperson Senior Colonel Chen Xi announced on July 9 that PLA party organizations at all levels have been directed to treat study of Xi Jinping's July 1 speech — delivered at the CCP's 105th founding anniversary rally — as a 'major political task,' using a four-method study framework (word-for-word, in-depth, connected to actual conditions, and under strict supervision). This is standard political work content following a major leadership address; its value is as a baseline record of how the MND frames the transmission of Xi's ideological directives down through the force, specifically the linkage drawn between party loyalty, the 2027 centenary of army building target, and 'combat readiness' as unified institutional obligations.
Translation

On the afternoon of July 9, Senior Colonel Chen Xi, spokesperson for the Ministry of National Defense, released information on recent military-related issues.

Reporter: General Secretary of the CPC Central Committee, President of the State, and Chairman of the Central Military Commission Xi Jinping delivered an important speech at the rally celebrating the 105th anniversary of the founding of the Communist Party of China, emphasizing that a strong country must have a strong military, and only with a strong military can the country be secure. How will the military study and implement the spirit of Chairman Xi's important speech?

Chen Xi: Standing at the strategic height of the overall situation of the Party's and the state's cause of development, Chairman Xi comprehensively reviewed and summarized the glorious journey traveled and the great achievements created by the Communist Party of China in uniting and leading the people of all ethnic groups across the country over the past 105 years, profoundly revealed the outstanding qualities and key secrets behind the Communist Party of China's ability to continuously forge brilliance, put forward explicit requirements of "five musts" (五个必须) for all members of the Communist Party of China on the new journey to strengthen their confidence and carry forward the struggle, and issued a great call to the entire Party and the people of all ethnic groups across the country to strive to create new historical brilliance.

Chairman Xi's important speech has further pointed the direction and provided guidance for achieving the Party's central task on the new journey in the new era. Only by studying more deeply can one act more resolutely. Party organizations at all levels in the military treat the study and implementation of Chairman Xi's important speech as a major political task, persisting in studying it word for word (原原本本学), studying it in depth and detail (深入细致学), studying it in connection with actual conditions (联系实际学), and studying it under strict supervision and solid guidance (严督实导学), all working in the same direction, conducting in-depth study and comprehension of the great significance, rich connotations, and practical requirements of Chairman Xi's important speech, studying and reflecting on Xi Jinping's thought on Party building (习近平党建思想), always unifying thought and action with the spirit of Chairman Xi's important speech and with the major decisions and deployments of the Party Central Committee and Chairman Xi, and serving the Party's historical mission. Officers and soldiers of the entire military persist in taking Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era as their guide, thoroughly implement Xi Jinping's thought on strengthening the military (习近平强军思想), resolutely safeguard the core (坚定维护核心), resolutely obey the Party's command (坚决听党指挥), carry forward the great founding spirit of the Party (伟大建党精神), put into practice the original aspiration and founding mission (初心使命), deeply advance struggle, combat readiness, and construction, effectively fulfill the people's military's mission tasks in the new era, and truly transform the fruits of study into a powerful force of will for forging firm political loyalty, strengthening organizational advantages, and tackling the hard task of the centenary of army building.
