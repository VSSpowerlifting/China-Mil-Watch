Profiles of the Most Beautiful Revolutionary Soldiers of the New Era | Zhou Hongchao, Researcher at a Rocket Force Unit: Climbing the Heights of Combat-Oriented Scientific Research

最美新时代革命军人风采丨火箭军某部研究员周宏潮：科研向战勇攀登
PLA Daily (解放军报) 6 August 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
Zhou Hongchao, a researcher and technical team leader at an unspecified Rocket Force unit, has spent over 30 years on missile weapons research, leading teams through system development, field debris recovery at impact zones, and adversarial exercises where her equipment initially failed before being improved and validated. The profile documents her as a recipient of three first-class and nine second-class military science and technology progress prizes and a Rocket Force-designated '十大砺剑尖兵' (Top Ten Sword-Sharpening Vanguard). This is standard political work content in the 'Most Beautiful Revolutionary Soldiers' series; its value is as a record of how the Rocket Force frames combat-oriented research culture and data discipline for its scientific and technical personnel, not as evidence of a specific capability or program.
Translation

Inside the command tent, a sharp and spirited female officer adjusts her plans in real time based on the battlefield situation. With her support, several missiles break through the "enemy's" pursuit, interception, and blockade to strike their targets with precision. She is Zhou Hongchao, the technical team leader for this exercise and a researcher at a Rocket Force unit. Over more than 30 years of military service, she has kept her sights fixed on innovation and breakthroughs in missile weaponry, leading her team to achieve major scientific research advances. She has won three first-class prizes and nine second-class prizes for military science and technology progress, and has been commended by the Rocket Force as one of its "Top Ten Sword-Sharpening Vanguards" (十大砺剑尖兵).

"As long as it concerns fighting wars, no matter how hard the bone, we must gnaw it down." Assigned the task of building a certain system and confronted with difficulties that exceeded expectations, Zhou Hongchao charged to the front and shouldered the responsibility herself. During the program development phase, she not only held her post and verified the code line by line, but also, whenever time allowed, went deep into each participating research unit to work around the clock alongside the equipment. After a full year, the system successfully passed verification.

Zhou Hongchao has always aimed the sights of her research at the bullseye of actual combat, spending years traveling between laboratories, test ranges, and exercise and training grounds. To obtain accurate test results, she leads her team to missile impact zones at the first opportunity to search for missile debris and craters. Deep in the desert, with wind and sand sweeping all around, everyone carefully examines traces at the scene, meticulously verifies parameters, and lets no detail go unexamined.

During one scientific research results appraisal, Zhou Hongchao discovered that certain data contained errors and that the labeling on individual charts was not sufficiently standardized. She immediately pointed it out: "Research conduct is directly connected to combat effectiveness. A moment's carelessness in peacetime can bring risks and hidden dangers in war." This rigorous work style has had a deep influence on team members. Everyone cross-checked item by item and verified chart by chart to ensure that all data were precise and all content was up to standard.

The road of scientific research holds not only the joy of success, but also the torment that follows failure. On one occasion, a certain type of equipment developed by the team she led suffered consecutive failures during adversarial exercises, drawing some doubts and criticism. As the technical lead, she bore enormous pressure, leading the team in earnest study and sustained effort. After nearly a year of improvements and upgrades, the equipment ultimately demonstrated its full power on the exercise ground.

Facing new circumstances and missions, Zhou Hongchao's gaze is resolute: "Launch new charges against one technical difficulty after another, and strive to write an outstanding answer sheet in the practice of strengthening the military through science and technology."

Original Chinese
中军帐内，一名英姿飒爽的女军官，依据战场态势及时调整方案。在她的助力下，数枚导弹突破“敌”围追堵截，精准命中目标。 她就是此次演练技术组长、火箭军某部研究员周宏潮。入伍30多年，她始终瞄准导弹武器创新攻关，带领团队取得重大科研突破，获军队科技进步一等奖3项、二等奖9项，被火箭军表彰为“十大砺剑尖兵”。 “只要事关打仗的事，再硬的骨头也要啃下来。”受领某系统建设任务，面对超出预期的难度，周宏潮冲锋在前、担当在肩。程序开发阶段，她不仅坚守工位逐行校验代码，而且一有时间就深入各参研单位，跟着设备连轴转。历时一年，该系统成功通过校验。 周宏潮始终把科研准星瞄准实战靶心，常年奔波于实验室、试验场和演训场。为获取准确试验结果，她第一时间带领团队赶往导弹落区寻找弹骸、弹坑。大漠深处风沙漫卷，大家认真查看现场痕迹，细致核验参数，不放过任何细节。 一次参加科研成果鉴定，周宏潮发现有些数据存在误差、个别图表标注不够规范，当即指出：“科研作风连着战斗力，平时的一丁点疏忽，打仗时就可能带来风险隐患。”这种严谨的作风，深深影响着团队成员。大家逐项核对、逐图校验，确保所有数据精准、内容规范。 科研路上并非只有成功的喜悦，也有失利后的煎熬。一次，她带领团队研制的某型装备，在对抗演练中接连失败，引来一些质疑。作为技术负责人，她承受着巨大压力，带领团队认真钻研、持续奋战。历时近一年的改进提升，最终该型装备在演练场上一展雄风。 面对新的形势和任务，周宏潮的眼神坚定：“向着一个个技术难关发起新的冲锋，努力在科技强军实践中书写优异答卷。”
