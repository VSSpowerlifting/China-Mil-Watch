Outlook · First Study | Raising the People's Army's Capability to Fight and Win

瞭望·第一学习丨提高人民军队打赢能力

About this analysis
Summary
An essay by the Xi Jinping Thought on Strengthening the Military Research Center, published in Outlook (瞭望) Issue 31 of 2026, lays out the doctrinal and capability priorities embedded in the 15th Five-Year Plan outline, centering on combat-realistic training (实战化军事训练), the development of unmanned intelligent combat forces (无人智能作战力量), and the construction of an intelligentized military system (智能化军事体系) that shifts command from 'experience-based' to 'data-driven' (数据驱动型指挥). The piece documents the PLA's current framing of the victory mechanism in intelligentized warfare—'detection equals destruction' (发现即摧毁), algorithm-based advantage, and system emergence (体系涌现)—as the conceptual baseline against which training, force structure, and operational method innovation are now being benchmarked. The 2027 centenary deadline is invoked explicitly as the mobilizing horizon, framing full-time combat readiness as the institutional standard the force is expected to meet.

Solidly Advancing Combat-Realistic Military Training (实战化军事训练)

Military training is the basic means of generating and improving combat capability and is the most direct form of military struggle preparation. To this end, the 15th Five-Year Plan outline makes explicit arrangements to solidly advance combat-realistic military training.

Solidly advancing combat-realistic military training goes directly to the fundamental requirements of training, making clear that training must be oriented toward the battlefield, toward the force, and toward the future—soldiers must train the way they fight, train hard in whatever combat requires, and concentrate intensive training on whatever the force lacks most.

— Train command, forging the powerful brain that decides future victory. At present, the form of warfare is accelerating its evolution toward informatization and intelligentization, and the weight of "ten thousand troops looking to command" (千军万马看指挥) is becoming ever more pronounced. Looking back at several recent local wars, the decisive factor has often been not merely the density of firepower but who first cuts the other side's command chain.

Therefore, training command means putting commanders into complex electromagnetic environments to "smell the smoke of battle," into multi-service joint live scenarios to feel the time differential, and into adversarial exercises to think about how to remedy shortcomings. Training command means training both the decisiveness to make up one's mind and the flexibility to make real-time adjustments—especially under the intense pressure of severed communications and chaotic intelligence—so that the ability to plan well and decide wisely is engraved into commanders' neural reflexes. Under the new situation, commanders must also be adept at using artificial intelligence and other decision-support tools to achieve a profound shift from "experience-based command" (经验型指挥) to "data-driven command" (数据驱动型指挥).

— Train coordination, forging the iron fist of the system that wins through jointness. Victory and defeat in war depend not on numbers but on separation and combination. Modern warfare is always joint, and there is no victory without jointness; integrated joint operations (一体化联合作战) have become the basic form of operations, with platform-based combat, system support, tactical action, and strategic sustainment as defining characteristics.

At present, following the formation of the new pattern in which the Central Military Commission (CMC) exercises overall control, the theater commands (战区) are responsible for warfighting, and the services are responsible for building, the joint logistics support and joint operations command organizations have successively taken their positions. The key is to truly implement the word "joint" in training—to drive the Army, Navy, Air Force, Rocket Force, and Joint Logistic Support Force to find their respective interfaces and rhythms within the large joint training system, achieving "shared bloodlines" (血脉相通) among the services. For example, intensifying coordinated training among the services, advancing deep coupling of combat and training, and improving joint command, joint operations, and joint support capabilities. In recent years, a Navy brigade assembled multiple services to go to sea for training, the Air Force conducted base-level adversarial exercises with each side serving as the other's opponent, and the Information Support Force invited neighboring units to serve as examiners—"open-door training" (开门练兵) has achieved good results.

— Train the spirit, tempering the blood and courage (血性胆魄) to dare to fight and be certain of victory. Facing informatized and intelligentized warfare, long-range precision strikes, cognitive offense and defense, and sustained attrition place greater demands on the will and resilience of officers and soldiers than ever before. For example, unmanned aerial vehicle operators staring at screens for more than ten consecutive hours, electronic countermeasure units against unmanned systems operating blind under heavy jamming, and submarine officers and soldiers enduring long periods in sealed compartments—all require combat spirit.

Chairman Xi Jinping has pointed out that forces must be tempered and trained through arduous and rigorous training, in near-combat environments, and in severe and complex military struggles, guiding officers and soldiers to firm up their ideals and convictions, hone their combat will, and forge their combat style, always maintaining the tenacious combat spirit of "fearing neither hardship nor death" (一不怕苦、二不怕死).

Training combat spirit means placing forces in perilous situations, dangerous situations, changing situations, and difficult situations—on high plateaus and in cold regions, in distant seas and great oceans, in the Gobi Desert—and tempering and training them under conditions of high heat, extreme cold, high humidity, oxygen deprivation, fatigue, and hunger, so as to truly achieve both "daring to charge" and "fighting skillfully," binding combat spirit and the capability to fight and win firmly together.

Accelerating the Development of Advanced Combat Capabilities

Science and technology is the most active and revolutionary factor in military development; every major scientific and technological advance and innovation brings about profound changes in the form of warfare and the mode of operations. Modern military competition is to a large extent a contest of "time differentials" (时间差): whoever first transforms the technology in hand into systematized combat capability holds the entry ticket to the next war. To this end, the 15th Five-Year Plan outline proposes accelerating the development of advanced combat capabilities.

Accelerating the development of advanced combat capabilities has become an inevitable measure for seizing the commanding heights of future warfare and achieving a leapfrog improvement in our military's combat capability.

— Better enabling combat capability development in all respects through scientific and technological innovation. In intense military competition, key core technologies cannot be obtained by asking, buying, or begging. Only by persisting in self-reliance, proceeding from national and military conditions, and vigorously advancing independent innovation and original innovation can we achieve more "zero-to-one" (从0到1) breakthroughs and create growth poles for new-quality combat capabilities (新质战斗力).

Therefore, we must unswervingly place scientific and technological innovation in a prominent position in building and strengthening the military. We must improve strategic perception of trends in military science and technology development—when disruptive frontier technologies are still in their embryonic stage, we must make sharp judgments, seize them resolutely, and continuously develop them, so as to stay one step ahead of the adversary and seize the initiative. We must fully leverage the institutional advantage of our country's socialist system in concentrating resources to accomplish major tasks, integrating high-quality resources from all parties to form a powerful synergy for collaborative breakthroughs. We must greatly improve the capability for operational application, resolutely bridge the "last mile" from scientific research to the battlefield, closely unify static laboratory technology with dynamic battlefield application, and ensure that the fruits of scientific and technological innovation enter the training ground, are embedded in operational formations, and permeate the system of operational methods (战法体系) at the earliest opportunity.

— Growing and strengthening new-type combat forces such as unmanned intelligent systems. The report of the 20th National Congress of the Party emphasized "increasing the proportion of new-domain new-quality combat forces (新域新质作战力量), accelerating the development of unmanned intelligent combat forces (无人智能作战力量), and coordinating the development and use of the cyber-information system (网络信息体系)"—this is a forward-looking deployment oriented toward the future. In multiple wars in recent years, unmanned aerial vehicles have shifted from supporting roles to leading roles. First-person-view (FPV) unmanned aerial vehicles guided by fiber optic cable, costing only a few thousand dollars, can destroy armor worth millions of dollars. Cyberspace and the electromagnetic domain have shifted from a support domain to a primary combat domain, and the cognitive domain has become a new battlefield for subduing the enemy without fighting.

Therefore, we must advance the scaled, combat-realistic, and systematized development of new-domain new-quality combat forces, accelerate the development of unmanned intelligent combat forces and countermeasure capabilities, and strengthen the upgrading and transformation of traditional combat forces. Unmanned intelligent combat forces should be integrated into the operational system in the posture of a main force and leading actor: give them a position in the organizational structure, give them opponents in training, and give them scenarios in operational methods. Strengthen strategic pre-positioning for operations in new domains such as cyberspace, deep space, and the deep sea to enhance strategic initiative. Persist in using the new to lead the old and integrating new and old—be adept at using the penetration capability of unmanned swarms to open gaps for conventional firepower, and adept at using cyber-electromagnetic disruption to pave the way for long-range precision strikes, while strengthening cognitive offense and defense capabilities.

— Accelerating the development of an agile and efficient intelligentized military system. If advanced weapons and equipment and new-quality combat forces are the sharp spear and solid shield, then the intelligentized military system is the operating system that commands these assets and forces. In today's world, intelligentization has become the theme of the new round of military revolution, and warfare competition has entered a new stage of algorithm-based victory (算法制胜). Whoever first builds an intelligentized military system will achieve faster operational decision-making, more accurate situational awareness, more optimal force allocation, and higher offensive and defensive effectiveness, thereby seizing the initiative on the future battlefield.

Accelerating the development of an intelligentized military system with the characteristics of our military requires emphasis on systemic thinking and exerting effort at multiple levels.

At the perception level, focus on building an intelligent intelligence reconnaissance system, using artificial intelligence technology to achieve automatic collection, intelligent analysis, and precise delivery of massive amounts of information, advancing battlefield situational awareness from "seeing" to "understanding."

At the decision-making level, focus on developing intelligent command-and-control and operational decision-making systems, converting algorithmic advantage into decision-making advantage, and achieving a leap from human-brain decision-making to "human-machine collaborative decision-making" (人机协同决策).

At the action level, focus on building an intelligent offense-defense and support system, forming a complete intelligentized operational chain encompassing perception, decision-making, offense and defense, and support.

At the comprehensive level, focus on improving institutional norms, talent systems, and training mechanisms, coordinating intelligentized technology research and development, equipment iteration, and operational method innovation, and effectively resolving problems such as poor integration of intelligent systems, insufficient algorithm adaptation, and weak operational effectiveness.

Profoundly Grasping the Laws and Characteristics of Modern Warfare

At present, the form of warfare and the mode of operations in the new era are undergoing disruptive changes. "Overall, modern warfare has indeed undergone profound changes. These changes may appear dazzling, but there are laws to be found behind them; the fundamental change is that the mechanism for achieving victory in war (战争的制胜机理) has changed." Chairman Xi Jinping has pointed this out. Therefore, the report of the 20th National Congress of the Party made explicit arrangements for "studying and mastering the characteristics and laws of informatized and intelligentized warfare." The 15th Five-Year Plan outline proposes accelerating the integrated development of mechanization, informatization, and intelligentization (机械化信息化智能化融合发展).

Only by clarifying what wars will be fought in the future and how they will be fought can we anchor the logical foundation of war preparation and identify the correct direction for combat capability development.

— Advancing with the times to innovate military strategic guidance. As the top-level design for war preparation and war conduct, military strategy that is scientifically accurate represents the greatest probability of success.

Today, the temporal and spatial attributes of warfare have long surpassed traditional understanding; military strategy must change as circumstances change, examining and planning for war with an entirely new perspective.

First, closely follow the major trends of the era with keen perception. As the group breakthroughs of emerging scientific and technological achievements redraw the face of warfare, adversaries' operational thinking and tactical forms continue to evolve and iterate, and the form of warfare is leaping from the physical domain to the information domain and cognitive domain. Only by closely tracking these deep-level changes can we formulate strategic guidance that conforms to objective reality.

Second, persist in and develop "you fight your way, I fight my way" (你打你的、我打我的), be adept at leveraging comparative advantages, and seek strategies for defeating the enemy through asymmetric competition.

Third, better combine the profound power of people's war (人民战争) with the advantages of modern technology. Although informatized and intelligentized warfare has a high technological threshold, justice remains the political logic that determines victory or defeat; we must be adept at using new technologies and new means to empower people's war, so that this traditional advantage radiates new vitality in the new era.

— Focus on thoroughly researching the mechanism for achieving victory in intelligentized warfare. The mechanism for achieving victory is the internal, determinative operational law and causal relationship of warfare; failing to understand the mechanism for achieving victory will cause one to lose direction. The key to winning mechanized warfare lies in destroying the adversary with physical energy; the key to winning informatized warfare lies in paralyzing the enemy's system through precision strikes. Several recent local wars indicate that penetrating the secrets of victory in future warfare requires grasping the underlying laws—knowing not only what is so but also why it is so—to provide solid theoretical support for operational method innovation.

Therefore, we must profoundly understand the leading role of information, recognizing that information is the core element running through modern warfare—whoever possesses real-time, precise, and comprehensive information superiority will seize the initiative on the battlefield. We must profoundly comprehend the effectiveness of "system emergence" (体系涌现), recognizing that modern warfare, under the powerful support of network systems, involves the resonance and synergistic release of information flows, energy flows, material flows, and cognitive flows, with victory and defeat increasingly dependent on the power of system emergence. We must profoundly grasp the "ubiquitous intelligence" (智能泛在) effect, recognizing that artificial intelligence technology endows operational systems with an unprecedented powerful intelligent brain, and that this empowerment is making operational actions more precise and swift. We must pay close attention to the decisive significance of speed, recognizing that in intelligentized warfare "detection equals destruction" (发现即摧毁) has become the norm—whoever can preempt the enemy within the "observe-orient-decide-act" (观察—判断—决策—行动) closed loop will gain battlefield advantage.

— Strengthening the normalized and diversified employment of military forces. Since the 18th National Congress of the Party, our military has innovated the mode of military force employment, achieving a historic leap from "raising troops for a thousand days, using them for a single moment" (养兵千日、用兵一时) to "raising troops for a thousand days, using them for a thousand days" (养兵千日、用兵千日). The People's Army, closely aligned with its mission tasks, has optimized its strategic posture and force disposition, driving a profound transformation in force employment from passive response to active shaping, from phased tasks to normalized operations, and from single-service actions to joint operational system employment, effectively expanding the temporal and spatial dimensions of military struggle.

Looking to the future, we must better leverage our military's strategic functions in shaping the situation, managing crises, deterring conflict, and winning wars.

Dialectically unify the training of troops with the employment of troops. The understanding that training is actual combat and that missions are the test is becoming a universal consensus and operational standard throughout the military; through the dynamic process of executing diversified tasks, we should better verify training effectiveness, expose problems and shortcomings, and improve operational capability in real time.

Combine military struggle with strategic deterrence. Through normalized combat patrols, maritime patrols, distant-sea joint exercises and training, and other actions, clearly demonstrate the resolve to defend core interests, convey credible and reliable deterrence signals, and shape a strategic posture favorable to us.

Link single-direction actions with overall systemic employment. Strengthen the coordinated linkage of actions across strategic directions and military domains, flexibly employing diverse forms such as rights-protection escort, combat patrols, maritime patrols, and distant-sea exercises and training, forming a systemic combined force that integrates near and far and combines offense and defense.

A strong nation must have a strong military. In 2027, the Chinese People's Liberation Army will mark the centenary of its founding. Meeting the requirements of the Party and the people, comprehensively improving the capability to fight and win, and achieving full-time combat readiness and the ability to fight at any moment—this is the finest tribute this glorious and heroic force can offer.

因此，必须坚定不移把科技创新摆在强军兴军突出位置。提高对军事科技发展趋势的战略感知力，当颠覆性前沿科技还处在萌芽期时，就敏锐地判断、坚定地抓住、持续地壮大，做到先敌一筹、快人一步、占据主动。充分发挥我国社会主义集中力量办大事的制度优势，整合各方优质资源，形成协同攻关的强大合力。大力提高实战运用力，坚决打通从科研到战场的“最后一公里”，把静态的实验室技术与动态的战场应用紧密统一起来，让科技创新成果第一时间进入训练场、嵌入作战编成，渗透到战法体系之中。 ——做大做强无人智能等新型作战力量。党的二十大报告强调“增加新域新质作战力量比重，加快无人智能作战力量发展，统筹网络信息体系建设运用”，这是面向未来的前瞻部署。近年来的多场战争中，无人机从配角变成主角。使用光纤引导的第一视角无人机（FPV），成本仅几千美元，却能摧毁几百万美元的装甲。网电空间从支援域变成主战域，认知域更成为不战而屈人之兵的新战场。 因此，必须推进新域新质作战力量规模化、实战化、体系化发展，加快无人智能作战力量及反制能力建设，加强传统作战力量升级改造。无人智能作战力量应以主力和主角的姿态融入作战体系：在编制上给它位置，在训练上给它对手，在战法上给它场景。强化网络空间、深空、深海等新域作战的战略预置，以增强战略主动。坚持以新带旧、新老融合，既善用无人集群的突防能力给常规火力开缺口，又善用网电破击为远程精确打击作铺垫，强化认知攻防能力。 ——加快建设敏捷高效的智能化军事体系。如果说先进武器装备和新质作战力量是锋利的矛和坚固的盾，那么智能化军事体系就是驾驭这些装备和力量的操作系统。当今世界，智能化成为新一轮军事革命主题、战争博弈进入算法制胜新阶段。谁先建成智能化军事体系，谁就能实现作战决策更快、态势感知更准、力量调配更优、攻防效能更高，掌握未来战场主动权。 加快建设有我军特色的智能化军事体系，重在坚持体系思维，在多个层面发力。感知层面，着力构建智能情报侦察体系，利用人工智能技术实现海量信息自动采集、智能分析和精准推送，使战场态势从看得见走向看得懂。 决策层面，着力发展智能指挥控制与作战决策系统，将算法优势转化为决策优势，实现从人脑决策到“人机协同决策”的跨越。 行动层面，着力打造智能攻防与保障体系，形成包含感知、决策、攻防、保障的完整智能化作战链条。 综合层面，着力完善制度规范、人才体系和训练机制，统筹智能化技术研发、装备迭代、战法创新，有效解决智能体系融合不畅、算法适配不足和实战效能不强等问题。 深刻把握现代战争规律特点 当前，新时代的战争形态、作战方式正在发生颠覆性变化。“总的看，现代战争确实发生了深刻变化。这些变化看上去眼花缭乱，但背后是有规律可循的，根本的是战争的制胜机理变了。”习近平主席这样指出。因此，党的二十大报告对“研究掌握信息化智能化战争特点规律”作出明确部署。“十五五”规划纲要提出，加快机械化信息化智能化融合发展。 只有把未来打什么仗、怎么打仗搞清楚，才能锚定战争准备的逻辑基点，找准战斗力建设的方向。 ——与时俱进创新军事战略指导。作为战争准备和战争实施的顶层设计，军事战略科学准确，就是最大的胜算。 如今，战争时空属性早已超出传统认知，军事战略必须因变而变，用全新的视野审视战争、谋划战争。 一是以敏锐嗅觉密切关注时代大势。随着新兴科技成果的群体性突破重绘战争面貌，对手的作战思想和战术样式不断演进迭代，战争形态正从物理域向信息域、认知域飞跃。紧盯这些深层变化，才能制定出符合客观实际的战略指导。 二是坚持和发展“你打你的、我打我的”，善于发挥比较优势，在非对称竞争中寻找破敌之策。 三是把人民战争深厚伟力与现代技术优势更好结合起来。信息化智能化战争虽然技术门槛高，但正义性仍然是决定胜负的政治逻辑，必须善于运用新技术、新手段赋能人民战争，使这一传统优势在新时代焕发新的生机活力。 ——着力把智能化战争制胜机理研究透。制胜机理是战争内在的、起决定作用的运行法则和因果联系，搞不清制胜机理将迷失方向。打赢机械化战争的重点在于以物理能量摧毁对手，制胜信息化战争的关键在于以精确打击瘫痪敌方体系。近几场局部战争表明，参透未来战争制胜奥秘，关键要抓规律性问题，做到知其然更知其所以然，为战法创新提供厚重理论支撑。 因此，应深刻理解信息主导作用，认清信息是贯穿现代战争的核心要素，谁掌握了实时、精准、全面的信息优势，谁就能掌握战场主动权；深刻领悟“体系涌现”效能，认清现代战争是在网络系统强力支撑下，信息流、能量流、物质流和认知流同频共振、聚力释能，胜负越来越依赖于体系涌现的威力；深刻把握“智能泛在”效应，认清人工智能技术赋予作战体系前所未有的强大智慧大脑，这种赋能正在使作战行动更加精准、迅捷；高度关注速度的决胜意义，认清智能化战争中“发现即摧毁”已成常态，谁能在“观察—判断—决策—行动”的闭环中先机制敌，谁就能赢得战场优势。 ——加强军事力量常态化多样化运用。党的十八大以来，我军创新军事力量运用方式，实现了从“养兵千日、用兵一时”到“养兵千日、用兵千日”的历史跨越。人民军队紧扣使命任务优化战略布局和力量布势，推动兵力运用从被动应对向主动塑造、从阶段性任务向常态化运行、从单一军兵种行动向联合作战体系运用深刻转型，有效拓展了军事斗争的时空维度。 面向未来，必须更好发挥我军在塑造态势、管控危机、遏制冲突、打赢战争方面的战略功能。 将练兵与用兵辩证统一起来。训练就是实战、任务就是检验，正成为全军普遍共识与行动准则，应通过遂行多样化任务动态过程，更好检验训练成效、暴露问题短板，即时提升实战能力。 把军事斗争与战略威慑结合起来。采取常态化战巡警巡、远海联合演训等行动，清晰展示捍卫核心利益的决心，传递可信可靠的威慑信号，塑造于我有利的战略态势。 把单一方向行动与全局性体系运用贯通起来。加强各战略方向、各军事领域行动的统筹联动，灵活运用维权护航、战巡警巡和远海演训等多种形式，形成远近相济和攻防兼备的体系合力。 强国必须强军。2027年，中国人民解放军将迎来建军一百周年。面向党和人民的要求，全方位提高打赢能力，做到全时待战、随时能战，是我们这支光荣的英雄的部队为之最好的纪念。 （执笔：周俊杰 王璐颖 文 | 习近平强军思想研究中心
