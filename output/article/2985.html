About this analysis
Summary
PLA Daily published a behind-the-scenes companion piece to Season Two of the micro-documentary series 'Strengthening the Military Through Thought, Cultivating a New Generation' (强军思想育新人), profiling three servicemembers: Z-10 armed helicopter pilot Zhao Xiaolei, People's Armed Police Second Mobile Corps bomb disposal squad leader La Wanliang, and Rocket Force First-Class Sergeant Major (一级军士长) Hou Changling, who served as backup gold-standard operator (金牌号手) during the September 2024 ICBM test launch into the Pacific. The article is standard political work content — its value is as a record of how PLA media frames the soldier-family tension to build institutional loyalty and recruitment appeal among junior personnel and the public. The Hou Changling passage incidentally confirms his unit's role in the September 2024 launch, but the article adds no operational detail beyond what was announced at the time.

Stories from Behind the Camera of "Strengthening the Military Through Thought, Cultivating a New Generation (强军思想育新人)," Season Two —

One Military Uniform, Two Places of Longing

■ PLA Daily Reporters: Xie Fei, Wei Qiaoqiao, Cao Chunyao

Poster Design: Wang Xu

On August 1, Season Two of the micro-documentary series "Strengthening the Military Through Thought, Cultivating a New Generation" officially launched. The production team continued the filming style of Season One, recording from the first-person perspective of officers and soldiers the stories of three protagonists and their struggle to strengthen the military and win battles. On camera, pilot Zhao Xiaolei pilots his warbird and surveys ten thousand li of rivers and mountains; Rocket Force bugler Hou Changling holds his post and hoists aloft the great nation's long sword; bomb disposal expert La Wanliang faces life and death head-on to defuse crises — their formidable capabilities command admiration. Off camera, another identity shared by all three protagonists left a deep impression on the production team.

Deep winter in Liaoyang, the north wind cuts like a blade. A Z-10 armed helicopter hovers slowly; Zhao Xiaolei leaps from the cockpit, lips frozen to a bluish-purple, yet his eyes hold a light within.

Over the years, he has trained hard in pursuit of a single goal: to fly an advanced aircraft type. In last year's annual training assessment, he ranked first among pilots who graduated in the same cohort, earning his place as a genuine training standout.

When filming wrapped, Zhao Xiaolei smiled and brought up something unrelated to training: "My child was just born. I've lost ten jin this month."

We teased him — "No wonder you look better on camera" — and he scratched the back of his head and said: "I can fly for two hours in the air without getting nervous, but changing a diaper leaves me drenched in sweat."

In that moment, he was no longer the sharp-eyed pilot soaring through the clouds on screen. Stripped of the edge his uniform confers, he was simply an ordinary father at a complete loss in the face of his child.

The harder the wings that fly across mountains and rivers, the softer the heart that longs for home. What Zhao Xiaolei guards is, on one side, ten thousand li of open sky, and on the other, the hope that a new life has brought to his own small family.

Time turns, and high summer has arrived. On the training ground of the People's Armed Police Second Mobile Corps, the perceived temperature has broken 40 degrees Celsius. Bomb disposal squad leader La Wanliang must nonetheless don a bomb disposal suit weighing 30.7 kilograms and complete an 800-meter assault run.

The reporter tried putting on the gear — iron-plate-like armor encasing the entire body, leaving only the sound of labored breathing and a heartbeat like a drumroll. Only in that moment did we understand: every time a bomb disposal expert walks toward danger, he faces the entire world alone.

In fact, before filming began, La Wanliang had quietly sought out the production team to ask whether the shooting schedule could be moved faster. We learned that La Wanliang's child was about to turn one year old, and that he had already submitted a leave request, hoping to rush home in time to hear that one word: "Baba." The production team immediately decided to split into two groups and push forward simultaneously, compressing what had been a one-week plan into four days. Everyone ran without pause, without a single word of complaint. Because we understood: off camera, someone was waiting for a father to come home safe.

During a break in filming, La Wanliang took out a photo of his child tucked inside his notebook and showed it to us. A comrade nearby leaned over and called out: "Squad leader, by the time you get back, the kid won't even recognize you!" La Wanliang laughed and threw a punch at his comrade's arm, but when he looked back at the photo, his gaze grew ever more tender.

La Wanliang said that every time he walks toward an explosive device, what sustains his fearless advance — beyond the mission on his shoulders — is the small smiling face in that photograph.

In the rainy season, we plunged deep into the mountains to interview Hou Changling, a "PLA Advanced Individual in Military Training (全军军事训练先进个人)" and First-Class Sergeant Major (一级军士长) of a Rocket Force unit.

In September 2024, the People's Liberation Army Rocket Force successfully launched one intercontinental ballistic missile carrying a training simulation warhead into relevant high-seas areas of the Pacific Ocean. As a gold-standard operator (金牌号手), Hou Changling served as the backup, responsible for full-process technical guidance.

"Did you feel any regret at not being the one to press the launch key yourself?" we could not help but ask during the interview.

This battle-weathered veteran paused, then said: "Actually, the moment the missile lifted off, I secretly wiped away a tear — because I had steadily 'handed' this missile over to younger comrades."

His eyes held regret, and relief, and above all, anticipation.

Back in the family quarters of the garrison, Hou Changling's ten-year-old son came running from a distance, holding up a drawing to show his father — a Dongfeng missile.

"This kid can now recognize several missile types!" Hou Changling flipped through his son's sketchbook, a father's pride tucked in the corners of his eyes.

We asked the boy whether his classmates knew what his father did. He straightened his neck: "They know! My dad launches missiles! I'm going to join the Rocket Force too!"

A father's steadfast devotion plants in his child's heart the seed of military service. Soldiers guard national security, and in doing so also guard the admiration their children hold for them. Guarding the nation and guarding the family — this is how it passes from generation to generation through the bloodline.

After Season Two aired, one comment in the comment section caught our eye: "I really want to see what these hardcore heroes look like behind the scenes!"

We are grateful to have had the opportunity to report from the front lines. The quiet warmth that the camera never captured, the words from the heart that were never written into the script — we tell them to you here.

Guarding the nation has never been a debt owed to one's family. It is the protection of countless small families. Soldiers dedicate their youth and their blood to the cause of military strengthening (强军事业), and they also reserve the softest place in their hearts for their own small families.

One military uniform, two places of longing; soldiers guard the nation, and in doing so also guard the home.

《强军思想育新人》第二季镜头外的故事—— 一身戎装 两处牵挂 ■解放军报记者 谢 菲 危乔巧 曹春耀 海报制作：王 旭 8月1日，《强军思想育新人》系列微纪录片第二季正式上线。主创团队延续第一季的拍摄风格，以官兵第一视角记录了3位主人公矢志强军打赢的奋斗故事。镜头里，飞行员赵霄雷驾驭战鹰俯瞰万里河山，火箭军号手侯长岭坚守阵地托举大国长剑，排爆手喇万良直面生死拆解危机，硬核实力让人叹服；镜头外，3位主人公的另一个相同身份，也带给主创团队很深的触动。 辽阳深冬，朔风如刀。直-10武装直升机缓缓悬停，赵霄雷跃出机舱，嘴唇冻得泛出青紫，眼里却藏着光。 这些年，他苦练本领，只为了一个目标：飞上先进的型号。去年年度训练考核，他拿下同期毕业飞行员第一名，成为名副其实的训练尖子。 拍摄结束，聊天中赵霄雷笑着说起一件和训练不相干的事：“我的孩子刚出生，这个月我瘦了10斤。” 我们打趣他“难怪上镜更帅了”，他挠了挠后脑勺说：“我在空中飞两个小时都不紧张，给孩子换个尿布却能忙出一身汗。” 那一刻，他不再是镜头里目光锐利、驰骋云天的飞行员。褪去军装赋予的锋芒，他只是一个面对孩子“手足无措”的普通父亲。 飞越山河的翅膀有多硬，想家的心就有多软。赵霄雷守护的，一边是万里长空，一边是自己小家里新生命带来的希望。 时序辗转，盛夏已至。武警第二机动总队训练场上，体感温度突破40摄氏度。排爆班长喇万良，却要穿上重达30.7公斤的排爆服，完成800米奔袭。 记者试着穿上这身装备，铁皮般的铠甲包裹全身，耳边只剩下喘息声和擂鼓般的心跳声。那一刻我们才懂得，排爆手每一次走向危险，都是独自在面对整个世界。 其实，拍摄前喇万良曾悄悄找到摄制组，询问能否加快拍摄进度。我们得知，喇万良的孩子快满一周岁了，他此前已经打了休假报告，想赶回家听那一声“爸爸”。摄制组当即决定兵分两路推进，把原定一周的计划压缩到4天。所有人连轴奔波，没有一句怨言。因为我们清楚，镜头之外，有人在等一个父亲平安回家。 拍摄间隙，喇万良拿出夹在笔记本里的孩子照片给我们看。旁边战友凑过来喊了一声：“班长，等你回去，娃都不认识你啦！”喇万良笑着捶了战友一拳，可再看向照片时，他的目光愈发温柔。 喇万良说，每一次走向爆炸物，支撑他无畏向前的，除了肩上的使命，还有照片里那张小小的笑脸。 雨季，我们一头扎进深山，采访“全军军事训练先进个人”、火箭军某部一级军士长侯长岭。 2024年9月，中国人民解放军火箭军向太平洋相关公海海域，成功发射1发携载训练模拟弹头的洲际弹道导弹。作为金牌号手，侯长岭却担任备份，负责全程技术指导。 “没能亲手按下发射键，遗憾吗？”采访中，我们忍不住问他。 这个历经风雨的老兵顿了顿说：“其实导弹升空的那一刻，我偷偷抹了把泪，因为我把这枚导弹稳稳‘交’给了更年轻的战友。” 他的眼神里有遗憾、有欣慰，更有期待。 回到营院家属区，侯长岭10岁的儿子远远跑来，手里举着一张画给爸爸看，上面画的是一枚东风导弹。 “这小子，现在认得好几种导弹型号！”侯长岭翻出孩子的速写本，眼角藏着一份属于父亲的骄傲。 我们问小朋友，同学知不知道他的爸爸是干什么的。他脖子一挺：“知道！我爸爸是发射导弹的！以后我也要当火箭军！” 父辈的坚守，在孩子心中埋下从军的种子。军人守护国家安全，也在守护孩子心中对爸爸的崇拜。守国与守家，就这样在血脉中代代相传。 第二季节目播出后，评论区中的一条留言撞入眼底：“好想看看这些硬核英雄背后的样子！” 很庆幸，我们拥有赴一线采访的机会。那些镜头里没有呈现的脉脉温情，那些未曾写进脚本的心里话，我们在这里讲给你们听。 守国，从来不是对家的亏欠，而是对无数个小家的守护。军人把青春和热血献给强军事业，也把内心最柔软的地方留给自己的小家。 一身戎装，两处牵挂；军人守国，也在守家。 请扫描二维码观看相关视频
