Multiple Chinese Peacekeeping Units Celebrate the 105th Anniversary of the Founding of the Chinese Communist Party

中国多支维和部队庆祝中国共产党成立105周年
PLA Daily (解放军报) 30 June 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
On June 29, five PLA overseas peacekeeping units — the 24th contingent's Chinese battalion in southern Lebanon, the 3rd quick-reaction detachment and 6th helicopter detachment in Abyei, the 12th infantry battalion in South Sudan (Juba), and the 16th engineer detachment in South Sudan (Wau) — held Party anniversary ceremonies including oath renewals, Party history classes, and a badge-pinning ceremony for new members. This is standard political work content coordinated across deployed units for the Party's founding anniversary; its value is as a record of how the PLA frames loyalty and combat readiness to personnel serving under UN mandates, not as evidence of a new policy or operational posture. The 6th helicopter detachment's Party committee call to establish a "Party member assault flight crew" for rainy-season operations is the one operationally specific detail, though the article does not indicate whether such a crew was actually formed.
Translation

Polishing the True Colors of Loyalty on the Peacekeeping Battlefield — Multiple Chinese Peacekeeping Units Celebrate the 105th Anniversary of the Founding of the Chinese Communist Party

Members of China's 12th Peacekeeping Infantry Battalion to South Sudan (Juba): veteran Party members pin Party member badges on new Party members. Photo by Wang Liguo.

PLA Daily, Beijing, June 29 — On the occasion of the 105th anniversary of the founding of the Chinese Communist Party, officers and soldiers of multiple overseas peacekeeping units held activities in their respective mission areas to revisit their founding aspirations and mission, and to express their steadfast resolve to follow the Party's command and fulfill their mission.

At local time on June 29, in southern Lebanon, inside the Chinese battalion of our 24th peacekeeping contingent to Lebanon, the five-starred red flag rose slowly to the solemn strains of the national anthem. Officers and soldiers stood at attention with heads held high, saluting in silence. After the flag-raising ceremony, Party members faced the bright red Party flag and renewed their oath of Party membership. "I volunteer to join the Chinese Communist Party…" — the oath resounded throughout the peacekeeping camp.

In distant Abyei, our 3rd peacekeeping quick-reaction detachment held themed Party classes, Party history story-sharing sessions, and other activities to mark the Party's birthday. On June 28, the quick-reaction detachment's leadership delivered a lecture titled "Strengthen Faith and Conviction, Forge Absolute Loyalty," inspiring officers and soldiers to render meritorious service on the peacekeeping battlefield. Officers and soldiers were filled with fighting spirit, vowing to deliver a qualified answer sheet through concrete action.

Our 6th peacekeeping helicopter detachment to Abyei held a themed Party Day activity on the flight apron. All Party members renewed their oath of Party membership. New Party member Zhang Tao solemnly swore before the Party flag: "It is an incomparable honor to join the Party at a peacekeeping post. I will do everything in my power to fulfill my mission." Aviation maintenance backbone Wu Jun stated that his Party membership motivates him to pursue excellence and ensure the combat aircraft are ready to take off at any moment. The detachment's Party committee called for the establishment of a Party member assault flight crew to bravely take on urgent, difficult, dangerous, and arduous tasks in rainy-season conditions, strictly observe discipline, and demonstrate the image of a force for peace. At the close of the activity, Party members solemnly signed their names before the Party flag as a pledge of their commitment.

In the South Sudan (Juba) mission area, our 12th peacekeeping infantry battalion also held a themed Party Day activity. During the ceremony to renew the oath of Party membership, veteran Party members pinned Party member badges on new Party members; through Party history study, red-themed film screenings, and sharing of founding aspirations, officers and soldiers further consolidated the will and strength to fulfill their peacekeeping mission.

In the South Sudan (Wau) mission area, our 16th peacekeeping engineer detachment held an oath-taking ceremony before the Party flag. Officers and soldiers expressed that they would inscribe their absolute loyalty to the Party onto every blue-helmet post.

From Lebanon to South Sudan, from Abyei to Wau, all peacekeeping officers and soldiers expressed a shared sentiment during the activities: they will keep the string of combat readiness taut at all times, hold fast to their posts and duties, complete every peacekeeping task to the highest standard, and prove worthy of the trust placed in them by the motherland and the people.

(Compiled from contributions by Qu Fatong, Yang Biao, Huang Hao, Wang Shuai, and PLA Daily special correspondent Wang Yi)

Original Chinese
维和战场擦亮忠诚底色 ——中国多支维和部队庆祝中国共产党成立105周年 中国第12批赴南苏丹（朱巴）维和步兵营老党员为新党员佩戴党员徽章。王利国摄 解放军报北京6月29日电 在中国共产党成立105周年之际，我多支海外维和部队官兵在各自任务区举行活动，重温初心使命，表达听党指挥、履行使命的坚定决心。 当地时间6月29日，黎巴嫩南部，我第24批赴黎维和部队中国营内，五星红旗在庄严国歌声中冉冉升起。官兵昂首挺立，肃穆敬礼。升旗仪式后，党员们面向鲜红党旗，重温入党誓词。“我志愿加入中国共产党……”誓言响彻维和营区。 在遥远的阿卜耶伊，我第3批维和快反分队开展主题党课、党史故事分享等活动迎接党的生日。6月28日，快反分队领导以“坚定信仰信念、铸牢绝对忠诚”为题授课，激励官兵在维和战场建功立业。官兵斗志昂扬，誓言用实际行动交出合格答卷。 我第6批赴阿卜耶伊维和直升机分队在停机坪举行主题党日活动。全体党员重温入党誓词，新党员张涛面向党旗庄严宣誓：“能在维和战位入党，无比光荣！我将全力履行使命。”机务骨干吴俊表示，党员身份激励他精益求精，保障战鹰随时升空。分队党委号召成立党员突击机组，在雨季环境下勇挑急难险重任务，严守纪律，展现和平之师形象。活动尾声，党员们郑重在党旗前签名铭志。 在南苏丹（朱巴）任务区，我第12批维和步兵营也开展了主题党日活动。重温入党誓词仪式上，老党员为新党员佩戴党员徽章；通过党史学习、红色观影、初心分享，官兵们进一步凝聚了履行维和使命的意志力量。 在南苏丹（瓦乌）任务区，我第16批维和工兵分队举行向党旗宣誓活动。官兵们表示，要把对党的绝对忠诚，镌刻在每一个蓝盔战位上。 从黎巴嫩到南苏丹，从阿卜耶伊到瓦乌，全体维和官兵在活动中表达了共同的心声：将时刻紧绷战备之弦，坚守履职战位，以最高标准完成每一项维和任务，不负祖国和人民重托。 （综合瞿发统、杨彪、黄浩、王帅、解放军报特约记者王轶来稿）
