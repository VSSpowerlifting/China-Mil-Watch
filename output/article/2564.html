Victory Lessons from the Green Pitch

About this analysis
Summary
A commentary in Chinese military media by Yi Jiayan uses the 2025 FIFA World Cup final—Spain's 1–0 extra-time defeat of Argentina at MetLife Stadium on July 19—as a sustained vehicle for PLA training and readiness messaging, drawing explicit analogies between Spain's system superiority (体系) and PLA doctrine on system-of-systems confrontation (体系对抗), Tuchel's command failures and the imperative to upgrade command training, VAR and AI-enabled refereeing and the push for 科技练兵活动 (science and technology military training activities), and Spain's squad discipline and the demand for iron-discipline forces. The piece fits a well-documented genre of PLA political-education commentary that uses popular cultural events to reinforce current doctrinal priorities—here specifically joint operations integration, commander initiative under uncertainty, and technology-driven tactics and training methods (战法训法)—and its value is as a record of how those priorities are packaged for a junior-officer audience, not as evidence of any new policy or capability development.

Victory Lessons from the Green Pitch

■ Yi Jiayan

"Energy can infect everyone, and it will never be extinguished..." On the evening of July 19, local time, at MetLife Stadium in New Jersey, New York, accompanied by the farewell strains of "Dai Dai," cascading ribbons and golden confetti fluttered down as more than eighty thousand spectators slowly departed the stands bathed in the slanting rays of the setting sun.

With that, the United States-Canada-Mexico World Cup—39 days, 104 matches—wrote its final chapter.

The celebrated Dutch football manager Michels once said: "Football is war." Indeed, every fiercely contested pitch compresses the logic of two armies in confrontation; every sweeping, high-stakes match conceals lessons for defeating the enemy in modern warfare.

For soldiers, watching exercises play out on the green pitch is more than spectating; the way of victory lies in constant reflection and constant readiness. In the night skies over the North American continent, the contest on the green pitch has now concluded—but looking back at every match, any soldier who thinks about and prepares for war can read the logic of warfare and draw the strength of victory from that spinning football. Because the truly strong never pass up any opportunity to learn, even if it is a football match.

I

Extra time in the final, the atmosphere thick with tension. In the 106th minute, a lofted delivery floated down from the air; Spanish player Ferran Torres struck it with force, the ball cutting a sharp straight line and shaking the net, transforming a long agony of suspense into the entire team's jubilation in an instant.

1–0. From the scoreline alone, it was just a single goal's difference—yet the statistics revealed Spain's comprehensive dominance throughout the final. Over the course of the match, Spain completed 20 shots, 12 on target, while Argentina managed only 2 shots and 0 on target. A prominent football commentator identified the secret of Spain's victory: a superior system (体系).

The football king Pelé once said: "Football is a team sport; one person cannot win a match." This World Cup final reminded us once again that football is a sport of eleven people—winning requires the strength of the team. No matter how capable a single superstar may be, it cannot overcome the opponent's powerful, interlocking system.

Is this not also the law of victory in modern warfare? In modern warfare, joint operations and system-versus-system confrontation (体系对抗) have long since replaced individual combat. Just as the "one-touch passing" on the football pitch requires each combat element to complete information transfer and fire coordination within milliseconds. This demands that we work harder to train within the system and train the system itself (强化体系练、突出练体系), actively embed ourselves in the operational chain, strengthen inter-service and inter-branch interdependent training, strengthen training to integrate new domains and new forces into the operational system, continuously enrich system-of-systems operational experience, raise system-of-systems operational capability, and forge elite forces that win through system superiority.

II

"A one-goal lead could not preserve half a match's glory; Argentina's two goals turned the tide, and the 'Three Lions' youth, full of expectation, saw it all dissolve into a sigh the moment the decisive blow arrived." After the United States-Canada-Mexico World Cup semifinal, netizens described England's regret at winning and then losing in these terms.

What caused the defeat? After the match, media, fans, and former players all questioned England manager Tuchel's conservative in-match tactical arrangements and substitution decisions.

First he tacitly permitted a defensive retreat after going ahead on the scoreboard; then he actively neutralized his own team's most threatening mode of attack; finally, a substitution before the final whistle left a massive defensive void in the central area outside his own penalty box... After the match, a leading British media outlet enumerated England manager Tuchel's three command blunders, and the conclusion can be summed up in a phrase we know well: "One incompetent general exhausts the entire army (一将无能，累死三军)."

The ancients said: "A general's strength lies in strategy, not in courage." On the football pitch, the manager is that "general." So it is on the pitch; so it is even more on the battlefield. War is the greatest system of uncertainty; no operational plan can anticipate every variable.

A commander's on-the-spot decisions and a unit's rapid response are often more important than pre-arranged plans. The "improvised combination" on the football pitch and "adapting to circumstances" (随机应变) on the battlefield are, in essence, both about keen perception of the situation and precise grasp of timing. It must be recognized in particular that the role of command confrontation has risen to unprecedented heights in modern warfare. Commanders at every level must comprehensively upgrade command training, fix their gaze on "the next war," constantly harbor "concern" about capability, and constantly pose "questions" about victory—only then can they comprehensively raise their warfighting readiness capability for the new era and firmly grasp the initiative in future warfare.

III

Among the unforgettable moments of the United States-Canada-Mexico World Cup, there is certainly this one: the decisive last-minute strike (绝杀).

"Stoppage-time thunder, extra-time breakthroughs—one decisive goal after another ensured that every hard-fought battle retained its reversals and miracles." "A single second reversed joy and sorrow, sending a match on the verge of conclusion surging back into towering waves of heat." "The final instant rewrote fate; suspense and drama stretched to the limit—this is football's most stirring hot-blooded romance."...

One thought heaven, one thought hell—extreme suspense also conceals the profound truth of winning through change in the arena of contest. From despair to hope, from tears to laughter, football tells us in the most brutal and yet most generous way: as long as the final whistle has not blown, one must never give up. Is life not the same? We always think fate has been written, that the road ahead is blocked—not knowing that the turning point is often hidden in the next sprint, the next contest for the ball. This is precisely football's metaphor for life.

Comrade Mao Zedong pointed out: "It often happens that favorable circumstances and the recovery of initiative arise from 'persisting just a little longer.'" Do not despair at the edge of the abyss and lament that the road is exhausted—wonders and marvels are often found precisely in desperate straits. On the future battlefield, dangerous situations, perilous situations, and stalemates are unavoidable. At such moments, what is most needed is to clench our teeth and "persist just a little longer." Only by preparing for both the expected "ten thousand" and the unexpected "one-in-ten-thousand," and by training hard the capability to fight uphill battles and break through counterattack situations, can we transform disadvantage into advantage, transform passivity into initiative, and ultimately fight and win.

IV

At this World Cup, VAR (Video Assistant Referee), semi-automated offside recognition technology, and intelligent data analysis systems were widely applied—technology is profoundly changing the game of football. Coaching teams adjust tactics through real-time data; players monitor physical indicators through wearable devices; referees ensure fairness through technological means. Football is no longer a purely physical contest; it is also a contest of technology and data.

So too on the battlefield. From precision guidance to unmanned combat, from cyberspace to artificial intelligence, technology is reshaping the form of warfare. Whoever can lead by one step in technological innovation will hold the initiative on the future battlefield. Behind every exquisite combination on the football pitch lies the product of big-data training; behind every precision strike on the battlefield lies the support of technological power.

Technology is the primary combat capability; innovation is the primary driving force. Raising the combat effectiveness of our forces requires us to actively carry out science and technology military training activities (科技练兵活动), make full use of cutting-edge technological achievements such as intelligent technology, actively develop intelligent simulation and modeling, war-gaming confrontation, and network confrontation exercise-and-training systems, fully release the comprehensive effects of innovation in training methods, drive innovation in tactics and training methods (战法训法) supported by technology, and comprehensively raise the level of training and the capability to fight and win.

V

Spain's championship victory offers many lessons to summarize, but one is often overlooked: discipline.

"Talent determines a team's ceiling, while discipline fortifies the baseline of victory... Tactically precise execution of orders and a composed, restrained mindset on the pitch kept Spain's formation intact and free of vulnerabilities through 120 minutes of hard fighting, ultimately allowing them to claim the crown through ordered structure." A football professional wrote these words.

Indeed, in football, discipline is the skeleton of tactics, supporting the precision of every run and every pass-and-cut combination; discipline is the hub of coordination, condensing the heartbeats of eleven people into a moving fortress; discipline is above all the crystallization of will, enabling individuals to remain subordinate to the overall arrangement even under extreme fatigue. In a certain sense, the soul of football lies not only in superior strength, but also in iron-like execution and unspoken trust.

"Without discipline, we would be like loose sand, crushed grain by grain, quickly, by the enemy." The verse at the Memorial Hall of the War of Chinese People's Resistance Against Japanese Aggression serves as a sobering reminder. Discipline is like a furnace, forging the sinews and bones of a steel torrent; orders are like mountains, marking the standard for advance and retreat in attack and defense. Strengthen discipline, and revolution is invincible. In modern warfare, the operational space is full-domain and multi-dimensional, operational elements are highly interconnected, and the operational tempo has accelerated to an unprecedented degree—this demands all the more that we use iron discipline to forge iron will, temper iron style, and build iron forces.

VI

This World Cup was a "twilight of the gods," and also a "dawn of new stars."

Ronaldo, Modrić, Neymar... A generation of legends walked onto the pitch; every match could be their World Cup swan song. At the same time, players born in 2000 or later accounted for roughly forty percent of participants. England's squad included 13 players born after 2000; Spain's included 12. The 19-year-olds Yamal and Cubarsí started in the World Cup final. "They are not 'children'—they are warriors." Spain manager De la Fuente's eyes reddened after the match.

The passing of the old and the coming of the new is a law of nature and a law of history. In the history of warfare, new weapons replacing old weapons, new tactics replacing old tactics, and a new generation replacing the old generation are irresistible historical trends. History teaches us: whoever can embrace the power of youth will win the future.

Young officers and soldiers are the rising sun of the military, the living source of combat power generation. Their thinking is swift as lightning, their spirit sharp as a rainbow; their fingertips can marshal torrents of data, their eyes can pierce the fog of the battlefield. By cultivating and entrusting important responsibilities to young officers and soldiers, letting the edge of youth be honed in realistic combat training, the People's Army will possess its most vital element and its most enduring driving force—and will surely, on the journey of building a strong nation and a strong military, guard a youthful China with a youthful self.

VII

In truth, the changes of war far surpass those of football, and its mysteries are beyond what football can compare to. Football has a common saying: "That's football!" The military world also has a common saying: "That's war!" When speaking of football's appeal, people often say: "Football is round!" When speaking of the appeal of military affairs, people also say: "War is alive!"

Different trades are as far apart as mountains, yet the underlying principles are not. Reading the logic of victory on the World Cup pitch, contemplating the true essence of winning in a contest of strategies, using the pitch to illuminate military strategy (韬略) and using victory and defeat to examine gains and losses—all of this helps us to advance steadily and adapt with composure in the grand game of the era's shifting winds, accumulate strength in silence, press forward with resolve amid changing circumstances, build a steel Great Wall, and guard the peace of our mountains and rivers.

绿茵赛场的胜战启示 ■壹嘉彦 “能量能感染所有人，而它永远不会熄灭……”当地时间7月19日傍晚，纽约新泽西体育场，伴着《Dai Dai》惜别的歌声，漫天飞舞的彩带和金屑瓣瓣纷落，八万多观众缓缓离开夕阳斜洒的看台。 至此，为期39天104场比赛的美加墨世界杯完成终章。 荷兰足球名帅米歇尔斯有一句名言：“足球即战争。”的确，每个针锋相对的赛场，都浓缩着两军对垒的逻辑；每场大开大合的较量，都隐藏着现代战争克敌制胜的启示。 对军人来说，绿茵演兵，不止于观赛；胜战之道，在于常思常备。在北美大陆的夜空，绿茵角逐已然落幕，但回望每场比赛，任何一个思战务战的军人，都能从那飞旋的足球中，读懂战争的逻辑、汲取胜战的力量——因为真正的强者，从不会放过任何一次学习的机会，哪怕它是一场足球赛。 一 决赛加时，气氛浓稠。第106分钟，摆渡来球凌空飘落，西班牙球员费兰·托雷斯一脚劲射，足球划出锋利直线，震颤球网，漫长煎熬顷刻化作全队狂欢。 1比0，从比分看，仅是一球之差，但数据却呈现出西班牙队在这场决赛中全方位的压制。整场比赛，西班牙队完成20次射门、12次射正，而阿根廷队仅有2次射门、0次射正。对此，一位著名足球评论员点出西班牙队赢球秘诀：体系强。 球王贝利曾说：“足球是一项团队运动，一个人无法赢得比赛。”这场世界杯决赛再次启示我们，足球是11个人的运动，想要获胜必须依靠团队的力量；单一巨星能力再强，也抵不过对方环环相扣的强大体系。 这何尝不是现代战争的制胜法则？现代战争，联合作战、体系对抗，早已取代单打独斗。正如足球场上“一脚出球”式的传递，各作战要素需要在毫秒之间完成信息流转、火力协调。这就要求我们进一步在强化体系练、突出练体系上下功夫，主动把自己嵌入作战链条，强化军兵种互为条件训练，强化新领域新力量融入作战体系训练，不断丰富体系作战经验、提高体系作战能力，锻造体系制胜的精兵劲旅。 二 “一球领先没能守住半场荣光，阿根廷队两粒进球扭转乾坤，‘三狮少年’满腔期许，终在绝杀来临的瞬间化作一声叹息。”美加墨世界杯半决赛后，网友这样描写英格兰队先赢后输的遗憾。 败因何在？赛后，媒体、球迷、名宿，都对英格兰队主帅图赫尔保守的临场战术安排和换人调整提出质疑。 先是比分领先后默许退守，再是主动废掉本队最具威胁的进攻方式，后是终场前换人导致己方大禁区外围中路出现巨大防守真空……赛后，英国权威媒体在罗列英格兰队主帅图赫尔三大指挥昏招后的感叹，可以归结为我们熟悉的那句：“一将无能，累死三军。” 古人云：“将在谋而不在勇。”足球场上，主帅就是那个“将”。球场如此，战场更如此。战争是最大的不确定性系统，任何作战计划都无法预判所有变数。 指挥员的临机决断、部队的快速响应，往往比预先方案更加重要。足球场上的“即兴配合”与战场上的“随机应变”，本质上都是对形势的敏锐洞察和对时机的精准把握。尤其要看到，指挥对抗的作用在现代战争中空前上升，各级指挥员只有全面升级指挥训练，紧盯“下一场战争”，常怀能力之“忧”，常发胜战之“问”，才能全面提高新时代备战打仗能力，牢牢掌握未来战争的主动权。 三 提起美加墨世界杯令人难忘的时刻，肯定有这样一个瞬间：绝杀。 “补时惊雷、加时破局，一粒粒绝杀球，让每一场鏖战都留有反转与奇迹。”“一秒逆转悲欢，让即将落幕的战局再度掀起滔天热浪。”“终局一瞬改写命运，跌宕悬念拉满，这是足坛最动人的热血浪漫。”…… 一念天堂，一念地狱，极致悬念也暗含博弈场上变局制胜的深刻奥义。从绝望到希望，从泪水到欢笑，足球用最残酷也最慷慨的方式告诉我们：只要终场哨没有吹响，就永远不能放弃。人生何尝不是如此？我们总以为命运已经写定，前路已经堵死，却不知转机往往就藏在下一个奔跑、下一次拼抢之中，这正是足球对于人生的隐喻。 毛泽东同志指出：“往往有这种情形，有利的情况和主动的恢复，产生于‘再坚持一下’的努力之中。”莫临绝境叹途穷，奇瑰多在绝境中。未来战场上，难免遇到危局、险局、僵局，这个时候，最需要我们咬紧牙关，“再坚持一下”。只有既想“一万”也想“万一”，练强打逆风仗、破逆袭战的能力，才能变不利为有利，变被动为主动，最后战而胜之。 四 本届世界杯，VAR（视频助理裁判）、半自动越位识别技术、智能数据分析系统被广泛应用，科技正在深刻改变足球比赛。教练团队通过实时数据调整战术，球员通过可穿戴设备监测体能指标，裁判通过科技手段确保公正——足球已不是单纯的体力较量，更是科技与数据的博弈。 战场亦然。从精准制导到无人作战，从网络空间到人工智能，科技正在重塑战争形态。谁能在科技创新上领先一步，谁就能在未来战场上占据主动。足球场上每一次精妙配合的背后是大数据训练的成果，战场上每一次精确打击的背后是科技力量的支撑。 科技是第一战斗力，创新是第一驱动力。提高部队战斗力，要求我们积极开展科技练兵活动，充分利用智能技术等前沿科技成果，积极开发智能化模拟仿真、兵棋对抗、网络对抗演训系统，充分释放训练手段创新的综合效应，推动科技支撑的战法训法创新，全面提升训练水平和打赢能力。 五 西班牙队夺冠，可以总结的经验很多，而这样一条，常常被人忽视，那就是纪律。 “天赋决定球队的上限，而纪律筑牢取胜底线……令行禁止的战术执行、克制沉稳的赛场心态，让西班牙队在120分钟鏖战中阵型不散、破绽不生，最终凭借规整秩序摘得桂冠。”有足球专业人士这样写道。 的确，对于足球而言，纪律是战术的骨架，支撑起每一次跑位与传切的精密；纪律是协同的枢纽，将11个人的心跳凝结成一座移动的堡垒；纪律更是意志的凝聚，让个体在极限疲劳中依然服从整体的棋局。从某种意义上说，足球之魂，既在于实力过硬，也在于铁一般的执行与无言的信任。 “没有纪律，我们会像散沙一般，被敌人一粒一粒，很快地踩碎。”中国人民抗日战争纪念馆的诗词让人警醒。纪律如熔炉，铸就钢铁洪流之筋骨；号令如山岳，标定攻防进退之准绳。加强纪律性，革命无不胜。现代战争，作战空间全域多维，作战要素高度联动，作战节奏空前加快，更加要求我们用铁的纪律凝聚铁的意志、锤炼铁的作风、锻造铁的队伍。 六 这届世界杯，是“诸神的黄昏”，也是“新星的黎明”。 C罗、莫德里奇、内马尔……一代传奇走向赛场，每一场比赛都可能是他们的世界杯绝唱。与此同时，出生于2000年及以后的球员占到四成左右。英格兰队内有13人是“00后”，西班牙阵中有12人是“00后”。19岁的亚马尔和库巴西首发站上世界杯决赛舞台。“他们不是‘小孩’，是战士。”西班牙主帅德拉富恩特赛后红了眼眶。 新旧交替，是自然规律，也是历史规律。战争史上，新式武器取代旧式武器、新战法取代旧战法、新一代人取代老一代人是不可抗拒的历史潮流。历史启示我们，谁能拥抱年轻的力量，谁就能赢得未来。 青年官兵是军队的朝阳，是战斗力生成的源头活水。他们思维如电、锐气如虹，指尖可运筹数据洪流，眼底能洞穿战场迷雾。培养重用青年官兵，让青春的锋芒在实战化训练中砥砺，人民军队便拥有了最活跃的因子、最持久的动力，必将在强国强军征程上，以青春之我，守护青春之中国。 七 其实，战争的变化远胜于足球，其奥妙也是足球无法比拟的。足球界有句惯用语：“这就是足球！”军事界也有句惯用语：“这就是战争！”讲足球的魅力时人们常说：“足球是圆的！”谈军事的魅力时人们也说：“战争是活的！” 隔行如隔山，隔行不隔理。读懂世界杯赛场上的胜战逻辑，参悟博弈对决里的制胜真谛，以赛场明韬略，以胜负鉴得失，有助于我们更好地在时代风云的宏大棋局里稳扎稳打、从容应变，于无声处积蓄力量，于变局中笃定前行，筑钢铁长城，守山河安澜。
