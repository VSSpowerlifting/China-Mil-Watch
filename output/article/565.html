About this analysis
Summary
An author affiliated with the National University of Defense Technology published a doctrinal commentary in official military media calling on PLA officers and cadres to deepen study of Xi Jinping's theoretical line following his speech at an all-military senior cadre training course, framing the exercise as 理论武装 (theoretical arming) essential to political loyalty, combat effectiveness, and organizational purity. The piece explicitly targets 'depoliticization' and 'nationalization of the military' as live ideological threats requiring active refutation, and invokes the Yan'an Rectification Movement and the 1949 Danyang consolidation training as historical precedents for the current 思想整风 (ideological rectification) and 政治整训 (political consolidation training) campaign. This is standard political-work content; its value is as a record of how the PLA frames the current rectification drive to senior officers in the approach to the PLA centenary in 2027, not as evidence of a new policy line.

Persisting in Using the Party's Innovative Theory to Consolidate Foundations and Cultivate Vitality (固本培元) — Studying and Implementing the Spirit of Chairman Xi's Important Speech at the Opening Ceremony of the All-Military Senior Cadre Training Course

■ Hu Yuxuan

Editor's Note: "We must earnestly study the Party's innovative theory, master the Marxist positions, viewpoints, and methods that run throughout it, and use theoretical clarity to guarantee political firmness." Not long ago, at the opening ceremony of the all-military senior cadre training course, Chairman Xi profoundly expounded on the extreme importance of theoretical arming (理论武装), further pointing the way and providing guidance for carrying out ideological rectification (思想整风) and deepening political consolidation training (政治整训) on the new journey. We must study and comprehend this deeply, resolutely implement it, and persistently use the Party's innovative theory to consolidate foundations and cultivate vitality, driving the People's Army to greet the centenary of the founding of the armed forces with a wholly new political bearing.

A political party that wishes to stand at the forefront of the era cannot for a single moment do without theoretical innovation; an armed force that wishes to advance toward world-class status cannot for a single moment relax theoretical arming. Since its founding, the People's Army has always persisted, under the Party's leadership, in using advanced theory to clarify direction, consolidate will, and unite strength, thereby advancing continuously from victory to victory. Forging ahead on the new journey, confronting the grand blueprint of a strong nation and strong military and the tests of high winds and surging waves (风高浪急), there is even greater need to persistently use the Party's innovative theory to consolidate foundations and cultivate vitality, to use theoretical clarity to guarantee political firmness, and to provide a firm ideological guarantee for achieving the centenary of the founding of the armed forces struggle objectives on schedule and for high-quality advancement of national defense and military modernization.

I

Thought is the vanguard of action; theory is the guide to practice. Persisting in using the Party's innovative theory to consolidate foundations and cultivate vitality is a precious experience of our Party in building and governing the military, and also an important magic weapon (法宝) for the People's Army's development and growth and for defeating the enemy.

An inevitable requirement for forging political loyalty. "Theoretical cultivation is the core of a cadre's overall quality; theoretical maturity is the foundation of political maturity; political firmness stems from theoretical clarity." During the Yan'an period, the broad ranks of Party members, cadres, and revolutionary soldiers relied precisely on the drive to "eat millet and storm the mountain of theory," studying Marxism-Leninism hungrily and thirstily under arduous conditions, and thereby steadfastly maintained the correct political direction without wavering, laying a solid foundation for seizing final victory in the War of Resistance Against Japan and the Chinese revolution. History and practice have proven that theoretical clarity enables political firmness, and that "those without theory are easily 'captured.'" At present, the struggle in the ideological domain is sharp and complex; the contest between consolidating roots and destroying roots, between forging the soul and corroding the soul (铸魂与蛀魂), has not ceased for a single moment. We must persistently strengthen the arming of Party innovative theory, guide military Party members and cadres to consciously become firm believers in and faithful practitioners of the Party's innovative theory, continuously consolidate the foundation of faith, replenish the calcium of the spirit (补足精神之钙), and steady the helm of thought, ensuring that the People's Army at any time and under any circumstances listens to the Party and follows the Party.

An inevitable requirement for forging war-winning capability. Theory is the weapon of thought and bears on the building of combat effectiveness. Comrade Mao Zedong once pointed out: "If our Party has one hundred to two hundred comrades who have systematically and not fragmentarily, practically and not emptily mastered Marxism-Leninism, the combat strength of our Party will be greatly elevated." Historically, one very important reason why our military has always been able to maintain powerful combat effectiveness is that it has at all times paid close attention to the infusion of progressive thought and the arming of scientific theory. During the War of Liberation, our military carried out the army rectification movement (整军运动) centered on the "speak bitterness" (诉苦) campaign and the "three investigations" (三查), guiding officers and soldiers to strengthen theoretical study and ideological remolding, enabling large numbers of "liberated soldiers" (解放战士) to overcome non-proletarian thinking, thereby effectively uniting, purifying, and consolidating the troops and substantially raising their combat effectiveness. History and practice have eloquently proven that only by persistently using the Party's innovative theory to consolidate foundations and cultivate vitality, educating and guiding military Party members and cadres to master the military outlook and methodology that runs throughout it, can we provide fundamental ideological leadership and powerful spiritual motivation for being able to fight and to win, ensuring that the troops respond to the call, fight upon arrival, and win every battle (召之即来、来之能战、战之必胜).

An inevitable requirement for eternally preserving purity and glory. The Party's innovative theory is permeated with the fundamental requirements for a Communist's worldview, outlook on life, and values, as well as Party character cultivation, and is a powerful weapon for military Party members and cadres to temper their Party character and purify their souls. On the eve of the liberation of Shanghai, our Party and military conducted consolidation training (整训) at Danyang; the core content of the training was to strengthen work style and discipline through studying the policy for entering the city. Through this consolidation training, the troops strictly observed the discipline for entering the city; the scene of tens of thousands of officers and soldiers sleeping in the streets without disturbing the citizens shook the world and won the hearts of the people, achieving the dual victory of "military entry into the city" and "political entry into the city." This fully confirms that strict discipline and an excellent work style stem from sustained ideological education and theoretical nourishment. Using the ideological weapon of the Party's innovative theory helps military Party members and cadres uphold great virtue (明大德), observe public virtue (守公德), and be strict with private virtue (严私德), deeply investigating and correcting problems at their ideological roots, and eternally preserving the nature and purpose (性质宗旨) of the People's Army.

II

Studying the Party's innovative theory is not hollow or abstract, but concrete and practical. Only by distinguishing right from wrong in the process of measuring against the standard (对标对表), unifying thought in the process of clearing away the turbid and elevating the pure (激浊扬清), and avoiding rigidity in the process of upholding the correct while innovating (守正创新), can we truly achieve the consolidation of foundations and cultivation of vitality.

Distinguishing right from wrong, refuting erroneous statements. The history of our Party and military is a history of continuously struggling against all manner of erroneous thinking. During the Yan'an Rectification Movement, it was precisely through systematic study of theory that subjectivism, sectarianism, and Party formalism (党八股) and other erroneous thinking were thoroughly settled accounts with, achieving unprecedented unity of the entire Party in thought, politics, and organization. Since the new era, each successive concentrated intra-Party education campaign has also been permeated with the main thread of using the Party's innovative theory to clear away ideological fog and refute erroneous trends of thought, powerfully guiding officers and soldiers to resolutely resist and oppose such erroneous political viewpoints as "depoliticization of the military" (军队非党化、非政治化) and "nationalization of the military" (军队国家化). On the road ahead, only by continuously arming the mind with the Party's innovative theory can we keenly perceive the political essence of all manner of erroneous trends of thought and resolutely struggle against them with no compromise in actual action, thereby ensuring that the People's Army does not change its nature, does not change its color, and does not change its character (不变质、不变色、不变味).

Eliminating interference, unifying thought and understanding. Thought is the vanguard of action; unifying thought is the prerequisite for unifying action. At the time, after the Three Great Campaigns achieved victory, the Kuomintang reactionaries, in order to gain time to catch their breath, launched a "peace offensive," and some cadres in our Party and military began to waver over whether to cross the river. In response to this phenomenon, Comrade Mao Zedong wrote a New Year's message titled "Carry the Revolution Through to the End," drawing a clear line between revolution and compromise, and pointing out that it was necessary to "carry the great People's War of Liberation through to the end." After the article was published, troops, factories, and enterprises organized study sessions one after another, laying a solid ideological foundation for seizing comprehensive victory in the War of Liberation. At present, achieving the centenary of the founding of the armed forces struggle objectives has entered a critical period of breaking through passes and seizing strongholds (闯关夺隘), a fortified-position period of strenuous effort (吃劲奋斗), and a sprint period of adding fuel and pressure (加油加压), with a complex situation and arduous tasks. Only by unremittingly strengthening the arming of the Party's innovative theory can we clarify muddled understanding on key questions, eliminate all manner of interference before fortified-position tasks, and thereby gather the powerful force of united advance.

Upholding the correct while innovating, avoiding rigidity and dogmatism. Marxism is a theory of development and innovation; its powerful vitality lies precisely in its creativity. However, in reality some people, in the process of studying theory, have failed to grasp the dialectical relationship between upholding the correct (守正) and innovation, falling into the predicament of rigidity and dogmatism. Historically, Wang Ming's "Left" adventurism brought catastrophic losses to the cause of the Chinese revolution; the root of its error lay in divorcing itself from the reality of the Chinese revolution and mechanically applying Marxism, engaging in dogmatism. This lesson profoundly warns us that in persisting in using the Party's innovative theory to consolidate foundations and cultivate vitality, we must absolutely never merely know how to one-sidedly cite individual words and phrases, but must grasp the theoretical character of Marxism's keeping pace with the times (与时俱进), and learn to use the Marxist positions, viewpoints, and methods to concretely analyze and resolve real-world problems.

III

The Way cannot be discussed while seated; principles cannot be talked about in empty terms. In persisting in using the Party's innovative theory to consolidate foundations and cultivate vitality, military Party members and cadres must achieve conscientious and proactive study, timely follow-up study, study linked to reality, and study with firm belief and firm practice (笃信笃行), striving to become models of diligent study and good thinking, models of emancipating the mind and keeping pace with the times, and models of applying what is learned and achieving results through application.

Study conscientiously and proactively. Those who know it are not as good as those who love it; those who love it are not as good as those who delight in it. For theoretical study to achieve real results, it is necessary to stimulate intrinsic enthusiasm for learning, transforming "I am made to study" into "I want to study." Military Party members and cadres must profoundly recognize the extreme importance of theoretical study, establish lofty aspirations in theoretical study, endure the cold and the loneliness, calm the mind to read thoroughly and study diligently, firm up the will to study, strengthen the determination to study, and intensify the consciousness of study, truly treating study as a political responsibility, a spiritual pursuit, and a way of life.

Study with timely follow-up. Marxism is an open theoretical system that continuously develops with the development of the era and practice; in studying Marxist theory, one should ensure that every step forward in the Party's theoretical innovation is matched by a step forward in theoretical arming. Therefore, military Party members and cadres must achieve timely follow-up study, view theoretical study with a connected and developmental perspective, persist in studying the latest achievements of the Party's innovative theory at the first opportunity, proactively keep pace with the rhythm of theoretical innovation, not allow "blank periods" in theoretical study or "time lags" (时间差) in ideological understanding, thereby always maintaining political acuity, theoretical systematicity, and ideological advancement.

Study linked to reality. Practicality is a distinctive characteristic of Marxism. In the Party's history, whenever the principle of linking theory with practice has been upheld well, the cause of the Party and the people has been able to continuously achieve victory. Military Party members and cadres studying the Party's innovative theory must vigorously promote the Marxist study style of linking theory with practice, link it to the contradictions and problems encountered in troop building, reform, and preparation for war and fighting, link it to personal ideological reality and work reality, study with problems in mind, think against problems, and analyze toward problems, genuinely transforming the fruits of study into ideas for planning work, methods for resolving difficult problems, and measures for driving development.

Study with firm belief and firm practice (笃信笃行). The key to improving the quality of theoretical study lies in believing through study and acting through study. Only by continuously strengthening political identification, ideological identification, theoretical identification, and emotional identification with the Party's innovative theory can one truly achieve the integration of study, thought, and application, and the unity of knowledge, belief, and action (知信行统一). Military Party members and cadres must, from the historic achievements and historic transformations achieved by the Party and the people's cause since the new era, profoundly perceive the truth appeal and practical power of Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era, transform the fruits of study into firm ideals and convictions, strong political capability, a powerful sense of mission and responsibility, and an excellent work style, and use new elevations in theoretical study to open up new vistas in all work.

(Author's unit: National University of Defense Technology)

坚持用党的创新理论固本培元 ——学习贯彻习主席在全军高级干部培训班开班式上的重要讲话精神 ■胡宇萱 编者按 “要认真学习党的创新理论，掌握贯穿其中的马克思主义立场观点方法，以理论上的清醒保证政治上的坚定。”前不久，在全军高级干部培训班开班式上，习主席深刻阐明了理论武装的极端重要性，为新征程上开展思想整风、深化政治整训进一步指明了方向，提供了遵循。我们要深入学习领会、坚决贯彻落实，坚持不懈用党的创新理论固本培元，推动人民军队以崭新政治面貌迎接建军一百周年。 一个政党要站在时代前列，一刻也离不开理论创新；一支军队要迈向世界一流，一刻也不能放松理论武装。人民军队自诞生以来，就始终坚持在党的领导下以先进理论明确方向、凝聚意志、团结力量，从而不断从胜利走向胜利。奋进新征程，面对强国强军的宏伟蓝图与风高浪急的风险考验，更加需要坚持不懈用党的创新理论固本培元，以理论上的清醒保证政治上的坚定，为如期实现建军一百年奋斗目标、高质量推进国防和军队现代化提供坚强思想保证。 一 思想是行动的先导，理论是实践的指南。坚持用党的创新理论固本培元，是我们党建军治军的宝贵经验，也是人民军队发展壮大、克敌制胜的重要法宝。 铸牢政治忠诚的必然要求。“理论修养是干部综合素质的核心，理论上的成熟是政治上成熟的基础，政治上的坚定源于理论上的清醒。”延安时期，广大党员干部、革命军人正是靠着“吃小米饭，攻理论山”的劲头，在艰苦条件下如饥似渴学习马列主义，才始终坚持正确政治方向不动摇，为夺取抗日战争和中国革命的最终胜利奠定了坚实基础。历史和实践证明，理论清醒才能政治坚定，“没有理论的人容易被‘俘虏’”。当前，意识形态领域斗争尖锐复杂，固根与毁根、铸魂与蛀魂的较量一刻也未停歇。必须持之以恒加强党的创新理论武装，引导军队党员干部自觉做党的创新理论的坚定信仰者和忠实践行者，不断筑牢信仰之基、补足精神之钙、把稳思想之舵，确保人民军队在任何时候任何情况下都听党话、跟党走。 锻造胜战能力的必然要求。理论是思想的武器，事关战斗力建设。毛泽东同志曾指出：“如果我们党有一百个至二百个系统地而不是零碎地、实际地而不是空洞地学会了马克思列宁主义的同志，就会大大地提高我们党的战斗力量。”历史上，我军之所以能够始终保持强大战斗力，时刻注重进步思想贯注和科学理论武装是一个非常重要的原因。解放战争时期，我军开展了以诉苦和“三查”为中心内容的整军运动，引导官兵加强理论学习和思想改造，使大批“解放战士”得以克服非无产阶级思想，从而有效团结、纯洁和巩固部队，大幅度提高了部队战斗力。历史和实践雄辩地证明，只有坚持不懈用党的创新理论固本培元，教育引导军队党员干部掌握贯穿其中的军事观和方法论，才能为能打仗、打胜仗提供根本的思想引领和强大的精神动力，确保部队召之即来、来之能战、战之必胜。 永葆纯洁光荣的必然要求。党的创新理论贯穿着共产党人世界观、人生观、价值观与党性修养的根本要求，是军队党员干部锤炼党性、净化灵魂的强大武器。解放上海前夕，我党我军在丹阳开展整训，整训核心内容是通过学习入城政策加强作风纪律。通过这次整训，部队严格遵守入城纪律，数万官兵露宿街头、不扰市民的画面震撼世界、赢得民心，实现了“军事进城”与“政治进城”的双重胜利。这充分印证，纪律严明和优良作风源于持续的思想教育和理论滋养。运用党的创新理论这一思想武器，有助于军队党员干部明大德、守公德、严私德，从思想根源上深入查纠问题，永葆人民军队的性质宗旨本色。 二 学习党的创新理论不是空洞的、抽象的，而是具体的、实践的。只有在对标对表中明辨是非、在激浊扬清中统一思想、在守正创新中避免僵化，才能真正实现固本培元。 辨析是非，批驳错误言论。我党我军的历史，就是一部不断同各种错误思想作斗争的历史。在延安整风运动中，正是通过系统地学理论，深刻清算了主观主义、宗派主义、党八股等错误思想，实现了全党思想上、政治上、组织上的空前团结统一。新时代以来，历次党内集中教育，也都贯穿着用党的创新理论廓清思想迷雾、批驳错误思潮的主线，有力引导官兵坚决抵制和反对“军队非党化、非政治化”和“军队国家化”等错误政治观点。前进道路上，只有持续用党的创新理论武装头脑，才能敏锐地洞察各种错误思潮的政治本质，并以实际行动坚决斗争、毫不妥协，从而确保人民军队不变质、不变色、不变味。 排除干扰，统一思想认识。思想是行动的先导，统一思想是统一行动的前提。当年，三大战役取得胜利后，国民党反动派为争取喘息时间，发起了“和平攻势”，我党我军有些干部对于是否打过江去开始摇摆不定。针对这一现象，毛泽东同志写了题为《将革命进行到底》的新年献词，划清了革命与妥协的界限，指出必须“把伟大的人民解放战争进行到底”。文章刊发后，部队、工厂、企业纷纷组织学习，为夺取解放战争的全面胜利奠定了坚实思想基础。当前，实现建军一百年奋斗目标到了闯关夺隘的关键期、吃劲奋斗的攻坚期、加油加压的冲刺期，形势复杂、任务艰巨。唯有驰而不息加强党的创新理论武装，才能在关键问题上澄清模糊认识，在攻坚任务前排除各种干扰，从而凝聚起团结奋进的强大力量。 守正创新，避免僵化教条。马克思主义是发展的理论、创新的理论，它的强大生命力就在于它的创造性。然而，现实中有的人却在学习理论的过程中未能把握守正与创新的辩证关系，陷入僵化教条的困境。历史上，王明“左”倾冒险主义给中国革命事业带来惨重损失，其错误根源就在于脱离中国革命实际，对马克思主义生搬硬套，搞教条主义。这一教训深刻警示我们，坚持用党的创新理论固本培元，决不能只会片面引用个别词句，而是必须把握马克思主义与时俱进的理论品格，学会运用马克思主义的立场观点方法来具体分析和解决现实问题。 三 道不可坐论，理不能空谈。坚持用党的创新理论固本培元，军队党员干部要做到自觉主动学、及时跟进学、联系实际学、笃信笃行学，努力成为勤奋学习、善于思考的模范，解放思想、与时俱进的模范，学以致用、用有所成的模范。 自觉主动学。知之者不如好之者，好之者不如乐之者。理论学习要取得实效，必须激发内在的学习热情，变“要我学”为“我要学”。军队党员干部必须深刻认识到理论学习的极端重要性，在理论学习上树立高远追求，耐得住清冷和寂寞，静下心来通读苦读，坚定学习意志、增强学习定力、强化学习自觉，真正把学习作为一种政治责任、一种精神追求、一种生活方式。 及时跟进学。马克思主义是随着时代和实践发展而不断发展的开放的理论体系，学习马克思主义理论，应做到党的理论创新每前进一步，理论武装就要跟进一步。因此，军队党员干部要做到及时跟进学，用联系的、发展的眼光看待理论学习，坚持第一时间学习党的创新理论最新成果，主动跟上理论创新的节拍，不让理论学习留有“空白期”、思想认识出现“时间差”，从而始终保持政治上的敏锐性、理论上的系统性和思想上的先进性。 联系实际学。实践性是马克思主义的鲜明特点。在党的历史上，什么时候理论联系实际坚持得好，党和人民事业就能够不断取得胜利。军队党员干部学习党的创新理论，必须大力弘扬理论联系实际的马克思主义学风，联系部队建设改革和备战打仗中遇到的矛盾问题，联系个人思想实际和工作实际，带着问题学、对着问题思、瞄着问题剖，切实把学习成果转化为谋划工作的思路、破解难题的办法、推动发展的举措。 笃信笃行学。提高理论学习的质量，关键在于学而信、学而行。只有不断增强对党的创新理论的政治认同、思想认同、理论认同、情感认同，才能真正做到学思用贯通、知信行统一。军队党员干部要从新时代以来党和人民事业取得的历史性成就、历史性变革中，深刻感悟习近平新时代中国特色社会主义思想的真理魅力和实践伟力，把学习成果转化为坚定的理想信念、过硬的政治能力、强烈的使命担当、优良的工作作风，以理论学习的新提升开创各项工作的新局面。
