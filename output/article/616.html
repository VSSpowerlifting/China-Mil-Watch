Advancing High-Quality Development of National Defense and Military Modernization Through Concrete Work and Concrete Results — All Levels Across the Armed Forces Deepen the Study and Education Campaign to Establish and Practice a Correct Outlook on Political Performance (正确政绩观)

以实干实绩推进国防和军队建设高质量发展——全军各级推动树立和践行正确政绩观学习教育走深走实

About this analysis
Summary
Across the PLA and People's Armed Police, a campaign to instill a "correct outlook on political performance" (正确政绩观) is running in parallel with the broader ideological rectification (思想整风) and political consolidation and training (政治整训) that Xi Jinping launched at an April 8 senior cadre training course; the article names units down to a brigade of the 75th Group Army, an Eastern Theater Command Air Force base, a Cyberspace Force unit, an Information Support Force unit, and a Military Space Force unit as participants, with the CMC dispatching five inspection and supervision groups to enforce compliance. The campaign documents the PLA's current institutional effort to root out what it frames as distorted performance incentives — careerism, formalism, and factional loyalty — by tying cadre evaluation explicitly to combat-effectiveness outcomes and the "15th Five-Year Plan" launch year. The framing points to leadership concern that previous rectification cycles left residual "harmful influences and entrenched malpractices" (流毒积弊) unremedied; what remains unknown is whether the problem lists compiled by individual units reflect genuine organizational dysfunction or pro forma compliance with a centrally mandated exercise.

Advancing High-Quality Development of National Defense and Military Modernization Through Concrete Work and Concrete Results — All Levels Across the Armed Forces Deepen the Study and Education Campaign to Establish and Practice a Correct Outlook on Political Performance (正确政绩观)

Xinhua News Agency reporters Mei Shixiong and Zhang Yongjian; PLA Daily reporter Li Bingzheng

With an attitude of bearing the highest responsibility to the Party and the people, strengthen the ideological and behavioral consciousness to establish and practice a correct outlook on political performance (正确政绩观), and uphold the principle of delivering political performance for the people and through concrete work.

Integrating the arming of minds with the transformation of thinking, tempering Party character cultivation through genuine and deep study; combining organizational examination and self-inspection, identifying the root causes of problems through profound reflection; coordinating "immediate correction" with "long-term establishment," advancing rectification and remediation to strict and solid standards… Since the launch of the study and education campaign to establish and practice a correct outlook on political performance (正确政绩观), all levels across the armed forces have deeply grasped the decisive significance of the "Two Establishments" (两个确立), strengthened the "Four Consciousnesses" (四个意识), reinforced the "Four Confidences" (四个自信), upheld the "Two Safeguards" (两个维护), implemented the system of Chairman of the Central Military Commission responsibility, earnestly implemented the decisions and deployments of the Party Central Committee, the Central Military Commission (CMC), and Chairman Xi Jinping, and deeply studied and implemented the spirit of Chairman Xi's important speeches and important instructions. Closely aligned with the goals and requirements of the study and education campaign, and coordinated with the armed forces' ideological rectification (思想整风) and deepened political consolidation and training (政治整训), all levels have organized and implemented the campaign with a strong sense of political responsibility and mission, advancing study, examination, and correction in an integrated manner, and converting the gains of the study and education campaign into the driving force and practical results for promoting high-quality development of the troops.

The CMC dispatched five inspection and supervision groups to conduct checks and oversight; various departments and units separately dispatched supervision groups to carry out routine oversight. Pressure and accountability were transmitted level by level, with strict correction of deviations. With the focus on advancing the resolution of prominent problems, the groups jointly studied issues, deliberated on countermeasures, and worked through difficulties together with officers and soldiers of the troops, driving the various deployment requirements of the study and education campaign to take root and produce results, and ensuring that study is of quality, examination has force, and correction is effective.

Closely Aligned with Ideological Rectification (思想整风) for Deep Study and Deep Comprehension

— Correcting Deviations in the Outlook on Political Performance (政绩观) at the Ideological Root

Always maintain the purity and glory of the people's army, and greet the centenary of the founding of the army with a completely renewed political bearing.

On April 8, Chairman Xi attended the opening ceremony of the senior cadre training course for the entire armed forces and delivered an important speech, making important deployments for carrying out ideological rectification (思想整风) and deepening political consolidation and training (政治整训) at this new juncture. On the journey of strengthening the military in the new era, Chairman Xi has led the entire armed forces back to Gutian to seek roots and origins and set things right, then to Yan'an to restore orthodoxy and temper resolve, ceaselessly advancing the political building of the military (政治建军), aligning thinking and aligning the ranks, and leading the people's army to always strive and strengthen itself under the Party's banner.

All levels across the armed forces have earnestly studied and implemented the spirit of Chairman Xi's important speeches, persisted in carrying out ideological rectification (思想整风) with the spirit of thorough self-revolution, restored orthodoxy and consolidated foundations through study, reflection, and practice, resolutely purged ideological impurities from the depths of the soul and corrected what has been inverted and distorted, and consciously engaged in work and endeavors with a new work style, new state, and new bearing, winning the trust of officers and soldiers.

Persisting in original and faithful study and comprehension —

For whom is political performance (政绩) built? What kind of political performance should be built? What should political performance be built on? These are the ideological questions that the study and education campaign must first resolve. All levels across the armed forces have persisted in using Excerpts from Xi Jinping's Discussions on Establishing and Practicing a Correct Outlook on Political Performance (《习近平关于树立和践行正确政绩观论述摘编》) and Xi Jinping on Strengthening and Invigorating the Military (《习近平论强军兴军》) as basic teaching materials, comprehending the essence and key points article by article, paragraph by paragraph, word by word and sentence by sentence, promptly following up on studying Chairman Xi's latest important speeches and important instructions, carefully appreciating Chairman Xi's intense sense of historical responsibility and deep feelings for the people, and deeply grasping the important significance, value orientation, and practical requirements of firmly establishing a correct outlook on political performance (正确政绩观). A certain brigade of the Army's 75th Group Army developed a study approach of "thematic research, immersive study, and scenario-based discussion," with Party committee standing members taking the lead in sharing gains in connection with study and reflection, sharing experiences in connection with work history, and sharing plans in connection with post responsibilities, achieving the goal that study yields thought, study yields comprehension, and study yields results. A certain base of the Eastern Theater Command Air Force, in response to the reality of troops conducting cross-regional mobility and being highly dispersed, built a study chain of "Party committee leading study, aircrew researching study, and combat positions practicing study," with leadership team members going down to the front lines to give lectures and guidance, "moving" the study classroom from agency conference rooms to the front lines of grassroots training.

Deepening discussion, analysis, and correction of deviations —

"Why did you join the Party? Why did you join the military? Why do you serve as a general?" Confronted with these thunderously sobering and searching questions, the broad ranks of Party member cadres placed themselves in the picture, placed their responsibilities in the picture, and placed their work in the picture, seeking gaps, tracing root causes, and strengthening cultivation from the standpoint of Party character, consciously drawing clear lines between genuine loyalty and false loyalty, between shouldering responsibility and failing to act or acting slowly, between working and creating versus seeking fame and personal gain, between exercising power impartially versus using power for private benefit… deeply rectifying ideological understanding and correcting value deviations. A certain naval base, focusing on which remaining harmful influences and entrenched malpractices (流毒积弊) have not yet been fully cleaned out and which erroneous ideological concepts have not yet been genuinely corrected, had Party committee leadership members participate in dual organizational life (双重组织生活) as ordinary Party members, taking the lead in examining, inspecting, and reflecting, and openly exposing their thinking. A certain unit of the Academy of Military Sciences, with an eye toward resolving root ideological problems such as ideals and convictions, Party character cultivation, and the moral character of officials, educated and guided Party member cadres not to dilute, avoid, or conceal shortcomings, and to strictly examine and reflect with an attitude of self-examination and deep root-tracing; each round of discussion and analysis was substantive and not a formality.

Examining and drawing lessons from positive and negative models —

Models are visible philosophy; lessons are a ruler with teeth. Each unit made good use of the "two mirrors" of positive and negative model cases: earnestly studying the correct outlook on political performance (正确政绩观) embodied in positive models, while also deeply analyzing the nature, harm, causes, and lessons of the deviations in the outlook on political performance (政绩观) of negative models, clarifying which "red lines" must not be crossed, which "bottom lines" must not be breached, and which "minefields" must not be entered, guiding Party member cadres to know shame and know restraint, and to act with a vigilant heart. A certain unit of the People's Armed Police organized agency cadres to visit the Beijing Aerospace Flight Control Center and other sites, tempering Party character and drawing strength from the vivid practice of aerospace personnel in establishing and practicing a correct outlook on political performance (正确政绩观) and driving innovative development of the aerospace enterprise. A certain academy of the National Defense University persisted in treating model cases as "living teaching materials" and warning education as a "sobering agent," studying notifications of disciplinary and legal violations, reading confessions of persons who committed disciplinary and legal violations, and watching warning education films, enabling Party member cadres to understand laws and discipline, know rules and regulations, and maintain reverence.

Persisting in Turning the Blade Inward for Genuine Examination and Real Correction

— Winning the Trust of Officers and Soldiers Through Tangible and Perceptible Results

Adhering to a problem-oriented approach is an important method of thinking and working, and also a powerful driver for deepening the study and education campaign. All levels across the armed forces have tackled rectification with an attitude of not waiting and not relying on others, correcting problems immediately, confronting difficulties head-on to resolve hard problems, and allowing officers and soldiers to genuinely feel the new atmosphere and new changes brought about by the study and education campaign.

Deep examination and careful scrutiny, firmly establishing targets for problem rectification —

Only by accurately "profiling" problems can rectification be practical. Each unit, distinguishing different levels and different types, organized Party member cadres to closely connect thinking, work, and responsibilities, and to compile lists of problems by cross-referencing Chairman Xi's important discussions on establishing and practicing a correct outlook on political performance (正确政绩观), the key problems to be resolved in the study and education campaign, and problems fed back through discipline inspection and supervision, inspection and oversight tours (巡视巡察), and audit supervision; leading cadres placed themselves "in the picture" and did not act as "outsiders," taking the lead in genuine, real, and deep examination, identifying problems precisely and specifically, with people, events, and thinking all made visible. A certain unit of the Cyberspace Force persisted in integrating organizational examination with individual examination and higher-level supervision with lower-level reporting, highlighted key industry sectors, combed through and examined problems in connection with the progress of consolidation and remediation, and at the same time focused on collecting opinions and suggestions from the troops both online and offline, dynamically updating the problem list.

Opening the door to accept advice, accepting evaluation and supervision from officers and soldiers —

In the voices of officers and soldiers lies the wisdom and strength to drive rectification and implementation. Party committees of regiment-level and above units genuinely walked the mass line by "truly opening the door" and "daring to open the door," proactively seeking strategies, needs, and assessments from officers and soldiers; conducting on-site research in various directions, holding discussions and conversations at various levels, and conducting questionnaire surveys at various echelons; through repeated heart-to-heart conversations and individual online messages, many opinions and suggestions from officers and soldiers entered the decision-making and planning of Party committee agencies. A certain unit of the Information Support Force carefully sorted through problems where "manifestations are below but roots are above," built a closed-loop chain of "asking, researching, answering, and supervising," and ensured that urgent, difficult, worrying, and anticipated issues (急难愁盼) raised by the grassroots "received a response in every case and had a resolution in every instance." A certain academy of the National Defense University of Science and Technology conducted immersive research, sitting on the same bench as officers, soldiers, and students, with feeling and warmth, listening to their thoughts and ideas, and asking about their hardships and difficulties, examining and reflecting on the deviations in the work guidance of Party committee agencies.

Genuine examination and real correction, practicing the original intent of establishing credibility through action (徙木立信) —

Can problems strongly reflected by the grassroots be truly taken seriously and quickly resolved? Have all commitments made been honored? All levels persisted in advancing the rectification and remediation of the study and education campaign in an integrated manner with the cleanup and remediation of deepened political consolidation and training (政治整训), firmly grasping key industry sectors and areas of civil-military intersection for strict management and governance, following up with strengthened strict supervision and solid guidance, and driving all sectors to break through unspoken rules and entrenched malpractices (潜规积弊) and purify the atmosphere. Starting with strict correction from Party committee agencies and leading cadres: problems that can be resolved immediately must absolutely not be dragged out; problems that are difficult to resolve in the short term must be firmly held and concentrated upon for breakthrough, genuinely converting the problem list into a list of duties to be fulfilled. A certain unit of the Military Space Force coordinated the deepening of the study and education campaign and the advancement of problem rectification with the consolidation and governance of key industry sectors, and combined it with doing good things, doing practical things, and resolving difficult things for officers and soldiers; rectifying one problem and closing the account on one, resolutely eliminating paper-only rectification and false rectification.

Emphasizing the Integration of Study and Application to Promote Transformation

— Concentrating Efforts to Drive High-Quality Development of the Troops

Establishing and practicing a correct outlook on political performance (正确政绩观) must ultimately be reflected in driving high-quality development. All levels persisted in combining the study and education campaign with driving combat readiness training, planning and construction, and grassroots construction, allowing "proceeding from reality and acting according to objective laws" to enter thinking and work guidance, and driving high-quality development through genuine hard work.

Devoting all thought and energy to war preparation and war-fighting —

If the battlefield cannot be won, everything equals zero. For combat effectiveness to be solid, combat readiness training must be real and concrete. Correcting the "flowery scaffolding" (花架子) of showy but impractical performances on the training ground, replaced by a training surge of full personnel, full equipment, and everyone striving to outdo one another; Party committee agencies providing forward guidance no longer engage in "paper-based strategizing" (纸上谈兵), but jointly work with grassroots officers and soldiers to break through the bottlenecks and blockages in combat effectiveness building; scientific research personnel go deep into exercise and training front lines, focusing on correcting deviations such as "emphasizing papers over application, emphasizing quantity over quality"… Throughout the entire armed forces, the drive and enthusiasm for accelerating the improvement of warfighting capability surges everywhere. Each theater command has cross-checked problems identified through inspection research, exercises, and training, refined the examination and correction of deviations in the outlook on political performance (政绩观) in the area of combat readiness and warfighting, and made whether it is conducive to improving combat effectiveness the hard standard for measuring all work, driving the firm establishment of the combat effectiveness standard.

Advancing planning and construction with a scientific and pragmatic attitude —

Being scientific and pragmatic is the way to plan affairs and also the method for accomplishing them. This year is the inaugural year of the "15th Five-Year Plan" (十五五) and also a critical year for the assault to build the military for its centenary; all levels have educated and guided Party member cadres to clearly understand the situation and tasks, strengthen their sense of mission and responsibility, vigorously stimulate the intrinsic motivation to work and create, and from the very start of this period firmly establish and solidly implement the correct outlook on political performance (正确政绩观). Each department and unit has seized the favorable opportunity of the study and education campaign, using the correct outlook on political performance (正确政绩观) to drive the formulation and execution of the "15th Five-Year Plan," planning for the future and strategizing for the long term, scientifically setting goals and tasks, refining and breaking down plans and measures, listing timetables, formulating roadmaps, and clarifying responsible parties, using reform to break through difficult problems, using innovation to increase vitality, and using concrete work to seek concrete results, striving to get the "15th Five-Year Plan" off to a good start.

Grasping grassroots work and laying foundations with the steadiness of sustained long-term effort —

The foundation for achieving the goal of a strong military lies at the grassroots level, and its vitality lies at the grassroots level; continuously grasping grassroots work and laying foundations is "latent achievement" (潜绩) that benefits the long term. Party committees and leading cadres at all levels have consciously strengthened the thinking of strengthening the foundation and consolidating the base, established the clear orientation of vigorously grasping the grassroots, and made great efforts to do work that manages fundamentals, benefits the long term, and builds staying power, continuously consolidating the foundations of troop building and combat effectiveness. Leadership team members of a certain unit of the Rocket Force went deep into the grassroots for immersive research and dissection of specific cases, implementing "one company, one policy" (一连一策) assistance and guidance for companies that had not been rated as advanced for many years, driving comprehensive progress and comprehensive excellence in grassroots construction; a certain unit of the Joint Logistic Support Force deeply carried out the activity of "going to the grassroots, grasping real conditions, and resolving difficult problems," with grassroots construction featuring fewer cold notifications and more warm mentoring and assistance, driving the resolution of a batch of practical problems that had long troubled the grassroots, and enabling the combat fortresses (战斗堡垒) at the grassroots level to be built stronger.

Party organizations and the broad ranks of Party member cadres across the entire armed forces are uniting more closely around the Party Central Committee with Comrade Xi Jinping at its core, using Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era as guidance, deeply implementing Xi Jinping's Thought on Strengthening the Military (习近平强军思想), consciously establishing and practicing a correct outlook on political performance (正确政绩观), working with greater high-spirited fighting will and a more pragmatic work style, going all out to fight and win the assault battle to achieve the centenary goal of the founding of the army, and striving to create real achievements that can withstand the tests of practice, the people, and history!

(Xinhua News Agency, Beijing, May 24)

以实干实绩推进国防和军队建设高质量发展 ——全军各级推动树立和践行正确政绩观学习教育走深走实 新华社记者梅世雄、张勇健 解放军报记者李兵正 以对党和人民高度负责的态度，增强树立和践行正确政绩观的思想自觉和行动自觉，坚持为人民出政绩、以实干出政绩。 把武装思想与改造思想贯通起来，以真学深悟锤炼党性修养；把组织查摆与个人检视结合起来，以深刻反思找准问题症结；把“当下改”与“长久立”统筹起来，以严实标准推进整改整治……树立和践行正确政绩观学习教育开展以来，全军各级深刻领悟“两个确立”的决定性意义，增强“四个意识”、坚定“四个自信”、做到“两个维护”，贯彻军委主席负责制，认真贯彻落实党中央、中央军委和习近平主席决策部署，深入学习贯彻习主席重要讲话和重要指示精神，紧扣学习教育目标要求，坚持与军队开展思想整风、深化政治整训统筹起来，以强烈的政治责任感和使命感认真组织实施，一体推进学查改，把学习教育收获转化为推动部队高质量发展的动力和实效。 中央军委派出5个督导组开展检查督导，各部门各单位分别派出督导组搞好常态督导，层层督责传压、从严纠偏正向，以推进解决突出问题为重点，与部队官兵共同研究、共商对策、共破难题，推动学习教育各项部署要求落地见效，确保学有质量、查有力度、改有成效。 紧扣思想整风深学深悟 ——从思想根子上校正政绩观偏差 始终保持人民军队纯洁光荣，以崭新政治面貌迎接建军一百周年。 4月8日，习主席出席全军高级干部培训班开班式并发表重要讲话，对在新的关口上开展思想整风、深化政治整训作出重要部署。新时代强军征程上，习主席率领全军重回古田寻根溯源、拨乱反正，再到延安正本清源、加钢淬火，一刻不停推进政治建军，整齐思想、整齐队伍，引领人民军队始终在党的旗帜下奋斗强军。 全军各级认真学习贯彻习主席重要讲话精神，坚持以彻底的自我革命精神开展思想整风，在学思践悟中正本清源、固本培元，切实从灵魂深处剔除思想杂质、把颠倒扭曲的东西正过来，自觉以新的作风、新的状态、新的面貌干事创业，取信官兵。 坚持原原本本学习领悟—— 政绩为谁而树？树什么样的政绩？靠什么树政绩？这是学习教育首先要解决的一个思想问题。全军各级坚持以《习近平关于树立和践行正确政绩观论述摘编》《习近平论强军兴军》等为基本教材，逐篇逐段、逐字逐句深悟精髓要义，及时跟进学习习主席最新重要讲话和重要指示精神，悉心体悟习主席的强烈历史担当和深厚为民情怀，深刻领悟树牢正确政绩观的重要意义、价值取向、实践要求。陆军第七十五集团军某旅走开“专题式研究、沉浸式研学、情景式研讨”学习路子，党委常委带头结合学习思考谈收获、结合工作经历谈体会、结合岗位职责谈打算，做到学有所思、学有所悟、学有所得。东部战区空军某基地针对部队跨区机动、高度分散的实际，构建起“党委领学、机组研学、战位践学”的学习链路，班子成员下沉一线宣讲辅导，把学习课堂从机关会议室“搬”到基层练兵一线。 深化讨论辨析纠偏正向—— “入党干什么？当兵干什么？为将干什么？”面对这些振聋发聩的责切叩问，广大党员干部把自己摆进去、把职责摆进去、把工作摆进去，从党性上找差距、查根源、强修养，自觉划清真忠诚与伪忠诚的界限、担当尽责与不作为慢作为的界限、干事创业与追名逐利的界限、秉公用权与以权谋私的界限……深入匡正思想认知、校正价值偏差。海军某基地围绕还有哪些流毒积弊没有清干净、还有哪些错误思想观念没有真正改到位，党委班子成员以普通党员身份参加双重组织生活，带头检视查摆反思、晾晒曝光思想。军事科学院某部着眼解决好理想信念、党性修养、官德人品等思想根子问题，教育引导党员干部不淡化、不回避、不遮丑，以反躬自省、深挖根源的态度从严检视反思，一场场讨论辨析不虚不空、不走过场。 对照正反典型省察镜鉴—— 榜样是看得见的哲理，教训是长了牙的戒尺。各单位用好正反面典型案例“两面镜子”，既认真学习正面典型蕴含的正确政绩观，又深刻剖析反面典型政绩观偏差的性质、危害、原因、教训等，搞清楚哪些“红线”不能踩、哪些“底线”不能越、哪些“雷区”不能闯，引导党员干部知耻知止、警心策行。武警部队某部组织机关干部走进北京航天飞行控制中心等，从航天人树立和践行正确政绩观、推动航天事业创新发展的生动实践中淬炼党性、汲取力量。国防大学某学院坚持把典型案例作为“活教材”，把警示教育作为“清醒剂”，学习违纪违法案例通报、阅看违纪违法人员忏悔录、观看警示教育片，让党员干部懂法纪、明规矩、知敬畏。 坚持刀口向内真查实改 ——以可感可及成效取信官兵 坚持问题导向，是重要的思想方法和工作方法，也是推动学习教育走深走实的有力牵引。全军各级以不等不靠、马上就改的态度抓整改，动真碰硬解难题，让官兵真切感受到学习教育带来的新气象新变化。 深查细照，立牢问题整改靶标——
