Braving the Scorching Sun, Battling the Heat Waves, Forging the Blade! Forces Across the Military Consolidate the Foundation for Victory in Sweltering Conditions

顶烈日 战热浪 淬锋芒！全军部队酷暑环境夯实打赢根基
PLA Daily (解放军报) 28 July 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
During the 三伏 (sanfu) peak-heat period, PLA Daily reports multi-service high-temperature training across China, citing a motor transport regiment under the Zhengzhou Joint Logistic Support Center conducting heavy-equipment transfer exercises in 45°C surface heat, CNS Changsha (长沙舰) maintaining combat posts in 60°C engine rooms in the South China Sea, Air Force units over Lingnan running formation and penetration-strike sorties, a PAP Guangxi Corps detachment testing UAV performance parameters in extreme heat, and a Rocket Force unit adopting staggered-peak and rotational training schedules. The article is a seasonal composite training report — its value is as a baseline record of how the PLA frames 夏练三伏 (summer heat training) as a readiness discipline spanning logistics, naval engineering, aviation, and missile forces, not as evidence of changed posture or new capability.
Translation

Forces Across the Military Carry Out Solid Combat-Realistic Training Under High-Temperature Conditions — Braving the Scorching Sun, Battling the Heat Waves, Forging the Blade

PLA Daily report by correspondent Fan Enda: As the hottest days of summer (三伏) arrive, the heat waves surge and troops are busy tempering themselves on the battlefield. On the open ocean, naval officers and soldiers hold their posts in high-temperature compartments and rapidly respond to emergencies; deep in dense forests, officers and soldiers of an Army combined-arms brigade face the heat waves head-on, conducting intensive live-equipment training…

As midsummer sets in and high temperatures persist across much of the country, units are proactively benchmarking themselves against combat requirements, closely aligning with the characteristics of high-temperature environments, and organizing high-intensity combat-realistic training. Officers and soldiers are forging their combat ethos and honing the hard skills needed to win victory amid sweltering heat.

[Caption: A Navy flotilla organizes replenishment training. Photo by Li Fengxiang.]

Heat waves sweep across the front lines of exercises and training, and the fervor for troop training continues to rise. In the heartland of central China, surface temperatures have exceeded 45 degrees Celsius; a motor transport regiment under the Zhengzhou Joint Logistic Support Center organized a heavy-equipment transfer and transport exercise, with officers and soldiers pressing forward into the oncoming heat to complete in sequence such training subjects as armored vehicle loading and securing, long-distance movement, and battlefield unloading. In the South China Sea, the Navy's CNS Changsha (长沙舰) pressed forward through the waves, with localized engine room temperatures reaching as high as 60 degrees Celsius; officers and soldiers held their combat posts, monitoring equipment operating conditions in real time and checking for safety hazards. Over Lingnan, the blazing sun hung high as multiple waves of aircraft from an Air Force unit took to the skies to conduct training subjects including formation coordination and penetration strikes… Units across the military have made high-temperature training a "required course" for enhancing combat effectiveness, training from a position of difficulty and strictness, and guiding officers and soldiers to challenge complex environments and physiological limits, continuously raising their solid capabilities to carry out diverse missions.

Units persist in letting combat requirements drive training, setting difficult and dangerous conditions based on high-temperature complex environments, and comprehensively tempering officers' and soldiers' emergency response, warfighting, and combined-arms operational capabilities. An Air Force unit exploited airspace conditions of intense heat, strong light, and complex air currents to organize tactical confrontation exercises, tempering pilots' on-the-spot judgment and mobile strike capabilities. Multiple field-encampment detachments from Army and People's Armed Police (武警) units advanced into high-temperature unfamiliar terrain and conducted in sequence full-element exercises and breakthroughs on difficult and critical training subjects, practicing command, coordination, and response under extreme conditions.

Summer training during the three hottest periods (夏练三伏) can effectively test the true combat-readiness levels of personnel and equipment under harsh environmental conditions. A detachment of the People's Armed Police Guangxi Corps conducted multiple rounds of extreme test flights to determine the performance parameters of unmanned aerial vehicles in high-temperature environments, refined dedicated flight contingency plans, and improved the combat effectiveness of equipment under extreme weather conditions. An Air Force unit compiled high-temperature training data from previous years, precisely calculated the limits of personnel physical endurance and the thresholds of equipment performance degradation, compiled an equipment maintenance manual for high-temperature environments, and refined procedures for seasonal fault inspection and emergency repair.

Scientific training organization improves the quality and effectiveness of troop training. In response to the realities of summer high-temperature training — including high physical exertion, elevated risk of heat stroke, and frequent equipment malfunctions — units are optimizing training organization models, strengthening logistic support and supply, and resolutely overcoming rough training methods and fatigue-inducing training.

A Rocket Force (火箭军) unit dynamically adjusted its training plan in accordance with summer climate characteristics, adopting models such as staggered-peak training and rotational training, advancing in a coordinated manner the consolidation of basic subjects alongside high-intensity confrontation and live training, and steadily improving the quality and effectiveness of troop training. A Navy unit carefully prepared heat-prevention and cooling supplies and heat-relief beverages and delivered them to the front lines of training. An Air Force station routinely carries out heat-prevention and cooling measures and medical standby duty for officers and soldiers, providing fixed-point support at airport front-line positions and elsewhere… Units are continuously studying and summarizing the patterns and characteristics of training under high-temperature conditions, providing solid support for the orderly conduct of combat-realistic training.

In recent days, officers and soldiers across the military have braved the scorching sun, battled the heat waves, and forged the blade — making up for training shortfalls and consolidating the foundation for winning victory in sweltering conditions, continuously driving a surge in summer combat training and war preparedness, and comprehensively enhancing units' capabilities to carry out missions at all times, across all territories, and in all weather conditions.

Original Chinese
全军部队扎实开展高温条件下实战化练兵 顶烈日 战热浪 淬锋芒 解放军报讯 记者范恩达报道：三伏热浪起，沙场砺兵忙。大洋之上，舰艇官兵坚守高温舱室，快速处置特情；密林深处，陆军某合成旅官兵直面热浪，紧张开展实装实训…… 进入盛夏，全国多地持续高温。各部队主动对标实战要求，紧贴高温环境特点，组织高强度实战化训练。官兵在蒸腾热浪中锤炼战斗作风，砥砺打赢硬功。 海军某支队组织补给训练。李凤祥摄 热浪席卷演训一线，练兵热潮持续高涨。中原腹地，地表温度突破45摄氏度，郑州联勤保障中心某汽车团组织重装倒运演练，官兵迎着扑面热浪，连贯完成装甲装载加固、长途机动、战地卸载等课目训练；南海海域，海军长沙舰破浪前行，机舱局部温度高达60摄氏度，官兵坚守战位，实时监测设备工况、排查安全隐患；岭南上空，烈日高悬，空军某部多批次战机升空，开展编队协同、突防突击等课目训练……各部队把高温练兵作为提升战斗力的“必修课”，从难从严施训，引导官兵挑战复杂环境和生理极限，不断提高遂行多样化任务的过硬本领。 各部队坚持实战牵引，立足高温复杂环境设难置险，全方位锤炼官兵应急应战与协同作战能力。空军某部利用高温强光、气流复杂的空域条件组织战术对抗，锤炼飞行员临机研判、机动突袭能力。陆军、武警部队多支野外驻训分队挺进高温陌生地域，连贯开展全要素演练和重难点课目攻关，在极限条件下练指挥、练协同、练处置。 夏练三伏，能有效检验恶劣环境下人员装备的真实作战水平。武警广西总队某支队通过多轮极限试飞，摸清无人机高温环境下性能参数，完善专项飞行预案，提升极端天候条件下装备实战效能。空军某部梳理历年高温训练数据，精准测算人员体能耐受极限与装备性能衰减阈值，编写高温环境下装备维护手册，细化季节性故障排查与应急抢修流程。 科学组训提升练兵质效。针对夏季高温训练体能消耗大、中暑风险高、装备故障多发等实际，各部队优化组训模式、强化保障供给，坚决克服粗放施训、疲劳训练。 火箭军某部结合夏季气候特点动态调整训练方案，采取错峰施训、轮训等模式，统筹推进基础课目巩固与高强度对抗实训，稳步提升练兵质效；海军某部精心准备防暑降温物资与解暑饮品，送至训练一线；空军某场站常态做好官兵防暑降温、医疗值守工作，在机场一线等进行定点保障……各单位不断研究总结高温条件下训练特点规律，为有序开展实战化训练提供坚实支撑。 连日来，全军官兵顶烈日、战热浪、淬锋芒，在酷暑环境下补齐训练短板、夯实打赢根基，持续掀起夏季练兵备战热潮，全面提升部队全时段、全地域、全天候遂行任务能力。
