Strong Military Forum | Carry Forward the Spirit of the War of Resistance, Unite Strength to Build a Strong Military

强军论坛丨弘扬抗战精神 聚力强军兴军
PLA Daily (解放军报) 7 July 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
On the 89th anniversary of the Marco Polo Bridge Incident (July 7), PLA Daily published a political education commentary calling on officers and soldiers to carry forward the 抗战精神 (spirit of the War of Resistance) and link historical memory directly to contemporary war preparation and military strengthening. The piece explicitly condemns Japan's handling of its wartime historical record and frames vigilance against Japanese militarism as an active, ongoing obligation rather than a ceremonial one. This is standard anniversary political work content; its value is as a record of how the institution connects Party-curated wartime history to the 忧患意识 (hardship awareness) and 使命意识 (mission awareness) it wants junior officers to internalize, not as evidence of a policy shift toward Japan.
Translation

Eighty-nine years ago today, the Marco Polo Bridge Incident erupted, marking the beginning of the entire Chinese nation's War of Resistance Against Japan. In recent days, military personnel and civilians across the country have commemorated the 89th anniversary of the outbreak of the War of Resistance in various forms. For officers and soldiers at large, remembering the history of the War of Resistance means, above all, carrying forward the spirit of the War of Resistance (抗战精神) and uniting strength to build and strengthen the military, contributing wisdom and strength to the comprehensive advancement of national rejuvenation and the great cause of national revitalization through Chinese-path modernization (中国式现代化).

The memory has never faded; history has never receded. On July 7, 1937, Japanese aggressors, driven by the criminal ambition to annex all of China by force, brazenly shelled Wanping City, manufacturing the Marco Polo Bridge Incident that shocked China and the world. Beiping and Tianjin were in crisis! North China was in crisis! The Chinese nation was in crisis! Confronted with an unprecedented existential threat to the nation and the people, on July 8 the Central Committee of the Chinese Communist Party issued the "Telegram of the Chinese Communist Party on the Japanese Military's Attack on the Marco Polo Bridge," calling upon: all compatriots throughout China, the government, and the military to unite and build a solid Great Wall of a national united front to resist the invasion of the Japanese aggressors. Actively promoted by the Chinese Communist Party, the Anti-Japanese National United Front (抗日民族统一战线) based on Kuomintang-Communist cooperation was formally established.

Chairman Xi has profoundly observed: "In the arduous War of Resistance Against Japan, all sons and daughters of China fought for the survival of the nation, for national rejuvenation, and for the justice of humanity. The breadth of social mobilization, the depth of national awakening, the tenacity of the will to fight, and the firmness of the conviction of certain victory all reached unprecedented heights." Confronted with the barbaric invasion of the Japanese aggressors, the sons and daughters of China rallied tightly under the banner of the Anti-Japanese National United Front advocated and established by the Chinese Communist Party. Countless patriotic officers and soldiers fought bloody battles; people from all walks of life were of one mind, demonstrating to the world the patriotic sentiment that every citizen bears responsibility for the rise and fall of the nation (天下兴亡、匹夫有责), the national integrity of facing death with equanimity and refusing to yield even unto death (视死如归、宁死不屈), the heroic spirit of fearing no brutality and fighting to the last drop of blood (不畏强暴、血战到底), and the unshakeable conviction of persevering through every setback (百折不挠、坚忍不拔). It was precisely under the firm leadership of the Chinese Communist Party, inspired by the great spirit of the War of Resistance, and through arduous and bloody struggle that the Chinese people utterly defeated the Japanese aggressors, achieved the great victory of the War of Resistance, and created "a marvel in the history of war, a heroic feat of the Chinese nation, and a momentous and earth-shaking achievement."

History is the best textbook and the best sobering agent. Commemorating the 89th anniversary of the outbreak of the War of Resistance means remembering history, safeguarding peace, and remaining vigilant against the resurgence of Japanese militarism. What is infuriating is that for a long time, Japan has not only failed to deeply reflect on its historical crimes and draw lessons from history, but has instead deliberately evaded and obscured its war responsibilities, continuously promoted an erroneous historical view of World War II, and distorted, denied, and even glorified its history of aggression and colonialism. However, history is history, and facts are facts. No one can ever change history and facts. In the face of ironclad evidence, any attempt to distort the history of World War II or glorify the war of aggression absolutely cannot succeed.

The War of Resistance Against Japan imparts to us a lesson written in blood and fire: a strong nation must have a strong military, and only with a strong military can the nation be secure. Without a powerful military, there can be no powerful nation. On the new journey, to prevent the historical tragedy from repeating itself and to prevent the process of the great rejuvenation of the Chinese nation from being interrupted, we must build a consolidated national defense and a powerful people's army commensurate with China's international standing and suited to the nation's security and development interests. As revolutionary soldiers of the new era, we must vigorously carry forward the great spirit of the War of Resistance, strengthen our sense of hardship awareness (忧患意识), crisis awareness (危机意识), and mission awareness (使命意识), reinforce our sense of responsibility and duty, unite strength in training for war and preparing for war, forge stronger capabilities and more reliable means, resolutely defend national sovereignty, security, and development interests, and effectively fulfill the mission tasks of the new era entrusted by the Party and the people.

Original Chinese
89年前的今天，卢沟桥事变爆发，标志着中国全民族抗战的开端。近段时间，全国军民采取不同形式纪念全民族抗战爆发89周年。对于广大官兵来说，铭记抗战历史，最重要的是弘扬抗战精神、聚力强军兴军，为以中国式现代化全面推进强国建设、民族复兴伟业贡献智慧和力量。 记忆从未褪色，历史从未远去。1937年7月7日，日本侵略者为达到以武力吞并全中国的罪恶野心，悍然炮轰宛平城，制造了震惊中外的卢沟桥事变。平津危急！华北危急！中华民族危急！面对空前的亡国亡种危机，7月8日，中共中央发出《中国共产党为日军进攻卢沟桥通电》，呼吁：全中国同胞，政府与军队，团结起来，筑成民族统一战线的坚固长城，抵抗日寇的侵略。在中国共产党的积极推动下，以国共合作为基础的抗日民族统一战线正式形成。 习主席深刻指出，“在艰苦卓绝的抗日战争中，全体中华儿女为国家生存而战、为民族复兴而战、为人类正义而战，社会动员之广泛，民族觉醒之深刻，战斗意志之顽强，必胜信念之坚定，都达到了空前的高度。”面对日本侵略者的野蛮入侵，中华儿女在中国共产党倡导建立的抗日民族统一战线旗帜下紧紧团结起来，千千万万爱国将士浴血奋战，各界民众万众一心，向世界展示了天下兴亡、匹夫有责的爱国情怀，视死如归、宁死不屈的民族气节，不畏强暴、血战到底的英雄气概，百折不挠、坚忍不拔的必胜信念。正是在中国共产党的坚强领导下，在伟大抗战精神的激励下，经过艰苦卓绝的浴血奋战，中国人民彻底打败了日本侵略者，取得抗战的伟大胜利，创造了“战争史上的奇观，中华民族的壮举，惊天动地的伟业”。 历史是最好的教科书，也是最好的清醒剂。纪念全民族抗战爆发89周年，就是要铭记历史、维护和平，警惕日本军国主义死灰复燃。令人愤慨的是，长期以来，日本不仅没有深刻反省历史罪责、汲取历史教训，反而刻意回避和模糊其战争责任，不断宣扬错误的二战史观，歪曲、否认甚至美化侵略和殖民历史。然而，历史就是历史，事实就是事实，任何人都不可能改变历史和事实。在铁证面前，任何歪曲二战历史、美化侵略战争的企图都不可能得逞。 抗日战争带给我们一个血与火的启示：强国必须强军，军强才能国安。没有一支强大的军队，就不可能有强大的国家。新征程上，不让历史悲剧重演，不让中华民族伟大复兴的进程被打断，就必须建设同我国国际地位相称、同国家安全和发展利益相适应的巩固国防和强大人民军队。作为新时代革命军人，必须大力弘扬伟大抗战精神，增强忧患意识、危机意识、使命意识，强化责任担当、聚力练兵备战，锻造更强大的能力、更可靠的手段，坚决捍卫国家主权、安全、发展利益，有效履行党和人民赋予的新时代使命任务。
