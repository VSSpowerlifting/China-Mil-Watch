The Military-Wide First Training Course on Enhancing Political Cadres' Ability to Organize and Conduct Political Education Concludes

全军首期提升政治干部组教施教能力培训班结业
PLA Daily (解放军报) 4 August 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
The National Defense University's College of Political Science concluded a one-month military-wide training course for propaganda and education backbone personnel from corps-level-and-above units, centered on improving political cadres' ability to organize and conduct political education (组教施教) through a 'five-in-one' teaching model and a three-day live-case graduation assessment simulating a major-mission propaganda backdrop with 24 embedded scenario categories. The course was explicitly framed as an extension of political rectification (政治整训), which documents the PLA's ongoing effort to standardize and professionalize grassroots ideological work capacity rather than simply transmit doctrine. The trainee accounts of past dysfunction—education that followed process without combat integration, methods that were stale but unreplaced—are the article's most useful baseline: they record what the institution itself identifies as the persistent weak links this course is designed to fix.
Translation

● Propaganda and education backbone personnel from units at the corps level and above participated in the training ● Explored and implemented a "five-in-one" teaching model

Reported by Liao Shikuan and Liu Jiali for PLA Daily: In the summer, the campus of the National Defense University's (国防大学) College of Political Science was lush with greenery as the military-wide first training course on enhancing political cadres' ability to organize and conduct political education (组教施教) successfully concluded. During the training, multiple propaganda and education backbone personnel from units at the corps level and above across the entire military gathered together to undergo one month of intensive tempering.

This training deeply studied and implemented Chairman Xi's important statements on ideological and political education, closely centered on the new situations and new tasks facing ideological and political education, and followed the overall approach of "studying theory, absorbing tradition, analyzing mechanisms, and drilling capabilities." It explored and implemented a "five-in-one" teaching model of "thematic instruction + problem-solving + experience exchange + situational experience + live-case practical training," helping trainees broaden their thinking on organizing and conducting education and comprehensively forging strong capabilities and qualities.

According to the relevant official in charge, this training covered instructional modules including guidance on theory and regulations, solving difficult problems in education management, mobilization and motivation empowerment, routine ideological work, and improving the effectiveness of education reform. During the training period, they organized a total of 13 thematic guidance sessions, 5 rounds of problem-solving efforts, 3 concentrated seminars, and 5 experience-sharing sessions, with rich and substantive content and tightly linked segments.

"We treated this training as an extended classroom for deepening political rectification (政治整训), threading the standard requirement of 'educators must first be educated, and those who forge the soul must first have their own soul forged' throughout the entire training." This official explained that the training explored the construction of a teaching content system deeply integrating "theory + tradition + mechanisms," maintaining throughout a commitment to aligning instruction with job positions, aligning practical operations with practice, and aligning outcomes with application, and working hard to resolve the key and difficult problems facing units in organizing and conducting education.

On the podium, expert professors imparted theoretical knowledge while propaganda and education backbone personnel from the troops shared practical insights; during discussions, trainees from various services and arms were mixed into groups to pool their wisdom and solve educational problems together… During instructional sessions, instructors not only vividly explained theoretical viewpoints and traced the historical context of their formation and development, but also closely combined the actual job situations and practical needs of trainees to impart operational skills, ensuring that trainees "know not only what, but also why." During seminars and exchanges, instructors focused on guiding trainees to find ideas, methods, and countermeasures grounded in policies and regulations, and to make genuine efforts and put in real work toward improving their capabilities.

During the training, the college carefully designed practical training segments to help trainees open up the chain of converting learning into application, and to temper and enhance their on-the-job capabilities through the mutual reinforcement of learning and application. One trainee said: "The training not only let us 'eat our fill,' but also helped us 'learn how to sow seeds.'"

"How do we strengthen the basic skills of knowing the situation, reasoning, counseling, and resolving difficulties?" "How can ideological and political education be made comprehensible, resonant, and moving for officers and soldiers?"… Faced with common questions raised by trainees, the college moved the training classroom into situational teaching halls and red-themed venues, organizing multiple distinctive immersive on-site teaching sessions.

At the Situational Teaching Hall of the Communist Manifesto (《共产党宣言》情境教学馆), trainees followed holographic imagery to trace the birth of this classic work, touching the power of truth at close range. At the Historical Situational Teaching Hall of Military Political Work (军队政治工作历史情境教学馆), instructors organized on-site teaching around the theme of "deeds speak louder than words; educators must first be educated," and trainees deepened their understanding of the "lifeline" role of political work through the blending of scene and sentiment. At a special organizational life session (组织生活会) held at the site of the First National Congress of the Communist Party of China, each trainee carefully examined and analyzed themselves, took stock of their gains and reflections, and underwent ideological tempering through "flushing red faces" and "breaking into a sweat."

"Political instructors are the vanguard in disseminating the Party's innovative theories and the main force in carrying out routine ideological work; they must possess strong abilities to organize and conduct education." The college's leadership explained that, in order to enhance trainees' ability to solve practical problems, this training invited expert professors from inside and outside the military as well as outstanding political instructors from across the entire military to deliver lectures, helping political cadres broaden their thinking and perspectives and master teaching methods. Trainees were encouraged to speak freely about solving common grassroots problems and to share fresh, firsthand experience from the front lines. "'Breaking the ice' in the way problems are solved requires that thinking break through first." One trainee stated that the lectures by expert professors provided new ideas for educational innovation and helped everyone break out of the fixed thinking of traditional education management.

As the training drew to a close, a full-process, full-element, live-case graduation assessment lasting three consecutive days commenced as scheduled. The assessment simulated the propaganda and education backdrop of a major mission, embedded 24 categories of complex scenarios, and authentically replicated various sudden and special situations. Trainees broke down service and branch boundaries and were mixed into groups, divided into three functional sub-groups for education planning, organizational implementation, and effectiveness verification. Through sub-group work, interactive questioning, and cross-evaluation, they completed within the allotted time such tasks as Party committee deliberation on education, education preparation, plan drafting, tracking and supervision, and quality and effectiveness assessment.

"Everyone is in training; every moment is inside a situation." Reflecting on their experience, one trainee said that this training comprehensively tempered their integrated capabilities of "managing education, organizing education, conducting education, and evaluating education" (抓教、组教、施教、评教). Together they refined more than 10 high-quality primary teaching plans and produced dozens of supporting practical operational results, providing reference for subsequent organizing and conducting of education.

This training adhered to the integrated development of education and training throughout, consistently gripping the capability orientation, emphasizing thinking training, reinforcing problem-driven approaches, and closely aligning with job needs. During the training preparation phase, we grounded ourselves in the new situations, new tasks, and new requirements of propaganda and education work, focused on the weak links in Party committee organs' organizing and conducting of education and the difficult, painful, and blocked points in grassroots education, and carefully designed the curriculum system on the basis of extensively soliciting opinions and suggestions from the troops, ensuring that topic settings closely matched needs and were practical and useful.

We constructed the teaching system against the backdrop of the troops' combat readiness tasks and the real-world problems of officers and soldiers, closely integrated training with practice, and relied on training subjects to carry out forward-looking, breakthrough, and original exploratory research, promoting the conversion of learning into application and producing a series of training results that are "ready to use as-is and doable by following along."

This training not only focused on consolidating trainees' theoretical foundations, but also worked hard to enhance their integrated capabilities of "managing education, organizing education, conducting education, and evaluating education." Through theoretical infusion, mutual learning and reference, and combat-realistic training, we hope to cultivate a group of "seed members" (种子队员) for organizing and conducting education who understand the rules, are skilled at overall coordination, and know how to operate.

When I first received notice to participate in the military-wide first training course on enhancing political cadres' ability to organize and conduct education, I had some concerns: would it still be the same "old routine" of reading documents and lecturing on theory, ending as always with "the suitcase carried in, the notebook closed on the way out, and everything handed back to the teacher two days later"?

After the course began, I found it was completely different. This training zeroed in on the difficult points of organizing and conducting education in the troops; the content was packed with substantive material, the methods were flexible and varied, and it had strong guidance, operability, and practical effectiveness. In particular, the training organization model of "everyone takes the podium to speak, everyone gets hands-on practice, and every day prompts reflection" effectively promoted the integration of knowledge and action and the conversion of learning into application, giving us an entirely new understanding of how to organize and conduct education, and truly achieving "coming with problems, leaving with gains."

The in-depth discussions with comrades from units across the various services and arms also benefited me greatly. Some comrades frankly admitted that in the past, when managing education they always thought it was enough to just follow the process through to the end, without close enough integration with actual combat requirements. This training's emphasis on live-case deduction helped us identify the methods and pathways for organizing education around combat readiness. Other comrades said that in the past they always suffered from "old methods no longer working, new methods not yet mastered." This training taught us many new immersive and interactive measures for organizing and conducting education, and we now have a practical "toolbox" for organizing and conducting education.

The end of training is also the starting point of practice. Returning to the unit, I will integrate what I have learned and gained into daily work, convert the training results into concrete actions guiding the unit in carrying out education, serve well as the "administrator" of plan management and control, the "dispatcher" of resource coordination, and the "quality inspector" of quality improvement and effectiveness enhancement, and strive to contribute to improving the quality and effectiveness of ideological and political education.

Original Chinese
●军级以上单位宣传教育骨干参训 ●探索推行“五位一体”教学模式 解放军报讯 廖诗宽、刘甲立报道：夏日，国防大学政治学院校园内满目苍翠，全军首期提升政治干部组教施教能力培训班顺利结业。培训期间，来自全军军级以上单位的多名宣传教育骨干齐聚一堂，接受为期一个月的集中淬火。 此次培训深入学习贯彻习主席关于思想政治教育的重要论述，紧紧围绕思想政治教育面临的新形势新任务，按照“学理论、悟传统、析机理、练能力”总体思路，探索推行“专题教学+难题破解+经验交流+情境体验+实案实训”“五位一体”教学模式，帮助参训学员拓宽组教施教思路，全面锻造过硬能力素质。 据相关负责人介绍，此次培训涵盖理论法规辅导、抓教难题破解、动员激励赋能、经常性思想工作、教育转改增效等教学板块。培训期间，他们共组织13场专题辅导、5轮难题攻关、3次集中研讨和5场经验分享，内容丰富充实、环节衔接紧密。 “我们将此次培训作为深化政治整训的延伸课堂，把‘教育者先受教育、铸魂者先铸其魂’的标准要求贯穿培训始终。”这名负责人介绍，此次培训探索构建“理论+传统+机理”深度融合的教学内容体系，全程坚持教学对接岗位、实操对接实践、成果对接运用，着力破解部队组教施教面临的重难点问题。 讲台上，专家教授讲授理论知识，部队宣传教育骨干分享实践感悟；讨论中，各军兵种学员混编分组，集智破解教育难题……培训授课中，教员不仅生动阐释理论观点，回溯其形成发展的历史脉络，还紧密结合学员岗位实际和现实需求传授实操技巧，确保“知其然更知其所以然”。研讨交流中，教员注重引导学员立足政策法规找思路、找方法、找对策，在提升本领上使实劲、下功夫。 培训中，该校精心设计实训环节，帮助学员打通学用转化链路，在学用相长中淬炼提升岗位任职能力。一名学员说：“培训不仅让我们‘吃饱了饭’，更帮助我们‘学会播种’。” “怎么练强知情、说理、疏导、解难基本功”“思想政治教育如何才能让官兵听得懂、有共鸣、受触动”……面对学员提出的共性问题，该校将培训课堂搬进情境教学馆和红色场馆，组织多场别开生面的沉浸式现地教学。 在《共产党宣言》情境教学馆，学员跟随全息影像探寻经典著作诞生历程，近距离触摸真理的力量；在军队政治工作历史情境教学馆，教员围绕“身教重于言教，教育者先受教育”主题组织现地教学，学员在情景交融中进一步认识政治工作的“生命线”作用；在中共一大会址召开的专题组织生活会上，每名学员认真检视剖析，盘点收获体会，在“红脸”“出汗”中接受思想洗礼。 “政治教员是传播党的创新理论的先锋队，是做好经常性思想工作的主力军，必须具备过硬组教施教能力。”该校领导介绍，为提升参训学员解决实际问题的能力，此次培训邀请军内外专家教授、全军优秀政治教员登台授课，帮助政治干部拓宽思维视野、掌握教学方法；鼓励学员围绕解决基层常见难题畅所欲言，分享来自一线的鲜活经验。“‘解题’方式破冰，思想要先突围。”一名学员表示，专家教授的授课，提供了教育创新的新思路，帮助大家跳出了传统抓教的固有思维。 培训尾声，一场连续3天的全流程全要素实案化结业考核如期展开。考核模拟重大任务宣传教育背景，嵌入24类复杂场景，真实还原多种突发特情。学员打破军兵种界限混合编组，划分为教育筹划、组织实施、成效检验3个功能小组，通过分组作业、互动质询、交叉点评等方式，在规定时间内完成党委议教、教育准备、方案拟制、跟踪督导、质效考评等工作。 “人人都在训练中，时时都在情况里。”谈及参训感受，一名学员说，这次培训全方位锤炼了“抓教、组教、施教、评教”综合能力，他们共打磨出10余份高质量教学主案，形成数十项配套实操成果，为后续组教施教提供借鉴参考。 这次培训坚持教育训练一体化展开，全程扭住能力指向、突出思维训练、强化问题牵引、紧贴岗位需求。培训筹备阶段，我们立足宣传教育工作新形势、新任务、新要求，聚焦党委机关组教施教薄弱环节、基层教育难点痛点堵点，在广泛征求部队意见建议基础上精心设计课程体系，确保课题设置紧贴需求、务实管用。 我们以部队练兵备战任务、官兵现实问题为背景构建教学体系，将培训与实践紧密结合，依托训练课目开展前瞻性、突破性、原创性探索研究，促进学用转化，形成一系列“拿来就能用、照着就能做”的培训成果。 此次培训不仅注重夯实学员理论根基，更着力提升其“抓教、组教、施教、评教”综合能力。通过理论贯注、互学互鉴、实战实训等方式，我们希望培育一批懂规律、善统筹、会操作的组教施教“种子队员”。 接到参加全军首期提升政治干部组教施教能力培训班通知之初，我有些担心：会不会仍是念文件、讲理论的“老一套”，最后一如既往“箱子一提去了、本子一合回了，过两天全还给老师了”。 开班之后，我发现完全不一样。这次培训紧盯部队组教施教难点，内容干货满满，方式灵活多样，具有很强的指导性、操作性、实效性。尤其是“人人上台讲、个个上手练、天天促思考”的组训模式，有效促进了知行贯通、学用转化，让我们对如何组教施教有了全新认识，真正实现了“带着问题来，装着收获走”。 与来自各军兵种部队战友的深入研讨，更让我获益良多。有的战友坦言，以前抓教育总想着按流程走完就行，与实战要求结合不够紧密，这次培训注重实案推演，帮助我们摸准了围绕备战打仗抓教育的方法路径；还有的战友表示，以前总苦于“老办法不管用、新办法不会用”，这次培训学到不少沉浸式、互动式组教施教新举措，我们组教施教有了实用“工具箱”。 培训的终点也是实践的起点。回到部队，我将把所学所获融入日常工作，将培训成果转化为指导部队开展教育的实际行动，当好计划管控的“管理员”、资源统筹的“调度员”、提质增效的“质检员”，努力为提升思想政治教育质效贡献力量。
