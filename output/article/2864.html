PLA Garrison in Macao Holds Reception Celebrating the 99th Anniversary of the Founding of the Chinese People's Liberation Army

驻澳门部队举行庆祝中国人民解放军建军99周年招待会
PLA Daily (解放军报) 31 July 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
The PLA Macao Garrison held its annual founding-anniversary reception at Taipa Barracks on July 31, attended by roughly 200 guests including CPPCC Vice Chairman He Houhua, Chief Executive Sam Hou Fai, and Garrison Commander Major General Chen Wensheng and Political Commissar Major General Lian Wei. Chen's remarks foregrounded the garrison's role as a '定海神针' (stabilizing anchor) and tied future work explicitly to Xi's inspection instructions and the 15th Five-Year Plan's opening phase. This is standard garrison political-work content; its value is as a record of how the Macao Garrison frames its legitimacy to local elites and patriotic organizations, not as evidence of any change in posture or mission.
Translation

On the morning of July 31, the People's Liberation Army Garrison in Macao held a reception at the Taipa Barracks to celebrate the 99th anniversary of the founding of the Chinese People's Liberation Army. The guests of honor were: He Houhua, Vice Chairman of the National Committee of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference; Sam Hou Fai, Chief Executive of the Macao Special Administrative Region; Zheng Xincong, Director of the Liaison Office of the Central People's Government in the Macao Special Administrative Region; Bian Lixin, Commissioner of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the People's Republic of China in the Macao Special Administrative Region; Major General Chen Wensheng, Commander of the Macao Garrison, and Major General Lian Wei, Political Commissar of the Macao Garrison; and Zheng Yongchun, President of the Legislative Assembly of the Macao Special Administrative Region.

Major General Lian Wei, Political Commissar of the Macao Garrison, presided over the reception. At 10:30 a.m., accompanied by the solemn strains of the "March of the Chinese People's Liberation Army," the reception formally commenced. At the event, Macao Garrison Commander Major General Chen Wensheng and Macao SAR Chief Executive Sam Hou Fai each delivered remarks. The reception concluded with guests from all sectors joining together to view a photo exhibition commemorating the 90th anniversary of the victory of the Long March of the Chinese Workers' and Peasants' Red Army.

In his remarks, Garrison Commander Chen Wensheng warmly recalled the glorious journey traveled by the people's army under the leadership of the Chinese Communist Party and its historic contributions to national rejuvenation, reviewed the Macao Garrison's fulfillment of its defense duties over the 27 years since its stationing, and expressed gratitude for the long-standing care and support extended to the garrison by the SAR government and all sectors of Macao society.

Chen Wensheng stated that since stationing, the Macao Garrison has strictly implemented the "one country, two systems" principle, the Basic Law of the Macao SAR, and the Garrison Law; has maintained a firm grip on political development and combat readiness training; has carried forward the tradition of loving Macao and being close to the people; has lawfully fulfilled its defense duties; and has played the important role of a "stabilizing anchor" (定海神针) for safeguarding Macao's prosperity and stability, earning widespread praise from Macao compatriots. The Macao Garrison will thoroughly implement the spirit of Chairman Xi's important instructions during his inspection of the garrison, ground its work in the construction tasks of the opening phase of the 15th Five-Year Plan, anchor its war-preparedness efforts to achieving the centenary goal of the armed forces, continuously consolidate its foundational development and enhance its defense capabilities, take root in the Hao River and safeguard Macao's long-term peace and stability, and strive to be loyal guardians in whom the Party, the people, and Macao compatriots can place their trust and confidence.

In her remarks, Macao SAR Chief Executive Sam Hou Fai extended festival congratulations to the officers and soldiers of the Macao Garrison and expressed gratitude for the garrison's sustained and strong support for the SAR's development.

Approximately 200 guests from all sectors attended the reception by invitation, including principal officials of the Macao SAR Government; heads of central government agencies stationed in Macao and mainland Chinese-funded institutions in Macao; National People's Congress deputies and CPPCC members from the Macao delegation; student representatives from universities and previous military summer camps; and Macao patriotic and Macao-loving organizations.

Authors: Li Dongdong, Zhou Yafeng, Deng Jiaxuan

Original Chinese
7月31日上午，中国人民解放军驻澳门部队在氹仔军营举行招待会，庆祝中国人民解放军建军99周年。 全国政协副主席何厚铧，澳门特别行政区行政长官岑浩辉，中央人民政府驻澳门特别行政区联络办公室主任郑新聪，中华人民共和国外交部驻澳门特别行政区特派员公署特派员卞立新，驻澳门部队少将司令员陈文胜、少将政治委员练伟，澳门特别行政区立法会主席张永春担任主礼嘉宾。 驻澳门部队少将政治委员练伟主持招待会。上午10时30分，伴随着庄严的《中国人民解放军军歌》，招待会正式开始。会上，驻澳门部队少将司令员陈文胜和澳门特别行政区行政长官岑浩辉分别致辞。最后，各界来宾共同参观了纪念中国工农红军长征胜利90周年图片展。 驻澳门部队司令员陈文胜在致辞中，深情回顾在中国共产党领导下人民军队走过的光辉历程、为民族复兴作出的历史性贡献，回顾驻澳门部队进驻27年来履行防务情况，感谢特区政府和澳门社会各界长期以来对驻军的关心支持。 陈文胜表示，驻澳门部队进驻以来严格落实“一国两制”方针、澳门特区政府基本法与驻军法，狠抓政治建设、练兵备战，传承爱澳亲民传统，依法履行防务职责，发挥维护澳门繁荣稳定的“定海神针”重要作用，广受澳门同胞好评。驻澳门部队将深入贯彻习主席视察驻军重要指示精神，立足“十五五”开局阶段建设任务，锚定实现建军一百年奋斗目标推进备战工作，持续夯实建设根基、提升防务能力，扎根濠江、守护澳门长治久安，争做党和人民放心、澳门同胞信赖的忠诚卫士。 澳门特别行政区行政长官岑浩辉在致辞中，向驻澳门部队官兵致以节日的祝贺，对驻军长期以来对特区发展的有力支持表示感谢。 澳门特别行政区政府主要官员，中央驻澳机构和驻澳中资机构负责人，澳区全国人大代表、政协委员，高校及往届军事夏令营学生代表，澳门爱国爱澳社团等约200名各界人士应邀出席招待会。 作者：李冬冬、周亚峰、邓家轩
