Strong Military Forum | On the New Journey, the August 1st Military Flag Is Calling

强军论坛丨新征程上，八一军旗在召唤
PLA Daily (解放军报) 31 July 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
A PLA political commentary published ahead of Army Day 2025 calls on officers and soldiers to treat the approach of the PLA's centenary founding anniversary on August 1, 2027 as the defining deadline for achieving the 建军一百年奋斗目标 (centenary army-building goal), framing the intervening two years as a 'critical period' requiring intensified war preparation and military training. The piece is standard political work content for the Army Day cycle; its analytical value is as a record of how PLA media is calibrating urgency around the 2027 milestone, reinforcing that date as an organizing pressure point rather than introducing any new policy line or operational development.
Translation

On the New Journey, the August 1st Military Flag Is Calling

■ Zhang Shunliang

August 1st — a day that marks the glory and dreams of the people's armed forces.

Year after year, August 1st comes; and now August 1st is here again. The Chinese People's Liberation Army is about to celebrate its 99th birthday.

Ninety-nine years of wind rolling the battle flag red; ninety-nine years of hot blood forging loyalty. One military flag, dyed red through a century of trials; one military spirit (军魂), casting ten thousand li of mountains and rivers. The crimson August 1st Military Flag carries the momentous years of an armed force, inscribes the unyielding backbone of a nation, and bears witness to the magnificent journey from the founding of the army to the strengthening and revitalization of the military.

From the thunderclap at dawn over Nanchang that ignited the spark of armed revolution, to the surging tide of military strengthening in the new era with armored forces striding boldly forward — generation after generation of revolutionary soldiers have carried forward glorious traditions, continued the red bloodline, remained committed to strengthening the military and preparing for war, and concentrated their efforts on tempering the sword for victory. The battle flag has always stood tall atop mountains and rivers, shining at the forefront of the drive to build a strong military.

The red of the August 1st Military Flag is the "red of the original aspiration (初心红)" dyed by the blood of martyrs, the "red of faith (信仰红)" forged by the soldiers' wholehearted loyalty, and the "red of a prosperous age (盛世红)" upon which the nation's peace and security rests.

At this very moment, officers and soldiers guarding the borders sleep with their weapons at the ready, holding fast to their posts, safeguarding the tranquility of the frontier with their youth; great-power warships cleave through waves and patrol the deep blue, building an iron wall to secure the maritime gateway; guardians of the blue sky soar on the wind and look down upon the heavens, protecting ten thousand li of sky and clouds with their lofty aspirations.

Regardless of what post they occupy, what work they are responsible for, or what mission they undertake, every officer and soldier must always hold the great cause of family and nation in their hearts, keep the great enterprise of military strengthening in mind, plant faith deep in their hearts and carry their mission on their shoulders, interpret the soldier's sense of responsibility through tireless striving, write the answer sheet of military strengthening through courageous struggle, and let the August 1st Military Flag shine with the light of victory at all times.

Only by knowing where one comes from can one understand where one is going. The founding of the army was to break through the darkness and establish our military spirit (军魂); the strengthening of the military is to defend the territory, pacify the nation, protect the country, and deter threats from afar. The crimson August 1st Military Flag carries the glory of victory, and also embodies the great expectations of the era and the future of a strong military.

On August 1st, 2027, the Chinese People's Liberation Army will celebrate the 100th anniversary of its founding. Looking toward tomorrow, the time has come for a critical period in achieving the centenary goal of army building (建军一百年奋斗目标). This is the solemn responsibility of the era that the people's armed forces must shoulder, and the historical answer sheet they must deliver. The mission is glorious; the responsibility is great.

The banners surge; thunder and wind stir. Standing at "99" and gazing toward "100," the military flag flutters and flies in the wind — the signpost lies underfoot, the dawn lies ahead. Revolutionary soldiers of the new era are born at the right time, yet carry heavy responsibilities on their shoulders; those fortunate enough to be part of this must also work hard within it. This is both a gift of life and a stage for striving.

The red of the August 1st Military Flag is red because the original aspiration remains unchanged, red because loyalty is forever steadfast, red because the struggle never ceases. Chairman Xi has pointed out that the greater the cause, the more it is filled with challenges, and the more it requires bearing heavy burdens with full awareness of their weight. On the new journey, the situation is more severe, the tasks are more arduous, and the mission is more glorious. Every officer and soldier must carry on the past and open up the future with a high-spirited bearing and a striving posture, forging ahead with determination, working hard and practically, and overcoming difficulties and obstacles.

Where the military flag points, that is where the mission reaches; where the war drums sound, that is where the charge must go. "The best way to celebrate the anniversary of a great revolution is to concentrate attention on the unfinished tasks of the revolution." The soldier's mission, in its most essential interpretation (硬核的诠释), is to sharpen military skills and capabilities and advance toward war; the glory of the military flag, in its most substantive foundation (厚重的底色), is to strive and forge ahead, strengthening and revitalizing the military.

The glory of history has already been inscribed in the annals; a brand-new journey is now unfolding underfoot. Standing at a new historical starting point, let us forge our souls with loyalty, strengthen our capabilities through military training, fulfill our pledges through dedication, and achieve merit through hard work — refining our military skills and capabilities at our posts, facing difficulties with courage and determination, merging the passion of youth into the cause of military strengthening, and pouring the sweat of our striving onto the path of military strengthening, so that the August 1st Military Flag may forever fly high on the new journey.

Original Chinese
新征程上，八一军旗在召唤 ■张顺亮 8月1日，一个标注着人民军队光荣与梦想的日子。 岁岁“八一”，今又“八一”。中国人民解放军将迎来99岁生日。 99年风卷战旗红，99载热血铸忠诚。一面军旗，染红百年风雨；一腔军魂，铸就万里山河。鲜红的八一军旗，承载着一支军队的峥嵘岁月，镌刻着一个民族的不屈脊梁，见证着从建军之始到强军兴军的壮阔征程。 从南昌城头惊雷破晓、点燃武装革命星火，到新时代强军浪潮奔涌向前、铁甲雄师阔步前行，一代代革命军人弘扬光荣传统、赓续红色血脉，矢志强军备战、聚力砺剑打赢，猎猎军旗始终屹立于山川之巅、闪耀在强军潮头。 八一军旗红，是先烈热血染就的“初心红”，是将士赤诚铸就的“信仰红”，是祖国安宁依托的“盛世红”。 此时此刻，戍边官兵枕戈待旦、坚守战位，以青春守望边关安宁；大国战舰劈波斩浪、巡弋深蓝，以铁壁筑牢海上门户；蓝天卫士御风翱翔、傲视苍穹，以壮志守护万里云天。 无论身处什么岗位、负责什么工作、承担什么任务，每名官兵始终胸怀家国大义、心系强军伟业，把信仰根植心底、把使命扛在肩头，用不懈拼搏诠释军人担当，用英勇奋斗书写强军答卷，让八一军旗时刻闪耀胜利光芒。 知其所来，才能明其将往。建军，是破开黑暗、立我军魂；强军，是守土安邦、护国御远。鲜红的八一军旗承载着胜利荣光，更寄托着时代厚望、强军未来。 2027年8月1日，中国人民解放军将迎来建军100周年。瞩望明天，实现建军一百年奋斗目标到了关键期。这是人民军队必须扛起的时代重任、必须交出的历史答卷。使命光荣，责任重大。 旌旗奋，风雷动。站在“99”，眺望“100”，军旗猎猎、迎风飘扬，路标就在脚下，曙光就在前方，新时代革命军人生逢其时，也重任在肩，有幸身处其中更要干在其中。这是人生的际遇，也是奋斗的舞台。 八一军旗红，红在初心不改，红在忠诚永固，红在奋斗不息。习主席指出，越是伟大的事业，越是充满挑战，越需要知重负重。新征程上，形势更加严峻、任务更加艰巨、使命更加光荣。每名官兵必须以昂扬面貌和奋进姿态继往开来、砥砺前行，苦干实干、攻坚克难。 军旗所向，即是使命所至；战鼓所鸣，必是冲锋所向。“庆祝伟大革命的纪念日，最好的办法是把注意力集中在没有完成的革命任务上。”军人的使命，硬核的诠释是精武强能、向战而行；军旗的荣光，厚重的底色是拼搏奋进、强军兴军。 历史荣光已然镌刻史册，崭新征程正在脚下铺展。站在新的历史起点，让我们以忠诚铸魂、以练兵强能、以奉献践诺、以实干建功，立足岗位精武强能，直面困难勇毅攻坚，将青春热血融入强军事业，把拼搏汗水洒在强军征途，让八一军旗在新征程上永远高高飘扬。
