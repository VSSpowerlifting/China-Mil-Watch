Focusing on Improving Inspection and Supervision Work Methods: What Lessons Can Be Drawn from These Two Units

聚焦改进检查督导工作方式，看这两个单位有何借鉴之法
PLA Daily (解放军报) 12 August 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
A regiment of the Xinjiang Military District and a brigade of the Eastern Theater Command Navy have each revised their internal inspection and supervision (检查督导) procedures after headquarters cadres issued formal criticism notices against grassroots units for training schedule adjustments and supply management lapses that were caused by objective conditions—altitude weather at 5,100 meters in the Karakoram range and a mid-rotation position move, respectively. Both units now require inspectors to apply a 'three distinctions' (三个区分) framework separating objective constraints from subjective failures before issuing qualitative findings, and the Xinjiang regiment has added a fault-tolerance mechanism and embedded assistance (蹲点帮带) rotations to replace door-to-door inspection visits. The article documents a recognized institutional pathology in PLA headquarters-grassroots relations—inspectors inflating or mislabeling findings to demonstrate their own diligence, what the brigade leadership names as a deviation in the 'view of achievements' (政绩观)—and the two cases together extend a visible pattern of PLA political work media pushing back against formalism in unit oversight, though whether these brigade- and regiment-level procedural fixes propagate upward or laterally remains unaddressed.
Translation

Inspection and supervision is an important means by which higher headquarters guide and assist the building of grassroots units and advance the implementation of grassroots work. However, in practice, because a small number of headquarters cadres hold muddled views and maintain an unreliable work style, inspection and supervision becomes distorted and goes off course—not only failing to help grassroots units identify and resolve problems, but also disrupting the normal order of grassroots units. This issue's "Special Focus" centers on the experiences of a certain regiment of the Xinjiang Military District and a certain brigade of the Eastern Theater Command Navy in improving their inspection and supervision work methods, in the hope of offering readers some reflection and insight.

—The Editors

Certain Regiment of the Xinjiang Military District——

Inspection and Supervision: Listen More to What the Grassroots Has to Say

■ Gao Chuanshuo, Teng Yufei

"According to the plan, shouldn't specialized skills training be organized at this time?" Some time ago, headquarters cadres from a certain regiment of the Xinjiang Military District went to grassroots units to conduct inspection and supervision. Checking against the training weekly schedule, they found that the 7th Red Company was conducting physical fitness training during the time slot designated for specialized skills training, and accordingly concluded that the company's training ethos was unreliable and that it was not organizing training according to plan.

However, the company had good reason for doing so: the field training site where they were stationed was located deep in the Karakoram range between two mountains, at an altitude of over 5,100 meters, with complex and changeable weather conditions that easily affected outdoor training subjects such as physical fitness training. Initially, they attempted to move training to a sunroom, but the indoor space was limited and some training items still could not be conducted; over time, the personnel's training scores declined noticeably. After some deliberation, the company therefore decided to adapt to local conditions and adjust training content at any time based on weather.

Just before the headquarters inspection team arrived, the company had swapped the scheduled times for physical fitness training and specialized skills training due to weather conditions—and was caught in the act.

What was in fact a pragmatic measure adapted to local conditions was about to result in a public criticism notice. Company Commander Zhao was filled with bitterness. He watched the headquarters inspection team gradually recede into the distance, his hand gripping the training weekly schedule alternately loosening and tightening, until finally he could only heave a long sigh.

At the subsequent two-way review meeting between headquarters and grassroots units, Company Commander Zhao recounted the experience: "We did not fully follow the training weekly schedule, but we only adjusted the time sequence in which the various training subjects were conducted—we absolutely did not engage in skewed or missed training. This was something we were compelled to do by objective factors. It is beyond reproach for headquarters to act by the book, but one cannot be overly doctrinaire and rigid; when pointing out problems, one must also consider the actual circumstances of the grassroots units…"

Company Commander Zhao's remarks resonated with the soldier representatives present. Some said that in plateau areas the climate can differ greatly within just a few kilometers, and that when the company considered adjusting uniforms based on actual conditions, it was publicly criticized by headquarters for "failing to wear uniforms uniformly as required." Others frankly stated that when a sudden family crisis arose, they obtained company approval to receive a mobile phone to contact family members, only to be criticized by headquarters for "unauthorized use of a mobile phone"…

Company Political Instructor Wang also recounted his own experience: during an inspection and supervision visit, headquarters found that some oxygen cylinders were empty and therefore concluded that the company had "inadequate support and was not thoroughly implementing the oxygen-refilling system." However, this was because the company had suffered a sudden electrical fault the previous evening, rendering the oxygen generators equipped to some squads and platoons inoperable, leaving personnel with no choice but to use the oxygen cylinders.

"When the oxygen generators cannot be used, isn't using oxygen cylinders for support precisely what oxygen cylinders are for? Surely we shouldn't, for the sake of passing inspection, leave the oxygen cylinders sitting on a shelf and make the soldiers tough it out? Before headquarters reaches a conclusion, shouldn't it first understand the relevant circumstances, or at least give the grassroots unit an opportunity to explain?" Political Instructor Wang's questions left more than a few of the headquarters cadres present flushed with embarrassment.

After a long silence, Section Chief Fan of the troop management section took the lead in self-reflection: "The purpose of headquarters inspection and supervision is to rectify problems and standardize development—not to issue public criticism notices for the sake of issuing public criticism notices. There is nothing wrong with grassroots units making reasonable adjustments based on actual circumstances; we truly should not generalize from one instance."

Headquarters personnel then engaged in reflection one after another, clarifying that compliance of ledgers and synchronization of plans should not be the sole standard for measuring work, recognizing that methods such as checking for problems through a screen and making judgments by mechanically copying regulations are manifestations of being divorced from grassroots realities, and determining that these must be rectified.

After the meeting, the regiment routinely carried out embedded assistance (蹲点帮带), selecting headquarters cadres to be stationed permanently at grassroots sites, transforming "door-to-door supervision and inspection" into "embedded point assistance," helping headquarters cadres abandon "office thinking" and "one-size-fits-all modes," and genuinely going into the grassroots to understand actual conditions and confront contradictions and problems head-on. To improve the quality and effectiveness of inspection, supervision, and guidance work, they also established a fault-tolerance and error-correction mechanism that distinguishes whether the cause of a problem is subjective dereliction of duty and responsibility or objective constraints; for problems of an incipient or tendential nature, or problems caused by objective reasons, the emphasis is on on-site reminders, immediate correction, and guidance toward standardization, with excessive public criticism notices and repeated accountability strictly prohibited.

In addition, the regiment opened up channels for grassroots feedback, established a commander's mailbox, and routinely collected grassroots difficulties, personnel demands, and work suggestions, creating reverse pressure on headquarters to firmly establish a service-to-the-grassroots mindset, improve work methods, and transform work style.

In recent days, the regiment's troop management section has taken the lead in coordinating with various functional sections to investigate and identify construction difficulties at field training sites, and has compiled a list of difficulties to be resolved and a list of time-limited rectification and assistance responsibilities, receiving favorable reviews from the grassroots.

Certain Brigade of the Eastern Theater Command Navy——

Public Criticism Notices: Don't Jump to Conclusions Lightly and Carelessly

■ Pan Wei, Wang Yinyin, Dong Shizhao

"Time was pressing and the inventory and registration of supplies was still in progress—surely that's no basis for concluding that we had 'slack thinking' and a 'loose work style'? Our company commander only wanted to explain the specific circumstances, yet that became 'an improper attitude'…" In the height of summer, when the leadership of a certain brigade of the Eastern Theater Command Navy went to the grassroots to conduct research, a recounted experience by Second-Class Staff Sergeant Yang Gang of a certain company drew their attention.

Previously, the brigade's headquarters inspection team had gone to a field training site to conduct inspection and supervision. Because the company's personnel had only recently moved into the site and some work was still in progress, there were some lapses in internal affairs standards and the implementation of regulations, which the inspection personnel recorded. Company Commander Chen had wanted to offer an explanation, but was seen as making excuses, and the company was criticized for "slack thinking and loose work style among personnel during field training, and an improper rectification attitude on the part of the unit's cadres."

"We did indeed have a problem with inadequate inventory and registration of supplies at the time, but that is a completely different matter from having problems with thinking and work style." Yang Gang believed that there was nothing wrong with headquarters conducting inspection and guidance work, but that it also had to be truthful and realistic and could not make reckless conclusions.

After conducting a thorough investigation, the brigade leadership found that similar situations had been far from rare in past headquarters inspection and supervision processes: a certain company's clerk, having too much work on hand, failed to register the company's routine work in a timely manner and was characterized by headquarters as "failing to fulfill duties and responsibilities"; a certain company's equipment maintenance was inadequate and the company was criticized by headquarters for "insufficient combat-readiness awareness"… In the eyes of a small number of headquarters cadres, it seemed that the higher the "pitch" of criticism, the better it demonstrated their own achievements.

In response to these situations, the brigade's headquarters cadres frankly acknowledged their own concerns: "If inspection and supervision only records minor and miscellaneous matters, it seems to reflect a low level of competence"; "If the problem list is not comprehensive, there is worry that leadership will think one's work is not conscientious"; "Amplifying problems in the telling can better serve as a warning"…

"When all is said and done, it is still a deviation in the view of achievements (政绩观)—treating grassroots problems as one's own accomplishments." On this, the brigade leadership took a clear-cut stance: "It is not that the grassroots cannot withstand criticism—it is that headquarters' work thinking and guidance methods have problems!" They pointed out incisively that the purpose of inspection and supervision is to help grassroots units identify the root cause of illness and excise the lesion, and that this must be done in a truthful, realistic, and case-by-case manner. "Escalating issues and slapping on labels (上纲上线、乱扣帽子)" not only damages the image and authority of headquarters, but also buries what are originally clear and specific problems beneath sweeping empty talk and stock phrases, leaving grassroots personnel confused and unable to carry out rectification effectively.

To this end, the brigade required headquarters to transform its work style and stipulated that inspection and supervision must achieve "three distinctions" (三个区分): distinguish "insufficient experience" from "ideological deviation"; distinguish "improper methods" from "absence of responsibility"; distinguish "objective constraints" from "subjectively lowering standards." In addition, they established a review mechanism for public criticism notices, requiring that any content involving qualitative assessments must be accompanied by specific factual evidence and a statement of grassroots circumstances, to prevent problems such as subjective conjecture, generalizing from one instance, and deliberate escalation.

The grassroots units feel most acutely whether headquarters' work style has genuinely changed. Recently, when Staff Officer Sun of the brigade's troop management section led a team to inspect a grassroots unit, he found that some of a certain company's combat-readiness supplies were not stored in a standardized manner. After investigation, he learned that this was because the company had just completed a position rotation. He therefore did not register this as a problem, but instead provided on-site guidance on standardizing the arrangement of supplies and urged the company to complete rectification within a set time. The company's political instructor was deeply moved: "Now headquarters inspections have less 'label-slapping' and 'board-hitting,' and more 'taking the pulse and diagnosing the illness' and 'prescribing and dispensing medicine.' Everyone's spirit for getting things done is smoother and their drive is stronger."

Original Chinese
检查督导是机关指导帮建基层、推进基层工作落实的重要手段。但在实际工作中，因为少数机关干部存在模糊认识、工作作风不实，导致检查督导变形走样，不仅无法帮助基层发现解决问题，还会干扰基层正常秩序。本期“特别关注”，聚焦新疆军区某团和东部战区海军某旅改进检查督导工作方式的经历，希望能够给大家带来一些思考与启示。 ——编 者 新疆军区某团—— 检查督导：多听听基层怎么说 ■高传硕 滕宇飞 “按照计划，此时应该组织专业技能课目训练才对吧？”前段时间，新疆军区某团机关干部前往基层进行检查督导。他们对照训练周表发现，红七连在专业技能课目训练时间展开体能训练，于是认为该连训风不实，不按计划组织训练。 然而，该连此举事出有因：他们所在驻训点位地处喀喇昆仑腹地的两山之间，海拔5100多米，气候复杂多变，体能训练等户外训练课目很容易受到影响。起初，他们尝试将训练地点转至阳光板房，但室内空间有限，部分训练项目仍旧无法进行，时间久了，官兵训练成绩下滑明显。于是，经过一番商讨，该连决定因地制宜，随时依据天气情况调整训练内容。 此次机关检查组到来前，该连因天气原因，将体能训练与专业技能课目训练的组织时间进行了对调，结果便被“抓”了个正着。 本是因地制宜的务实之举，却将因此面临通报批评，该连赵连长满心苦涩。他看着机关检查组渐渐远去，攥着训练周表的手松了又紧，最终只得长叹一声。 在随后召开的机关基层双向讲评会上，赵连长讲述了这段经历：“我们确实没有完全按照训练周表组训，但只是调整了各项训练展开的时间顺序，绝没有偏训漏训，这是受到客观因素影响不得已而为之。机关照章办事无可非议，但不能过于教条死板，指出问题也要考虑基层实际情况……” 赵连长的发言引发了官兵代表的共鸣。有人说，高原地区十里之外气候便大不相同，连队考虑根据实际情况调整着装，却被机关通报“未按要求统一着装”；还有人坦言，自己家中突逢变故，经连队批准后领取手机联系家人，结果因此被机关批评“违规使用手机”…… 某连王指导员也讲述了自身经历：机关检查督导时，发现部分氧气瓶为空瓶状态，因此认定该连“保障不到位，充氧制度落实不彻底”。然而，这是因为该连前一天晚上电路突发故障，部分班排配备的制氧机无法使用，官兵只能启用氧气瓶。 “在制氧机无法使用时用氧气瓶进行保障，不正是氧气瓶的作用所在吗？难道要为了迎检，把氧气瓶束之高阁，让战士硬扛？机关下定结论之前，是否应该先了解相关情况，或者给基层一个解释说明的机会？”王指导员的问话，让不少参会的机关干部面红耳赤。 沉默半晌，部队管理股范股长带头展开自省：“机关检查督导的目的是纠治问题、规范建设，而不是为了通报批评而通报批评。基层依据实际情况作出合理调整没有问题，我们确实不应以偏概全。” 随后，机关人员纷纷进行反思，明确台账合规、计划同步不应当是衡量工作的唯一标准，认清隔着屏幕查问题、照搬条文做判断等工作方法是脱离基层实际的表现，必须予以纠治。 会后，该团常态化开展蹲点帮带，选派机关干部常驻基层点位，变“上门督查”为“蹲点帮带”，帮助机关干部摒弃“办公室思维”“一刀切模式”，真正走进基层，了解实际情况、直面矛盾问题。为提高检查督导和指导帮带工作质效，他们还建立容错纠错机制，区分问题发生原因是主观失职失责，还是客观条件受限，对苗头性、倾向性或客观原因导致的问题，以现场提醒、当场纠偏、指导规范为主，杜绝过度通报、重复问责。 此外，该团还畅通基层意见反馈渠道，开设首长信箱，常态化收集基层困难、官兵诉求、工作建议，倒逼机关树牢服务基层意识、改进工作方式、转变工作作风。 连日来，该团部队管理股牵头统筹各业务股室，摸排驻训点位存在的建设难题，并梳理解难清单和限时整改帮带责任清单，受到基层好评。 东部战区海军某旅—— 通报批评：别轻易随便下结论 ■潘 伟 汪银银 董仕昭 “时间紧急，物资登记统计还在进行中，不能因此就认定我们‘思想懈怠’‘作风松散’吧？我们连长只是想说明具体情况，却成了‘态度不端正’……”盛夏时节，东部战区海军某旅领导前往基层展开调研，其间，某连二级上士杨刚讲述的一段经历，引起了他的关注。 前期，该旅机关检查组前往野外驻训点进行检查督导。因为当时该连官兵进驻点位不久，部分工作仍在推进，所以在内务规范和制度落实方面存在一些疏漏，被检查人员登记在册。该连陈连长本想解释说明，却被认为是找借口开脱，批评该连“野外驻训期间官兵思想懈怠、作风松散，带兵干部整改态度不端正”。 “我们当时确实存在物资登记统计不到位的问题，但这和思想作风有问题完全是两码事。”杨刚认为，机关检查指导工作没有问题，但也要实事求是，不能妄下定论。 经过一番了解，该旅领导发现，过往机关进行检查督导的过程中，类似情况并不鲜见：某连文书因手头工作较多，没有及时登记连队经常性工作，被机关定性为“履职尽责不力”；某连装备保养不到位，被机关批评“实战意识不强”……在少数机关干部看来，似乎批评的“调门”越高，越能彰显自己的成绩。 针对这些情况，该旅机关干部坦陈自身顾虑：“检查督导只记小事杂事，显得水平不高”“问题清单列不全，担心领导认为自己工作不认真”“把问题放大来讲，能更好地起到警示作用”…… “说来说去，还是政绩观出现了偏差，把基层的问题当作自己的成绩。”对此，该旅领导态度鲜明，“不是基层经不起批评，而是机关工作思路和指导方式有问题！”他们一针见血指出，检查督导的目的是帮助基层找准病根、拔除病灶，必须实事求是、就事论事。“上纲上线、乱扣帽子”不仅损害机关形象威信，还会把原本明晰具体的问题掩盖在大而化之的空话套话之中，导致基层官兵不明所以，难以整改到位。 为此，该旅要求机关转变工作作风，明确检查督导必须做到“三个区分”：区分“经验不足”与“思想偏差”、区分“方法不当”与“责任缺失”、区分“客观条件所限”与“主观降低标准”。此外，他们还建立通报批评审核机制，凡涉及定性评价内容，必须附上具体事实依据和基层情况说明，防止出现主观臆断、以偏概全、刻意上纲上线等问题。 机关作风转变成效如何，基层感受最真切。近日，该旅部队管理科孙参谋带队前往基层检查时，发现某连部分战备物资摆放不规范。经过了解，他得知这是因为该连刚完成阵地轮换，便没有登记这一问题，而是现场指导规范物资布设，督促连队限期完成整改。对此，该连指导员颇为感慨：“现在机关检查少了‘扣帽子’‘打板子’，多了‘把脉问诊’‘开方抓药’，大家干事创业的心气更顺了、劲头更足了。”
