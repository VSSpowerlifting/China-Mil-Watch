About this analysis
Summary
The PLA Navy's first cohort of female carrier-based helicopter student pilots — including Han Meng, named in the account — completed daytime solo carrier-landing assessments in Bohai Bay under high-wind, high-sea conditions, with the full cohort passing and night carrier landings and formation flying next in the pipeline. The article documents the Navy's structured methodology for this cohort: an 'instructor-led flights, layered progression, graduated solo release' (教官带飞、分层递进、梯次放单) model with sortie-by-sortie data review, which the framing presents as a replicable pipeline template rather than a one-off milestone. What remains unknown is the cohort's size, the specific helicopter type flown, and which carrier or carrier-training vessel was used.
First public reporting of the PLA Navy's inaugural cohort of female carrier-based helicopter pilots completing solo carrier-landing qualification, marking a documented milestone in naval aviation personnel development with specific named individuals and training methodology details.
Translation

Navy's First Cohort of Female Carrier-Based Aircraft Student Pilots Complete Solo Carrier-Landing Assessment

Reported by Jia Yizhen and correspondent Qian Xiaohu for the PLA Daily: Bohai Bay, clouds racing and waves surging. Recently, the Navy's first cohort of female carrier-based aircraft student pilots all passed the daytime solo carrier-landing assessment. This marks an important step forward in the Navy's cultivation of female carrier-based aviation personnel, and a further strengthening of the Naval Aviation talent pipeline (人才梯队).

"Take off!" At around 10 a.m., following the command from the control tower, female student pilot Han Meng steadily advanced the throttle and the aircraft lifted off the ground. Winds were strong and waves ran high over the sea surface, and the fuselage shook violently. Inside the cockpit, Han Meng made fine adjustments to the collective with her left hand while lightly holding the cyclic with her right, precisely correcting her heading.

The aircraft skimmed low over the sea; the powerful airflow churned up by the rotor blades swept up a mist of water, carving a white ribbon across the blue waves — a wake swallowed almost instantly by the swells. Entering the carrier-landing approach segment, the ship swayed with the swells and the deck rose and fell unpredictably. Han Meng kept her eyes fixed on the changes in the airflow at the ship's stern, carefully cross-checking altitude and airspeed, her fingertips reading the feedback from the control stick. As the landing signals officer's commands came through, Han Meng seized the brief window of relative stability in the ship's motion, decisively reduced throttle and lowered the collective, and the helicopter touched down on the ship with a near-zero-overload attitude in one smooth motion, completing a "three-point" landing in a single fluid sequence.

"When landing on the ship, it felt as though an invisible 'high wall' had appeared before me, and the psychological pressure surged suddenly," Han Meng said, barely concealing her excitement as she stepped down from the aircraft. Turbulence at sea, deck heave, and wake disturbance all test precision of control and psychological resilience (心理韧性); every micro-adjustment of a control surface and every degree of heading correction requires tempering through a thousand trials.

The solo carrier landing is a critical threshold in the growth of a carrier-based aircraft pilot, and the "qualification certificate" for entry into real maritime combat. The leadership of the training unit introduced that during the first cohort of female student pilots' carrier-landing subject training, they implemented an organization-and-training model of "instructor-led flights, layered progression, and graduated solo release" (教官带飞、分层递进、梯次放单), fully drawing out the female student pilots' qualities of composure, attentiveness, and smooth control inputs, while simultaneously reinforcing weak areas such as attitude perception, instrument interpretation, and deviation correction. Training plans were tailored individually, subject by subject, with strict oversight throughout — from ground simulation and deduction, to airborne execution, to debrief and critique. After each sortie landed, instructors immediately led the students in pulling flight parameter data, replaying the control trajectory second by second, and characterizing, attributing causes to, and devising corrective measures for each control deviation, with corrections implemented on the spot. Through the closed-loop training cycle of "fly once, evaluate once, improve once" (飞一次、评一次、升一次), the first cohort of female carrier-based aircraft student pilots achieved steady improvement in ship-aircraft coordination capability.

This correspondent observed on site that after the first female student pilot successfully landed on the ship, several more aircraft followed in close succession under the precise coordination of the landing signals officers, taking off and landing in an orderly sequence, and the entire first cohort of female carrier-based aircraft student pilots successfully completed the assessment. In the next phase, they will proceed in turn to training and assessment in high-difficulty subjects including formation flying and night carrier landings.

Original Chinese
海军首批舰载机女飞行学员完成着舰单飞考核 解放军报讯 贾亦真、记者钱晓虎报道：渤海湾，云飞浪卷。日前，海军首批舰载机女飞行学员全部通过昼间着舰单飞考核。这标志着海军舰载机女飞行人才培养工作迈出重要一步，海军航空兵人才梯队建设进一步完善。 “起飞！”上午10时许，随着塔台指令下达，女飞行学员韩梦稳推油门，战机离地升空。海面风急浪高，机身剧烈颠簸。座舱内，韩梦左手微调总距，右手轻抵驾驶杆，精准修正航向。 战机掠海飞行，旋翼激起的强气流裹挟着水雾，在碧波上犁开一道白练，尾迹转瞬被涌浪吞没。进入着舰航段，舰船随涌浪摇摆，甲板起伏不定。韩梦紧盯舰尾流场变化，细心比对高度、速度，指尖感知操纵杆反馈。引导员口令传来，韩梦抓住舰船短暂平稳窗口，果断收油、放总距，直升机以近乎零过载姿态轻盈触舰，“三点式”着舰一气呵成。 “着舰时眼前仿佛有一堵无形的‘高墙’，心理压力骤增。”走下战机，韩梦难掩兴奋，海上乱流、甲板升沉、尾流扰动，无不考验着操纵精度与心理韧性，每一次舵面微调、每一度航向修正，都需要千锤百炼。 着舰单飞是舰载机飞行员成长的关键关口，也是通向海天实战的“资格证”。承训单位领导介绍，首批女飞行学员着舰科目训练中，他们推行“教官带飞、分层递进、梯次放单”组训模式，充分挖掘女飞行学员沉稳细致、操纵柔和的特质，同步补强姿态感知、仪表判读和偏差修正等薄弱环节。训练计划逐人逐科目量身定制，从地面模拟推演、空中实施到复盘讲评，全程严格把关。每个架次落地后，教官都会第一时间带领学员调取飞参数据，逐秒回放操纵轨迹，围绕动作偏差定性、归因、定策，立行立改。通过“飞一次、评一次、升一次”的闭环训练，首批舰载机女飞行学员舰机协同能力得到稳步提升。 记者在现场看到，首名女飞行学员着舰成功后，数架战机在引导员精准调度下，衔接紧密、起落有序，首批舰载机女飞行学员全部顺利完成考核。下一步，她们将依次展开编队飞行、夜间着舰等高难科目训练考核。
