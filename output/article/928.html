Retracing the Path of the Northeast Old Aviation School's Original Aspiration | Visiting the Site of the Northeast Old Aviation School in Mudanjiang

重走东北老航校初心路丨走进牡丹江东北老航校旧址
PLA Daily (解放军报) 1 June 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
The Northern Theater Command Air Force sent 12 officer and soldier representatives to Mudanjiang as the fourth stop of a multi-city heritage tour, "Retracing the Path of the Northeast Old Aviation School's Original Aspiration (重走东北老航校初心路)," visiting the former site of the Northeast Old Aviation School on the occasion of its 80th founding anniversary. The activity documents standard PLAAF political-work practice: using institutional history—here the 1946 school's improvisations such as "using alcohol in place of aviation fuel (酒精代替航油)" and "going directly to the Type 99 advanced trainer (直上九九高教机)"—to frame hardship and rule-breaking as Party-sanctioned virtues for current personnel. Its value is as a record of how the Northern Theater Command Air Force packages founding-era resilience narratives for junior officers, not as evidence of a training or posture change.
Translation

This year marks the 80th anniversary of the founding of the Northeast Old Aviation School. The school's forebears built it on the ruins of war and took flight amid cannon fire and smoke, forging the spirit of "unity and struggle, hard work and entrepreneurship, courage in self-sacrifice, and blazing new trails (团结奋斗、艰苦创业、勇于献身、开拓新路)," inspiring generation after generation of Air Force personnel to press forward with courage. Recently, the Northern Theater Command Air Force organized a themed practical activity titled "Retracing the Path of the Northeast Old Aviation School's Original Aspiration (重走东北老航校初心路)," in which 12 outstanding officer and soldier representatives followed the historical trajectory of the Northeast Old Aviation School to visit Ürümqi, Yan'an, Tonghua, Mudanjiang, Mishan, Changchun, and other locations, touring important former sites and venues from the founding period of the People's Air Force. They traced the footsteps of struggle, reflected on the original aspiration and mission (初心使命), carried forward the red lineage by looking back on the road traveled, and worked to restore and carry forward fine traditions. In this installment, let us together visit the site of the Northeast Old Aviation School in Mudanjiang.

At the fourth stop of "Retracing the Path of the Northeast Old Aviation School's Original Aspiration," the Northern Theater Command Air Force organized 12 outstanding officer and soldier representatives to visit the site of the People's Air Force Northeast Old Aviation School in Mudanjiang. The officers and soldiers reaffirmed their oath of Party membership at Qihang Square and signed the commemorative flag, solidifying their original aspiration and mission; they paused and gazed intently in the exhibition center, looking back on the hardships of the founding years; and beside the former site's meteorological station and the rammed-earth runway, they traced the footsteps of their forebears' struggle. Through on-site instruction, discussion, and analysis, they experienced in an immersive way the immense and mighty power of the Old Aviation School spirit.

In April 1946, as the military situation grew urgent, the Northeast Old Aviation School—having only recently been established in Tonghua—came under frenzied bombing by Nationalist aircraft. In order to preserve its strength and avoid the enemy's sharp edge, the Old Aviation School was ordered to move north and relocate to Mudanjiang. There, the Old Aviation School took off and grew, creating miracles in world aviation history such as "using alcohol in place of aviation fuel (酒精代替航油)" and "going directly to the Type 99 advanced trainer (直上九九高教机)," and pilots independently trained by our Party and our military flew into the blue skies of the motherland for the first time.

↑ Horses pulling aircraft during a transfer.

The move from Tonghua to Mudanjiang was carried out simultaneously by air and ground. Aircraft that could fly were flown to Hailang Airfield; the remaining aircraft and equipment were transported by train. Upon reaching Laoyeling, the grade was too steep and the train was forced to stop. People then called out in unison and pushed the train, step by step, up over Laoyeling. When the train reached Ning'an, the railway bridge was out; people unloaded the aircraft and equipment and switched to horse-drawn carts for transport, giving rise to the spectacle in the history of the People's Air Force's development of "people pushing trains (人推火车)" and "horses pulling aircraft (马拉飞机)."

↑ Taking turns using a bicycle pump to inflate aircraft tires.

On June 1, 1946, the Old Aviation School formally opened for classes in Mudanjiang. On June 6, flight training began. At the time, materiel and equipment were in short supply; multiple aircraft shared a single propeller in rotation, bicycle pumps were used to inflate aircraft tires, and hand cranks were used to start aircraft engines—scenes rare in world aviation history that were commonplace in the aviation school's training. With no textbooks, the people of the Old Aviation School wrote their own; with no aeronautical charts, they drew their own; with no teaching aids, they made their own; with aviation fuel in severe shortage, the Old Aviation School organized technical personnel to repeatedly tackle the problem through experimentation, successfully achieving the use of alcohol as a substitute for aviation fuel in flight training, overcoming one difficulty after another through ingenuity and tenacity.

↑ A Type 99 advanced trainer aircraft of that era preparing for takeoff.

In organizing flight training, the world has always followed the practice of flying primary trainers first, then intermediate and advanced trainers. But the decision-makers of the Old Aviation School boldly broke with convention: faced with a situation in which even primary trainers were scarce, they put the captured Type 99 advanced trainer directly into frontline instruction. On July 21, 1946, flight student Wu Yuanren successfully completed the first solo flight by a student; thereafter, all students successively flew solo. "Going directly to the Type 99 advanced trainer" created a miracle in the history of aviation training and demonstrated the fundamental character of the Old Aviation School's people—daring to forge ahead, daring to experiment, and blazing new trails (敢闯敢试、开拓新路).

In October 1946, due to changes in the military situation, the Northeast Old Aviation School was compelled to leave Mudanjiang and relocate once again.

Retracing the path of the original aspiration—the spirit is passed on forever. Next stop: Mishan!

(Zhang Wei, Tian Ye, Wang Zhen, Liu Hanbao, Liao Jie, Xu Jian)

Original Chinese
今年是东北老航校建校80周年，老航校先辈在战争废墟上建校、在炮火硝烟中起飞，铸就了“团结奋斗、艰苦创业、勇于献身、开拓新路”的精神，激励一代代空军人奋勇前行。近日，北部战区空军组织“重走东北老航校初心路”主题实践活动，12名优秀官兵代表沿着东北老航校历史变迁轨迹走进乌鲁木齐、延安、通化、牡丹江、密山、长春等地参观探访人民空军初创时期的重要旧址场馆，追寻奋斗足迹、感悟初心使命，在回望来路中赓续红色血脉，更好地恢复和弘扬优良传统。本期，让我们一同走进牡丹江东北老航校旧址。 “重走东北老航校初心路”第四站，北部战区空军组织12名优秀官兵代表来到了牡丹江人民空军东北老航校旧址。官兵们在启航广场重温入党誓词、在纪念旗上签字，筑牢初心使命；在展陈中心驻足凝视，回望创业艰辛；在旧址气象台、三合土跑道旁，追寻先辈奋斗足迹。通过现地授课、讨论辨析等形式，沉浸式感悟老航校精神的磅礴伟力。 1946年4月，随着战局吃紧，刚在通化成立不久的东北老航校，遭到国民党飞机的狂轰滥炸。为保存实力、避敌锋芒，老航校奉命向北转移，迁往牡丹江。在这里，老航校起飞成长，创造了“酒精代替航油”“直上九九高教机”等世界航空史上的奇迹，我党我军自主培养的飞行员首次飞上了祖国的蓝天。 ↑马拉飞机转场。 从通化到牡丹江的转移，空地同时进行。能飞的飞机从空中飞到海浪机场，其余飞机和装备物资用火车运输。当行至老爷岭时，因坡度太大，火车被迫停了下来。于是，人们喊着号子推火车，一步一步把火车推上了老爷岭。车到宁安，铁路桥中断，人们便把飞机和器材卸下来，改用马车拉运，诞生了人民空军发展史上“人推火车”“马拉飞机”的奇观。 ↑轮流使用自行车打气筒给飞机轮胎打气。 1946年6月1日，老航校在牡丹江正式开学上课。6月6日，开始组织飞行训练。当时物资器材紧缺，多架飞机轮流使用一副螺旋桨、用自行车打气筒给飞机轮胎充气、手摇摇把启动飞机发动机等，这些世界航空史上罕见的场景，在航校训练中司空见惯。没有教材，老航校人就自己编写；没有航图，老航校人就自己绘制；没有教具，老航校人就自己制作；航油严重短缺，老航校就组织技术人员反复攻关试验，成功实现酒精替代航油用于飞行训练，用智慧和坚韧攻克了一个又一个难关。 ↑当年的九九高级教练机准备起飞。 世界上组织学习飞行，历来都是先飞初级教练机，再飞中、高级教练机，但老航校的决策者们却大胆打破常规，面对连初级教练机都匮乏的困境，他们将缴获的“九九”高级教练机直接投入教学一线。1946年7月21日，飞行学员吴元任顺利完成首个学员单飞，之后，所有学员都陆续放了单飞。“直上九九高教机”，创造了航空训练史上的奇迹，彰显了老航校人敢闯敢试、开拓新路的精神底色。 1946年10月，由于战局变化，东北老航校不得不离开牡丹江再次搬迁。 重走初心路，精神永传承，下一站，密山见！ （张伟 田野 王朕 刘汉宝 廖劼 徐建）
