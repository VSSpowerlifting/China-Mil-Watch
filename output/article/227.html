These "Successors" in Camouflage Continue the Glory in the Name of Heroes

这群身着迷彩的“后来人”，以英雄之名续写荣光
PLA Daily (解放军报) 13 May 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
Gansu Province's military-civilian authorities have named and established 16 Red Militia Model Units after revolutionary martyrs, each tied to a specific historical site or hero — including the Wang Xueli Militia Company at Shenjialing, the Dong Zhentang Militia Company in Gaotai, the Jinta County Aerospace Militia Company, and the Wushaoling Road-Protection Tibetan Militia Company — following a 'one unit, one policy' (一队一策) framework administered by the Provincial Military District. The article documents how the Gansu Military District is using 'red heritage' (红色基因) branding to address two persistent militia problems simultaneously: low training participation and weak specialist capability, particularly in ethnic minority and high-altitude units, where bilingual instruction and paired garrison-unit training were required to raise basic pass rates. The framing points to a broader PLA effort to make militia units operationally credible in niche missions — aerospace recovery support, disaster relief, road clearance with UAV integration — while using hero-naming as the institutional mechanism for sustaining recruitment motivation and political cohesion.

In the Name of Heroes, Assembled on the Land of Longyuan (陇原大地)

— Reflections on Gansu Province's Military-Civilian Joint Initiative to Establish Red Militia Model Units

■ Mo Fuchun, Liu Kai, China Defense News reporter Jia Baohua

The land of Longyuan is a red and fertile soil soaked in the blood of revolutionary martyrs. From the spark of Nanliang to the Grand Junction at Huining, from the Western Route Army's (红西路军) bloody battles along the Hexi Corridor to the decisive charge of the Lanzhou Campaign, one stirring revolutionary story after another is scattered like sparks across these mountains, plains, and loess hills. In recent years, Gansu Province's military and civilian authorities have fully excavated their "red heritage," naming and establishing 16 Red Militia Model Units after heroes. Inspired by the spirit of martyrs and heroes, how do these new-era "successors" (后来人) in camouflage forge the soul of their convictions, temper their capabilities, and shoulder their responsibilities? In late spring, reporters traveled into the land of Longyuan to seek out the moving stories of the Red Militia Model Units.

Engraving the Heroes' Names into the Spiritual Bloodline

On a spring day at Shenjialing in Lanzhou, the greenery was deepening. In front of the memorial pavilion at the site where Martyr Wang Xueli fell, all militia members of the Wang Xueli Militia Company stood in neat formation as a special education session—a dialogue across time and space with the martyrs—was underway.

"Old Regimental Commander, Shenjialing, where you once charged forward carrying the battle flag, is now guarded by us. Your battle flag has not faded; your soldiers have not fallen behind…" The voice of political instructor Li Weimin echoed through the valley. He told reporters that the company had distilled a 16-character company motto: "Unwavering loyalty to the Party, skilled at fighting hard battles, courageous and tenacious, daring to win (铁心向党、善打硬仗、勇猛顽强、敢于胜利)." "This is a true portrait of Wang Xueli's life, and it is also the spiritual core of our company."

To brand this spiritual core into the heart of every militia member, they regularly visit the Shenjialing battle site to absorb the heroic spirit, have established a joint education and study mechanism with the unit in which Wang Xueli served, and invite the hero's descendants to recount the deeds of their forebears. "Every time I hear the stories of our predecessors, it is like a baptism of the soul," one militia member wrote after a session.

"The smoke of battle has long cleared, the mountains and rivers are at peace; the heroic soul abides forever, the founding aspiration endures. Naming units after heroes and educating people with the spirit of martyrs is not a simple act of 'putting a name on something'—it is about passing the red genes (红色基因) down through the militia ranks like a torch." So said a leader of the Lanzhou Garrison Command.

This concept runs through the entire process of establishing Gansu Province's 16 Red Militia Model Units. A leader of the Provincial Military District's Political Work Bureau introduced that, following a "one unit, one policy (一队一策)" approach, they planned along the red resource systems of the Red Army Long March, the Red Western Route Army's campaigns, and the liberation of Gansu, relying on major historical sites within the province, the places where heroic models fell, and the birthplaces of revolutionary predecessors, to name and establish 16 Red Militia Model Units—actively building a mobile "red classroom" (红色课堂) that cultivates loyalty and sows faith.

In Gaotai County along the Hexi Corridor, militia members of the Dong Zhentang Militia Company are bringing red stories to life in ways the masses love to see and hear. The company selected talented core members to form a red culture propaganda team, adapting the Western Route Army's combat history and stories of martyrs into catchy clapper ballads (快板) and widely sung folk songs, and going from village to village on a touring lecture circuit.

Militia member Wang Liping was originally a shy young woman who was so nervous during her first lecture that she repeatedly forgot her lines. Today, she can stand before hundreds of people and deliver her account with composure and deep feeling. Whenever she recounts General Dong Zhentang's heroic fight to the death at Gaotai, the audience invariably falls into solemn silence, visibly moved. "It is not that we lecture vividly—it is that General Dong's deeds are too weighty and his spirit too overwhelming," Wang Liping said with emotion. "Every time I reach the moment of the general's death, I can truly feel the weight of the responsibility on my shoulders."

"Though I have heard the stories of revolutionary martyrs many times, every time I revisit them it is a baptism of the soul, and every act of remembrance makes me feel the mission pressing down on my shoulders all the more." Militia backbone Zhao Jiangting said with deep feeling that inheriting the spirit of martyrs is never just a slogan—it is a solemn promise to be fulfilled for a lifetime.

Tempering the Ability to Win with Courage and Fighting Spirit (血性胆气)

"Evacuation of residents in the landing zone is complete." "Communications lines are connected and normal." "Landing point location has been confirmed." … Not long ago, a spaceflight launch and return search-and-rescue emergency exercise was launched on the Hexi Gobi. At the commander's order, the Jinta County Aerospace Militia Company (航天民兵连) rapidly deployed and swiftly reached its designated positions.

"The ultimate measure of the results of red inheritance is, in the end, combat effectiveness." A leader of the Jinta County People's Armed Forces Department (人武部) cut straight to the point.

Jinta County is one of the key hosting and support areas for spaceflight launches and return search-and-rescue operations. The county's Aerospace Militia Company bears responsibility each time for security alert, route patrol, communications support, and emergency search-and-rescue missions. To ensure nothing goes wrong, before every mission they conduct repeated live-force, live-equipment drills throughout the support area, fully anticipate all types of contingencies, and then spend consecutive days and nights grinding away at their skills at their posts.

"We have a tradition: before every major mission, we organize militia members to go to the Dongfeng Revolutionary Martyrs Cemetery and report to the aerospace martyrs who rest there." Company Commander Wei Chengjun said solemnly. "Marshal Nie Rongzhen and more than 700 martyrs are watching us—who would dare to let them down?" For them, every support mission is a battle, and the high-spirited posture of sleeping with weapons at the ready (枕戈待旦) and ensuring nothing goes wrong is the finest inheritance of the "Two Bombs, One Satellite" (两弹一星) spirit.

"The martyrs, looking back, should smile with satisfaction; those who carry the banner forward will always come." A leader of the Provincial Military District's War Readiness and Construction Bureau introduced that the 16 Red Militia Model Units established from the outset a clear orientation of "orienting toward war and fighting (向战为战)," adhering to the approach of "mission-driven, combat-oriented, specialized and intensive training, strengthening the force through science and technology (任务牵引、实战导向、专业精训、科技强兵)," and continuously enhancing their capacity to fulfill new-era mission tasks in areas including aerospace support, road clearance, reconnaissance and early warning, and emergency rescue.

The Wushaoling Road-Protection Tibetan Militia Company (乌鞘岭护路藏族民兵连) is stationed year-round in high-altitude mountain areas at over 3,500 meters elevation, bearing responsibility for road protection and support along key transportation arteries including the Lianhuo Expressway and the Lanxin Railway. At the time of its naming and flag-presentation ceremony, this unit faced the practical difficulties of a shortage of specialist backbone personnel and a weak training foundation. The Wuwei Military Sub-District (武威军分区) joined with local Party committees and governments to tailor a training and capability-building "package" for the unit: given the high proportion of ethnic minority members, Tibetan officers and militia instructors were selected as training backbone personnel, and bilingual Tibetan-Chinese professional course lesson plans were compiled; paired assistance-training relationships were formed with garrison units and highway traffic police brigades in the area, specialist instructors were regularly invited to guide the unit, and militia backbone members were sent to partner units for on-the-job training.

After this customized "training checklist" was implemented, the company's training participation rate and course pass rate steadily improved. Early this year, a sudden heavy snowfall struck Tianzhu County, leaving many vehicles stranded on the expressway due to the weather disaster. Upon receiving the call for help, the Wushaoling Road-Protection Tibetan Militia Company responded to the order and handled the situation efficiently, earning unanimous praise from military and civilian leaders and local residents.

"Inheritance is not about polishing the glory of the past over and over again—it is about using today's solid capabilities to let the heroic spirit continue to shine into the future." A leader of the Provincial Military District's War Readiness and Construction Bureau said that, with an eye toward the trends of informatized and intelligentized (信息化智能化) warfare, they have fully introduced advanced science and technology, equipping each militia unit with high-tech equipment including unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs), infrared reconnaissance, and emergency communications systems, and establishing joint education and training mechanisms with defense industry enterprises and high-technology units, so that the courageous fighting spirit (血性胆气) to take on hard battles and the intelligent power (智慧力量) enabled by science and technology illuminate each other.

At the foot of the Qilian Mountains, a live-force exercise was underway. "The mission force is about to pass through. It has come under enemy attack; the road ahead is damaged. Order your unit to organize rapid clearance!" Upon receiving the order, the Liu Liyun Militia Platoon (刘理运民兵排) moved out swiftly. Multi-rotor UAVs took to the air, transmitting real-time footage of the damaged road section, the extent of the damage, and available detour routes back to the command vehicle. Simultaneously, local transportation engineers embedded in the militia platoon rapidly retrieved the three-dimensional model of the road section, used survey UAVs to generate a terrain map of the landslide area, and loaders and excavators carried out precision operations guided by "data navigation"… A road clearance mission that would normally require at least three hours was completed in just two hours, ensuring the mission force passed through on schedule.

Making "Camouflage Green" a Reliable Presence

The ultimate test of a unit's development is whether it can step up when the people need it.

Last summer, a severe rainstorm struck Longnan without warning. Upon receiving the rescue order, the Chen Hongjun Militia Company (陈红军民兵连) assembled at lightning speed and rushed through the rain to the stricken villages. The rain was too fierce for vehicles to pass, so militia members waded through waist-deep water on foot toward the villages. That night, they searched house by house and successively evacuated more than 70 stranded residents; many members were covered in mud, their lips purple from the cold. An elderly person who had been rescued gripped a militia member's hand and would not let go: "Now that you're here, we're saved."

"At the time I wasn't thinking much—I just felt that I'm a militia member, so I should be the first one to go in." Militia backbone Ma Jianjun said. Standing beneath a banner bearing a hero's name, "being the first to go in" has become an unspoken instinct for action—the oath that Martyr Chen Hongjun fulfilled with his life has now been transformed into the red gene (红色基因) that this unit passes down without end.

In December 2023, after the Jishishan earthquake struck, the Linxia Ethnic Unity Militia Platoon (临夏民族团结民兵排) responded to the order and rushed to the disaster area at the first moment. They set up tents in the rubble, unloaded supplies, established hot food distribution points, and rapidly erected a tent school at the resettlement site, helping more than 110 students in the disaster area resume their studies… For four consecutive days and three nights, the militia members did not get a single full night's sleep.

Able to step up at critical moments, reliable in ordinary days. The Gulang County Babusha Forest Farm (古浪县八步沙林场) is located on the southern edge of the Tengger Desert. Once, yellow sand blanketed the sky here, and the drifting sand devoured farmland and villages at a rate of 7.5 meters per year; because one stepped into sand within eight paces of leaving home, the place was named "Babusha" (Eight-Step Sand). In the 1980s, the "Six Elders" (六老汉) declared war on the desert through a joint household contracting arrangement, building a green barrier across the vast sea of sand over three generations. Today, the Babusha Militia Company (八步沙民兵连) stands beside the sandy hollow that the "Six Elders" reclaimed, taking up the shovels of the older generation and continuing the fight against wind and sand.

Spring here has none of the gentleness of apricot blossoms and spring rain—only gales strong enough to knock a person off their feet and sand grains that sting the face. The window period for sand suppression and afforestation each year falls precisely during the two windiest months. Militia members dig trenches, lay straw, and press down grid squares (草方格) into the sand against the wind; by the end of a day, their mouths, ears, and hair are full of sand. Since its establishment, the Babusha Militia Company has completed the laying of straw grid squares over 800 mu of land and planted nearly ten thousand trees of various species, building a solid sand-prevention barrier on the southern edge of the desert.

"At first I felt it was hard—the sand stinging my face was painful—but watching this forest grow bit by bit, I felt it was worth it." Militia Company Commander Kui Guofa pointed to the oasis behind him and said, "The 'Six Elders' dared to turn desert into oasis. If we young successors can't hold it, we'd be letting down our predecessors and letting down this company banner."

During this year's "May Day" holiday, the site of the Red Army's Grand Junction at Huining welcomed wave after wave of visitors. Among the flowing crowds, flashes of camouflage green moved back and forth. Militia members of the Grand Junction Militia Company (大会师民兵连) stood under the blazing sun, directing traffic at the scenic area entrance, supporting elderly visitors in the corners of exhibition halls, reminding tourists to watch their step on steep staircases, and from time to time pointing out the mountain paths the Red Army once walked and recounting the stories of the Red Army's grand junction that local residents know well.

"I came here to trace the roots of red history, and I didn't expect that the moment I stepped off the vehicle, this 'camouflage green' would become the most reliable guide." Mr. Liu, a visitor from Nanjing, Jiangsu, stood beneath the Junction Tower and reflected: "On this red land, seeing these young people, I feel that the predecessors from the stories have not gone far."

From Shenjialing to Babusha, from the Qilian Mountains to the Hexi Gobi, the banners of 16 Red Militia Model Units flutter in the wind across the land of Longyuan. Beneath those banners are every tactical movement refined again and again on the training ground; the unhesitating "I'll go first" when disaster strikes; the straw grid squares laid year after year at the edge of the wind and sand; the cup of hot water handed to a visitor at a scenic area, the helping hand extended. The bloodline of heroes is quietly carried forward in one concrete and minute act after another; the red seeds take root and sprout in the steadfast perseverance of a group of ordinary militia members.

Across a thousand li of Longyuan, red and fertile land. In those years, the martyrs transformed their lives into the last starlight before dawn on this ground; today, these "successors" in camouflage are, in the name of heroes, continuing to write the glory that belongs to this era—when the motherland and the people need them, those resounding cries of "Present!" are the finest answer.

陇原大地，是一片被革命先烈鲜血浸染的红色沃土。从南梁星火到会宁会师，从西路军浴血河西到兰州战役决战冲锋，一段段可歌可泣的革命往事，如星火般散落在这片山川塬峁间。近年来，甘肃省军地充分挖掘“红色家底”，以英雄之名命名组建16支红色民兵示范分队。在英烈精神的感召下，这群身着迷彩的新时代“后来人”，如何铸牢思想之魂、锤炼过硬本领、扛起责任担当？暮春时节，记者走进陇原大地，探寻红色民兵示范分队的感人故事。 把英雄的名字刻进精神血脉 春日的兰州沈家岭，绿意渐浓。王学礼烈士牺牲地纪念亭前，王学礼民兵连全体民兵整齐列队，一堂跨越时空、对话英烈的专题教育课正在进行。 “老团长，当年您扛旗冲锋的沈家岭，如今由我们来守。您的战旗，没褪色；您的兵，没掉队……”指导员李为民的声音在山谷间回荡。他告诉记者，连队凝练了“铁心向党、善打硬仗、勇猛顽强、敢于胜利”16字连训，“这是王学礼一生的真实写照，也是我们连队的精神内核。” 为了把这份精神内核烙进每个民兵心里，他们定期前往沈家岭战斗遗址感悟英雄精神，和王学礼生前所在部队建立联教共学机制，邀请英雄后代讲述先辈事迹。“每一次听先辈故事，都像是一次灵魂洗礼。”一位民兵在课后这样写道。 “硝烟远去山河安，英魂永驻初心存。以英雄之名命名，以先烈精神育人，不是简单的‘冠名’，而是要让红色基因在民兵队伍中薪火相传。”兰州警备区领导说。 这一理念贯穿于甘肃省16支红色民兵示范分队的组建全过程。该省军区政治工作局领导介绍，他们按照“一队一策”的思路，沿红军长征、红西路军征战、解放甘肃等红色资源体系规划，依托省内重大历史发生地、英模人物牺牲地、革命前辈诞生地，命名组建16支红色民兵示范分队，积极打造涵养忠诚、播种信仰的流动“红色课堂”。 在河西走廊高台县，董振堂民兵连的民兵们，正用群众喜闻乐见的方式让红色故事“活”起来。连队遴选才艺骨干组建红色文化宣传小分队，将西路军战斗历史、英烈故事等改编成朗朗上口的快板、传唱度高的乡土民歌，走村入户巡回宣讲。 民兵王丽萍原本是个腼腆的姑娘，初次宣讲时紧张到频频忘词。如今，她已能面对数百名群众从容讲述、深情演绎。每每讲到董振堂将军血战高台、以身殉国的壮举，现场群众总会肃然静立、为之动容。“不是我们宣讲得生动，是董将军的事迹太过厚重、精神太过震撼。”王丽萍动情地说，“每次讲到将军牺牲的那一刻，我都能真切感受到肩上扛着的责任与分量。” “革命先烈的故事虽然听了很多次，但每一次重温都是一次灵魂洗礼，每一次缅怀都倍感使命在肩。”民兵骨干赵江廷深有感触地说，传承英烈精神从来不是一句口号，而是要一辈子践行的庄严承诺。 用血性胆气锤炼打赢本领 “落区群众疏散完毕”“通信线路联通正常”“落点位置已确定”……前不久，一场航天发射和返回搜救应急演练在河西戈壁拉开帷幕，随着指挥员一声令下，金塔县航天民兵连快速出动，迅速抵达指定位置。 “检验红色传承成效的标尺，说到底还是战斗力。”金塔县人武部领导一语道破关键。 金塔县是航天发射和返回搜救的重要承载地和保障区之一，该县组建的航天民兵连每次都担负着安全警戒、线路巡护、通信保障、应急搜救等任务。为了确保万无一失，每次执行任务前，他们都要在保障区域实兵实装反复演练，充分预想各类突发情况，然后接连多天夜以继日铆在战位苦练本领。 “我们有一个传统，每次执行重大任务前，都要组织民兵到东风革命烈士陵园，向长眠于此的航天先烈们报告。”连长魏成俊郑重地说，“聂荣臻元帅和700多位英烈看着我们呢，谁敢掉链子？”对他们而言，每一次保障任务都是一次战斗，枕戈待旦、万无一失的昂扬姿态，就是对“两弹一星”精神最好的传承。 “先烈回眸应笑慰，擎旗自有后来人。”省军区战备建设局领导介绍，16支红色民兵示范分队组建之初就立起“向战为战”的鲜明导向，坚持“任务牵引、实战导向、专业精训、科技强兵”思路，围绕航天保障、道路抢通、侦察预警、应急救援等，持续增强履行新时代使命任务能力。 乌鞘岭护路藏族民兵连常年驻守海拔3500多米的高原山区，担负着连霍高速、兰新铁路等交通要道的护路保障任务。命名授旗之初，这支队伍面临专业骨干缺、训练底子薄的现实困难。武威军分区联合地方党委政府为其量身定制训练提能“套餐”：针对少数民族队员占比高的实际，遴选藏族干部和民兵教练员担任组训骨干，组织编写藏汉双语专业课目教案；与辖区驻军部队、高速交警大队结成帮训对子，定期邀请专业教员来队指导，选派民兵骨干到挂钩单位跟班作业。 一套定制的“训练清单”落地后，该连参训率和课目合格率稳步提升。今年初，一场大雪突袭天祝县，许多车辆因天气灾害在高速公路上趴窝。收到求助后，乌鞘岭护路藏族民兵连闻令而动、高效处置，受到军地领导和当地群众一致好评。 “传承，不是一遍遍擦拭过去的荣光，而是用今天的过硬实力，让英雄精神在未来继续发光。”省军区战备建设局领导说，着眼信息化智能化趋势，他们充分引入高新科技，为各民兵分队配备无人机、红外侦察、应急通信等高新装备，与军工企业、高新技术单位建立联教联训机制，让敢打硬仗的血性胆气和科技赋能的智慧力量交相辉映。 祁连山下，一场实兵演练正在展开。“任务部队即将过境，遭敌袭击，前方道路损坏，命令你部迅速组织抢通！”接到指令，刘理运民兵排迅疾出动。多旋翼无人机升空，将受损路段、损毁情况、迂回通道等画面实时回传指挥车。与此同时，编入民兵排的地方交通工程技术员快速调取该路段三维模型，利用测绘无人机生成塌方地段地形图，装载机、挖掘机在“数据导航”下精准作业……原本至少需要3小时完成的抢通任务，仅用2小时就完成道路修复，保障任务部队按时过境。 让“迷彩绿”成为踏实的依靠 检验一支队伍的建设成效，最终要看群众需要的时候能不能顶上去。 去年夏天，一场特大暴雨突袭陇南。接到救援命令后，陈红军民兵连火速集结，冒雨奔赴受灾村庄。雨势太猛，车辆无法通行，民兵队员们就蹚着齐腰深的水徒步向村里挺进。那一夜，他们挨家挨户搜寻，先后转移70多名被困群众，很多队员浑身是泥，嘴唇冻得发紫。一位被救出的老人握着民兵的手不肯松开：“你们来了，我们就有救了。” “当时没想那么多，就觉得我是民兵，就该第一个上。”民兵骨干马建军说。站在以英雄名字命名的旗帜下，“第一个上”已经成为一种无需言说的行动自觉——陈红军烈士用生命践行的誓言，如今已化作这支队伍传承不息的红色基因。 2023年12月，积石山地震发生后，临夏民族团结民兵排闻令而动，第一时间奔赴灾区。他们在废墟中搭帐篷、卸物资、开设热食供应点，在安置点快速搭建帐篷小学，帮助灾区110余名学生复学复课……连续四天三夜，民兵们没有睡过一个囫囵觉。 关键时刻顶得上，寻常日子靠得住。古浪县八步沙林场，地处腾格里沙漠南缘。曾经，这里黄沙漫天，风沙以每年7.5米的速度吞噬着农田和村庄，出门八步就是沙，故而得名“八步沙”。20世纪80年代，“六老汉”以联户承包的方式向沙漠宣战，用三代人的心血在茫茫沙海中筑起一道绿色屏障。如今，八步沙民兵连就在这片被“六老汉”治好的沙窝边上，接过老一辈的铁锹，继续与风沙较量。 这里的春天没有杏花春雨的温柔，只有刮得站不稳脚的狂风和打在脸上生疼的沙粒。每年压沙造林的窗口期，正是风最大的两个月。民兵们迎着风沙挖沟铺草、埋压方格，一天下来，嘴里、耳朵里、头发里全是沙子。自成立以来，八步沙民兵连已完成铺设草方格800亩，栽种各类林木近万株，在沙漠南缘筑起一道坚实的防沙屏障。 “刚开始觉得苦，风沙打在脸上生疼，但看着这片林子一点点长起来，就觉得值了。”民兵连长奎国发指了指身后的绿洲说，“‘六老汉’敢把沙漠变绿洲，我们这些年轻后生要是守不住，对不住先人，也对不住这面连旗。” 今年“五一”期间，红军会宁会师旧址迎来一拨又一拨游客。如织的人流中，一抹抹迷彩绿穿梭其间。大会师民兵连的民兵们顶着烈日，在景区入口疏导车辆，在展馆角落搀扶老人，在台阶陡坡处提醒游客注意安全，还不时为游客指一下当年红军走过的山路，讲一段当地群众熟知的红军会师故事。 “来这里是寻根红色历史的，没想到一下车，这抹‘迷彩绿’就成了最可靠的向导。”来自江苏南京的游客刘先生站在会师塔下感慨，“在这片红色土地上，看到这群年轻人，就觉着故事里的先辈并没有走远。” 从沈家岭到八步沙，从祁连山到河西戈壁，16支红色民兵示范分队的旗帜在陇原大地迎风飘扬。旗帜下，是训练场上反复打磨的每一个战术动作，是灾害来临时一句“我先上”的毫不犹豫，是风沙口上年复一年铺设的草方格，是景区里为游客递上的一杯热水、伸出的一次援手。英雄的血脉，就这样在一件件具体而细微的小事中悄然赓续；红色的种子，就这样在一群普通民兵的坚守中生根发芽。 千里陇原，红色热土。当年，先烈们在这里把生命化作黎明前最后的星光；今天，这群身着迷彩的“后来人”，正以英雄之名续写着属于这个时代的荣光——当祖国和人民需要的时候，那一声声响亮的“到”，就是最好的回答。 版式设计：扈 硕
