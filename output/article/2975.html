Hot Blood Flows at the Foot of Songmao Ridge — Visiting Zhongfu Village in Western Fujian, the Departure Point of the Red 9th Army Corps' Long March

■ PLA Daily correspondent Luan Cheng, photo report

Tourists visit the Red Army Bridge.

In midsummer, this correspondent traveled to the mountain region of western Fujian to visit Zhongfu Village in Changting County, at the foot of Songmao Ridge. This place was once an important battlefield of the Central Soviet Area's Fifth Counter-Encirclement Campaign, and also the departure point of the Red 9th Army Corps' Long March.

Songmao Ridge, with its treacherous terrain and dense forests, served as a critical eastern shield for Ruijin, the capital of the Chinese Soviet Republic. Turning back the clock to late September 1934: the Fifth Counter-Encirclement Campaign in the Central Soviet Area had reached a white-hot intensity, Nationalist Army forces were closing in step by step, and a strategic withdrawal of the Red Army was imminent. On September 23, the Battle of Songmao Ridge commenced. The Red 9th Army Corps, the Red 24th Division, and local Changting armed forces — more than 30,000 men in total — engaged the enemy in a bloody battle at Songmao Ridge.

It was an extraordinarily brutal fight. The enemy poured thousands upon thousands of artillery shells into Songmao Ridge every day, while the Red Army was poorly equipped and casualties mounted without cease. At the most desperate moment of the fighting, the Fujian Military Region mobilized more than 1,600 new soldiers to reinforce the Red 9th Army Corps, who were thrown into battle immediately upon arrival.

Upon entering Zhongfu Village, a Hakka covered bridge built during the Ming Dynasty comes into view; local residents call it the "Red Army Bridge." According to accounts, the Red Army once treated villagers' illnesses on this covered bridge and also set up a recruitment station there. To this day, a carved mark remains visible on several bridge pillars at approximately 1.5 meters above the ground — the "conscription height line." At the time, the conscription standard was "taller than the rifle, join the Red Army." Because the "Hanyang Arsenal" rifles used by the Red Army measured 1.5 meters in length with bayonet fixed, and soldiers had to carry their rifles while on the march, recruits were required to be at least 1.5 meters tall.

"To defend Songmao Ridge is to defend Changting, to defend Ruijin!" During the Battle of Songmao Ridge, more than 200 hot-blooded young men from the local area rushed to the front. On a display panel at the Red Army Bridge, this correspondent saw a moving story: an elderly man named Luo Yunran, after four of his sons had joined the Red Army and fallen one after another, resolutely sent his last two sons to enlist. In the face of those around him urging him to stop, he said: "Even if our family line is cut off, we must follow the Red Army and make revolution!"

The Red Army officers and soldiers fought with their lives for seven days and seven nights, at tremendous cost delaying the Nationalist Army's advance and winning precious time for the central organs and the main force of the Central Red Army to carry out their strategic withdrawal.

Seven days later, on September 30, 1934, the Red 9th Army Corps received the order for strategic withdrawal. Officers and soldiers pulled back from Songmao Ridge and convened a farewell rally for the Soviet Area masses in Zhongfu Village. After drinking the rice wine the villagers offered them, still bearing the smoke and grime of battle, they set out to assemble at Yudu, and then stepped onto the ten-thousand-li march.

The Departure Ceremony Before the Ancestral Hall

■ PLA Daily correspondent Luan Cheng, photo report

A miniature sculpture inside the exhibition hall at the Red 9th Army Corps' Long March departure site.

One ancestral hall. One set of sculptures. Together they present the scene from more than 90 years ago when the Red 9th Army Corps bade farewell to the Soviet Area and set out on its tragic and heroic march.

On the morning of September 30, 1934, officers and soldiers of the Red 9th Army Corps — having just withdrawn from the Battle of Songmao Ridge — convened a farewell rally for the Soviet Area masses in front of the Guanshou Ancestral Hall in Zhongfu Village, Changting County, Fujian. Army Corps Chief of Staff Guo Tianmin spoke with passionate conviction, raising his fist and crying out; the soldiers stood in formation, solemn and sorrowful. At three o'clock that afternoon, amid a fine drizzle, under the reluctant gaze of their fellow villagers, the Red Army officers and soldiers split into two columns and moved in the direction of Yudu — taking the first step of the ten-thousand-li Long March.

松毛岭下 热血涌流 ——探访闽西中复村红9军团长征出发地 ■解放军报记者 栾 铖 摄影报道 游客参观红军桥。 盛夏，记者来到闽西山区，探访松毛岭脚下的长汀县中复村。这里曾是中央苏区第五次反“围剿”的重要战场，也是红9军团长征出发地。 松毛岭地势险要、森林茂密，是中华苏维埃共和国首府瑞金东面的重要屏障。时光回溯至1934年9月下旬，中央苏区第五次反“围剿”进入白热化，国民党军步步逼近，红军战略转移迫在眉睫。9月23日，松毛岭战斗打响。红9军团、红24师和长汀地方武装共3万余人，在松毛岭与敌展开血战。 这是一场极为惨烈的战斗。敌人每天将成千上万发炮弹投向松毛岭，而红军装备简陋，牺牲人数不断增加。战事最艰难的时候，福建军区动员1600多名新战士补充到红9军团，随即便投入战斗。 刚进中复村，一座建于明代的客家廊桥映入眼帘，当地群众称之为“红军桥”。据介绍，红军曾在这座廊桥上为老百姓看病，也曾在此设立征兵处。时至今日，几根桥柱离地约1.5米处，仍可看见一道刻痕，那是“征兵身高线”。当时，征兵规定“人比枪高当红军”。因为红军用的“汉阳造”步枪带枪刺长1.5米，行军时要背着枪跑，所以要求参军者身高至少1.5米。 “保卫松毛岭，就是保卫长汀、保卫瑞金！”松毛岭战斗期间，当地200多名热血男儿奔赴战场。记者在红军桥上的展板上，看到一个感人故事：有位叫罗云然的老人，在4个儿子参加红军先后牺牲后，毅然将最后两个儿子送来报名。面对周遭人们的劝阻，他说：“就是断了香火，也要跟着红军干革命！” 红军将士浴血奋战7天7夜，以巨大代价，迟滞了国民党军的进攻，为中央机关和中央红军主力实施战略大转移赢得了宝贵时间。 7天后，1934年9月30日，红9军团接到战略转移的命令。官兵们撤下松毛岭，在中复村召开告别苏区群众大会。喝完村民捧上的米酒，带着一身战火硝烟，他们向于都集结，随后踏上万里征途。 祠堂前的出征式 ■解放军报记者 栾 铖 摄影报道 红9军团长征出发地陈列馆内的微缩雕塑。 一座祠堂，一组雕塑，呈现了90多年前红9军团告别苏区、悲壮出征的场景。 1934年9月30日上午，刚从松毛岭战斗撤出的红9军团官兵，在福建长汀县中复村观寿公祠前召开告别苏区群众大会。军团参谋长郭天民慷慨陈词、挥拳呐喊，战士们整装肃立、神色悲壮。当天下午3时，红军官兵冒着蒙蒙细雨，在乡亲们依依不舍的目光中，兵分两路，向于都方向转移，由此迈出了万里长征的第一步。 版面设计：王云苗
