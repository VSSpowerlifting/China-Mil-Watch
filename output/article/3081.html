About this analysis
Summary
The PAP Liaoning Corps Huludao Detachment has conducted military-civilian co-building (双拥共建) work in Jianchang County and Lianshan District over several years, including establishing a swimwear factory in Linjiadian Village in 2019, running an educational assistance program called 'Little Buds' (小蓓蕾) since 2001, and co-staffing docent tours at the Tashan Blocking Battle Memorial Hall with students from Tashan August 1st Hope School. This is standard political work content documenting the PAP's civil-relations and ideological education activities; its value is as a record of how the institution frames the military-people bond and patriotic education for junior personnel, not as evidence of a new policy or operational posture.

One "Heart-Connecting Road," Brimming with the Bond Between Fish and Water — A Record of the PAP Liaoning Corps Huludao Detachment Serving the Local People

■ Sun Jili, Sun Xindi

Photo ①: Members of the PAP Liaoning Corps Huludao Detachment's assistance work team visit and offer condolences to those they are paired with for support.

Photo ②: Inside the Tashan Blocking Battle Memorial Hall, soldiers of the PAP Huludao Detachment and students from Tashan August 1st Hope School serve as paired docents.

Photo ③: At the swimwear factory in Linjiadian Village, Huludao City, members of the detachment's assistance work team talk with villagers who work there.

From June to July of this year, heavy rains struck multiple locations across Liaoning Province in succession.

Tong Qing, an officer of the PAP Liaoning Corps Huludao Detachment, and his comrades stood ready to deploy at any moment. Fortunately, early warnings were timely and the affected areas were limited, and no serious disaster materialized.

"One of our most important missions is to protect the people and serve the people — wherever there is a need, we must be there," said Tong Qing. Over the years he has served in Huludao, he has followed the detachment's vehicles across every township in the city: sometimes for emergency rescue and disaster relief, sometimes to support industrial development, sometimes for military-civilian co-building (双拥共建)… The wheels have left crisscrossing tracks across the land of western Liaoning, linking city and countryside, connecting mountains and sea, and forging a "heart-connecting road" (连心路) between the detachment's officers and soldiers and the local people.

A Swimwear Factory Built in a Mountain Hollow

It was a weekend. While scrolling through his WeChat Moments, Tong Qing suddenly noticed that Li Xiaohua — a villager from the village he is paired with for assistance — had posted new photos showing her son, Hu Zhenhai, busy at work in the village's swimwear factory.

Not long ago, Tong Qing had called to encourage Hu Zhenhai to return to the village to develop his future there, but Hu had felt there was no future in making swimwear in a mountain hollow. How had he turned around and come back so quickly? With the peak tourist season now in full swing in this coastal city and orders surging, could the swimwear factory's production capacity keep up? With these thoughts in mind, Tong Qing brought along members of the detachment's assistance work team and drove straight to Linjiadian Village in Jianchang County, the village they are paired with for assistance.

Outside the blue-roofed factory building, the moment everyone stepped out of the vehicle, they heard the "da-da-da" of sewing machines. Pulling fabric taut, pressing foot pedals, feeding material at a steady pace… Inside the factory, workers were bent over their sewing machines, rushing to finish swimwear garments. Among the crowd, Tong Qing spotted Hu Zhenhai at once — he was counting inventory — and walked over to tease him: "Zhenhai, when did you get back to the village?"

Hu Zhenhai set down the order sheet in his hand and smiled with an honest grin: "Big Brother Tong, don't bring that up. I was narrow-minded — I underestimated our village's industry. Lately everyone has been taking turns working overtime until 11 at night, and we still can't keep up with the orders going out!"

Linjiadian Village is located in the north-central part of Jianchang County. It is a typical mountain village with a weak industrial base. More than ten kilometers away, Peitun Village in Yaowang Manchu Township, Xingcheng City, presents an entirely different picture: the local area is home to a nationally renowned swimwear specialty industry, the village collective economy has grown substantially, large numbers of local villagers are employed, and skilled workers earn more than ten thousand yuan a month.

"Our starting points were about the same, but they earn money right at their doorstep. It would be a lie to say we weren't envious." Hu Zhenhai's words gave voice to the feelings of many villagers.

In 2019, the PAP Huludao Detachment and Linjiadian Village were paired for co-building. "At the outset of the assistance work, we made a special trip to Xingcheng City to conduct research on the swimwear industry, and found that many manufacturers, even operating at full capacity, still faced the predicament of having too many orders to deliver on time," said the head of the detachment's assistance work team.

"At that time, the assistance work team and the village Party branch put their heads together. Xingcheng had more orders than it could produce — could the village consider building a factory too, to take on cut-and-make swimwear orders?" said Zhao Defu, secretary of the village Party branch.

After research and deliberation, in October of that year, the assistance work team helped the village take over an idle factory building and purchase thirty sewing machines, then brought in a master craftsman from a swimwear factory in Xingcheng City to run a training class. Zhao Defu explained: "The training class was held right on the production floor — whatever skills the job required, that is what the instructor taught." After systematic training, villager Yang Huaile joined the swimwear factory as a worker. "The factory pays by the piece. Once you're skilled, you can earn more than 6,000 yuan a month," said Yang Huaile, beaming.

The Soldiers of the People Nurture the "Little Buds"

Leaving the swimwear factory, the assistance work team drove on along a country lane. Not far ahead, a familiar figure sat beneath an old locust tree enjoying the shade. They stopped the vehicle and looked — it was Wang Yulan, the grandmother of Xiao Yu, one of their educational assistance recipients.

Seeing that it was Tong Qing and the others, Wang Yulan wasted no time pulling the officers and soldiers by the hand and leading them inside: "Come in, come in, have some water and rest a while."

Xiao Yu is eleven years old and is the paired assistance recipient of Huang Jie, a sergeant and member of the assistance work team. Xiao Yu's parents are divorced, and after her father died in an accident when she was seven, she has lived with her elderly grandparents. Previously, during a military-civilian co-building activity, the assistance work team learned of Xiao Yu's situation from her school teachers and began including her as an educational assistance recipient.

"Xiao Yu has always been a sensible child. Recently, with Little Huang tutoring her, her math scores have improved quite a bit…" As everyone talked about Xiao Yu's studies and daily life, when the conversation turned to her future, Wang Yulan's eyes reddened. Choking back tears, she said: "You've seen the situation at home. But no matter how hard things get, we have to let this child go to school…"

The work team members sat in a circle, holding the old woman's hands, and spoke to her word by word, each word grounded in concrete action: "Auntie, please don't worry. Xiao Yu's school fees, her seasonal clothing, her books and pens and paper — we will find a way. We will absolutely never let this child be without schooling." Hearing this, the old woman could no longer hold back her tears.

Since 2001, the detachment has raised dedicated educational assistance funds under the "Little Buds" (小蓓蕾) program. It has also helped the August 1st Hope Primary School in Jianchang County — the school it is paired with for co-building — establish a national defense exhibition room and a national defense education corridor, and has selected outstanding backbone personnel to serve as off-campus counselors, regularly entering the campus to carry out national defense education. "Quite a few of the students who received assistance planted the seed of serving the country through military service in their hearts from a young age. After graduating from high school, student Chen Guanqun chose to enlist," said Huang Jie.

Before leaving, work team member Zhang Mingcong noticed a composition notebook lying open on a small square table. The essay title was "My Family." It read: "My grandpa and grandma take care of me in daily life — they are my family. The PAP uncles care about me in daily life — they are also my family. I must study hard, and when I grow up, help others just like the uncles do…"

"Red Epaulettes" Join Hands with Red Scarves

On the return journey, the assistance work team decided to make one more stop at the Tashan Blocking Battle Memorial Hall in Lianshan District, to see how the paired docent arrangement between the Red Scarf docents from Tashan August 1st Hope School and the detachment's soldiers was working out.

"Tashan has no pagoda, Tashan has no mountain — we of the 4th Column are the pagoda, we of the 4th Column are the mountain! As long as our 4th Column stands, the enemy shall not dream of crossing Tashan!" Upon entering the memorial hall, in front of the "Battle Assessment of the Day" display panel in the "Fierce Battle at Tashan" exhibition hall, PAP Huludao Detachment soldier Wang Tianyu was recounting this stirring pre-battle rallying cry of a People's Liberation Army commander to visitors.

Standing alongside Wang Tianyu was a Red Scarf docent — Dong Xinmu, a sixth-grade student at Tashan August 1st Hope School. In a voice young yet resonant, he pointed to a grain sack inside a glass display case bearing nine bullet holes and explained: "The victory of the Tashan Blocking Battle could not have been achieved without the support of the people. Villagers near Tashan Fort Village braved a hail of bullets to deliver grain to the People's Liberation Army…"

The two docents — one adult, one child — presenting together, blending solemn composure with vivid childlike sincerity, drew repeated thumbs-up from visitors.

Tian Xiujuan, a teacher at Tashan August 1st Hope School and leader of the Red Scarf docent team, explained that Tashan August 1st Hope School is located near the Tashan Blocking Battle Memorial Hall. In 2019, to encourage students to carry forward the Tashan Spirit through concrete action, the school invited officers and soldiers from the detachment's "Red Epaulettes Party History Lecture Team" (红肩章党史宣讲团) to join with students in forming the "Beacon Fire Lecture Team" (烽火宣讲团), establishing a collaborative mechanism of "presenting together, preparing lessons together, and refining stories together," and exploring a co-building model of paired presentations by soldiers and students.

After joining the "Beacon Fire Lecture Team," Sergeant Guo Wei consulted multiple military history sources, compiled little-known stories and details from the Tashan Blocking Battle, and edited them into commentary suitable for students. "Breaking a stretch of military history down into individual small stories with human warmth makes it easier for children to listen attentively, remember, and retell well," said Guo Wei.

Looking at the lively scene inside the hall, Tong Qing reflected: "We named our lecture team after 'beacon fire' (烽火) because we hope that every presentation will plant a red spark in people's hearts, igniting the enthusiasm of the broad masses to love the Party, love the country, and love the military."

一条“连心路”，洒满鱼水情 ——武警辽宁总队葫芦岛支队服务驻地群众记事 ■孙继利 孙昕迪 图①：武警辽宁总队葫芦岛支队帮扶工作组成员看望慰问帮扶对象。图②：塔山阻击战纪念馆里，武警葫芦岛支队战士和塔山八一希望学校学生搭档讲解。图③：在葫芦岛市林家店村的泳装厂，支队帮扶工作组成员与务工村民交谈。 今年6月至7月，辽宁省多地接连遭遇暴雨袭击。 武警辽宁总队葫芦岛支队干部佟庆和战友们做好了随时出动的准备，所幸预警及时、受灾范围不大，没有造成严重灾情。 “我们的一个重要使命，就是保卫人民、服务人民，哪里有需要，我们就要出现在哪里。”佟庆说，在葫芦岛服役这些年，他跟着支队的车，跑遍了葫芦岛的各乡镇：有时是抢险救灾、有时是帮扶产业、有时是双拥共建……车轮在辽西大地上留下纵横交错的轨迹，串起城乡、连结山海，也筑起支队官兵和驻地群众的“连心路”。 山沟里建成泳装厂 正逢周末，佟庆翻看微信朋友圈时，忽然发现结对帮扶村村民李晓华新发布的照片里，她的儿子胡振海正在村里的泳装厂忙活。 前阵子佟庆打电话劝胡振海回村发展，他还觉得山沟沟里做泳装没有出路，怎么一转眼就回村了？眼下正值海滨城市旅游旺季，订单量激增，泳装厂的生产能力能不能跟上？想到这里，佟庆拉上支队帮扶工作组成员，驱车直奔结对帮扶的建昌县林家店村。 蓝顶厂房外，众人一下车，便听到“哒哒哒”的缝纫机声。拉直布料，脚踩踏板，匀速推送……厂房里，工人们正紧盯缝纫机赶制泳装成衣。人群中，佟庆一眼就看见了正在清点货物的胡振海，上前打趣道：“振海，啥时候回村的？” 胡振海撂下手里的单子，笑得憨厚：“佟大哥，可别提那茬了，是我眼界窄，小瞧了咱村的产业。最近大家每天轮流加班到晚上11点，还赶不上订单出货呢！” 林家店村位于建昌县‌中部偏北‌区域，是典型的山区村，产业基础薄弱。而10多公里外的兴城市药王满族乡裴屯村，则是另一番景象：当地坐拥全国知名泳装特色产业，村集体经济壮大，吸纳大量本地村民就业，熟练工月收入过万元。 “发展起点差不多，但人家在家门口就挣钱，说不羡慕是假的。”胡振海的一番话，道出众多村民的心声。 2019年，武警葫芦岛支队和林家店村结对共建。“帮扶之初，我们专程前往兴城市开展泳装产业调研，发现不少厂商即使满负荷生产，仍面临订单太多无法按时交付的困境。”支队帮扶工作组负责人说。 “那时候帮扶工作组跟村党支部一块琢磨，兴城的订单太多来不及生产，村里能不能考虑也建个工厂，承接泳装来料加工订单。”村党支部书记赵德富说。 经过调研论证，当年10月，帮扶工作组帮助村里盘下闲置厂房、购置30台缝纫设备，又从兴城市一家泳装厂请来老师傅，办起培训班。赵德富介绍：“培训班就开设在生产车间里，岗位需要什么技能，老师就教授什么内容。”经过系统培训，村民杨怀乐进入泳装厂务工。“工厂计件发工资，熟练后一个月能挣6000多元。”杨怀乐满脸笑意地说。 子弟兵呵护“小蓓蕾” 从泳装厂出来，帮扶工作组一行驾车沿乡村小道继续行驶。不远处，一个熟悉的人影坐在老槐树下纳凉。他们停车一看，正是助学帮扶对象小于的奶奶王玉兰。 见是佟庆等人，王玉兰不由分说，拉着官兵的手就往屋里走：“快进来喝口水歇歇。” 小于今年11岁，是帮扶工作组成员、中士黄杰的结对帮扶对象。小于父母离异，7岁那年父亲意外离世后，她就一直跟随年迈的爷爷奶奶生活。此前，在一次军民共建活动中，帮扶工作组从学校教师口中得知小于的情况，便开始将她作为助学帮扶对象。 “小于这孩子本就懂事，最近在小黄的辅导下，数学成绩又提升不少……”大家聊起小于的学习、生活，当谈到小于的将来时，王玉兰却红了眼眶，哽咽着说：“你们看到家里的情况了。但再咋难，我们也得让这个娃上学……” 工作组成员围坐一圈，拉着老人的手，话一句句递过去，句句都落在实处：“大娘，您放心，小于上学的学费、换季衣裳、书本纸笔，我们来想办法，绝不会让孩子没学上。”听到这里，老人的眼泪再也止不住。 2001年以来，该支队筹措“小蓓蕾”专项助学资金，还帮助结对共建的建昌县八一希望小学建成国防陈列室和国防教育长廊，并遴选优秀骨干担任校外辅导员，走进校园常态化开展国防教育。“不少受助学生从小心中埋下从军报国的种子，学生陈冠群高中毕业后就选择参军入伍。”黄杰说。 临走前，帮扶工作组成员张明聪看到小方桌上摊开着一本作文本。作文题目是《我的家人》，文中写道：“爷爷奶奶在生活中照顾我，是我的家人；武警叔叔在生活上关心我，也是我的家人。我一定要用功读书，长大后像叔叔们一样帮助别人……” “红肩章”牵手红领巾 返程途中，帮扶工作组决定再去趟连山区的塔山阻击战纪念馆，看看塔山八一希望学校的红领巾讲解员和支队官兵的搭档讲解效果如何。 “塔山没有塔，塔山没有山，我们四纵就是塔，我们四纵就是山！有我们四纵在，敌人就别想越过塔山！”走进纪念馆时，“鏖战塔山”展厅“当日战评”展板前，武警葫芦岛支队战士王天宇正为游客讲述解放军指挥员这段激昂的阵前动员。 与王天宇并排而立的，是红领巾讲解员——塔山八一希望学校六年级学生董歆沐。他用稚嫩却洪亮的声音，指着玻璃展柜里带着9个弹洞的粮袋介绍：“塔山阻击战的胜利，离不开人民群众的支援。塔山堡村附近村民冒着枪林弹雨将粮食送给解放军……” 一大一小两位讲解员搭档讲解，庄重沉稳与鲜活童真相融，引得参观者频频竖起大拇指。 红领巾讲解员领队、塔山八一希望学校教师田秀娟介绍，塔山八一希望学校坐落于塔山阻击战纪念馆附近。2019年，为鼓励学生以实际行动传承塔山精神，学校邀请支队“红肩章党史宣讲团”官兵与学生共同组成“烽火宣讲团”，建立“同台讲解、共同备课、一起打磨故事”协作机制，探索官兵和学生搭档讲解的共建模式。 加入“烽火宣讲团”后，中士郭伟翻阅多种军史资料，从中整理出塔山阻击战鲜为人知的故事与细节，编辑成适合学生的讲解词。“把一段段军史拆解成一个个有温度的小故事，更利于孩子们听得进、记得住、讲得好。”郭伟说。 看着场馆里热闹的景象，佟庆感慨道：“我们的宣讲团以‘烽火’命名，就是希望每一次宣讲，都在人们心中播下红色火种，点燃广大群众的爱党爱国爱军热情。” 图片摄影：潘泓宇、李丰洋 制图：扈 硕
