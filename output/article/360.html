A Record of the PAP Gansu Corps Jiuquan Detachment Serving Local Communities and Supporting Rural Revitalization

武警甘肃总队酒泉支队服务驻地群众、助力乡村振兴纪实
PLA Daily (解放军报) 17 May 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
The PAP Gansu Corps Jiuquan Detachment's assistance work team has been stationed in Guazhou Village, Jiuquan City since 2022, conducting rural revitalization work that includes funding a 1,400-square-meter seedling greenhouse, building an e-commerce brand for local honeydew melons, making regular welfare visits to priority monitored households (重点监测户), and running national defense education programs at Guazhou Town Central Primary School. This is standard PAP civil-military relations content (军民鱼水情); its value is as a record of how the institution frames the detachment's non-combat role to junior personnel and the public, documenting the specific programmatic mix — agricultural infrastructure, household welfare monitoring, and youth patriotic education — the PAP deploys in a Gansu Silk Road county. The article does not address operational readiness or unit posture.

The Spring Wind of the Qilian Mountains Warms the Heart

— A Record of the PAP Gansu Corps Jiuquan Detachment Serving Local Communities and Supporting Rural Revitalization

■ Zhang Liang

Photo ①: Members of the PAP Gansu Corps Jiuquan Detachment's assistance work team conducting research on honeydew melon cultivation in Guazhou Village. Photo by Mu Xinyuan.

Photo ②: The Fuluhua Field scenic area in Sandaogou Town, Guazhou County, Jiuquan City, Gansu Province. Photo provided by the Guazhou County Convergence Media Center.

Photo ③: Officers and soldiers of the PAP Gansu Corps Jiuquan Detachment visiting villagers' homes in Guazhou Village. Photo by Mu Xinyuan.

Graphic design: Hu Shuo

"The snow on the Qilian Mountains melts into clear springs that flow down; the soldiers, our own flesh and blood, bring the spring wind to warm our hearts…" In early May, under bright sunshine in Guazhou County, Jiuquan City, Gansu Province, several elderly people strummed the dombra on the cultural square of Guazhou Village, pouring out their feelings through ancient folk melodies.

Songs of praise for the soldiers drifted on the spring breeze across acre after acre of thriving melon fields, across farmyard courtyards swept spotlessly clean, and across schoolyards filled with laughter and joy.援建基础设施 (aid in building infrastructure), caring for people in difficulty, carrying out assistance for education and learning… Wherever the officers and soldiers of the PAP Gansu Corps Jiuquan Detachment have walked, their genuine love for the people has swept through like a spring breeze, bringing new hope to Gobi Desert villages and writing a chapter of the bond between the army and the people (鱼水篇章) on the ancient Silk Road.

1. The Greenhouse Nurtures the "Melon Babies"

"Old Ma, check the drainage pipe joints — don't let them leak!" "After watering, remember to loosen the soil around the melon seedlings!"

Standing at the edge of the field, Wang Lei, secretary of the Guazhou Village Party general branch, called out instructions at the top of his voice. For the melon seedlings transplanted into the ground at the end of April, this is a critical period of growth, and he comes to check the melon fields every single day.

"These seedlings all came out of the greenhouse," Wang Lei explained. Guazhou Village is located at the western end of the Hexi Corridor, with average annual rainfall of less than 50 millimeters; agriculture depends entirely on irrigation from the Shule River. Although the area has abundant sunlight and large day-to-night temperature swings — which make the melons and fruit exceptionally sweet — there has always been one bottleneck that cannot be bypassed: seedling cultivation.

"Our village has a few greenhouses, but they've been around for years and their insulation and moisture-retention are not ideal. When there's a cold or dry spring, the seedlings are prone to problems," villager Ma Ping told this reporter.

"With seedlings you get seven-tenths of the harvest; without seedlings you get nothing." When the PAP Gansu Corps Jiuquan Detachment's assistance work team first entered Guazhou Village in 2022, this was exactly the situation they faced — villagers relied mainly on small greenhouses they had built themselves to raise seedlings, or purchased seedlings from other areas.

"Over the years of assistance work, we have trained villagers in agricultural techniques, built sheep-raising pens, and purchased farm machinery, but every time we come to the village to conduct research, we find that villagers' needs, no matter how they are expressed, always come back to the same two words: 'seedling cultivation,'" members of the detachment's assistance work team reported the relevant situation to the detachment Party committee.

"This is precisely the urgent, difficult, and pressing problem (急难愁盼) of the masses." The Party committee immediately decided to apply to higher authorities for special assistance funds, with a plan to aid the village in building a batch of new-style greenhouse facilities for seedling cultivation, so that villagers could use their own locally grown seedlings to produce local melons.

During this period, the detachment's assistance work team came to Guazhou Village more frequently: they went into melon farmers' greenhouses and fields to research needs and design plans. Wang Lei told this reporter that the new greenhouse currently being planned and built covers more than 1,400 square meters; calculated at a savings of 0.2 yuan per seedling compared to purchasing from outside, it can save villagers approximately 20,000 yuan in seedling costs per year. During the off-season for seedling cultivation, out-of-season vegetables can also be grown, generating additional income for the village collective.

The melons are sweet, the fruit is sweet — but the lives of the people must be made sweeter too. To expand the market for Guazhou honeydew melons, over the past two years the detachment has joined with the village's share-based economic cooperative to vigorously develop e-commerce sales, building the "Guazhou Melon Baby" (瓜娃娃) brand, and broadening sales channels through offline activities such as folk culture exhibitions, honeydew melon tasting events, and specialty agricultural product promotion fairs.

"From seedling to melon, it is all bound up with the people's livelihoods," said a member of the detachment's assistance work team. "We have not only kept a close eye on honeydew melon sales channels, but have also launched an initiative within the detachment, calling on everyone to help publicize 'Melon Baby' for the village through every possible means."

Calculating this year's projected output and planning the construction of the new greenhouse, Wang Lei could not suppress the smile on his face: "Ever since the troops started helping us, 'Melon Baby' has been going from strength to strength!"

2. Paired Villages: "Visiting Relatives"

"Uncle Shi, we've come to see you!"

Not long ago, officers and soldiers of the PAP Jiuquan Detachment's Guazhou Squadron went to Guazhou Village on a visit. As they called out in front of the home of 73-year-old Shi Wenqing, the old man came out to greet them with delight: "You're here — come in and sit down!"

Shi Wenqing's son and daughter-in-law are not nearby, and his two grandsons board at school on weekdays. Although the old man can still manage daily life on his own, he suffers from multiple chronic illnesses. Every time the squadron's officers and soldiers visit the village, they always stop by his home.

"Uncle, how has your health been lately?" Squadron commander Li Yang set the gifts down beside the table and helped the old man to his seat.

"Same as always — I have to take my medicine on time every day. Luckily, the land has all been transferred to the cooperative, so I don't have to farm it myself; I just collect my share of the dividends at year's end." Old Shi Wenqing smiled. "The policies are good now, and you all keep thinking of me — I'm doing quite well…"

The old man held Li Yang's hand and looked at the certificates of merit posted on the wall: "Both grandsons are doing well in school. Last semester one of them won a certificate, and the other placed in the top ten of his class."

After sharing in Uncle Shi's joy, the officers and soldiers went to the home of another "relative," Shi Baoshan. In the courtyard, Shi Baoshan's grandmother was scattering corn kernels into the chicken coop. When she saw the soldiers walking in, she dropped her ladle and called into the house: "Baoshan, the people from the troops have come to see you!"

In the main room, Shi Baoshan, seated in a wheelchair, heard the commotion and strained to turn his head. Catching sight of that familiar olive-green uniform, he let out indistinct sounds of "ah, ah."

"Baoshan recognizes you!" Grandmother Shi said with a smile. "Every time you come, he's happy."

Shi Baoshan and his grandmother are among the village's priority monitored households (重点监测户). The 20-year-old Shi Baoshan is paralyzed due to illness and depends mainly on his grandmother for care. The soldiers stepped forward to help feed the chickens and sweep the courtyard, while Li Yang sat down beside Shi Baoshan and listened to Grandmother Shi recount the family's recent situation.

"Auntie, don't worry — if you run into difficulties, come find us." With that, Li Yang left his phone number. As they said their goodbyes, Shi Baoshan waved continuously; his grandmother held the soldiers' hands reluctantly: "Take care on your way — come again next time!"

"Every visit feels like going to see relatives," said squadron soldier Huang Jiang. "You can feel that they truly treat us as family."

3. Walking into the Schoolyard to "Draw the Sun"

The People's Armed Police officers and soldiers who march in neat formation into Guazhou Town Central Primary School are always met with an enthusiastic welcome from the students — for it means they will get to hear the PAP uncles tell stories of heroes again.

"We came to the school on this year's Learn from Lei Feng Commemoration Day," Sergeant Hao Zhijie told this reporter. The students' enthusiasm for learning from Lei Feng is very high. When he told stories about Lei Feng, the students listened with rapt attention.

The soldiers' "classroom" is not only inside the schoolroom but also on the sports ground. This time, the PAP soldiers demonstrated drill movements for the students — quick march, parade march, double march — all in perfect unison, drawing continuous exclamations of admiration from the students. "So cool! When I grow up I want to join the army too!" Second-grader Zhang Xiaodong stared at the soldiers in formation and excitedly imitated the saluting gesture.

Teaching students to stand at attention in military posture, sharing stories of military life, distributing donated stationery and extracurricular reading materials… At the end of the lively activities, the soldiers and students "drew the sun" together. The students drew suns one after another on the blackboard with colored chalk; some also wrote beside them: "Plant the sun, warm others." The sunlight fell on them, and they shone brilliantly.

"We want to use this method to 'plant a sun' in the children's hearts," said Bao Qingliang, political instructor of a squadron of the PAP Jiuquan Detachment. "To let them feel warmth, and in the future go on to warm others." In recent years, detachment officers and soldiers have regularly gone to schools to carry out national defense education and donate books and sports and cultural equipment. Last Children's Day (June 1st), the soldiers also invited teachers and students to visit the military camp.

"Soldiers are role models for children," said principal Wang Yugui. "The love and care that the soldiers give the children has a profound influence on them."

In several middle schools across Guazhou County, more than a dozen students are the shared concern of the detachment's officers and soldiers — at the start of each academic year, scholarship funds are delivered to them on time; every summer, the detachment invariably receives several letters of good news reporting that sponsored students have been admitted to key high schools and universities.

A thousand years ago, a poet captured the desolation and loneliness of the desert frontier with the line "the spring wind does not pass beyond Yumen Pass." Today, the spring wind has already arrived on the Gobi Desert, and the spring light is glorious beneath the Qilian Mountains. The moving story of the profound bond between the army and the people (军民鱼水情深) vividly presents the era's tableau of unity between the military and government and between the military and the people (军政军民团结).

“祁连山上的雪哟，化成清泉流下来；亲人子弟兵哟，带来春风暖心怀……”5月初，甘肃省酒泉市瓜州县艳阳高照，瓜州村文化广场上，几位老人弹起冬不拉，用古老的民歌调子直抒胸臆。 赞美子弟兵的歌谣，伴着春风飘过一亩亩生机勃勃的瓜田，飘过打扫得干干净净的农家庭院，也飘过充满笑语欢声的校园。援建基础设施、关爱困难群众、开展助学兴教……在武警甘肃总队酒泉支队官兵走过的地方，爱民真情如春风拂过，为戈壁村庄带来新的希望，在丝路古道写下鱼水篇章。 1 温棚育出“瓜娃娃” “老马，检查下水管接口，可别漏水！”“浇完水记得给瓜苗松松土啊！” 站在地头，瓜州村党总支书记王磊扯着嗓子指挥。对4月底刚移栽到地里的瓜苗来说，眼下正是生长的关键时期，他天天都要到瓜田查看。 “这些瓜苗都是温棚移出来的。”王磊介绍，瓜州村地处河西走廊西端，年均降水量不足50毫米，农业全靠疏勒河灌溉。虽然光照充足、昼夜温差大，种出的瓜果格外甜，可有个关口始终绕不过——育苗。 “我们村有几个温棚，但是年头久了，保温、保湿效果都不太理想，遇到春寒春旱，瓜苗就容易出问题。”村民马平告诉笔者。 “有苗七分收，没苗一场空。”2022年，武警甘肃总队酒泉支队帮扶工作队第一次走进瓜州村时，面临的就是这样的状况——村民主要依靠自家搭建的小温棚育苗，或从外地购买瓜苗。 “帮扶这几年，我们为村民培训农业技术、修建绵羊养殖圈舍、购买农机，但每次来村里调研，发现村民的需求说来说去，还是绕不开‘育苗’二字。”支队帮扶工作队成员将相关情况呈报给支队党委。 “这就是群众急难愁盼的问题。”党委当即决定，向上级申请专项帮扶资金，计划为村里援建一批新型温室大棚用于育苗，让村民用上自家苗，种出本地瓜。 这段时间，支队帮扶工作队到瓜州村来得更勤了：他们走进瓜农的温棚和地头，调研需求、设计方案。王磊告诉笔者，规划在建的新大棚占地1400余平方米，按每株苗比外购便宜0.2元计算，一年可为村民节省约2万元购苗费用；育苗淡季，还可种植反季节蔬菜，为村集体增加收入。 瓜甜果甜，更要让群众生活也甜起来。为让瓜州蜜瓜销路更广，这两年支队联合村股份经济合作社大力发展电商销售，打造“瓜娃娃”品牌，并通过民俗展览、蜜瓜品鉴会、特色农产品推介会等线下活动，拓宽销售渠道。 “从苗到瓜，都是群众民生所系。”支队帮扶工作队成员说，“我们不但一直关注蜜瓜销路，在支队内部也发起倡议，号召大家通过各种方式帮助村里宣传‘瓜娃娃’。” 计算着今年的预计产量，规划着新大棚的建设，王磊脸上的笑意止不住：“自从有了部队帮扶，‘瓜娃娃’走得越来越稳了！” 2 结对村里“走亲戚” “史大爷，我们来看您啦！” 前不久，武警酒泉支队瓜州中队官兵到瓜州村走访。来到73岁的老人史文清家门前，刚一招呼，老人就欣喜地迎了出来：“你们来了，快进屋坐坐！” 史文清的儿子儿媳都不在身边，两个孙子平日住校。虽然老人生活尚能自理，却身患多种慢性病。中队官兵每次到村里走访，总要去他家中看看。 “大爷，您最近身体咋样？”大队长李洋把慰问品放在桌旁，扶着老人坐下。 “还是老样子，每天都得按时吃药。幸亏土地都流转给合作社了，不用自己种，年底分红就行。”史文清老人笑了笑，“现在政策好，你们又老惦记着，我过得挺好……” 老人拉着李洋的手，看向墙上贴的奖状：“两个孙子学习还争气，上学期一个拿了奖状，一个考了班里前十。” 分享了史大爷的喜悦，官兵又来到另一户“亲戚”石宝山家。院子里，石宝山的奶奶正往鸡窝里撒玉米粒，见官兵们走进来，她扔下瓢就往屋里喊：“宝山，部队上的人来看你了！” 堂屋里，坐在轮椅上的石宝山听见动静，努力转动脖子。看见那一身熟悉的橄榄绿，他发出含混不清的“啊啊”声。 “宝山认得你们！”石奶奶笑着说，“每次你们来，他都高兴。” 石宝山祖孙俩是村里的重点监测户。20岁的石宝山因病瘫痪，主要靠奶奶照顾。官兵上前帮忙喂鸡、打扫庭院，李洋坐到石宝山身边，听石奶奶讲家中近况。 “大娘，您放心，有难处就找我们。”说完，李洋留下自己的电话号码。告别时，石宝山不住地挥手，奶奶不舍地拉着官兵的手：“你们慢走，下回还来啊！” “每次来，都像走亲戚。”中队战士黄江说，“能感觉到，他们真的把我们当亲人。” 3 走进校园“画太阳” 迈着整齐步伐走进瓜州镇中心小学的武警官兵，总能获得学生们的热烈欢迎——这意味着又能听武警叔叔讲英雄故事了。 “今年学雷锋纪念日那天我们来过学校。”中士郝智杰告诉笔者，学生们学雷锋热情很高。他讲雷锋故事，学生们听得格外入迷。 官兵的“课堂”不仅在教室里，也在操场上。这次，武警战士们为学生展示队列动作，齐步、正步、跑步，整齐划一，学生们不住地发出赞叹声。“太帅了！我长大了也要当兵！”二年级学生张小东盯着列队的战士，兴奋地模仿着敬礼的姿势。 教学生们站军姿、分享军旅生活、分发捐赠的文具和课外读物……热闹的活动最后，官兵和学生们一起“画太阳”。学生们用彩色粉笔在黑板上画出一个个太阳，有的还在旁边写上：“种下太阳，温暖他人。”阳光照上去，闪闪发光。 “我们想通过这种方式，在孩子们心里‘种下一个太阳’。”武警酒泉支队某中队指导员鲍庆亮说，“让他们感受到温暖，将来也去温暖别人。”近年来，支队官兵定期到学校开展国防教育，捐赠图书和文体器材。去年“六一”，官兵还邀请师生走进军营参观。 “军人是孩子们的榜样。”校长王玉贵说，“子弟兵给孩子们的爱和关怀，对他们的影响很深。” 在瓜州县的多所中学里，还有十几名学生，是支队官兵共同的牵挂——每学年开学，一笔笔助学金就会准时送到他们手中；每年夏天，支队总能收到几封受资助学生被重点高中、大学录取的喜报。 千百年前，诗人以“春风不度玉门关”道出大漠边塞的荒凉寂寞。而今，戈壁滩上春风已至、祁连山下春光正好，军民鱼水情深的动人故事，生动呈现着军政军民团结的时代画卷。
