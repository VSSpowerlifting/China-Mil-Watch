What They Brought Back from Grassroots Units Was a "Problem-Resolution Receipt" Signed and Confirmed by Officers and Soldiers

他们从基层单位带回的，是官兵签字确认的“解难回执单”
PLA Daily (解放军报) 6 July 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
The PAP Hainan Corps Sanya Detachment has replaced its previous practice of headquarters cadres compiling problem ledgers during grassroots embedded assignments with a system of 解难回执单 ("problem-resolution receipts") — documents signed by officers and soldiers confirming that specific difficulties were actually resolved, with outcomes tied to cadres' promotions and awards. The article documents a named internal reform campaign targeting formalism in the detachment's political work inspection system, framed around the concept of 正确政绩观 (correct view of performance). This is standard political work content; its value is as a record of how PAP leadership is institutionalizing accountability mechanisms to pressure headquarters cadres away from box-checking inspections and toward substantive burden-reduction at the company level.
Translation

The PAP Sanya Detachment Takes Concrete Measures to Resolve Difficulties and Reduce Burdens at the Grassroots — Cadres Assigned to Help Units Bring Back "Problem-Resolution Receipts"

■ Huang Wentao, Mao Wenxiu, PLA Daily Reporter Li Youzhi

Recently, a group of headquarters cadres from the People's Armed Police Hainan Corps Sanya Detachment completed their embedded assistance assignments and returned from the grassroots. Unlike before, what they brought back from grassroots units was no longer a ledger of problems or a rectification checklist, but rather "problem-resolution receipts" (解难回执单) signed and confirmed by officers and soldiers.

Speaking to this change, Section Chief Yin of the detachment headquarters — who has not been in the post long — told this reporter a story.

Shortly after taking up his post, he went to a certain company for an embedded assignment and spent one week compiling nearly 30 issues in areas such as Party branch deliberation procedures and internal affairs detail standards, then required the company to formulate rectification measures item by item and submit weekly progress reports.

Section Chief Yin had originally felt that his grassroots work that time was thorough and solid, but the grassroots response threw cold water on him. The company's political instructor reported to him: "We noticed many of the problems on the list long ago, but they are difficult to fix on our own — they require the detachment headquarters and sister units to work together to resolve them." An ordnance technician was even more blunt: "Plenty of problems were listed, but the difficulties with equipment storage that we had previously raised were not mentioned at all."

"The purpose of an embedded assignment is not to 'find fault.' True performance (政绩) lies not only in discovering problems, but even more in whether those problems are actually solved." These pointed words from the detachment's leadership struck Section Chief Yin deeply. He soon voluntarily requested to return to the same company for a second assignment. This time, his work focus was not to carry a "notebook" and record problems, but to pull up a "stool" and immerse himself in the squads and platoons, listening earnestly to the genuine thoughts of officers and soldiers.

After conducting in-depth heart-to-heart talks with officers and soldiers, Section Chief Yin found that many married personnel were visibly troubled when talking about practical problems such as family members' visits and children's school enrollment: the company's garrison was far from the city, housing was tight when family members came to visit, quality educational resources in the surrounding area were scarce, and family members of personnel living apart from their spouses faced serious difficulties with employment and children's schooling. Previously, the company had tried many approaches to address this, but with little effect.

After getting a clear picture of the actual situation, Section Chief Yin promptly made a special report to the detachment Party committee and proactively coordinated with relevant functional departments, resolving the school enrollment difficulties for the children of several officers and soldiers. Beyond that, the detachment Party committee attached great importance to the situation Section Chief Yin had reported and has already included advancing the renovation and upgrading of the family housing building in its work plan.

Since the beginning of this year, the detachment has seized on the opportunity of carrying out study and education on establishing and practicing a correct view of performance (正确政绩观) to take aim at chronic maladies such as formalism, and through a series of measures that both break old practices and establish new ones, has driven the work of resolving difficulties and reducing burdens at the grassroots to produce tangible results:

Multiple redundant ledgers have been abolished, and the written materials that grassroots companies submit monthly to the headquarters have been substantially streamlined; the system of "problem-resolution receipts" (解难回执单) for grassroots assistance assignments has been comprehensively implemented, with officer and soldier satisfaction and the actual effectiveness of resolving difficulties and helping those in need established as the primary criteria for evaluating the quality and effectiveness of headquarters cadres' assistance work; a first-contact responsibility system (首接负责制) for grassroots difficulties has been established and improved, eliminating the phenomenon of grassroots units repeatedly filing reports and writing explanatory statements; the effectiveness of reducing burdens at the grassroots has been linked to headquarters cadres' commendations, awards, promotions, and rank advancements, vigorously correcting the deviation in the view of performance (政绩观) that "emphasizes traces over results" (重痕迹、轻实效)… As one practical measure after another has been implemented, grassroots units' satisfaction with headquarters work has risen substantially.

"Establishing and practicing a correct view of performance (正确政绩观) means doing more real work and not engaging in empty formalism." The detachment's leadership told this reporter that as a number of difficulties that have long plagued the grassroots — such as the difficulty of accessing medical care for remote units — have been effectively resolved, officers and soldiers' enthusiasm and motivation for training and combat readiness are higher and stronger. Recently, the detachment has successfully completed multiple major duty security tasks, the overall excellence rate in military training assessments has risen markedly, and the level of the unit's all-around development has reached a new height.

武警三亚支队出实招为基层解难减负—— 帮建干部带回“解难回执单” ■黄文涛 毛文秀 解放军报记者 李由之 近日，武警海南总队三亚支队一批机关干部完成蹲点帮建，从基层归来。与以往不同的是，他们从基层单位带回的，不再是问题台账或整改清单，而是官兵签字确认的“解难回执单”。 谈及这一变化，任职时间不长的该支队机关尹股长给记者讲了一个故事—— 上任不久，他到某中队蹲点，用一周时间梳理出支部议事流程、内务细节规范等方面的问题近30条，并要求中队逐项制订整改措施、每周报送完成进度。 尹股长原本感觉自己这次下基层工作很扎实，可基层的反应给他泼了一盆冷水。中队指导员向他反映：“清单上的不少问题我们早有察觉，但仅靠一己之力难以根治，需要支队机关和兄弟单位携手推进解决。”一名军械员更是直言：“问题列了不少，我们此前反映的装备保管方面难题却只字未提。” “蹲点的目的不是‘找茬’。真正的政绩，不仅仅在于发现问题，更在于是否解决了问题。”该支队领导一针见血的话语，让尹股长深受触动。很快，他主动申请再次进驻该中队。这一次，他的工作重点不是带着“本子”记录问题，而是端起“板凳”扎进班排，认真倾听官兵的真实心声。 尹股长与官兵深入谈心后发现，不少已婚官兵聊起家属来队、子女入学等现实问题时愁眉不展：中队营区距离市区较远，家属来队住房紧张，周边优质教育资源稀缺，两地分居官兵家属就业、子女入学面临重重困难。此前，中队为此想过不少办法，但收效甚微。 摸清实情后，尹股长迅速向支队党委作了专题汇报，并主动对接相关业务部门，协调解决了多名官兵子女的入学难题。不仅如此，支队党委对尹股长反映的情况高度重视，已将推动家属楼升级改造列入工作计划。 今年以来，该支队以开展树立和践行正确政绩观学习教育为契机，向形式主义等沉疴积弊亮剑，以一系列破立并举的实招硬招，推动为基层解难减负工作落地见效—— 取消多项冗余台账，大幅精简基层中队每月上报机关的文字材料；全面推行帮建基层“解难回执单”制度，将官兵满意度、解难帮困实效作为评价机关干部帮建质效的主要标准；建立完善基层难题首接负责制，杜绝基层重复打报告、写情况说明的现象；将为基层减负成效与机关干部评先评优、晋职晋衔挂钩，大力纠治“重痕迹、轻实效”的政绩观偏差……随着一项项务实举措实施，该支队基层单位对机关工作的满意度大幅提升。 “树立和践行正确政绩观，就是要多干实事、不做虚功。”该支队领导告诉记者，随着偏远单位就医难等一批长期困扰基层的难题得到有效解决，官兵练兵备战热情更高、动力更足。近期，该支队圆满完成多项重大执勤安保任务，军事训练考核整体优秀率明显提升，部队全面建设水平迈上新高度。
