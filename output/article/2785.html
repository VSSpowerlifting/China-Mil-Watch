Reflections on Two Online Messages from Officers and Soldiers: A Report on an Air Force Brigade's Ongoing Deepening of Political Rectification Training

空军某旅持续深化政治整训见闻：对两条官兵网上留言的反思
PLA Daily (解放军报) 3 August 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
An unidentified Air Force brigade has used the PLA's internal Strong Military Network (强军网) to run a grassroots feedback platform called 'Sunshine Military Affairs — All Questions Answered' (阳光兵事 有问必答), through which enlisted personnel and junior officers submitted complaints that exposed two specific command failures: an organ staff officer who prioritized visible 'quick-win' (短平快) tasks over foundational unit-building during a grassroots assistance posting, and a brigade leader who let a publicly committed fix to merit-citation quotas stall for months once superior scrutiny moved elsewhere. Both cases prompted formal institutional responses — revised evaluation rules that weight foundational work over highlights, and a new normalized supervision chain (常态化监督链条) — framing the platform as a mechanism for political rectification training (政治整训) rather than routine grievance handling. The article documents a recurring PLA command pathology: cadres calibrating effort to what superiors can see rather than what combat readiness requires, and it records how one brigade is attempting to use bottom-up transparency to correct that incentive structure, though whether the revised rules alter behavior beyond this unit remains unknown.

Reflections on Two Online Messages from Officers and Soldiers

— A Report on an Air Force Brigade's Ongoing Deepening of Political Rectification Training

How much supervisory power and influence can online messages from officers and soldiers have over a Party committee and its organs? Since last year, a certain Air Force brigade has used the Strong Military Network (强军网) to launch a message platform called "Sunshine Military Affairs — All Questions Answered" (阳光兵事 有问必答), encouraging grassroots officers and soldiers to supervise and evaluate the work of the brigade Party committee and its organs, and to consult and provide feedback on problems in daily work, training, and construction, as well as to offer opinions and suggestions. Since the platform was established, officers and soldiers have enthusiastically submitted hundreds of messages, many of which contain feedback on the brigade Party committee and organ work that has deeply moved the brigade Party committee.

Using the opinions of officers and soldiers as a mirror to reflect on the details of their work, and then conducting discussions and analysis — this has become a powerful handle (抓手) for deepening their political rectification training (政治整训). In recent times, two messages from officers and soldiers have again moved them deeply.

Message from Officers and Soldiers ①

During the period when an organ cadre was stationed at our company to assist in its development, he was very attentive to several high-profile tasks — upgrading the honor room and building the culture wall — and pushed them forward quickly. However, he has repeatedly failed to respond to issues we raised multiple times, including the gap in the cultivation of company backbone personnel and the lack of unified standards in daily management. We do not know when progress on these matters will be made.

— Xiao Lin, Party branch committee member and grassroots atmosphere supervisor, a certain guard company

After Receiving the Message

When he first saw this message, the person in question — Staff Officer Zhang from the organ — was somewhat indignant: "The renovation of the honor room was a debt owed to this company for many years, and the cultural activities were something the soldiers themselves had asked for. My time helping the unit was short and the tasks were heavy — I prioritized solving these problems. How can the grassroots still be ungrateful?"

But on reflection, Staff Officer Zhang found his confidence somewhat lacking. Asking himself honestly, he had to admit that during the assistance work he did harbor a mentality of emphasizing visible achievements (显绩) over hidden achievements (隐绩).

At the outset of his posting, he had investigated and identified the deep-seated, prominent contradictions constraining the company's development — irregular implementation of organizational life (组织生活) systems, rote Party member education, and a severe gap in the backbone of specialist personnel. At the time, he had also thought about settling in and resolving these issues one by one. But when he privately calculated the "time account" and "energy account," he found: upgrading the honor room and building cultural highlights could produce results in a month or two and generate experience, making them immediately visible to leaders and comrades. Whereas regularizing the company's organizational life and cultivating a tiered backbone — these routine, foundational tasks were slow work, requiring at least half a year before any improvement would show, by which point his own assistance assignment would already be over, and the results would inevitably be invisible and intangible.

After weighing the options, driven by the mentality of "producing results quickly and leaving a good impression," he placed these problems that could not show results quickly into the "long-term planning" folder, and directed his main energy toward advancing the two "quick-win" (短平快) high-profile tasks. When the assistance period ended, Staff Officer Zhang returned to his department with his "results" and received praise from his department leadership. For a time, he even felt that his approach to work showed both vision and effectiveness.

During study and education sessions, this message caught the attention of the brigade Party committee. When organ cadres were organized to discuss it, some frankly admitted that in the past, when evaluating the effectiveness of organ-linked assistance to grassroots units, they sometimes focused primarily on whether there were immediate results and whether highlights had been produced. With that orientation in place, it was inevitable that some comrades would exert effort in that direction.

"The grassroots criticism is correct. Reflecting on myself, the old thinking of 'station at a unit, leave a highlight' (蹲点留亮点) is, in essence, an unwillingness to put in effort on latent achievements (潜绩), a reluctance to gnaw on hard bones, and insufficient courage and initiative in taking responsibility." Staff Officer Zhang voluntarily stepped forward to conduct self-criticism. "Placing the satisfaction of superiors first and putting combat readiness and grassroots needs second — at bottom, this is a mentality of seeking quick gains and instant benefits (急功近利), and my outlook on political achievements (政绩观) has gone off course."

As the discussion deepened, a consensus became increasingly clear: when the outlook on political achievements is misaligned, the exercise of power will inevitably go astray. Deepening political rectification training means rectifying this utilitarian thinking of "emphasizing appearances over inner strength, emphasizing short-term effects over long-term effects," and keeping the focus on the ability to fight and win, genuinely placing the foundation of assistance work on things that lay a solid foundation and serve long-term interests.

Once thinking was unified, rectification was promptly launched. The brigade Party committee revised the "Detailed Rules for Evaluating Grassroots Linked-Assistance Work" (基层挂钩帮建工作考评细则), further increasing the weight of foundational work such as organizational development and education management, reducing the proportion of bonus points for highlights, and stipulating that assistance work without a problem checklist and without officer-and-soldier evaluations would not be accepted upon completion. A mechanism was established to close out assistance work ledger items, with effectiveness tracked and assessed for a full year.

Not long ago, Staff Officer Zhang returned to the guard company with a rectification checklist, advancing item by item the foundational work of regularizing organizational life, cultivating a tiered backbone, and unifying management standards, with progress publicly posted on the platform throughout for supervision.

Party Committee Reply: Thank you to Xiao Lin for the feedback identifying this problem. We have begun to rectify issues such as organ assistance to grassroots units "emphasizing highlights over foundations," and have ordered the relevant responsible party to return to the company for a "re-do" of the assistance work, striving to complete several routine and foundational tasks that officers and soldiers care about and are watching. Please continue to follow up with supervision and actively offer opinions and suggestions.

Message from Officers and Soldiers ②

At a "Soldier Reception Day" (士兵接待日) event at the beginning of the year, a brigade leader took ownership of the problem we raised — that the allocation of merit citations and commendations was not sufficiently scientific. Everyone was very pleased and felt there was hope for the problem to be resolved. But at several subsequent events, the reply we received each time was "currently being processed." We do not know when this problem will be resolved.

— Squad Leader Cai, a certain company

After Receiving the Message

That morning, when this brigade leader opened his computer and saw this message, he felt his face flush with heat.

At the "Soldier Reception Day" event at the beginning of the year, this brigade leader had attended with organ personnel. During the event, Squad Leader Cai and several comrades from a certain company reported that their specialty had a large personnel base and heavy daily operational and training tasks, but the annual quotas for merit citations and commendations and the indicators for non-commissioned officer selection and promotion were relatively few. They suggested that the corresponding indicators and quotas be increased. After learning of the situation, this brigade leader felt the problem would not be difficult to resolve, so he rose to respond, voluntarily took ownership of the problem, and promised to reply as soon as possible.

But while taking ownership of the problem was easy, resolving it was not. What appeared to be merely a matter of adjusting quota proportions in fact required multiple organ departments to conduct research, demonstration, and coordination, and after a plan was produced it still had to be submitted to the Party committee meeting for deliberation. This leader led the relevant departments through several rounds of research, and just as he was about to produce a preliminary plan, multiple tasks — live-fire exercises, specialty skills competitions, and others — arrived in succession, and the matter temporarily went no further.

During the study and education sessions organized by the brigade Party committee in conjunction with deepening political rectification training, everyone paid close attention to this message from officers and soldiers. When asked about the background and circumstances, this brigade leader explained with a red face.

"At first I thought: since I had made a commitment to the grassroots officers and soldiers, I should explain and respond in a timely manner and overcome difficulties to fulfill my promise. But later, as tasks piled up, I found it hard to keep up. Subconsciously, I felt that live-fire exercises and specialty competitions were 'hard tasks' (硬任务) that superiors were closely watching — there could be not the slightest slackening. Whereas the demands of the grassroots were 'soft tasks' (软任务) that superiors would not pay attention to and no one would press on — it would be fine to set them aside for a while. So I had the relevant departments mark it 'currently being processed' and went off to attend to other work…"

"Behind a leader stands thousands of troops. Whether work is being done and how well it is being done — the eyes of officers and soldiers are all watching. Every action and movement is connected to the image and authority of the Party organization and its leading cadres." After learning of the situation, Party committee members subjected this brigade leader to serious criticism. Everyone felt that while this appeared to be a problem of work coordination, it was in fact a matter of Party member cadres having insufficient initiative and awareness, and an inadequate understanding of the weight of credibility attached to the power in their hands.

In the course of discussion and analysis, everyone placed themselves in the picture and placed their responsibilities in the picture, pointing directly at some common ailments (共性病灶) in the fulfillment of duties and responsibilities. For example: some, feeling unsupervised, exercised power arbitrarily and capriciously, handling or delaying the demands of officers and soldiers as they pleased; some had a weak sense of putting the grassroots first, keeping their eyes only on the "hard indicators" (硬指标) that superiors were watching and implementing selectively, picking and choosing their work; some had not firmly established the combat effectiveness standard (战斗力标准), with work that was showy but insubstantial and of little help in improving the quality and effectiveness of training and combat readiness; and so on.

"We must deeply understand that each broken promise, each delay, consumes the trust of officers and soldiers in the Party organization and damages the foundation of the force." The brigade Party committee further made clear that the power in the hands of leading cadres is connected to the trust of officers and soldiers and bears on training and combat readiness. The more important the post, the greater the responsibility, and the greater the need to proactively accept supervision.

In accordance with the principle of "clarifying responsibilities through lists, enabling free expression through the platform, and maintaining full transparency throughout" (清单明责、平台畅言、全程透明), they established and improved a normalized supervision chain (常态化监督链条). Through the mechanism of "standing committee members taking the lead, discipline inspection overseeing, departments acting in coordination, and implementation being assessed for results" (常委牵头、纪检督办、部门联动、落实问效), they treat supervision as a tightening band (紧箍咒) for regulating the exercise of power and a whetstone for tempering the courage to take responsibility, resolutely rectifying problems of inaction such as "quick to make statements, slow to implement" (表态快、落实慢), and compelling leading cadres to face problems directly in their work and reach directly into the hearts of soldiers (直抵兵心).

After the meeting, this brigade leader immediately led the relevant departments in launching a new round of research, examining commendation indicator allocations for each specialty against policies and regulations item by item; and in conjunction with the realities of soldier selection and retirement work, further revised and improved the detailed rules for selection and promotion assessment…

(This group of articles was compiled by Yao Shiquan)

Party Committee Reply:

Previously, the problem taken ownership of by the brigade leader — "insufficient merit citation quotas and non-commissioned officer selection and promotion indicators for a certain specialty" — was not followed up on and resolved in a sufficiently timely manner. We sincerely apologize and offer self-criticism to everyone here. We have now, based on relevant policies and incorporating opinions from both the grassroots and the organs, preliminarily studied and produced resolution measures, which are being published on the platform for solicitation of opinions and suggestions; once approved, they will be implemented as soon as possible. Everyone is welcome to supervise the entire process and offer reminders at any time.

Original Chinese
对两条官兵网上留言的反思 ——空军某旅持续深化政治整训见闻 官兵网上留言，能对党委机关有多大监督力和影响力？去年以来，空军某旅依托强军网开通“阳光兵事 有问必答”留言平台，鼓励基层官兵就旅党委和机关工作进行监督评议，也可就日常工作、训练、建设等各方面存在的问题进行咨询反馈，并提出意见建议。平台开设以来，官兵踊跃留言数百条，其中有不少对旅党委和机关工作的反馈，让该旅党委深受触动。 对照官兵意见反思细节过程，进行讨论辨析，这成为他们深化政治整训的一个有力抓手。近段时间，官兵的两条留言，又让他们深受触动。 官兵留言① 一名机关干部在我们连蹲点帮建期间，对荣誉室升级、文化墙打造这几项亮点工作很上心，推进也很快，但对我们多次提出的连队骨干培养断档、日常管理标准不统一等问题迟迟没有答复，不知这些工作何时才有进展？ ——某警卫连支委、基层风气监督员 小 林 收到留言后 起初看到这条留言，当事人、机关张干事有些不服气：“荣誉室改造是这个连队多年的欠账，文化活动也是战士们提过的需求，我这次帮建时间短任务重，优先解决了这几个问题，咋基层还不领情？” 但仔细一想，张干事的底气就有些不足了。扪心自问，他在帮建中确实存在一些重显绩、轻隐绩的心态。 蹲点之初，他就调研掌握了该连组织生活制度落实不规范、党员教育照本宣科、专业骨干断层严重等制约连队建设的深层突出矛盾，当时也想过沉下心来逐项破解，可私下里算算“时间账”“精力账”，他发现：升级荣誉室、打造文化亮点，一两个月就能有成果且形成经验，让领导和战友一眼就能看得见。而帮连队规范组织生活、培养骨干梯队这些经常性基础性工作，都是慢功夫，少说要半年才能见起色，到时候自己的帮建任务都结束了，成效难免看不见、摸不着。 权衡再三，抱着“尽快出成效、留下好印象”的心态，他就把这些一时不好见成效的问题放进了“长期规划”文件夹，把主要精力放在推进2项“短平快”的亮点工作上。帮建期满，带着“成果”回到科室的张干事受到了部门领导表扬，他也一度觉得自己抓工作有思路、有成效。 学习教育时，这条留言引起了旅党委的注意。组织机关干部讨论时，有人坦言，咱们以往检验机关挂钩帮建成效，有时重点看有没有立竿见影、有没有帮出亮点，导向摆在那里，难免有些同志往这个方向使劲。 “基层批评得对。我反躬自省，‘蹲点留亮点’的老思路，本质是不愿在潜绩上下功夫，不敢啃硬骨头，担当作为不够。”张干事主动站出来开展自我批评，“把上级满意放在首位，把练兵备战和基层需求放在其次，说到底是急功近利心态在作祟，我的政绩观出现了偏差。” 随着讨论深入，共识愈发清晰：政绩观出现错位，权力运行必然走偏。深化政治整训，就是要纠治这种“重门面轻内功、重短效轻长效”的功利化思维，着眼能打胜仗，把帮建的落脚点真正放到打基础、利长远的事上。 统一思想后，整改随即推开。旅党委修订《基层挂钩帮建工作考评细则》，进一步提升组织建设、教育管理等基础性工作权重，压缩亮点加分占比，明确无问题清单、无官兵测评的帮建不予验收；建立帮建台账销号机制，成效跟踪问效一整年。 前不久，张干事带着整改清单再次来到警卫连，逐项推进组织生活规范、骨干梯队培养、管理标准统一等基础工作，进度全程在平台公示接受监督。 党委回帖：感谢小林反馈指出的问题，我们已着手纠治机关帮建基层“重亮点、轻基础”等问题，并责令相关责任人到连队“回炉”帮建，争取完成好官兵关心关注的几项经常性基础性工作。请大家继续跟进监督、积极提出意见建议。 官兵留言② 年初一次“士兵接待日”活动中，一名旅领导认领了我们反映的立功受奖名额设置不够科学问题，大家都很高兴，觉得解决问题有希望了。可后来几次活动，我们每次等到的答复都是“正在办理中”，不知这个问题何时能得到解决？ ——某连 蔡班长 收到留言后 那天早上，打开电脑看到这条留言，这名旅领导顿觉脸上有些发烫。 年初那次“士兵接待日”活动，这名旅领导带机关人员参加。活动中，某连蔡班长和几名战友反映，他们这个专业人员基数大，日常作业和训练任务繁重，但每年立功受奖名额和军士选晋指标却偏少，建议能增加相应指标和名额。了解情况后，这名旅领导觉得解决问题难度不大，就起身回应，主动认领问题并承诺尽快答复。 可问题认领容易，解决起来却并不容易。看起来只是个调整名额比例的问题，实际却要机关多个部门进行调研论证沟通协商，拿出方案后还要报党委会研究。这名领导带相关部门调研了几次，刚想拿个初步方案，实弹射击、专业比武等多项任务就接踵而来，这事便暂时没了下文。 旅党委结合深化政治整训组织的学习教育中，大家对这条官兵留言很是关注。问起前因后果，这位旅领导才红着脸做了解释。 “我一开始想着，既然对基层官兵作出了承诺，就应该及时解释回应，并克服困难履行诺言。可后来手头事一多，就有些顾不过来，潜意识里觉得实弹射击、专业比武是上级紧盯的‘硬任务’，不能有丝毫松懈；而基层诉求是上级不会关注，也没人催的‘软任务’，先放一放没关系，就让相关部门标注‘正在办理中’，然后忙其他工作去了……” “领导干部身后有千军万马，工作干没干、干得怎么样，官兵们一双双眼睛都在盯着，这一举一动都关系着党组织和领导干部的形象威信啊。”了解情况后，党委成员对这名旅领导进行了严肃批评。大家感到，这看似是工作统筹的问题，实则是党员干部主动作为意识不够，对手中权力的公信力分量认识不足。 讨论辨析中，大家把自己摆进去、把职责摆进去，直指履职尽责时的一些共性病灶。比如，有的感觉无人监督，用权任性随意，对官兵诉求想办就办、想拖就拖；有的基层至上意识淡漠，只盯着上级关注的“硬指标”选择性落实，工作挑肥拣瘦；有的没有牢固立起战斗力标准，工作华而不实，对提升练兵备战质效帮助不大，等等。 “咱们要深知，一次次爽约、一次次拖延，消耗的是官兵对党组织的信任，损害的是部队根基。”旅党委进一步明确，领导干部手中的权力，连着官兵信任、关乎练兵备战，岗位越重要、责任越重大，越需要主动接受监督。 按照“清单明责、平台畅言、全程透明”的原则，他们建立完善常态化监督链条，通过“常委牵头、纪检督办、部门联动、落实问效”机制，把监督当作规范用权的紧箍咒、砥砺担当的磨刀石，坚决纠治“表态快、落实慢”等不作为问题，倒逼领导干部工作直面问题、直抵兵心。 会后，这名旅领导立即带领相关部门展开新一轮调研，逐项对照政策法规研究各专业表彰指标设置；结合士兵选退工作实际，进一步修订完善选晋考评细则…… （此组稿件由姚十全整理） 党委回帖： 前期，旅领导认领的“某专业立功受奖名额和军士选晋指标偏少”问题，跟进解决不够及时，在此向大家诚恳道歉并检讨。现在，我们根据相关政策，综合基层和机关意见，初步研究拿出了解决措施，在平台发布并征求意见建议，获得通过后会尽快落实。欢迎大家全程监督、随时提醒。
