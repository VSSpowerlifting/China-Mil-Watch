Micro-Documentary "Thought on Strengthening the Military Nurtures a New Generation" Records the Dream-Chasing Stories of Young Officers and Soldiers in the New Era

微纪录片《强军思想育新人》记录新时代青年官兵的逐梦故事
PLA Daily (解放军报) 14 May 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
The Political Affairs Department of the PLA News and Communication Center released a three-episode micro-documentary, 'Thought on Strengthening the Military Nurtures a New Generation,' timed to May Fourth Youth Day, profiling three named servicemembers: Wu Jingwei, a border defense company commander at Arxan, Inner Mongolia; Wang Tongyan, a First-Class Master Sergeant submariner; and Cui Chong, a post-2000 Y-20 pilot. The piece reports total views surpassing 100 million across major Chinese platforms. This is standard political work content produced for a youth-oriented propaganda cycle; its value is as a record of how PLA institutional media frames Xi Jinping's Thought on Strengthening the Military (习近平强军思想) as the formative force behind individual servicemember development, using personal loyalty narratives and major-platform placement to reach audiences beyond the force itself.
Translation

The political affairs micro-documentary "Thought on Strengthening the Military Nurtures a New Generation" records the dream-chasing stories of young officers and soldiers in the new era — The Power of Thought, the Answer Sheet of Growth

■ PLA Daily Reporters Xie Fei, Zhang Dongpan, Li Chen

Recently, the three-episode political affairs micro-documentary "Thought on Strengthening the Military Nurtures a New Generation," produced by the Political Affairs Department of the PLA News and Communication Center, swept across the entire internet, with major mainstream portal websites and news clients placing it at the top of their homepages.

From the ice-bound, snow-wrapped northern frontier, to the sunless depths of the ocean, to the vast sky where the "Kunpeng" spreads its wings — in the videos, three young officers and soldiers from different units speak in measured tones, recounting their stories of striving toward the light and chasing their dreams under the nourishment of Xi Jinping's Thought on Strengthening the Military (习近平强军思想).

The power of thought works silently, like a gentle rain, yet it always carves deep marks in the growth rings of one's life. Through the growth of these three young officers and soldiers, internet users have seen youth and the era resonating at the same frequency: Chairman Xi's navigation of the effort to strengthen the military has opened a new journey, profoundly reshaping what an army looks like, and shaping from the inside out the thinking and conduct of a generation of young officers and soldiers.

Wind, Snow, and Miles of Road — Blood Still Burning Hot

Arxan, Inner Mongolia: covered in ice and snow for eight months of the year, with minimum temperatures reaching minus 45 degrees Celsius.

Just after the Spring Festival of 2026, Arxan was still draped in silver and white when the production crew of "Thought on Strengthening the Military Nurtures a New Generation" arrived at an Army border defense company garrisoned there.

Twelve years ago, on the eve of the Spring Festival, Chairman Xi braved the bitter cold to inspect this company, warmly visiting and offering words of comfort to the border-defense officers and soldiers, and encouraging everyone to keep their duties firmly in mind, to press forward with continued effort, and to stand their posts, keep their watch, and guard the border well for the motherland.

At that time, one of the main subjects of this micro-documentary — Wu Jingwei, now the company commander — was still a young soldier with a face full of youthful inexperience.

"When we took the group photo, because I was quite young at the time, I kept nervously shrinking back. Chairman Xi gently patted my shoulder twice and said, 'Young man, come closer to me.'" The commander-in-chief's care and expectations inspired Wu Jingwei to take root at the frontier, train hard to build his skills, and grow from a young soldier into a border defense company commander.

How does one use the language of the camera to tell the story of twelve years of transformation? The production crew turned their lenses on that patrol route buried under waist-deep snow. Reporters struggled for half a day carrying their equipment, their toes already numb from the cold. And Wu Jingwei? He walked at the front of the formation, turned back, and flashed a grin — his eyes seeming to shine with a light. Over these years, Wu Jingwei and his comrades have set out on this patrol route more than 1,000 times, covering more than 30,000 kilometers, guarding the boundary markers for the motherland.

That same gleam appeared in the eyes of the documentary's other subjects as well: at a naval port dock, helmsman-signalman technician and First-Class Master Sergeant Wang Tongyan and his comrades stood ready in full gear, preparing to submerge and sail out once more; high above in the blue sky, a post-2000 pilot, Cui Chong, challenged difficult and dangerous training subjects again and again, piloting the "Kunpeng" to soar through the clouds.

"Higher than the mountains are the people; clearer than the water are the hearts of the soldiers." Through them, the reporters used their cameras to capture the most moving qualities of young officers and soldiers: a composure and resolve that far exceeds their years, a patriotic sentiment that shoulders the weight of the mission, and an original aspiration (初心) that, beneath the uniform and through the tempering of time, remains as fierce and burning as ever.

Lightweight Expression, Technology-Enabled

Is it worth waiting a long time for the perfect light and shadow of a single shot?

During the few-minute window of sunrise, a Y-20 slowly rolled out of the hangar, the morning light tracing its silhouette — for that one moment of golden radiance, the production crew waited two days.

An excellent micro-documentary demands that the most refined content be presented through the most extreme camera work. This means every frame must be meticulously crafted, and every detail pursued to the highest standard.

At a time when AI can generate cinematic special-effects shots in dozens of seconds, this series of micro-documentaries — insisting on being "hand-crafted" (手搓) and painstakingly polished — strives to present to audiences the hardships of the combat post (战位) and the growth of officers and soldiers in an authentic way. Although each episode's filming encountered different difficulties — freezing rain at the naval port made the submarine's hull slippery, the damp cold at the garrison caused lenses to fog up, and the cold at the frontier caused drones to "go on strike" — the reporters' hearts beat fervently alongside these young officers and soldiers the entire time, always ready to capture their most moving moments: candid shots of the young pilot's hawk-like gaze, conveying the self-confidence of young officers and soldiers through their resolve; the lens trained on frost-covered cold-weather face masks and densely packed handwritten annotations, letting the images "speak for themselves."

Seize the details, letting lightweight expression achieve great effect with little force (四两拨千斤); technology-enabled, making the visual impact more powerful. This micro-documentary employs multiple visual technology techniques to give the work a sense of the era's texture. In the second episode, "Dive Deep, Grow Upward," a hand-drawn animated boat by a child makes a light transition, transforming into a submarine cleaving through the waves, reflecting how the boat of dreams sets sail in the tide of the era. In the third episode, "'Chong' into the Clouds," a boy's paper airplane transforms into the Y-20 "big aircraft," carrying the dream of the blue sky.

The dream of youth, passing through the clouds, ultimately becomes the "Kunpeng" — this is one person's story of growth, but is it not also the story of a great transformation of an era?

From the Small to the Great, Perceiving Mighty Power

Thought may seem intangible, yet it changes an era. How can the power of thought be made visible? The answer lies not in hollow, rigid slogans, but in authentic and nuanced expression.

After "Thought on Strengthening the Military Nurtures a New Generation" went online, it received widespread attention across the entire internet, with total views rapidly surpassing 100 million. Internet users left comments in large numbers, saying the micro-documentary shows not only the awe inspired by the nation's major weapons systems, but more importantly the most endearing image of new-era soldiers nurtured by Xi Jinping's Thought on Strengthening the Military —

The soldier who once took a group photo with Chairman Xi at the outpost has grown into an outstanding grassroots troop leader, and has in turn trained batch after batch of young officers and soldiers who fear neither hardship nor cold;

A veteran squad leader with 29 years of military service has dived into the ocean again and again, dedicating the best years of his youth to the motherland's submarine cause, and his son has chosen to sit the entrance examination for a naval academy, continuing to guard the motherland's maritime frontier;

The flight cadet representative who reported to Chairman Xi chose, after graduation, to go to a front-line combat unit, becoming a post-2000 Y-20 pilot, piloting the "Kunpeng" on voyages of tens of thousands of miles, flying beyond the nation's borders.

The era shapes youth; youth does not fail the era.

"Their generation had to wait for equipment to arrive, whereas I was able to fly the 'big aircraft' right after graduation." This gratifying growth and inheritance is both a vivid footnote to the mighty power of thought and a qualified answer sheet submitted by the People's Army in the new era.

Where the camera points, there are the real faces of the grassroots; what the images carry is the subtle pulse of the era. This "sense of mission to record the era" (记录时代的使命感) is precisely what drove the production team not to give up in the face of so many difficulties.

The program was released on the occasion of Youth Day on May Fourth. Numerous internet users clicked "like" on their screens: "From the small to the great, it reflects the development of the Chinese military"; "In this era, young officers and soldiers complete a spiritual baptism and an ideological purification, their souls more pure and their conduct more hardened." These heartfelt evaluations have made the reporters deeply understand: only when ideological substance (思想性), artistic quality (艺术性), and technical capability (技术性) are fused together can a truly compelling work be created.

Thought, like a spring breeze bringing gentle rain — wherever there are seeds of youth, a forest will take root and grow. On the new journey of strengthening and revitalizing the military, the cameras of military journalists will continue to be trained on young faces, wholeheartedly telling the stories of their growth as they write the answer sheet of military strengthening with their youthful selves and their striving selves.

时政微纪录片《强军思想育新人》记录新时代青年官兵的逐梦故事—— 思想的力量 成长的答卷 ■解放军报记者 谢 菲 张东盼 李 辰 近日，解放军新闻传播中心时政部摄制的3集时政微纪录片《强军思想育新人》在全网刷屏，各大主流门户网站、新闻客户端纷纷在首页置顶。 从冰封雪裹的北疆，到不见阳光的深海，再到“鲲鹏”振翅的长空，视频中，3位来自不同部队的青年官兵娓娓道来，讲述自己在习近平强军思想哺育下追光逐梦的奋斗故事。 思想的力量润物无声，却总能在成长的年轮里刻下深深印痕。透过3位青年官兵的成长，网友看到了青春与时代的同频共振：习主席领航强军开启新征程，深刻重塑着一支军队的样子，也由内而外塑造着一代青年官兵的思想和作风。 风雪万里 热血滚烫 内蒙古阿尔山，一年8个月被冰雪覆盖，最低气温达零下45摄氏度。 2026年春节刚过，阿尔山还是一片银装素裹，《强军思想育新人》摄制组来到戍守在这里的陆军某边防连。 12年前的春节前夕，习主席冒着严寒视察这个连队，亲切看望慰问戍边官兵，勉励大家牢记职责，再接再厉，为祖国站好岗、放好哨、守好边。 那时，这部微纪录片的主角之一——该连连长吴景伟，还是一名满脸青涩的战士。 “合影时，由于我当时年龄比较小，一直紧张地向后躲。习主席两次轻轻拍着我的肩膀说‘小伙子，靠我近一些’。”统帅的关怀和期许，激励吴景伟扎根边关、苦练本领，从一名年轻战士成长为一名边防连长。 怎样用镜头语言讲述12年的蜕变？摄制组将镜头对准那条积雪齐腰深的巡逻路。记者扛着设备艰难跋涉半日，脚趾已冻得失去知觉。吴景伟呢？他走在队伍最前面，回头朝大家咧嘴一笑，眼神里仿佛闪着光——这些年，吴景伟和战友们1000多次踏上这条巡逻路，行程3万多公里，为祖国守护界碑。 闪烁的光芒，同样出现在纪录片其他主人公的眼中：军港码头，舵信技师、一级军士长王同严和战友们整装待发，准备再次下潜远航；蓝天之上，“00后”飞行员崔翀一次次挑战险难课目，驾驭“鲲鹏”搏击云霄…… “比山更高的是人，比水更清澈的是战士们的心。”从他们身上，记者用镜头捕捉到青年官兵最动人的气质：是远超年龄的沉稳坚毅，是扛起使命的报国情怀，是戎装之下历经岁月洗礼依旧炽热滚烫的不变初心。 轻量表达 技术赋能 为了一个镜头的最佳光影长久等待，值不值得？ 几分钟的日出窗口期，运-20缓缓出库，晨光勾勒出它的轮廓——为了这一瞬的金色光芒，摄制组等了两天。 一部优秀的微纪录片，要求用最极致的镜头展现最精华的内容。这意味着，每一帧画面都要精细打磨，每一个细节都力求精益求精。 当AI可以在几十秒内生成如电影般的特效镜头，这部坚持“手搓”、耗时打磨的系列微纪录片，力图把战位艰辛和官兵成长真实呈现给受众。尽管每集拍摄时都遇到不同的困难——军港的冻雨让艇面打滑，驻地的湿冷让镜头起雾，边关的寒冷让无人机“罢工”——但记者的心始终跟随这群青年官兵热烈跳动，时刻准备捕捉他们最动人的瞬间：抓拍年轻飞行员鹰一样的眼神，坚定中透着青年官兵的自信；镜头对准结满冰霜的防寒面罩、密密麻麻的手写标注，让画面“开口说话”…… 抓住细节，让轻量化表达“四两拨千斤”；技术赋能，让视觉效果更具冲击力。这部微纪录片以多种视觉技术手段赋予作品时代质感。第二集《向下深潜 向上生长》中，儿童手绘的动漫小船轻盈转场，化为劈波斩浪的潜艇，映照梦想之舟在时代浪潮中扬帆远航。第三集《“翀”上云霄》，少年的纸飞机化为运-20“大飞机”，承载蓝天梦想…… 少年之梦，穿过云层，终成“鲲鹏”——这是一个人的成长故事，何尝不是一个时代的巨变？ 以小见大 感悟伟力 思想看似无形，却改变着一个时代。怎样让思想的力量被看见？答案，不在空洞生硬的口号里，而在真实细腻的表达中。 《强军思想育新人》上线后，获得全网广泛关注，总浏览量迅速突破1亿。网友们纷纷留言表示，微纪录片展现的不只是国之重器带来的震撼，更是习近平强军思想哺育下新时代军人最可爱的模样—— 当年在哨所与习主席合影的战士，成长为优秀基层带兵人，又带出一批批不惧苦寒的青年官兵； 从军29年的老班长，一次次深潜大洋，把最好的青春献给祖国的潜艇事业，儿子又选择考入海军院校，继续守卫祖国海疆； 向习主席汇报的飞行学员代表，毕业后选择奔赴一线作战部队，成为“00后”运-20飞行员，驾驭“鲲鹏”远航万里，飞出国门…… 时代造就青年，青年不负时代。 “他们那一代人是人等装备，而我一毕业就能飞上‘大飞机’。”喜人的成长和传承，既是思想伟力的生动注脚，也是人民军队在新时代交出的合格答卷。 镜头所向，是基层真实的面孔；画面所载，是时代细微的脉动。这种“记录时代的使命感”，正是拍摄团队在诸多困难前不放弃的动力。 节目推出之际，恰逢五四青年节。众多网友在屏端点赞：“以小见大，折射出中国军队的发展”“这个时代，青年官兵完成精神上的洗礼、思想上的净化，灵魂更加纯洁、作风更加过硬”……这些发自内心的评价，让记者深知：唯有思想性、艺术性与技术性相融，才能成就深入人心的好作品。
