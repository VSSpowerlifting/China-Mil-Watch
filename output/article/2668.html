Wang Ji: Never a Broken Halberd

About this analysis
Summary
Wang Ji, a researcher at the National University of Defense Technology's School of Computer Science, lost both legs to acute illness at 43, returned to work in a wheelchair at 45, and over the following decade led interdisciplinary teams to complete a military-directed high-assurance software (高可信软件技术) project concluded in 2021 and rated at the international forefront, while also training more than 120 defense-focused graduate students and winning the First Prize of the Military Science and Technology Progress Award at 55. The article documents NUDT's institutional framing of high-assurance software — the security and reliability layer for critical weapons systems — as a strategic priority predating Western 'software-defined warfare' discourse by nearly two decades, with Wang Ji's 2003 paper positioned as the field's domestic origin point. The profile's placement in PLA Daily functions as political-work content, using Wang Ji's biography to model the 'Galaxy Spirit' (银河精神) of self-strengthening sacrifice for junior defense researchers, not as evidence of a new capability or policy line.

Wang Ji: Never a Broken Halberd

■ PLA Daily reporters Wang Tonghua, Wang Tianyi, Guo Fengkuan

On a summer morning, the Xiang River flows northward, and the star city of Changsha is still asleep. The trembling of a hand-cranked wheelchair stirs the night light in a hospital ward.

In the hours that follow, the wheelchair folds into a car, boards a plane, crosses the clouds, and arrives in a distant city — coming to rest at the site of an academic conference, until a voice rings out: "Please welcome Researcher Wang Ji of the School of Computer Science, National University of Defense Technology…"

At that moment, the man who is nearly "standing" on half a body in the wheelchair instantly becomes the focus of the entire room: so this is Wang Ji!

Who is Wang Ji?

In the China Computer Federation, he has served as director and deputy director of two specialized committees. On a national defense 973 Program project, he served as "chief technical expert." In the Great Hall of the People, he was commended as a "National Model of Self-Improvement." On the campus of the National University of Defense Technology, he is "Professor Wang," who has won the hearts of countless students…

An academician of the Chinese Academy of Sciences praised his contributions: "He is one of the principal pioneers of high-assurance software technology (高可信软件技术) in our country."

An airport worker remembered his hurried figure: "He is the unforgettable 'wheelchair scientist' in the crowd!"

A surgeon was moved to tears by his fortitude: "I have never seen anyone suffering such severe illness still insisting on working…"

Who is Wang Ji?

He was admitted to the National University of Defense Technology before the age of 14, was promoted to professor at 33, received funding from the National Science Fund for Distinguished Young Scholars at 38, and won the Second Prize of the National Science and Technology Progress Award at 43…

This is Wang Ji — famous from a young age, his path strewn with flowers.

At 43, he contracted a life-threatening acute illness, narrowly survived, and then lost both legs. He fought through major hemorrhage surgery, flap surgery, and vascular reconstruction surgery, one ordeal after another — and at 51, he was struck by uremia…

This, too, is Wang Ji — having endured great hardship, ceaselessly striving to strengthen himself (自强不息).

At 45, he returned to his post in a wheelchair, presided over the completion of more than ten major projects including National Natural Science Foundation and National Key R&D Plan projects, trained more than 120 master's and doctoral students for national defense research, and at 55 was awarded the First Prize of the Military Science and Technology Progress Award…

This, too, is Wang Ji — ten years facing the wall (面壁十年), forging a shield for the nation.

Wang Ji — this is a name born in the era of "prepare for war, prepare for famine, serve the people." When his parents embedded an ancient weapon into this name, they perhaps did not imagine what profound reverberations it would set off across his life —

He sat for the military academy entrance examination, joined the army, and took up the halberd to guard the state; he devoted himself to high-assurance software research, forging a "secure weapon" for informatized warfare; and in the face of the blows of fate, he remained unyielding throughout — his body confined to the narrow space of a wheelchair, his heart bearing the sharp edge of a long halberd.

"Your name is Wang Ji — you must never let the halberd sink into the sand (折戟沉沙)!" In 2013, after losing both legs, Wang Ji told himself.

From that point on, he took the wheelchair as his war chariot and code as his sword, fighting his way through one barrier after another in the field of high-assurance software, and proved through 13 years of charging forward: Wang Ji — never a broken halberd!

Never a broken halberd, because of the call of mission —

In 2022, a leading Western think tank published a report proposing that in the age of intelligentization (智能化时代), "software defines warfare." The security and reliability of software has become the key factor determining victory or defeat in future wars.

Wang Ji had been paying attention to this issue many years earlier. In 2003, Wang Ji and his doctoral supervisor published the paper "High-Assurance Software Engineering Technology," becoming the first in China to put forward the concept of "high-assurance software technology (高可信软件技术)," laying the foundational bedrock for "software-defined warfare."

He blazed a trail alone into uncharted territory. From that point on, Wang Ji led his team in writing line after line of "high-assurance" code, fitting one "security lock" after another onto the nation's critical weapons systems.

In 2015, a major project was formally initiated — the only high-assurance software project across the entire military directed at the urgent needs of the state and the armed forces.

Who would take the lead as chief technical expert? Wang Ji volunteered: "Let me do it — I will definitely carry it; and if you don't let me lead it, I still want to join the research group."

At that point, only two years had passed since that life-or-death surgery. His body had not yet recovered, yet Wang Ji wheeled himself back to campus, leading more than a dozen interdisciplinary, cross-unit research teams in an all-out assault, fighting selflessly.

One battle lasting six years. In 2021, the project was successfully concluded. The comprehensive evaluation report determined: the overall technical level is at the international forefront, with some comparable indicators leading internationally.

That same year, Wang Ji fell ill again — his kidneys, already damaged by his earlier severe illness, went into failure, and from then on he required dialysis multiple times each week.

Before Wang Ji entered university, his high school teacher had assessed him: "He has ideals and ambition, greatly admires figures like Luo Jianfu, and very much wants to get into a good university and become a scientist in the future."

Luo Jianfu — one of New China's first aerospace microelectronics experts, who, in order to break through foreign technological blockades, continued pressing forward with scientific research in the terminal stage of cancer, until he collapsed at his post.

Half a lifetime later, Wang Ji became a scientist, and on his own mission journey became, in another sense, a "Luo Jianfu."

Never a broken halberd, because of the forging of spirit (精神的熔铸) —

When Wang Ji first started work, he worked in "Galaxy Tower" (银河楼). It was there that China's "Galaxy" (银河) hundred-million-operations-per-second supercomputer was born. His doctoral supervisor, Chen Huowang — overall head of the "Galaxy" software team — became the guide who led him onto the path of serving the nation through science.

After returning to his post following his severe illness, Wang Ji moved into "Tianhe Tower" (天河楼). It was there that "Tianhe-2" (天河二号), which claimed the global supercomputing "six-peat" championship, was born. His wife, Huang Chun — head of the "Tianhe" compiler team — is the comrade-in-arms fighting alongside him.

The campus path from "Galaxy" to "Tianhe" passes a wall bearing the school motto. In the motto inscribed on that wall, Chairman Xi clearly defined the positioning of this military academy: "a high ground for cultivating high-quality new-type military personnel and for independent innovation in national defense science and technology."

Passing the motto wall again and again, walking into "Galaxy" and "Tianhe," Wang Ji feels as though he is advancing within the gaze of the supreme commander's attention, walking within the inheritance of the "Galaxy Spirit" (银河精神).

Some moments in time are destined to be carved into the dual growth rings of life and mission.

When Wang Ji was passing through the critical period in the hospital, the cold Changsha winter had just ended and all things on earth were returning to life. That spring, the Party's goal of strengthening the military in the new era opened a new journey for the People's Army. Wang Ji, who had just lost both legs, felt that everyone around him was charging forward: "I must keep up with the formation!"

By the time Wang Ji's body began to recover, it was already deep autumn. Chairman Xi inspected the National University of Defense Technology and issued the call to "strive to occupy a place in forward-looking and strategic fields."

Carefully absorbing the supreme commander's charge, Wang Ji felt surging within him "an urgent and glorious sense of mission": "There is so much to be done — I truly cannot sit still!"

And so, still in the hospital ward, he began reading papers and holding video conferences; after returning to his post, he raced against time even more — traveling on day trips whenever possible, holding three online meetings during the seven-day Spring Festival holiday, and receiving project briefings every three days during summer vacation…

Witnessing Wang Ji's charging figure year after year, his middle-school-aged daughter planned to write a work titled "The Steps": "Father resolved to build a new house with tall steps… The steps are not merely steps — they also symbolize Father's self-respect and ideals."

At 22, Wang Ji wrote in his application to join the Party: "My growth has been relatively smooth; I have not personally experienced the tempering of hardship and setbacks. I am resolved, under the Party's cultivation and education, to temper my will, and under no circumstances of hardship and difficulty will I ever retreat."

Twenty-two years later, in the face of enormous hardship and setbacks, he remained committed to the cause of high-assurance software, made important contributions to the reliability and security of key software for major national weapons programs, and honored his pledge to the Party with "high-assurance faith (高可信的信仰)."

Never a broken halberd, because of the gathering of strength —

When Wang Ji suffered his grave illness, the two statements made by his mother, Liu Guizhi, moved all who heard them.

The first was during Wang Ji's emergency rescue, when school leaders informed her of his condition. Upon hearing that her son would lose his limbs, the old mother's heart was cut to pieces — her lips bitten purple — yet the first words she spoke were: "We have caused trouble for the organization!"

The second was after Wang Ji returned to his post. Colleagues, seeing him working himself to exhaustion, asked Liu Guizhi to persuade him to slow down. Liu Guizhi shook her head and said: "Wang Ji's second life was given by the Party — it should be dedicated to the Party. Even if he works himself to collapse, that is as it should be!"

How pitiable is the heart of every parent under heaven. Liu Guizhi was not without compassion for her son — she simply carried too much gratitude in her heart.

Liu Guizhi, now 81, has never forgotten that deep night when Wang Ji suddenly fell ill: school leaders rushed to the hospital and personally implored the doctors: "Save him at any cost!"

In the living room of Wang Ji's home, a chart has been posted for 13 years — no one has had the heart to take it down. It is the "rotation schedule" that teachers and students at the school spontaneously drew up to take turns looking after Wang Ji during the more than 100 days he lay in the intensive care unit. The paper has yellowed and curled at the edges, but every name on it remains clearly legible.

Liu Guizhi also treasures a certificate that bears no designated "organizational letterhead." It was issued by the local Bureau of Veterans Affairs, and reads: Wang Ji and his wife are both active-duty military personnel, too busy with work to accompany their elderly mother, and relevant departments are requested to provide assistance. Liu Guizhi has never once used this certificate, yet every time she takes it out she feels an extraordinary warmth.

"My 'rebirth' was never a lone battle," Wang Ji once confided to others. "I am not that strong — on the nights when I could not hold on, it was countless people who lit the light for me."

"Ji" (戟) is a cold weapon that gleams with a cold light. Yet over these years, Wang Ji's face has always worn a warm smile.

That warmth is the gathering and transmission of countless strengths. It gives Wang Ji the courage to level every obstacle in his path, and it also ignites his resolve to repay with gratitude and to charge forward without stopping —

"As long as my mind can still think and my hands can still move, I will not stop."

Wang Ji — never a broken halberd!

王戟：永不折戟 ■解放军报记者 王通化 王天益 郭丰宽 盛夏清晨，湘江北流，星城长沙仍在熟睡。一辆手摇轮椅的震颤，唤醒了医院病房的夜灯。 此后几小时，这辆轮椅钻进汽车、登上飞机、越过云层，然后，抵达远方的城市，停在某学术会议现场，直到一个声音响起：“有请国防科技大学计算机学院王戟研究员……” 那一刻，轮椅上近乎以半个身体“站”着的人，顿时成为全场焦点：原来，他就是王戟！ 王戟是谁？ 在中国计算机学会，他分别担任过两个专委会的主任、副主任；在某国防973项目，他担任“技术首席专家”；在人民大会堂，他被表彰为“全国自强模范”；在国防科技大学校园，他是圈粉无数的“王老师”…… 一位中国科学院院士为他的贡献点赞：“他是我国高可信软件技术领域的主要开创者。” 一位机场工作人员记住了他匆匆的身影：“他是人群中让人难忘的‘轮椅科学家’！” 一位外科医生为他的坚强潸然落泪：“从没见过遭受如此大病痛的人，还在坚持工作……” 王戟是谁？ 不满14岁考上国防科技大学，33岁晋升教授，38岁获国家杰出青年科学基金资助，43岁获得国家科技进步奖二等奖…… 这，是王戟——年少成名，鲜花满径。 43岁患上“九死一生”的急症，幸运闯过了生死线，又不幸失去双腿；大出血手术、皮瓣手术、血管重建手术，一关一关斗罢病魔，51岁时又患上尿毒症…… 这，也是王戟——历经磨难，自强不息。 45岁坐上轮椅重返岗位，主持完成国家自然科学基金、国家重点研发计划等10多项重大项目，为国防科研培养120多名硕士博士，55岁荣获军事科技进步奖一等奖…… 这，还是王戟——面壁十年，为国铸盾。 王戟，这是一个诞生在“备战、备荒、为人民”年代的名字。当初，父母将一件古老的兵器融入这个名字时，或许没有想到将在他的人生激荡出多么深远的回响—— 报考军校、参军入伍，执戟以卫社稷；深耕高可信软件研究，打造信息化战争的“安全利器”；面对命运重击，始终不屈不挠，身囿方寸轮椅，心有锋锐长戟。 “你叫王戟，决不能折戟沉沙呀！”2013年，失去双腿后，王戟告诉自己。 此后，他以轮椅为战车、以代码为利剑，在高可信软件领域一路闯关夺隘，用13年的冲锋证明：王戟，永不折戟！ 永不折戟，因为使命的召唤—— 2022年，西方一家顶级智库发布报告提出，智能化时代“软件定义战争”。软件的安全性、可靠性成为左右未来战争胜负的关键。 早在多年前，王戟就已关注这个问题。2003年，王戟与导师发表论文《高可信软件工程技术》，在我国率先提出“高可信软件技术”概念，为“软件定义战争”打下底层基石。 独向荒蹊筑大道。从此，王戟带着团队敲下一行行“高可信”代码，为国之重器安上一道道“安全锁”。 2015年，某重大项目立项，这是全军唯一面向国家和军队亟需的高可信软件项目。 谁来牵头当技术首席专家？王戟主动请缨：“让我当，我一定会担起来；如果不让我当，我也要加入课题组。” 那时，距离那场生死攸关的手术仅仅两年。身体尚未康复，王戟便摇着轮椅重返校园，带领十几支跨学科、跨单位的科研队伍，全力攻坚，忘我奋战。 一战就是6年。2021年，项目顺利结题。综合评价报告认定：整体技术水平处于国际前列，部分可比指标国际领先。 这一年，王戟再次病倒，曾经因重病受损的肾脏发生衰竭，从此每周要进行多次透析。 上大学前，王戟的高中老师曾评价他：“有理想、有志气，很钦佩罗健夫一样的人物，很想考一个好大学，将来当一名科学家。” 罗健夫，新中国第一批航天微电子专家，为打破国外技术封锁，癌症晚期仍坚持科研攻关不停，直至病倒在岗位。 半生过后，王戟当上科学家，也在自己的使命征途上，成为另一种意义上的“罗健夫”。 永不折戟，因为精神的熔铸—— 刚参加工作时，王戟在“银河楼”上班。那里，诞生了我国“银河”亿次巨型计算机。他的导师陈火旺，“银河”软件团队总负责人，成为他科研报国的引路人。 重病后归队，王戟搬进了“天河楼”。那里，诞生了摘得全球超算“六连冠”的“天河二号”。他的爱人黄春，“天河”编译团队负责人，是他并肩战斗的同路人。 从“银河”到“天河”的校园路，要经过一面训词墙。墙上的训词中，习主席鲜明标定出这所军校的定位：“高素质新型军事人才培养和国防科技自主创新高地”。 一次次经过训词墙，走进“银河”“天河”，王戟感觉像是行进在统帅关注的目光里，行走在“银河精神”的传承里。 有些时间节点，注定将刻进生命和使命的双重年轮。 王戟在医院度过危险期的时候，长沙寒冬刚过，大地万物回春。那个春天，党在新时代的强军目标开启了人民军队新征程。刚刚失去双腿的王戟感到，周围的人都在冲锋，“我必须得跟上队伍”！ 王戟身体开始康复的时候，已是深秋时节。习主席视察国防科技大学，发出“努力在前瞻性、战略性领域占有一席之地”的号召。 悉心领悟统帅嘱托，王戟心中激荡着“一种迫切而光荣的使命感”：“要干的事情太多了，真是坐不住！” 于是，还在病房，他就开始看论文、开视频会；重返岗位后，他更是争分夺秒，出差尽量当天往返，春节7天开了3次线上会议，暑假里每3天听一次项目汇报…… 目睹王戟经年累月的冲锋身影，上中学的女儿打算写一部名为《台阶》的作品：“父亲立志造一座高台阶的新屋子……台阶不仅是台阶，还象征着父亲的自尊和理想。” 22岁时，王戟在入党志愿书里写道：“我的成长过程比较顺利，没有亲身经历艰难和挫折的磨炼。我决心在党的培养和教育下，磨炼意志，无论在何种艰难困苦的环境中，绝不退缩。” 22年后，面对巨大艰难挫折，他矢志高可信软件事业，为国家重大型号工程关键软件的可靠性安全性作出重要贡献，用“高可信的信仰”兑现了对党的承诺。 永不折戟，因为力量的汇聚—— 王戟遭受重大疾病，母亲刘桂枝的两次表态，听者无不动容。 一次是在王戟抢救期间，学校领导向她通报病情。听说儿子截肢，老母亲心如刀绞、嘴唇咬得发紫，说出的第一句话却是：“给组织添麻烦了！” 另一次是在王戟返岗后，同事看他拼命工作，便让刘桂枝劝劝他。刘桂枝摇了摇头说：“王戟的第二次生命是党给的，应该献给党，即使累倒了也是应该的！” 可怜天下父母心。刘桂枝不是不怜惜儿子，而是心里装着太多感恩。 81岁的刘桂枝至今难忘，王戟突发疾病的那个深夜，学校领导匆匆赶到医院，当面恳请医生：“不惜一切代价抢救他！” 在王戟家的客厅里，有一张表格贴了13年，谁也舍不得摘下。那是王戟躺在重症监护室100多天里，学校师生自发前往照看的“轮班表”。纸张已经泛黄卷边，但每一个名字都清晰可见。 刘桂枝还珍藏着一张没有指定“单位抬头”的证明。那是当地退役军人事务局出具的，上面写着：王戟和妻子都是现役军人，工作繁忙无暇陪伴老母亲，请有关部门提供方便。刘桂枝始终没用过这张证明，可每次拿出来都觉得格外温暖。 “我的‘重生’，从来不是孤军奋战。”王戟曾对人坦言，“我没有那么强大，撑不住的黑夜，是无数人为我点亮了光。” “戟”是一件寒光闪闪的冷兵器。这些年，王戟的脸上却总是挂着温暖的笑容。 这温暖，是无数力量的汇聚和传递。它给予王戟踏平坎坷的勇气，也激发他感恩回报、冲锋不止的决心—— “只要脑子还能思考，手还能动，我就不会停下。” 王戟，永不折戟！
