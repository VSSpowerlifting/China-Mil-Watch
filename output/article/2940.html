Forging Indomitable Will, Forever Maintaining the Posture of Charging Forward — The Advanced Deeds of "Model of the Era" Wang Ji Inspire Party Members, Cadres, and Troops to Press Forward with Courage and Resolve

磨砺顽强意志 永葆冲锋姿态——“时代楷模”王戟先进事迹激励广大党员干部和部队官兵勇毅前行
PLA Daily (解放军报) 5 August 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
NUDT College of Computer Science researcher Wang Ji — a wheelchair-bound dialysis patient and leading figure in China's high-assurance software (高可信软件) field — has been designated a 'Model of the Era' (时代楷模), prompting a coordinated study campaign across PLA units including a brigade of the 76th Group Army on the high-altitude plateau, a Central Theater Command Air Force brigade conducting night flight training in North China, and researchers at the Naval Research Institute and Academy of Military Sciences. The article is standard political work content: its value is as a record of how the PLA frames scientific perseverance, loyalty, and combat-oriented research to personnel across warfighting, academic, and defense-industrial roles simultaneously. The explicit linkage of Wang Ji's high-assurance software work to 'independently controllable' defense systems and the 'digital-intelligence sharp blade' (数智利刃) documents how the institution is using the campaign to reinforce messaging around software security and indigenous capability development, though the article provides no evidence of specific programmatic or procurement outcomes.
Translation

A diligent young student who entered the National University of Defense Technology (NUDT) before the age of 14; a military researcher confined to a wheelchair; a vanguard fighter who has endured more than 700 dialysis sessions yet continues to charge at the front lines of national defense scientific research; a principal pioneer in China's field of high-assurance software (高可信软件)… In recent days, the advanced deeds of Wang Ji — "Model of the Era" (时代楷模) and researcher at NUDT's College of Computer Science — have been widely reported, generating an enthusiastic response across military and civilian circles. People have expressed that they will take Wang Ji as a model, remain loyal to the country, strive ceaselessly for self-improvement, press forward with courage and resolve from their posts, and concentrate their efforts on advancing the cause of building a strong nation and a strong military.

"The key to understanding 'why Professor Wang Ji can do it' lies in never forgetting his original aspiration and being politically strong (不忘初心政治强), keeping the nation in his heart and excelling in his professional work (心系家国业务精), fearing no hardship and maintaining strict self-discipline (不畏磨难自律严), and being willing to dedicate himself with deep commitment (甘于奉献情怀深)." Said Li Jun, a professor at the Political Academy of the National Defense University, Wang Ji has set an example for military academy instructors. "Standing at the three-foot lectern, we must sharpen our professional capabilities, put our hearts into the work of forging the soul and nurturing talent (铸魂育人), disregard personal gain or loss, face difficulties head-on, and give our all to cultivating qualified personnel for the cause of strengthening the military."

National defense scientific research demands zero margin for error, and front-line military training likewise tolerates not the slightest negligence. On the snow-covered plateau at an altitude of more than 4,000 meters, officers and soldiers of a brigade under the Army's 76th Group Army used breaks between training sessions to study Wang Ji's advanced deeds. Yang Weigang, the political instructor of the brigade's "All-Around Construction Model Company" (全面建设标兵连), said: "Wang Ji's words — 'As long as my mind still works and my hands can still move, I will absolutely never stop' — inspire us officers and soldiers training at high altitude. Everyone has expressed their determination to learn Wang Ji's never-give-up fighting spirit, to tackle the difficult problems of high-altitude combat operations to the highest standard, and to forge strong combat capabilities."

At an airfield in North China, amid sweltering heat, a brigade of the Central Theater Command Air Force conducted high-intensity day-and-night flight training in an orderly manner. After training concluded, the unit immediately organized officers and soldiers to study Wang Ji's story of struggle. Xia Chunyang, company commander of a company in the brigade, said that Wang Ji, still charging forward despite the torment of illness, has set a glorious example for officers and soldiers throughout the armed forces. We will draw on the strength of this model, fear neither the test of scorching heat nor the challenge of missions, and continuously forge our capacity to execute missions around the clock and in complex environments.

Conduct scientific research oriented toward the battlefield; aim at winning and break through difficult barriers. Gao Weipeng, a researcher at a center under the Naval Research Institute, was deeply moved: "In today's era, scientific and technological innovation has become the main battlefield of strategic competition among major powers, and the road to strengthening the military through science and technology has never been smooth. Wang Ji was willing to sit on the 'cold bench' (坐'冷板凳'), spending more than 30 years rooted at the front lines of high-assurance software research and development, solidifying the national defense software security line, and setting an example for military scientific researchers. We must advance systematic scientific research and tackle key problems based on the needs of strengthening the military and preparing for war, and serve the goal of a strong military and victory in war with hard-core research achievements that are independently controllable."

Yi Zibo, an assistant researcher at the Military Science Information Research Center of the Academy of Military Sciences, stated that scientific research in the information domain often requires "gnawing on hard bones" and "charging through dangerous shoals," and demands above all the strength of will to surpass physical limits and confront challenge after challenge. They will study and carry forward the ever-shining Galaxy Spirit (银河精神), and, like Wang Ji, polish every line of code, optimize every algorithm, and organize every simulation exercise with an attitude of extreme rigor and responsibility, striving to ensure that research results can withstand the test of actual combat, and continuously forging a "digital-intelligence sharp blade" (数智利刃) to defeat the enemy.

Illness erodes his body, yet it cannot crush the unbowed spine of a scientific research soldier. This spirit of ceaseless self-improvement and striving toward the light has moved strivers in every walk of life. The team of Academician Liu Zhihong at the National Clinical Research Center for Kidney Diseases at the Eastern Theater Command General Hospital once spared no effort in treating Wang Ji. Ge Yongchun, deputy chief physician at the center, said: "Under the torment of illness, the spiritual strength of his self-reliance and self-improvement is deeply moving. He inspires us to pursue excellence in every diagnosis and treatment and for every patient." Upon learning that Wang Ji would need continuous blood purification therapy during a recent visit to Nanjing for a conference, the academician's team immediately convened to discuss a treatment plan and tailored multiple dynamically adjustable contingency plans specifically for him.

Central South University organized faculty and students to study Wang Ji's advanced deeds through multiple formats including live broadcasts and documentaries. Huang Kai, deputy director of the university Party committee's student affairs department, said: "Wang Ji is confined to a small wheelchair, yet his heart is always filled with a grand vision of serving the nation, bringing profound inspiration to the work of cultivating virtue and nurturing talent (立德树人) in universities. We will continuously innovate the methods of ideological guidance, lead young students to proactively integrate their personal development into the great cause of building a strong nation and national rejuvenation, and strive to grow into builders of the new era who dare to take on heavy responsibilities and forge ahead with courage and resolve."

At the Air Force Aviation University, newly admitted flight cadets of the class of 2026 listened to Wang Ji's moving story. Flight cadet Shi Haoran said: "We must learn Professor Wang Ji's spirit of 'never breaking the halberd' (永不折戟), keep firmly in mind our original aspiration and mission of flying valiantly for the Party, train hard on the ground and fly with precision in the air, and grow as soon as possible into eagles guarding the blue skies of the motherland."

With the Xiang River as backdrop and lights as tribute, on August 3rd a spectacular themed light show along "One River, Two Banks" (一江两岸) in Changsha was staged in honor of "Model of the Era" and NUDT researcher Wang Ji. The words "Learn from Comrade Wang Ji, Model of the Era" and "A Scientific Research Vanguard Fighter Who Never Stops Charging" blazed across the night sky. Wang Ji's "charge from a wheelchair" (轮椅上的冲锋) is inspiring ever more people — on the new journey of building a strong nation and a strong military, to move forward with gratitude and drive, to strive ceaselessly for self-improvement, and to unite in struggle for the timely realization of the centenary goal of army building (建军一百年奋斗目标), for accelerating the building of the people's armed forces into a world-class military, and for comprehensively advancing the great cause of building a strong nation and national rejuvenation through Chinese-style modernization (中国式现代化).

(Xinhua News Agency, Beijing, August 5th)

Original Chinese
不满14岁考入国防科大的勤学少年、坐着轮椅的军队研究员、历经700多次透析仍然冲锋在国防科研一线的尖兵、我国高可信软件领域的主要开拓者……近日，“时代楷模”、国防科技大学计算机学院研究员王戟的先进事迹一经报道，在军地引发热烈反响。大家纷纷表示，要以王戟为榜样，忠诚报国、自强不息，立足岗位勇毅前行，聚力推进强国强军事业。 “王戟教授‘为什么能’的关键密码，就是不忘初心政治强、心系家国业务精、不畏磨难自律严、甘于奉献情怀深。”国防大学政治学院教授李峻说，王戟为军校教员树立了榜样。“立足三尺讲台，我们要精进专业本领，用心做好铸魂育人工作，不计得失、迎难而上，全力以赴为强军事业培育合格人才。” 国防科研要求万无一失，一线练兵同样容不得半点疏漏。海拔4000多米的雪域高原，陆军第76集团军某旅官兵利用训练间隙学习王戟先进事迹。该旅“全面建设标兵连”指导员杨伟刚说：“王戟那句‘脑子好使、手还能用，就绝不停下’，激励着我们高原驻训官兵。大家表示，要学习王戟永不言弃的拼劲，以极致标准攻克高原作战难题，锤炼过硬战斗本领。” 华北某机场热浪蒸腾，中部战区空军某旅跨昼夜高强度飞行训练有序展开。训练结束后，部队随即组织官兵学习王戟的奋斗故事。该旅某连连长夏春阳说，王戟深受病痛折磨依旧冲锋不止，为全军官兵树立起光辉典范。我们将汲取榜样力量，不惧酷暑考验、不畏任务挑战，持续锤炼全时段、复杂环境下遂行任务能力。 面向战场搞科研，瞄准打赢闯难关。海军研究院某中心科研人员高伟鹏深受触动：“当今时代，科技创新已经成为大国战略博弈主战场，科技强军之路从无坦途。王戟甘坐‘冷板凳’，30多年扎根高可信软件攻关一线，筑牢国防软件安全防线，为军事科研人树立了典范。我们要立足强军备战需求推进体系化科研攻关，以自主可控的硬核科研成果服务强军胜战。” 军事科学院军事科学信息研究中心助理研究员易子博表示，信息领域科研时常要“啃硬骨头”“闯险滩”，尤其要有超越身体极限、直面重重挑战的意志品质。他们将学习传承闪耀不息的银河精神，像王戟一样以极端严谨负责的态度打磨每一行代码、优化每一套算法、组织每一次模拟推演，力求科研成果经得起实战检验，持续锻造克敌制胜的“数智利刃”。 病痛侵蚀着他的身体，却压不垮一名科研战士不屈的脊梁。这份自强不息、向阳奋进的精神，打动着各行各业的奋斗者。东部战区总医院国家肾脏疾病临床医学研究中心刘志红院士团队曾全力救治王戟。中心副主任医师葛永纯说：“病痛折磨下，他自立自强的精神力量令人动容。他激励我们，对待每一次诊疗、每一名患者都要追求极致。”获悉王戟近日赴南京参会需连续性血液净化治疗后，院士团队第一时间研讨诊疗方案，为其量身制定多套动态调整预案。 中南大学组织师生通过观看直播、纪实片等多种形式，学习王戟先进事迹。校党委学生工作部副部长黄凯说：“王戟身囿方寸轮椅，胸中常怀报国宏图，为高校立德树人工作带来深刻启示。我们将不断创新思想引领方式，引导青年学子主动将个人发展融入强国建设、民族复兴伟业，努力成长为敢挑重担、勇毅拼搏的新时代建设者。” 在空军航空大学，2026年新录取飞行学员聆听了王戟的感人故事。飞行学员史浩冉说：“我们要学习王戟教授‘永不折戟’的精神，牢记为党奋飞的初心使命，地面苦练、空中精飞，早日成长为守护祖国蓝天的雄鹰。” 湘江为幕，灯火为敬。8月3日，长沙“一江两岸”主题灯光秀璀璨上演，致敬“时代楷模”、国防科技大学研究员王戟。“向时代楷模王戟同志学习”“冲锋不止的科研尖兵”等字样闪耀夜空。王戟“轮椅上的冲锋”正激励着更多人——在强国强军新征程上，感恩奋进、自强不息，为如期实现建军一百年奋斗目标、加快把人民军队建成世界一流军队，为以中国式现代化全面推进强国建设、民族复兴伟业而团结奋斗。 （新华社北京8月5日电）
