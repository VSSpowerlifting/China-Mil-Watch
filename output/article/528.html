Chengdu, Sichuan Province Strengthens Military Facilities Protection by Leveraging Government Services Platform

四川省成都市依托政务服务平台加强军事设施保护
PLA Daily (解放军报) 21 May 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
Since early 2025, Chengdu's National Defense Mobilization Office (国动办) and the Garrison Command's Mobilization Department have jointly operated a Military Facilities Protection Consultation and Review System that has processed more than 1,500 construction project applications through the city's existing government services platform, averaging 2.7 working days per review across city and district levels. The article documents how a structural change — the two organs forming a joint office under the Chengdu National Defense Mobilization Committee (国动委) during national defense mobilization adjustment and reform (国防动员调整改革) — created the administrative precondition for routing military facilities protection reviews through civilian bureaucratic infrastructure rather than ad hoc garrison coordination. The framing points to a broader effort to regularize military-civilian coordination on land use by embedding it in standardized government workflows, though the article does not specify which garrison units or military facilities management units are involved or how objection rates have changed.

Chengdu, Sichuan Province Strengthens Military Facilities Protection by Leveraging Government Services Platform — Online Project Submission, Joint Military-Civilian Review

■ Wang Leilei, Yang Shiqiao

[Diagram: Operational diagram of Chengdu, Sichuan Province's Military Facilities Protection Consultation and Review System]

"In the past, submitting a construction project application sometimes meant making many trips just to get a single stamp. Now information travels online — it saves both worry and effort." Recently, the head of a land-use entity in Wuhou District, Chengdu, Sichuan Province, was in high spirits after a project was smoothly approved. Like that entity, when planning construction projects that may affect military facilities, multiple units in Chengdu need only submit project applications online, without having to coordinate face-to-face with approving authorities, and can receive feedback within a short period of time.

It is reported that since early 2025, the city has used its Military Facilities Protection Consultation and Review System to complete reviews of more than 1,500 construction projects that may affect military facilities, with an average processing time of 2.7 working days per project — a substantial improvement in approval efficiency.

"Two years ago, things looked nothing like this." The head of the National Defense Mobilization Office (国防动员办公室) of Chengdu's Xinjin District noted that previously, when planning construction projects that might affect military facilities, land-use entities had to coordinate on their own with garrison troops, military organs, and other units. The project submission and approval process was irregular and inefficient, consuming considerable effort.

"By leveraging the government services platform, military facilities protection work has been brought onto a standardized track (规范化轨道)." The city's National Defense Mobilization Office (国动办) and the Garrison Command's Mobilization Department reached a consensus through research: seizing the opportunity presented by the upgrade of the city's engineering project review platform, they would establish a Military Facilities Protection Consultation and Review System covering both the city and district levels. Within the framework of that platform, the goal was to achieve online project submission, preliminary review by relevant departments, submission of specific opinions by military facilities management units, and unified feedback of opinions by the system. In the course of national defense mobilization adjustment and reform (国防动员调整改革), the city's National Defense Mobilization Office and the Garrison Command's Mobilization Department formed a joint office under the city's National Defense Mobilization Committee (国动委), jointly assuming responsibilities for national defense mobilization planning and construction, comprehensive coordination, and related duties. This change created favorable conditions for leveraging the government services platform to standardize military facilities protection work.

"While submitting the project application to the District Planning and Natural Resources Bureau, you send the project application form over the internet to the National Defense Mobilization Office project approval desk at the District Government Services Center — no need to make a separate trip." The head of a land-use entity in Chengdu's Jinniu District remarked with deep feeling that submitting project applications, which a few years ago required running around to multiple offline offices, has now become a single online submission — far more convenient and efficient.

After the National Defense Mobilization Office project approval desk personnel at the district level conduct a reasonableness review of the project application, they forward it via a dedicated account to the Military Affairs Section (军事科) of the District People's Armed Forces Department (人武部). The District National Defense Mobilization Office and the District People's Armed Forces Department Military Affairs Section serve as the director unit and deputy director unit, respectively, of the District National Defense Mobilization Committee Joint Office; regular joint meetings and joint handling of situations by personnel from both units have become standard practice.

Personnel from the District People's Armed Forces Department Military Affairs Section display information about the construction project — including longitude and latitude coordinates and building dimensions (length, width, and height) — on a map, and through comparison determine whether the project falls within the scope of military facilities protection. If there are objections, the relevant information is compiled, printed, and delivered in person to the military facilities management unit to solicit its opinion.

The military facilities management unit, in accordance with legal provisions, organizes personnel to conduct an assessment, determining whether the project upon completion would affect the safety and operational effectiveness (使用效能) of military facilities, and provides timely feedback to the District People's Armed Forces Department Military Affairs Section based on the assessment results. Based on the feedback from the District People's Armed Forces Department Military Affairs Section, the District National Defense Mobilization Office informs the land-use entity of the review result for the project; if approval is not granted, careful explanatory work is carried out.

The operation of the Military Facilities Protection Consultation and Review System depends on close cooperation between the city- and district-level National Defense Mobilization Offices and the two levels of military organs. The former are primarily responsible for interfacing with government departments and project-submitting entities, completing tasks such as application acceptance, registration and confirmation, and delivery of opinions; the latter are primarily responsible for interfacing with local military facilities management units, completing tasks such as project verification, written consultation for opinions, and feedback of opinions — achieving both clear delineation of responsibilities and close coordination with seamless integration (无缝衔接).

The review of projects submitted by land-use entities is conducted in accordance with laws and regulations including the Law on the Protection of Military Facilities (军事设施保护法). The Chengdu National Defense Mobilization Committee Joint Office guides military and civilian staff to accurately grasp the content of legal provisions and to insist on lawful review and conduct in accordance with regulations (依法审查、按章办事), through organizing thematic study sessions, using meetings as training opportunities, and conducting targeted education.

"The local government services platform has provided favorable operating conditions for the review system, and has also set high standards for our work." A relevant official from the Chengdu National Defense Mobilization Office stated that in the next phase, they will conduct in-depth analysis of typical cases arising from their work, promote successful experience and practices, and address shortcomings, so that the Military Facilities Protection Consultation and Review System operates more smoothly and efficiently, and so as to further mobilize the enthusiasm and initiative (积极性主动性) of the general public in protecting military facilities.

**Background Information**

**Relevant Provisions of the Law on the Protection of Military Facilities**

When local people's governments at or above the county level formulate national economic and social development plans or arrange construction projects that may affect military facilities protection, and when relevant departments of the State Council and local people's governments formulate territorial spatial plans (国土空间规划) and other plans, they shall give consideration to the needs of military facilities protection and shall solicit written opinions from the relevant military organs in accordance with regulations. Where necessary, the local people's government may, together with relevant departments and relevant military organs, conduct an assessment of the construction project.

When relevant departments of the State Council or relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level approve the construction projects specified in the preceding paragraph, they shall review whether opinions have been solicited from military organs in accordance with regulations; where opinions have not been solicited from military organs as required, they shall require that opinions be supplementarily solicited; where changes to the content of a construction project occurring during the approval process may affect military facilities protection, opinions from the relevant military organs shall be solicited again.

The relevant military organs shall provide written reply opinions within thirty days of receiving the written consultation; where it is necessary to request instructions from a higher military organ or where survey, measurement, or testing is required, the reply period may be appropriately extended, but shall generally not exceed ninety days.

四川省成都市依托政务服务平台加强军事设施保护—— 项目网上申报 军地联手把关 ■王雷雷 杨世乔 四川省成都市军事设施保护意见征求审查系统运行图 “过去申报建设项目，有时为了盖一个章要跑好多趟，现在是信息在网上跑，省心又省力。”近段时间，四川省成都市武侯区某用地单位负责人因项目顺利获得批准而心情舒畅。像这家单位一样，在规划可能影响军事设施的建设项目时，成都市多家单位只需在网上提交项目申报书，无需自行与审批单位面对面协调，便可在短时间内得到反馈信息。 据悉，自2025年初以来，该市运用军事设施保护意见征求审查系统，完成1500多个可能影响军事设施的建设项目审查，平均每个项目办理时间为2.7个工作日，审批效率大幅提高。 “两年前还不是这番景象。”成都市新津区国防动员办公室负责人表示，以前规划可能影响军事设施的建设项目时，用地单位需要自行与驻军部队、军事机关等单位对接，项目申报和审批流程不规范、效率低，牵涉了不少精力。 “依托政务服务平台，将军事设施保护工作纳入规范化轨道。”该市国动办与警备区动员处在调研中取得共识：以市工程项目审查平台升级为契机，建立覆盖市、区两级的军事设施保护意见征求审查系统，在该平台框架下，做到项目网上申报、有关部门初步把关、军事设施管理单位提出具体意见、系统统一反馈意见。在国防动员调整改革中，该市国动办与警备区动员处组成市国动委联合办，共同担负国防动员规划建设、综合协调等职责。这一变化为依托政务服务平台、规范军事设施保护工作创造了良好条件。 “在向区规划和自然资源局申报项目的同时，将项目申报书从互联网上发给区政务服务中心国动办项目审批席位人员，不需要再专门跑一趟。”成都市金牛区一家用地单位负责人深有感触地说，现在申报项目，已由前几年的线下多头跑变为线上一次申报，方便快捷多了。 区国动办项目审批席位人员对项目申报书进行合理性审查后，通过专门账号，将其转发给区人武部军事科人员。区国动办与区人武部军事科，分别是区国动委联合办的主任单位、副主任单位，两家单位人员定期参加联席会议、共同处置情况已成为工作常态。 区人武部军事科人员将建设项目的经纬度坐标、建筑长宽高等信息显示在地图上，通过比对，确定该项目是否处于军事设施保护范围之内。如有异议，则将相关信息整理打印，当面交付军事设施管理单位，征求其意见。 军事设施管理单位依据法律规定，组织力量进行评估，判断该项目建成后会不会影响军事设施安全和使用效能，并根据判断结果，及时向区人武部军事科反馈意见。根据区人武部军事科反馈的意见，区国动办告知用地单位对该项目的审查结果，如果未获批准，则认真做好解释说明工作。 军事设施保护意见征求审查系统的运行，有赖于市、区两级国动办与两级军事机关的通力合作。前者主要负责对接政府部门、项目申报单位，完成申报受理、登记确认、意见送达等工作；后者主要负责对接属地军事设施管理单位，完成项目核查、函询意见、反馈意见等工作，做到了既职责界限清晰，又密切协同、无缝衔接。 对用地单位申报项目的审查，依据的是军事设施保护法等法律法规。成都市国动委联合办通过组织专题学习、以会代训、开展针对性教育等方式，引导军地工作人员准确把握法律规定内容，坚持依法审查、按章办事。 “地方政务服务平台为审查系统提供了良好的运行条件，也对我们的工作标准提出很高的要求。”成都市国动办相关负责人表示，下一步，他们将对工作中的典型案例深入剖析，推广经验做法、改进工作不足，让军事设施保护意见征求审查系统运行更顺畅、更高效，进一步调动起广大群众保护军事设施的积极性主动性。 背景资料 军事设施保护法有关规定 县级以上地方人民政府编制国民经济和社会发展规划、安排可能影响军事设施保护的建设项目，国务院有关部门、地方人民政府编制国土空间规划等规划，应当兼顾军事设施保护的需要，并按照规定书面征求有关军事机关的意见。必要时，可以由地方人民政府会同有关部门、有关军事机关对建设项目进行评估。 国务院有关部门或者县级以上地方人民政府有关部门审批前款规定的建设项目，应当审查征求军事机关意见的情况；对未按规定征求军事机关意见的，应当要求补充征求意见；建设项目内容在审批过程中发生的改变可能影响军事设施保护的，应当再次征求有关军事机关的意见。 有关军事机关应当自收到征求意见函之日起三十日内提出书面答复意见；需要请示上级军事机关或者需要勘察、测量、测试的，答复时间可以适当延长，但通常不得超过九十日。 制图：扈 硕
