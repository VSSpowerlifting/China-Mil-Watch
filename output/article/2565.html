Exercises Doctrine

From Substitute Sparring Partner to All-Army Champion, from Joint Logistics Soldier to Battlefield Warrior

从替补陪练逆袭为全军冠军，从联勤兵成长为赛场勇士
PLA Daily (解放军报) 25 July 2026
View original source ↗
About this analysis
Summary
Staff Sergeant First Class Li Jinshan, assigned to a base under the Shenyang Joint Logistics Support Center, won the guard unit group (警卫组) paired bayonet championship at the 2025 All-Army Special Bayonet Competition held in northern Jiangxi — the first such all-army competition in fifty years — after advancing from a supplementary sparring-partner slot during the Joint Logistics Support Forces' centralized training team selection. The article documents the PLA's deliberate effort to rehabilitate bayonet fighting as a vehicle for cultivating combat spirit (战斗精神) after the subject was restored as an elective in the 2018 Military Physical Training Outline, and the framing of a logistics soldier as a 'combatant' points to an institutional push to close the perceived gap between support and combat identity within the joint logistics system.
Translation

Tempering the Blade (淬刃)

■ Dai Xiangyu; Zhao Jiaqing, special correspondent for PLA Daily

A base under the Shenyang Joint Logistics Support Center conducts bayonet combat training. Photo by Luo Hengxin

Li Jinshan demonstrates bayonet techniques for fellow soldiers. Photo by Luo Hengxin

In wartime, crossing bayonets with the enemy in close combat is a testament to a soldier's courage and fighting spirit; in peacetime, facing a powerful adversary and daring to draw the sword is equally a display of a soldier's sincerity and boldness. No matter how the form of warfare changes, the "bayonet spirit (刺刀精神)" will never fade.

In 2018, bayonet fighting was listed as an elective training subject in the Military Physical Training Outline (《军事体育训练大纲》), formally returning to the military physical training system of our armed forces. Today, bayonet fighting is undoubtedly an effective means of tempering officers and soldiers' will to fight with bravery and tenacity, and of cultivating the combat spirit (战斗精神).

Li Jinshan, a Staff Sergeant First Class at a base under the Shenyang Joint Logistics Support Center, has loved listening since childhood to his grandfather—a Korean War veteran—recount stories of life-and-death struggles with the enemy on the battlefield. He firmly believes that the fearless courage of a soldier to meet the enemy head-on and dare to draw the sword, and the decisive spirit to face life and death with bayonets drawn, must be forged and passed down through actual combat. He has devoted himself to studying bayonet techniques, honing his skills through day-after-day training.

In 2025, the All-Army Special Bayonet Competition reopened its contest after a fifty-year hiatus. In an instant, the finest troops from across the entire army gathered, with the strong competing against the strong. Drawing on the foundation built through years of hard training, Li Jinshan was selected for the Joint Logistics Support Forces' centralized training team, where he met Zhang Jinming—a legendary figure in our military's bayonet fighting history—who had come to impart techniques and answer questions.

In 1975, at the bayonet competition of the Third All-Army Sports Games, Zhang Jinming won the All-Army individual paired-bayonet (对刺) championship. With a wooden rifle, a battle cry, and a decisive strike, his swift bayonet techniques and bold fighting style deeply influenced a generation of officers and soldiers.

On the training ground, as the centralized training team members were conducting paired-bayonet drills, Li Jinshan's sharp thrusts, calm adaptability, and imposing fighting edge made Zhang Jinming dimly see his younger self. At the moment of parting, Zhang Jinming encouraged Li Jinshan to train hard and strive to pass the selection, achieving good results in the All-Army Special Bayonet Competition.

Having received the veteran's recognition, Li Jinshan's resolve to pursue his dream grew ever stronger. In the training team's first round of selection for ten starting positions, fellow soldiers from his unit were eliminated one after another, while he placed thirteenth. Thanks to supplementary sparring-partner slots that were added, he obtained the opportunity to remain with the team and continue training.

In bayonet combat, the premium is on speed, ferocity, agility, and precision. Zhang Jinming shared with everyone during his instruction: "Of all martial arts, only speed is undefeatable (天下武功，唯快不破). Back on the competition floor, even when I exposed my attack direction, opponents still could not cope—that was entirely due to speed and instantaneous explosive power." Hearing these words, Li Jinshan calculated inwardly: the training team was not short of specialists in boxing and fencing, and his only slight advantage was strength, while his rifle-drawing speed and on-the-spot reactions were not outstanding. To achieve a breakthrough, he would have to improve his speed and instantaneous explosive power.

Refusing to lag behind, Li Jinshan resolved to shed the sparring-partner label, compete for a starting position, and fight on the All-Army competition floor. During the day, he wrapped resistance bands around himself, hung water bottles, and strapped on sandbags to refine his bayonet movements, never stopping his daily sprinting and thrusting drills; deep into the night, he added extra work on his own, using barbell bars and elevated platforms to strengthen his power training.

As the centralized training continued, the intense summer heat of northern Jiangxi was also a test for Li Jinshan, accustomed as he was to the cool climate of the northeast. To adapt to the environment, he repeatedly conducted bayonet training in the blazing sun wearing his combat uniform, his clothes soaked through with sweat all day long, refusing to give up even when exhausted to the point of collapse. Diligence is rewarded: after nearly two months of grueling training, he reduced his body fat percentage from 15% to 11%, bench-pressed 125 kilograms, broke 2.85 meters in the standing broad jump, and his overall physical fitness steadily ranked among the top of the training team members.

On a summer night, a gentle breeze brushed the treetops, and moonlight filtered through the gaps in the leaves, casting dappled shadows. In this quiet moment, a wondrous transformation of life breaking free from its chrysalis was unfolding in silence—a cicada shedding its shell (金蝉脱壳). Standing beneath the tree, Li Jinshan witnessed this scene and could not help but reflect: tempering the blade (淬刃) is precisely this—tearing apart the old self through a thousand hammerings and a hundred refinements, and being reborn from the ashes!

Bayonet fighting is not only a contest of strength but even more a contest of wits. Li Jinshan understood clearly that brute force alone is insufficient for victory, and that strategy and technique are especially critical. He repeatedly reviewed the instructional videos Zhang Jinming had left for the training team, making the signature "feint-thrust" technique ("骗刺") of luring the enemy, building momentum, and launching an instantaneous thrust the focus of his study.

During competition preparation, Li Jinshan and his fellow soldier Neng Mingchang formed a training partnership. At first, Li Jinshan's fighting style was straightforward and one-dimensional; the coaching staff pointed out that his physical fitness was outstanding but his tactics were lacking. Neng Mingchang could often execute unexpected tactical moves, but his strength and speed were slightly weaker. The two targeted their respective weaknesses for improvement, devoting themselves to studying fencing tactics and the offensive-defensive essentials of traditional martial arts, breaking down the footwork angles of top competitors, correcting their movements in front of mirrors, reviewing video recordings after sparring matches, and seeking out coaches late at night to identify shortcomings. Through studying the interplay of movement and stillness, opening and closing, hardness and softness (动静开合、刚柔并济), they filled in their weak points and refined their techniques.

In August, the training team's second round of selection began. It was conducted in a round-robin format with a best-of-three-bouts system; striking the opponent's chest, abdomen, or shoulder scored a point. Li Jinshan fought steadily and performed well. In the final bout, his opponent was his most familiar partner—Neng Mingchang. In routine training, Li Jinshan had been at a disadvantage against him on multiple occasions.

The whistle blew, battle cries shook the air, and the two sides fought to a 2-2 tie. At the critical moment, Li Jinshan fixed his gaze on his opponent's rhythm, stepped forward with a feint thrust to the center to draw him into a defensive crouch, and the moment Neng Mingchang exposed an opening, he swiftly sidestepped and dodged, used his rifle butt to deflect the wooden rifle, concentrated his force, and drove a powerful thrust home—one decisive strike for victory, sending Neng Mingchang stumbling backward to the ground.

"The speed was too fast—there was absolutely no time to react!" Neng Mingchang exclaimed after the bout. The coaching staff's post-match analysis found that Li Jinshan's rifle strike was lightning-fast, and his footwork incorporated the martial arts "empty stance" (虚步), making the interplay of offense and defense, feint and reality, impossible to read.

Thirteen wins and two losses—Li Jinshan won the championship in this selection competition and secured the coveted entry ticket to the competition as he had wished.

Over the three months of centralized training, Li Jinshan reversed course from substitute sparring partner to first place in the selection competition, locking in his deployment slot. Looking back on the road traveled, his eyes filled with tears. All those days of perseverance and tempering had finally been rewarded.

On the competition grounds in northern Jiangxi, the All-Army Special Bayonet Competition officially commenced, with top competitors gathering to contend. As a joint logistics soldier (联勤兵) whose primary duty is support, Li Jinshan was not initially regarded as a favorite. Before the competition, the coaches also told him to keep a level mindset and strive to demonstrate his fighting spirit. Li Jinshan, however, was full of confidence in himself: though combat positions have their division of labor, a soldier's fighting spirit (军人血性) knows no distinction by post—once on the competition floor, joint logistics soldiers must also strive for outstanding results.

The paired event was held first. Li Jinshan and Neng Mingchang coordinated with tacit understanding, successfully advancing out of the group stage. After a brief rest, Li Jinshan also competed in the individual event, taking first place in his group to advance.

Back-to-back competitions caused Li Jinshan's wrist to become uncomfortable. Weighing the situation, the coaches advised him to withdraw from the individual event and concentrate on protecting his chances in the paired event, where his prospects were better. He flatly refused: "As long as I can still stand on the competition floor, I will absolutely never withdraw of my own accord." In the first round of the individual event elimination bracket, Li Jinshan, fighting through injury, narrowly lost to an opponent from the Beijing Garrison Command and was eliminated just short of the top sixteen.

In the paired event, he and his teammate fought through round after round. In the semifinal, they won a hard-fought victory, though his wrist was struck hard again. Their opponents in the final were formidable—a powerful combination of the individual event champion and the individual event bronze medalist.

Before the bout, he and Neng Mingchang used every available moment to review video recordings and analyze tactics, settling on a strategy of pinning down and harassing the opponents while concentrating force for key strikes, determined to give everything in one all-out effort.

The final got underway. Neng Mingchang engaged in agile close-in fighting to disrupt the opponents, while Li Jinshan repeatedly launched his rifle, seizing the initiative. The opponents quickly counterattacked, and the score rose alternately until it reached 2-2. In the heat of the battle, an opponent's rifle tip accidentally struck Li Jinshan in the neck, resulting in a foul being called. With new injuries compounding old ones and bouts of dizziness striking intermittently, Li Jinshan abandoned his conservative tactics, pressed forward with all his strength, and simultaneously with his opponent completed a valid scoring strike.

Under the rules, when valid scoring strikes are equal, the side with fewer fouls wins. Throughout the entire final, Li Jinshan and Neng Mingchang committed zero fouls, and relying on their composed fighting style and skilled technique, they took the championship in the guard unit group (警卫组) paired bayonet event! Applause rang out across the competition grounds; Li Jinshan and his teammate embraced warmly and celebrated their victory with abandon.

From substitute sparring partner to All-Army champion, from joint logistics soldier to battlefield warrior, Li Jinshan has proven through sweat and perseverance: a joint logistics soldier is also a combat soldier, and a support member is also a combatant. The wooden rifle, whose torch has been passed down across half a century, remains as sharp as ever in the hands of officers and soldiers of the new era.

淬 刃 ■戴翔宇 解放军报特约记者 赵佳庆 沈阳联勤保障中心某基地开展刺杀对抗训练。罗恒鑫摄 李金山为战友演示刺杀动作。罗恒鑫摄 战争年代，与敌人刺刀见红，是军人勇气和血性的见证；和平年代，直面强敌勇于亮剑，亦是军人赤诚与胆魄的彰显。不管战争形式如何变化，“刺刀精神”永不褪色。 2018年，刺杀被《军事体育训练大纲》列为选训内容，正式回归我军军事体育训练体系。在当下，刺杀无疑是磨砺官兵英勇顽强的意志品质、培育战斗精神的一种有效途径。 沈阳联勤保障中心某基地一级上士李金山，从小就喜欢听参加过抗美援朝战争的姥爷讲战场上与敌人殊死搏斗的故事，他坚信军人狭路相逢、敢于亮剑的无畏胆气，直面生死、刺刀见红的决胜气魄，要在实战中淬炼传承。他潜心钻研刺杀技术，在日复一日的训练中打磨技能。 2025年，全军专项刺杀比武时隔50年重揭战幕。一时间，全军精锐齐聚、强者云集。凭借多年来刻苦训练打下的基础，李金山入选联勤保障部队集训队，并在这里见到了前来授技解惑的我军刺杀传奇人物张金明。 1975年，第三届全军体育运动会刺杀比赛中，张金明摘得全军对刺个人冠军。一杆木枪、一声呐喊、一次决胜，他迅猛的刺杀技法、果敢的拼杀作风，深深影响着一代官兵。 训练场内，集训队员正在进行对刺训练，李金山凌厉的突刺、冷静的应变、逼人的锐气，让张金明依稀看见年轻时的自己。临别之际，张金明勉励李金山好好训练，力争通过选拔，在全军专项刺杀比武中取得好成绩。 得到前辈认可，李金山的逐梦之志愈发坚定。集训队首轮选拔10个正选席位，同单位战友纷纷止步，他位列第13名。得益于增补的陪练名额，他获得了留队继续训练的机会。 刺杀博弈，贵在快、狠、敏、准。张金明在指导时和大家分享道：“天下武功，唯快不破。当年在赛场上，即便我暴露刺杀方位，对手仍难以招架，靠的就是速度与瞬时爆发力。”听着这话，李金山在心里盘算着，集训队里不乏拳击、击剑专业尖子，自己唯有力量稍占上风，出枪速度、临场反应均不突出。想要取得突破，就要提高自己的速度与瞬时爆发力。 不甘人后，李金山立志撕掉陪练标签，冲击正选资格、征战全军赛场。白天，他缠阻力带、吊水瓶、绑沙袋打磨刺杀动作，每日奔袭、突刺训练从不间断；夜深人静，他独自加码，用杠铃杆、高台等强化力量训练。 随着集训的持续进行，赣北的酷暑对于习惯了东北凉爽气候的李金山来说也是一个考验。为了适应环境，他身着作训服在烈日下反复进行刺杀训练，衣衫终日汗透，练到精疲力尽仍不放弃。天道酬勤，经过近两个月的苦练，他将体脂率从15%降至11%，卧推125公斤，立定跳远突破2米85，综合体能素质稳居集训队员前列。 夏夜，微风拂过树梢，月光透过叶隙洒下斑驳光影。在这静谧的时刻，一场生命破茧新生的奇妙蜕变正在悄然上演——金蝉脱壳。伫立树下的李金山目睹这一幕，不禁感悟：淬刃，便是在千锤百炼中撕裂旧我、涅槃重生！ 刺杀不仅是力的比拼，更是智的较量。李金山深知，蛮力不足以制胜，谋略技法尤为关键。他反复研看张金明留给集训队的教学视频，将那套诱敌造势、瞬间起刺的“骗刺”绝招列为自己学习的重点。 备赛时，李金山与战友能明昌组成训练搭档。起初，李金山打法刚直单一，被教练指出体能突出但战术欠缺；能明昌经常能打出出其不意的战术，但力量和速度稍弱。二人针对性补强，潜心钻研击剑战术与传统武术攻防精髓，拆解高手步法角度，对镜规范动作，对抗赛后录像复盘，深夜请教练查摆短板，在动静开合、刚柔并济的研习中补齐弱项、精进技法。 8月，集训队第二轮选拔开启。采取车轮对战、三局两胜制进行，击中对手胸腹肩即可得分。李金山稳扎稳打、战绩不俗。收官一战，对手是他最为熟悉的搭档——能明昌。在平时训练中，李金山多次在对阵中处于劣势。 哨声吹响，杀声震天，双方激战至2比2平。紧要关头，李金山紧盯对手节奏，跨步佯刺中路诱其俯身防御，待能明昌露出空隙，他迅速侧步闪身，枪托拨开木枪，聚力迅猛突刺，一击制胜，将能明昌震退倒地。 “速度太快，完全来不及反应！”赛后，能明昌赞叹。教练组复盘研判，李金山出枪迅疾，步法融汇武术虚步，攻防虚实难料。 13胜2负，这次选拔赛李金山勇夺冠军，如愿拿到了比武的入场券。 集训的3个月里，李金山从替补的陪练队员逆袭为选拔赛头名,锁定出征席位。回首来路，他热泪盈眶。这么多天的坚守磨砺，终有回报。 赣北赛场，全军专项刺杀比武正式打响，高手齐聚争锋。作为一名以保障为主业的联勤兵，起初李金山并不被看好。赛前教练也让他放平心态，力求展现作风。李金山却对自己充满信心：战位虽有分工，军人血性却不分岗位，踏上赛场，联勤兵也要力争佳绩。 双人赛率先进行，李金山与能明昌配合默契，成功从小组赛中突围。短暂休息后，李金山又在个人赛中出战，拿下小组头名晋级。 接二连三的比赛，让李金山的手腕出现不适。权衡之下，教练劝他放弃个人赛，力保机会更大的双人项目。他断然拒绝：“只要还能站上赛场，我就绝不主动放弃。”个人赛淘汰赛第一轮，带伤征战的李金山惜败来自北京卫戍区对手，遗憾止步16强。 双人赛，他和队友一路闯关。半决赛中，他们艰难赢得胜利，不过他的手腕再次受到重击。决赛的对手十分强大——个人赛冠军和季军的强劲组合。 赛前，他与能明昌抓紧时间复盘录像、研判战术，确定了牵制袭扰、重点突击的策略，决心全力一搏。 决赛拉开战幕，能明昌灵活缠斗干扰对手，李金山频频出枪，抢占先机。对手迅速反扑，比分交替上升战至2比2平。激战间，对手枪尖误伤李金山颈部，被判犯规。新伤旧痛叠加、眩晕不时袭来，李金山放下保守战术，奋力强攻，与对手同时完成有效命中。 按照规则，有效命中相同时，犯规更少一方获胜。整场决赛，李金山与能明昌零犯规，依靠沉稳作风与娴熟技法拿下警卫组双人对刺冠军！赛场掌声响起，李金山与队友热烈相拥，尽情庆祝胜利。 从替补陪练逆袭为全军冠军，从联勤兵成长为赛场勇士，李金山以汗水与坚守印证：联勤兵亦是战斗兵，保障员亦是战斗员。半个世纪薪火相传的木枪，在新时代官兵手中锋芒依旧。
