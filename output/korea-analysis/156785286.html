MAKRI Press Release: Identification and Return Ceremony of the Late Private Jo Cha-sang, ROK 6th Division (October 15, 2026)

IPR source-grounded analysis, machine-assisted and reviewed by an editor. Claims attributed to the Ministry of National Defense are not independently corroborated merely because they appear in an official release.

The Ministry of National Defense Agency for KIA Recovery and Identification (MAKRI) announced the identification of remains excavated in 2010 as those of Private Jo Cha-sang, and held a return ceremony on October 15, 2026, in Gimhae City. The identification was achieved through re-analysis of previously inconclusive DNA samples, approximately 76 years after the soldier's reported death in August 1950.

Remains Excavation and Physical Recovery

According to the release, the remains were first discovered in June 2010 at Hill 490 on Maebongsan Mountain in Junggu-ri, Hyoryeong-myeon, Gunwi-gun. A specialist MAKRI team subsequently recovered the left and right femurs. The release does not describe other skeletal elements. Discovery in 2010 should not be confused with the much later identification of Private Jo Cha-sang.

Claim type: Attributed official-source interpretation.

Original-source paragraph references: 10, 12.

Verbatim Korean evidence
◦국방부 유해발굴감식단(이하 국유단, 단장 전병희)은 2010년 6월 대구광역시 군위군 효령면 중구리 매봉산 일대에서 발굴한 유해의 신원을 국군 제6사단 소속의 고 조차상 일병으로 확인했다.
□ 고인의 유해는 2010년 6월 군위군 중구리 매봉산 490고지에서 유해발굴을 진행하던 중 최초 식별됐으며, 이후 국유단 전문 발굴팀이 고인의 좌우 넓적다리뼈를 수습했다.

DNA Identification: Prior Failure and Subsequent Confirmation

The release states that a DNA sample was collected from the family in 2012, but that the analytical techniques then available did not confirm a family relationship. It reports that additional testing of residual samples in March 2026 confirmed that the deceased and Jo Ok-ran were siblings. The document cites technical limitations for the initial failure; it does not establish whether any other factors contributed. These conclusions remain attributed to the issuing authority.

Claim type: Attributed official-source interpretation.

Original-source paragraph references: 13, 15.

Verbatim Korean evidence
□ 유가족 유전자 시료는 2012년 채취했으나, 당시 유전자 분석 기술로는 가족관계를 확인할 수 없었다.
◦국유단은 고인의 유해에서 추출한 유전자와 유가족 유전자 시료를 분석했으나, 안타깝게도 당시 유전자 분석 기술로는 가족관계를 확인할 수 없었다. 올해 3월, 유해시료 및 유가족 유전자 잔여 시료를 추가 검사한 결과 고 조차상 일병과 조옥란 씨가 남매관계임을 확인할 수 있었다.

Reported Service Record and Circumstances of Death

The release states that Private Jo Cha-sang was born in August 1930 in Bubuk-myeon, Miryang City, enlisted at the Army Training Center on July 17, 1950, and was killed in action on August 14, 1950, in the 'Battle near Gunwi-Uiheung.' The release further describes that battle as a defensive engagement by the 7th Regiment, ROK 6th Division, against North Korean 1st and 8th Division forces from August 13 to 28, 1950. These biographical and operational details are presented as established facts by the issuing authority; they are government assertions and are not independently verified by these sources.

Claim type: Attributed official-source interpretation.

Original-source paragraph references: 17, 18.

Verbatim Korean evidence
◦고인은 1930년 8월 경상남도 밀양시 부북면에서 9남매(7남 2녀) 중 장남으로 태어났다. 이후 1950년 7월 17일 육군훈련소로 입대한 고인은 제6사단 소속으로 적과 싸우다 1950년 8월 14일 ‘군위-의흥 부근 전투’에서 전사했다.
◦‘군위-의흥 부근 전투’는 국군 6사단 7연대가 1950년 8월 13일부터 28일까지 대구 군위군 효령면 미사리 일대에서 북한군 1·8사단의 공격을 방어한 전투다.

Cumulative Programme Statistics as Reported

The release characterises Private Jo Cha-sang as the fourteenth individual whose identity MAKRI confirmed in the year of the release, and the 282nd fallen ROK soldier returned to family since the programme commenced in April 2000. These figures are presented as programme metrics by the issuing authority and reflect the government's own accounting; they cannot be verified from the source material alone.

Claim type: Attributed official-source interpretation.

Original-source paragraph references: 11.

Verbatim Korean evidence
◦고인은 올해 국유단이 열네 번째로 신원을 확인한 호국영웅이며, 2000년 4월 유해발굴사업 개시 이래 가족의 품으로 돌아간 282번째 국군 전사자다.

Return Ceremony Conduct and Items Presented

The release states that the return ceremony was held at the home of Jo Ok-ran in Gimhae City in accordance with the bereaved family's wishes, and that MAKRI Director Jeon Byeong-hui presented the family with a National Defense Hero Return plaque, an identity confirmation notice, and a box containing excavated personal effects. The release also records a statement attributed to Jo Ok-ran referencing her mother's dying wish that her brother's remains be found and interred at a national cemetery. These are attributed statements and reported procedural facts; the emotional characterisation reflects the family representative's quoted words as rendered in the release.

Claim type: Attributed official-source interpretation.

Original-source paragraph references: 19, 20, 21.

Verbatim Korean evidence
□ 이번 귀환 행사는 유가족 뜻에 따라 경상남도 김해시에 위치한 고인의 여동생인 조옥란 씨의 자택에서 진행됐다.
◦유가족 대표 조옥란(1946년생, 80세) 씨는 “오빠 유해를 찾았다고 하니 심장이 덜덜 떨려서 잠을 못 잤다”며 “엄마가 돌아가실 때 나한테 ‘너라도 꼭 오빠 유해를 찾아서 국립묘지에 안장하라’고 유언을 남기셨는데 이렇게 찾게 해주셔서 감사하다”고 소회를 밝혔다.
◦행사를 주관한 전병희 국유단장은 유가족에게 호국영웅 귀환 패와 신원확인 통지서, 발굴 유품이 담긴 호국의 얼 함(函)을 전달했다. 이어 고인의 참전 경로와 유해발굴 경과, 신원확인까지의 과정을 설명했다.

Public Participation Appeal and Programme Access Information

The release includes a public appeal stating that DNA sample collection is open to relatives up to the eighth degree of kinship on both paternal and maternal sides, and that a reward of 10 million won is payable upon confirmed identification. It also notes that individuals with limited mobility may contact the number 1577-5625 for in-person sample collection. The release further asserts that finding bereaved families is becoming increasingly difficult due to the aging of veterans and their relatives. These are policy statements and programme descriptions issued by the government authority; their operational accuracy is not assessable from the source text alone.

◦유가족 유전자 시료 채취는 6·25 전사자의 유가족으로서 전사자 기준으로 친·외가 8촌까지 신청 가능합니다. 제공하신 유전자 정보를 통해 전사자의 신원이 확인될 경우 1,000만 원의 포상금을 지급합니다. 자세한 내용은 “정부 24” 홈페이지에서도 확인하실 수 있습니다.

Limits of the evidence

All factual claims in this analysis derive exclusively from a single government press release issued by MAKRI. Biographical details, service records, battle descriptions, DNA analysis conclusions, programme statistics, and quoted statements are reproduced as attributed government assertions and have not been cross-checked against independent sources. Machine translations are unreviewed and may contain rendering errors. The release date of October 15, 2026, is noted as stated in the source; no independent verification of this date or any other claim is possible from the provided material. No inference is drawn regarding intent, downstream effects, or any approval processes not described in the source. The captured Korean source header gives October 15, 2026 as a Wednesday, although that date was a Thursday; the body of the release correctly states Thursday. Both header translations preserve and identify the discrepancy.

Official source: Republic of Korea Policy Briefing. Evidence is confined to the cited Korean text; no media reproduced.
