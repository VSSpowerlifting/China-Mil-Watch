Singapore Desk

One publication family: official releases from the Singapore Ministry of Defence, which publishes for itself and for the Singapore Armed Forces.

Live — collecting

Promoted 2026-09-21 (DECISION_LOG), after 33 elapsed shadow-collection days, completed Day 7 and Day 14 human checkpoint reviews, and owner sign-off. The corpus began as a one-time governed promotion of 57 corrected shadow records; the same adapter, already run daily by the shadow collector since 2026-08-19, is now also invoked by the ordinary scheduled production pipeline, so the corpus grows from further scheduled runs going forward. Two records stay held out of the promotable set for unrepaired charset damage.

Limits of this desk, stated plainly

  • This is not whole-of-government coverage, not armed-forces-wide, and not comprehensive Singapore defense coverage.
  • Other ministries, the parliamentary Hansard record, unit and recruitment pages, magazine features, social media, and procurement notices are all outside the declared scope.
  • The corpus began as a one-time governed promotion; new records are now collected by the ordinary scheduled production pipeline, subject to the same held-record exclusions.
  • Two records are held out of the promoted corpus: their CJK text was damaged by a historical extractor defect the correction overlay could not repair, and they are excluded rather than published with damaged text. See shadow/singapore_mindef/CORRECTIONS.md for which records and why.

These are properties of the desk a reader needs before citing it — see Coverage for what each collection run actually returned.
