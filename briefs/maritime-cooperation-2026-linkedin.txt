Maritime Cooperation 2026: One Exercise, Two Official Accounts

Singapore's MINDEF places the naval exercise at Zhanjiang within RSS Steadfast's wider deployment. Its closing release and China's defense ministry both emphasize bilateral trust. Reading those accounts together preserves both contexts.

The dates are less tidy: MINDEF gives 5–9 September; China's ministry gives 3–9 September. The preserved records do not resolve that difference. Nor do the participants' statements of benefit provide independent measures of performance.

This retrospective compares five official-source records. It distinguishes the ministries' assertions, PLA Daily's reporting and the interpretation built from them.
